Ten Legal Due Diligence Priorities for Hotel Investors

REBusinessOnline — Thu, 10 Sep 2026 15:08:25 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

This article outlines key legal due diligence priorities for investors in hotel acquisitions, emphasizing that hotel deals often fail due to a lack of understanding of the business implications of property and operational issues, rather than obscure legal technicalities. The author, drawing on decades of experience, highlights the importance of thoroughly examining franchise agreements, management contracts, environmental conditions, and entitlements. Investors are advised to scrutinize assumable financing and ground leases, understand the labor landscape, and verify what assets and liabilities are truly being acquired beyond just the real estate. The piece underscores that hotels are operating businesses, not just real estate assets, requiring a comprehensive legal review that goes beyond standard property due diligence.

Report: Tariffs Won’t Impact Every U.S. Market in the Same Way

Connect CRE — Fri, 11 Sep 2026 05:01:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Trade Policy

A recent report by Cushman & Wakefield analyzes the uneven impact of U.S.-Canada tariffs across different U.S. markets, noting that states like Maryland, Kentucky, Texas, and Michigan have above-average exposure due to their reliance on heavily tariffed imports such as automotive parts and aluminum. Despite this, the report indicates that trade accounts for a relatively small portion of these states' GDP. The analysis suggests several implications for commercial real estate, including increased demand for third-party logistics and bonded warehouses as companies adopt 'just-in-case' inventory strategies and seek to delay tariff payments. Additionally, tariffs could accelerate onshoring trends, particularly in the automotive sector, and lead to higher development costs due to material-cost inflation, potentially shifting developer focus towards higher-conviction projects.
