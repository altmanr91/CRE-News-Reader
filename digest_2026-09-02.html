Tax-Exempt Commercial Mortgage-Backed Securities Are Having a Moment

Commercial Observer — Tue, 01 Sep 2026 20:00:08 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

The tax-exempt commercial mortgage-backed securities (CMBS) market is gaining traction as a financing mechanism for affordable housing in the U.S., driven by new federal rules and increased attention from ratings agencies and major banks. This structure offers lower capital costs compared to traditional taxable CMBS or agency-backed affordable housing loans, making it attractive to investors, especially in a higher interest rate environment. The market's evolution is supported by the strong credit performance of affordable housing and the ability to attract a broader institutional investor base, leading to increased demand and liquidity for these securitized offerings.

DATA POINTS

Spread savings between tax-exempt and taxable affordable housing securitizations: 50 to 60 basis points

Systima Capital Management's July 2026 tax-exempt affordable housing bond deal oversubscription: More than eight times oversubscribed, with over $1.2 billion in orders from 19 institutional investors

Federal housing bill's lowered tax-exempt private activity bond financing threshold for 4% LIHTCs: 25% from 50%
