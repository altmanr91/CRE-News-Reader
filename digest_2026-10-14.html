Inflation Accelerates to 3.7% as Gasoline Prices Surge

Connect CRE — Wed, 14 Oct 2026 12:57:24 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Inflation Report

U.S. consumer prices saw an accelerated increase in September, with annual inflation reaching 3.7%, up from 3.4% in August. This surge was primarily driven by a significant 9.1% jump in gasoline prices, which accounted for over half of the monthly increase. Core inflation, excluding volatile food and energy components, also rose to 2.6% annually, suggesting broader inflationary pressures beyond just energy costs. Shelter costs continued their upward trend, contributing to the overall inflationary environment.

Data Center Chip Market Set to Reach $1 Trillion by 2035

Connect CRE — Wed, 14 Oct 2026 01:10:40 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Technology Report

A new report by DC Market Insights projects the global data center chip market to exceed $1 trillion by 2035, growing from $314.87 billion in 2025 at a CAGR of 12.49%. North America is leading this growth, driven significantly by AI accelerators, which constituted 53.9% of the market in 2025. The report highlights a structural shift where accelerators now hold more value than CPUs, memory, or networking individually, with custom in-house silicon expected to nearly double its market share by 2035 as hyperscalers increasingly design workload-specific chips.

Former religious buildings could be blessing for multifamily investors

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

This article explores the growing trend of converting former religious buildings into multifamily housing, highlighting their unique appeal to investors and cities seeking to expand housing options. These properties often boast established locations in dense neighborhoods, distinctive architecture that offers a competitive advantage in leasing, and a positive community reception due to preservation efforts. While presenting challenges such as unusual unit configurations and higher utility costs, experienced teams can mitigate these issues. The conversions contribute to the broader adaptive reuse movement, offering a sustainable way to add housing and expand the tax base without requiring new land.
