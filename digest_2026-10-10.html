Northern Virginia Office Vacancy Falls Again as AI, Defense Demand Builds

Commercial Observer — Fri, 09 Oct 2026 17:45:10 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Office Report

Northern Virginia's office market experienced a sixth consecutive quarter of declining vacancy rates in Q3 2026, reaching 21.7%, driven by strong demand from artificial intelligence, technology, and defense sectors. The market recorded 672,378 square feet of positive net absorption, largely due to a significant relocation by a government agency. Despite a slowdown in overall leasing activity, new leases and relocations are dominating the market, indicating a shift from the previous renewal-focused era. This tightening supply is beginning to bolster rents, particularly for Class A properties, while new construction remains minimal and redevelopment proposals are high.

DATA POINTS

Q3 2026 Net Absorption: 672,378 square feet

Office Vacancy Rate Q3 2026: 21.7%

Total Available Space Q3 2026: 33.6 million square feet

Peak Available Space 2023: 45.1 million square feet

Q3 2026 Leasing Activity: 1.3 million square feet

AI, Tech, Defense Net New Space Q3 2026: 300,000+ square feet

Relocations and New Leases Share of 2026 Leasing: 58%

Class A Direct Asking Rents: $42.51 per square foot per year

Overall Direct Asking Rents: $38.13 per square foot per year

New Construction Underway: 34,000 square feet

Office Inventory Proposed for Redevelopment: 10+ million square feet

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Northern Virginia's office vacancy rate has fallen for six consecutive quarters to 21.7%. Total available space is at its lowest level in a decade at 33.6 million square feet, a 25.5% decrease from the 2023 peak of 45.1 million square feet. Class A direct asking rents reached $42.51/SF/year, while overall direct asking rents averaged $38.13. Asking rents for Trophy, Class A, and Class B properties have risen since 2022, while Class C rents have declined. Only 34,000 square feet of new office construction is underway, with over 10 million square feet proposed for redevelopment.
