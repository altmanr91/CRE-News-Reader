How A New Financing Model Helps Spur More Mixed-Income Housing

Bisnow News Feed — Tue, 28 Jul 2026 16:36:54 -0400

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

A new report from the Urban Institute highlights MassHousing's Bringing Innovation to Lending and Development (BILD) program as a promising model for spurring mixed-income housing development. The program, which uses public funds as both debt and equity, aims to fill capital stack gaps for projects stalled by high interest rates and construction costs. MassHousing has already committed $31 million in equity and over $189 million in debt through the program, funding six projects totaling 605 units. The BILD program's innovative approach, including a subordinate loan and preferred equity with lower return requirements, helps mitigate risk for lenders and makes projects financially feasible while maintaining affordability.

A nice surprise: Some good news for the Chicago area’s office market

REJournals — Wed, 29 Jul 2026 10:01:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Office Report

Metropolitan Chicago's office market experienced a positive shift in the second quarter, with both downtown and suburban areas recording strong positive net absorption, signaling a potential stabilization in tenant demand. Downtown Chicago saw over 1.08 million square feet of positive absorption, its best performance since early 2021, while the suburban market added another 605,806 square feet. This improvement is attributed to companies leasing more space in higher-quality buildings and a decrease in available sublease space, suggesting a cautious return to office commitments despite a slower economy and hybrid work models.

DATA POINTS

Downtown Chicago Q2 2026 Net Absorption: 1.08 million square feet

Suburban Chicago Q2 2026 Net Absorption: 605,806 square feet

Metropolitan Chicago Q2 2026 Total Net Absorption: 1.7 million square feet

Suburban Chicago Office Vacancy Rate: 26.1%

Downtown Chicago Office Vacancy Rate: 24.9%

Suburban Chicago Q2 2026 Leasing Activity: 1.05 million square feet

Downtown Chicago Q2 2026 Leasing Activity: 1.31 million square feet

Suburban Office Properties Traded Q2 2026: 11 properties

Total Sales Volume Suburban Office Q2 2026: $72.08 million

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

The suburban Chicago office market's vacancy rate fell to 26.1% from 26.6% a year ago. Class-A properties accounted for nearly three-quarters of the positive absorption in the suburbs and 55% of all leasing activity. Downtown Chicago's vacancy rate fell to 24.9% after reaching a record high in Q1 2026. Sublease space in downtown Chicago dropped to its lowest level since the pandemic began. Mid-sized suburban office buildings (20,000-100,000 SF) are outperforming the rest of the sector with consistent leasing activity and below-average vacancy rates. Office investment sales in the suburbs totaled $72.08 million across 11 properties in Q2 2026.

Lineage Fined For Rotting Food, Swarms Of Flies At Burned LA Cold Storage Warehouse

Bisnow News Feed — Tue, 28 Jul 2026 15:32:23 -0400

SUMMARY

CRE Legal / Regulatory

Lineage Logistics is facing daily fines and community backlash due to unsanitary conditions at its 500,000-square-foot cold storage warehouse in Boyle Heights, Los Angeles, which suffered a major fire in June. Residents are reporting pervasive odors from rotting food and an infestation of flies and rats, prompting calls for a mandatory evacuation. The Los Angeles County Department of Public Health has cited Lineage for nuisance conditions, imposing a $500 daily fine until the issues are resolved. The City of Los Angeles has mandated that Lineage remove all spoiled food by August 14, threatening legal action if the deadline is not met.
