AI Has Saved Time But Muddied Traffic Data For CRE Marketing Execs

Bisnow News Feed — Fri, 24 Jul 2026 13:20:14 -0400

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

Commercial real estate marketing executives are finding that artificial intelligence has significantly streamlined their workflows, compressing tasks that once took weeks into mere days. However, this rapid adoption of AI has introduced complexities in accurately tracking the effectiveness of marketing strategies, particularly concerning web traffic data. While AI tools have elevated output expectations and reduced costs for materials like building renderings, the inability of large language models to directly link to company websites can lead to misattributed organic search traffic, obscuring the true impact of AI-generated leads. Industry leaders emphasize the importance of a closed-loop environment for sensitive information and a tailored marketing approach that considers the distinct priorities of tenants (operational aspects) versus investors (portfolio-level execution and risk mitigation).

SUMMARY

Market Research / Earnings Report

Rexford Industrial Realty, a Los Angeles-based REIT, is significantly increasing its disposition strategy, planning up to $2 billion in sales of "noncore assets" totaling approximately 8 million square feet. Despite negative rent spreads on renewals, Rexford maintains strong occupancy and plans to start 1.2 million square feet of new projects this year.
