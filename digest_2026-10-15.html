Power Availability Defining Most Coveted Locations in CRE

Commercial Observer — Thu, 15 Oct 2026 13:00:08 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / AI & Power Infrastructure

A new report from the Urban Land Institute (ULI) and Heitman highlights that power availability and robust broadband connections are becoming critical determinants of commercial real estate value and investment, driven by the surge in demand for artificial intelligence. The report projects global data center investment to reach $7.6 trillion by 2030, with $3 trillion directly tied to real estate. Markets capable of quickly producing new power sources and leveraging existing grid infrastructure will gain a competitive advantage, with industrial facilities, pre-approved powered land, and battery storage properties also poised to benefit. The authors emphasize that real estate values will increasingly be shaped by power availability, proximity to users, and technological readiness, influencing property design and requiring resilient power sources for automation and robotics in industrial assets.

Insurance Is Taking a Bigger Bite Out of Apartment Revenue

Commercial Observer — Wed, 14 Oct 2026 22:25:06 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Insurance Costs

Apartment insurance costs, after five years of increases, saw a modest 2.2% decline in 2025, according to CRED iQ's analysis. This relief, however, comes after a significant 28.5% jump in 2023, leaving current premiums more than double their 2019 levels. The Sun Belt markets, which experienced the sharpest increases, are now seeing the most significant rate reductions, with Florida, Texas, and Louisiana recording drops of 9.8%, 8.4%, and 10.5% respectively in 2025. Conversely, coastal apartment owners, particularly in California, continue to face rising premiums, with a 6.3% increase in California apartments in 2025. A key takeaway is that while the rate of increase has slowed, apartment revenue growth has not kept pace with the surge in insurance costs, leading to a larger portion of revenue being consumed by premiums.

CMBS Special Servicing Rate Climbs to New Post-2013 High

Connect CRE — Thu, 15 Oct 2026 01:42:35 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / CMBS Report

The CMBS special servicing rate increased by seven basis points in September to 11.49%, reaching its highest point since February 2013. This rise followed a more significant 33-basis-point increase in August. While the overall rate climbed, performance varied by property type, with retail, lodging, and multifamily seeing increases, while mixed-use, office, and industrial experienced declines. New transfers to special servicing totaled approximately $2.65 billion, with office properties representing the majority of these transfers.

Scarcity, Privacy and Authenticity Are Luxury Development’s Calling Cards Now

Commercial Observer — Thu, 15 Oct 2026 11:00:50 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

This opinion piece argues that the definition of luxury in residential real estate has shifted from quantifiable metrics like square footage and amenity counts to experiential qualities such as privacy, authenticity, craftsmanship, and a genuine sense of place. The author, a developer, emphasizes that discerning buyers are increasingly seeking homes that respond thoughtfully to their neighborhood's history and character, rather than following generic formulas. Key supporting points include the importance of natural materials, durable construction, integrated outdoor spaces, and bespoke amenities that foster community and connection to the local environment. The author concludes that creating lasting value requires developers to invest in elements that ensure a building's longevity, functionality, and contextual relevance, even if these investments don't offer immediate marketing payoffs.
