Looking Past the Noise: A More Nuanced View of Multifamily

Commercial Observer — Wed, 12 Aug 2026 04:00:24 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

This article, based on a podcast discussion, argues that the multifamily market is moving beyond broad national trends to a phase defined by local realities, operational discipline, and selective opportunities. The author emphasizes the market's resilience despite a historic supply wave, noting that national rent declines and vacancy increases have been modest. While acknowledging a 'choppy' and 'renter-favorable' environment, the piece highlights that strong absorption data contradicts some negative perceptions. The author concludes that future performance will increasingly depend on execution, asset positioning, and local market dynamics as new supply normalizes and demand proves more durable.

SME Capital Ventures is transferring ownership of 441 West 37th Street, a nine-unit condominium building in Hudson Yards, to its condominium board following an investigation by the New York Attorney General's office. A Record Rent, a Demand Reversal and a New Model for the West Side: Chicago industrial at mid-year

REJournals — Tue, 11 Aug 2026 10:05:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

Chicago's industrial market achieved a record average asking net rent of over $8 per square foot in Q2 2026, with robust first-half leasing activity totaling 25 million square feet. While the market is healthy, a notable shift has occurred where small-bay infill product has lagged behind big-box absorption, driven by major retailers and third-party logistics firms. Class A industrial properties continue to see rent increases due to tenant demand for quality and functionality, while Class B and C rents have plateaued or decreased. Developers are increasingly focusing on community benefits, such as job creation, green space, and clean energy, to secure entitlements and financing for urban infill projects. The market is also seeing a rise in sale-leaseback transactions as corporate owners seek to unlock capital from appreciated real estate assets, with strong buyer demand compressing single-tenant net lease cap rates.

DATA POINTS

Average asking net industrial rent (Q2 2026): $8+ per square foot

First-half industrial leasing activity (2026): 25 million square feet

Cap rate compression for single-tenant net lease: 25 basis points over the past year

SUMMARY

Infrastructure Investment / Government Policy

Texas Governor Greg Abbott has implemented a temporary pause on new power connections for data centers, a move intended to address the escalating power demands from the rapidly expanding data center industry.
