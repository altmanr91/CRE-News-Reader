Sunday Summary: The Rent is Too Damn Low!

Commercial Observer — Sun, 16 Aug 2026 13:00:15 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

This article provides a market commentary on New York City's real estate landscape, focusing on the ongoing debate about housing affordability. It discusses the impact of rent freezes on rent-stabilized apartments, noting that some landlords are withholding units due to high renovation costs and insufficient rental income. The piece also examines the initial effects of the pied-à-terre tax on luxury apartments, finding a modest increase in sales over $5 million, though a dip in the ultra-luxury segment. Ultimately, the author suggests that increasing housing supply, rather than taxes or freezes, is the most effective solution for long-term affordability.
