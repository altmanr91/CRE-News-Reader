Opportunity Zone 2.0: Beefing Up an Investment Tool

Connect CRE — Fri, 10 Jul 2026 05:01:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

The article discusses the evolution of the Opportunity Zone program, highlighting the transition from the original 2017 framework to the new "Opportunity Zone 2.0" introduced as part of the One Big Beautiful Bill Act. This updated legislation aims to enhance the program's attractiveness to investors by making tax benefits permanent, offering a rolling five-year deferral period for capital gains, and providing specific incentives for rural development. Commercial real estate attorneys note that these changes, particularly the elimination of fixed deadlines and the reduced "substantial improvement" requirement for rural properties, are expected to drive an influx of new investments into Qualified Opportunity Funds (QOFs). SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

This article argues that the prevailing narrative of "oversupply" in the Sunbelt multifamily market is overly simplistic and misleading. While acknowledging the significant increase in new apartment deliveries between 2023 and 2025, the author and industry experts contend that treating the Sunbelt as a monolithic market ignores crucial differences in local economies, demographics, and demand drivers.
