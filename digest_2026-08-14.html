SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

Despite persistent inflation, stable interest rates, trade uncertainty, and a softening labor market in the first half of 2026, the commercial real estate sector has demonstrated resilience. The Real Estate Question About Quantum Computing

Connect CRE — Thu, 13 Aug 2026 22:48:28 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Technology Infrastructure

JLL's "Future of Quantum Real Estate" report indicates that quantum computing infrastructure is expanding faster than the necessary real estate to support it. While currently concentrated on university campuses, the private sector is expected to play a much larger role in the next decade. Quantum real estate, distinct from traditional data centers, requires specialized facilities with features like dilution refrigerators, vibration isolation, and electromagnetic shielding, varying based on the quantum technology modality. The sector is poised to transition from public ownership to greater private-sector participation, with over $4 billion in quantum-related real estate projects globally either planned or under construction.

Javits Center Expansion, Tech Upgrades Boosting NYC Hospitality Industry

Commercial Observer — Thu, 13 Aug 2026 19:37:03 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Hospitality

The Javits Center in New York City has completed a $5 million technology upgrade, installing Wi-Fi 7 to enhance its appeal for major conferences and events. This upgrade follows a $1.5 billion expansion completed in 2021, which added 1.2 million square feet, including a large ballroom and a green rooftop pavilion. The improvements aim to boost the city's hospitality industry by attracting more large-scale conventions, with the Javits Center estimating its bookings generated over 100,000 hotel stays in 2025. The increased capacity and technological capabilities are enabling the center to host a wider range of events, from medical conferences to large fan festivals, and are expected to drive further growth in the surrounding area, including new hotel developments.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE
