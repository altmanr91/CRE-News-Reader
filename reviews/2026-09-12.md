### Should Have Been Filtered

- "Andreessen Horowitz to Open West Palm Office for Defense-Tech Bets" — This is a venture capital/defense-tech company leasing office space; the real estate is incidental to the corporate expansion story, not a CRE transaction of investor/lender significance. The tenant is a VC firm, not a real estate principal. — Fix: Add filter rule to exclude office leases by non-CRE corporate tenants (PE/VC, tech, defense contractors) where the lease is reported as corporate news rather than market data.

- "Cadence McShane Construction Company wins Multifamily Project of the Year at REDnews Real Estate Awards" — This is a promotion/award announcement for a trade contractor, not a CRE transaction or market development. — Fix: Exclude award/promotion articles for contractors and service providers; these are marketing content, not CRE intelligence.

- "Friedman's Restaurant Expands with Upper West Side Location" — A single-unit restaurant expansion lease/acquisition is a retail business transaction, not CRE investment-grade content. This belongs in retail brokerage news, not a CRE digest for investors and lenders. — Fix: Exclude single-unit retail tenant expansions and QSR leases under 10,000 SF unless part of significant portfolio activity.

### Data Quality

- "Gardner Tanenbaum Completes $60M Adaptive Reuse Project in Downtown Oklahoma City" — $/SF metric is implausible: $13,953/SF on a 4,300 SF multifamily building is absurd. This appears to be a data extraction error; likely the 4,300 SF figure is wrong (should be substantially larger) or the project cost is miscaptured. — Fix: Require validation: recalculate $60M ÷ 265 units = $226k/unit (plausible for adaptive reuse), then back out true SF. Flag extraction for manual review before publication.

- "In Health Care Real Estate, Creativity Abounds — It Has To" — Companies_people list includes regulatory/contextual entities (AECOM Tishman, Gilbane, KPMF as general contractors; Zetlin & De Chiara as law firm) mixed with actual owners. The pipeline entries are incomplete/truncated and lack deal metrics. This reads as an opinion/vendor commentary piece with embedded case studies, not a transaction or market research digest item. — Fix: Either exclude opinion pieces with embedded pipeline references, or require complete deal metrics and clear primary transaction focus.

- "Fertility Provider Opens Clinic At 1900 Lawrence Office Tower: The Denver Deal Sheet" — Tenant company name "Spring Fertility" is provided but no transaction principal data (landlord, broker, lease terms, rent) is captured. This is a near-empty record. — Fix: Require minimum data fields for lease articles: landlord, lease rate (annual or $/SF), and tenant rep broker before inclusion.
