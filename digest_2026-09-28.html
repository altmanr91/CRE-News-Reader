NMHC Survey Highlights Challenging Environment for Apartment Development

Connect CRE — Mon, 28 Sep 2026 14:49:13 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Development Survey

The National Multifamily Housing Council's (NMHC) Q3 2026 survey indicates a challenging landscape for apartment development, primarily due to escalating construction costs, economic uncertainty, and subdued rent growth. A significant portion of developers reported initiating fewer projects, citing financial infeasibility and economic concerns as key deterrents. Despite these headwinds, a notable segment of respondents maintains an optimistic outlook for overall construction conditions over the next 6-12 months, with expectations for improved debt and equity financing conditions.

Perkins&Will transforms former industrial barge terminal into regional park in Minneapolis

REJournals — Mon, 28 Sep 2026 14:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Development

Perkins&Will, in collaboration with the Minneapolis Park and Recreation Board and the City of Minneapolis, has transformed a former industrial barge terminal into the new 20-acre Upper Harbor Regional Park and Riverfront Trail in North Minneapolis. The park's design, which involved extensive community engagement over three years, prioritizes ecological restoration, river access, and flexible gathering spaces, with future amenities to be phased in alongside affordable housing development. This approach, termed "just green enough," addresses community concerns about potential displacement and aims to reconnect residents with the Mississippi River while restoring habitat and integrating stormwater infrastructure.
