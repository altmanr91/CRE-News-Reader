What we learned from Zillow’s courtroom clash with Chicago MLS and Compass

The Real Deal — Chicago — Sat, 04 Jul 2026 13:30:00 +0000

SUMMARY

CRE Legal / Regulatory

A federal judge is deliberating a dispute between Zillow, Compass, and Chicago's multiple listing service (MLS), Midwest Real Estate Data (MRED), regarding control over residential real estate listings. Zillow alleges that MRED's decision to cut off its data feed in May was a coordinated boycott to protect Compass's private exclusive listings, which Zillow claims create a 'velvet rope' effect in the market. Compass and MRED, however, assert that Zillow failed to comply with objective criteria rules for listing filtering, with MRED's CEO defending the decision as an enforcement of fair listing practices. The outcome of this case could significantly impact the future of residential inventory control and the traditional MLS ecosystem nationwide.
