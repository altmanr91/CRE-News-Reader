Commercial Observer — Fri, 30 Oct 2026 20:54:54 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

The impending dissolution of white-shoe law firm Weil, Gotshal & Manges, which occupies 447,000 square feet at BXP's General Motors Building, presents a significant opportunity for the Manhattan office market. Despite the firm's lease commitment until 2034, the sudden availability of this Class A space is well-timed given the borough's booming office market, characterized by strong leasing activity and rapidly shrinking supply for trophy assets. Market experts anticipate voracious demand for the space, potentially allowing BXP to secure significantly higher rents than the current in-place rate, which is reportedly under $150 per square foot, compared to current trophy asking rents approaching $200 per square foot.
