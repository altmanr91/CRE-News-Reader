Now what? Rafael Cestero on the rent freeze aftermath

The Real Deal — New York — Thu, 02 Jul 2026 23:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

Rafael Cestero, CEO of Community Preservation Corporation, discusses the implications of New York City's recent rent freeze on rent-stabilized buildings. He argues that while the freeze was expected, the critical question is what policy changes will follow to sustain these properties. Cestero highlights the significant decline in rent-stabilized portfolio values since 2019 and warns of further degradation without relief on operating expenses. He advocates for short-term solutions like a public insurance vehicle and property tax abatements, alongside long-term reforms such as replacing the Rent Guidelines Board with a formula-based rent-setting system to depoliticize the process and attract private capital for maintenance. Cestero expresses concern over stagnant repair and maintenance spending, a key indicator of physical distress, and the potential for private capital to withdraw if lenders underwrite with zero rent growth and rising expenses, which would further strain the system.
