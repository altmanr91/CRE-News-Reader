Liability Litigation Is Choking Multifamily Housing

Commercial Observer — Thu, 03 Sep 2026 11:00:34 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

This article argues that escalating liability insurance costs and reduced availability are severely impacting the multifamily housing sector across the U.S., particularly for smaller landlords. The author contends that "nuclear verdicts" (jury awards over $10 million) and "social inflation" have led insurers to tighten terms, raise prices, and reduce coverage, creating a systemic problem that threatens housing development and affordability. The piece proposes several solutions, including lenders integrating insurance into credit underwriting as a nuanced risk variable, insurers expanding partnership-oriented underwriting to reward risk-control investments, and regulators implementing targeted tort reforms to restore predictability to liability outcomes. Additionally, the author suggests investing in common data standards for loss control and developing programs to provide protection and predictability for mid-market operators, emphasizing that a dysfunctional insurance market ultimately harms renters through increased costs and constrained housing supply.
