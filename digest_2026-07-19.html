NY Dirt: Mamdani begins Rental Ripoff rollout, starting small

The Real Deal — New York — Sat, 18 Jul 2026 23:30:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani's administration has released its "Rental Ripoff Report," outlining 23 initiatives aimed at addressing tenant complaints and improving living conditions. The report details plans for increased enforcement on landlords, including immediate actions like enhanced inspection scheduling and sending inspectors for every new heat complaint. However, many of the proposed reforms, such as cracking down on mold remediation and expanding the city's lien authority, will require formal rulemaking, City Council approval, or legal action. The Mayor's Office to Protect Tenants will coordinate with Council members to advance these legislative changes, with initial discussions already underway with the Housing and Buildings Committee chair.

How the Pfizer building scare could push developers to rethink vertical conversion plans

The Real Deal — New York — Sat, 18 Jul 2026 12:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

The near collapse of the Pfizer building conversion project has prompted real estate developers and city officials to re-evaluate the risks and regulations associated with vertical office-to-residential conversions, particularly those involving significant overbuilds. The incident is expected to lead to new regulations or stricter enforcement, potentially increasing the cost and complexity of such projects. This could diminish the value of air rights, which have historically been a lucrative asset for owners of buildings targeted for conversion, as lenders may become more cautious and developers less willing to pay a premium for the added development potential.
