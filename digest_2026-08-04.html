Fertile ground for industrial tenants? In the Chicago area it’s increasingly in the I-90 Northwest corridor

REJournals — Tue, 04 Aug 2026 09:58:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Industrial Report

The I-90 Northwest corridor of the Chicago industrial market is experiencing a significant migration of tenants from other submarkets, including O'Hare, Central DuPage, and Northwest Cook. This trend is driven by the availability of newer, more efficient facilities and lower occupancy costs, while still providing access to the same labor pool and transportation infrastructure. Approximately 65% of the 2 million square feet of new leasing activity in the I-90 Northwest submarket since January 2025 has come from companies relocating from other Chicago-area submarkets, indicating a deliberate shift by businesses seeking long-term operational and financial advantages.
