Sunday Summary: Welcome Back, Speyer!

ACQUISITION

Commercial Observer — Sun, 11 Oct 2026 13:00:13 +0000

SUMMARY

Tishman Speyer has reacquired the ground lease for the iconic Chrysler Building in New York City, committing $235 million for property improvements. This marks Tishman Speyer's return to ownership of the skyscraper, which it previously held from 1997 to 2008. The deal comes at a time when the Manhattan office market is experiencing strong momentum, with robust consumer spending and high stock market performance contributing to a positive economic outlook for New York's asset-based economy. The new ground lease is set for 150 years, signaling a long-term investment in the historic property.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Property Name: Chrysler Building

Address: 405 Lexington Avenue, New York City, NY*

Notable Features: Iconic, centrally located skyscraper in Midtown Manhattan.

Project Notes: Tishman Speyer previously owned the ground lease from 1997 to 2008. The property was sold in 2019 to RFR Realty for $150 million, but RFR was later pushed out due to missed rent payments and needed improvements. The new ground lease term is 150 years.
