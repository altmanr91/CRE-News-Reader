Six considerations for spec suites in an evolving office market

REJournals — Fri, 16 Oct 2026 12:34:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

This article argues that speculative, or "spec," suites are becoming the preferred option for office tenants due to their speed and value, shifting away from traditional tenant allowances. The author contends that modern businesses prioritize smarter use of space and nimble decision-making, which spec suites facilitate by offering move-in ready, furnished, and technologically equipped spaces with predetermined costs and occupancy dates. Landlords are advised to focus on creating high-quality, hospitality-focused spaces that balance collaboration and focus, rather than maximizing density. The article emphasizes that spec suites demonstrate landlord investment and stability, appealing to tenants seeking long-term partnerships and a high-performing office environment that can compete with remote work.

Consumer Goods Companies Scrutinize CRE Portfolios As Technology Spending Lags

Connect CRE — Thu, 15 Oct 2026 19:32:06 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Industry Report

A recent JLL report highlights that consumer goods companies are intensely scrutinizing their real estate portfolios, driven by supply chain disruptions and evolving consumer spending patterns. The survey of 48 industry leaders indicates a strong focus on supply chain examination, significantly higher than the all-industry average. Despite this heightened focus on real estate strategy, technology investment in CRE for consumer goods firms lags behind other sectors, suggesting a potential gap in leveraging data for optimal decision-making. The report also notes workforce downsizing and an expectation for increased office use rates among these companies, underscoring a dynamic approach to portfolio management.

Opinion / Market Commentary
