How Landlords Are Cutting Their Eviction Costs

Commercial Observer — Tue, 25 Aug 2026 04:00:02 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

This article, authored by the CEO of PandaGuarantee, argues that landlords can significantly reduce eviction costs by utilizing institutional rent guarantee insurance. The author contends that the largest component of eviction costs is lost rent during lengthy court proceedings, particularly in New York City where such costs can range from $15,000 to $50,000 per contested eviction. The piece highlights how the 2019 Housing Stability and Tenant Protection Act (HSTPA) in New York, which capped security deposits, has inadvertently made it harder for 'borderline' applicants to secure housing and for landlords to mitigate risk. The author advocates for a three-track applicant approval process that incorporates institutional lease guarantee bonds for these middle-tier applicants, asserting that this approach can cut financial damage from defaults by covering missed rent quickly, thereby transforming a landlord's risk profile.
