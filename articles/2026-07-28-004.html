Legal AI Firm Sandstone Signs 14K-SF Lease at 141 East Houston Street

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Mon, 27 Jul 2026 19:51:11 +0000

SUMMARY

Legal AI firm Sandstone has signed a five-year lease for 13,794 square feet across two floors at 141 East Houston Street in Manhattan's Lower East Side. The transaction, with an asking rent of $93 per square foot, reflects a continuing market trend where high-quality, newly built, and fully furnished spaces are attracting tenants. The building was developed by East End Capital, Grandview Partners, and KPG Funds between 2020 and 2022.
