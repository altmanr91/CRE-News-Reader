RSA Properties to Develop 130,000 SF Industrial Facility for Camco Chemical in Northern Kentucky

DEVELOPMENT

REBusinessOnline — Wed, 02 Sep 2026 12:23:53 +0000

SUMMARY

RSA Properties LLC is developing a 130,000-square-foot build-to-suit warehouse and distribution center for Camco Chemical in Independence, KY. The new facility will be located within Camco Chemical's existing campus on Holton Drive, on a site previously occupied by an IRS facility. Furlong Building is serving as the general contractor for the project.
