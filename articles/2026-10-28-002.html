JLL Optimized Cash Flow in Q3 With Growth in Office, Data Centers

Commercial Observer — Wed, 28 Oct 2026 14:34:11 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Earnings Report

JLL reported robust third-quarter 2026 earnings, driven by strong performance in its office, industrial, and data center sectors. The firm saw a 27% increase in leasing services revenue and a 21% rise in capital markets business, leading to a 12% overall revenue growth to $7.3 billion. JLL plans a $500 million share repurchasing program, reflecting confidence in continued momentum and efficient operations despite a slight slowdown in capital markets deal-making attributed to interest rate hikes.
