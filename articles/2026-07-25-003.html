Carlyle Group, Haussmann Submit Plans for 99 Units at Brooklyn’s 566 Grand Avenue

DEVELOPMENT

Commercial Observer — Fri, 24 Jul 2026 18:00:25 +0000

SUMMARY

Carlyle Group and Haussmann Development have submitted plans to the New York City Department of Buildings for a 12-story, 99-unit mixed-use apartment complex in Brooklyn's Crown Heights neighborhood. The proposed development at 566 Grand Avenue will include ground-floor retail and a range of amenities. The 99-unit count suggests the developers may be aiming to utilize the 485-x tax abatement program while avoiding its higher construction wage requirements for projects with 100 or more units.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Address: 566 Grand Avenue, Brooklyn, NY

Units: 99

Notable Features: Proposed 12-story mixed-use building with ground-floor retail and amenities including a pet spa, tenant theater, game room, children's playroom, coworking space, gym, and outdoor lounge.

Project Notes: Plans filed with the New York City Department of Buildings. The 99-unit count may be intended to qualify for the 485-x tax abatement program without triggering higher construction wage requirements.
