Data Center Chip Market Set to Reach $1 Trillion by 2035

Connect CRE — Wed, 14 Oct 2026 01:10:40 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Technology Report

A new report by DC Market Insights projects the global data center chip market to exceed $1 trillion by 2035, growing from $314.87 billion in 2025 at a CAGR of 12.49%. North America is leading this growth, driven significantly by AI accelerators, which constituted 53.9% of the market in 2025. The report highlights a structural shift where accelerators now hold more value than CPUs, memory, or networking individually, with custom in-house silicon expected to nearly double its market share by 2035 as hyperscalers increasingly design workload-specific chips.

DATA POINTS

Global data center chip market value (2025): US$314.87 billion

Projected global data center chip market value (2035): US$1 trillion

CAGR (2025-2035): 12.49%

AI accelerator spending (2025): US$169.77 billion

AI accelerator market share (2025): 53.9%

Custom in-house silicon market share (2025): 16.6%

Projected custom in-house silicon market share (2035): 29.7%
