Taconic, NREA Add Premium Indian Restaurant to Hell’s Kitchen Mixed-Use

LEASE

Connect CRE — Mon, 24 Aug 2026 22:01:40 +0000

SUMMARY

Taconic Partners and National Real Estate Advisors have secured a new lease with Samudhra, a premium Indian restaurant and lounge, at The Ellery, a luxury mixed-use residential development in Hell's Kitchen. This 10,000-square-foot lease marks Samudhra's first New York City location, enhancing the property's retail and dining offerings for both residents and the surrounding neighborhood.
