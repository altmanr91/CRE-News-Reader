$27M Lakeview co-op building renovation tops Chicago’s construction permits: Week ending July 23, 2026

CONSTRUCTION

The Real Deal — Chicago — Thu, 23 Jul 2026 21:30:00 +0000

SUMMARY

The largest construction permit issued in Chicago last week was for a $27 million infrastructure renovation at a co-op building in Lakeview. The project at 655 West Irving Park Road involves replacing water risers, waste and vent piping, and HVAC piping, along with installing new ceilings on three floors. Other significant permits included a $23.2 million interior renovation for an apartment building in Auburn Gresham, a $17.5 million restroom alteration project at O'Hare International Airport, and a $15 million new residential building in the West Loop.
