Winstead PC adds shareholder to Real Estate Development & Investments Practice Group in Houston

REJournals — Fri, 04 Sep 2026 09:35:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Promotion

McCall Grimes has joined Winstead PC's Houston office as a Shareholder in the Real Estate Development & Investments Practice Group. Grimes brings extensive experience advising sponsors, developers, and investors on various real estate and strategic transactions, including joint ventures, acquisitions, development projects, financings, and fund formations. His background includes serving as General Counsel for The Dinerstein Companies, a prominent multifamily and student housing developer, where he advised on nationwide transactions and investments.
