Owner-User Acquires South Brooklyn Mixed-Use

SALE

Connect CRE — Wed, 09 Sep 2026 21:04:36 +0000

SUMMARY

GD Capital has acquired a mixed-use property at 179 Ave. U in Brooklyn's Gravesend neighborhood from G4 Development Group for $1,536,000. The transaction was facilitated by Marcus & Millichap, which identified an owner-user for the property, allowing the seller to achieve a price above typical investor rates while providing the buyer with office space for their team.
