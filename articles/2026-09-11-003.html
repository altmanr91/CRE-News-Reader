Jay Roberts of Prosper Group: 5 Questions

ACQUISITION

Commercial Observer — Thu, 10 Sep 2026 20:35:05 +0000

SUMMARY

Developer Jay Roberts, CEO of Prosper Group, recently acquired a 1-acre waterfront site along the Miami River in Miami's Brickell district for $50 million. The site, which includes 66 Southwest Sixth Street and 625 Southwest First Avenue, is slated for a two-phase luxury high-rise development, with the first phase planned as a 60-story residential tower with 181 units. Roberts, who transitioned from Wall Street, emphasizes his firm's focus on best-in-class waterfront development sites and leveraging his finance background for capital raising and joint venture structuring.
