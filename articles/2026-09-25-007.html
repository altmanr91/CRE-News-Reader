Skyline Developers Completes 97-Unit Apartment Building in Midtown Manhattan

CONSTRUCTION

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 25 Sep 2026 14:27:28 +0000

SUMMARY

Skyline Developers has completed 18W55, a 97-unit apartment building in Midtown Manhattan. The 25-story property, which also includes 5,265 square feet of retail space, began construction in mid-2023 and is now fully occupied after leasing launched nine months ago.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: 18W55

Address: 18 W. 55th St., New York City, NY

Size: 5,265 SF

Units: 97

Current Occupancy: 100.0%

Completion: September 2026

Notable Features: Features studio, one-, two- and three-bedroom floor plans, a fitness and wellness center, coworking suite, private cinema, golf simulator, and social lounge.

Project Notes: Construction began in mid-2023 and topped out in summer 2024. Leasing launched approximately nine months prior to completion.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

DEVELOPER/SPONSOR: Skyline Developers

ARCHITECT: Morris Adjmi Architects
