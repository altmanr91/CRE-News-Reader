CoreWeave Doubles Headquarters Space to 62K SF

LEASE

Connect CRE — Mon, 06 Jul 2026 21:36:18 +0000

SUMMARY

CoreWeave, a rapidly expanding artificial intelligence company, has significantly increased its corporate headquarters footprint at The Eisenhower in Livingston, NJ. The firm signed a 31,000-square-foot expansion, bringing its total occupancy to 62,000 square feet, less than a year after its initial lease. The property's extensive on-site amenities, numerous conference rooms, auditorium, backup generator, and walkable retail were key factors in CoreWeave's decision to expand within the building, as no other competitive options in the submarket could meet their specific requirements.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Property Name: The Eisenhower

Address: 290 W. Mount Pleasant Ave., Livingston, NJ

Size: 31,000 SF

Notable Features: Features an expansive list of on-site amenities, 10+ conference rooms and auditorium, back-up generator, and walkable retail.

Project Notes: This transaction represents an expansion of an existing lease, doubling the tenant's footprint to 62,000 square feet.
