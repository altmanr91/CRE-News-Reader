Obra Supplies $31M Refi on Arizona Northrop Grumman-Leased Office Property

REFINANCE

Commercial Observer — Wed, 01 Jul 2026 16:24:02 +0000

SUMMARY

Silver Creek Development has secured $31.4 million in bridge debt from Obra Real Estate to refinance a single-tenant office complex in Gilbert, AZ. The 2021-built property is fully leased to a global aerospace and defense technology company, reflecting continued demand for high-quality assets with institutional-grade tenancy and experienced sponsorship. Arrow Real Estate Advisors arranged the transaction, which closed in under 30 days.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Address: 1715 West Elliot Road, Gilbert, AZ

Size: 120,294 SF

Loan Amount: $31.4M

Loan Per SF: $261/SF (calculated)

Occupancy at Close: 100.0%

Year Built: 2021

Notable Features: Located within the 63-acre Gilbert Spectrum Business Park, 20 miles southeast of Downtown Phoenix.

Project Notes: Silver Creek acquired the property for $43.1 million in early 2021.
