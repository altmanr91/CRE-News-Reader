UBS and CBRE sign leases at Opus’ Arcadia office building in Edina

LEASE

REJournals — Mon, 27 Jul 2026 09:38:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Opus' Arcadia office building in Edina, Minnesota, currently under construction, has reached 71% preleased status with significant commitments from UBS Financial Services Inc. and CBRE. UBS will occupy the entire fifth floor, while CBRE has leased a portion of the third floor. Opus and its affiliated companies will also take a substantial amount of space, including the entire top floor. The strong leasing momentum reflects continued demand for high-quality office environments that offer exceptional amenities, premier locations, and a superior workplace experience.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Property Name: Arcadia

Address: 5115 Eden Ave., Edina, MN

Size: 118,000 SF

Current Occupancy: 71.0%

Completion: December 2026

Notable Features: Class-A office building with a glass-façade, dramatic lobby, collaborative gathering spaces, conference rooms, employee training room, private tenant clubroom, fitness center, and a landscaped outdoor terrace with fire pits and grilling stations.

Project Notes: Broke ground in September 2025 on the former Eden 100 site in Edina's Grandview District.
