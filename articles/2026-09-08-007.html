Waterfall Asset Management, Delaware Life Provide $64.7M Refinancing for Office Property in Torrance, California

REFINANCE

REBusinessOnline — Tue, 08 Sep 2026 13:23:44 +0000

SUMMARY

Waterfall Asset Management and Delaware Life have provided a $64.7 million refinancing loan for The Torrance, a Class A office property in Torrance, California. The financing will support the building's current leasing strategy. The property has undergone significant modernization efforts, including recent upgrades to its elevator, HVAC systems, common areas, roof, amenities, and exterior.
