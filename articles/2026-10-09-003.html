Law Firm Morgan & Morgan Signs 71K-SF Lease at One Seaport Plaza

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Thu, 08 Oct 2026 21:16:28 +0000

SUMMARY

Personal injury law firm Morgan & Morgan has signed a 70,602-square-foot direct lease at One Seaport Plaza in Lower Manhattan, expanding its footprint within the building where it previously held a sublease. The firm initially moved into the property in Q3 2023, occupying 34,792 square feet. This new long-term agreement provides a more permanent home for the law firm.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Property Name: One Seaport Plaza

Address: 199 Water Street, New York City, NY

Size: 70,602 SF

Year Built: 1984

Notable Features: The building spans over 1 million square feet and offers amenities including a cafe and lounge area.

Project Notes: Morgan & Morgan previously occupied 34,792 square feet in the building under a sublease, relocating from Midtown in Q3 2023.
