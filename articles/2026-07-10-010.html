JLL Arranges $352M Loan for Refinancing of Midtown Manhattan Office Building

REFINANCE

REBusinessOnline — Thu, 09 Jul 2026 14:56:15 +0000

SUMMARY

JLL has arranged a $352 million floating-rate loan for the refinancing of 425 Lexington Avenue, a 750,000-square-foot office building in Midtown Manhattan. The loan was pre-placed with funds and accounts managed by BlackRock through Goldman Sachs, with Vanbarton Group as the borrower. The 31-story property was 99 percent leased at the time of closing and recently underwent $35 million in capital improvements, including a new amenity center.
