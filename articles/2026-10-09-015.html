Germantown Developer Reveals Schedule for Mixed-Use Project

DEVELOPMENT

Connect CRE — Fri, 09 Oct 2026 14:44:05 +0000

SUMMARY

Proffitt Dixon Partners has announced the construction timeline for Ferra, a new mixed-use development in Nashville's Germantown neighborhood. The project, which will feature 325 apartments and ground-level retail and restaurant space, is slated to break ground in 2027 with delivery expected in 2028. This follows the firm's recent completion of the 345-unit Chamberlain House in the same area. Additionally, Proffitt Dixon Partners is marketing a portion of the site for a potential hotel development.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: mixed-use

Property Name: Ferra

Address: 1000 Second Ave. N, Nashville, TN

Units: 325

Site Area: 5.98 acres

Notable Features: Planned to include a rooftop pool and amenity area, with ground-level retail and restaurant space.

Project Notes: The 5.98-acre site was previously acquired for $32.53 million in 2021. A 0.8-acre parcel of the site is being marketed for a potential hotel development.
