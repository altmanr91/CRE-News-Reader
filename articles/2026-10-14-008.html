Partnership Buys 4.3-Acre Industrial Outdoor Storage Facility Near San Antonio Airport

ACQUISITION

REBusinessOnline — Wed, 14 Oct 2026 13:59:48 +0000

SUMMARY

A partnership between Apricus Realty Capital and ABR Capital Partners has acquired a 4.3-acre industrial outdoor storage (IOS) facility in San Antonio. The property, which was fully leased at the time of sale, is strategically located near San Antonio International Airport and offers specialized features including crane-served warehouse space and a concrete storage yard.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Address: 8799 Crownhill Blvd, San Antonio, TX

Site Area: 4.3 acres

Notable Features: Adjacent to San Antonio International Airport; features crane-served warehouse space, maintenance facilities, a concrete storage yard, and multiple drive-in loading positions.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: Apricus Realty Capital

BUYER: ABR Capital Partners
