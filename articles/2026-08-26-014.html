Southfield’s REDICO launches healthcare investment and operating platform

REJournals — Tue, 25 Aug 2026 15:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Development

REDICO, a Southfield, Michigan-based real estate firm, has partnered with One Orchard to launch InfraMed Properties, a new healthcare infrastructure investment and operating platform. InfraMed will focus on acquiring, developing, owning, leasing, and managing medical outpatient properties nationwide. The platform is launching with a 25-asset portfolio across 16 states, anchored by credit-backed tenants affiliated with leading health systems, and aims to achieve over $1 billion in investments through programmatic equity and debt financing.
