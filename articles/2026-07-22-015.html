Boxabl ignites SPAC market, goes public at $3.5B valuation

The Real Deal — National — Tue, 21 Jul 2026 16:56:31 +0000

SUMMARY

Non-CRE / Business News

Boxabl, a Las Vegas-based modular housing startup, has gone public on the Nasdaq after completing its merger with special purpose acquisition company FG Merger II Corp. The transaction, which valued the combined entity at $3.5 billion, will provide Boxabl with increased access to capital to expand its production and development of prefabricated building systems. The company, known for its "Casita" tiny homes, aims to scale its operations following the public listing.
