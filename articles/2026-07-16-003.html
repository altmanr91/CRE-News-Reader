Bridges Development Group Buys Kingswood Center in Brooklyn for $31M

ACQUISITION

Commercial Observer — Wed, 15 Jul 2026 19:20:17 +0000

SUMMARY

Bridges Development Group has acquired Kingswood Center, a 229,926-square-foot shopping center in Brooklyn's Midwood neighborhood, for $31 million. The sale represents a significant discount from its previous trade in 2020. The buyer secured $34.65 million in acquisition financing to implement a business plan focused on property investment and leasing the substantial vacant space. The seller, PNC's Midland Loan Services, took possession of the property in 2023 after the previous owner defaulted on a CMBS loan.
