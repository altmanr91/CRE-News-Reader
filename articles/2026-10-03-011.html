North Hollywood Apartments Trade to Locally Based LLC

SALE

Connect CRE — Fri, 02 Oct 2026 21:41:45 +0000

SUMMARY

A 41-unit multifamily property in North Hollywood, which had been under the same ownership for over 30 years, has been sold for nearly $5.89 million. The transaction closed at a 5.7% cap rate based on in-place income. The buyer, a San Fernando Valley-based LLC, plans to undertake common area and interior upgrades to reposition the asset.
