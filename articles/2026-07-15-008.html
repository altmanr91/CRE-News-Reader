Haven Housing Acquires 309-Unit Apartment Community in Kyle, Texas

ACQUISITION

REBusinessOnline — Tue, 14 Jul 2026 15:23:34 +0000

SUMMARY

California-based investment firm Haven Housing has acquired Hays Park Apartments, a 309-unit multifamily community in Kyle, Texas. The garden-style property features a mix of one- and two-bedroom units and offers amenities such as a clubhouse, two pools, a fitness center with yoga studios, and a business center. Walker and Dunlop brokered the transaction.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Hays Park Apartments

Address: 828 W Bebee Rd, Kyle, TX*

Units: 309

Site Area: 14.9 acres

Notable Features: Garden-style property with a clubhouse, two pools, fitness center with yoga studios, and a business center.
