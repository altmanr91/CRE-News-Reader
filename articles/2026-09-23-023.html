Hunt Sports Development Plans Mixed-Use District At Toyota Stadium In Frisco: The DFW Deal Sheet

DEVELOPMENT

Bisnow Latest News Feed — Tue, 22 Sep 2026 18:24:38 -0400

SUMMARY

Hunt Sports Development, in collaboration with Peak Curated and Venu, is planning a new mixed-use district adjacent to Toyota Stadium in Frisco, TX. The development is slated to include a hotel, various restaurants, and a convention center, enhancing the area around the existing stadium.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: mixed-use

Property Name: Toyota Stadium Mixed-Use District

Address: 9200 World Cup Way, Frisco, TX

Notable Features: Adjacent to Toyota Stadium, includes a hotel, restaurants, and a convention center.
