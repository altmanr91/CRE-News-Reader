FPA Multifamily Acquires 237-Unit, Three-Building Apartment Portfolio in Denver

ACQUISITION

REBusinessOnline — Tue, 22 Sep 2026 13:13:29 +0000

SUMMARY

FPA Multifamily has acquired a three-building, 237-unit apartment portfolio in Denver from entities associated with 2580 South University Co. LLC. The portfolio consists of Tabor House, Post House, and Heritage House, all located on South University Boulevard. The sellers had recently renovated a portion of the units across the portfolio, which offers a shared amenity package including a clubhouse, pool, and parking garages.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Tabor House, Post House, and Heritage House

Address: 2512 S. University Blvd, 2550 S. University Blvd, 2580 S. University Blvd, Denver, CO

Units: 237

Notable Features: The portfolio includes Tabor House (82 units, built 1967), Post House (64 units, built 1974), and Heritage House (91 units, built 1968). Units feature air conditioning, dishwashers, and oversized private balconies. Shared amenities include a clubhouse, pool, sauna, community kitchen, billiards, ping pong table, and parking garages. Tabor House has a fitness center, while Heritage House and Post House offer steam rooms.

Project Notes: The sellers recently renovated approximately a dozen units across the portfolio.
