Trammell Crow Co. Completes 181,000 SF Mixed-Use Project in Phoenix

DEVELOPMENT

REBusinessOnline — Tue, 01 Sep 2026 13:50:38 +0000

SUMMARY

Trammell Crow Co. has completed an 8-acre, 181,000-square-foot mixed-use development in Phoenix. The project includes a 145,000-square-foot Class A office building, a 25,000-square-foot flagship grocery store, and 11,000 square feet of additional retail space. The grocery store opened in July 2026, and the office building is ready for occupancy.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: mixed-use

Size: 181,000 SF

Site Area: 8 acres

Notable Features: The headquarters building features a community garden, rooftop deck, fitness center, and culinary kitchens.

Project Notes: The office building serves as a corporate headquarters for a major grocery chain.
