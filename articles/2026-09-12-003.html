In Health Care Real Estate, Creativity Abounds — It Has To

Commercial Observer — Fri, 11 Sep 2026 19:28:53 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

This article summarizes key discussions from Commercial Observer's National Healthcare Forum, where capital planning executives, legal experts, construction managers, and project leaders convened to address the evolving landscape of healthcare real estate. Experts emphasized the need for creative strategies in funding and improving aging infrastructure, moving from reactive maintenance to proactive master planning. Discussions highlighted challenges such as regulatory pressures, accelerated project timelines, and the shift towards outpatient services requiring more convenient, community-based locations. A central theme was the importance of infrastructure resiliency and involving the right stakeholders early in the planning process to minimize operational disruptions.
