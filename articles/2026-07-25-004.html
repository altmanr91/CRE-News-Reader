Lightstone Capital Refis San Diego Apartments With $34M Loan

LOAN

Commercial Observer — Fri, 24 Jul 2026 17:47:31 +0000

SUMMARY

Lightstone Capital has provided a $34.25 million bridge loan to developer Jeff Svitak for the newly completed Kaya Apartments, a 78-unit multifamily project in San Diego's Bankers Hill neighborhood. The fully-leased property, which debuted last year, offers a range of unit types and community amenities designed to foster a sense of community. This financing underscores Lightstone Capital's strategy to support well-located assets in strong markets.
