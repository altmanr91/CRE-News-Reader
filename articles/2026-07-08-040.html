Milhaus Receives Approval to Develop Kansas City Affordable Apartments

DEVELOPMENT

Connect CRE — Tue, 07 Jul 2026 20:54:30 +0000

SUMMARY

Milhaus has secured Low-Income Housing Tax Credits from the Missouri Housing Development Commission, along with a tax abatement and Housing Trust Fund allocation, to develop the Linwood & Troost Apartments in Kansas City. This project will transform two vacant lots into a 194-unit, 100% affordable residential community across two four-story buildings. This marks Milhaus's inaugural Low-Income Housing Tax Credit development in the Kansas City market.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Linwood & Troost Apartments

Address: 3200 Troost Ave, Kansas City, MO

Units: 194

Completion: 2028

Notable Features: 100% affordable residential development located on the Troost MAX transit corridor with transit-supportive infrastructure.

Project Notes: Funding includes 4% federal Low-Income Housing Tax Credits, a tax abatement from Kansas City’s Planned Industrial Expansion Authority, and $1.5 million from the Kansas City Housing Trust Fund. Construction is expected to begin by the end of 2026.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

DEVELOPER/SPONSOR: Milhaus
