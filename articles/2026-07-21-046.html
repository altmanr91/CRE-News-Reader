Oceanwide Plaza Sale Primed To Move Forward

SALE

Bisnow News Feed — Mon, 20 Jul 2026 19:11:43 -0400

SUMMARY

A bankruptcy judge has indicated approval for the sale of the unfinished Oceanwide Plaza in Downtown Los Angeles, paving the way for the long-stalled $1.2 billion project to finally move forward. The city of Los Angeles withdrew its objections after provisions were added to the liquidation plan, requiring the proposed buyer, KPC Square LLC, to address blight and graffiti within 90 days and close the sale within six months. This development increases the likelihood of the prominent mixed-use development reaching substantial completion after sitting dormant for over six years.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: mixed-use

Property Name: Oceanwide Plaza

Address: 1527 E 92nd St, Los Angeles, CA*

Sale Price: $470.0M

Total Project Cost: $1.20B

Notable Features: Incomplete mixed-use development that has been stalled for over six years.

Project Notes: The sale is part of a liquidation plan in bankruptcy court. The buyer is required to initiate graffiti removal and blight abatement within 90 days and close the sale within six months, with potential for extension by unanimous agreement.
