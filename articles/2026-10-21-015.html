Appian Investments Acquires 19-Story Greenboro Office Tower

ACQUISITION

Connect CRE — Wed, 21 Oct 2026 14:13:29 +0000

SUMMARY

Appian Investments has acquired Renaissance Plaza, a 19-story office tower in downtown Greensboro, for $22 million. The 287,000-square-foot property, built in 1990, recently underwent nearly $4 million in capital improvements by the seller, Richardson Properties, enhancing its lobby, common areas, and building systems. The tower boasts a strong tenant mix from the financial and legal sectors.
