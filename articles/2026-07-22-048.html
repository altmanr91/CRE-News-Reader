GSA Head Says Federal Government Has 'Place To Play' In Southwest D.C. Redevelopment

Bisnow News Feed — Tue, 21 Jul 2026 13:50:48 -0400

SUMMARY

Infrastructure Investment / Government Policy

The head of the General Services Administration (GSA) indicated that the federal government plans to take a more active role in the redevelopment of 250 acres in Southwest D.C., situated between the National Mall and the waterfront. This marks a shift from the GSA's previous strategy of selling federally owned properties individually without a master plan. Administrator Ed Forst suggested the government could act as a "first mover" to catalyze the transformation, potentially using models like the successful redevelopment of The Yards in Southeast D.C. This new approach, emphasizing collaboration with stakeholders, aims to accelerate the revitalization of the corridor, addressing concerns about the loss of government control over significant real estate.
