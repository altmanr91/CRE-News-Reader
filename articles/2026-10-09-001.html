Manhattan’s Office Sector Has Become the Corporate World’s Surest Bet

Commercial Observer — Fri, 09 Oct 2026 15:22:28 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

Manhattan's office sector is experiencing a robust recovery, with significant increases in leasing volume and direct asking rents, alongside a notable decrease in vacancy rates. This positive trend is broad-based across major industries, indicating a resilient market not reliant on any single segment. Despite global economic uncertainties, experts believe New York City remains a highly desirable business hub, attracting diverse industries and benefiting from a widespread return to in-office work. The market's only potential challenge is a looming lack of new supply, as much of the upcoming construction is already pre-leased.
