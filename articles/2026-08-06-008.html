Lindenmeyr Munroe Signs 100,043 SF Industrial Lease in North Houston

LEASE

REBusinessOnline — Wed, 05 Aug 2026 14:56:13 +0000

SUMMARY

Lindenmeyr Munroe, a distributor of printing and packaging paper, has signed a 100,043-square-foot industrial lease at Building 3 of Patriot Business Park in North Houston. The property is a newly constructed, three-building development. Stream Realty Partners represented the landlord, a partnership between Investment & Development Ventures and Standard Real Estate Investments, while Cushman & Wakefield represented the tenant.
