Foreclosure Auction on Horizon for Dallas Highrise

FORECLOSURE

Connect CRE — Mon, 28 Sep 2026 16:06:17 +0000

SUMMARY

A 33-story office tower in downtown Dallas' Arts District, 2100 Ross Ave., is facing a foreclosure auction due to an alleged default on a $79 million loan. The property, owned by Woods Capital and Pacific Elm Properties, was placed into receivership after the loan matured without full repayment or refinancing. The auction is scheduled for October 6, though a resolution with lenders could still avert the sale.
