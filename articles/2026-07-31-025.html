JLL Nearly Doubles Profits, Fueled By Leasing Rebound

Bisnow News Feed — Thu, 30 Jul 2026 09:45:18 -0400

SUMMARY

Market Research / Earnings Report

JLL reported a significant increase in net income for the second quarter, nearly doubling to $215.6 million, driven by a strong rebound in leasing and capital markets activity. The brokerage giant saw a 24% rise in leasing revenue and a 19% increase from capital markets, signaling an acceleration in commercial real estate despite broader economic uncertainties. CEO Christian Ulbrich emphasized the company's resilient business model, high client retention, and strategic investments in data and AI as key drivers of durable organic growth. CFO Kelly Howe noted that despite market volatility, JLL anticipates continued deal momentum, supported by pent-up demand and liquid debt markets.
