DEVELOPMENT

Commercial Observer — Mon, 27 Jul 2026 11:00:49 +0000

SUMMARY

Camber Property Group, an affordable housing developer, is celebrating its tenth year in business with two significant projects in New York City. The firm has commenced construction on the $167 million redevelopment of Chelsea Beacon, transforming the former Bayview Correctional Facility into 131 affordable and supportive housing units with community spaces. Additionally, Camber was selected for the $62 million modernization of NYCHA's Unity Towers in Coney Island. These projects highlight the firm's expertise in redeveloping distressed assets and creating new affordable housing, contributing to the city's ambitious housing goals.
