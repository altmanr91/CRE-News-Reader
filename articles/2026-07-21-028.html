Concord Capital looks to add two stories, 70 units to latest River North conversion project

DEVELOPMENT

The Real Deal — Chicago — Mon, 20 Jul 2026 22:41:07 +0000

SUMMARY

Concord Capital is undertaking a $20 million renovation and conversion of a 48,000-square-foot office building at 226 West Ontario Street in Chicago into a 70-unit apartment complex. The project involves adding two stories to the existing structure. The developer acquired the building for $4.1 million and secured a $16 million construction loan from First Bank Chicago, with completion anticipated by February 2028. This conversion reflects a broader trend in the market to repurpose older office buildings into residential spaces amidst rising rents and a constrained construction pipeline.
