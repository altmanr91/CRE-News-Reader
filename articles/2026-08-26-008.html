ANF Tops Out Luxury Seniors Housing Community in Delray Beach, Florida

CONSTRUCTION

REBusinessOnline — Tue, 25 Aug 2026 14:04:13 +0000

SUMMARY

ANF Group Inc. has topped out All Seasons Delray, a luxury senior living community under development in Delray Beach, Florida. The project, developed by Beztak, will offer 153 units, including independent and assisted living residences, with completion anticipated in the first quarter of 2028.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: seniors housing

Property Name: All Seasons Delray

Address: 15650 Lyons Road, Delray Beach, FL

Units: 153

Completion: Q1 2028

Notable Features: Amenities will include a swimming pool, landscaped courtyard, pub, theater, library, game room, fitness center, pottery and arts studios, multipurpose activity spaces and an onsite medical center.

Project Notes: Construction began in late 2025. The community will feature 92 independent living apartments and 61 assisted living residences.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

GENERAL CONTRACTOR: ANF Group Inc.

DEVELOPER/SPONSOR: Beztak
