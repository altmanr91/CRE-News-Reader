Par 3: Trio of Phoenix-area golf courses sells to East Coast investor for $57M

ACQUISITION

The Real Deal — National — Thu, 02 Jul 2026 16:30:00 +0000

SUMMARY

The Walden Group, a New Jersey-based real estate investment firm, has acquired three golf courses in the Phoenix metropolitan area from Thompson Golf Group for a total of $57 million. The portfolio includes Dove Valley Ranch Golf Club in Cave Creek, Power Ranch Golf Club in Gilbert, and Longbow Golf Club in Mesa. Thompson Golf Group will continue to operate the properties under a multiyear agreement, allowing them to focus on their management business while Walden Group expands its national golf portfolio.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: golf course

Sale Price: $57.0M

Site Area: 506 acres

Project Notes: Longbow Golf Club sold for $23.5 million, Power Ranch Golf Club for $17.1 million, and Dove Valley Ranch Golf Club for $16.4 million.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: The Walden Group — Steven Schorr, Founder

SELLER: Thompson Golf Group

LENDER: Davidson Kempner
