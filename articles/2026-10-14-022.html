Opinion / Market Commentary

Access to reliable and affordable electric power has become the paramount factor influencing site selection decisions for large-scale industrial projects, such as factories and data centers. This critical shift means that power availability now outweighs traditional considerations like government incentives and the local talent pool in determining where major developments are located.
