Portman Acquires Land in North Houston, Receives Construction Financing for 714,339 SF Industrial Project

CONSTRUCTION

REBusinessOnline — Wed, 19 Aug 2026 14:25:56 +0000

SUMMARY

Portman has acquired land and secured construction financing for Gateway 1960, a 714,339-square-foot industrial project in North Houston. The development will feature three buildings with speculative office space, LED lighting, and dock-high loading doors. Construction is slated to commence this month and conclude in the third quarter of 2027.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Property Name: Gateway 1960

Address: 620 Gellhorn Dr, Houston, TX*

Size: 714,339 SF

Completion: 3Q 2027

Notable Features: Project will include a 440,323-square-foot cross-dock structure, a 157,723-square-foot front-load building, and a 116,293-square-foot rear-load facility, all with speculative office space, LED lighting, and dock-high loading doors.

* Address sourced separately, not in original article.
