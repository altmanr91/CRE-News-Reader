Three Tenants Sign Leases at Shops at Starwood in Frisco

LEASE

REBusinessOnline — Mon, 06 Jul 2026 16:09:30 +0000

SUMMARY

Three new tenants have signed leases totaling 5,944 square feet at The Shops at Starwood, a 90,667-square-foot retail center in Frisco, Texas. The new establishments, including a smashburger and cocktail concept, a cafe, and a barbershop, are all expected to open in 2026. Whitestone REIT owns the property.
