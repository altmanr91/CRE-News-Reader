Endeavor boosts Austin office pipeline with 5th and Walsh project

DEVELOPMENT

The Real Deal — Texas — Wed, 22 Jul 2026 20:22:59 +0000

SUMMARY

Endeavor is moving forward with its 5th and Walsh project in Downtown Austin, a five-story, 198,000-square-foot office and retail building that will also serve as the developer's new headquarters. The project, which includes 21,000 square feet of ground-floor retail and a subterranean parking garage, is slated for completion in late 2028. Despite construction not yet commencing, nearly half of the office space is already pre-leased, indicating strong market demand.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Property Name: 5th and Walsh

Address: 1200 West 5th Street, Austin, TX

Size: 198,000 SF

Current Occupancy: 50.0%

Completion: late 2028

Notable Features: Includes 21,000 square feet of ground-floor restaurant and retail space and a below-grade parking garage.

Project Notes: Plans for the project were initially submitted in March 2022. Endeavor will relocate its headquarters from 500 West 5th Street to this new building upon completion.
