Charney Companies, Tavros Break Ground on Fifth Gowanus Development

DEVELOPMENT

Connect CRE — Fri, 02 Oct 2026 21:31:36 +0000

SUMMARY

Charney Companies and Tavros have broken ground on 175 Third St., a 27-story mixed-use tower in Gowanus, Brooklyn. This project, which will feature over 1,000 apartments including approximately 250 affordable units, is the fifth development in the neighborhood by the co-developers and is part of the larger Gowanus Wharf development. The design incorporates a significant public waterfront esplanade, enhancing community access to the canal.
