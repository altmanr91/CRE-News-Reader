Watermark Capital Moves Forward With School-to-Resi Conversion in Crown Heights

CONSTRUCTION

Commercial Observer — Thu, 10 Sep 2026 21:18:01 +0000

SUMMARY

Watermark Capital Group is moving forward with the conversion of a former Roman Catholic school campus in Crown Heights, Brooklyn, into a 42-unit residential building and a community facility. The developer secured a $16.48 million construction loan from Genesis Capital for the project, which involves a vertical expansion of an existing building. Watermark Capital Group previously obtained a ground lease for the site from St. Teresa of Avila Roman Catholic Church.

DATA POINTS

Notable Features: The project includes a 42,181-square-foot residence and a 57,449-square-foot community facility.

Project Notes: The site was previously a school campus owned by St. Teresa of Avila Roman Catholic Church. Watermark Capital Group obtained a ground lease for the site in April 2024, which was modified in June 2025.
