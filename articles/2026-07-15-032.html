Dallas apartment pipeline gets a boost with pair of financing deals

LOAN

The Real Deal — Texas — Tue, 14 Jul 2026 19:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Two separate apartment projects in the Dallas-Fort Worth metroplex recently secured financing. Endeavor Real Estate obtained funding for Lucille, a 22-story luxury high-rise in Dallas's Uptown district, which will include 265 units and ground-floor retail. Separately, StreetLights Residential secured $54 million in equity financing for a 22-story apartment tower in Plano, part of The Park at Legacy redevelopment. Both projects are slated to begin construction later this summer, with the Endeavor project targeting a late 2028 completion.
