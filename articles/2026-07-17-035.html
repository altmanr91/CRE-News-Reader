Prologis extends San Jose streak with $12M building buy

ACQUISITION

The Real Deal — San Francisco — Thu, 16 Jul 2026 22:45:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Prologis has acquired a 40,600-square-foot tech and research building in San Jose for $11.6 million, continuing its expansion in Silicon Valley. The logistics real estate firm purchased the property from a Burlingame-based group, which had acquired the building just nine months prior for $9.2 million. This acquisition adds to Prologis's significant holdings in the region, where it is the largest commercial property owner.
