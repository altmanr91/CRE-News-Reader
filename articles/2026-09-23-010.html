CBRE Negotiates $60M Sale of Milwaukee Multifamily Portfolio

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Wed, 23 Sep 2026 13:44:06 +0000

SUMMARY

CBRE has facilitated the $60 million sale of a 374-unit multifamily portfolio in Milwaukee, comprising St. James Place and Arbor Ridge Apartments. The properties are situated in the Calumet Farms neighborhood. CBRE also arranged financing for the undisclosed buyer.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: St. James Place and Arbor Ridge Apartments

Units: 374

Sale Price: $60.0M

Notable Features: St. James Place offers 236 units from 675 to 1,060 square feet; Arbor Ridge features 138 units from 788 to 1,125 square feet.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

SELLER: Weidner Apartment Homes

SELLER BROKER: CBRE — Sean Beuche; Matson Holbrook; Gretchen Richards
