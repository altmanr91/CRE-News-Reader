JLL Brokers Sale of 552,550 SF Office Complex in Houston’s Galleria District

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 02 Oct 2026 15:31:56 +0000

SUMMARY

JLL has brokered the sale of Park Towers, a 552,550-square-foot office complex in Houston's Galleria district. Regent Properties sold the two 18-story buildings to Interra Capital Group, which secured acquisition financing through Morgan Stanley. The property was 89.5 percent leased at the time of closing, and Transwestern has been tapped for leasing.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Property Name: Park Towers

Size: 552,550 SF

Occupancy at Sale: 89.5%

Year Built: 1972

Notable Features: Two 18-story buildings, renovated between 2016 and 2022, with amenities including a fitness center, tenant lounge, game room, conference facilities, executive boardroom, and full-service deli.
