Hines Acquires 99 West Paces Apartments in Atlanta for $240M

ACQUISITION

REBusinessOnline — Wed, 07 Oct 2026 15:20:22 +0000

SUMMARY

Hines Global Income Trust Inc. (HGIT) has acquired 99 West Paces, a 312-unit luxury apartment tower in Atlanta's Buckhead district. The property, built in 2023, was 95 percent occupied at the time of sale and was reportedly purchased for approximately $240 million. This acquisition marks Hines' entry into the Atlanta market and is part of a larger $500 million investment by HGIT across multiple cities.
