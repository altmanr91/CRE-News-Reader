Prologis Beats Expectations With Record Leasing and Data Center Growth

Commercial Observer — Thu, 16 Jul 2026 20:22:05 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Earnings Report

Prologis, the world's largest warehouse landlord, reported strong second-quarter 2026 earnings, exceeding expectations with record leasing activity and significant growth in its data center development pipeline. The company commenced $1.6 billion in new projects and acquired $1.8 billion in new real estate during the quarter. Notably, year-to-date data center starts, totaling $4 billion, have already surpassed full-year guidance, including an $800 million, 260-megawatt build-to-suit data center campus, which Prologis plans to sell upon completion. The REIT's CEO highlighted the strategic advantage of leveraging existing land, customer relationships, and operating capabilities to expand into data center and energy businesses, despite acknowledging growing challenges with approvals and entitlements for data center projects.
