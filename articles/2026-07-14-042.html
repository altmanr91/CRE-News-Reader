Condo High-Rise Launches Construction in Sunnyside

CONSTRUCTION

Connect CRE — Mon, 13 Jul 2026 21:00:10 +0000

SUMMARY

Construction has commenced on a 19-story condominium tower at 51-02 Roosevelt Ave. in Sunnyside, a project being developed by Hiwin Group USA. The building will introduce 84 residential units, ground-floor retail, commercial office space, and community-oriented medical facilities to the neighborhood. Acre NY Realty, handling sales and marketing, notes the project's significance as a rare high-rise addition with strong transit access, poised to become a local landmark.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: condo

Address: 51-02 Roosevelt Ave., New York City, NY

Units: 84

Completion: 2029

Notable Features: Includes ground-floor retail, commercial office space, community-oriented medical space, residential recreation areas, terraces, and parking access.
