Marcus & Millichap Brokers Sale of 13,079 SF Retail Strip Center in Little Elm, Texas

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Mon, 14 Sep 2026 14:36:13 +0000

SUMMARY

Marcus & Millichap has facilitated the sale of India Bazaar Plaza, a 13,079-square-foot retail strip center in Little Elm, Texas. The property, built in 2018, was fully leased at the time of sale to an ethnic grocer and a salon. Both the seller and the buyer were private investors who chose to remain anonymous.
