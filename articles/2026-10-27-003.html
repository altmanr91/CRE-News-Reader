Takenaka Under Contract to Buy One Broadway for $170M

ACQUISITION

Commercial Observer — Tue, 27 Oct 2026 14:00:17 +0000

SUMMARY

Takenaka Corporation is under contract to acquire One Broadway, a 230,000-square-foot office building in New York City, for $170 million from Rockwood Capital and Midtown Equities. The historic 12-story property, which was gut-renovated in 2020 and is fully leased to a strong tenant mix, was previously purchased by the sellers in 2018 for $140 million with plans for repositioning. This acquisition aligns with Takenaka's strategy of collecting unique trophy properties in the U.S.
