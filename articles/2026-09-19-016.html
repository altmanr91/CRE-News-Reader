Pegasus Completes Project Management for Two LA Retail Developments

Connect CRE — Fri, 18 Sep 2026 22:04:18 +0000

SUMMARY

Non-CRE / Business News

Pegasus recently completed project management for two retail developments in Los Angeles. The projects included the first Raising Cane's location in Westwood Village and a new Honey Baked Ham franchise in Sherman Oaks. Pegasus served as the owner's project management advisor, guiding both projects from planning through completion, emphasizing thoughtful planning, clear communication, and disciplined execution.
