Verizon To Sell 274 Retail Locations, Lay Off 500 Office Workers

SALE

Bisnow News Feed — Fri, 17 Jul 2026 11:22:53 -0400

SUMMARY

Verizon is divesting 274 of its corporate-owned retail locations as part of a broader corporate restructuring initiated last year. This move will reduce its corporate store count to 1,000, supplemented by 5,000 franchised outlets, and will impact approximately 2,500 retail employees. The company aims to maintain at least 1,000 corporate-owned stores over the next three years to support its long-term strategy, while also working with franchise operators to enhance the customer experience. This follows previous efforts to optimize its real estate footprint, including a significant office lease consolidation in New York City and the sale of a subsidiary's headquarters in Florida.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: retail

Project Notes: Verizon previously sold 179 corporate-owned locations and closed one retail store last year as part of its restructuring.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

SELLER: Verizon

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Retail vacancy was flat quarter-over-quarter at 6%, which is below the historical average of 7.4%. Limited new construction has helped keep availability rates low despite some bankruptcies in the sector.
