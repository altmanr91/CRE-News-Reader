Lincoln Property acquires Spectrum Companies in Carolinas expansion

ACQUISITION

The Real Deal — National — Thu, 16 Jul 2026 20:35:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Lincoln Property Company has acquired Charlotte-based developer The Spectrum Companies for an undisclosed sum, integrating Spectrum's operations into its Carolinas division. This strategic acquisition aims to accelerate development activities and facilitate further property acquisitions, particularly leveraging Spectrum's expertise in multifamily residential projects. The move is expected to be seamless due to the companies' shared vision and the existing relationship between their leadership.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: Lincoln Property Company — Johnny Harris, Legacy Partner, Carolinas Division Founder; Johno Harris, Senior Executive Vice President

SELLER: The Spectrum Companies — Darryl Dewberry, Executive Chairman

SPONSOR PIPELINE

The Edge office complex: Acquired in a Boston suburb for $61 million in July, comprising seven buildings and 962,000 square feet.
