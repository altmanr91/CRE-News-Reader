Cravey Real Estate Services closes lease of newly built warehouse in Corpus Christi

LEASE

REJournals — Mon, 28 Sep 2026 16:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Cravey Real Estate Services has completed the lease of a newly constructed 10,902-square-foot industrial warehouse in Corpus Christi, Texas, to PK Companies Group, LLC. The facility, located in Wildcat Industrial Park, offers modern construction, heavy power capacity, and a secured 3.25-acre yard, making it ideal for industrial service users supporting the regional energy and petrochemical sectors. The transaction highlights the strong demand for functional industrial properties in Northwest Corpus Christi, with the park emerging as a preferred location for industrial and energy-related tenants.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Address: 1222 Cathead Road, Corpus Christi, TX

Size: 10,902 SF

Year Built: 2024

Site Area: 4.25 acres

Notable Features: Crane-ready building engineered for a 10-ton overhead bridge crane, with a fully stabilized, fenced, and gated 3.25-acre yard for outdoor storage and fleet operations.

Project Notes: Landlord completed tenant improvements including expanded office buildout, enhanced yard fencing, and upgraded gated access points prior to occupancy.
