Swickard Acquires 90% Stake in Portland’s Park Avenue West

ACQUISITION

Connect CRE — Wed, 23 Sep 2026 14:02:01 +0000

SUMMARY

Jeff Swickard, CEO of Swickard Group, has acquired a 90% majority interest in Park Avenue West, a prominent mixed-use tower in downtown Portland. This acquisition, valued at over $100 million, marks another significant investment for Swickard in the city, bringing his total investment in Portland over the past year to nearly $250 million. The existing owners, TMT Development and others, will retain a 10% stake in the property.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: mixed-use

Property Name: Park Avenue West

Address: 760 S.W. Ninth Ave., Portland, OR

Sale Price: $100.0M

Year Built: 2016

Notable Features: 30-story tower across from Director Park and Nordstrom, featuring luxury apartments, office space, and ground-floor retail.

Project Notes: This acquisition represents a 90% interest in the property.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE
