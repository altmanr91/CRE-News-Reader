“Less than a Telluride”: Jay Martin dares tenants to buy Harlem building

The Real Deal — New York — Sat, 18 Jul 2026 13:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

This article discusses the significant devaluation of rent-stabilized multifamily buildings in New York City, particularly in Harlem, following the enactment of the Housing Stability and Tenant Protection Act in 2019. It highlights a specific 24-unit building at 502 West 135th Street, currently listed for $1.375 million, which represents a more than 60% drop in value since its 2019 purchase for $3.6 million. The piece uses this example to illustrate how legislative changes and rising interest rates have made ownership of such properties financially challenging, even suggesting that tenants would find it more expensive to own than to rent due to operating expenses exceeding potential rent revenue.
