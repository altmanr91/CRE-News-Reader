Westbridge Realty Group Files Plans for Three 99-Unit Buildings in the Bronx

Commercial Observer — Fri, 28 Aug 2026 17:20:49 +0000

SUMMARY

Development

Westbridge Realty Group has filed plans for three new residential buildings in the Soundview neighborhood of the Bronx, totaling 297 units. Each building is designed with 99 units, a common strategy among developers to qualify for the city's 485-x tax incentive while avoiding costly construction wage mandates associated with projects of 100 units or more. The proposed developments include significant commercial space, indicating a mixed-use approach.
