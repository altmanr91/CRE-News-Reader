Gilbane Development breaks ground on Southern Gateway for Western Kentucky University

DEVELOPMENT

REJournals — Mon, 26 Oct 2026 13:43:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Gilbane Development has broken ground on "The Southern Gateway," the initial phase of the $350 million "Elevate WKU" public-private partnership with Western Kentucky University. This multi-year initiative aims to modernize on-campus housing, replacing aging infrastructure with a student-centered residential experience. The first phase, scheduled to open in fall 2028, will deliver an approximately 1,000-bed residential community designed to foster connection and academic success for students.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: student housing

Property Name: The Southern Gateway

Beds: 1,000

Total Project Cost: $350.0M

$/Bed: $350,000/bed (calculated)

Completion: Fall 2028

Phase I: The Southern Gateway

Total Project (All Phases): Elevate WKU

Notable Features: Designed to optimize the student experience with a mix of private and semi-private suite-style rooms, lounges, shared spaces, community kitchens, a 24-hour study area, new dining options, and a multipurpose classroom.

Project Notes: This is the first phase of a broader housing transformation plan for Western Kentucky University, replacing Hugh Poland and Douglas Keen Halls.
