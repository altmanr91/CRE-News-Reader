NYC Lawmakers Consider Mandating Lease Extensions For Retailers

Bisnow Latest News Feed — Wed, 16 Sep 2026 21:56:53 -0400

SUMMARY

CRE Legal / Regulatory

New York City lawmakers are considering a bill that would mandate lease extensions for retail tenants, aiming to stabilize the retail leasing market. The proposed legislation seeks to address challenges faced by retailers, but critics express concern that such a mandate could have unintended negative consequences for both tenants and landlords.
