Westphal Company Lands $56M Refi for San Diego Apartments

REFINANCE

Commercial Observer — Thu, 13 Aug 2026 19:30:34 +0000

SUMMARY

The Westphal Company has secured $56.1 million in permanent, fixed-rate financing for its 354-unit Wintercrest Village Apartments in Lakeside, an unincorporated community in eastern San Diego County. Northmarq arranged the 10-year, interest-only, nonrecourse loan through its Fannie Mae platform, enabling the borrower to extract equity while preserving cash flow and waiving all operating escrows. This refinancing highlights a strategic move to optimize the asset's financial structure amidst a stabilizing San Diego multifamily market.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Wintercrest Village Apartments

Address: 12002 Wintercrest Drive, Lakeside, CA

Units: 354

Loan Amount: $56.1M

Loan Per Unit: $158,475/unit (calculated)

Notable Features: Garden-style community with one- and two-bedroom apartments, two swimming pools and spas, outdoor courtyards, and fitness rooms.

Project Notes: The nonrecourse loan carries a 10-year term with interest-only payments for the full term. The lender waived all operating escrows.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

SPONSOR: The Westphal Company

LENDER: Fannie Mae

MORTGAGE BROKER: Northmarq — Aaron Beck; Conor Freeman; Robbie Driscoll

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

San Diego's multifamily market stabilized in Q2, with vacancy at 5% and asking rents rising 0.5% to $2,598 per month. Investment sales slowed by 25% year-over-year, but median pricing increased 25% to $415,000 per unit.
