Related Urban targets aging Coconut Grove housing for two-tower project

DEVELOPMENT

The Real Deal — Miami — Tue, 21 Jul 2026 21:59:18 +0000

SUMMARY

Related Urban Development Group has filed plans for Magnus Grove and Stirrup Plaza, a two-tower multifamily project in Coconut Grove, Miami. The development aims to replace aging public housing with 750 new apartments, including affordable housing units under Florida's Live Local Act, along with retail, a library, and parking. This project aligns with Related Urban's strategy of partnering with Miami-Dade County to modernize public housing sites with higher-density, mixed-income developments, despite local opposition to the Live Local Act.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Magnus Grove and Stirrup Plaza

Address: 3150 and 3160 Mundy Street and 3181 Douglas Road, Miami, FL

Size: 7,300 SF

Units: 750

Site Area: 6.1 acres

Notable Features: Includes 7,300 square feet of retail, a 7,650-square-foot library, and a 796-space parking garage. Located within Miami-Dade’s Rapid Transit Zone near the Douglas Road Metrorail Station.

Project Notes: Project seeks to demolish a 100-unit apartment building (built 1975) and a 65-unit building (built 1982), while preserving a 68-unit apartment building and a standalone wellness center. At least 40 percent of units must be offered at below-market rates to qualify for the Live Local Act.
