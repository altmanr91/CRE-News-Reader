Cushman & Wakefield Arranges $250M Refinancing for 506-Unit Multifamily Property in Seattle

REFINANCE

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 21 Aug 2026 15:17:14 +0000

SUMMARY

Cushman & Wakefield has arranged a $250 million refinancing for Museum House, a 506-unit multifamily property in Seattle. The financing was secured on behalf of a pension trust and provided by Barings. Completed in 2025, the property includes both market-rate and income-restricted affordable homes, along with ground-floor retail space.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Museum House

Address: 707 Terry Ave, Seattle, WA*

Units: 506

Loan Amount: $250.0M

Loan Per Unit: $494,071/unit (calculated)

Year Built: 2025

Notable Features: Features 404 market-rate apartments and 102 income-restricted affordable homes through Seattle’s Multifamily Tax Exemption program, with two residential towers connected by a glass-enclosed skybridge.
