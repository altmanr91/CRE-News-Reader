Huff, Niehaus & Associates Brokers Sale of Quality Inn & Suites Hotel in Horse Cave, Kentucky

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Mon, 10 Aug 2026 13:51:42 +0000

SUMMARY

Huff, Niehaus & Associates has brokered the sale of the 68-room Quality Inn & Suites hotel in Horse Cave, Kentucky. The firm represented the undisclosed buyer in the transaction. The seller and sale price were not disclosed.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: hotel

Property Name: Quality Inn & Suites

Address: 425 Flint Ridge Road, Horse Cave, KY

Keys: 68

Notable Features: Located along the I-65 corridor; amenities include complimentary breakfast, a seasonal outdoor pool, Wi-Fi, free parking with RV/bus space, a business center, self-serve laundry facilities, and pet-friendly rooms.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER BROKER: Huff, Niehaus & Associates
