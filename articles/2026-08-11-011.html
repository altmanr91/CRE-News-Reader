KBS closes sale of seven-building business campus in Plano

SALE

REJournals — Tue, 11 Aug 2026 10:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

KBS has completed the sale of Tollway North Office Park, a seven-building Class-A business campus in Plano, TX, to BH Properties. The 297,591-square-foot property is located within the Legacy Business Park, a premier business destination in the Dallas-Fort Worth metro area. The transaction underscores the continued investor interest in well-located assets within this high-growth market, driven by strong population and employment gains.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Property Name: Tollway North Office Park

Address: 6505 Windcrest Dr, Plano, TX*

Size: 297,591 SF

Notable Features: Seven-building Class-A business campus with dedicated exterior suite entrances, 10- to 13-foot finished ceilings, and above-market parking ratio.

* Address sourced separately, not in original article.
