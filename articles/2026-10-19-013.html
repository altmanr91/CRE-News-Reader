Stream Realty Partners to oversee leasing at 900,000-square-foot office development in Chicago’s Fulton Market

REJournals — Mon, 19 Oct 2026 14:15:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Leasing Assignment

Stream Realty Partners has been appointed by ONNI Group to manage the leasing of 357 Green, a forthcoming 900,000-square-foot, 30-story office tower in Chicago's vibrant Fulton Market District. The development, designed by SCB, will offer extensive amenities including diverse food and beverage options, ample parking, and private terraces on each office floor. Its strategic location near the Fulton Market gateway sign and visibility from the Kennedy Expressway are expected to attract a prominent anchor tenant.
