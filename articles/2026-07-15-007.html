Joint Venture Buys Two Student Housing Communities Totaling 1,128 Beds Near Texas A&M University

ACQUISITION

REBusinessOnline — Tue, 14 Jul 2026 15:26:40 +0000

SUMMARY

A joint venture between PCCP LLC and The Dinerstein Cos. has acquired two adjacent student housing communities near Texas A&M University in College Station. The properties, now rebranded as Sterling College Station, offer a combined 1,128 beds across 350 units. The community features a variety of amenities including a pool, fitness center, and sports courts.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: student housing

Property Name: Sterling College Station

Units: 350

Beds: 1,128

Notable Features: Garden- and cottage-style units with one-, two-, and four-bedroom configurations. Amenities include a pool, fitness center, business center with cybercafé, private study rooms, basketball and beach volleyball courts, outdoor grilling stations, and a clubhouse with a community kitchen, game lounge, and pool tables.

Project Notes: Formerly known as 2818 Apartments and Parkway Place, the two adjacent communities have been combined and rebranded.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: PCCP LLC

BUYER: The Dinerstein Cos.
