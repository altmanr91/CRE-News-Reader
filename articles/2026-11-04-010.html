Horvath & Tremblay closes sale of two retail properties in Wisconsin

SALE

REJournals — Wed, 04 Nov 2026 16:42:13 +0000

SUMMARY

Horvath & Tremblay has facilitated the sale of two retail properties in Wisconsin for a combined total of $9.5 million. The New Berlin City Center, a fully leased 20,286-square-foot, eleven-tenant retail center, sold for $4.2 million. Separately, the Market Place at Sun Prairie, a 14,974-square-foot, seven-tenant retail plaza, was acquired for $5.3 million. Both properties are 100% occupied with strong tenant mixes and benefit from strategic locations within established commercial corridors, offering excellent visibility and regional connectivity.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

SELLER BROKER: Horvath & Tremblay — Marc Sabai; Mark Heidecke
