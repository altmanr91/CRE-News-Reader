Cabot Properties closes disposition of industrial portfolio in Minneapolis, Chicago markets

SALE

REJournals — Mon, 03 Aug 2026 10:02:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Cabot Properties has completed the sale of the North Central Portfolio, an 11-property industrial portfolio spanning approximately 2.5 million square feet across the Greater Chicago and Minneapolis markets. The portfolio, comprising both developed and acquired assets, was purchased by a global asset manager. The properties boast modern industrial specifications and high occupancy rates, reflecting strong tenant demand in their respective submarkets.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Property Name: North Central Portfolio

Size: 2,500,000 SF

Notable Features: Modern industrial specifications including 32-foot clear heights and ESFR sprinkler systems.

Project Notes: Portfolio includes assets both developed and acquired by Cabot Properties.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

SELLER: Cabot Properties

BUYER: prominent global asset manager

SELLER BROKER: CBRE Capital Markets

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

As of Q1 2026, the North Kane industrial submarket had a 5% vacancy rate, Central Kane had a 6.6% vacancy rate, and the West Suburbs submarket of Chicago had a 2.7% vacancy rate. In Minneapolis, the Northwest submarket had a 5.2% vacancy rate and the Southwest submarket had a 6.8% vacancy rate.
