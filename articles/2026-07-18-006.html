Partnership to Develop 265-Unit Multifamily Project in Uptown Dallas

DEVELOPMENT

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 17 Jul 2026 14:50:08 +0000

SUMMARY

A partnership between Endeavor Real Estate Group and Canyon Partners Real Estate is set to develop Lucille, a 265-unit multifamily project in Uptown Dallas. The 22-story building will also include 10,000 square feet of retail space and is expected to break ground in the coming weeks with completion anticipated in late 2028. German bank Helaba is providing the construction financing for the project.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Lucille

Address: 2700 McKinney Ave, Dallas, TX

Size: 10,000 SF

Units: 265

Completion: late 2028

Notable Features: 22-story building with below-grade parking, rooftop lounge with pool, cabanas, private dining, outdoor grilling, coworking space, business center, fitness center, and indoor dog grooming spa.
