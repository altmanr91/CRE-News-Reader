Magnus Breaks Ground on 226,800 SF Industrial Building at 803 Park in Columbia, South Carolina

CONSTRUCTION

REBusinessOnline — Thu, 17 Sep 2026 13:30:44 +0000

SUMMARY

Magnus Development Partners has commenced construction on Gateway 2, a 226,800-square-foot industrial building within the 803 Park industrial campus in Columbia, SC. This marks the fourth building in the campus, which is planned to eventually include five buildings totaling nearly 1 million square feet. The project is expected to be delivered by the second quarter of 2027, and the developer has engaged Colliers to handle leasing and marketing.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Property Name: Gateway 2 at 803 Park

Address: 803 Park, Columbia, SC

Size: 226,800 SF

Completion: Q2 2027

Total Project (All Phases): nearly 1 million square feet across five buildings

Notable Features: Adjacent to Columbia Metropolitan Airport; features CSX rail service, 18 dock doors, 50- by 54-foot bay spacing, and an ESFR fire protection system.

Project Notes: Three buildings totaling 625,440 square feet have been delivered and are fully leased.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

DEVELOPER/SPONSOR: Magnus Development Partners

LEASING AGENT: Colliers — Chuck Salley; Thomas Beard; John Peebles
