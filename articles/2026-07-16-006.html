Cushman & Wakefield Arranges $95.7M in Financing for Metro Houston, Dallas Affordable Housing Portfolio

LOAN

REBusinessOnline — Wed, 15 Jul 2026 15:10:06 +0000

SUMMARY

Cushman & Wakefield has secured $95.7 million in financing for a two-property affordable housing portfolio in the Houston and Dallas metro areas. The financing package includes $76.2 million in senior debt from Benefit Street Partners and a $19.5 million mezzanine loan from CCL Capital. The portfolio, owned by Sundance Bay, consists of 626 units across two properties built in 2021 and subject to Housing Finance Corp. income restrictions.
