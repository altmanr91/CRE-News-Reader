JPMorgan Funnels $205M to Refi Blackstone’s East Hotel in Miami

REFINANCE

Commercial Observer — Mon, 13 Jul 2026 20:50:20 +0000

SUMMARY

J.P. Morgan Chase has provided a $205 million refinancing loan to Blackstone Real Estate for the East Miami hotel in the Brickell district. This transaction follows Blackstone's acquisition of the 39-story, 352-room luxury hotel last year for approximately $300 million. The financing marks the first loan placed on the property by Blackstone since its purchase.
