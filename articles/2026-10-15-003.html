Get to Know the Broker: Patrick Southern of Serhant

Commercial Observer — Thu, 15 Oct 2026 11:15:51 +0000

SUMMARY

Feature / Profile

This article profiles Patrick Southern, a residential broker at Serhant, highlighting his significant transaction volume in New Jersey, particularly Jersey City. Southern aims to elevate Jersey City's residential market to rival Manhattan's luxury appeal. He recounts his career's largest deal, managing the sale of a 700-property portfolio for a hedge fund, and discusses his current goal of building a new development business in New Jersey with Ryan Serhant.
