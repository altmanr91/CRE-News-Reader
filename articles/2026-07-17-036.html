San Jose’s deal for Urban Catalyst building faces pushback over resi goals

The Real Deal — San Francisco — Thu, 16 Jul 2026 22:06:20 +0000

SUMMARY

Development

A proposed 276-unit apartment project in downtown San Jose, known as The Gifford, is facing delays in its approval process due to neighborhood opposition regarding its design and compatibility with surrounding single-family homes. This development is linked to a separate agreement where the City of San Jose plans to acquire the historic Knox-Goodrich building from Urban Catalyst for $3.6 million, with the expectation that Urban Catalyst would then commence construction on The Gifford. Despite city officials asserting the independence of the two approvals, the intertwined nature of the deals has drawn additional scrutiny as the city aims to meet its state-mandated housing creation goals.
