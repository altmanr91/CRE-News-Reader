New Empire Receives $75M in Construction Financing for Brooklyn Multifamily Project

LOAN

REBusinessOnline — Tue, 22 Sep 2026 13:56:18 +0000

SUMMARY

New Empire has secured $75 million in construction financing for a new multifamily development in Brooklyn's Flatbush neighborhood. The capital stack includes a $58 million senior loan from Madison Realty Capital and a $17 million mezzanine loan from Naftali Credit Partners. The project will feature 131 residential units and ground-floor commercial space.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Address: 757 Flatbush Ave., Brooklyn, NY

Units: 131

Loan Amount: $75.0M

Loan Per Unit: $572,519/unit (calculated)

Notable Features: Designed by Morali Architects with interiors by Paris Forino, the nine-story building will offer studio, one-, two- and three-bedroom units.
