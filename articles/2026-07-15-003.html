London Nightclub Operator Buys 675 Hudson Street for $100M

ACQUISITION

Commercial Observer — Tue, 14 Jul 2026 21:08:20 +0000

SUMMARY

London-based club operator Caprice Holdings has acquired the five-story Herring Building at 675 Hudson Street in New York City for $100 million from Aurora Capital Associates. The buyer plans to convert the historic 19th-century property into a private club, a New York outpost of its Annabel's brand, pending approval from the New York City Landmarks Preservation Commission for proposed alterations including a rooftop addition. HSBC provided $165 million in financing for the acquisition.
