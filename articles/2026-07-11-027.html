There are no offices being built in Philadelphia

The Real Deal — Philadelphia — Fri, 10 Jul 2026 19:39:22 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Office Report

Philadelphia's office development pipeline has stalled, with no new projects underway following the completion of a 438,000-square-foot build-to-suit for an insurance company. This lack of new construction is largely attributed to a scarcity of anchor tenants willing to commit to space years in advance. While the central business district saw a drop in vacancy to 19.8% and positive absorption in Q2, this was primarily driven by the insurance company's move. Overall office inventory and sublease space are decreasing, partly due to conversions, though suburban office leasing activity has also significantly slowed.
