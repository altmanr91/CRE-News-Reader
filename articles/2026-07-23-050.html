Startup Building AI Agents For Landlords Raises $12M

Bisnow News Feed — Wed, 22 Jul 2026 17:14:32 -0400

SUMMARY

Non-CRE / Business News

Uniti, a proptech startup specializing in AI agents for landlord operations and leasing, has successfully closed a $12 million Series A funding round. The New York-based company's AI agents automate various tasks, including scheduling tours, managing inquiries, handling collections, and processing payments for landlords. This funding will enable Uniti to expand its business and accelerate growth across multiple verticals, particularly in operationally intensive asset classes like self-storage, senior living, and manufactured housing.
