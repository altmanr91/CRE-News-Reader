KAI, SmithGroup help deliver long-awaited aquatics center to DeSoto

CONSTRUCTION

REJournals — Mon, 20 Jul 2026 09:35:00 +0000

SUMMARY

The City of DeSoto officially opened its new $53 million Aquatics & Recreation Center (ARC) in April, a project that has been envisioned by residents for over a decade. Designed in partnership by KAI and SmithGroup, the 75,000-square-foot, two-story facility aims to serve both community recreation needs and provide a competitive venue for various sports. The center is part of a larger master plan for Curtistene S. McCowan Park, which includes future amenities like an amphitheater and walking trails.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: recreation center

Property Name: Aquatics & Recreation Center (ARC)

Address: 1400 Academy Way, DeSoto, TX

Size: 75,000 SF

Total Project Cost: $53.0M

$/SF: $707/SF (calculated)

Completion: April 2026

Notable Features: Features an 8-lane competition pool, leisure pool with observation decks, a 400-foot suspended fitness track, fitness and strength training areas, meeting rooms, locker rooms, gymnasiums, administrative offices, and social gathering spaces.

Project Notes: The project is part of a larger master plan for Curtistene S. McCowan Park, which includes future plans for an amphitheater, water features, sculpture garden, walking trails, and additional sports fields.
