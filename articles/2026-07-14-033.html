Hines snags Downtown Austin office high-rise for $733 per sf

ACQUISITION

The Real Deal — Texas — Mon, 13 Jul 2026 19:28:53 +0000

SUMMARY

Hines has acquired the 405 Colorado Street office tower in Downtown Austin from Brandywine Realty Trust for $151 million, or approximately $733 per square foot. The Class A, 25-story building, completed in 2021, is fully leased. Hines' global co-head of investment management noted that fully leased trophy office buildings are key drivers of the office market's recovery.
