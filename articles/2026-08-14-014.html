Northmarq provides $68.76 million in financing for three-proprty apartment portfolio in Houston, San Antonio

LOAN

REJournals — Thu, 13 Aug 2026 16:29:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Northmarq has secured $68.76 million in permanent fixed-rate financing for a three-property Class A multifamily portfolio totaling 940 units across Houston and San Antonio, Texas. The financing, arranged on behalf of Francis Property Management through Freddie Mac, features a 10-year term with full-term interest-only payments for each loan. The portfolio includes Holden Heights Apartments and Estancia San Miguel Apartments in Houston, and The Montecristo Apartments in San Antonio, all offering a range of amenities and strategic access to employment and retail centers.
