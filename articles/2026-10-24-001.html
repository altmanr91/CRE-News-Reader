CP Group Sells Third Boca Raton Office for $79M

SALE

Commercial Observer — Fri, 23 Oct 2026 19:55:27 +0000

SUMMARY

CP Group and Harrison Street Asset Management have sold One Boca Place, a 286,890-square-foot office complex in Boca Raton, FL, for $78.9 million to Golden East Investors. The transaction was financed by City National Bank of Florida with an $82.1 million loan. This sale represents the third office property CP Group has divested in Boca Raton since the summer, following the sales of Two Town Center and the BRiC campus.
