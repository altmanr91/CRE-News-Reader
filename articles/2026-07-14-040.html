Events Operator Glasshouse Signs 30-Year Lease for 3 World Trade Center

LEASE

Connect CRE — Mon, 13 Jul 2026 21:25:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Glasshouse, an events and experiential venue operator, has signed a 30-year lease for 66,436 square feet at 3 World Trade Center in New York City. This transaction, structured as a three-party agreement, marks Glasshouse's fourth Manhattan location and is considered one of the most significant experiential leasing deals in the city recently. The new venue is expected to draw over 250,000 visitors annually, enhancing the vibrancy and appeal of the World Trade Center campus and Lower Manhattan.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: retail

Property Name: 3 World Trade Center

Address: 3 World Trade Center, New York City, NY

Size: 66,436 SF

Notable Features: Located within the World Trade Center campus.

Project Notes: 30-year lease agreement for a portion of the ground floor and the entirety of the second and third floors.
