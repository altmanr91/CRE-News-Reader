Inland Mortgage Capital closes $44 million in bridge loans for projects in Wisconsin, Illinois and California

LOAN

REJournals — Tue, 22 Sep 2026 17:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Inland Mortgage Capital recently closed nearly $44 million in non-recourse, first mortgage bridge loans across four projects in California, Illinois, and Wisconsin. These transactions, completed within a three-month period, highlight the lender's strategy of providing flexible financing for transitional assets with experienced sponsors. The loans included an acquisition loan for an industrial project in Paramount, California, and refinancing for properties in Chicago and Naperville, Illinois.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

LENDER: Inland Mortgage Capital, LLC

SPONSOR PIPELINE

44,000-square-foot shallow bay industrial project: Acquisition loan in Paramount, CA

32,000-square-foot collection of buildings: Refinancing in Lincoln Park, Chicago

Three-story, mixed-use property: Refinancing in downtown Naperville, IL
