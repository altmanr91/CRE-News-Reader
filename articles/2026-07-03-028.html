Judge keeps lawsuit alive over $184M LaSalle Street conversion fight

The Real Deal — Chicago — Thu, 02 Jul 2026 19:33:57 +0000

SUMMARY

CRE Legal / Regulatory

A Cook County judge has allowed a lawsuit to proceed against a joint venture involving Marc Calabria and Primera Group, accused of misappropriating a development plan for a $184 million office-to-residential conversion at 105 West Adams Street in Chicago. Celadon Partners and Blackwood Group allege that Primera principal Gabriel Martinez, initially engaged to acquire the property on their behalf, instead partnered with Calabria to pursue the redevelopment themselves. The plaintiffs are seeking $15 million in damages and are still considering their involvement in the 400-apartment project, which had previously secured nearly $68 million in tax-increment financing from the Chicago City Council.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

The Loop area of Downtown Chicago is undergoing restoration, with developers focusing on converting vacant office space to residential, hotel, or retail uses, or upgrading amenities to attract tenants. Some downtown buildings are selling at discounts of up to 75 percent.
