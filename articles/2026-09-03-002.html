Get to Know the Broker: Libby McKinney Tritschler of William Raveis Real Estate

Commercial Observer — Thu, 03 Sep 2026 11:15:45 +0000

SUMMARY

Feature / Profile

This article profiles Libby McKinney Tritschler, Executive Vice President of Sales and founder of the On The Harbor real estate team at William Raveis Real Estate. With over 20 years of experience, she specializes in the Southport, Fairfield, and Westport markets in Connecticut, having closed over $800 million in sales. McKinney Tritschler emphasizes community connection and trust in her approach to real estate, aiming to be a trusted advisor for generations of families rather than focusing solely on sales goals. She highlights two significant transactions: the $16 million sale of 66 Beachside Avenue, a 7-acre family property, and the $12 million sale of 260 Willow Street, where she represented both the seller and buyer.
