Proptech Firm Dwelly Raises $170M For Its AI-Forward Acquisition Plan

Bisnow News Feed — Tue, 28 Jul 2026 14:35:50 -0400

SUMMARY

Non-CRE / Business News

Proptech firm Dwelly has secured $170 million in new financing, comprising $95 million in equity and a $75 million debt facility, to fuel its acquisition strategy. The UK-based company, founded in 2023, specializes in acquiring small property management and brokerage firms, integrating their databases, and leveraging AI to enhance operational efficiency and reduce costs. This capital infusion will enable Dwelly to expand its portfolio of acquired listing companies, which currently manages 15,000 properties and generates $466 million in annual rent. The firm's approach, which includes using AI agents to manage a higher volume of units and streamline tasks like maintenance requests, reflects a broader industry trend among multifamily operators seeking to optimize efficiency amidst rising costs and flatlining property incomes.
