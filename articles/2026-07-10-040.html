Blue Owl Launches Data Center Infrastructure Venture

Bisnow News Feed — Thu, 09 Jul 2026 15:53:43 -0400

SUMMARY

Development

Blue Owl Capital has launched Kirkwood Infrastructure Group, a new venture focused on developing, owning, and operating fiber networks to support the burgeoning data center industry. Kirkwood will serve hyperscalers, data center operators, and communication providers, integrating the operations of South Reach Networks, a fiber infrastructure company Blue Owl acquired last year. The new entity plans to expand its network with 200 miles of conduit and high-capacity fiber across Louisiana and Mississippi, capitalizing on the significant investment in digital infrastructure driven by AI adoption.

SPONSOR PIPELINE

Meta data centers in Louisiana: Provided $3B in financing as part of a $29B partnership with Pimco for construction.

Data center project in Northern Virginia: Provided nearly $1B to recapitalize the project.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Goldman Sachs predicts $7.6 trillion in investment by big tech companies by 2031 due to exploding AI adoption. Blue Owl's private credit funds received $5.4 billion in redemption requests in Q1 and $4.7 billion in Q2, leading to capped withdrawals.
