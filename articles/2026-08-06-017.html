JAMS Relocating New York Offices to 3 Times Square

LEASE

Connect CRE — Wed, 05 Aug 2026 21:38:13 +0000

SUMMARY

JAMS, a provider of alternative dispute resolution services, has signed an 11-year lease to relocate its New York Resolution Center to 3 Times Square. The company will occupy 55,335 square feet across the top two floors of the building, with plans to move from its current offices at 620 Eighth Ave. in the fourth quarter of 2027. This significant commitment highlights the ongoing demand for high-quality, well-located office properties in Midtown Manhattan that offer premier amenities and excellent transit connectivity.
