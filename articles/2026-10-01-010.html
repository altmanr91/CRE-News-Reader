JLL Arranges $41.1M Construction Loan for Long Island Seniors Housing Project

LOAN

REBusinessOnline — Thu, 01 Oct 2026 14:10:09 +0000

SUMMARY

JLL has secured a $41.1 million construction loan for the Benchmark at East Islip, a seniors housing development on Long Island. The project, a joint venture between National Development and an affiliate of Benchmark Senior Living, will feature 64 assisted living units and 26 memory care apartments, totaling 104 licensed beds. Construction is currently in progress and is slated for completion in early 2028.

DATA POINTS

Property Name: Benchmark at East Islip

Units: 90

Notable Features: Amenities will include multiple dining venues, a theater, fitness center, salon, and various outdoor spaces.
