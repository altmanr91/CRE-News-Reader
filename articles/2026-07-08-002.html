Affinius Capital Provides $188M Refi for N.J. Mixed-Use Apartment Complex

REFINANCE

Commercial Observer — Tue, 07 Jul 2026 20:28:20 +0000

SUMMARY

A joint venture comprising PCCP, Claremont Development, and Stanbery Development Group has secured a $188 million refinancing package for District 15Fifteen, a newly opened 498-unit mixed-use multifamily property in Parsippany, NJ. Affinius Capital provided the debt, which will support the lease-up and stabilization of the asset. The property is considered a high-quality asset in a supply-constrained, transit-oriented submarket with compelling fundamentals.

Notable Features: Mixed-use property with three buildings, including 58,800 square feet of retail space and an 853-space parking garage. Amenities include a fitness center, multiple courtyards, a pool, a roof deck, and a dog spa.

Project Notes: The property opened in June 2026.
