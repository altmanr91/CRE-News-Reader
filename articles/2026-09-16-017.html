Northmarq Arranges Refi for Historic Koreatown Retail Center

REFINANCE

Connect CRE — Tue, 15 Sep 2026 21:52:27 +0000

SUMMARY

Northmarq's Debt + Equity teams in Newport Beach and Los Angeles successfully arranged a $29.3 million refinancing for Chapman Market, a historic multi-tenant dining and entertainment center in Los Angeles's Koreatown. The financing was secured on behalf of Arc Capital Partners through a correspondent relationship with Voya Investment Management. The property's prime location and the sponsor's institutional quality attracted significant interest from various capital sources, ultimately leading to competitive terms from Voya.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: retail

Property Name: Chapman Market

Address: 3465 W. 6th St., Los Angeles, CA

Size: 41,241 SF

Loan Amount: $29.3M

Loan Per SF: $710/SF (calculated)

Notable Features: Historic multi-tenant dining and entertainment center located in the vibrant Koreatown nightlife district.
