Mohr Capital breaks ground on 372,000-square-foot warehouse in Houston

CONSTRUCTION

REJournals — Mon, 17 Aug 2026 09:06:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Mohr Capital has commenced construction on Oates 610, a 372,000-square-foot Class A industrial cross-dock warehouse in Houston. The project, located in the Northeast Submarket, is expected to be delivered in the second quarter of 2027. Its strategic location near major interstates and the Port of Houston is anticipated to provide excellent access for future tenants.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Property Name: Oates 610

Address: 117 Oates Road, Houston, TX

Size: 372,000 SF

Completion: Q2 2027

Site Area: 23 acres

Notable Features: Cross-dock facility with 36-foot clear heights, 450-foot building depth, 80 dock-high doors, four drive-in doors, ESFR sprinkler system, and 91 trailer parking spaces. Strategically positioned near I-610, I-10, and US-90, offering convenient access to the Port of Houston.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

DEVELOPER/SPONSOR: Mohr Capital

LEASING AGENT: JLL — Richard Quarles; Joe Berwick

GENERAL CONTRACTOR: Cadence McShane
