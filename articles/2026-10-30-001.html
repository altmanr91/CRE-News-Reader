Jason Maurer Joins SL Green as Managing Director

Commercial Observer — Fri, 30 Oct 2026 15:30:28 +0000

SUMMARY

Promotion

Jason Maurer has been appointed Managing Director at SL Green Realty, where he will be responsible for overseeing retail leasing across the company's portfolio and leading office leasing for Green Property Services, SL Green's third-party asset management platform. Maurer brings two decades of experience in retail and office leasing, having previously served as Executive Vice President and Head of Leasing for North America at Brookfield Properties (now BGRE). His expertise is expected to enhance SL Green's retail portfolio in New York and contribute to the growth of Green Property Services.
