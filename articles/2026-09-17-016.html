Naftali Group Brings CorePower Yoga to Gramercy

LEASE

Connect CRE — Wed, 16 Sep 2026 22:06:49 +0000

SUMMARY

CorePower Yoga, the largest yoga studio brand in the nation, is set to open its first Gramercy location at The Willow, a new luxury residential development by Naftali Group. The studio will occupy 3,100 square feet of retail space on the ground floor of the recently opened boutique-scale residential building. This addition is expected to enhance the wellness offerings for residents and the surrounding neighborhood, aligning with the building's design philosophy of integrating with Gramercy's character.
