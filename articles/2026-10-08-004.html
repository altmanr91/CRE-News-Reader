AI Platform Synthesia Moves HQ to 50K SF at GFP’s 675 Avenue of the Americas

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Thu, 08 Oct 2026 14:30:16 +0000

SUMMARY

AI video platform Synthesia has signed a 50,000-square-foot lease for its new U.S. headquarters at 675 Avenue of the Americas in New York City's Flatiron District. The move will nearly quadruple the company's office footprint, allowing for significant headcount expansion and the creation of enhanced employee and customer experience spaces, including a studio for custom avatar creation and video program collaboration. The company is relocating from 245 Fifth Avenue.
