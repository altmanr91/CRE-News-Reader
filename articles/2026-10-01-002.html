Manhattan’s Available Office Supply Falls to Lowest Level Since 2020: Colliers

Commercial Observer — Thu, 01 Oct 2026 15:14:03 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Office Report

Manhattan's office market demonstrated robust performance in the third quarter of 2026, with leasing volume surpassing both five-year and 10-year quarterly averages, according to Colliers. The quarter marked significant milestones, including four consecutive quarters of leasing exceeding 10 million square feet for the first time since 2002, and the strongest third quarter on record for Midtown South. This strong activity led to the lowest available office supply since August 2020 and the 10th consecutive quarter of tightening availability, a trend not seen since 2007. Average asking rents also reached their highest levels since July 2020, indicating a rapid recovery and a new chapter for the market.
