Tishman Speyer Signs 150-Year Ground Lease to Reposition Chrysler Building

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Wed, 07 Oct 2026 13:04:36 +0000

SUMMARY

Tishman Speyer has signed a 150-year ground lease for the iconic Chrysler Building in New York City, taking over stewardship from The Cooper Union for the Advancement of Science and Art, which owns the land. The agreement includes a commitment from Tishman Speyer to invest $235 million in renovations and upgrades to reposition the 77-story office tower. The repositioning strategy will focus on converting a significant portion of vacant space into pre-built office suites to attract new tenants, addressing the building's high vacancy rate and aiming to revitalize its market presence.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Property Name: Chrysler Building

Address: 405 Lexington Avenue, New York City, NY

Total Project Cost: $235.0M

Notable Features: Landmarked 77-story office tower; repositioning plans include converting 75% of vacant space into pre-built office suites.

Project Notes: The Cooper Union for the Advancement of Science and Art evicted previous leaseholder RFR Holding in November 2024 due to default on ground-rent payments. The new ground lease is for 150 years.
