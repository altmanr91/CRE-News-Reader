Marcus & Millichap Brokers Sale of 645-Unit Self-Storage Facility in San Antonio

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 18 Sep 2026 14:49:28 +0000

SUMMARY

Marcus & Millichap has brokered the sale of a 645-unit self-storage facility in San Antonio. The property, built in 2023 and previously operated by CubeSmart, was acquired by Public Storage, which has since rebranded the facility. The transaction involved a New Jersey-based limited liability company as the seller.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: self-storage

Size: 115,240 SF

Occupancy at Sale: 89.0%

Year Built: 2023

Site Area: 10.6 acres

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

SELLER BROKER: Marcus & Millichap — Jon Danklefs

BUYER: Public Storage

OWNER: CubeSmart
