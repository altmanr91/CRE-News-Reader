Skin Care Brand FaceGym to Open New Tribeca Location at 110 Reade Street

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Fri, 16 Oct 2026 15:40:35 +0000

SUMMARY

FaceGym, a skin care brand specializing in "facial fitness" studios, has signed a 10-year lease for 2,400 square feet at 110 Reade Street in Tribeca, New York City. The new location is expected to open in October 2026. The landlord's broker noted that Tribeca has become a prime destination for luxury wellness and beauty, making it an ideal fit for FaceGym's brand identity and customer engagement strategy.
