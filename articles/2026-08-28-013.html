Core Spaces delivers 6,000 new student housing beds at colleges across country

CONSTRUCTION

REJournals — Fri, 28 Aug 2026 15:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Core Spaces has delivered approximately 6,000 new student housing beds across six purpose-built student housing communities, completing all projects ahead of schedule for the 2026 academic year. These developments expand Core's national portfolio in Tier 1 university markets, emphasizing thoughtful design, innovation, and a hospitality-driven approach to student living. The successful and early delivery of these large-scale communities highlights the strength of Core's integrated platform and the disciplined execution of its development, construction, and operational teams.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: student housing

Beds: 6,000

Completion: 2026 academic year

Project Notes: All six projects were completed at least six weeks ahead of move-in and under budget.
