Zurich Affiliate Acquires San Francisco Offices with Leasing Upside

ACQUISITION

Connect CRE — Wed, 23 Sep 2026 21:53:53 +0000

SUMMARY

An affiliate of Zurich Alternative Asset Management has acquired 410 Townsend, a 78,455-square-foot historic office building in downtown San Francisco, for $47.4 million. The seller was a joint venture between New York Life Real Estate Investors and Bridgeton Holdings. The property, built in 1912, offers significant leasing upside with its current 65% occupancy, reflecting a strengthening market for well-located, quality assets in San Francisco.
