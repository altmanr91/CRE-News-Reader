McShane Construction Company to build 93-unit affordable-housing community in Madison

CONSTRUCTION

REJournals — Wed, 22 Jul 2026 16:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

McShane Construction Company has been selected by Lincoln Avenue Communities to build Timberline Terrace, a 93-unit affordable housing community in Madison, Wisconsin. The podium-style building will feature five wood-framed stories over a below-grade precast garage and is designed to achieve Wisconsin Green Built Homes Gold Zero certification, incorporating sustainable features like a solar array and high-efficiency heat pumps. Completion is anticipated in fall 2027.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Timberline Terrace

Address: 4504 Verona Road, Madison, WI

Units: 93

Site Area: 1.1 acres

Notable Features: Designed to achieve Wisconsin Green Built Homes Gold Zero certification, incorporating a solar array, high-efficiency heat pumps, and upgraded performance windows. Amenities include a 4,000-square-foot plaza with a playground and grill stations, a playroom, community room, lounge, community service space, and fitness area.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

GENERAL CONTRACTOR: McShane Construction Company

DEVELOPER/SPONSOR: Lincoln Avenue Communities

ARCHITECT: Knothe & Bruce Architects

ENGINEER: The Sigma Group
