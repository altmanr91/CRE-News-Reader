Sunday Summary: Market Slowing Down? Speeding Up? How About Both?

Commercial Observer — Sun, 25 Oct 2026 13:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

This article provides a comprehensive overview of the commercial real estate market, highlighting a mixed environment where significant capital continues to flow into major projects while other segments experience a slowdown. Billionaire Ken Griffin secured $3.3 billion in construction financing for a massive office tower in New York City, and UDR partnered with Apollo Global Management in a $2 billion joint venture for 19 multifamily communities. Despite these large deals, Manhattan's investment sales volume dropped 27% last quarter, though overall dollar volume was up year-over-year. Corporate earnings reports from industry giants like Blackstone showed profit dips, attributed to rising bond yields and higher capital costs, while CBRE reported strong revenue growth. South Florida, however, remains a hotbed of activity, with robust demand for luxury condos and significant office and refinancing deals. The market appears to be navigating a complex landscape of high-value transactions and broader economic headwinds.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Manhattan investment sales dropped 27% last quarter, but the dollar volume was 4% higher than the same period last year. Lease sweeteners in the office market are largely in eclipse from their 2024 peak. U.S. Treasury yields hit the highest level in decades in Q3 2026, negatively impacting investor sentiment and transaction activity.
