Shops at Sunset Place owner seeks taxing district to raise $149M

The Real Deal — Miami — Wed, 15 Jul 2026 20:30:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Development

Midtown Development is seeking approval from Miami-Dade County to establish a special taxing district to finance $149 million in infrastructure for its planned Downtown SoMi mixed-use redevelopment in South Miami. The proposed district would issue tax-exempt bonds, with repayment through property assessments, to fund various infrastructure improvements including roads, utilities, a public parking garage, and a chilled water system. This nine-figure boost is crucial for the long-awaited redevelopment of the 10.2-acre former Shops at Sunset Place, which is slated to begin infrastructure construction in 2027.
