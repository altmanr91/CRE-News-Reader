Gaia Lands Another Extension for Williamsburg Apartments’ $48M Loan

LOAN

Commercial Observer — Fri, 04 Sep 2026 15:40:09 +0000

SUMMARY

GAIA Real Estate has secured a second one-year extension for its $48 million loan on a multifamily property at 55 Hope Street in Williamsburg, Brooklyn, pushing the maturity date to August 2027. The developer acquired the 117-unit apartment building in August 2022 for $80 million and has since increased net operating income through active management and property enhancements. Despite interest from other lenders for a full refinancing, GAIA opted for the extension due to the current interest rate environment, citing a strong loan-to-value ratio and debt service coverage.
