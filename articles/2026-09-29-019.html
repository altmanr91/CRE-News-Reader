JV Pays $220M for Alpharetta Hotel

ACQUISITION

Connect CRE — Tue, 29 Sep 2026 14:17:30 +0000

SUMMARY

A joint venture between Endeavor Real Estate Group and Cox has acquired The Hotel at Avalon, a 330-key hotel in Alpharetta, from Crescent Real Estate for $220 million. The hotel, which opened in 2018, is part of the larger Avalon mixed-use district and includes the Alpharetta Conference Center.
