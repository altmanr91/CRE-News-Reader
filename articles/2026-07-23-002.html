It’s Finally Showtime Again at Hollywood’s Cinerama Dome

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Wed, 22 Jul 2026 19:35:28 +0000

SUMMARY

Sony Pictures Entertainment and its subsidiary, Alamo Drafthouse Cinema, have signed a long-term lease to restore and reopen the iconic Cinerama Dome and the adjacent 14-screen former ArcLight Cinemas complex in Hollywood, six years after their closure. The Cinerama Dome, a Los Angeles Historic Cultural Monument, will preserve its unique curved screen and large-format presentation, while the neighboring multiplex will reopen as an Alamo Drafthouse with dine-in service. This significant lease is seen as a strong vote of confidence in the future of theatrical entertainment and the demand for shared moviegoing experiences, marking an exciting new chapter for a beloved cultural landmark.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: retail

Property Name: Cinerama Dome / Dome Entertainment Center

Address: 6360 Sunset Boulevard, Los Angeles, CA

Notable Features: Los Angeles Historic Cultural Monument with an 86-foot-wide curved screen, known for 70-millimeter film presentations. The adjacent multiplex will feature 4K laser projection and 70-millimeter cinematic capabilities.

Project Notes: The Cinerama Dome is expected to reopen in early 2028, with renovations beginning in August 2026. The property closed in March 2020, and ArcLight announced in 2021 it would not reopen its theaters.
