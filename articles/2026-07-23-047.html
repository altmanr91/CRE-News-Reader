Proposed EB-5 Rule Clamping Down On Bridge Financing Rattles Sector

Bisnow News Feed — Wed, 22 Jul 2026 15:32:22 -0400

SUMMARY

CRE Legal / Regulatory

The U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS) has released a draft rulebook for the EB-5 program, introducing significant changes that could impact real estate development financing. A key proposal restricts the use of bridge financing, a popular method for EB-5 investors to qualify for visas, by imposing stricter job creation standards. This shift aims to enhance program integrity but has raised concerns among industry stakeholders that it could disqualify many projects currently in the pipeline and reduce investment flow. The industry is preparing to submit public comments, hoping USCIS will adjust the rules to prevent disruption.
