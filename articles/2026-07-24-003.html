Fashion Nova Expands L.A. Footprint With 133K-SF Warehouse Deal

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Thu, 23 Jul 2026 22:20:03 +0000

SUMMARY

Fashion Nova has expanded its logistics operations in Southern California by signing a lease for a 132,888-square-foot warehouse in Santa Fe Springs. This transaction significantly increases the fast-fashion retailer's footprint in the area. The deal reflects a broader trend of increasing leasing momentum in the Los Angeles County industrial market, which has seen a substantial rise in activity and positive net absorption.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Address: 12301 Hawkins Street, Santa Fe Springs, CA

Size: 132,888 SF

Rental Rate: $14.76/SF/yr

Site Area: 5.4 acres

Notable Features: The recently renovated facility includes 12,004 square feet of office space, 30-foot clear heights, 18 dock-high doors, two ground-level doors, and 175 parking spaces.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

LANDLORD: Clarion Partners

TENANT: Fashion Nova

LEASING AGENT: JLL

TENANTS AT PROPERTY

Fashion Nova

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Los Angeles County's industrial market saw 15.7 million square feet of leasing activity in Q2, a 40% increase year-over-year, and 2.8 million square feet of positive net absorption, marking its third consecutive positive quarter. Vacancy rates declined to 5% from Q1, while average asking rents dropped 7% year-over-year to $1.19 per square foot per month, and 32.4% from Q2 2023. The Mid-Counties submarket recorded 1.1 million square feet of positive absorption and an average asking rate of $1.23 per square foot.
