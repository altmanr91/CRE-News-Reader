Pinnacle Group Sells Rights to Upper West Side Apartment Tower for $88M

SALE

Commercial Observer — Tue, 18 Aug 2026 19:20:52 +0000

SUMMARY

Pinnacle Group has sold its lease rights and a 50% stake in the land rights for a 173-unit apartment building at 323 West 96th Street in Manhattan to Lightstone Group for approximately $88 million. The transaction, which included an $11 million surplus for bondholders, follows Pinnacle's recent offloading of a $128 million condominium and commercial portfolio across New York City. The sale is part of Pinnacle's efforts to address significant debt after placing a large rent-stabilized portfolio into bankruptcy last year.

Project Notes: Pinnacle Group secured the leasehold in 2007 and acquired a land rights stake in 2016 for $27 million. The property carried an existing loan balance of approximately $65 million as of March 2026, which was transferred to a Barclays-sponsored CMBS trust in 2023.
