Marcus & Millichap Closes Sale of Downtown Westfield Mixed-Use

SALE

Connect CRE — Fri, 14 Aug 2026 20:59:56 +0000

SUMMARY

Marcus & Millichap has brokered the sale of a 17,831-square-foot mixed-use property in downtown Westfield, NJ, for $5.26 million. The three-story building, which was renovated in 2014, features nine suites with a diverse mix of service-oriented tenants. The property's premier downtown location and immediate proximity to the train station were key factors in generating significant investor interest.
