Munish Viralam of Newmark: 5 Questions

Commercial Observer — Tue, 04 Aug 2026 19:30:04 +0000

SUMMARY

Feature / Profile

Munish Viralam, formerly of CBRE, has joined Newmark as Executive Vice Chairman to lead the firm's newly centralized real estate strategy and consulting group. His role involves scaling Newmark's strategic advisory arm and assisting corporate tenants in New York City with their real estate strategies and negotiations. Viralam emphasizes a transactional-based, analytical approach that integrates Newmark's diverse strengths, including corporate advisory, research, workplace strategy, and capital markets, to provide comprehensive long-term occupancy solutions for clients. He notes the strong demand for high-quality office assets in New York City, driven by companies reinvesting in their occupancy post-COVID-19 to attract talent and scale businesses, despite a tight market for such properties.
