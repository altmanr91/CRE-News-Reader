Jacobson Properties Arranges $13M Sale of Medical Office Building in Liverpool, New York

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Thu, 23 Jul 2026 14:59:16 +0000

SUMMARY

Jacobson Properties has facilitated the $13 million sale of a 38,913-square-foot medical office building in Liverpool, New York. The property was fully leased at the time of sale, and the brokerage firm also secured long-term lease extensions with two tenants prior to the transaction. Both the seller and the institutional healthcare real estate investment firm that acquired the asset chose to remain anonymous.
