Opportunity and uncertainty? That’s Pacer.ai’s outlook for the holiday shopping season

REJournals — Mon, 26 Oct 2026 09:35:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Retail Trends

Placer.ai's latest holiday retail research indicates that the 2026 holiday shopping season will be characterized by both opportunity and uncertainty, with forecasts for higher sales tempered by economic pressures influencing consumer spending and shopping locations. The analysis highlights a trend towards earlier big-ticket purchases, driven by October promotions, while traditional peak shopping days like Black Friday remain crucial for electronics and home furnishings. For smaller-ticket items, the opportunity shifts closer to Christmas, with affordable gifts expected to appeal to budget-conscious consumers. The report also suggests that shopping centers can capitalize on changing consumer behavior by offering dining, entertainment, and seasonal events to enhance the visitor experience and encourage longer dwell times.

DATA POINTS

Increase in early shopping for electronics stores (2019-2025): 4 percentage points

Increase in early shopping for home furnishings chains (2019-2025): 3.4 percentage points

Electronics stores visit increase (July-September): 0.4%

Top year-over-year visit growth (July-September): Bookstores

Second highest year-over-year visit growth (July-September): Off-price apparel retailers

Third highest year-over-year visit growth (July-September): Hobby, gift and craft stores

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Between 2019 and 2025, the share of October-through-December visits to electronics stores occurring before Thanksgiving week increased by 4 percentage points, and for home furnishings chains, that share climbed by 3.4 percentage points. From July to September, bookstores recorded the strongest year-over-year visit growth, followed by off-price apparel retailers and hobby, gift and craft stores. Visits to traditional apparel brands, department stores, and home furnishings chains fell year-over-year, while electronics stores saw a modest 0.4% increase.
