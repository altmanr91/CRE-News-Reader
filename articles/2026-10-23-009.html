Mercor Signs 25,550 SF Office Lease at One World Trade Center in Lower Manhattan

LEASE

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 23 Oct 2026 13:34:39 +0000

SUMMARY

Mercor, a company focused on human intelligence for the AI economy, has signed a 25,550-square-foot office lease on the 77th floor of One World Trade Center in Lower Manhattan. The Durst Organization, which developed the iconic building in partnership with The Port Authority of New York and New Jersey, was represented by Newmark and internal agents. This lease signifies continued demand for prime office space in a prominent downtown location.
