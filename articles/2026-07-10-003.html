Bobby Zar Snaps Up 827 Broadway for $19M Via Short Sale

ACQUISITION

Commercial Observer — Thu, 09 Jul 2026 20:23:25 +0000

SUMMARY

ZG Capital Partners, led by Bobby Zar, has acquired the interconnected buildings at 827-831 Broadway and 47 East 12th Street in Greenwich Village for $18.5 million through a short sale. This transaction represents a significant discount from its previous sale price over a decade ago, reflecting the challenges faced by the prior owners whose redevelopment plans were halted by landmark designation. The acquisition adds to ZG Capital's holdings in the area, following its successful leasing of a nearby property.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Address: 827-831 Broadway, New York City, NY

Sale Price: $18.5M

Notable Features: The Broadway buildings have landmark status due to their cultural and historical significance, having housed the Wilson Sewing Machine Company, A.A. Vantine, and served as studios for artists like Willem de Kooning.

Project Notes: The previous owners, Quality Capital and Caerus, acquired the buildings in 2015 for $60 million with plans for a 14-story office and retail building, but these plans were blocked in October 2017 when the New York City Landmarks Preservation Commission granted landmark status to 827-831 Broadway.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE
