Extell Finalizes Deal to Buy 110 East 55th Street for $65M

ACQUISITION

Commercial Observer — Mon, 17 Aug 2026 21:09:25 +0000

SUMMARY

Extell Development has finalized the acquisition of 110 East 55th Street, a 19-story office building in Midtown, from the Parkoff Organization for $65 million. The transaction follows a pre-foreclosure suit filed against the property in May, alleging a loan default by Parkoff. This acquisition further expands Extell's portfolio of Midtown assets, with the developer having been in contract for the property since January.
