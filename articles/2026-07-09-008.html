Lee & Associates Arranges Sale of 113,904 SF Industrial Building in Lewisville, Texas

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Wed, 08 Jul 2026 15:18:28 +0000

SUMMARY

Lee & Associates has facilitated the sale of a 113,904-square-foot industrial building in Lewisville, Texas. Bleecker Partners acquired the property, which is fully occupied by Andes Coil Processes. The seller's identity was not disclosed.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Address: 202 S. Railroad St., Lewisville, TX

Size: 113,904 SF

Year Built: 1983

Notable Features: Features 35-foot clear heights.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER BROKER: Lee & Associates — Tomas Wilson; Alex Wilson

SELLER BROKER: NAI Robert Lynn — Todd Hubbard
