Chicago’s $4M-plus home market cools from 2025’s record pace, but demand stays hot

The Real Deal — Chicago — Thu, 09 Jul 2026 20:30:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Residential Sales Report

Chicago's luxury residential market, while not matching the record-setting pace of 2025, continues to demonstrate strong demand and high prices in 2026. The market has recorded 62 sales of $4 million or more by mid-year, placing it as the third-highest on record. A persistent lack of inventory is driving prices up, with some homes going under contract within 48 hours and the average time on market significantly lower than the national average.

DATA POINTS

Number of $4M+ home sales (mid-2026): 62

Number of $4M+ home sales (mid-2025): 76

Number of $4M+ home sales (mid-2022): 70

Highest home sale price (mid-2026): $10.5 million

Highest home sale price (mid-2025): $12 million

Average days on market (Chicagoland): 27 days

Average days on market (nationwide): 52 days
