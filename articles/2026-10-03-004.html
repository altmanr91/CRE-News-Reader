Sony in Talks to Anchor Tishman Speyer’s 99 Hudson Boulevard

Commercial Observer — Fri, 02 Oct 2026 17:53:33 +0000

SUMMARY

Development

Sony is reportedly in negotiations to become the anchor tenant at Tishman Speyer's proposed 99 Hudson Boulevard in Hudson Yards, New York City. The electronics giant is seeking a larger, more collaborative workspace than its current 568,000-square-foot headquarters at 11 Madison Avenue. The planned 48-story, 1.256 million-square-foot office tower, designed by Henning Larsen, is expected to be an all-electric LEED Gold building, with construction slated to begin in December and occupancy by 2030. Tishman Speyer is seeking $92 million in property and mortgage tax exemptions from the city's Industrial Development Agency to support the project.

TENANTS AT PROPERTY

Sony

FINANCING

The project is supported by a $1.477 billion loan and $1.2 billion in equity.
