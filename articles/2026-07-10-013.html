AlffCo signs 20,936-square-foot lease at Catalyst Omaha

LEASE

REJournals — Thu, 09 Jul 2026 16:32:10 +0000

SUMMARY

AlffCo, a national facility services provider, has signed a 64-month lease for 20,396 square feet at Catalyst Omaha, where it will establish its new headquarters. The company, which previously operated from two separate locations, will consolidate all departments onto a single floor in a custom-built space designed to enhance collaboration and efficiency. The landlord noted that AlffCo's decision to locate at Catalyst reinforces the property's appeal to growing and innovative companies.
