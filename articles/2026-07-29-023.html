Rapper Rick Ross Joins 670-Unit Miami Gardens Condo Project

DEVELOPMENT

Bisnow News Feed — Tue, 28 Jul 2026 15:42:13 -0400

SUMMARY

Rapper Rick Ross has joined the development team for EnSima, an eight-story, 670-unit condominium project with 165,000 square feet of retail in Miami Gardens. Ross is partnering with EnSima Miami I, a company led by Shalom Arik Maimon, Ali Yesil, David Shapiro, and Lior Suchard. The project, located near Hard Rock Stadium, is expected to open its sales gallery in September, with units starting at approximately $593 per square foot.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: condo

Property Name: EnSima

Size: 165,000 SF

Units: 670

Notable Features: Located about five minutes away from Hard Rock Stadium.

Project Notes: Units are starting at approximately $593 per square foot, with a 650 SF one-bedroom unit starting at $450,000, a 900 SF two-bedroom unit at $580,000, and a 1,200 SF three-bedroom unit at $770,000.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

DEVELOPER/SPONSOR: EnSima Miami I — Shalom Arik Maimon; Ali Yesil; David Shapiro; Lior Suchard; Rick Ross

GENERAL CONTRACTOR: TerraForm Development
