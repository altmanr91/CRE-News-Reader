Lennar, TPG pick up entitled resi development site in Saratoga for $110M

ACQUISITION

The Real Deal — San Francisco — Mon, 06 Jul 2026 21:16:41 +0000

SUMMARY

Lennar Corporation and TPG have acquired a 11.6-acre entitled residential development site in Saratoga, California, for $109.7 million from Vineyard One Holdings, an LLC associated with City Connect Companies. The site, known as Vineyard One, is approved for 52 single-family homes and 12 affordable accessory dwelling units, with construction expected to commence shortly. This transaction represents one of the final large undeveloped parcels in Saratoga, a market with a median single-family home price of $4.1 million.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

The median sale price for a single-family home in Saratoga is $4.1 million. The City of Saratoga is mandated to plan for at least 1,700 new residential units by 2031.
