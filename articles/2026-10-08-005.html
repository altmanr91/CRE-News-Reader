Interactive Floor Plans Platform Planpoint Exits Stealth

Commercial Observer — Thu, 08 Oct 2026 13:00:17 +0000

SUMMARY

Non-CRE / Business News

Planpoint, a Montreal-based real estate marketing platform, has officially launched from stealth mode, offering interactive floor plans and site maps for property sales and rentals. The software-as-a-service platform allows prospective buyers and renters to explore available units, view 3D tours, and access real-time property information. Developers and property teams are already utilizing the platform to streamline their marketing efforts and keep property details current across various presentations.

DATA POINTS

Total unit views on platform: 24.7 million

Total floor plan downloads on platform: 15.3 million
