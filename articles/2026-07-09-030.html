Sellers from Lake Forest are now buyers of $9M Lake Geneva home

ACQUISITION

The Real Deal — Chicago — Wed, 08 Jul 2026 21:09:46 +0000

SUMMARY

William and Lois McEssy, who previously sold a Lake Geneva property for $20 million, have acquired another home in the area for $8.8 million. The transaction for the N1539 Lakeside Lane property was an off-market deal, a common occurrence in the highly competitive Lake Geneva luxury real estate market, which is a popular destination for Chicago's elite.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: single family

Size: 6,200 SF

Sale Price: $8.8M

$/SF: $1,419/SF (calculated)

Notable Features: The property does not have direct lake access.

Project Notes: The deal was made off-market.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: Individual — William McEssy; Lois McEssy

BUYER BROKER: Lake Geneva Area Realty — Sal Dimicelli

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Bidding wars are common for Lake Geneva property, with about 70 percent of sales conducted off-market and most deals made with cash.
