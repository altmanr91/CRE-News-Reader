CBRE closes $11 million refinance for office property in Edina

REFINANCE

REJournals — Tue, 25 Aug 2026 17:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

CBRE Capital Markets secured an $11 million refinancing loan for Grandview Square, a fully leased Class-A office property in Edina, Minnesota. The five-year, fixed-rate, interest-only loan was arranged on behalf of Capital Partners. The property's recent renovation and strong tenant occupancy underscore its appeal in a well-connected, amenity-rich district.

DATA POINTS

Size: 98,561 SF

Notable Features: Class-A office property located in the walkable Grandview district, offering amenities such as heated underground parking, a fitness center, golf simulator, and tenant lounge. Renovated in 2025, it provides convenient access to downtown Minneapolis and the airport.
