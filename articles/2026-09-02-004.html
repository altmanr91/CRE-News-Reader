Nuveen Green Capital Closes $173M C-PACE Loan on Miami-Area Condos Project

LOAN

Commercial Observer — Tue, 01 Sep 2026 19:00:38 +0000

SUMMARY

Nuveen Green Capital has provided a $172.5 million C-PACE loan to Shoma Group for the development of its Shoma Bay mixed-use condominium project in North Bay Village, FL. This transaction is significant as Nuveen Green Capital is the sole debt provider in the capital stack, making it one of Florida's largest C-PACE-financed projects. The development will feature 333 condo units and retail space, including a large supermarket, and is expected to contribute to the ongoing evolution of North Bay Village as a dynamic waterfront community.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: mixed-use

Property Name: Shoma Bay

Address: 1850 John F. Kennedy Causeway, North Bay Village, FL

Units: 333

Loan Amount: $172.5M

Loan Per Unit: $518,018/unit (calculated)

Notable Features: Waterfront project with a rooftop pool deck, wellness spa, two-story fitness center, padel court, and coworking spaces.

Project Notes: The C-PACE loan is the sole debt provider in the capital stack, supplemented by condo sales deposits and equity.
