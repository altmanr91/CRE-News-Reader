Subtext Completes 769-Bed Student Housing Development Near Arizona State University

CONSTRUCTION

REBusinessOnline — Thu, 17 Sep 2026 13:14:09 +0000

SUMMARY

Subtext, in partnership with FrontRange Capital Partners and TPG Real Estate Partners, has completed VERVE Tempe, a 769-bed student housing development near Arizona State University. The 15-story community offers 240 units and a wide array of amenities designed for student living, including a rooftop pool, fitness center, and study lounges. Kennedy Wilson provided the financing for the project.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: student housing

Property Name: VERVE Tempe

Address: 1047 S. Terrace Road, Tempe, AZ

Size: 479,388 SF

Units: 240

Beds: 769

Notable Features: 15-story community with a rooftop pool, clubroom, two-story fitness center, golf simulator, and 1,800 square feet of ground-floor retail space.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

DEVELOPER/SPONSOR: FrontRange Capital Partners

DEVELOPER/SPONSOR: TPG Real Estate Partners

ARCHITECT: ESG Architecture & Design
