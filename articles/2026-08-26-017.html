Marcus & Millichap Brokers Sale of 490-Unit Multifamily in St. Louis

SALE

Connect CRE — Tue, 25 Aug 2026 21:54:08 +0000

SUMMARY

Marcus & Millichap has brokered the sale of Trinity Park Apartments, a 490-unit multifamily property in North St. Louis, through an online live bid auction. The property was marketed as a value-add opportunity due to its below-market occupancy, offering the new owner significant lease-up potential. The transaction involved investment specialists from Marcus & Millichap's Chicago Oak Brook, Indianapolis, and St. Louis offices, who represented both the seller and procured the buyer.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Trinity Park Apartments

Address: 11065 Dunklin Drive, St. Louis, MO

Units: 490

Site Area: 24.08 acres

Notable Features: Comprises 34 residential buildings with a mix of one-bedroom and two-bedroom residences.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

SELLER BROKER: Marcus & Millichap — Ryan D. Engle, Senior Managing Director Investments; Andrean Angelov, Investment Specialist; Aaron Kuroiwa, Investment Specialist; Adam Sklaver; Philip Kates
