Chicago’s Prudential Plaza to become permanent home of to Siskel & Ebert statue

REJournals — Tue, 27 Oct 2026 14:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Development

Wanxiang America Real Estate Group, owner of Chicago's Prudential Plaza, announced the planned installation of a life-size bronze sculpture honoring film critics Gene Siskel and Roger Ebert. The six-foot-five interactive statue, titled "Siskel & Ebert: Two Thumbs Up," will be created by acclaimed Illinois sculptor Preston Jackson and is expected to be installed in early 2028. The project is spearheaded by Chicago film producer Pam Kasper and funded through corporate donations, public fundraising, and a Hollywood memorabilia auction.
