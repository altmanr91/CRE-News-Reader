Draper and Kramer closes $93.5 million refinance loan for 28-story multifamily tower in Chicago’s Fulton Market neighborhood

REFINANCE

REJournals — Mon, 14 Sep 2026 14:02:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Draper and Kramer's Commercial Finance Group secured a $93.5 million refinance loan for The Elizabeth, a 28-story multifamily tower in Chicago's Fulton Market. The property, completed in 2024, features 350 rental units and 10,000 square feet of retail. This transaction is part of several deals the firm has closed in the Fulton Market area this year, reflecting continued activity in the Class A multifamily sector.
