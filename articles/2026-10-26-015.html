Partners Development breaks ground on 234,600-square-foot industrial park in Seguin

CONSTRUCTION

REJournals — Mon, 26 Oct 2026 09:32:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Partners Development has broken ground on Seguin Commerce Park, an eight-building, 234,600-square-foot industrial development in Seguin, TX. The first phase, consisting of two 40,000-square-foot buildings, is slated for delivery in May 2027. The project is being marketed for sale or lease, capitalizing on Seguin's robust population growth and strong manufacturing employment base.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Property Name: Seguin Commerce Park

Size: 234,600 SF

Completion: May 2027

Phase I: Two 40,000-sq.-ft. buildings at 1781 and 1775 N Heideke Street

Site Area: 20 acres

Notable Features: Buildings range from 17,000 to 40,000 sq. ft. with 25-foot clear heights, crane-ready capability for some buildings, grade-level doors with dock-high well capability, 800-amp power, ESFR-ready sprinkler systems, and dedicated secured yards. Each building includes a 1,600-sq.-ft. finished office or build-to-suit option.

Project Notes: The 20-acre site was previously acquired by Partners Development in February 2025.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

DEVELOPER/SPONSOR: Partners Development

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Seguin's population grew 28.4% between 2020 and 2024, with nearly 18% of its workforce employed in manufacturing. Other industrial investments in the city include Ranch Hand's planned 400,000-sq.-ft. manufacturing facility and KTH Parts Industries' planned $127 million automotive manufacturing facility.
