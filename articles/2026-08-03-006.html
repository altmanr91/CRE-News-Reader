Texas Icon Rob Killen: Guiding complex development toward civic alignment

REJournals — Mon, 03 Aug 2026 10:02:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Feature / Profile

This article profiles Rob Killen, a founding partner at Killen, Griffin & Farrimond PLLC, highlighting his more than two decades of work in land use, zoning, economic development, and infrastructure in San Antonio. Killen specializes in guiding complex development projects through the often-conflicting priorities of property owners, cities, and residents, aiming for outcomes that benefit both private investment and the public good. His approach emphasizes problem-solving, building relationships, and translating project benefits to diverse stakeholders to secure necessary entitlements and approvals. Killen's extensive civic leadership further informs his professional work, enabling him to foster alignment and achieve durable project successes.
