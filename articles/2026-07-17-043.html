Industrial Rebound Fuels New Wave Of Speculative Projects

Bisnow News Feed — Thu, 16 Jul 2026 14:12:18 -0400

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

The U.S. industrial market is experiencing a resurgence in speculative development, driven by tightening vacancy rates, increased logistics demand, and new federal incentives for domestic manufacturing. Major players like Prologis are significantly expanding their development pipelines, with the company breaking ground on more new projects in the first half of the year than in all of 2025. This renewed confidence is fueled by strong leasing activity, particularly for large blocks of space, and improving market fundamentals across numerous markets, despite ongoing economic and geopolitical uncertainties. Developers are increasingly comfortable initiating new projects, including later phases of previously paused pandemic-era developments, as tenant demand continues to absorb available space.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

U.S. industrial market leasing dynamics are generally tilting back toward landlords, with large swaths of available space being steadily leased. Occupiers are inking deals despite the volatile economic and geopolitical landscape, including the U.S. war with Iran blocking the Strait of Hormuz. The 'One Big Beautiful Bill Act' passed in July 2025, providing tax and policy incentives to bring manufacturing back to the U.S., has injected certainty into developers' calculus and broadened the ecosystem of industrial occupiers.
