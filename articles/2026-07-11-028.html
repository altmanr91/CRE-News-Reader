Microsoft exec pays $20M for Hunts Point mansion amid company layoffs

SALE

The Real Deal — Seattle — Fri, 10 Jul 2026 17:05:34 +0000

SUMMARY

Celebrity Residential / Non-CRE

Microsoft's chief marketing officer, Takeshi Numoto, and his wife, Mari, purchased a 7,200-square-foot lakefront residence in Hunts Point, Washington, for $19.6 million. The property was sold by Z&M Mansion LLC for less than its appraised value and original asking price. This transaction represents one of the largest residential purchases in Washington state this year.
