Sunday Summary: A Clear September Day

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

This article reflects on the 25th anniversary of 9/11, highlighting the resilience and redevelopment of the World Trade Center site and Lower Manhattan. It details Silverstein Properties' recent groundbreaking on 2 World Trade Center, a 2 million-square-foot office tower anchored by American Express, a significant milestone for the area. The piece also notes the broader transformation of Lower Manhattan into a more diverse mixed-use neighborhood with increased residential density, and observes a surprising trend of bidding wars for office space, even in Class B buildings, driven by employers' focus on amenities for talent recruitment and retention. Several notable office property sales and listings in Manhattan are also mentioned, indicating a dynamic market.
