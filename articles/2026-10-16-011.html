Adolfson & Peterson completes renovation and 106-bed expansion at Cross Creek Hospital in Austin

CONSTRUCTION

REJournals — Fri, 16 Oct 2026 13:39:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Adolfson & Peterson Construction has completed a significant renovation and expansion project at Cross Creek Hospital in Austin, Texas. The project added 106 beds, bringing the hospital's total capacity to 196 licensed beds, and included a 96,580-square-foot two-story building addition. This expansion, undertaken by a joint venture between Ascension Seton and Acadia Healthcare, aims to address the growing demand for behavioral health services in the region.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: healthcare

Property Name: Cross Creek Hospital

Address: 8402 Cross Park Dr, Austin, TX*

Size: 96,580 SF

Beds: 106

Notable Features: Expansion included therapy and exam rooms, administration rooms, noisy and quiet areas, and an outdoor patient courtyard. Renovations covered the dining hall, kitchen, administrative space, and outpatient area offices.

Project Notes: The project was a phased construction, increasing the hospital's total licensed bed count to 196. New parking areas and a water quality system upgrade were also part of the project.
