Aggregate Buys $58M Northern Virginia Portfolio

ACQUISITION

Commercial Observer — Fri, 28 Aug 2026 19:10:20 +0000

SUMMARY

Aggregate Real Estate Investors has acquired an 11-building industrial and retail portfolio in Northern Virginia from Clarke-Hook Corporation for $58 million. The 320,000-square-foot portfolio, which was 94% leased to over 90 tenants at the time of sale, includes properties in Fairfax and Loudoun counties. Aggregate plans aesthetic upgrades and new management initiatives to create additional value, aligning with their strategy to target value-add deals in the Mid-Atlantic region.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial / retail

Size: 320,000 SF

Sale Price: $58.0M

Occupancy at Sale: 94.0%

Notable Features: Portfolio includes West Fairfax Commerce Center, University Commerce Center, Dulles Trade Center, and McLean Commerce Center.

Project Notes: The portfolio was 94% leased to more than 90 tenants at the time of the transaction.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE
