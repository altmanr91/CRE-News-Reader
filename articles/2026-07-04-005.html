Life Time, Midas Hospitality to bring wellness-centered residential project to Richmond Heights

DEVELOPMENT

REJournals — Fri, 03 Jul 2026 16:02:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Life Time and Midas Hospitality are partnering to develop Life Time Living, a seven-story mixed-use project in Richmond Heights, MO. This wellness-centered luxury residential concept will feature 263 apartment residences integrated with a 110,000-square-foot Life Time athletic country club and 6,000 square feet of retail space. The development aims to create a fully integrated lifestyle experience, enhancing the area's walkability and urban appeal.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: mixed-use

Property Name: Life Time Living

Size: 116,000 SF

Units: 263

Notable Features: Features a 30,000-square-foot rooftop pool and outdoor amenity deck, state-of-the-art fitness and recovery spaces, and dedicated group fitness studios.

Project Notes: Planned as part of the broader Boulevard South mixed-use development along Brentwood Boulevard.
