AI Infrastructure Firm Treeswift Signs 7K-SF Lease at 256 West 38th Street

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Wed, 29 Jul 2026 19:14:34 +0000

SUMMARY

Treeswift, an AI infrastructure technology company, has signed a four-year lease for 7,487 square feet on the entire 11th floor at 256 West 38th Street in Midtown Manhattan. This relocation represents an expansion of the company's New York City operations. The landlord's broker noted that AI companies are actively driving office leasing demand in Manhattan, seeking efficient and well-connected headquarters that support collaboration and attract talent, a trend reflected in Treeswift's move.
