Flipping the Script on “Safe” Haven Bonds

Connect CRE — Wed, 15 Jul 2026 21:09:31 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

This market commentary argues that the war in Iran has fundamentally altered traditional fixed-income safe havens, shifting investor preference from long-duration Treasuries and investment-grade bonds to floating-rate, senior-secured credit. The author highlights the outperformance of senior loan ETFs, which offer floating coupons, minimal price sensitivity to interest rate changes, and senior positions in the capital structure, making them attractive in a 'higher-for-longer' interest rate environment. While acknowledging risks such as income sensitivity to rate cuts, borrower fragility, and liquidity concerns, the piece concludes that continued geopolitical instability and inflation impulses will likely sustain demand for floating-rate credit, suggesting a tactical overweight to these strategies for investors tolerant of credit and liquidity risk.
