Stockdale Found A Gap In The Debt Market. Then It Seized An Opportunity

Bisnow Latest News Feed — Fri, 02 Oct 2026 18:35:09 -0400

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

Stockdale, a Los Angeles-based firm, has launched a new credit strategy to capitalize on a perceived gap in the debt market. The strategy will focus on originating loans ranging from $15 million to $75 million, specifically targeting distressed assets and asset classes. This move allows Stockdale to provide financing solutions in a segment where traditional lenders may be more hesitant.
