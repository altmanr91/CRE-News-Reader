JLL Brokers $147M Sale of Seniors Housing Portfolio in Metro Atlanta

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Thu, 06 Aug 2026 14:26:51 +0000

SUMMARY

JLL's National Seniors Housing Capital Markets team has brokered the sale of a five-property seniors housing portfolio located in various submarkets north of Atlanta. The portfolio, comprising 611 units and 701 beds across assisted living and memory care facilities, was acquired by an institutional investor in an all-cash transaction. The seller was a joint venture between Centric Development and Sage Equities, which developed the properties between 2018 and 2022. Claiborne Senior Living has been retained by the new owner to continue managing the communities.

DATA POINTS
