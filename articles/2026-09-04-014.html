PREMIER Design + Build Group adds general superintendent in Columbus

REJournals — Thu, 03 Sep 2026 16:06:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Promotion

David Lewis has been appointed General Superintendent at PREMIER Design + Build Group, operating out of the firm's Columbus, Ohio, office. In this role, Lewis will oversee field operations for the company's projects in Ohio, focusing on project execution, safety, quality, and team development. He brings over 40 years of construction experience, including three decades in field management, with a track record of successfully delivering large-scale industrial and commercial projects.
