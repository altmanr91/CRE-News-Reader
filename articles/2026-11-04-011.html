Mid-America Real Estate negotiates sale of 50,100-square-foot retail center in Flint

SALE

REJournals — Wed, 04 Nov 2026 16:17:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Mid-America Real Estate-Michigan, Inc. facilitated the sale of Buick City Center, a 50,100-square-foot retail center in Flint, Michigan. The property, which is 78% leased and features a strong tenant mix, is strategically located along Flint's main retail corridor near the I-75 interchange. Mid-America Real Estate represented both the buyer and the seller in this transaction.

DATA POINTS

Address: 3620–3640 Miller Road, Flint, MI

Notable Features: Located along Flint's primary retail corridor, just west of the I-75 interchange.
