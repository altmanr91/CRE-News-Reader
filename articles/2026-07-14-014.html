Cushman & Wakefield: Chicago-area industrial leasing reaches highest mid-year total since 2022

REJournals — Tue, 14 Jul 2026 09:39:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Industrial Report

The Chicago industrial market demonstrated strong resilience in mid-2026, with new leasing activity reaching its highest mid-year total since 2022, totaling 21.8 million square feet. The overall vacancy rate remained stable at 4.8% for the third consecutive quarter, indicating a healthy balance between supply and demand. Demand was particularly robust in the big-box segment, reinforcing Chicago's status as a premier logistics hub. Rental rates continued to grow, with the average net asking rent reaching $7.55 per square foot, reflecting sustained occupier commitment despite economic uncertainties.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Chicago's industrial vacancy rate held steady at 4.8% for the third consecutive quarter. The market recorded 21.8 million square feet of new leasing activity through mid-year 2026, an 11.1% increase year-over-year. The overall average net asking rent reached $7.55 per square foot, a nearly 1% increase year-over-year and 2.8% quarter-over-quarter. 6 million square feet of new industrial space was completed, with 13.9 million square feet still under construction. The I-80 and I-55 Corridor submarkets led in new leasing activity with 5.1 msf and 3.7 msf respectively.
