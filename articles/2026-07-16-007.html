Versal Negotiates Sale of 464-Unit Self-Storage Facility in Lubbock, Texas

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Wed, 15 Jul 2026 15:02:38 +0000

SUMMARY

Versal, a self-storage brokerage firm, has facilitated the sale of All American Storage, a 464-unit self-storage facility in Lubbock, Texas. The property, encompassing 54,453 net rentable square feet, was sold by a Waco-based seller to a South Florida-based buyer. The sales price was not disclosed.
