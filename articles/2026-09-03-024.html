Houston Industrial Supply Boom Tempered By Lack Of Equity

Bisnow Latest News Feed — Wed, 02 Sep 2026 19:18:06 -0400

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

Houston's industrial market is experiencing a significant boom in new supply, but this growth is being tempered by a scarcity of equity financing. Despite the challenges in securing capital, developer demand for new projects remains robust, indicating underlying strength in the market. The ongoing development activity suggests that while financing is a hurdle, it has not completely stalled the expansion of industrial real estate in the region.
