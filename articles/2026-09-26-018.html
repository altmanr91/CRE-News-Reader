Hudson Pacific Sells Nearly Vacant San Francisco Office Complex for $65M

SALE

Connect CRE — Fri, 25 Sep 2026 20:49:18 +0000

SUMMARY

Hudson Pacific Properties has sold a two-building office and former retail complex in downtown San Francisco for $65.5 million. The complex, totaling 284,000 square feet, was largely vacant at the time of sale, with one building 36% occupied and the other entirely vacant. Hudson Pacific had secured entitlements to convert a portion of the former retail building to office use, and the sale allows the company to reduce capital and re-leasing risk while enhancing financial flexibility.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Property Name: 875 and 899 Howard

Address: 875 Howard Street, San Francisco, CA

Size: 284,000 SF

Sale Price: $65.5M

Project Notes: 875 Howard was 36% occupied with a major lease expiring in 2027. 899 Howard was fully vacant, and Hudson Pacific secured entitlements to convert two floors to office use prior to sale.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

SELLER: Hudson Pacific Properties, Inc. — Victor Coleman, Chairman and CEO
