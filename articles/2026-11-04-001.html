Community Benefits Agreements Can Build Trust in Data Center Development

Commercial Observer — Wed, 04 Nov 2026 15:26:16 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

This article argues that Community Benefits Agreements (CBAs) are an increasingly vital tool for data center developers to navigate growing community scrutiny and regulatory challenges. The authors contend that traditional outreach is no longer sufficient, as evidenced by recent governmental actions in Texas, New York, and Pennsylvania to assess or regulate data center impacts. CBAs, as voluntary agreements between developers and communities, can foster trust and transparency by outlining specific commitments such as workforce development, infrastructure improvements, or environmental investments. By demonstrating a commitment to local priorities and engaging proactively, developers can mitigate opposition, streamline the entitlement process, and ensure projects are completed on time and within budget, ultimately addressing the exponential demand for data centers.
