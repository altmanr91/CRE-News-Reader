Gilroy multifamily project scores record $183M construction loan

LOAN

The Real Deal — San Francisco — Wed, 08 Jul 2026 17:52:01 +0000

SUMMARY

Dwight Mortgage Trust has provided a record-setting $183 million construction loan for a 530-unit luxury multifamily community in Gilroy, CA. The project, developed by Ten South Construction, will feature four mid-rise apartment buildings and 33 townhome buildings, with 243 units designated as income-restricted affordable housing. This development aims to address the increasing housing demand in the Silicon Valley area, driven by the artificial intelligence boom.

DATA POINTS

Notable Features: 243 units will be income-restricted affordable housing for households earning between 60% and 80% of area median income.

Project Notes: The project will consist of four mid-rise apartment buildings and 33 townhome buildings, along with a clubhouse and leasing office.
