Marcus & Millichap: Expect a more balanced Milwaukee multifamily market in 2026

REJournals — Thu, 16 Jul 2026 10:04:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Multifamily Report

The Milwaukee multifamily market is poised for a more balanced year in 2026, characterized by fewer new apartment deliveries, sustained renter demand, and improving occupancy rates, which are expected to bolster rent growth. Despite a sluggish employment picture, the market's resilience is attributed to residents renting for longer durations due to rising home prices and limited housing inventory. This trend, coupled with a significant slowdown in new construction, is contributing to healthy apartment fundamentals, with vacancy projected to decline and effective rents anticipated to rise.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Milwaukee's multifamily market is seeing a significant slowdown in new apartment construction, with approximately 1,050 new units expected in 2026, representing only 0.6% inventory growth. This contrasts with over a decade of elevated development that expanded inventory by roughly 17%. Several key submarkets, including Downtown Milwaukee, Brown Deer-Whitefish Bay, and Washington and Ozaukee counties, are projected to have no significant apartment completions this year. Waukesha County is expected to see an 80% drop in deliveries. Racine is an exception, with over 400 units expected, its largest annual delivery total on record. Multifamily transaction volume in the 12 months ending March climbed nearly 35%, the strongest annual increase since 2022, with buyers targeting older Class B and Class C apartment communities for approximately $100,000 per unit in areas like Timmerman Airport-Washington Park, Miller Valley-Marquette, and the Lower East Side.
