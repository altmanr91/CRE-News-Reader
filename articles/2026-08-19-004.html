Betting on South Beach Luxury Shopping, Vivienne Westwood Plans to Open Store

DEVELOPMENT

Commercial Observer — Tue, 18 Aug 2026 17:55:31 +0000

SUMMARY

Luxury fashion brand Vivienne Westwood is moving forward with plans to open a boutique in Miami Beach's South Beach, nearly two years after acquiring the property at 736 Collins Avenue. The brand submitted an application to build out the 1921-era building, which will feature a ground-floor retail space, executive offices on the second floor, and a bridal showroom with a rooftop deck on the top floor. The project aims to blend the building's Art Deco and Mediterranean Revival heritage with contemporary design elements, signaling a bet on the resurgence of high-end retail in the South Beach area.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: retail

Address: 736 Collins Avenue, Miami Beach, FL

Size: 4,356 SF

Sale Price: $6.0M

Year Built: 1921

Notable Features: The project honors the building's 1921 Art Deco and Mediterranean Revival heritage while introducing contemporary elements like Florida limestone cladding, polished brass storefront, and a landmark pink neon signature.

Project Notes: The 14,692-square-foot building was previously acquired by Vivienne Westwood in 2024 for $6 million.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

OWNER: Vivienne Westwood

PLANNER: National Permit Group — Daniel Gabay
