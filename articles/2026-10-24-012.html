Associated Bank completes $16.5 million loan for 34-unit apartment property in Libertyville

LOAN

REJournals — Fri, 23 Oct 2026 14:17:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Associated Bank has provided a $16.5 million loan for the acquisition of Eleven5 Lake Apartments, a mixed-use property in Libertyville, Illinois. The property, which sold for $22 million, features 34 apartment units and 25,147 square feet of ground-floor commercial space. The apartments were 97% occupied at the time of acquisition, while the commercial space was fully leased to five tenants.
