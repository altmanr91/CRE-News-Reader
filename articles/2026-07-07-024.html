$32M Surf Club Four Seasons condo under contract to flip for big profit

SALE

The Real Deal — Miami — Mon, 06 Jul 2026 19:45:00 +0000

SUMMARY

A luxury condo at the Four Seasons Residences at the Surf Club in Surfside, FL, is under contract for an asking price of $31.5 million. The 4,640-square-foot unit, which was purchased last summer for $18.4 million, is poised for a significant profit flip. This transaction is part of a broader trend of luxury home contracts in Miami-Dade County, with nine such deals totaling $88.9 million in asking dollar volume reported last week.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Nine luxury home contracts were signed in Miami-Dade County between June 29 and July 5, totaling $88.9 million in asking dollar volume. The average asking price for condos was $21.7 million, at an average of $4,400 per square foot, and they spent an average of 139 days on the market. Single-family homes had an average asking price of $6.5 million and spent 87 days on the market. In New York, 15 luxury homes went into contract with a combined asking price of $152.8 million, spending an average of 498 days on the market.
