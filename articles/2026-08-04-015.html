Paladin Expands SoCal Workforce Portfolio with Bellflower Acquisition

ACQUISITION

Connect CRE — Mon, 03 Aug 2026 21:52:47 +0000

SUMMARY

Paladin Realty Partners has acquired Brownstone Apartments, a 17-unit multifamily community in Bellflower, CA, expanding its Southern California workforce housing portfolio. The firm plans to implement a value-add strategy to modernize the 1967-built property, aiming to enhance resident quality of life while preserving affordable workforce housing in a desirable infill location. California Bank & Trust provided the debt financing for the acquisition.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Brownstone Apartments

Address: 9559 Flower St, Bellflower, CA*

Units: 17

Year Built: 1967

Notable Features: Comprises nine one-bedroom units, seven two-bedroom units, and one studio unit.

Project Notes: Acquired as part of a value-add strategy to modernize the property and preserve affordable workforce housing.
