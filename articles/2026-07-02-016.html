States defeat Trump administration in HUD homelessness funds case

The Real Deal — National — Wed, 01 Jul 2026 19:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

CRE Legal / Regulatory

A U.S. District Judge ruled that the Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD) violated federal law by making changes to its Continuum of Care program, which provides billions in funding to states and municipalities for homelessness initiatives. The court found that HUD's policy changes, which would have shifted funding from permanent housing to temporary housing with work requirements and imposed a cap on permanent housing support, demonstrated "unreasoned decision making" and failed to consider the harm caused. This ruling allows a coalition of 20 states, led by New York, to continue receiving funds under the program's previous structure, which largely supported permanent housing.
