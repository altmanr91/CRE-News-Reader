Adolfson & Peterson Completes Renovation of Residence Hall at University of Texas San Antonio

CONSTRUCTION

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 07 Aug 2026 15:28:59 +0000

SUMMARY

Adolfson & Peterson Construction has completed the renovation of Chisholm Hall, a 120,860-square-foot residence hall on the University of Texas at San Antonio campus. The project involved modernizing building systems, upgrading bathroom vanity areas, and installing new acoustical ceilings, lighting, mechanical unit ventilators, and hallway ductwork across 267 rooms. The hall offers double and triple-occupancy rooms.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: student housing

Property Name: Chisholm Hall

Size: 120,860 SF

Units: 267

Notable Features: Features two fully furnished layout options, including double- and triple-occupancy rooms.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

GENERAL CONTRACTOR: Adolfson & Peterson Construction

ARCHITECT: Chesney Morales Architects

OWNER: University of Texas at San Antonio
