3650 Capital Furnishes Construction Loan for Yonkers Self-Storage

LOAN

Connect CRE — Mon, 10 Aug 2026 21:16:35 +0000

SUMMARY

3650 Capital has provided a $24 million senior construction loan for the completion of a 949-unit self-storage development in Yonkers, NY. The project, sponsored by Cayre Equities and KCT Inc., will be branded and operated by CubeSmart. This financing structure allowed the sponsors to emerge from a court-supervised reorganization, resolve outstanding claims, and accelerate project completion in a supply-constrained market.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: self-storage

Address: 1060 Nepperhan Avenue, Yonkers, NY

Units: 949

Loan Amount: $24.0M

Loan Per Unit: $25,290/unit (calculated)

Notable Features: Will be branded and operated by CubeSmart.

Project Notes: Financing provided as exit capital concurrent with the borrower's emergence from a court-supervised reorganization. Loan features a 36-month initial term with one six-month extension option.
