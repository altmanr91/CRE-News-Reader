Daniel Management Group awarded new apartment assignment in Mundelein

REJournals — Tue, 29 Sep 2026 14:06:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Promotion

Daniel Management Group (DMG) has been awarded the management assignment for Lakeside Village Apartments in Mundelein, Illinois, expanding its partnership with Hawthorne Management Services. The 144-unit multifamily community features newly renovated units and a range of amenities, situated near Diamond Lake, Hawthorne Mall, and a METRA station. DMG will oversee a rebrand, clubhouse upgrades, outdoor area improvements, ongoing unit renovations, and a new website for the property.
