Venture X Coworking Location to Open in Pompano Beach

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Tue, 11 Aug 2026 21:40:08 +0000

SUMMARY

Priddy Spaces has signed a 10-year lease for 20,000 square feet to open a new Venture X coworking location in Pompano Beach, FL. The space, which will occupy three floors of a 44,102-square-foot office building, is part of Grover Corlew's Mayla Pompano mixed-use development. This expansion reflects the significant transformation and growth in Pompano Beach, driven by new residential development, improved infrastructure, and a vibrant local economy, making it an attractive location for businesses and talent.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Property Name: Mayla Pompano

Address: 2335 East Atlantic Boulevard, Pompano Beach, FL

Size: 20,000 SF

Year Built: 2020

Notable Features: The coworking outpost will feature 85 private office suites and four meeting rooms, spanning the building's second, third, and fourth floors. The office building is part of a larger mixed-use development that includes a 355-unit apartment building and two other office properties.

Project Notes: The office building is within Grover Corlew’s Mayla Pompano mixed-use development.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE
