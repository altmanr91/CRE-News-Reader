Partnership Unveils Plans for Redevelopment of Former Brooklyn Macy’s Building

DEVELOPMENT

Connect CRE — Wed, 08 Jul 2026 21:36:35 +0000

SUMMARY

A partnership comprising United American Land, The Jackson Group, and Dreamscape Retail & Entertainment has announced plans for BKX, a significant redevelopment of the former Macy's building at 422 Fulton St. in Brooklyn. This ambitious project aims to transform the site into one of New York City's largest retail destinations, offering approximately 440,000 square feet across five levels. The development is designed to accommodate flagship retail, immersive entertainment, hospitality, and experiential concepts, moving beyond traditional retail to create a comprehensive destination for modern consumer engagement.
