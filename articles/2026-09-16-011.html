Newmark closes sale of 115,512-square-foot shopping center in Oak Hill

SALE

REJournals — Wed, 16 Sep 2026 14:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Newmark has successfully brokered the sale of Oak Hill Plaza, a 115,512-square-foot neighborhood shopping center in Austin, Texas. Partners Real Estate sold the asset to Trademark Property. The plaza, which is 92.5% leased to a diverse mix of retailers, benefits from its strategic location at a highly trafficked intersection and anchors an affluent retail corridor.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: retail

Property Name: Oak Hill Plaza

Size: 115,512 SF

Occupancy at Sale: 92.5%

Site Area: 11.28 acres

Notable Features: Located at the highly trafficked intersection of U.S. Highway 290 West and Texas State Highway 71, with 668 parking spaces.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

SELLER: Partners Real Estate

BUYER: Trademark Property

SELLER BROKER: Newmark — Conor Lalor, President, Head of Retail Capital Markets; Kyle Minter, Senior Managing Director; James Sharpe V, Director; Cole Frantz, Associate Director
