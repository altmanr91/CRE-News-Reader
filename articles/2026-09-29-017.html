Orion Acquires 88K-SF Boone Retail Center

ACQUISITION

Connect CRE — Tue, 29 Sep 2026 14:49:28 +0000

SUMMARY

Orion Real Estate Group has acquired Watauga Village, an 88,800-square-foot, Food Lion-anchored neighborhood shopping center in Boone, North Carolina, for $26.1 million. This acquisition marks Orion's first shopping center in the state, expanding its portfolio beyond single-tenant, triple-net properties. The fully leased center benefits from its strategic location in Boone's commercial hub, near Appalachian State University and a regional medical center.
