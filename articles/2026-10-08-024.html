Lawmakers Punt On Stripping Approvals From 15 Projects In Data Center Alley

Bisnow Latest News Feed — Wed, 07 Oct 2026 19:33:08 -0400

SUMMARY

CRE Legal / Regulatory

Loudoun County lawmakers have postponed a decision on whether to revoke approvals for 15 data center projects, a move that has sparked significant legal and financial concerns. The proposed rollback in Data Center Alley, a critical hub for data infrastructure, highlights the tension between rapid development and community planning. The delay allows for further consideration of the potential economic impact and legal challenges associated with rescinding previously granted project approvals.
