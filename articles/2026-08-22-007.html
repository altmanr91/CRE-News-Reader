Newmark Secures $45.9M in HUD Financing for Two Multifamily Communities in Grand Junction, Colorado

LOAN

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 21 Aug 2026 15:15:25 +0000

SUMMARY

Newmark has secured $45.9 million in FHA-insured HUD Section 223(f) financing for two multifamily communities in Grand Junction, Colorado. The financing package covers Nexus Apartments, a 122-unit property completed in 2024, and The Eddy Apartments, a 96-unit property completed in 2022. The Eddy Apartments notably offers riverfront trail access and amenities through a shared-use agreement with an adjacent RV park.
