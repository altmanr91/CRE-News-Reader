Locally Based Investors Acquire Encinitas Offices from Family Trust

ACQUISITION

Connect CRE — Fri, 31 Jul 2026 21:28:17 +0000

SUMMARY

Locally based investors DMJ Capital Partners, LLC and StoneSteps Real Estate, LLC have acquired a 40,270-square-foot multi-tenant office property in Encinitas, CA, for $13.5 million. The property, located at 169 Saxony Rd., was previously owned by a family trust for over 24 years. The sale reflects strong investor demand for well-located office assets in the San Diego market, according to the seller's broker.
