Endeavor Purchases 5-Story Charlotte Office Building

ACQUISITION

Connect CRE — Fri, 23 Oct 2026 13:42:30 +0000

SUMMARY

Endeavor Real Estate Group has acquired Carnegie Point, a five-story, 108,000-square-foot office building in Charlotte's SouthPark submarket, for $38.8 million. The Class A property, which was recently renovated by the seller, Redline Property Partners, is approximately 92% leased with a strong tenant mix. This acquisition marks Endeavor's initial entry into the Charlotte market.
