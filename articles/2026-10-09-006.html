CBL Properties Receives $135M Refinancing for Mall in St. Louis

REFINANCE

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 09 Oct 2026 15:37:09 +0000

SUMMARY

CBL Properties has secured a $135 million refinancing for West County Center, a regional mall in St. Louis. The new five-year loan, with a fixed interest rate of 7.4 percent, replaces a maturing $136.4 million mortgage. This refinancing provides CBL with approximately $2 million in net proceeds and is expected to generate an additional $7 million in annual free cash flow by removing the previous loan's cash management sweep. This transaction resolves CBL's final near-term debt maturity.

Notable Features: Anchor tenants include Macy's, Dick's Sporting Goods, and the only Nordstrom store in the state.

Project Notes: CBL acquired its joint venture partner's interest in the mall and two other retail properties in 2024 for $22.5 million.
