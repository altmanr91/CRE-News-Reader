Columbia University Acquires Student Housing in Morningside Heights for $122M

ACQUISITION

Commercial Observer — Fri, 17 Jul 2026 18:54:49 +0000

SUMMARY

Columbia University has acquired a newly redeveloped student housing project in Morningside Heights, Manhattan, for $122 million. The property, located at 99 Claremont Avenue, was sold by a joint venture between Charney Companies and Criterion Real Estate Capital. Originally a smaller dorm, the 92-year-old building was converted into luxury student housing with over 300 units and extensive amenity space, and will serve as the first dedicated residence hall for Columbia's School of General Studies.
