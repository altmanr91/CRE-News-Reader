AV Management Nabs SoHo Mixed-Use for $43M

ACQUISITION

Connect CRE — Wed, 12 Aug 2026 22:07:20 +0000

SUMMARY

AV Management has acquired 73-75 Sullivan St., a newly constructed mixed-use property in Manhattan's SoHo neighborhood, for $43.333 million. The property features 11 residential units and ground-floor commercial space. The sale highlights robust investor demand for high-quality, free-market multifamily assets in prime Manhattan locations, reflecting the enduring strength of the city's investment market.
