Newmark Brokers $27.2M Sale of R&D Facility in El Segundo, California

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Mon, 31 Aug 2026 14:36:28 +0000

SUMMARY

Majestic Realty has acquired a 56,815-square-foot research-and-development (R&D) and service facility in El Segundo, CA, from Hackman Capital Partners for $27.2 million. The property, fully leased to an electric vehicle manufacturer on a triple-net basis, was originally built in 1967 and underwent renovations in 2020. Newmark brokered the sale, highlighting the asset's strategic location near Los Angeles International Airport and its dual function as a customer service center and secure R&D hub.
