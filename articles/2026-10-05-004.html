Affinius Capital Provides $95M Construction Loan for Oregon State Student Housing

LOAN

Commercial Observer — Mon, 05 Oct 2026 16:44:25 +0000

SUMMARY

Affinius Capital has provided a $94.8 million construction loan to Fields Holdings for the development of Zev Corvallis, a 13-story student housing complex near Oregon State University. The project aims to address the growing demand for student housing in Corvallis, particularly high-rise developments, driven by increasing university enrollment. The lender noted the project's potential to elevate student housing standards in the market.
