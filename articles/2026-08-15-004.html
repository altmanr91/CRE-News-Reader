St. Regis Bal Harbour Resort Secures $263M Refi After Special Servicing

REFINANCE

Commercial Observer — Fri, 14 Aug 2026 17:35:26 +0000

SUMMARY

Fortress Investment Group has provided a $263 million refinancing loan for the St. Regis Bal Harbour Resort, an oceanfront property in Bal Harbour, FL. The financing package, which includes $188 million in assumed existing debt and $70 million in new debt, rescued the 213-room resort from special servicing. The property, owned by Al Rayyan Tourism Investment Company (ARTIC), was valued at $311 million in public mortgage documents.

Notable Features: Oceanfront property with two pools, a 14,000-square-foot spa, five dining outposts, 13,000 square feet of event space, and a condo component.

Project Notes: The property was previously acquired for $213 million in 2014. A $188 million CMBS loan tied to the resort went into special servicing in 2025 due to the lender's requirement for a replacement interest rate cap, despite the loan remaining current and the resort maintaining a debt service coverage ratio above break-even.
