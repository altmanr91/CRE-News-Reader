Nomura Prices Largest Single-Bank SASB CMBS in Two Years

LOAN

Commercial Observer — Fri, 24 Jul 2026 10:00:46 +0000

SUMMARY

Nomura Securities' commercial real estate lending platform, which launched last summer, has closed its largest sole-bank single-asset, single-borrower (SASB) CMBS deal in nearly two years. The $719 million KELR 2026-MF transaction is backed by a portfolio of 12 multifamily properties and one student housing asset owned by Keller Investment Properties across Utah, Nevada, and Arizona. The floating-rate, interest-only loan has a two-year term with three one-year extension options and refinances existing debt while funding closing costs and reserves. This deal marks Keller Investment Properties' debut in the SASB CMBS market, a significant shift from their historical reliance on agency debt.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Units: 3,321

Loan Amount: $719.0M

Notable Features: Portfolio includes a student housing property serving Utah Valley University.

Project Notes: Keller Investment Properties acquired the portfolio between 2001 and 2022 and has invested $46 million in capital improvements. The CMBS loan refinances $696 million in existing debt and funds $22.2 million in closing-related costs and reserves. The loan has a two-year term with three one-year extension options.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

LENDER: Nomura Securities — Larry Kravetz, Head of U.S. CRE and CMBS; Frank Gilhool, Head of U.S. CRE Warehousing

SPONSOR: Keller Investment Properties — Matt Bowen, Executive Vice President; Scott Keller, Owner

MORTGAGE BROKER: Newmark

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Multifamily deals continue to be well received by CMBS bond investors. The KELR 2026-MF deal's AAA bonds priced at 135 basis points over the Secured Overnight Financing Rate. The KELR deal's bond classes priced inside of Blackstone's BX Trust 2026-PNDA deal, despite KELR having higher leverage at 77 percent compared to PNDA's 70 percent.
