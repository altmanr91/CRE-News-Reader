Prologis’ European Rival Segro Accepts $18.8B Takeover Offer

ACQUISITION

Commercial Observer — Tue, 04 Aug 2026 19:11:25 +0000

SUMMARY

Prologis, the world's largest industrial landlord, is set to expand its global footprint significantly with the acquisition of Segro, the United Kingdom's largest REIT. The $18.8 billion all-equity deal, which values Segro's equity, will create a combined portfolio of approximately $269 billion in assets under management. Segro's board, after initially resisting three prior offers, has now recommended the acquisition, which is expected to close in the first half of 2027, pending regulatory and shareholder approvals. The acquisition will boost Prologis' European operating portfolio by 47% and its European land bank by 126%, integrating Segro's extensive U.K. and European logistics and data center assets.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Size: 117,000,000 SF

Sale Price: $18.80B

Project Notes: The acquisition is expected to close in the first half of 2027, subject to legal, regulatory, and Segro shareholder approvals. Prologis will apply for a secondary listing on the London Stock Exchange as part of the deal.
