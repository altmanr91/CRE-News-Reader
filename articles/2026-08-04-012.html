Fertile ground for industrial tenants? In the Chicago area it’s increasingly in the I-90 Northwest corridor

REJournals — Tue, 04 Aug 2026 09:58:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Industrial Report

The I-90 Northwest corridor of the Chicago industrial market is experiencing a significant migration of tenants from other submarkets, including O'Hare, Central DuPage, and Northwest Cook. This trend is driven by the availability of newer, more efficient facilities and lower occupancy costs, while still providing access to the same labor pool and transportation infrastructure. Approximately 65% of the 2 million square feet of new leasing activity in the I-90 Northwest submarket since January 2025 has come from companies relocating from other Chicago-area submarkets, indicating a deliberate shift by businesses seeking long-term operational and financial advantages.

DATA POINTS

New leasing activity in I-90 Northwest submarket (since Jan 1, 2025): 2 million square feet

Relocations from other Chicago submarkets to I-90 Northwest: 1.3 million square feet (65% of new leasing)

I-90 Northwest submarket positive absorption (Q1 2026): 178,099 square feet

Speculative industrial space delivered in I-90 Northwest (2023-2024): 4 million square feet

Leased speculative industrial space in I-90 Northwest: 3.6 million square feet

Occupancy cost reduction for relocating tenants: 30% to 50%

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

The O'Hare, Central DuPage, and Northwest Cook submarkets all recorded negative absorption during the first quarter of 2026, contrasting with the positive absorption in the I-90 Northwest submarket. Data center development in the I-90 corridor, including projects by Microsoft and Compass, faces challenges such as power availability and infrastructure requirements, with industrial activity remaining the dominant driver.
