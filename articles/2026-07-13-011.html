A surge in overbidding, an office boom flows south and a dead dead-mall deal

The Real Deal — San Francisco — Sun, 12 Jul 2026 15:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Multi-Market Report

San Francisco's housing market is experiencing an unprecedented surge in overbidding, with 144 homes selling for at least $1 million over list price in the first half of this year, a 1,700% increase from the previous year. This intense competition is driven by new wealth from the artificial intelligence sector and a booming stock market, creating a challenging environment for even affluent buyers. Meanwhile, the city's commercial real estate market is seeing a peculiar recovery, with high demand for trophy office spaces despite a citywide vacancy rate of 28-32%. The AI boom is also driving San Francisco companies to expand into larger campuses in Silicon Valley, where office vacancy rates have fallen to 14.1%. In other news, the planned acquisition of the 1.2 million-square-foot San Francisco Centre Mall by Presidio Bay Ventures and the Prado Group has fallen through, returning the property to the market.
