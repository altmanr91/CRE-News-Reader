GM drives into Palo Alto with 340K sf Bay Area campus lease

LEASE

The Real Deal — San Francisco — Thu, 02 Jul 2026 19:49:21 +0000

SUMMARY

General Motors has signed a lease for nearly 340,000 square feet at the Stanford Research Park in Palo Alto, consolidating its Bay Area operations into a new campus. The automaker plans to house up to 1,500 employees, including new hires, in a mix of office and R&D lab space, with occupancy expected to begin next year. Landlords Harvest Properties and TPG Real Estate are investing millions in renovations to add amenities such as a fitness center, cafeterias, and an outdoor amphitheater, reflecting current tenant demands for enhanced worker facilities.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Property Name: Stanford Research Park

Address: 3411 Hillview Avenue, Palo Alto, CA

Size: 340,000 SF

Notable Features: Campus will include a mix of offices and research and development labs, with amenities such as a new fitness center, cafeterias, and an outdoor amphitheater.

Project Notes: Harvest Properties and TPG Real Estate acquired the property from Broadcom in 2025 for $220 million. The campus will consolidate hundreds of General Motors employees from across the Bay Area.
