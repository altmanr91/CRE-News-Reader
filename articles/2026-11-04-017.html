Microsoft Pursuing $340M Atlanta-Area Data Center

DEVELOPMENT

Connect CRE — Wed, 04 Nov 2026 14:48:14 +0000

SUMMARY

Microsoft is planning a new $340 million data center campus in Floyd County, near Atlanta, comprising three buildings totaling nearly 1.7 million square feet. The project is expected to create 150 jobs and is slated for completion in 2032. This development continues Microsoft's significant investment in Georgia's data center infrastructure.
