Rexford Sells $1.3B in SoCal Industrial Assets as Leasing Rebounds

SALE

Commercial Observer — Fri, 23 Oct 2026 19:25:07 +0000

SUMMARY

Rexford Industrial Realty, a Los Angeles-based REIT, sold 25 industrial properties totaling 5.5 million square feet for $1.3 billion in Southern California during the third quarter, including a $1.2 billion portfolio sale to EQT Real Estate. This marks a significant step in Rexford's $2 billion portfolio realignment strategy, shifting from buyer to seller. Despite rising Treasury yields impacting buyer capital costs, the company anticipates completing the remaining $500 million in planned sales by year-end. Concurrently, Rexford reported strong leasing activity, executing 116 leases covering 3.74 million square feet, leading to an increase in same-property occupancy to 96.9% and positive net effective rent spreads.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Size: 5,500,000 SF

Sale Price: $1.30B

Project Notes: The portfolio sale to EQT Real Estate had a weighted average remaining lease term of 2.7 years with rents nearly 30% above market rates. Rexford also sold three properties totaling 251,000 square feet to owner-users for $86 million.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

SELLER: Rexford Industrial Realty — Laura Clark, CEO; John Nahas, Chief Operating Officer; Michael Fitzmaurice, Chief Financial Officer

BUYER: EQT Real Estate

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Rexford's same-property occupancy rose to 96.9% in Q3 from 94.9% in Q2, though slightly below 97.1% a year earlier. Net effective rent spreads on new leases increased 7.1% in Q3, compared with declines of 2.8% in Q2. Leasing activity was driven most heavily by light manufacturing and advanced manufacturing (about one-third), with another third from logistics, distribution, and warehouse.
