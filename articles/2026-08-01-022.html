Artificial Intelligence Companies Reshaping Dublin Office Demand As OpenAI Confirms HQ

LEASE

Bisnow News Feed — Fri, 31 Jul 2026 02:31:57 -0400

SUMMARY

OpenAI has leased 88,000 square feet at the Tropical Fruit Warehouse in Dublin, Ireland, for its new European headquarters, with plans to create 250 new roles over the next two years. This move signifies Dublin's growing importance as a hub for global innovation and technology, particularly for artificial intelligence companies. The transaction follows a trend of AI firms establishing significant operational bases in the city, contributing to declining office vacancy rates and rising prime rents.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Property Name: Tropical Fruit Warehouse

Size: 88,000 SF

Project Notes: OpenAI previously occupied coworking space in Dublin and initially took space earmarked for TikTok before TikTok vacated in late 2024.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Dublin's office vacancy has fallen for five consecutive quarters, reaching 12.2% overall and 9.8% in Dublin 2. Top-tier space vacancy is 5.2%. The current office pipeline is 910,000 SF, the lowest since 2010. Prime rents are around €65 per SF and could reach €70-€80 per SF in the next 6-12 months. A Savills analysis suggests every €1B of generative AI venture capital raised translates to approximately 43,000 SF of additional office demand in San Francisco, potentially leading to 755,000 SF of demand from European-headquartered AI companies by mid-2028.
