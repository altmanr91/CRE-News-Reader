CRE Future Leaders: Draper and Kramer’s Lacy Guice

REJournals — Fri, 17 Jul 2026 10:32:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Feature / Profile

This article profiles Lacy Guice, a leasing specialist at Draper and Kramer, highlighting her career trajectory and impact in the commercial real estate industry. Guice, a recipient of the Multi-Housing News Excellence Award for Leasing Professional of the Year in 2025, transitioned into property management after a career in live sound and production. She emphasizes the importance of community, problem-solving, and teamwork in her role, and expresses a desire for continued professional growth and leadership development within the industry.
