Is the suburban Chicago office exodus ending? Vacancy rates edge lower

The Real Deal — Chicago — Thu, 16 Jul 2026 16:15:01 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Office Vacancy Report

Suburban Chicago office vacancy rates have shown their first quarterly decrease since 2019, dropping from 33.4% to 33.1%. This positive shift is partly attributed to the redevelopment of obsolete office space, which has reduced the overall suburban office inventory from 100.5 million square feet to 93.2 million square feet. The market continues to see a 'flight to quality,' with trophy office properties experiencing the lowest vacancy rates and driving net absorption up by 32,000 square feet for the quarter.
