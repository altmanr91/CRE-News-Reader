Two St. Charles senior living communities trade for $74M as hot streak continues

SALE

The Real Deal — Chicago — Tue, 21 Jul 2026 19:50:19 +0000

SUMMARY

Two senior living communities in St. Charles, IL, were sold for a combined total of $74 million, continuing a strong trend in the Chicago-area senior living market. Preservation Equity Fund Advisors acquired the 108-unit Carroll Tower for $23.5 million from 3 Diamond Development Group, which was facing foreclosure on the property. Separately, National Healthcare Properties purchased The River Glen of St. Charles for $40 million from Citrine Investment Group, which specializes in repositioning distressed senior living assets.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: senior living

Project Notes: Carroll Tower was facing foreclosure due to a matured $20 million mortgage. Citrine Investment Group acquired The River Glen of St. Charles in 2023 for $19 million with a $13 million mortgage.

SPONSOR PIPELINE
