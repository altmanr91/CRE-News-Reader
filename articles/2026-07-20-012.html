Evanston greenlights apartment complex despite historic Black church’s objections

DEVELOPMENT

The Real Deal — Chicago — Mon, 20 Jul 2026 11:30:00 +0000

SUMMARY

The Evanston City Council has approved zoning for a five-story, 30-unit apartment complex at 1103 and 1105 Emerson Street, despite objections from the adjacent Ebenezer AME Church. The church raised concerns about the development's height, density, potential impact on its structural integrity, and noise from future tenants. As a result, the developer, PM Properties, agreed to a "good neighbor" ordinance, which includes specific requirements for building materials, noise monitoring, and a taller fence for the church's parking lot.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Address: 1103 and 1105 Emerson Street, Evanston, IL

Units: 30

Notable Features: Three units are designated as affordable housing for residents earning 40% of the area's median income.

Project Notes: The development includes a 'good neighbor' ordinance requiring PM Properties to obtain city approval for exterior materials, replace building elevations, submit documents on the church's condition, avoid rooftop sound systems, build a taller fence for the church, and implement onsite noise monitoring.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

DEVELOPER/SPONSOR: PM Properties
