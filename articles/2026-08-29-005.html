City Realty Buys Brookline Multifamily Portfolio for $23M

ACQUISITION

Connect CRE — Fri, 28 Aug 2026 21:20:07 +0000

SUMMARY

City Realty has acquired a nine-building multifamily portfolio in Brookline, MA, for $23.15 million. The portfolio comprises 49 residential units and three commercial spaces, totaling 65,262 square feet with a 95% occupancy rate. The properties attracted significant investor interest due to their prime location near major medical and academic institutions, with the potential for value creation through future unit renovations.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

The sale attracted nationwide interest from private and institutional investors, indicating strong demand for well-located multifamily assets in Greater Boston. Investor conviction in Brookline remains high despite uncertainty surrounding a proposed rent-control ballot initiative.
