From “fixed-rate is for suckers” to “no return of capital”: A timeline of S2 Capital’s fall from grace

The Real Deal — Texas — Mon, 13 Jul 2026 14:30:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

This article chronicles the rise and fall of S2 Capital, a multifamily investment firm founded by Scott Everett. Initially lauded for its rapid growth and value-add strategy in Sun Belt markets, the firm faced significant challenges as interest rates rose. Despite efforts to adapt, including launching a private REIT to consolidate distressed assets and secure better debt terms, S2 Capital ultimately experienced equity wipeouts in its first fund and the REIT, leading to foreclosures and investor losses. The narrative highlights Everett's initial dismissive attitude towards fixed-rate debt, which became a point of contention as market conditions shifted.
