Douglaston Realty Offloads 7 Cornelia Street in West Village for $25M

SALE

Commercial Observer — Fri, 10 Jul 2026 16:51:43 +0000

SUMMARY

A shell corporation, with financing arranged by an investor linked to SoJo Spa Club owner Eun Rae Jo, has acquired 7 Cornelia Street in the West Village for $25 million. Douglaston Realty Management and its partners sold the five-story prewar building, which features 47 apartments and two ground-floor retail spaces. The transaction was financed with a $17.6 million mortgage from Woori America Bank.
