JLL Arranges Sale of Two Twin Cities Seniors Housing Properties

SALE

Connect CRE — Tue, 11 Aug 2026 21:22:47 +0000

SUMMARY

JLL's Seniors Housing Capital Markets team facilitated the sale of a two-property seniors housing portfolio, Boulder Ponds Senior Living and Round Lake Senior Living, totaling 216 units in the Twin Cities area of Minnesota. Trident Development sold the assets to LTC Properties, Inc., with Lifespark continuing as the operator. The communities offer a range of services including independent living, assisted living, and memory care.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: seniors housing

Property Name: Boulder Ponds Senior Living and Round Lake Senior Living

Units: 216

Notable Features: Both communities offer independent living, assisted living, and memory care services. Boulder Ponds Senior Living, completed in 2020, is a 95-unit community with approximately 98% occupancy. Round Lake Senior Living, opened in 2023, is a 121-unit community with convenient access to major employment centers, retail amenities, and healthcare services.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

SELLER: Trident Development

BUYER: LTC Properties, Inc.

SELLER BROKER: JLL

OWNER: Lifespark
