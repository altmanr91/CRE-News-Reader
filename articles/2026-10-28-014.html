The ROI of move-in week: Why a disconnected process hurts your bottom line

REJournals — Wed, 28 Oct 2026 09:42:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Non-CRE / Business News

This opinion piece argues that the move-in process for new residents, often viewed as a mere administrative task, is a critical and measurable performance metric directly impacting resident retention and Net Operating Income (NOI). The author contends that disconnected systems and fragmented communication during move-in create significant stress for both residents and property management teams, leading to lower satisfaction and reduced likelihood of lease renewals. By adopting a unified, end-to-end technological solution that streamlines the entire onboarding journey, operators can enhance resident satisfaction, reduce operational costs, and ultimately improve their bottom line in a competitive rental market.
