Newmark Sees Double-Digit Growth in Q3 Despite Capital Markets Dip

Commercial Observer — Thu, 29 Oct 2026 15:42:04 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Earnings Report

Newmark reported robust financial results for the third quarter of 2026, achieving double-digit year-over-year growth in total revenue and setting the stage for its best full-year performance in company history. The firm's management services and leasing segments were key drivers of this growth, with leasing revenue notably boosted by demand from AI, data center, and technology sectors. Despite a dip in capital markets revenue due to macroeconomic factors like high interest rates, CEO Barry Gosin expressed confidence in the firm's long-term growth strategy, emphasizing continued investment, global expansion, and the strength of its brand as he prepares to transition to chairman.
