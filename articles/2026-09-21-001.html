CMBS Conduit Capital Focuses Heavily on Multifamily, Office Sectors

Commercial Observer — Mon, 21 Sep 2026 13:02:13 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / CMBS Data

The latest conduit CMBS data from CRED iQ indicates a lending market that has become more selective rather than more cautious, with multifamily and office sectors emerging as exceptions to the trend of increased equity requirements for borrowers. Multifamily loans saw an increase in loan-to-value (LTV) to 62% and a significant drop in rates to 6%, signaling strong lender confidence. Office loans also experienced higher LTVs at 49.5%, but with thinner debt service coverage ratios, suggesting lenders are underwriting more aggressively based on perceived cash flow stabilization rather than a full return of confidence. Other property types, including retail, self-storage, and industrial, faced stricter lending terms with lower LTVs and higher debt yields, requiring more sponsor equity despite easing rates.

DATA POINTS

Multifamily LTV: 62%

Multifamily Loan Rate: 6%

Office LTV: 49.5%

Office Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR): 1.94x

Retail LTV: 47.8%

Retail Debt Yield: 20.3%
