The NRP Group marks groundbreaking of 336-unit multifamily community in Columbus

CONSTRUCTION

REJournals — Thu, 06 Aug 2026 09:44:00 +0000

SUMMARY

The NRP Group has broken ground on OSU East, a 336-unit market-rate multifamily community in Northwest Columbus, Ohio. This project marks the developer's first market-rate community in the city and is situated on a 27.5-acre site that will also include 138 for-sale townhomes by M/I Homes. The development is strategically located near major employment centers, transit corridors, and amenities, addressing the strong demand for high-quality rental housing in a rapidly growing residential corridor. The project is supported by equity from Carlyle Group and financing from PNC Bank, with the City of Columbus acting as a key partner.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: OSU East

Address: 181 Taylor Ave, Columbus, OH*

Units: 336

Site Area: 27.5 acres

Notable Features: Located near Ohio State University Airport and within the Worthington School District, offering proximity to Dublin's employment and lifestyle hubs. Features include a clubhouse, fitness center, coworking space, resort-style pool, and an 11-foot-wide bike path along the frontage.

Project Notes: The 27.5-acre site will also include 138 for-sale townhomes developed by M/I Homes of Central Ohio LLC, with their construction expected to begin in spring 2027.
