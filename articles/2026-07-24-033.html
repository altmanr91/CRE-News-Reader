UrbanStreet targets former Motorola office for residential conversion

DEVELOPMENT

The Real Deal — Chicago — Thu, 23 Jul 2026 15:45:00 +0000

SUMMARY

UrbanStreet Group is planning a residential conversion of a former Motorola office building in Schaumburg, IL, as part of its larger District at Veridian development. The project involves transforming the existing 360,200-square-foot building and constructing a new four-story sister building, creating approximately 300 residential units. This redevelopment is a key component of the $1 billion mixed-use megadevelopment, which aims to establish a walkable, urban-style downtown in the suburban area.

SPONSOR PIPELINE

The District at Veridian (first phase): A nine-story apartment building with 321 units and 60,000 square feet of retail space, anchored by Fresh Market. Secured a $94 million construction loan from Bank OZK in 2025, with a total project cost of $175 million. Expected completion by early 2027.

The District at Veridian (subsequent phases): Future phases are set to expand retail space to 200,000 square feet and include over 600 residential units, a 30,000-square-foot retail building, a parking garage, and a 150-key hotel.

Northgate at Veridian: A residential development by D.R. Horton, connected to the main complex by 18 row houses.
