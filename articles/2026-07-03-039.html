JLL Arranges Sale of Lincoln Park Retail Center to Curbline Properties

SALE

Connect CRE — Thu, 02 Jul 2026 20:01:14 +0000

SUMMARY

JLL has arranged the sale of Clybourn Center, a 33,140-square-foot unanchored retail strip center in Chicago's Lincoln Park neighborhood, to Curbline Properties. The property, which was 91.2% occupied at the time of sale, features a strong tenant mix anchored by medical and wellness uses. Its strategic location directly across from the massive Foundry Park mixed-use redevelopment project is expected to benefit the center significantly.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: retail

Property Name: Clybourn Center

Address: 2029-2073 N. Clybourn Ave., Chicago, IL

Size: 33,140 SF

Occupancy at Sale: 91.2%

Notable Features: Unanchored strip center with 86 surface-level parking spaces, a rare amenity for street-front retail in this dense urban corridor. Located across from the Foundry Park mixed-use redevelopment.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: Curbline Properties

SELLER BROKER: JLL — Michael Nieder, Managing Director; Brian Page, Director; Steve Schwartz
