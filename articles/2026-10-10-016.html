Merchants Capital Secures $98M for VA Affordable Development

LOAN

Connect CRE — Fri, 09 Oct 2026 21:02:28 +0000

SUMMARY

Merchants Capital has secured $97.8 million in financing for the extensive rehabilitation of Stuart Gardens, a 491-unit affordable multifamily property in Newport News, Virginia. The financing package includes $62.8 million in HUD 221(d)(4) permanent financing and a $35 million equity bridge loan from Merchants Bank. Developed by Vitus Group, the project will preserve affordable housing for families earning 60% of the area median income and incorporate green and resilient retrofits.
