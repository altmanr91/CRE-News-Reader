One Cardinal Way wins Large Community of the Year award

REJournals — Tue, 11 Aug 2026 15:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Association / Leadership News

One Cardinal Way, a luxury apartment community in St. Louis, was honored with the Large Community of the Year award by the National Apartment Association at its 2026 Excellence Awards. The 29-story, 297-unit tower, a 50/50 partnership between the St. Louis Cardinals and The Cordish Companies, has maintained high occupancy since its 2020 opening. The award recognizes the property's unique residential experience, which offers residents direct views into Busch Stadium and exclusive access to Cardinals gameday events, along with extensive amenities within the Ballpark Village development.
