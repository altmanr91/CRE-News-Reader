Momentum, hope and positivity: They were on the menu at Cleveland Commercial Real Estate Summit

REJournals — Thu, 02 Jul 2026 09:57:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

The 12th annual Cleveland Commercial Real Estate Summit, hosted by Midwest Real Estate News and Northeast Ohio Properties magazines, conveyed an optimistic outlook for the Cleveland commercial real estate market. Panelists highlighted strong momentum in the healthcare, retail, and multifamily sectors, alongside the enduring resilience of the industrial market. While the office sector faces national challenges, positive signs are emerging, particularly in leasing activity for high-quality Class-A properties. The overall sentiment was that better times are ahead for Cleveland's commercial real estate.
