Pebb buys office building for over $70M as Miami Beach strives to shed party-town image

ACQUISITION

The Real Deal — Miami — Thu, 23 Jul 2026 18:30:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Pebb Capital has acquired the Uno South Pointe office building in Miami Beach for over $70 million, signaling continued investor confidence in South Beach's emergence as a serious office market. The seven-story, nearly 97,500-square-foot building, which was renovated last year and is planning a penthouse addition, is approximately 97 percent leased. The transaction underscores a broader effort by market stakeholders and officials to transform the area from a party destination into a vibrant office hub.
