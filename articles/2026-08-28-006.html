Partnership Refinances 408-Unit Multifamily Property in Princeton, Texas

REFINANCE

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 28 Aug 2026 15:34:49 +0000

SUMMARY

A partnership between Core Spaces and Harrison Street Asset Management has refinanced Oxenfree at Princeton, a 408-unit multifamily property located in Princeton, Texas. The community, completed in late 2025, features a mix of rentable townhomes and detached single-family homes. Newmark arranged the undisclosed loan amount through GID.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Oxenfree at Princeton

Units: 408

Year Built: 2025

Notable Features: Offers a mix of rentable townhomes and detached single-family homes with private outdoor spaces and attached garages, and includes a pool, fitness center, sports court, and coworking space.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

SPONSOR: Core Spaces

SPONSOR: Harrison Street Asset Management

LENDER: GID

MORTGAGE BROKER: Newmark — Jonathan Firestone; Blake Thompson; Clint Frease; Travis Bailey; Josh Francis
