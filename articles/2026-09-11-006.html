Joint Venture Delivers 686,400 SF Speculative Industrial Park in Buford, Georgia

CONSTRUCTION

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 11 Sep 2026 13:15:33 +0000

SUMMARY

A joint venture between Trammell Crow Co. and a CBRE Japan-led consortium has completed the Buford Creek Business Center, a three-building speculative industrial park totaling 686,400 square feet in Buford, Georgia. The project offers a mix of cross-dock and rear-load facilities designed to meet diverse tenant needs in the growing Atlanta industrial market.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Property Name: Buford Creek Business Center

Size: 686,400 SF

Completion: September 2026

Notable Features: Building 100 is a 349,440-square-foot cross-dock warehouse with 40-foot clear heights; Buildings 200 and 300 are 168,400-square-foot rear-load buildings with 36-foot clear heights.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

DEVELOPER/SPONSOR: Trammell Crow Co.

DEVELOPER/SPONSOR: CBRE Japan

ARCHITECT: Atlas Collaborative

GENERAL CONTRACTOR: The Conlan Co.
