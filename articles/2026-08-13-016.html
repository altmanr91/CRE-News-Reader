U.S. Multifamily Gains Momentum in Q2 2026 as Absorption Surges

Connect CRE — Wed, 12 Aug 2026 21:49:50 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Multifamily Report

The U.S. multifamily market experienced significant momentum in Q2 2026, driven by a surge in apartment demand and a moderation in new construction. Net absorption nearly doubled the previous quarter's figures, leading to a notable decline in the national vacancy rate. This positive shift, coupled with easing supply growth, positions the multifamily sector for a strong rent recovery, according to CBRE.

DATA POINTS

Q2 2026 Net Absorption: 167,500 units

Q2 2026 National Multifamily Vacancy Rate: 4.3%

Q2 2026 New Supply Delivered: 77,700 units

Q2 2026 Average Monthly Rent: $2,257

Q2 2026 Multifamily Investment Volume: $34.9 billion

Multifamily Share of Total CRE Investment Volume Q2 2026: 27%

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

The national multifamily vacancy rate of 4.3% in Q2 2026 is below its long-term average of approximately 5.0%. New supply growth decreased by 14% year-over-year in Q2 2026. Average monthly rent increased 0.5% year-over-year and 1.5% quarter-over-quarter. Multifamily investment volume was down 2.7% from a year earlier.
