CBRE arranges sale of 28,150-square-foot redevelopment site in Chicago’s Wrigleyville submarket

SALE

REJournals — Wed, 08 Jul 2026 09:19:00 +0000

SUMMARY

A joint venture between SNS Realty Group and North Park Ventures has acquired a 28,150-square-foot redevelopment site in Chicago's Wrigleyville submarket for $6.25 million. The buyers successfully secured a zoning change to B2-5, enabling the development of a five-story, 99-unit apartment building. The property, formerly home to a glass manufacturing company, is strategically located near Wrigley Field and a CTA station, an area experiencing significant residential growth.
