Gantry Arranges $15.4M in Financing for Two Retail Properties in Monterey Bay Region

REFINANCE

REBusinessOnline — Thu, 02 Jul 2026 14:37:23 +0000

SUMMARY

Gantry has secured $15.4 million in permanent financing for two distinct neighborhood retail properties in California's Monterey Bay region. The loans, arranged for two separate private real estate investors, include a 10-year fixed-rate nonrecourse loan for Creek Bridge Village in Salinas and a 10-year fixed-rate loan from a regional bank for Brown Ranch Marketplace in Capitola. Both properties feature a strong tenant mix, with one anchored by a popular grocery store.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

MORTGAGE BROKER: Gantry — Tom Dao; Jake Davis

LENDER: Gantry's correspondent life company lender

LENDER: Regional Bank
