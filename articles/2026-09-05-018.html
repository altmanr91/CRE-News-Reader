Iconic Rancho Cordova Furniture Showroom Listed for Sale

Connect CRE — Fri, 04 Sep 2026 20:48:36 +0000

SUMMARY

Property Listing / For Sale

The former Beck's Furniture showroom and distribution center in Rancho Cordova, a prominent fixture along the Highway 50 corridor for nearly 50 years, has been listed for sale. The expansive 208,800-square-foot property, which includes both showroom and warehouse space, is being marketed by Cushman & Wakefield on behalf of the Beckinger Family Ownership. The property's institutional scale and community recognition are highlighted as key attributes for potential buyers.
