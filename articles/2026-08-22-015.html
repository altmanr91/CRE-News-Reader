Northmarq provides $102 million in capitalization for Baytown industrial park

LOAN

REJournals — Fri, 21 Aug 2026 13:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Northmarq's Houston Debt + Equity team secured $102 million in capitalization for the second phase of Port 99, a three-building, Class A industrial park in Baytown, Texas. The financing, arranged for Provident Realty Advisors and its joint venture partner Junction Commercial Real Estate, will fund the immediate development of nearly 1.35 million square feet of distribution and warehouse space. This project aims to meet the strong demand for industrial facilities in the rapidly expanding Southeast (Port) Houston submarket, driven by port activity and the recently completed Grand Parkway expansion.
