NFL’s Larry Ogunjobi turns developer with $16M spec home in Biscayne Point

The Real Deal — Miami — Thu, 16 Jul 2026 21:13:53 +0000

SUMMARY

Property Listing / For Sale

NFL player Larry Ogunjobi has entered the luxury spec home development market with the listing of his first project, a newly completed waterfront mansion in Miami Beach's gated Biscayne Point. The 4,300-square-foot property, dubbed "Glasshouse," is listed for $15.9 million. Ogunjobi acquired the site in 2024 for $4.6 million and developed the home through his platform, First of Many, aiming to capitalize on Miami's strong demand for move-in ready luxury residences.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Demand for luxury spec homes in Miami Beach is high, driven by wealth migration and buyers seeking move-in ready mansions in prime locations. Over 30 spec homes are currently listed for at least $10 million across Miami Beach.
