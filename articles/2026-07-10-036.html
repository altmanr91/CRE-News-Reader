The Sunbelt and Multifamily: Oversupply Isn’t the Whole Story

Connect CRE — Thu, 09 Jul 2026 21:32:04 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

This article argues that the prevailing narrative of "oversupply" in the Sunbelt multifamily market is overly simplistic and misleading. While acknowledging the significant increase in new apartment deliveries between 2023 and 2025, the author and industry experts contend that treating the Sunbelt as a monolithic market ignores crucial differences in local economies, demographics, and demand drivers. They emphasize that absorption rates, rather than just delivery numbers, provide a more complete picture, with some markets like Austin demonstrating strong demand that outpaced new supply, while others experienced softer absorption. The experts suggest that the current imbalance is more of a temporary timing issue, as projects started during a boom period completed amidst tighter capital markets and a slowdown in migration, rather than a fundamental, permanent oversupply across the entire region.
