NYC Plans 215-Unit Bronx Apartment Building

DEVELOPMENT

Connect CRE — Fri, 04 Sep 2026 22:12:05 +0000

SUMMARY

The New York City Department of Housing Preservation & Development (HPD) has filed plans for a 215-unit apartment building in the Melrose neighborhood of the Bronx. The proposed 20-story development, located on a city-owned lot, will also include 7,000 square feet of commercial space and various amenities. HPD has selected Procida Cos. and Nos Quedamos as developers for the project, which aims to advance the Melrose Commons Urban Renewal Plan by providing new affordable housing.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Address: 3116–3124 Third Ave., Bronx, NY

Size: 176,000 SF

Units: 215

Notable Features: Includes 7,000 square feet of commercial space, a community room, second-floor terrace, and roof terrace.

Project Notes: The project requires the city to remove an 85-foot height limit for new multifamily developments in the vicinity and create an Urban Development Action Area Program for income-restricted and affordable housing.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

OWNER: New York City Department of Housing Preservation & Development (HPD)

DEVELOPER/SPONSOR: Procida Cos.

DEVELOPER/SPONSOR: Nos Quedamos
