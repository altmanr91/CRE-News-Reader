Cushman & Wakefield Negotiates $83M Sale of Whole Foods-Anchored Shopping Center in Doral, Florida

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 04 Sep 2026 13:47:11 +0000

SUMMARY

Cushman & Wakefield has brokered the $83 million sale of Doral Marketplace, a newly constructed, 83,365-square-foot shopping center in Doral, Florida. The property, anchored by a prominent grocery store, was fully leased at the time of sale to a strong mix of national retailers. A high-net-worth family office, advised by Lincoln Property Co., acquired the asset from Atlanta-based SJC Ventures.
