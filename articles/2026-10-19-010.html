Partnership Completes $636M Rehabilitation of Two Brooklyn Affordable Housing Communities

CONSTRUCTION

REBusinessOnline — Mon, 19 Oct 2026 14:32:10 +0000

SUMMARY

A partnership has completed the $636 million rehabilitation of Reid Apartments and Park Rock, two affordable housing communities in Brooklyn, New York City. The project involved extensive upgrades to 1,696 units across 87 buildings, improving interiors, exteriors, mechanical systems, and security. This significant investment also ensures the long-term affordability of the communities, which house approximately 3,000 residents.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Reid Apartments and Park Rock

Units: 1,696

Total Project Cost: $636.0M

Notable Features: Rehabilitation project preserving affordability status for 1,696 units across 87 buildings.

Project Notes: Upgrades included unit interiors, building exteriors, mechanical and utility systems, security, and resident services. New storefronts, community spaces, free Wi-Fi, landscaping, and expanded support services were also introduced.
