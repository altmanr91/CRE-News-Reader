CBRE closes sale of Sheetz convenience store in Gahanna

ACQUISITION

REJournals — Fri, 04 Sep 2026 15:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

A single-tenant Sheetz convenience store located at 495 Crescent Circle in Gahanna, Ohio, was acquired by 495 Crescent Circle Gahanna Ohio LLC for $3.4 million. The transaction was an all-cash 1031 exchange for the buyer. The property is secured by a 15-year triple-net ground lease with the convenience store chain, offering long-term income stability.
