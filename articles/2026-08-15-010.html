Partnership Nears Completion of $158M Workforce Housing Project in Mount Vernon, New York

CONSTRUCTION

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 14 Aug 2026 14:22:06 +0000

SUMMARY

A partnership between Simone Development Cos. and Stagg Group is nearing completion on Qwest Towers, a $158 million workforce housing project in Mount Vernon, New York. The development will feature two buildings, 15 and 12 stories tall, offering a total of 228 residences with a mix of studios, one-, two-, and three-bedroom units. The first building is expected to be completed by the end of the year, with the second following a few months later.
