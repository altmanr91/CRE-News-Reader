RMR Lines Up $272M Mortgage Financing for Cedars-Sinai MOBs

LOAN

Connect CRE — Tue, 13 Oct 2026 20:26:02 +0000

SUMMARY

RMR Group Inc. has arranged $272.1 million in interest-only mortgage financing for two medical office buildings in Los Angeles, which are anchored by Cedars-Sinai Medical Center. The loan, provided by Morgan Stanley and BNP Paribas, has a two-year initial term with extension options and was used to refinance existing debt. The properties are owned by a joint venture, with Diversified Healthcare Trust holding a 20% equity interest.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: medical office

Address: 8631 and 8635 W. Third St., Los Angeles, CA

Size: 330,000 SF

Loan Amount: $272.1M

Notable Features: Two 11-story medical office buildings connected to Cedars-Sinai Medical Center by skybridge, with two structured parking garages.

Project Notes: Proceeds were used to repay approximately $267 million of existing mortgage debt scheduled to mature in February 2027 and to fund closing costs. The loan has a two-year initial term with three one-year extension options and bears interest at one-month Term SOFR plus 180 basis points.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

SPONSOR: RMR Group Inc.

SPONSOR: Diversified Healthcare Trust

LENDER: Morgan Stanley

LENDER: BNP Paribas
