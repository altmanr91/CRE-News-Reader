Goulston & Storrs Adds Brian Grindall to Real Estate Practice

Commercial Observer — Tue, 28 Jul 2026 20:44:08 +0000

SUMMARY

Feature / Profile

Brian Grindall, a seasoned commercial real estate attorney, has joined Goulston & Storrs as a director in its Washington, D.C. office, bolstering the firm's real estate practice. Grindall brings over two decades of experience advising on a wide range of CRE transactions, including acquisitions, dispositions, financing, leasing, and development, as well as joint ventures and distressed property restructurings. His addition is expected to enhance the firm's capacity to handle complex real estate matters for its clients.
