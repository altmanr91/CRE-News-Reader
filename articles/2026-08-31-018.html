Legal Sector Reaches Quarterly Leasing Record of 7 Million Square Feet

Connect CRE — Mon, 31 Aug 2026 15:11:33 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Office Leasing Report

The legal sector demonstrated exceptional strength in the second quarter of 2026, achieving a record 7.3 million square feet in office leasing activity, a 27% increase year-over-year. This robust performance contributed significantly to the overall office market, with legal firms accounting for 14% of total office leasing across 10 major markets. New York City led this surge, representing 30% of the quarterly volume, followed by strong activity in Chicago and the D.C. Metro area. The report anticipates continued positive contributions from the legal sector to U.S. office demand through the remainder of 2026.

DATA POINTS

Q2 2026 Legal Sector Leasing Volume: 7.3 million square feet

Year-over-year leasing volume increase: 27%

H1 2026 Legal Sector Leasing Volume: 12.2 million square feet

H1 2026 leasing volume increase from H1 2025: 17%

Law firms' share of total Q2 office leasing in 10 major markets: 14%

New York City's share of Q2 legal leasing volume: 30%

Share of H1 leases representing expansions: 43%
