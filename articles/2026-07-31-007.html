Welcome Group Underway on 250,000 SF Industrial Project in Pearland, Texas

CONSTRUCTION

REBusinessOnline — Thu, 30 Jul 2026 14:50:35 +0000

SUMMARY

Welcome Group is developing Lower Kirby Business Park, a 250,000-square-foot industrial project in Pearland, Texas. The 25-acre development will feature four tilt-wall buildings ranging from 25,000 to 100,000 square feet, with completion of Phase I expected in the second quarter of next year. The project is designed to offer flexible industrial space with modern features.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Property Name: Lower Kirby Business Park

Size: 250,000 SF

Completion: Q2 2027

Site Area: 25 acres

Notable Features: Four tilt-wall buildings with clear heights up to 34 feet, crane-ready capabilities, and flexible office buildouts.

Project Notes: Buildings will range in size from approximately 25,000 to 100,000 square feet.
