Pattillo Breaks Ground on 262,880 SF Industrial Facility in Huntsville

CONSTRUCTION

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 11 Sep 2026 13:14:27 +0000

SUMMARY

Pattillo Industrial Real Estate has commenced construction on Greenbrier Commerce Center, a 262,880-square-foot industrial development in Huntsville, Alabama. The facility, designed to accommodate single or multiple users, is expected to be delivered in August 2027. Colliers has been tapped to handle the leasing and marketing for the project, which also includes an adjacent build-to-suit site.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Property Name: Greenbrier Commerce Center

Size: 262,880 SF

Completion: August 2027

Notable Features: Features 32-foot clear heights, 46 dock-high doors, two drive-in doors, ESFR fire protection, clerestory windows, and concrete tilt-panel construction. Located near the Port of Huntsville.

Project Notes: Includes an adjacent build-to-suit site of up to 254,200 square feet, planned to be pad-ready in 2027.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

DEVELOPER/SPONSOR: Pattillo Industrial Real Estate

LEASING AGENT: Colliers — Tripp Alexander; Joe Azar; Miller Conaway
