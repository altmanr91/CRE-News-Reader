Charter Construction celebrates topping-out of The Motley in Midtown Nashville

CONSTRUCTION

REJournals — Tue, 06 Oct 2026 13:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Charter Construction has celebrated the topping out of The Motley's first residential tower, a 26-story building located at 1401 Church St. in Midtown Nashville. This milestone marks the completion of the concrete structure for the tower, which will feature 326 rental residences and ground-floor retail. The project is being developed by IPB Properties and is part of a larger three-tower community planned to include nearly 1,200 residences and various commercial uses.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: The Motley

Address: 1401 Church St, Nashville, TN

Units: 326

Completion: 2027

Phase I: 26-story residential tower

Total Project (All Phases): three-tower community with nearly 1,200 residences

Notable Features: Includes ground-floor retail and extensive resident amenities; design incorporates architectural references to the property's previous industrial use.

Project Notes: Developed on the former Country Delite Farms property.
