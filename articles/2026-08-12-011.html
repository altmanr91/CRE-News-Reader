Stream Realty Partners takes on leasing duties at Cummins Station in Nashville

REJournals — Tue, 11 Aug 2026 17:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Leasing Assignment

Stream Realty Partners has been appointed as the exclusive leasing agent for Cummins Station, a 400,000-square-foot adaptive reuse office and retail property in Nashville's Urban Core. The assignment highlights the property's appeal as a creative office and retail destination, offering a blend of historic character and modern amenities. The building provides flexible office spaces and a strong amenity package, making it attractive to a diverse range of tenants seeking an urban workplace.
