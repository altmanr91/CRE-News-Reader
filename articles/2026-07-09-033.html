Uptown Theatre owner seeks $200M overhaul with bigger city subsidy

The Real Deal — Chicago — Wed, 08 Jul 2026 15:30:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Development

The owner of Chicago's historic Uptown Theatre is seeking a significantly larger public subsidy to fund a $200 million renovation of the long-shuttered landmark. The developer argues that the proposed public funding, which is currently around half of the total project cost, is insufficient given the scale of the restoration and precedents set by other downtown projects. City officials, however, point to tax increment financing guidelines that typically cap public assistance at around 30-35% of total project costs, noting that the developer's current request far exceeds these parameters.

SPONSOR PIPELINE

Congress Theater: Redevelopment project that included affordable housing and 13,000 square feet of commercial space, with TIF assistance around 31% of total cost.

Foundry Park: A $3 billion megadevelopment seeking $202 million in TIF reimbursements.

Lincoln Yards: A megadevelopment that requested $490 million in TIF assistance.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Tax increment financing (TIF) guidance suggests funds should be around 30% of total project cost. TIF assistance for six approved LaSalle Street projects averaged 35%. TIF assistance for the Congress Theater restoration was around 31% of total cost.
