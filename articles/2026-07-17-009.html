Marcus & Millichap Arranges Sale of 33,744 SF Retail Center in Prairieville, Louisiana

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Thu, 16 Jul 2026 15:08:36 +0000

SUMMARY

Marcus & Millichap has facilitated the sale of Commerce Centre, a 33,744-square-foot retail center in Prairieville, Louisiana. An East Coast-based investor acquired the property from a local owner at the full asking price, though the specific sale price was not disclosed. The center was 72 percent occupied at the time of the transaction.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: retail

Property Name: Commerce Centre

Address: 17097 Airline Highway, Prairieville, LA

Size: 33,744 SF

Occupancy at Sale: 72.0%

Year Built: 2015

Site Area: 4 acres

Notable Features: Multi-tenant retail center with 14 suites.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: Unnamed East Coast-based investor
