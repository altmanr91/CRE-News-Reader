Mavik Capital Looks To Raise $1B, Targeting Distressed Real Estate

Bisnow News Feed — Fri, 10 Jul 2026 12:32:20 -0400

SUMMARY

Market Research / Fund Raise

Mavik Capital Management, a New York-based investment firm, is actively seeking to raise $1 billion for its new distressed real estate fund, VS3. The fund will target a variety of distressed commercial real estate assets, including hard assets and commercial mortgage-backed securities, capitalizing on market dislocations and the significant need for capital, restructurings, and recapitalizations. This initiative follows the success of two prior funds, VS1 and VS2, which collectively raised over $1 billion, demonstrating Mavik's established presence in the distressed asset space and its ability to act as an alternative lender amid a pullback from traditional regional players.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Many investors are seeking distressed commercial real estate deals due to post-pandemic shifts in the office sector and financial stress from elevated interest rates. Starwood Capital Group recently closed a $10.2 billion opportunistic real estate fund, Starwood Distressed Opportunity Fund XIII, which has already committed $3 billion of equity to 20 transactions.
