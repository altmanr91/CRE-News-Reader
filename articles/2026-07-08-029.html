Frank Gehry-designed “Icehenge” desk up for auction in Chicago

The Real Deal — Chicago — Tue, 07 Jul 2026 20:59:24 +0000

SUMMARY

Property Listing / For Sale

A unique green-tinted glass desk, known as "Icehenge" and designed by the late architect Frank Gehry, is being offered for auction by Freeman Auctioneers. The 7-ton, 16-piece desk was originally created for the Inland Steel Building in Downtown Chicago, where Gehry also held a small ownership stake for a period. The auction house estimates the desk will fetch between $100,000 and $200,000, with a starting bid of $50,000.
