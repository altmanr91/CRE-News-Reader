Law Firm Greenberg Traurig Takes 25K SF at Related Ross Dev in West Palm

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Mon, 10 Aug 2026 21:05:38 +0000

SUMMARY

Law firm Greenberg Traurig has signed a 10-year lease for approximately 25,000 square feet at the under-construction 15 CityPlace office development in West Palm Beach, FL. The firm will occupy the entire 12th floor of the 25-story building, with a move-in planned for fall 2027. This relocation signifies the firm's long-term commitment to the West Palm Beach market and its strategy to accommodate growing client demand.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Property Name: 15 CityPlace

Address: 15 CityPlace, West Palm Beach, FL

Size: 25,000 SF

Completion: 2027

Project Notes: The building is part of a larger development by Related Ross, which secured a $772 million construction debt package in 2025. The building is expected to span about 500,000 square feet.
