Hunt Midwest sells 1.5-million-square-foot retail supply center in Kansas City to Morgan Stanley

SALE

REJournals — Wed, 12 Aug 2026 18:30:20 +0000

SUMMARY

Hunt Midwest has sold the 1.5 million-square-foot Ace Hardware retail supply center, located within the KCI 29 Logistics Park in Kansas City, Missouri, to Morgan Stanley Investment Management. This acquisition aligns with Morgan Stanley's strategy to invest in mission-critical, net-leased real estate with strong tenant credit and institutional quality. The facility, which opened in 2025, serves as Ace Hardware's largest retail support center and is the largest distribution center in Kansas City by building footprint, underscoring the strategic importance of the KCI 29 Logistics Park as a premier destination for large-scale industrial development in the central U.S.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Property Name: Ace Hardware retail supply center

Address: 1881 Main St, Kansas City, MO*

Size: 1,500,000 SF

Year Built: 2025

Notable Features: Largest distribution center in Kansas City by building footprint, measuring a half mile from end to end. Located within KCI 29 Logistics Park, a 3,300-acre shovel-ready site with immediate access to Interstates 29 and 435, adjacent to Kansas City International Airport.

Project Notes: The facility is a build-to-suit for Ace Hardware and was honored with a 2026 Capstone Award by the Kansas City Business Journal.
