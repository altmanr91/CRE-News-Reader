Ralph Zucker’s luxury condo development in Asbury Park snags $186M loan

LOAN

The Real Deal — New York — Thu, 16 Jul 2026 17:15:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Inspired by Somerset Development, led by Ralph Zucker, secured a $186 million construction loan from Madison Realty Capital for its luxury condominium project, Lido Asbury Park, in Asbury Park, NJ. The financing, arranged by Meridian Capital Group, will fund the vertical development of the 112-unit beachfront property, which will also include ground-floor retail and extensive amenities. The project is notable for a penthouse unit that previously set a state record for the most expensive condominium sale.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: condo

Property Name: Lido Asbury Park

Address: 1201 Ocean Avenue North, Asbury Park, NJ

Units: 112

Loan Amount: $186.0M

Loan Per Unit: $1,660,714/unit (calculated)

Completion: 2027

Notable Features: Luxury for-sale residences with ocean views, direct access to the Asbury Park waterfront and boardwalk, ground-floor retail, swimming pool, wellness spa, fitness center, private landscaped park, and resident lounges.

Project Notes: A penthouse unit in the development previously sold for $7.6 million in July 2025, setting a record for the most expensive condominium sale in New Jersey history.
