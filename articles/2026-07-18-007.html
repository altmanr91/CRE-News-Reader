Eastham Capital Sells 200-Unit Apartment Complex in Humble, Texas

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 17 Jul 2026 14:34:27 +0000

SUMMARY

Eastham Capital has sold Eagle Crest, a 200-unit apartment complex in Humble, Texas. The garden-style property features one- and two-bedroom units, a pool, fitness center, and clubhouse. Eastham Capital, in a joint venture with Mosaic Residential, acquired the property in 2017 and undertook capital improvements. The buyer and sales price were not disclosed.
