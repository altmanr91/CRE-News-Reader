Mt. Prospect’s Gold + Oak expands design services

REJournals — Tue, 28 Jul 2026 09:51:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Non-CRE / Business News

Gold + Oak, the design division of Nicholas Family of Companies (NFOC), has launched Gold + Oak Interiors to offer full-service interior design for various property types including multifamily, hospitality, retail, and industrial projects. This expansion is supported by the hiring of three experienced designers from Eleni Interiors, a former project partner. The new division will be led by Katie Papanicholas, founder of Gold + Oak, and aims to enhance the end-to-end services provided under the NFOC umbrella. Additionally, Gold + Oak is expanding its retail presence with a second storefront in Oconomowoc, WI, located within the new Pabst59 mixed-use development.
