Raven Capital Completes 33-Story Multifamily High-Rise in Houston’s Museum District

CONSTRUCTION

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 10 Jul 2026 14:55:20 +0000

SUMMARY

Raven Capital has completed FORME, a 33-story multifamily high-rise in Houston's Museum District. The property features 475 units, including 55 boutique hotel suites, and offers a range of amenities such as a pool, fitness and recovery areas, and coworking space. The building also includes a cocktail lounge and raw bar on its 12th floor.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: FORME

Address: 5501 La Branch St, Houston, TX

Units: 475

Notable Features: Includes 55 boutique hotel suites, 20,000 square feet of fitness and recovery areas, and 20,000 square feet of coworking space. Residences feature walk-in closets and smart-home technology.

Project Notes: Rents start at $1,750 per month for a studio apartment.
