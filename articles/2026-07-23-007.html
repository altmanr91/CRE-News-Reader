Urban Logistics Realty to Develop Two Industrial Projects Totaling 341,000 SF in North Houston

DEVELOPMENT

REBusinessOnline — Wed, 22 Jul 2026 15:17:34 +0000

SUMMARY

Urban Logistics Realty, in a joint venture with Principal Asset Management, is developing two industrial projects totaling 341,000 square feet in North Houston. The projects, Urban NXS Fallbrook and Urban NXS West, will be a cross-dock and front-load industrial building, respectively. Construction is expected to commence by late summer and conclude in the second quarter of 2027, with financing provided by First United Bank & Trust.
