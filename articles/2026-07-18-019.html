Nobu co-founder buys Delray home, developer sells Bear’s Club mansion for $34M

The Real Deal — Miami — Sat, 18 Jul 2026 00:11:50 +0000

SUMMARY

Celebrity Residential / Non-CRE

This article reports on two separate high-value residential transactions in South Florida. In Delray Beach, Nobu Hospitality co-founder Meir Teper acquired a 7,800-square-foot waterfront home for $20 million. Separately, spec home developer Stuart Hankin sold a newly completed 10,900-square-foot mansion in Jupiter for $33.8 million to tech executive Derek Kreunen and Erin Kreunen. Both sales highlight the continued strong demand for luxury waterfront properties in the region.
