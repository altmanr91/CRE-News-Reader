KKR Posts Record Earnings as Fundraising Hits $133B

Commercial Observer — Thu, 30 Jul 2026 19:10:11 +0000

SUMMARY

Non-CRE / Business News

KKR reported record earnings in the second quarter of 2026, with fee-related earnings up 37 percent to $1.21 billion and total operating earnings increasing 29 percent to $1.53 billion, marking the highest quarterly total in the firm's history. The private equity giant raised $34 billion in new capital during the quarter, bringing its 12-month fundraising total to a record $133 billion. KKR is actively investing in AI infrastructure, having committed over $75 billion to digital infrastructure and power, and recently launched a $10 billion venture called Helix Digital Infrastructure to support data center build-outs.
