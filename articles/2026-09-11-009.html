Nordstrom Rack to Open 30,000 SF Store at Westpark Shopping Center in Metro Richmond

LEASE

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 11 Sep 2026 13:12:08 +0000

SUMMARY

Nordstrom Rack is set to open a new 30,000-square-foot store at Westpark Shopping Center in Glen Allen, a suburb of Richmond, VA. The new location will occupy the space previously held by another retailer, enhancing the tenant mix at the 179,973-square-foot retail power center. The landlord, InvenTrust Properties, secured the lease with the assistance of Cushman & Wakefield | Thalhimer.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: retail

Property Name: Westpark Shopping Center

Address: 9645 Broad St, Glen Allen, VA

Size: 30,000 SF

Notable Features: The retail power center spans 179,973 square feet.

Project Notes: The new store will backfill the former Painted Tree Boutiques space.
