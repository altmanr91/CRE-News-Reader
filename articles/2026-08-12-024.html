How Officials Are Picking Which Parts Of Philly Will Benefit From Opportunity Zones 2.0

Bisnow Latest News Feed — Tue, 11 Aug 2026 19:11:27 -0400

SUMMARY

Infrastructure Investment / Government Policy

Pennsylvania officials are in the process of selecting 217 census tracts across the state for the Opportunity Zones 2.0 program, with a submission deadline of September 28. Philadelphia is expected to have fewer designated tracts in this iteration compared to the initial Opportunity Zones program. The selection process aims to identify areas that will most benefit from the program's investment incentives.

DATA POINTS

Number of census tracts to be submitted by Pennsylvania: 217

Submission deadline for OZ 2.0 tracts: September 28
