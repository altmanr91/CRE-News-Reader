Lake Geneva home hits luxury waterfront market for $8M

The Real Deal — Chicago — Sat, 11 Jul 2026 13:30:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Property Listing / For Sale

A nearly 6-acre waterfront estate on Geneva Lake, known as Sidney Stables, has been listed for sale at $7.9 million. The luxury home, built in 2005, features five bedrooms, seven bathrooms, and 9,000 square feet of living space, along with 25 feet of lake frontage and a private pier. The property is situated on the south shore of Geneva Lake, an area known for its high-end estates and as a popular second-home destination for Chicago residents. The listing agent notes strong demand for waterfront properties in the current market.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Demand for waterfront properties on Lake Geneva is currently very strong. Neighboring properties include the 12-acre Aloha Lodge, which sold for $21.85 million in 2024, and the 102-year-old Allegheny mansion, listed for $24 million.
