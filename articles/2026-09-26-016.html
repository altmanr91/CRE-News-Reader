Legion, SMA Equities Partner on Upper East Side Residential

ACQUISITION

Connect CRE — Fri, 25 Sep 2026 21:17:39 +0000

SUMMARY

Legion Investment Group and SMA Equities have recapitalized three parcels on the Upper East Side of Manhattan, securing a $99 million acquisition loan from BDT & MSD Partners and Deutsche Bank. The partnership plans to develop an approximately 300,000-square-foot residential building at the site, with demolition already in progress. This project aims to continue the developers' legacy of high-quality residential developments in the area.
