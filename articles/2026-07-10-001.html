Rivani Sells Wynwood Jungle Retail Building in Miami for $26M

SALE

Commercial Observer — Thu, 09 Jul 2026 21:15:09 +0000

SUMMARY

Robert Rivani has sold the Wynwood Jungle retail building in Miami for $25.7 million to Tabani Group. The 39,000-square-foot property, located between 75 and 43 Northwest 23rd Street, was acquired by Rivani in 2021 for $13.3 million. The transaction was financed with a $15.7 million mortgage from Frost Bank.
