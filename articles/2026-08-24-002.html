Inland completes equity raise for QOZ project in downtown St. Paul

DEVELOPMENT

REJournals — Mon, 24 Aug 2026 09:50:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Inland Real Estate Investment Corporation has successfully completed an approximately $28 million equity raise for its Qualified Opportunity Fund, which holds an 89% ownership interest in a 16-story building at 386 Wabasha St. North in downtown St. Paul, MN. The property is undergoing an adaptive reuse conversion from an underutilized office building into a 178-unit multifamily residential community named "The Stella," which will also include street-level retail space. This project aligns with the evolving market dynamics and the increasing demand for urban living, contributing to the revitalization of downtown St. Paul.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: The Stella

Address: 386 Wabasha St. North, St. Paul, MN

Size: 2,338 SF

Units: 178

Notable Features: Located in a Qualified Opportunity Zone, with direct access to the Saint Paul Pedestrian Skyway System and proximity to regional transportation, employment hubs, dining, entertainment, and cultural amenities.

Project Notes: The project is an adaptive reuse of an office building into multifamily and retail, supported by historic tax credits and other financing structures. This is the sixth project completed in partnership between Inland and Kaeding, with four involving ground-up development or adaptive reuse.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

DEVELOPER/SPONSOR: Inland Real Estate Investment Corporation

DEVELOPER/SPONSOR: Kaeding Development Group LLC

GENERAL CONTRACTOR: R.E.C., Inc. d/b/a Ron Clark Construction & Design

OWNER: Bader Management, Inc.

FINANCING

The project is supported by historic tax credits and other financing structures, in addition to the $28 million equity raise from the Qualified Opportunity Fund.
