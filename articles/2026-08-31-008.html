Waterford Property Co. Breaks Ground on Affordable Housing Project in Milpitas, California

CONSTRUCTION

REBusinessOnline — Mon, 31 Aug 2026 14:43:01 +0000

SUMMARY

Waterford Property Co. has commenced construction on a 75-unit affordable multifamily development in Milpitas, California. This project, part of a larger master-planned community, will provide housing for families earning 30 to 70 percent of the area median income. The development is slated for completion in early 2028 and is being financed through a combination of 4 percent Low-Income Housing Tax Credits, private activity bonds, and both construction and permanent loan financing.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Address: 1397 California Circle, Milpitas, CA

Units: 75

Completion: early 2028

Notable Features: The community will feature two podium-level courtyards, a tot lot, barbecue and outdoor gaming area, lounge, community room, teen homework room, fully equipped community kitchen and dining area, entertainment space, a yoga/fitness studio, bike storage and electric vehicle charging stations.

Project Notes: The project is part of a larger residential master-planned community and will offer apartments to families earning between 30 percent to 70 percent of the area median income. Residents will have access to no-cost, onsite social services programming through a partnership with Pacific Housing Inc.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

DEVELOPER/SPONSOR: Waterford Property Co.

DEVELOPER/SPONSOR: C&C Development

ARCHITECT: KTGY Architecture

LENDER: California Bank & Trust

LENDER: Citibank

EQUITY/FINANCING: National Equity Fund

OWNER: Pacific Housing Inc.

FINANCING

Financed with 4 percent Low-Income Housing Tax Credits and private activity bonds.
