Davis acquires two-building medical portfolio in Quad Cities area

ACQUISITION

REJournals — Wed, 14 Oct 2026 13:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Davis has acquired a two-building outpatient medical portfolio totaling 51,856 square feet in the Quad Cities area for approximately $19 million. The Class A properties, located in Davenport, Iowa, and Silvis, Illinois, are fully leased to ORA Orthopedics under a new 15-year, triple-net master lease. This acquisition strengthens Davis' presence in the Midwest and aligns with its strategy of investing in high-quality medical properties with long-term tenancy.
