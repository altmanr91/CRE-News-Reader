295 Fifth Ave owners push for revival with refinancing

REFINANCE

The Real Deal — New York — Tue, 21 Jul 2026 19:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

A joint venture between PGIM, Tribeca Investment Group, and Meadow Partners secured a $228.9 million floating-rate, interest-only bridge loan to refinance 295 Fifth Avenue, also known as the Textile Building, in Midtown South. The refinancing follows a $350 million capital improvement project that added significant amenities and a penthouse to the 19-story office building. Despite the building being 50 percent leased, the owners are reportedly on a path to stabilization, with notable tenants having recently signed leases.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Midtown South accounted for nearly 45 percent of all leasing demand in Manhattan in April, securing four of the borough's five largest office leases. Manhattan's leasing activity saw 11 million square feet taken in Q2 and a first-half total of 22.8 million square feet, potentially making it the busiest leasing year since 2000.
