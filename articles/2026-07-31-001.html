Goldman Sachs Group Deploys $116M for Syracuse Affordable Housing Overhaul

LOAN

Commercial Observer — Thu, 30 Jul 2026 22:10:08 +0000

SUMMARY

Goldman Sachs' Urban Investment Group has provided a $116 million construction loan as part of a $269 million financing package for the redevelopment of Parkside Commons, an affordable housing complex in Syracuse, NY. The project, undertaken by BFC Partners and SAA Canopy Group, will transform the Section 8-backed property into 393 affordable apartments through a combination of renovations and new construction. The comprehensive financing also includes federal and state Low-Income Housing Tax Credits, with construction expected to begin in September and phased completion by late 2028.

Site Area: 15 acres

Notable Features: Section 8-backed affordable housing complex.

Project Notes: The 10-building complex, constructed in the mid-20th century, was previously acquired in late 2024. The project involves renovating six existing buildings and constructing two new ones, with four older structures slated for demolition and their sites reserved for a later development phase.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

SPONSOR: BFC Partners — Winthrop Wharton, Principal; Joseph Ferrara, Partner

SPONSOR: SAA Canopy Group — Connor Kenney, Co-Managing Partner; David Alexander, Co-Managing Partner

FINANCING

The $269 million financing package includes $88 million from federal Low-Income Housing Tax Credits and $13.6 million from state Low-Income Housing Tax Credits, with remaining funds from interest earnings on tax-exempt bond proceeds and interim project income.
