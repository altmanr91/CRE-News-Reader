“Diego & I”: SF apartment building formerly occupied by Frida Kahlo, Diego Rivera hits market

The Real Deal — San Francisco — Fri, 10 Jul 2026 20:18:07 +0000

SUMMARY

Property Listing / For Sale

An 11-unit apartment building on Telegraph Hill in San Francisco, notable for its historical connection to artists Frida Kahlo and Diego Rivera, has been listed for sale for $8 million. The Art Moderne property, built into a cliffside in 1938, is fully leased and represents a unique development that would be challenging to replicate under current seismic and permitting regulations. The building's full occupancy reflects San Francisco's tight housing market.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

San Francisco's housing market is rapidly tightening due to growing demand and shrinking supply.
