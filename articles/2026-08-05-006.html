What’s Next for the U.S. Office Market?

REBusinessOnline — Tue, 04 Aug 2026 15:47:25 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

The U.S. office market is showing signs of stabilization after the pandemic-induced downturn, with leasing activity remaining steady in Q2 2026, albeit still below pre-pandemic levels. The national vacancy rate has decreased, and rental rates are increasing, particularly for Class A properties. The "flight to quality" trend continues, with amenity-rich Class A spaces in vibrant neighborhoods attracting tenants, while new construction starts are at a 14-year low, leading to limited availability of large contiguous spaces.
