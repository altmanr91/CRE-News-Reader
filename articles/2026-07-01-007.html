Restoring An Industrial Core The Nonprofit Way: What Buffalo’s Northland Corridor Can Teach Other Cities

REBusinessOnline — Wed, 01 Jul 2026 11:54:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

This article argues that nonprofit-led redevelopment is a crucial strategy for revitalizing distressed industrial areas where traditional for-profit developers are hesitant to invest due to limited ROI potential. The author, an executive at Buffalo Urban Development Corp., highlights that nonprofits prioritize community outcomes, have access to diverse public funding, and can undertake complex, long-term projects like environmental remediation that for-profit entities often avoid. The Northland Corridor in Buffalo serves as a case study, demonstrating how a phased, mission-driven approach can create conditions for future private investment by focusing on workforce development and strategic site control, ultimately rebuilding economic drivers and fostering community trust.
