Publix to Anchor Retail Component of Matthews Planned Development

CONSTRUCTION

Connect CRE — Tue, 06 Oct 2026 15:02:25 +0000

SUMMARY

Pappas Properties has commenced the retail phase of its 82-acre Cadia Matthews mixed-use development, with sitework nearing completion and vertical construction anticipated to begin in late fall. The retail component, anchored by a grocery chain, will feature a 120,000-square-foot commercial village, including a bank and a 12,000-square-foot medical office building. The initial retail buildings are slated for delivery in June, followed by a quick-service restaurant next fall. The broader master-planned development will ultimately encompass 570 residential units, comprising both for-sale and rental properties.
