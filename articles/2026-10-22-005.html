Genting Lands $500M to Refi Mixed-Use Omni Center in Miami

REFINANCE

Commercial Observer — Wed, 21 Oct 2026 22:15:11 +0000

SUMMARY

Genting Americas secured a $500 million refinancing loan for its 1.5 million-square-foot mixed-use Omni Center in Miami. The property, acquired by Genting in 2011 through foreclosure, includes office space, a Hilton hotel, and a large parking garage. This financing comes three years after Genting's unsuccessful attempt to sell an adjacent 15-acre site, which it had planned to develop into a casino.
