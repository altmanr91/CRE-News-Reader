CBRE Secures $32.8M Refinancing for Hilton Garden Inn Hotel in Cupertino, California

REFINANCE

REBusinessOnline — Tue, 27 Oct 2026 14:44:01 +0000

SUMMARY

CBRE has secured a $32.8 million refinancing loan for the Hilton Garden Inn Cupertino, a 164-key hotel in Cupertino, California. The financing was arranged for a partnership between Heil & Associates and Terracommercial, who have owned and operated the property since 2007. The hotel offers a range of amenities including dining options, meeting space, and recreational facilities.
