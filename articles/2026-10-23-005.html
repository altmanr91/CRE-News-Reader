Carnegie Diner & Cafe Operator to Open New Restaurant at 7 Penn Plaza

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Thu, 22 Oct 2026 20:36:26 +0000

SUMMARY

Carnegie Hospitality, operator of Carnegie Diner & Cafe, has signed a tri-party deal to take over Smashburger's former lease at 7 Penn Plaza in Manhattan's Penn District. The agreement includes a 10-year lease extension for the 5,609-square-foot retail space, with plans for Carnegie Diner to open in November. This move allows Smashburger to focus on its broader New York metro locations.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: retail

Property Name: 7 Penn Plaza

Address: 370 Seventh Avenue, New York City, NY

Size: 5,609 SF

Notable Features: Space includes a 3,110-square-foot ground floor, an 865-square-foot mezzanine level, and a 1,634-square-foot basement.

Project Notes: This is a tri-party deal involving a lease takeover and a 10-year lease extension.
