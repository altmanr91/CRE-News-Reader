Kings Capital buys vacant FiDi Building, plans for conversion

ACQUISITION

The Real Deal — New York — Thu, 23 Jul 2026 22:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Kings Capital has acquired a 17-story vacant building at 69 Gold Street in Manhattan's Financial District from New York Presbyterian Hospital for $30.1 million. The development group plans to convert the former 90-unit staff housing into 108 market-rate residential units with ground-floor retail. The total project cost is estimated at $60 million, with a $41 million construction loan from Derby Copeland reportedly nearing completion. The building, vacant for nearly a decade, will undergo a full rehabilitation, including a new rooftop space.
