Oakland officials approve $125M sale of Coliseum, arena properties

SALE

The Real Deal — San Francisco — Tue, 14 Jul 2026 21:12:27 +0000

SUMMARY

Oakland officials have approved the sale of the city's stake in the Oakland Coliseum and adjacent Oakland Arena to Oakland Acquisition Company, a partnership between Loop Capital and the African American Sports and Entertainment Group, for $125 million. The transaction, which includes a deferred payment structure over seven years, aims to relieve the city of the financial burden of maintaining the vacant sports complex. A portion of the property, the arena parcel, will be acquired by Oak View Group, which plans to redevelop the 112-acre site into a mixed-use district featuring entertainment, retail, commercial, and residential components. The buyers envision the arena thriving as a concert and event venue, even without a major league sports tenant.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: mixed use

Property Name: Oakland Coliseum and Oakland Arena

Address: 7000 S Coliseum Wy, Oakland, CA*

Sale Price: $125.0M

Site Area: 112 acres

Notable Features: Site is located near the city's airport and freeway network.

Project Notes: Alameda County, which owns 50% of the property, negotiated a parallel agreement to sell its share for $115 million under similar deferred-payment terms. The city will receive $50 million when the arena sale closes, with most of the Coliseum site payment deferred over seven years. Buyers will pay $60 million for the stadium parcel in installments, plus an additional $15 million once development reaches the building permit stage. The city will also receive 6% of ticket revenue from both venues.
