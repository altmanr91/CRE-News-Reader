Social Housing Providers Turn To Bond Markets To Fund Expansions

Bisnow Latest News Feed — Mon, 12 Oct 2026 18:42:27 -0400

SUMMARY

Market Research / Funding Trends

Social housing providers are increasingly leveraging bond markets to finance their expansion efforts, a strategy driven by rising operational costs and reductions in traditional funding sources. Despite these financial pressures, the sector has demonstrated stable margins, which is a key factor in its ability to attract bond market investment, according to S&P Ratings.
