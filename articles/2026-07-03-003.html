D.C.’s Trophy Office Leasing Picks Up. The Rest, Not So Much.

Commercial Observer — Thu, 02 Jul 2026 19:15:32 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Office Report

Washington, D.C.'s office market is experiencing a bifurcated recovery, with strong leasing activity concentrated in trophy assets while the broader market struggles with high availability. Law firms and tech companies are driving the demand for new or recently renovated buildings, leading to significant lease transactions. Despite a substantial amount of office space being removed from the market for conversions, overall availability remains high, indicating a flight to quality among tenants.

DATA POINTS

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

D.C. office leasing volume reached 2 million square feet in Q2 2026, the highest since 2024. Class A office availability was 10% while overall availability increased to 24.2%. Class A asking rents rose over 5% year-over-year to $62.29/SF/year, while overall asking rents increased 2.9%.
