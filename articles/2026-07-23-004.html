JP Morgan Finances Recap of Industrial Portfolio With $209M Loan

LOAN

Commercial Observer — Wed, 22 Jul 2026 19:14:58 +0000

SUMMARY

A joint venture between Centerbridge Partners and Henderson Group secured a $208.5 million floating-rate loan from J.P. Morgan Chase to recapitalize a 40-asset industrial portfolio. The portfolio, spanning Pennsylvania and South Florida, encompasses 2.3 million square feet of last-mile warehouses, distribution sites, and shallow-bay facilities. The properties are 95 percent leased to a diverse tenant mix.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Size: 2,300,000 SF

Loan Amount: $208.5M

Occupancy at Close: 95.0%

Notable Features: Portfolio includes last-mile warehouses, distribution sites, and shallow-bay facilities with buildings ranging from 16,000 to 155,000 square feet.

Project Notes: Five-year, floating-rate loan for recapitalization.
