New York Life Sees Leasing Momentum at 757 Third Ave. Amid Continued Renovations

LEASE

Connect CRE — Wed, 29 Jul 2026 21:23:02 +0000

SUMMARY

New York Life has seen significant leasing activity at its 757 Third Avenue office property in Midtown East, with JLL completing 24,107 square feet of new leases and expansions. This leasing momentum coincides with ongoing renovations to the building's lobby and the introduction of a new prebuilt office program, enhancing the property's appeal to a diverse tenant mix.
