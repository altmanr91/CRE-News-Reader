Berkadia Secures $12M in LIHTC Equity for Michigan Housing Property

LOAN

Connect CRE — Tue, 08 Sep 2026 21:21:30 +0000

SUMMARY

Berkadia Affordable Housing secured $12.4 million in Low-Income Housing Tax Credit (LIHTC) equity for the development of Lighthouse Ridge Apartments, a 52-unit garden-style multifamily property in South Haven, Michigan. The financing, arranged for developers Wallick Development, Bogan Developments, and Mno-Bmadsen, will support the creation of new affordable housing options in the market. The community will feature two- and three-bedroom units across three buildings, along with a separate clubhouse offering various amenities.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Lighthouse Ridge Apartments

Units: 52

Loan Amount: $12.4M

Loan Per Unit: $238,462/unit (calculated)

Notable Features: Garden-style multifamily property with a separate clubhouse featuring community space, management office, laundry room, fitness center, and business center.

Project Notes: Financing secured through LIHTC equity.
