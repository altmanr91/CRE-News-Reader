Edgewood Capital Provides $28M Refi for Fort Lauderdale Condo Project

REFINANCE

Commercial Observer — Fri, 18 Sep 2026 18:54:28 +0000

SUMMARY

Latitude Group has secured a $27.9 million refinancing loan for its newly completed 22-unit condominium project in Fort Lauderdale, Florida. Edgewood Capital provided the financing for The Terraces, which is expected to debut this fall. BayBridge Real Estate Capital arranged the transaction for the developer.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: condo

Property Name: The Terraces

Address: 527 Orton Avenue, Fort Lauderdale, FL

Units: 22

Loan Amount: $27.9M

Loan Per Unit: $1,268,182/unit (calculated)

Occupancy at Close: 60.0%

Notable Features: Amenities include a fitness center, yoga studio, swimming pool, cabanas, and a community kitchen.

Project Notes: The 2.2-acre development site was previously acquired in 2020 for $3.3 million. A $24.5 million construction loan was secured in June 2024 from Linkvest Capital.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

SPONSOR: Latitude Group

LENDER: Edgewood Capital

MORTGAGE BROKER: BayBridge Real Estate Capital — AJ Felberbaum; Jay Miller; Spencer Miller; Noah Rothman
