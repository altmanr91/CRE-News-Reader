Target Inks 135K-SF Lease at Queens’ Rego Park Shopping Center

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Tue, 30 Jun 2026 20:46:21 +0000

SUMMARY

Target has signed a 15-year lease for 135,000 square feet at the Rego Park Shopping Center in Queens, bringing the multi-level, open-air mall to 99 percent occupancy. The retail giant will occupy space in the Rego Park II section of the center, which is anchored by a wholesale retailer and includes a strong mix of other national retailers.
