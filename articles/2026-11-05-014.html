Cushman & Wakefield takes over 7.5-million-square-foot property management portfolio in Louisville market

REJournals — Thu, 05 Nov 2026 14:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Promotion

Cushman & Wakefield has assumed management of a 7.5 million-square-foot portfolio of industrial, office, and retail properties in Louisville, KY, previously managed by NAI Fortis Group. This transition significantly expands Cushman & Wakefield's regional property management footprint to approximately 20 million square feet. The move was prompted by the planned retirement of NAI Fortis Group's founder and president, Jon Seiz, who sought to ensure continuity for clients and opportunities for his team, six of whom have joined Cushman & Wakefield.
