Stream Realty Partners secures full-building prelease at Summit Grove Business Park in Spring

LEASE

REJournals — Mon, 17 Aug 2026 09:17:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Stream Realty Partners has secured a full-building prelease for Building Two, a 90,961-square-foot industrial facility at Summit Grove Business Park in Spring, TX. The tenant, a manufacturer of precision-machined parts for the oil and gas industry, will occupy the entire building, bringing it to full occupancy ahead of its completion this month. This prelease highlights the strong demand for industrial space in North Houston, particularly for properties with strategic access to major transportation arteries.
