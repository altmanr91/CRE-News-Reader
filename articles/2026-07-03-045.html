Centerbridge Pays $750M For Stake In Family-Owned Development Firm

ACQUISITION

Bisnow News Feed — Thu, 02 Jul 2026 10:42:31 -0400

SUMMARY

Centerbridge Partners has acquired an ownership interest in Merritt Properties through a $750 million investment, with existing investor Almanac Realty Investors also participating. This capital infusion will enable Merritt Properties to expand its portfolio, particularly in the shallow-bay industrial sector, by acquiring and developing warehouses in Maryland, Virginia, North Carolina, and Florida, and exploring new markets. The deal also includes leadership changes at Merritt Properties, with Robb Merritt appointed CEO and Bobby Lanigan named President.

DATA POINTS

Sale Price: $750.0M

Project Notes: The investment valued Merritt Properties at $3 billion, including debt. Merritt Properties plans to focus on shallow-bay industrial buildings, which are 50,000 SF or smaller and can accommodate multiple tenants.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: Centerbridge Partners — Matt Dabrowski, Senior Managing Director and co-Head of Real Estate

SELLER: Almanac Realty Investors

OWNER: Merritt Properties — Robb Merritt, CEO; Scott Dorsey, Executive Chairman; Bobby Lanigan, President

EQUITY/FINANCING: Neuberger Berman

SELLER BROKER: Jefferies Private Capital Advisory

SELLER BROKER: CBRE

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Shallow-bay industrial buildings (50,000 SF or smaller) are currently undersupplied, contrasting with an oversupply of big-box warehouses following a pandemic-era construction boom.
