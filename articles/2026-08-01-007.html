JLL Brokers Sale of 113,823 SF Shopping Center in North Dallas

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 31 Jul 2026 14:21:00 +0000

SUMMARY

JLL has brokered the sale of Pavilion on Lovers Lane, a 113,823-square-foot shopping center in North Dallas. The two-building center, originally built in 1986 and renovated in 2023, was 99 percent leased at the time of sale. JLL also arranged acquisition financing for the undisclosed buyer.
