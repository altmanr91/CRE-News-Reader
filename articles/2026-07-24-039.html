Google recommits to Sunnyvale’s tightening office market in 2M sf lease renewal

LEASE

The Real Deal — San Francisco — Thu, 23 Jul 2026 22:48:23 +0000

SUMMARY

Google has renewed its lease for 2.1 million square feet across five buildings at Moffett Place and two buildings at Moffett Gateway in Sunnyvale, reaffirming its commitment to the Silicon Valley office market. This significant renewal signals a shift from the company's previous space-cutting strategy and contributes to reducing the substantial office availability in the region. The properties, developed by Jay Paul Company, are a key part of Silicon Valley's office landscape.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Property Name: Moffett Place / Moffett Gateway

Address: 151 to 1195 Borregas Avenue / 1225 Crossman Avenue, Sunnyvale, CA

Size: 2,100,000 SF

Notable Features: Moffett Place is the second-largest office complex in Silicon Valley.

Project Notes: Jay Paul Company developed Moffett Place and Moffett Gateway in 2016. Google previously exited offices at 237 Moffett Park Drive, moving Google Cloud operations to 100 and 200 West Caribbean Drive.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

LANDLORD: Jay Paul Company

TENANT: Google

Google

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Overall office availability in Silicon Valley is 18.2 percent, with Class A availability in Sunnyvale at 10.9 percent. Amazon recently leased 320,000 square feet in Sunnyvale. Apple acquired over $1 billion in offices in Sunnyvale and Cupertino last year, including a $162 million purchase at 684 West Maude Avenue last month. Fortinet bought two commercial buildings at 140 and 144 Commercial Street for $15 million last month, adding to a $47 million purchase of seven properties earlier this year.
