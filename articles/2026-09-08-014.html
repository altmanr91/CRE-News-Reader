Your snow contract is signed. Now what?

REJournals — Tue, 08 Sep 2026 09:58:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

This article provides property managers with a comprehensive guide on optimizing snow removal contracts and operations. It emphasizes the critical period between contract signing and the first snowfall, urging proactive measures to prevent winter complications. Key recommendations include transforming the scope into a detailed site plan, conducting preseason walkthroughs with the operations team to identify site-specific challenges, establishing clear decision-making chains for unexpected issues, and planning around property usage schedules. The article also highlights the importance of considering meltwater drainage and reviewing documentation and the first service event to make necessary adjustments for continuous improvement.
