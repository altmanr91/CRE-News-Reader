Newmark Arranges $130M Sale of NorthBay Healthcare Portfolio in Northern California

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Tue, 03 Nov 2026 16:29:50 +0000

SUMMARY

Newmark has brokered the sale of the NorthBay Health Medical Outpatient Building Collection, a portfolio of six fully leased healthcare buildings totaling 222,073 square feet in Fairfield and Vacaville, California. HealthCare Foundation acquired the portfolio for $130 million. The properties are fully occupied by a strong tenant mix, including medical outpatient facilities and office headquarters.
