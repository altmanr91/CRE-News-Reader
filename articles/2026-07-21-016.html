Housing Notes: Explaining the silver tsunami with surveys

The Real Deal — National — Mon, 20 Jul 2026 20:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

This article analyzes two seemingly contradictory surveys regarding Baby Boomer housing preferences: one by Freddie Mac suggesting most boomers plan to downsize, and another by NAR indicating boomers are buying larger homes. The author, a boomer who downsized, attributes the discrepancy to differing definitions of "downsizing" (e.g., square footage vs. maintenance burden) and the inherent unreliability of self-reported survey data versus actual transaction records. The piece concludes that while older boomers may sell smaller homes, many boomers are purchasing similar or larger properties, highlighting the limitations of surveys in capturing complex consumer behavior.

DATA POINTS

Boomer share of home sales: 41%

Freddie Mac 2024 survey finding (boomers planning to downsize): 66%

NAR Research survey size (July 2025): 173,250 home buyers

Freddie Mac 2024 survey size: 3,003 homeowners and renters aged 60-78
