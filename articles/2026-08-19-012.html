Northmarq closes refinance for 342-unit multifamily community in Houston

REFINANCE

REJournals — Tue, 18 Aug 2026 16:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Northmarq's Houston Debt + Equity team has successfully secured refinancing for The Mill Residences, a 342-unit mid-rise multifamily community in Houston's East End. The loan was arranged on behalf of Triten Real Estate Partners through one of Northmarq's correspondent balance sheet lending relationships. The property, built in 2025, offers luxury Class A apartments and resort-style amenities, benefiting from its prime location with access to public transit, dining, entertainment, and the burgeoning arts district.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: The Mill Residences

Address: 2315 Navigation Blvd, Houston, TX

Units: 342

Year Built: 2025

Notable Features: Luxury Class A studio, one-, and two-bedroom apartments with stone countertops, high ceilings, and multiple interior finish packages. Amenities include a fitness center, yoga studio, poolside cabanas, and a quiet lounge with high-speed connectivity for remote workers. Located in Houston's East End with convenient access to public transit, dining, live entertainment, and the arts district.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE
