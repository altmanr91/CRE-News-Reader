Chicago-based Draper and Kramer makes pair of appointments to board of directors

REJournals — Tue, 28 Jul 2026 10:02:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Promotion

Draper and Kramer, a Chicago-based real estate firm, has appointed Donald Huffner and Craig Robinson as independent members to its board of directors. Huffner brings extensive leadership experience from his previous roles as CEO and president of James Campbell Company and Collier Enterprises, as well as AIG Global Real Estate. Robinson, a seasoned executive and advisor in real estate business services, has a strong background in driving company growth and business transformation, currently serving on the boards of several property services and single-family rental market firms.
