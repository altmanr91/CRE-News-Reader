AmTrustRE Brings Financial District Office Tower to 100% Occupancy

LEASE

Connect CRE — Tue, 18 Aug 2026 22:07:09 +0000

SUMMARY

AmTrustRE has achieved 100% occupancy at its 59 Maiden Lane office tower in Manhattan's Financial District, a significant milestone ahead of the property's repositioning completion. The full occupancy was secured through a new 14,807-square-foot lease with ANV Group Holdings Ltd. and a 58,164-square-foot renewal by London Fischer LLP. This achievement validates AmTrustRE's investment strategy of repositioning well-located assets and enhancing the tenant experience.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Address: 59 Maiden Ln., New York City, NY

Size: 72,971 SF

Current Occupancy: 100.0%

Notable Features: The property is undergoing facade and lobby renovations, expected in 2027, and a redesigned public plaza, slated for completion later in 2026.
