Grassroots Analytics Doubles D.C. Footprint With 25K-SF Office Lease

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Fri, 30 Oct 2026 18:20:18 +0000

SUMMARY

Grassroots Analytics has significantly expanded its presence in Washington, D.C., by signing a 24,692-square-foot office lease across three floors at Arch Square. This relocation more than doubles the company's footprint in the city and brings the office component of the mixed-use property to full occupancy. The deal highlights continued demand for well-located office space in the current market.
