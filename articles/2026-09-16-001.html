U.S. Apartment Rents Decline for First Time in Eight Months: Report

Commercial Observer — Wed, 16 Sep 2026 14:00:44 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Rent Report

A recent report from CoStar Group's Apartments.com indicates that U.S. apartment rents experienced a slight decline in August, marking the first monthly decrease in eight months. The national average monthly rent fell by 0.03 percent to $1,751, a $1 drop from July. Despite this monthly dip, annual rent growth continues across the country, with the Pacific Northwest and Midwest leading with 2.2 percent year-over-year growth. The report suggests that while elevated supply levels are moderating pricing momentum, the August decline was milder than in previous years, signaling an improvement in pricing conditions.
