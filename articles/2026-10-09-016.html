Car Parts Supplier Spending $560M on West Point Plant Upgrade

CONSTRUCTION

Connect CRE — Fri, 09 Oct 2026 14:20:14 +0000

SUMMARY

Hyundai Transys is investing nearly $560 million to expand its manufacturing operations in West Point, Georgia. The project involves upgrading two existing plants and constructing a third facility to boost production capacity for electrified powertrains, primarily for hybrid vehicles. This expansion is expected to create 320 new jobs and support the company's global automotive customers in North America.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Address: 6801 Kia Parkway, West Point, GA

Total Project Cost: $560.0M

Project Notes: Expansion includes facility upgrades to plants one and two, and construction of a third plant. The output will primarily supply Kia.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

OWNER: Hyundai Transys
