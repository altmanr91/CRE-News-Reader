Downtown D.C. Office Conversion Secures $176M Loan, New Majority Partner

LOAN

Commercial Observer — Thu, 03 Sep 2026 19:25:51 +0000

SUMMARY

A major office-to-residential conversion project in downtown Washington, D.C., has secured $175.6 million in construction financing and a new majority partner. MetLife Investment Management acquired a 72% stake in the redevelopment of 1990 K Street NW for $58.7 million. The project, which will be rebranded as 1999 Eye Street NW, is part of the city's Housing in Downtown program, offering a 20-year tax abatement for such conversions.

Project Notes: The 2.6-acre site was previously acquired for $55.8 million in 2001. Demolition began in September 2025. The project benefits from D.C.'s Housing in Downtown program, which provides a 20-year tax abatement for office-to-residential conversions.
