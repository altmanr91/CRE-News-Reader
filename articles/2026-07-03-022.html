Nir Meir nears trial for fraud case

The Real Deal — New York — Thu, 02 Jul 2026 17:30:00 +0000

SUMMARY

CRE Legal / Regulatory

A New York County criminal court judge has tentatively set an October trial date for Nir Meir, the former HFZ Capital executive, in an alleged $86 million fraud case related to the XI condo project. Meir faces charges of grand larceny, conspiracy, and tax fraud, which have been consolidated into a single indictment. His attorney is seeking a resolution outside of trial, with a final court date scheduled for later this month to confirm the trial date.
