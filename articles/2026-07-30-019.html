AI Platform Clay Leases 44K SF with Swig Company

LEASE

Connect CRE — Wed, 29 Jul 2026 21:00:48 +0000

SUMMARY

The Swig Company has secured a 44,000-square-foot lease at The Mills Building in San Francisco's Financial District with AI platform Clay. This transaction highlights the continued appeal of downtown San Francisco for innovative companies seeking to attract talent and foster collaboration, with the building's focused spec suite initiative driving significant leasing momentum.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Property Name: The Mills Building

Address: 220 Montgomery St, San Francisco, CA

Size: 44,000 SF

Year Built: 1891

Project Notes: The building was refinanced two weeks prior to this lease announcement.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

LANDLORD: The Swig Company — Stacia Keisner, SVP of asset management; Cory Kristoff, EVP, director of leasing

TENANT: Clay

LEASING AGENT: Newmark — Bart O’Connor; Mark Geisreiter; Hannah Potter

TENANTS AT PROPERTY

Clay

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

The Mills Building has leased over 102,000 square feet year-to-date, representing approximately 21% of the property.
