Kissimmee Affordable Housing Project Rents Start at $592

CONSTRUCTION

Connect CRE — Wed, 28 Oct 2026 13:52:39 +0000

SUMMARY

HTG has broken ground on Kissimmee Cove, a 73-unit affordable housing community in Kissimmee, FL. The project is designed for income-qualifying residents, with monthly rents starting at $592. The development is supported by a diverse financing stack, including tax credits, CDBG-DR funding, and both construction and permanent loans.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Kissimmee Cove

Address: 1790 N Hoagland Blvd., Kissimmee, FL

Units: 73

Notable Features: Offers 62 one-bedroom and 11 two-bedroom apartments with full-size appliances and energy-efficient windows, along with a club room, fitness center, cybercafe, swimming pool, and social services.

Project Notes: Apartments are reserved for income-qualifying residents, with monthly rents ranging from approximately $592 to $1,660.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

DEVELOPER/SPONSOR: HTG

EQUITY/FINANCING: Raymond James

LENDER: Fifth Third Commercial Funding

LENDER: Grandbridge

LENDER: Freddie Mac

FINANCING

$9.37 million in FHFC tax credits; $8.4 million Community Development Block Grant–Disaster Recovery (CDBG-DR) loan; $12 million construction loan; $6.57 million permanent loan.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Average rents in the submarket now exceed $1,500 for a one-bedroom apartment.
