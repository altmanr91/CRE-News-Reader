Laney Development revives long-stalled with Durango Apartments plan

DEVELOPMENT

The Real Deal — Texas — Fri, 17 Jul 2026 21:21:24 +0000

SUMMARY

Laney Development is reviving its long-stalled Durango Apartments project in San Antonio, which has expanded in scope since its initial proposal nine years ago. The updated plans call for a 17-story building with at least 72 apartment units, spanning over 183,000 square feet. The development is undergoing city review, with construction anticipated to begin in early 2027.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Durango Apartments

Address: 427 South Presa Street, San Antonio, TX

Size: 183,000 SF

Units: 72

Original Plan: 12-story, 70-unit high-rise

Notable Features: Located with close access to East César E. Chávez Boulevard and pedestrian access to the River Walk, near the Tower of the Americas, Alamodome, and Briscoe Western Art Museum.

Project Notes: Project was first proposed nine years ago and has increased in scope from its original plan.
