Berkeley Garbe Underway on 150,000 SF Life Sciences Project in Billerica, Massachusetts

CONSTRUCTION

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 25 Sep 2026 14:32:17 +0000

SUMMARY

Berkeley Garbe, a joint venture between Berkeley Investments and GARBE, has commenced construction on a 150,000-square-foot life sciences facility in Billerica, Massachusetts. The project, situated on a 23-acre site, is designed to accommodate manufacturers in various high-tech industries including aerospace, robotics, and medical devices. Completion is anticipated for spring of next year, with Avison Young handling the leasing efforts.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: life sciences

Address: 161 Concord Road, Billerica, MA

Size: 150,000 SF

Completion: Spring 2027

Site Area: 23 acres

Notable Features: Building features will include a clear height of 28 feet and 264 car parking spaces, supporting manufacturers in industries such as aerospace, robotics, medical devices, semiconductors, computer hardware and battery technologies.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

DEVELOPER/SPONSOR: Berkeley Garbe

DEVELOPER/SPONSOR: GARBE

LEASING AGENT: Avison Young
