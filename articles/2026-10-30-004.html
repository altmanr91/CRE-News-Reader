AJ Capital Partners Lands $52M Refi for Princeton, N.J., Hotel

REFINANCE

Commercial Observer — Thu, 29 Oct 2026 20:03:55 +0000

SUMMARY

AJ Capital Partners has secured a $52 million refinancing loan for its Graduate by Hilton Princeton hotel in Princeton, NJ. The loan, originated by GreenOak, has a three-year term with two 12-month extension options. The 180-room hotel is strategically located directly across from Princeton University, aligning with AJ Capital's strategy of developing hospitality properties near major higher education institutions.
