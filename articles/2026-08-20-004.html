Dov Hertz Offloads Major Staten Island Industrial Site for $167M

ACQUISITION

Commercial Observer — Wed, 19 Aug 2026 21:05:43 +0000

SUMMARY

Jadian Capital has acquired a 53-acre industrial outdoor storage (IOS) site on Staten Island for $167 million from Dov Hertz's Kadima Industrial Partners. The property, located on the island's west shore, is fully leased and features both a waterfront dock and train access. This transaction highlights the strong demand from both users and capital for well-located IOS sites with excellent infrastructure proximity.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Address: 1900 and 1800 South Avenue, Staten Island, NY

Site Area: 53 acres

Notable Features: Features a waterfront dock and train access, fully leased to a strong tenant mix.

Project Notes: Seller previously acquired the two sites in 2020 and 2021 for a total of $79 million and invested approximately $10 million in capital projects.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: Jadian Capital

SELLER: Kadima Industrial Partners
