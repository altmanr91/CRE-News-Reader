Founders Group Sells 202-Unit Multifamily Community in Tucson

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 23 Oct 2026 13:33:05 +0000

SUMMARY

The Founders Group has sold The Place at 2120, a 202-unit multifamily community in Tucson, AZ, to AndMark for $18 million. The transaction was facilitated by Institutional Property Advisors, who represented both the seller and the buyer. The property, built in 1986, features a mix of one-, two-, and three-bedroom units and offers various community amenities, with most unit interiors having been recently upgraded.
