Investment Firms Silvercrest, i80 Group Lease Offices at 1345 Avenue of the Americas

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Thu, 01 Oct 2026 14:22:06 +0000

SUMMARY

Two investment firms, Silvercrest Asset Management and i80 Group, have signed new office leases at 1345 Avenue of the Americas in Midtown. Silvercrest committed to a 15-year lease for 41,000 square feet, while i80 Group secured a 10-year lease for approximately 17,000 square feet. The landlord noted strong leasing activity across its New York office portfolio, indicating the continued strength of the city's office market and the building's prime position.
