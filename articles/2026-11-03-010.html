Levey Group to Develop 445,750 SF Industrial Project in Northeast Houston

DEVELOPMENT

REBusinessOnline — Tue, 03 Nov 2026 16:16:51 +0000

SUMMARY

Levey Group is developing Levey East Belt, a 445,750-square-foot industrial project in northeast Houston. The development will feature a 270,400-square-foot front-load building and two rear-load buildings of 131,250 and 44,100 square feet. Permitting and approvals are expected to conclude in early 2027, with construction slated for 11 months, targeting an early 2028 delivery. Cushman & Wakefield is handling the leasing efforts for the project.
