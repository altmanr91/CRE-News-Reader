Stream Realty Partners negotiates 63,409-square-foot office lease in Lisle

LEASE

REJournals — Wed, 07 Oct 2026 15:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Sentinel Technologies has signed a full-building lease for 63,409 square feet at Corporetum Office Campus X in Lisle, IL, relocating its headquarters from Downers Grove. The move, which marks the company's first headquarters relocation in over 30 years, will consolidate its office, R&D, and technology lab functions. The building will undergo significant capital improvements to support Sentinel's long-term occupancy.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Property Name: Corporetum Office Campus X

Address: 410 Warrenville Road, Lisle, IL

Size: 63,409 SF

Notable Features: Four-story building located in Chicago's East/West Corridor along Interstate 88, offering prominent visibility and signage opportunities.

Project Notes: Sentinel Technologies is expected to relocate in Q1 2027. The building will undergo significant capital improvements as part of the new lease.
