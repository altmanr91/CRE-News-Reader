Brookfield-backed CSquare’s IPO missed target by $300M

The Real Deal — National — Thu, 16 Jul 2026 19:37:29 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Capital Markets

CSquare, a data center firm backed by Brookfield, completed its initial public offering, raising $1.05 billion by selling 50 million shares at $21 each. This amount fell $300 million short of its initial target, valuing the company at approximately $3.25 billion. The proceeds from the IPO are intended to repay a $771 million revolving credit facility and a $75 million promissory note held by Brookfield, which is expected to retain voting control post-offering. The company, formed last year through the merger of Cyxtera Technologies and Evoque Data Center Solutions, operates over 60 data centers across the U.S., Canada, and the U.K.
