Broadview Real Estate Unveils Plans for 32-Story Office Tower in Houston

DEVELOPMENT

REBusinessOnline — Thu, 22 Oct 2026 14:23:26 +0000

SUMMARY

Broadview Real Estate, a division of Broadview Capital, has announced plans for O27, a 32-story office tower in Houston's River Oaks area. The 520,000-square-foot development, designed by STG Design, will feature a distinctive geometric glass facade, street-level water features, and a landscaped terrace atop a nine-story parking deck. Completion of the project is anticipated in 2028.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Property Name: O27

Address: 4701 Westheimer Road, Houston, TX

Size: 520,000 SF

Completion: 2028

Site Area: 1.7 acres

Notable Features: 32-story tower with a textured, geometric facade of triangular glass panels; includes street-level water features, a landscaped terrace, and a penthouse crown. The lobby features a suspended sculpture and ceiling lights evoking Houston's waterways and 'Space City' reputation.
