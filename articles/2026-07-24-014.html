Associated Bank closes $6.9 million loan to finance industrial building in Pataskala

LOAN

REJournals — Thu, 23 Jul 2026 16:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Associated Bank has provided a $6.98 million term loan to Transport Properties and GFH Partners for an industrial building in Pataskala, Ohio. The property, completed in 2022, is fully leased and includes significant outdoor parking, aligning with Transport Properties' specialization in industrial outdoor storage.
