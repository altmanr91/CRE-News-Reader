Trump raked in billions last year, including real estate riches

The Real Deal — National — Wed, 01 Jul 2026 16:30:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Non-CRE / Business News

Donald Trump's financial disclosures reveal he earned at least $2.2 billion last year, a significant increase from $622 million in 2024, primarily driven by cryptocurrency ventures. While crypto was the largest revenue source, real estate continues to be a substantial part of his fortune, generating over $35 million from Middle Eastern licensing deals and an additional $20 million from agreements in Vietnam, Romania, Turkey, Indonesia, and India. His U.S. properties, including golf clubs and Mar-a-Lago, contributed over $122 million and $77 million in revenue, respectively.
