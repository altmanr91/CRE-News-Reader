The Data Drop: The profitability of rent-stabilized buildings

SALE

The Real Deal — National — Fri, 03 Jul 2026 12:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

This article from The Data Drop by TRD Data presents a weekly look at real estate numbers, focusing on several key market trends. It examines the profitability of rent-stabilized buildings in NYC, finding that even those with numerous housing code violations can still be profitable, despite some limitations in the data. The report also highlights that most NYC home sellers profited in 2025, with Manhattan being an exception due to its condo boom. Additionally, home flipping has declined across NYC and the state, coinciding with proposed legislation for a new tax on flippers. Finally, the article notes a significant housing boom in northern Collin County, Texas, particularly in Celina, driven by infrastructure development and population growth.
