Evelyn Capital Pays Discounted Price for Franklin Office Building

ACQUISITION

Connect CRE — Thu, 29 Oct 2026 15:15:02 +0000

SUMMARY

Evelyn Capital acquired Two Greenway Centre, a 156,000-square-foot office building in Franklin, TN, for $31 million from TA Realty. This transaction represents a significant discount from its previous sale price of $43.5 million in 2019. The acquisition continues Evelyn Capital's strategy of purchasing office assets at reduced prices, following their 2024 acquisition of Philips Plaza in downtown Nashville.
