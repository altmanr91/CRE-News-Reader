Chicago's Older Apartments Are Running Out Of Room To Raise Rent

Bisnow News Feed — Sun, 12 Jul 2026 13:00:17 -0400

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

Chicago's Class-C apartment market is facing significant challenges despite overall multifamily tailwinds, as rising operating costs for insurance, property taxes, and utilities are outpacing rent growth for older properties. This squeeze on margins is particularly acute for vintage stock, where some investors who focused on cosmetic upgrades in recent years deferred vital maintenance, making these properties harder to sell. While rent-by-necessity rates have increased substantially, they are now approaching affordability limits for workforce tenants, compressing the runway for further rent hikes. The market is seeing a return to more experienced operators who are selectively pursuing value-add opportunities, often with below-market rents or assumable low-rate debt, as new buyers face higher financing costs.
