Movers: Chicago’s multifamily momentum sparks CRE hiring spree

The Real Deal — Chicago — Mon, 06 Jul 2026 12:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Hiring & Promotions

Commercial real estate firms are expanding their multifamily investment sales and financing operations in Chicago, driven by the city's leading rent growth and increased sales volume. Murphy Real Estate Services promoted David Ariola to president to spearhead capital raising and new partnerships for value-add and ground-up multifamily developments. Walker & Dunlop relocated its Midwest multifamily investment sales leader, Matt Jones, to Chicago to leverage the robust market. Additionally, Inland Real Estate Group hired Jacqueline Dagan as associate general counsel and Dhaval Parikh as head of institutional capital formation to bolster its legal and capital raising capabilities.
