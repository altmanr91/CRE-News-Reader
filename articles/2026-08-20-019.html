Walker & Dunlop Arranges Financing for Stamford Residential Conversion

LOAN

Connect CRE — Wed, 19 Aug 2026 21:28:24 +0000

SUMMARY

Walker & Dunlop has secured a $74.5 million loan for the acquisition and redevelopment of The Caspian, a luxury condominium project in Stamford, CT. The financing, provided by an affiliate of Apollo Global Management, will facilitate the conversion of an existing 241,000-square-foot waterfront office building into 63 luxury residences. This project is strategically positioned to address the significant demand for new waterfront residential product in the supply-constrained Fairfield and Westchester counties.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: condo

Property Name: The Caspian

Address: 68-70 Seaview Ave., Stamford, CT

Units: 63

Loan Amount: $74.5M

Loan Per Unit: $1,182,540/unit (calculated)

Notable Features: Waterfront location with a 57-slip marina, private rooftop terraces, and structured parking.

Project Notes: Conversion of an existing seven-story office building into luxury condominium residences.
