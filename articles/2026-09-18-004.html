Juanita’s Foods to Consolidate L.A. Production at 120K-SF Facility

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Thu, 17 Sep 2026 19:25:26 +0000

SUMMARY

Juanita's Foods, an 80-year-old food manufacturer, is consolidating its Southern California production into a new 120,000-square-foot facility in Santa Fe Springs, CA. This move marks the first transaction supported by the 'Big 4' partnership, an economic development coalition formed by four Los Angeles County industrial cities to attract and retain businesses through coordinated incentives and expedited permitting. The company considered locations nationwide before deciding to remain in Southern California, citing the city's business approach and incentive package as key factors.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Address: 13100 Arctic Circle, Santa Fe Springs, CA

Size: 120,000 SF

Project Notes: The facility was acquired by LBA Logistics in December 2019. Juanita's Foods sold a majority stake to Apex Capital in May 2025.
