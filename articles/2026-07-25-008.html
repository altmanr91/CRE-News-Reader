Merritt Properties Acquires 11-Building Industrial Park in Jacksonville

ACQUISITION

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 24 Jul 2026 15:07:03 +0000

SUMMARY

Merritt Properties has acquired Center Point Business Park, an 11-building, 537,800-square-foot light industrial park in Jacksonville, FL, for $89.8 million. The property was 99 percent leased at the time of sale. This acquisition is a significant move for Merritt Properties, following a substantial $750 million investment from Centerbridge Partners aimed at fueling the company's growth strategy.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Property Name: Center Point Business Park

Address: 4801-6631 Executive Park Court, Jacksonville, FL

Size: 537,800 SF

Sale Price: $89.8M

Occupancy at Sale: 99.0%

Notable Features: Features 18- to 22-foot clear heights, rear-load configurations, dock-high loading, concrete tilt-wall construction, 107 dock doors, and 1,005 parking spaces. Located in the Butler corridor near the Florida East Coast Railway.
