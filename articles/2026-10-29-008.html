StreetLevel Investments Welcomes New Tenants to Mixed-Use Development in Forney, Texas

LEASE

REBusinessOnline — Thu, 29 Oct 2026 14:53:44 +0000

SUMMARY

StreetLevel Investments has announced a new wave of tenants for its Village at Gateway mixed-use development in Forney, Texas. The 152-acre project, located east of Dallas, is expanding its retail and entertainment offerings with several new operators. The development is also slated to include future multifamily units, a significant medical district, and additional retail square footage, indicating a comprehensive long-term vision for the site.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: mixed-use

Property Name: Village at Gateway

Address: Gateway Blvd, Forney, TX*

Site Area: 152 acres

Notable Features: Mixed-use development located approximately 20 miles east of Dallas, with future plans for multifamily units, a 50-acre medical district, and additional retail space.

* Address sourced separately, not in original article.
