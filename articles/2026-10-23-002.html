Northmarq Arranges $96M Refi for 1,461-Unit SoCal Portfolio

REFINANCE

Commercial Observer — Thu, 22 Oct 2026 21:45:54 +0000

SUMMARY

E&S Ring has secured $95.8 million in refinancing from Voya Investment Management for a six-property multifamily portfolio totaling 1,461 units across Southern California. The new financing package, arranged by Northmarq, consolidates existing debt, including four loans previously provided by Voya in 2007. The structure features a mix of fixed and floating-rate debt, interest-only payments, and specific prepayment provisions, with each property receiving a non-cross-collateralized loan.

DATA POINTS

Project Notes: The refinance covers existing debt across the portfolio, including four loans from Voya in 2007. The new financing is structured as six non-cross-collateralized loans, and includes a combination of fixed- and floating-rate debt, interest-only payments and prepayment provisions.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Greater Los Angeles multifamily vacancy held at 4.5 percent through the first half of the year, while average rent remained slightly below the level from one year ago. Investment activity accelerated, with more than 40 percent more properties trading in the first half than during the same period in 2025.
