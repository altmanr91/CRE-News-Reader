RCG Ventures Picks Up 207K-SF Tucker Retail Center

ACQUISITION

Connect CRE — Fri, 30 Oct 2026 14:16:42 +0000

SUMMARY

RCG Ventures has acquired the 207,000-square-foot Tucker Meridian Shopping Center in Tucker, Georgia, from Ben F. Kushner Company. The grocery-anchored retail center, developed in 2015 on the site of a former office park, was nearly fully occupied at the time of sale. This acquisition aligns with RCG Ventures' strategy of investing in multi-tenant, anchored shopping centers with long-term ownership potential.
