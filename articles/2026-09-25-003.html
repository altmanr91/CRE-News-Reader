A Resilient New York City Wins Big at Commercial Observer’s Power Gala

Commercial Observer — Thu, 24 Sep 2026 20:21:26 +0000

SUMMARY

Feature / Awards Gala

Commercial Observer's 19th annual Power Gala celebrated the resilience and recovery of New York City's commercial real estate sector, highlighting significant transactions and developments over the past year. The event, held at 7 World Trade Center, recognized key players who have contributed to the market's post-pandemic vigor, including record office rents and leasing demand. Awards were presented for Investment Deal of the Year, Finance Deal of the Year, Urban Developer of the Year, Office Deal of the Year, and the Executive Leadership Award, underscoring the industry's tenacity and transformative impact on the city's skyline.

DATA POINTS

Investment Deal of the Year: Naftali Group's $810 million acquisition of 800 Fifth Avenue in 2025

Finance Deal of the Year: J.P. Morgan Chase's $4.3 billion construction financing at One Beverly Hills

Urban Developer of the Year: Vornado Realty Trust for $2.5 billion revitalization of the Penn District, development of Sunset Pier 94 Studios, and the Citadel-anchored office tower at 350 Park Avenue

Office Deal of the Year: Silverstein Properties and American Express for the banking giant's nearly 2 million square feet global headquarters at 2 World Trade Center

Executive Leadership Award: Lisa Silverstein for transitioning Silverstein Properties' leadership and expanding its platform
