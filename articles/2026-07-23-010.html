Partnership Agrees to Acquire Providence Place Mall in Rhode Island for $133M

ACQUISITION

REBusinessOnline — Wed, 22 Jul 2026 14:31:27 +0000

SUMMARY

A partnership comprising Pyramid Management Group, Paolino Properties, and DW Partners has agreed to acquire Providence Place Mall, a 1.4 million-square-foot lifestyle center in Providence, Rhode Island, for $133 million. The property had been under court-ordered receivership for several years due to a loan default by its previous owner. The new ownership intends to continue an ongoing repositioning program at the mall.
