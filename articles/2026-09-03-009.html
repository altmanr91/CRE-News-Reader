ACP Negotiates $22.5M Sale of Retail Building Leased to Whole Foods in Nashua, New Hampshire

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Thu, 03 Sep 2026 13:03:24 +0000

SUMMARY

Atlantic Capital Partners (ACP) has negotiated the $22.5 million sale of a 44,538-square-foot retail building in Nashua, New Hampshire. The property, which opened in late 2024, is fully leased to a prominent grocery store and is part of the larger Webster Square shopping center. The seller and buyer were not disclosed.
