OPEN Impact Arranges 15-Year Occupancy for Brooklyn Charter School

LEASE

Connect CRE — Fri, 28 Aug 2026 21:51:56 +0000

SUMMARY

OPEN Impact Real Estate has arranged a 15-year lease for Launch Expeditionary Learning Charter School, securing approximately 77,000 square feet at 5902 Sixth Ave. in Brooklyn's Sunset Park. This transaction provides the charter school with a permanent, purpose-built educational campus, which is a rare find in the Brooklyn market. The school plans to relocate in July 2027 after facility modernizations.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: school

Address: 5902 Sixth Ave, Brooklyn, NY

Size: 77,000 SF

Notable Features: Purpose-built educational campus with a regulation-size gymnasium.

Project Notes: The property was originally occupied by Our Lady of Perpetual Help Catholic Academy.
