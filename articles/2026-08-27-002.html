T30 Capital Sells East Williamsburg Loft Building at 59 Bogart Street for $30M

ACQUISITION

Commercial Observer — Thu, 27 Aug 2026 18:46:40 +0000

SUMMARY

Local real estate investor Jo-Ann Obergfell, operating through 59 Bogart LLC, has acquired a 51-unit multifamily loft building at 59 Bogart Street in East Williamsburg, Brooklyn, from T30 Capital for $30 million. The property, originally an industrial building converted to residential lofts in 2005, attracted significant investor interest due to its transit-oriented location and value-add potential. The seller's broker noted strong demand for the asset, reflecting continued capital seeking opportunities in active investment markets.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Address: 59 Bogart Street, Brooklyn, NY

Units: 51

Sale Price: $30.0M

$/Unit: $588,235/unit (calculated)

Notable Features: Three-story building with one- to three-bedroom units, a lounge, gym, bike room, and communal outdoor spaces, located above the L train's Morgan Avenue station.

Project Notes: The property was converted from industrial to residential lofts in 2005. T30 Capital's predecessor entity, Sugar Hill Capital Partners, previously acquired the property for $39.5 million in 2016.
