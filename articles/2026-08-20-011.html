Incentives play a key role in Liberty, Missouri’s, industrial growth

REJournals — Thu, 20 Aug 2026 09:28:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

The city of Liberty, Missouri, has prioritized economic development by actively pursuing private investment and jobs, utilizing tax incentives and established programs to attract targeted industrial and manufacturing companies. This strategy has resulted in approximately $650 million in projects, adding over 6 million square feet of industrial space and more than 4,000 jobs to the area. The city's clear, predictable incentive programs and collaborative approach with developers and businesses are key factors in its success, providing certainty and agility in a competitive industrial market.
