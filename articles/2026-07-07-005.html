Barry LePatner of LePatner & Associates: 5 Questions

Commercial Observer — Mon, 06 Jul 2026 20:00:47 +0000

SUMMARY

Feature / Profile

Barry LePatner, founder of LePatner & Associates, discusses the persistent challenges facing the construction industry, including supply chain disruptions, labor shortages, and a reluctance to adopt new technology. He argues that these issues, exacerbated by past economic downturns and the COVID-19 pandemic, create significant uncertainty for project owners regarding timelines, costs, and material availability. LePatner advocates for greater integration of technology, particularly artificial intelligence, to enhance efficiency and mitigate risks, citing an example where a lack of due diligence on material sourcing nearly derailed a major warehouse project.
