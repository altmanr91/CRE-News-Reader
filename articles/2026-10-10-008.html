STRIVE Negotiates Sale of 16,599 SF Shopping Center in Granbury, Texas

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 09 Oct 2026 16:31:27 +0000

SUMMARY

STRIVE has brokered the sale of Fountain Oaks Shopping Center, a 16,599-square-foot retail property in Granbury, Texas. The center, built in 2013, was 96 percent leased at the time of sale with a strong tenant mix. The seller was undisclosed, as were the buyer and the sales price.
