Michael Troyanovsky of Regency Development Group: 5 Questions

Commercial Observer — Fri, 21 Aug 2026 17:45:51 +0000

SUMMARY

Feature / Profile

This article features an interview with Michael Troyanovsky of Regency Development Group, discussing the firm's focus on residential development in Bay Harbor Islands, FL. Troyanovsky highlights the area's appeal as an "overlooked hidden gem" offering boutique living for empty nesters and those seeking privacy. The firm has four active projects in the submarket, with units ranging from $2 million to $10 million, and has adapted its future plans to accommodate unit combinations for larger families.
