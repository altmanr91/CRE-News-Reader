Kolter, BH Inch Toward Buyout on Oceanfront Miami Beach Condo

ACQUISITION

Commercial Observer — Fri, 02 Oct 2026 21:05:42 +0000

SUMMARY

Kolter Group and BH Group are nearing completion of an oceanfront site assemblage in Miami Beach, acquiring the majority of units within the Port Royale condo building for $61.6 million. This acquisition, combined with previous purchases of two neighboring hotels, will pave the way for a new luxury condominium development. The site, spanning approximately 2 acres, is slated for a 17-story, 37-unit condo project with ground-floor retail, designed by Kobi Karp.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: condo

Property Name: Port Royale condo building

Address: 6969 Collins Avenue, Miami Beach, FL

Units: 163

Sale Price: $61.6M

Site Area: 2 acres

Notable Features: Oceanfront site assemblage between 69th and 71st streets, including two neighboring hotels; the Port Royale building was evacuated five years ago after being deemed unsafe.

Project Notes: The developers previously acquired the 34,980-square-foot Normandy Plaza Hotel at 6985 Collins Avenue for $18 million in 2023, and the Crystal Beach Suites at 6979 Collins Avenue for $26 million in January 2026, along with its 7,600-square-foot commercial condos for $14.4 million in July 2026. Wells Fargo increased a loan tied to these properties to $83.2 million in September 2026. Plans were filed in September 2026 to demolish the Normandy Plaza Hotel, and in 2025 for a 37-unit condo development.
