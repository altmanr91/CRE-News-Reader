Eight Retail Tenants to Open at Phase II of Bluhawk in Overland Park, Kansas

REBusinessOnline — Mon, 27 Jul 2026 14:12:07 +0000

SUMMARY

Development

Phase II of the Bluhawk mixed-use development in Overland Park, Kansas, will welcome eight new retail tenants, including a returning outdoor retailer and an Italian gelato brand making its market debut. These new additions are part of a larger expansion that connects the retail offerings with the existing sports park. The developer and manager of the project is Price Brothers, with several tenants already open in Phase II and others anticipated to open later this year and in summer 2027.
