Waldorf Astoria set to take over W South Beach

The Real Deal — Miami — Tue, 07 Jul 2026 20:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Development

The W South Beach hotel is set to undergo a significant renovation and rebrand as a Waldorf Astoria property, with an expected reopening in winter 2027. The project, led by owners Reuben Brothers, will include a redesign of 348 oceanfront guest suites, a new lobby, upgraded dining venues, and an enhanced spa and pool deck. The change in branding from Marriott's W to Waldorf Astoria will result in the termination of over 300 employees, though the owners anticipate creating new employment opportunities post-renovation. This renovation is part of Reuben Brothers' expanding hospitality portfolio in South Florida.

SPONSOR PIPELINE

JW Marriott Miami Turnberry Resort & Spa: Acquired a 25 percent stake in the 685-room resort in Aventura in 2020.

Chesterfield Hotel: Acquired the 53-room Palm Beach hotel for $42 million in 2022, recently renovated and set to reopen as The Vineta Hotel by Oetker Collection.
