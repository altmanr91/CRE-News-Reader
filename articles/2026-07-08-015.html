Thrive Senior Living buys land for senior living development in Ooltewah

ACQUISITION

REJournals — Tue, 07 Jul 2026 14:05:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Thrive Senior Living has acquired an 80-acre parcel in Ooltewah, Tennessee, for a new senior living development. The project will feature approximately 400 units across a full continuum of care, including 55+ single-family homes, independent living cottages and apartments, assisted living, and memory care. The development aims to provide a hospitality-driven lifestyle within a residential-scale environment, offering strong amenities and diverse living options to meet evolving resident needs.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: senior living

Property Name: Brooks Farm

Address: 9101 Amos Rd, Ooltewah, TN*

Units: 400

Site Area: 80 acres

Notable Features: Located within a growth corridor with access to retail, healthcare, churches, residential neighborhoods, and regional transportation routes.

Project Notes: The single-family homes within the development will be built by RP Communities.

* Address sourced separately, not in original article.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE
