Chicago’s top construction permits: Week ending July 17, 2026

CONSTRUCTION

The Real Deal — Chicago — Thu, 16 Jul 2026 22:42:03 +0000

SUMMARY

Onni Group secured a permit for a $190 million, 46-story residential tower at 931 North Halsted Street in Chicago's Near North Side. The project will feature 464 units, including 81 affordable units, along with extensive amenities. Additionally, Lathrop Preservation Phase IC obtained permits for an $85 million multi-building restoration of the historic Lathrop Homes, involving interior and structural alterations to six residential buildings. Amylu Foods LLC also filed plans for a $39.5 million commercial alteration at 1400 West 44th Street to expand a food manufacturing facility.
