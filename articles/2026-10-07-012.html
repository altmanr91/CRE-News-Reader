Cushman & Wakefield adds managing director in Detroit office

REJournals — Wed, 07 Oct 2026 15:26:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Promotion

Cushman & Wakefield has appointed Mike Valant as Managing Director in its Detroit office. Valant brings 15 years of commercial real estate experience, specializing in cross-market leasing and dispositions across various property types including office, industrial, investment, and retail sectors. His expertise spans complex transactions for both tenants and landlords, as well as buyers and sellers.
