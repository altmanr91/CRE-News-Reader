Whirlybird dreams: Ken Griffin wants helipad for Miami Beach megayacht marina

The Real Deal — Miami — Thu, 02 Jul 2026 21:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Development

Billionaire Ken Griffin is seeking approval from Miami Beach city commissioners to add a private helipad to his planned megayacht marina development at 120 MacArthur Causeway. The proposal, which would require an amendment to the city's land development regulations to allow private helipads as an accessory use in the industrial zoning district, aims to provide ultra-exclusive access for marina users to bypass Miami traffic. This helipad would complement the already approved marina, which includes eight megayacht slips, an owner's pavilion, and a crew operations center.
