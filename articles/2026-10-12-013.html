Century City’s Anderson Towers Refinanced for $550M

REFINANCE

Connect CRE — Mon, 12 Oct 2026 17:14:23 +0000

SUMMARY

Anderson Real Estate, a family-owned real estate firm, has secured a $550 million refinancing package for its Anderson Towers office campus in Century City. The two-building property, located at 1800 and 1900 Avenue of the Stars, is 98.1% leased and has undergone over $300 million in capital improvements as part of a multi-year repositioning strategy. The successful refinancing underscores the owner's confidence in the Century City market and the appeal of the upgraded office environment.
