Sunday Summary: The Deals Lit Up Like a Fireworks Display

Commercial Observer — Sun, 05 Jul 2026 13:00:51 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Weekly Summary

This article provides a summary of significant commercial real estate activity, highlighting major transactions and market trends. It covers Bridgepoint Group's $1.4 billion acquisition of Kayne Anderson Real Estate and Blackstone's $3.5 billion sale of three data centers to Digital Realty Trust. The piece also notes Starwood's closing of a $10.2 billion opportunistic fund for data center investment. Additionally, it details robust office leasing activity in New York City, Los Angeles, and Miami, with specific examples of large leases and rising rents, while also touching on challenges in the D.C. office market and the impact of rent freezes on New York City landlords.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

New York City office market shows strongest leasing velocity since 2002, with volume doubling and asking rents increasing. Law firms accounted for approximately 30% of the leasing activity in NYC. Los Angeles office market saw a 15% uptick in leasing from the previous quarter, totaling 4 million square feet, an 8% increase year-over-year. Miami's office leasing volume surged 45% quarter-over-quarter, with rents 57% higher than five years ago. The D.C. office market continues to struggle with high availability, except for trophy buildings.
