Lee & Associates brokers pair of industrial sales in Chicago

SALE

REJournals — Mon, 31 Aug 2026 15:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Lee & Associates brokered two separate industrial property sales in the Chicago market. In Cicero, a 15,170-square-foot industrial building on a 2.17-acre site, suitable for industrial outside storage, was sold by Iwema Excavating to a private investor. Separately, Holian Industries acquired a 5,500-square-foot industrial property in Chicago from Globe City Partners.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

SELLER: Iwema Excavating

SELLER BROKER: Lee & Associates — Dylan Maher, Senior Vice President; Brian Vanosky, Principal; Michael O’Leary, Principal

BUYER: Private Investor

BUYER: Holian Industries

SELLER: Globe City Partners

SELLER BROKER: Luri Properties — Roger Luri
