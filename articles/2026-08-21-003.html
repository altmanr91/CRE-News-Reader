New York City’s Technology Labor Market Has Surpassed San Francisco: Report

Commercial Observer — Thu, 20 Aug 2026 19:40:03 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Labor Report

New York City has surpassed the San Francisco Bay Area in total tech employment for the first time, according to a CBRE report. New York's tech workforce reached 394,300 in 2025, an 8.4% increase from 2022, while San Francisco's tech employment declined by 6% to 375,730 jobs over the same period. This shift is attributed to New York's diversified job market, with a significant portion of its tech workers employed in finance, insurance, and real estate, providing resilience against tech industry downturns. The city also benefits from a strong university system and comparatively lower wages, making it attractive for tech companies seeking East Coast headquarters. This growth is reflected in Manhattan's office market, where tech and AI companies are actively leasing significant space.
