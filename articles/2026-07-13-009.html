Marcus & Millichap closes sale of 25,113-square-foot shopping center in Houston

SALE

REJournals — Mon, 13 Jul 2026 09:21:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Marcus & Millichap has facilitated the sale of Seabrook Shopping Center, a multi-tenant retail property in Houston's Bay Area. The 25,113-square-foot center was sold by a local investor to a buyer who was introduced to the opportunity through an existing relationship after a previous acquisition in the Houston area. The property offers significant lease-up potential, with approximately 19,000 square feet of available space, which is expected to increase long-term net operating income.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: retail

Property Name: Seabrook Shopping Center

Address: 2100 E. NASA Parkway, Houston, TX

Size: 25,113 SF

Site Area: 1.55 acres

Notable Features: Located in the Bay Area near Space Center Houston, with visibility along a high-traffic corridor carrying over 19,000 vehicles per day.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

SELLER: Local Investor

SELLER BROKER: Marcus & Millichap — Scott Jones, Investment Specialist; Gus Lagos, Investment Specialist; Alex Wolansky, Investment Specialist
