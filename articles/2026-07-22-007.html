HLCI Completes 254,186 SF Industrial Project in Northwest Houston

CONSTRUCTION

REBusinessOnline — Tue, 21 Jul 2026 14:56:46 +0000

SUMMARY

Holt Lunsford Commercial Investments (HLCI) has completed Par 1960, a two-building industrial project totaling 254,186 square feet in northwest Houston. The development, which began construction in late 2024, offers modern industrial space with varying clear heights and loading configurations across its two buildings. Cushman & Wakefield has been appointed as the leasing agent for the property.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Property Name: Par 1960

Address: 11010 Jones Rd, Houston, TX*

Size: 254,186 SF

Completion: July 2026

Notable Features: Building 1 spans 204,375 square feet with 32-foot clear heights and a front-load configuration. Building 2 totals 49,811 square feet with 28-foot clear heights and a rear-load configuration.

Project Notes: Construction began in late 2024.
