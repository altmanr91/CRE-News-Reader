IRG Acquires 423K-SF Distribution Facility in St. Paul

ACQUISITION

Connect CRE — Mon, 31 Aug 2026 22:26:15 +0000

SUMMARY

Industrial Realty Group (IRG) has acquired a 422,727-square-foot industrial distribution facility in South St. Paul, Minnesota, for $21 million. The property, formerly the headquarters of an online and catalog retailer, is strategically located with excellent access to the broader Minneapolis-St. Paul interstate network. JLL has been retained to oversee leasing for the facility, which is well-suited for cross-dock distribution operations.
