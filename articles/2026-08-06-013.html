Northmarq leads sale and acquisition financing for 190-unit multifamily community in Jeffersonville

SALE

REJournals — Thu, 06 Aug 2026 09:37:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Northmarq's investment sales and debt & equity teams facilitated the sale and acquisition financing of Jeff on 10th, a 190-unit multifamily community in Jeffersonville, Indiana. The property, built in 2022, was sold by a regional multifamily developer to S&S Properties Investment. Northmarq also arranged permanent-fixed financing for the buyer through its Fannie Mae DUS platform, structured on a seven-year term.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Jeff on 10th

Address: 3500 Ellingsworth Dairy Drive, Jeffersonville, IN

Units: 190

Year Built: 2022

Notable Features: The community offers a resort-style swimming pool, 24-hour fitness center, outdoor grilling areas, community fire pits, a conference center, and a 24-hour pet spa. It is located near major employers and economic drivers including the 6,000-acre River Ridge campus.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

SELLER: Regional multifamily developer

BUYER: S&S Properties Investment

LENDER: Northmarq
