Walker & Dunlop Arranges $117.3M Refinancing of Texas Apartment Portfolio

REFINANCE

REBusinessOnline — Tue, 03 Nov 2026 16:42:24 +0000

SUMMARY

Walker & Dunlop has arranged $117.3 million in refinancing for a portfolio of three apartment communities totaling 960 units across Texas. The portfolio includes the 312-unit Austin Avenue in Georgetown, the 312-unit Aven Ridge in Leander, and the 336-unit Latitude Magnolia in Aubrey. The floating-rate, interest-only loans were provided by Benefit Street Partners to an undisclosed institutional investment group.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Units: 960

Loan Amount: $117.3M

Notable Features: The Austin-area communities were built in 2021 and 2022, offering one-, two-, and three-bedroom units, with Austin Avenue also including studio apartments. Latitude Magnolia was completed in 2021 and offers one- and two-bedroom units.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

LENDER: Benefit Street Partners

MORTGAGE BROKER: Walker & Dunlop — Jordan Casella; Aaron Appel; Adam Schwartz; Jonathan Schwartz; Keith Kurland; Christopher de Raet; Nicholas Gilhooley

FINANCING

Floating-rate, interest-only loans.
