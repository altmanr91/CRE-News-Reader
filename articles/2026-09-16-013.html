Harold E. Eisenberg Foundation expanding mentorship program to Indiana

REJournals — Wed, 16 Sep 2026 09:46:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Association / Leadership News

The Harold E. Eisenberg Foundation is expanding its real estate mentorship program to Indiana, offering college and university students in the greater Indianapolis area the opportunity to job shadow over 50 industry leaders. This expansion builds on the success of the Foundation's existing programs, including its annual Real Estate Challenge and Career Summit, which have seen significant participation from Indiana universities. The program aims to provide students with practical insights into various commercial real estate sectors and strengthen the talent pipeline in Indianapolis.
