Yamaha Divests U.S. Headquarters in Sale-Leaseback

SALE

Connect CRE — Mon, 05 Oct 2026 17:33:41 +0000

SUMMARY

Yamaha Motor Corporation, U.S.A. has completed a sale-leaseback transaction for its U.S. headquarters campus in Cypress, CA. The Walt Disney Co. acquired the 25-acre, 278,964-square-foot property for $115 million, intending to utilize it for back-office operations. Yamaha will continue to occupy the campus through December 2028 as it prepares for a planned relocation to Kennesaw, GA. The transaction highlights the strong demand for high-caliber assets in the local market.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Address: 6555 Katella Ave., Cypress, CA

Size: 278,964 SF

Sale Price: $115.0M

Site Area: 25 acres

Notable Features: Infill industrial-zoned property includes one industrial, one flex, and one office building.

Project Notes: Yamaha will lease back the property through December 31, 2028, as part of a planned relocation to Kennesaw, GA. The property has served as Yamaha's U.S. headquarters since 1979.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

SELLER: Yamaha Motor Corporation, U.S.A.

BUYER: The Walt Disney Co.

SELLER BROKER: Avison Young — Patrick Barnes; Paul Clark; Nick Slonek; Jae Estep; Jeff Estep, managing principal occupier services
