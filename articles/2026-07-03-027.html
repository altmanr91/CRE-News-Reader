“Disgusted”: Zillow antitrust fight with Chicago MLS, Compass draws out fiery testimony from CEOs

The Real Deal — Chicago — Thu, 02 Jul 2026 23:33:25 +0000

SUMMARY

CRE Legal / Regulatory

A federal court hearing in Chicago has intensified the antitrust dispute between Zillow, Midwest Real Estate Data (MRED), and Compass, focusing on MRED's private listing network and Compass's 'private exclusives.' Zillow alleges that MRED and Compass conspired to exclude it from the market by restricting access to pre-marketed home listings, raising anticompetitive concerns. MRED, which controls 98% of Chicagoland listings, countered that it severed Zillow's data feed due to Zillow's alleged breach of an agreement prohibiting listing discrimination based on agent affiliation. Compass defended its phased marketing strategy, claiming it benefits sellers with higher prices and faster sales, while Zillow's expert argued that private networks fragment the market and lead to lower sale prices.
