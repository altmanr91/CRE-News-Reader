The Plan: At the Windsor in Brooklyn’s Windsor Terrace, Assume Nothing

Commercial Observer — Wed, 22 Jul 2026 10:00:38 +0000

SUMMARY

Development

Local Development Group has launched sales for The Windsor, a new condominium development in Brooklyn's Windsor Terrace neighborhood. The project offers 98 condominium units ranging from studios to three-bedrooms, priced between $525,000 and $1.67 million, alongside 33 affordable rental units. The developer aimed to create a building that blends with the neighborhood's classic aesthetic while providing luxury amenities, with move-ins anticipated to begin in October.
