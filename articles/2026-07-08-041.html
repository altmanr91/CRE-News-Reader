Targus Relocates Headquarters to BGO-Owned Business Park

LEASE

Connect CRE — Tue, 07 Jul 2026 20:42:41 +0000

SUMMARY

Targus, a global leader in laptop cases and mobile computing accessories, has signed an 84,041-square-foot industrial lease at Anaheim Concourse, relocating its corporate headquarters to a newly constructed Class A facility. The move represents a strategic decision to upgrade to a modern building with enhanced functionality and a prime location, supporting the company's long-term growth while maintaining connectivity to its workforce and customer base. The transaction highlights the ongoing trend of occupiers prioritizing high-quality facilities in established infill markets.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Property Name: Anaheim Concourse

Address: 1206 N. Miller St., Anaheim, CA

Size: 84,041 SF

Notable Features: Class A facility with 32-foot clear heights, an ESFR sprinkler system, a secured truck court, and direct access to major freeway networks.
