AI Companies Sign Full-Floor Leases at Repositioned Flatiron District Building

LEASE

Connect CRE — Mon, 21 Sep 2026 22:14:21 +0000

SUMMARY

Two artificial intelligence companies, Normal Computing and Inspiren, have signed full-floor office leases totaling 16,734 square feet at 61 W. 23rd St. in Manhattan's Flatiron District. These transactions bring the recently repositioned building to full occupancy, highlighting the continued appeal of Midtown South for innovative firms seeking growth-supportive office space and competing for skilled talent.
