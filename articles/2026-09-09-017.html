IWG/Regus Leases Two Floors at Cohen Brothers’ 475 Park Ave. South

LEASE

Connect CRE — Tue, 08 Sep 2026 21:38:47 +0000

SUMMARY

International Workplace Group (IWG), parent company of Regus, has signed a 22,000-square-foot lease for two full floors at 475 Park Avenue South in Manhattan. This transaction, along with four other recent leases, expansions, and renewals, underscores strong tenant demand at the recently renovated Midtown South office tower. The new location will enable IWG/Regus to expand its footprint in the Midtown South submarket.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Address: 475 Park Ave. South, New York City, NY

Size: 22,000 SF

Notable Features: The property is a 35-story Midtown South tower that has recently undergone transformation.
