April Housing Underway on $21.5M Renovation of Arlington Affordable Housing Complex

CONSTRUCTION

REBusinessOnline — Mon, 05 Oct 2026 13:51:50 +0000

SUMMARY

April Housing, the affordable housing division of Blackstone Real Estate, has commenced a $21.5 million renovation of Mayfield Park, a 240-unit complex in Arlington, TX. This renovation is part of a resyndication effort designed to extend the property's affordability status for an additional 30 years. The upgrades will encompass interior unit improvements, new mechanical systems, and enhancements to common area amenities.
