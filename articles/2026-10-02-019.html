Front Range Obtains $75M Refi on St. Pete Apartments

REFINANCE

Connect CRE — Fri, 02 Oct 2026 13:56:45 +0000

SUMMARY

Front Range Partners secured a $75 million fixed-rate refinancing loan for EVO St. Petersburg, a 23-story luxury apartment complex in downtown St. Petersburg, FL. The financing was provided by Freddie Mac and arranged by Walker & Dunlop Capital Markets Institutional Advisory. The property, delivered in 2023, features 220 residential units and ground-floor retail, benefiting from its prime location near the Tampa Bay waterfront and various cultural attractions.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: EVO St. Petersburg

Address: 334 2nd Ave S, St. Petersburg, FL

Units: 220

Loan Amount: $75.0M

Loan Per Unit: $340,909/unit (calculated)

Year Built: 2023

Notable Features: 23-story high-rise with 26,000 square feet of indoor and outdoor amenities including a pool and wellness spaces, located two blocks from the Tampa Bay waterfront and within walking distance of major attractions.

Project Notes: Includes approximately 13,000 square feet of ground-floor retail.
