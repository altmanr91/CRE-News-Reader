Tyler Henritze’s Town Lane Exceeds Target with $1.9-Billion Fundraise

Connect CRE — Mon, 26 Oct 2026 12:34:57 +0000

SUMMARY

Capital Raise / Fund Close

Town Lane, a real estate investment firm co-founded by Tyler Henritze, has successfully closed its second opportunistic fund, Town Lane Real Estate Opportunities Fund II, at its hard cap of $1.9 billion. This fundraise significantly exceeded its initial target of $1.5 billion and the $1.25 billion raised for its predecessor fund in 2024. The fund will pursue a flexible investment strategy across various commercial real estate sectors and capital structures in the U.S., focusing on themes with strong supply and demand fundamentals amid current market dislocations.

DATA POINTS

Fund II Commitments: $1.9 billion

Fund II Target: $1.5 billion

Fund I Commitments: $1.25 billion
