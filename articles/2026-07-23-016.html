World Cup finals deliver last-minute windfall to NYC hotels

The Real Deal — National — Wed, 22 Jul 2026 22:15:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Hospitality Report

New York City hotels experienced a last-minute surge in revenue due to the World Cup finals, with an additional $74 million generated on July 17-18 from an influx of visitors from Spain and Argentina. This unexpected boost, along with an anticipated $35 million to $40 million from the championship match, is expected to bring total incremental hotel revenue close to the initial $300 million projection for the tournament. However, this late windfall was not enough to offset an otherwise sluggish summer, as hotel occupancy has generally trailed last year's levels and the industry continues to face challenges from higher operating costs, weaker international tourism, and a slower post-pandemic recovery.

DATA POINTS

Additional hotel revenue from World Cup finals (July 17-18): $74 million

Total hotel revenue from World Cup prior to finals: $252 million

Projected additional hotel revenue from championship match (July 19): $35 million to $40 million

Original World Cup hotel revenue projection: $300 million

Hotel employment vs. pre-Covid levels: 12.9% fewer workers

Visitor volumes vs. 2019 levels: 2.4% below

Average price per room in 2025: $333.71

Average price per room increase from 2024 to 2025: 5%

Average price per room increase from 2019 to 2025: 17%
