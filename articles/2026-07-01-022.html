Industrial Overtakes Multifamily In JLL Income Property Trust's Portfolio

Bisnow News Feed — Tue, 30 Jun 2026 13:34:30 -0400

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

JLL Income Property Trust's portfolio now allocates 38% to industrial assets, totaling $2.8 billion, surpassing its residential holdings of 36% or $2.3 billion. The REIT's CEO, Allan Swaringen, noted that industrial properties offer higher cash-on-cash returns compared to apartments in the current market, making them a more attractive investment. The firm is strategically targeting last-mile logistics facilities near key transportation hubs, which allows for higher rent potential and tenant attraction. This shift is also influenced by macroeconomic factors such as increased U.S. manufacturing and defense spending, as well as companies building inventory due to global tensions and climate concerns.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Industrial leasing activity saw 174 million square feet of new leases in Q1 2026, marking the best Q1 performance since 2022. Concurrently, only 54 million square feet of new industrial projects came online. This absorption, coupled with reduced construction, has led to a 5.2% increase in industrial rents over the last year, with average rents reaching $9.12 per square foot as of May.
