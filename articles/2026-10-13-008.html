JLL Arranges $237.7M Loan for Life Sciences Campus in Research Triangle Park

LOAN

REBusinessOnline — Tue, 13 Oct 2026 15:42:15 +0000

SUMMARY

JLL has arranged a $237.7 million loan from Starwood Property Trust for King Street Properties to finance Phase I of Pathway Triangle, a life sciences and advanced manufacturing campus in North Carolina's Research Triangle Park. Phase I includes four buildings totaling 581,789 square feet, with two buildings already completed and 73 percent leased, and the remaining two buildings recently commencing construction. The campus is strategically located in a key life sciences hub, with strong preleasing activity for its advanced manufacturing and lab spaces.
