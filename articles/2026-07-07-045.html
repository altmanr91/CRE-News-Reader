Microsoft Eliminates 2% Of Workforce, Plans More Cuts For Xbox

Bisnow News Feed — Mon, 06 Jul 2026 12:33:34 -0400

SUMMARY

Non-CRE / Business News

Microsoft is undergoing a significant restructuring, eliminating 4,800 positions globally and planning further cuts of 3,200 roles within its Xbox division. This move is attributed to the rapid advancements in artificial intelligence and the need to adapt to a changing technological landscape. The Xbox division, in particular, is facing a "reset" due to unhealthy operating margins and slower-than-expected growth in its Game Pass subscription service, leading to the sale or spin-off of several game development studios.
