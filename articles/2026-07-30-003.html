NYC Issues Partial Stop-Work Order at SL Green’s 750 Third Avenue Conversion

Commercial Observer — Wed, 29 Jul 2026 21:31:52 +0000

SUMMARY

Construction

New York City's Department of Buildings has issued a partial stop-work order at SL Green Realty's office-to-residential conversion project at 750 Third Avenue in Midtown East. The halt, affecting steel work above the eighth floor, was prompted by improperly welded steel columns and reported inconsistencies, though SL Green stated there are no structural issues and they are working to resolve the condition. This incident follows two other conversion project halts in Manhattan this month, indicating increased scrutiny on such developments.
