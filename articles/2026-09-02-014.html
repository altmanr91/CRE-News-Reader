Logistics Property Company closes 217,758-square-foot industrial lease at Missouri City’s CityPark Logistics Center

LEASE

REJournals — Tue, 01 Sep 2026 18:35:40 +0000

SUMMARY

Logistics Property Company has secured a 217,758-square-foot build-to-suit industrial lease with Standex GRID / Amran Instrument Transformers at CityPark Logistics Center in Missouri City, TX. The new facility, currently under construction and expected to complete in Q2 2027, will serve as a warehouse and manufacturing plant, consolidating the tenant's existing Texas operations into its largest North American location. The project benefits from its strategic Southwest Houston location with direct access to Beltway 8, proximity to Highway 90, and a strong local workforce, which were key factors in its selection.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Property Name: CityPark Logistics Center, Building 7

Size: 217,758 SF

Completion: Q2 2027

Notable Features: Cross-dock building with 32-foot clear height, ample car and trailer parking, direct access to Beltway 8, proximity to Highway 90.

Project Notes: Build-to-suit warehouse and manufacturing facility. Consolidates five Houston-area locations for the tenant.
