Phoenix Investors acquires 1.8 million square feet of industrial property in Saginaw area

ACQUISITION

REJournals — Tue, 01 Sep 2026 14:04:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Phoenix Investors has acquired a 1.8 million-square-foot industrial portfolio comprising three buildings in Buena Vista Charter Township, part of the Saginaw, Michigan area. The transaction involved a sale-leaseback arrangement, with Nexteer Automotive continuing to occupy 1.27 million square feet across two buildings. Phoenix Investors plans to make significant capital investments to reposition the remaining 552,000 square feet in Plant 5 for new industrial users, leveraging the property's established manufacturing infrastructure and strategic location to attract further investment to the region.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Property Name: Plants 5, 6/1, and 7 (former General Motors Saginaw Steering Gear complex)

Size: 1,800,000 SF

Notable Features: Located near I-75 and M-46 (Holland Road) within an established manufacturing corridor with strong ties to the automotive and advanced manufacturing industries.

Project Notes: Nexteer Automotive will lease back approximately 1.27 million square feet across two buildings (Plants 6/1 and 7). Approximately 552,000 square feet in Plant 5 is currently available for lease. Nexteer retained ownership of adjacent plants (Plant 3 and Plant 4) and land, including a solar field and test track.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: Phoenix Investors

SELLER: Nexteer Automotive

SELLER BROKER: CBRE — Andrew Baines; Matt Osiecki; Joe Kemp
