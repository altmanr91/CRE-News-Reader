Mandarin Oriental Brickell Key, Live Local high-rise among $420M-plus South Florida construction permits for week ending July 22, 2026

The Real Deal — Miami — Thu, 23 Jul 2026 15:54:37 +0000

SUMMARY

Construction

South Florida saw over $420 million in construction permits issued for the week ending July 22, 2026, highlighted by a $240 million permit for The Residences at Mandarin Oriental, Miami and Mandarin Oriental Hotel on Brickell Key. This redevelopment by Swire Properties will feature condos and a hotel across 1.35 million square feet, replacing the previously demolished hotel towers. Other significant permits included a $95 million mixed-use project by HA Emprendimientos, a $21.3 million mixed-use development by Lore Development Group and Element Development, and a $19 million municipal office building for the City of Miami Administration Building.

DATA POINTS

Largest new construction permit: $240 million for 1 Island Drive

Second-largest new construction permit: $95 million for 114 Northeast 22nd Street

Third-largest new construction permit: $21.3 million for 1750 Northwest 15th Street Road

City of Miami Administration Building permit: $19 million

Largest alteration permit: $22.5 million for Kaseya Center
