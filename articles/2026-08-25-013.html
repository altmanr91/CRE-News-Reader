StreetLevel Investments announces first tenants at Forney’s Village at Gateway

REJournals — Mon, 24 Aug 2026 14:15:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Development

StreetLevel Investments announced the opening of the first phase of Village at Gateway, a 152-acre mixed-use development in Forney, Texas. The initial phase, totaling 750,000 square feet, features a diverse mix of retail, entertainment, and restaurant tenants. The project is strategically located within the larger 2,000-acre Gateway master-planned development, capitalizing on Forney's significant population growth over the past decade.

DATA POINTS

Forney population growth (past decade): more than doubled

Forney current population: exceeding 40,000 residents

Kaufman County growth ranking: third fastest-growing county in the nation
