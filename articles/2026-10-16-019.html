The Ride, Like Most Business, Was About Navigating the Journey

Connect CRE — Thu, 15 Oct 2026 19:51:53 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Editorial

This article is an editorial piece reflecting on the author's cross-country bicycle ride and drawing parallels between the challenges of the journey and those encountered in business. The author, a managing director at Walker & Dunlop, undertook the 3,200-mile ride to raise money and awareness for No Kid Hungry. He argues that both long bike rides and business ventures require adaptability, the ability to change plans when facts shift, and the wisdom to select the right tools or strategies for specific conditions. He concludes that focusing on small, manageable segments of a larger goal is crucial for sustained progress and success, especially when facing difficult circumstances.
