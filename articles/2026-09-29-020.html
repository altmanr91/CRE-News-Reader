KKR, Mirastar Acquire Eight UK Logistics Properties from Ares

ACQUISITION

Connect CRE — Tue, 29 Sep 2026 12:51:22 +0000

SUMMARY

KKR and its European industrial and logistics platform, Mirastar, have acquired a portfolio of eight fully occupied logistics properties across the UK from Ares Real Estate funds. The 2.7-million-square-foot portfolio, valued at approximately $529 million, includes modern assets in established distribution markets such as the Midlands, South East, and North of England. KKR noted the strong fundamentals in the UK logistics sector, driven by robust occupier demand and limited supply, making this acquisition an attractive opportunity for durable income and growth.
