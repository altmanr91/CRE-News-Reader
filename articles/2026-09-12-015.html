AmTrustRE Completes 360 Lexington Avenue Repositioning

Connect CRE — Fri, 11 Sep 2026 21:34:34 +0000

SUMMARY

Construction

AmTrustRE has completed a comprehensive modernization and repositioning of 360 Lexington Avenue, a 268,000-square-foot office building in Manhattan. The project, led by MdeAS Architects, aimed to transform the mid-century modern property into a contemporary, hospitality-focused workplace. The repositioning enhances the building's appeal for next-generation office users, capitalizing on its prime location near Grand Central and Midtown's business district.
