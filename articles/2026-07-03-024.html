LCOR lands $193M construction loan for Edgewater tower amid flurry of apartment projects

LOAN

The Real Deal — Miami — Thu, 02 Jul 2026 22:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

LCOR secured a $192.5 million construction loan for a 544-unit luxury apartment tower in Miami's Edgewater neighborhood. The financing, provided by Natixis Corporate and Investment Banking, will support the development of the 39-story building, which will also include ground-floor retail and extensive amenities. Despite a regional oversupply in the multifamily market, developers remain optimistic about long-term demand and are strategically selecting submarkets for new projects.

Notable Features: The tower will include over 40,000 square feet of amenities, more than 10,000 square feet of ground-floor retail, and a 448-space parking garage.

Project Notes: The 1.1-acre site was previously acquired by LCOR in 2022 for $49 million. The construction loan matures in 2029.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

In 2024, 18,600 multifamily units were completed in South Florida, outpacing total leasing by approximately 20 percent. In 2025, 12,718 units were completed, exceeding total leasing by about 1,000 apartments. South Florida's median asking rent in May was $2,284, a 2.2 percent decrease year-over-year. Much of the new supply consists of four- and five-star luxury properties.
