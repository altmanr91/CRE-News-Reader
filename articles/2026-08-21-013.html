Entre Commercial Realty closes 16,932-square-foot industrial lease in Elk Grove Village

LEASE

REJournals — Thu, 20 Aug 2026 16:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Entre Commercial Realty brokered a 16,932-square-foot industrial lease in Elk Grove Village, Illinois, for a local diesel mechanic and fleet repair operator. The property at 675 Greenleaf Avenue features heavy-duty truck repair capabilities and a secured 1.3-acre lot, a key factor in attracting the tenant due to the scarcity of such yard space in the Chicago industrial market.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Address: 675 Greenleaf Ave, Elk Grove Village, IL

Size: 16,932 SF

Site Area: 1.3 acres

Notable Features: Equipped for heavy-duty truck repair with a fully secured 1.3-acre lot.
