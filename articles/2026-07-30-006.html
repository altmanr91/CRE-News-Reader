StreetLights Residential Underway on 22-Story Apartment Building in Plano

CONSTRUCTION

REBusinessOnline — Wed, 29 Jul 2026 15:15:14 +0000

SUMMARY

StreetLights Residential has commenced construction on a 22-story apartment building in Plano, TX. The project, situated on a 2.7-acre site within the former J.C. Penney headquarters campus, will feature 261 residential units, including one-, two-, and three-bedroom apartments, as well as three-bedroom townhomes. Construction began in June and is slated for completion in early 2029.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Address: 6501 Legacy Drive, Plano, TX

Units: 261

Completion: early 2029

Site Area: 2.7 acres

Notable Features: Amenities will include a three-tiered pool with private cabanas, sky lounge, golf simulator, library, fitness center, and a pet park.

Project Notes: The development is located within the former J.C. Penney headquarters campus.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

DEVELOPER/SPONSOR: StreetLights Residential
