FoxRock Buys Quincy Office At 78% Discount After Luring Tenant Away

ACQUISITION

Bisnow Latest News Feed — Thu, 22 Oct 2026 20:03:57 -0400

SUMMARY

FoxRock Properties acquired an office building at 1250 Crown Colony Drive in Quincy, MA, for $11.5 million, representing a significant discount from its previous sale price. The acquisition follows FoxRock's successful effort to relocate a major tenant from the acquired property to its adjacent building at 1200 Crown Colony Drive, which it also owns. This strategic move allowed FoxRock to secure the asset at a favorable price, highlighting its local market expertise and ability to create value through tenant repositioning.
