Avison Young closes sale of 200,696-square-foot food production facility in Pleasant Prairie

ACQUISITION

REJournals — Thu, 10 Sep 2026 09:13:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Royal Oak Realty Trust, a New York-based REIT, has acquired a 200,696-square-foot food production facility in Pleasant Prairie, Wisconsin, from EQT Real Estate. The state-of-the-art facility, built in 2017, is fully leased to a national bakery products supplier and is considered a mission-critical asset within the tenant's production and distribution network. The transaction highlights Pleasant Prairie's emergence as a key industrial market in the Midwest, drawing national occupiers due to its strategic location between Chicago and Milwaukee and access to major transportation networks.
