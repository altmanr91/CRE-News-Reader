Law firms double down at Chicago’s AMA Plaza amid foreclosure

LEASE

The Real Deal — Chicago — Tue, 14 Jul 2026 22:23:08 +0000

SUMMARY

Two law firms, Latham & Watkins and Swanson, Martin & Bell, have adjusted their lease agreements at AMA Plaza in downtown Chicago. Latham & Watkins expanded its footprint by 35,274 square feet, bringing its total to 194,000 square feet, and extended its lease by seven years. Swanson, Martin & Bell downsized its space from 91,000 square feet to 56,760 square feet but extended its lease for 12 years. These commitments are a positive sign for the building, which is facing a $372 million foreclosure lawsuit and is being marketed for sale by a court-appointed receiver.
