Get to Know the Broker: Michael Martirena of Compass

Commercial Observer — Thu, 05 Nov 2026 12:15:37 +0000

SUMMARY

Feature / Profile

This article profiles Michael Martirena, a luxury residential real estate advisor at Compass, highlighting his career achievements and personal insights. Martirena, part of the Ivan & Mike Team, specializes in high-net-worth client transactions across Miami-Dade, Broward, and Palm Beach counties in Florida. He closed $298.32 million in sales in 2025 and has surpassed $2 billion in career sales. Martirena emphasizes the importance of consistency, market knowledge, and building a business that can thrive independently.
