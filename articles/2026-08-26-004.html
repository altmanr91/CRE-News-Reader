The New School Sells Loeb Hall Residence to Hawkins Way Capital for $52M

ACQUISITION

Commercial Observer — Tue, 25 Aug 2026 19:45:52 +0000

SUMMARY

Hawkins Way Capital has acquired Loeb Hall, a 268-bed student residence hall near Union Square in Manhattan, from The New School for $51.5 million. This marks Hawkins Way Capital's third student housing acquisition in Manhattan this summer, demonstrating its strategy to expand its Found Study student housing platform. The property will undergo renovations and be repositioned to provide high-quality accommodations in a strong urban student housing market.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: student housing

Property Name: Loeb Hall

Address: 131-135 East 12th Street, New York City, NY

Beds: 268

Sale Price: $51.5M

$/Bed: $192,164/bed (calculated)

Notable Features: Located four blocks east of The New School's main University Center.

Project Notes: The property was previously used as a dormitory by The New School but was removed from its undergraduate housing options this year. It will be repositioned under Hawkins Way Capital's Found Study student housing platform.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

EQUITY/FINANCING: Varde Partners
