Related Companies Snags 150K-SF Lease at Upcoming Midtown Tower

LEASE

Connect CRE — Wed, 02 Sep 2026 21:14:21 +0000

SUMMARY

General Atlantic has signed a long-term lease for over 150,000 square feet at Related Companies' 625 Madison Avenue, an under-construction Midtown tower. The global investment firm will serve as the anchor tenant, occupying five floors for its global headquarters when the building opens in 2029. The new 53-story tower, designed by Foster + Partners, will feature column-free floor plates and an all-electric, LEED Platinum design.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Property Name: 625 Madison Ave.

Address: 625 Madison Avenue, New York City, NY

Size: 150,000 SF

Completion: 2029

Notable Features: 53-story tower designed by Foster + Partners, featuring Central Park views, column-free floor plates, and an all-electric and LEED Platinum design. Will include a restaurant and luxury retail at its base.

Project Notes: Related Companies purchased the site in 2024 and demolished the existing 1956-vintage building, which previously housed Nabisco and Related's headquarters.
