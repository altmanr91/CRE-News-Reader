Lift Partners picks up trio of Santa Clara industrial buildings for nearly $26M

ACQUISITION

The Real Deal — San Francisco — Tue, 07 Jul 2026 23:14:51 +0000

SUMMARY

Lift Partners has acquired a three-building industrial property in Santa Clara, CA, for approximately $25.8 million from an affiliate of Vantage Data Centers. The 7-acre site, located near San Jose Mineta International Airport, includes a mix of warehouse and service center space. The property's heavy industrial zoning and size make it a notable asset in the region, with potential for significant new development.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Address: 651 Martin Avenue, Santa Clara, CA

Size: 78,000 SF

Sale Price: $25.8M

Site Area: 7 acres

Notable Features: Heavy industrial zoning; located one block west of San Jose Mineta International Airport.

Project Notes: The site was previously proposed for a 468,000-square-foot data center by the seller, but the application was withdrawn. The property can accommodate up to 148,635 square feet of new development.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: Lift Partners

SELLER: Vantage Data Centers

SELLER BROKER: JLL — Erik Hanson

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Silicon Valley is a hub for industrial investment in the Bay Area. Recent transactions include Clarion Partners' $132.3 million acquisition of a 267,100-square-foot industrial building in Fremont, Redco Development's $42 million purchase of a 13-acre former UPS facility in Sunnyvale for redevelopment into a 265,000-square-foot industrial building, and Morgan Stanley Investment Management's $110.3 million acquisition of a two-building, 290,000-square-foot manufacturing property in Fremont.
