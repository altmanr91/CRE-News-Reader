Rexford Planning Up To $2B In Dispositions This Year

Bisnow News Feed — Mon, 27 Jul 2026 17:05:47 -0400

SUMMARY

Market Research / Portfolio Strategy

Rexford Industrial Realty plans to dispose of $1.5 billion to $2 billion of its industrial portfolio in 2026, a significant increase from its earlier guidance. The decision follows an extensive portfolio review that identified approximately 8 million square feet of assets that no longer align with the company's long-term strategy, characterized by limited value creation opportunities, elevated competitive supply, shorter lease durations, and above-market in-place rents. Proceeds from these sales will primarily be used to pay down $1 billion in debt maturing in 2027, enhancing financial flexibility and improving cash flow quality. Despite a Q2 net loss due to a noncash impairment from these planned sales, Rexford executives emphasized their continued conviction in the long-term strength of infill Southern California industrial real estate.

DATA POINTS

Full-year 2026 disposition guidance: $1.5B to $2B

Previous disposition guidance: $400M to $500M

Total portfolio size: 50M SF

Q2 2026 net loss attributable to common stockholders: $506.9M

Debt maturing in 2027: $1B

Percentage of total debt maturing in 2027: 31%

Q2 2026 leases signed: 117 leases for 2.1M SF

Q2 2026 average same-property portfolio occupancy: 95.7%

Q2 2026 rent growth: down 1%

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Industrial markets in Southern California were experiencing positive net absorption and declining vacancy in Q2 2026, though rent growth was down 1% for the quarter.
