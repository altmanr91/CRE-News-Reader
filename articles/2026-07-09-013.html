Transwestern acquires Twin Cities’ Forte Real Estate Partners

ACQUISITION

REJournals — Wed, 08 Jul 2026 16:12:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Transwestern has acquired Forte Real Estate Partners, a prominent commercial real estate firm in the Twin Cities, significantly expanding its service offerings and Midwest market presence. The acquisition integrates 12 new team members specializing in healthcare, office, and industrial tenant advisory, along with a substantial property management portfolio. This strategic move enhances Transwestern's capabilities in navigating complex real estate decisions for clients across various sectors.

DATA POINTS

Project Notes: Forte Real Estate Partners was established in 2021 through the merger of Excelsior Advisory and Paramount Real Estate Corp.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: Transwestern — Emily Nicoll, Managing Director and Minneapolis market leader

SELLER: Forte Real Estate Partners — Steve Brown; Jim Jetland; Philip Simonet

SELLER: Excelsior Advisory

SELLER: Paramount Real Estate Corp
