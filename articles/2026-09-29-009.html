Wells Fargo Signs 20,560 SF Office Lease Renewal in Southwest Austin

LEASE

REBusinessOnline — Tue, 29 Sep 2026 14:45:45 +0000

SUMMARY

Wells Fargo has renewed its 20,560-square-foot office lease within Building I at Uplands Corporate Center in southwest Austin. JLL represented the tenant, while Cushman & Wakefield represented the landlord, Drawbridge Realty, in the transaction.
