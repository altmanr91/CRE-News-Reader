MCB Picks Up 421K-SF Laredo Logistics Facility

ACQUISITION

Connect CRE — Fri, 02 Oct 2026 15:20:56 +0000

SUMMARY

MCB Real Estate, in partnership with Vinecrest Capital, has acquired Pinnacle One, a recently completed 421,182-square-foot Class A industrial cross-dock facility in Laredo, Texas. The property, built by Pure Development in 2024, is fully leased to Source Logistics, which uses the facility to serve its global clientele. The acquisition highlights the strong demand for logistics assets in Laredo, a critical trade hub.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Property Name: Pinnacle One

Address: 1401 Nicolas D. Hachar Road, Laredo, TX

Size: 421,182 SF

Occupancy at Sale: 100.0%

Year Built: 2024

Site Area: 25.82 acres

Notable Features: Class A cross-dock facility with 3,680 SF of office space, 32-foot clear heights, 109 dock-high doors, four drive-in ramps, 173 trailer parking spaces, and 206 car spaces. Located six miles from the World Trade Bridge and 18 miles from the Colombia Bridge.
