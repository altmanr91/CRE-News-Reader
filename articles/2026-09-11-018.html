Logos Faith Tests Modular Construction for Affordable Housing Scale

DEVELOPMENT

Connect CRE — Thu, 10 Sep 2026 21:25:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Logos Faith Development is exploring modular construction as a method to accelerate its affordable housing pipeline, which currently includes over 2,000 units across 30 faith-based partnerships. The non-profit is testing this approach at the Logos-Grant Housing Apartments in South Los Angeles, a project being developed in partnership with Model/Z. The goal is to evaluate if modular construction can deliver more units faster and at a lower cost, potentially accounting for half of Logos Faith's future developments, while still utilizing traditional construction methods for other projects.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Logos-Grant Housing Apartments

Address: 105th Street and Central Avenue, Los Angeles, CA

Notable Features: Project is a test case for modular construction methods.

Project Notes: The project is in the permitting stage, with Ready to Issue (RTI) permits completed in nine months, significantly faster than the typical 18 months.
