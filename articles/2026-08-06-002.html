Prosper and Versluys Close $50M Land Deal for $650M Miami Condo Tower

ACQUISITION

Commercial Observer — Wed, 05 Aug 2026 22:05:22 +0000

SUMMARY

A joint venture between Prosper Group and Versluys Group has acquired a 1-acre development site in Miami's Brickell district for $50 million, with plans to construct a 60-story, 181-unit luxury condominium tower. The project, estimated to cost $650 million, marks the Belgian developer Versluys Group's first venture into the U.S. market. The acquisition was financed with a $30.5 million loan from Vaster.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Address: 66 Southwest Sixth Street, Miami, FL

Units: 181

Sale Price: $50.0M

Loan Amount: $30.5M

Total Project Cost: $650.0M

Site Area: 1 acres

Notable Features: Waterfront site with over 300 feet of water frontage along the Miami River, near the Brickell City Center mixed-use complex.

Project Notes: The acquisition includes three parcels, two of which are adjacent inland spots. The site was under contract for at least eight months.
