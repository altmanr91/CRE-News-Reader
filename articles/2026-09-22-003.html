Ian Michael Klein, Rabina’s Development Head, Wanted to Be ‘a Real Estate Guy’

Commercial Observer — Tue, 22 Sep 2026 12:00:32 +0000

SUMMARY

Feature / Profile

This article profiles Ian Michael Klein, Rabina's head of development and construction, tracing his career path from an architecture background to a prominent role in real estate development. Klein's journey includes significant public-private partnership work at the NYCEDC, where he managed large-scale urban projects, followed by a tenure at Macklowe Properties focusing on complex condominium conversions. His experience in navigating the intricacies of both new construction and adaptive reuse, particularly the financial challenges of conversions, has shaped his approach. At Rabina, he applies this diverse expertise to oversee all development and project execution, notably on the recently completed 520 Fifth Avenue.
