66K-SF Roswell Retail Center Changes Hands

ACQUISITION

Connect CRE — Thu, 08 Oct 2026 14:15:48 +0000

SUMMARY

SouthCoast has acquired the Sweet Apple Village shopping center in Roswell, GA, for $20.25 million from Ziff Real Estate Partners. The 66,000-square-foot retail center, which was 98% leased at the time of sale, features a diverse mix of e-commerce-resistant tenants. This transaction represents the highest sale price for a retail property in the Roswell/Alpharetta submarket in the past four years.
