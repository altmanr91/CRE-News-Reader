In Likely a Culver City Record, Black Equities Buys Multifamily Asset for $106M

ACQUISITION

Commercial Observer — Fri, 10 Jul 2026 19:10:31 +0000

SUMMARY

Black Equities has acquired Access Culver City, a 115-unit mixed-use property with 31,000 square feet of ground-floor retail, for $106 million. The transaction, which involved seller Greystar, is believed to be the largest multifamily deal in Culver City's history. The acquisition highlights Culver City's strong appeal as a multifamily investment market, driven by its concentration of media and technology employers and supportive housing policies.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Culver City is one of Southern California's hottest multifamily investment markets, driven by media and technology employers and pro-development housing policies. The city has more than 4,500 housing units in the pipeline and is attracting significant multifamily investment within Los Angeles County.
