Manhattan’s Office Leasing Reaches Velocity Not Seen Since 2002: Report

Commercial Observer — Wed, 01 Jul 2026 16:17:22 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Office Report

Manhattan's office leasing activity in the first half of 2026 reached levels not seen since 2002, with nearly 23 million square feet leased, indicating a strong market recovery. Demand outpaced tightening supply, leading to a sharp increase in average asking rents, which rose 5.7 percent annually to $78.03 per square foot. Law firms and technology companies were major drivers of this activity, with professional services accounting for 30 percent of the quarter's leasing and AI-related leases surpassing the total for 2025.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Manhattan's office market is experiencing a significant recovery, with leasing velocity in the first half of 2026 being the strongest since 2002. Average asking rents increased by 5.7% annually, the sharpest midyear growth since 2016. The overall availability rate contracted to 13%, and sublease supply has dipped below pre-pandemic levels. Hudson Yards remains the priciest submarket, with average asking rents of $153 per square foot.
