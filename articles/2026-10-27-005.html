Real Estate Operating System HqO Launches AI Platform for Workflows, Data

Commercial Observer — Tue, 27 Oct 2026 12:00:29 +0000

SUMMARY

Non-CRE / Business News

HqO, a real estate operating system provider, has launched REX, an AI platform designed to streamline data and workflows across commercial real estate portfolios. The platform, built on HqO's Digital Grid, aims to provide a unified record for building data, enabling features like AI-powered service request routing, preventive maintenance automation, and proactive tenant health monitoring. REX is now available in 24 countries, with HqO also offering an 'AI builder group' to embed its team within client operations to facilitate adoption and maximize the platform's utility.
