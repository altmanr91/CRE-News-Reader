MasTec acquiring electrical contractor for $1.7B

The Real Deal — Miami — Wed, 08 Jul 2026 18:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Non-CRE / Business News

Infrastructure firm MasTec is set to acquire Ohio-based electrical contractor The Superior Group for $1.7 billion, comprising $1.2 billion in cash and $475 million in stock, with a potential earnout. This strategic acquisition aims to broaden MasTec's service offerings, particularly in specialized electrical work, and enhance its capacity to meet the increasing demand for data centers, power, and mission-critical infrastructure. The deal is expected to close by the end of July.
