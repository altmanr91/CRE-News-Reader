Joint Venture Buys 78-Unit Seniors Housing Complex in Metro Philadelphia

ACQUISITION

REBusinessOnline — Thu, 15 Oct 2026 13:57:47 +0000

SUMMARY

A joint venture between Senior Living Transformation Co. and Sabal Investment Holdings has acquired Daylesford Crossing, a 78-unit seniors housing complex in Paoli, PA. The property, which opened in 2015, offers assisted living and memory care services. Berkadia represented the seller, AEW Capital Management, in the transaction.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: seniors housing

Property Name: Daylesford Crossing

Address: 1450 Lancaster Ave, Paoli, PA*

Units: 78

Year Built: 2015

Notable Features: Offers assisted living and memory care services, with amenities including a media room, wellness room, library, salon, and an onsite healthcare facility.

* Address sourced separately, not in original article.
