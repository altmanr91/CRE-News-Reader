Marcus & Millichap closes sale of 9,767-square-foot retail center in Prosper

SALE

REJournals — Thu, 02 Jul 2026 15:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Marcus & Millichap has brokered the sale of Prosper Center, a 9,767-square-foot multi-tenant retail property in Prosper, Texas. The property, built in 2020, was approximately 65% occupied at the time of sale and features triple-net leases. The buyer, a private investor, intends to enhance the property's value by increasing occupancy and rental rates.

DATA POINTS

Site Area: 1.16 acres
