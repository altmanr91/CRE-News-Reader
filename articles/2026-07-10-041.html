Construction Begins On AmEx's World Trade Center HQ As Campus Enters 'Momentous' New Era

CONSTRUCTION

Bisnow News Feed — Thu, 09 Jul 2026 14:03:54 -0400

SUMMARY

Construction has officially begun on 2 World Trade Center, the final office tower in the World Trade Center campus, which will serve as the future headquarters for American Express. The 55-story, nearly 2 million-square-foot supertall is being developed by Silverstein Properties and is expected to be completed in 2031, at which point American Express will purchase and occupy the building. This groundbreaking marks a significant milestone for Lower Manhattan, symbolizing the area's resilience and continued growth as a global business hub, according to city officials and developers.
