Finish Strong: Building Better Retailer-Contractor Partnerships

REBusinessOnline — Thu, 22 Oct 2026 14:06:25 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

This article, authored by Justin Elder of Elder-Jones, Inc. and the Retail Contractors Association, emphasizes the critical role of strong retailer-contractor partnerships in successful retail construction projects. The author argues that while construction variables are unavoidable, cost and schedule surprises can be mitigated through disciplined preconstruction, clear decision-making, and robust project controls. Key supporting points include the necessity of early contractor involvement for technical completeness, building schedules around retail operations, and establishing measurable communication protocols to ensure early visibility and efficient change management.
