JPMorgan eyes $425M valuation in exit from Fisher Brothers’ Third Avenue office tower

SALE

The Real Deal — New York — Wed, 22 Jul 2026 20:45:00 +0000

SUMMARY

JPMorgan Asset Management is seeking to sell its 49% stake in the 43-story, 1 million-square-foot office tower at 605 Third Avenue in New York City, with Fisher Brothers planning to bring in a new partner and recapitalize the asset. The transaction is targeting a valuation of $425 million, with the new investor partnering with Fisher Brothers to reposition the property and elevate it within the Grand Central competitive set. The building is currently 84% leased, and the business plan anticipates a 60% increase in income over the next five years by re-marking expiring leases.
