St. Louis’ S.M. Wilson & Co. adds K-12 education construction consultant

REJournals — Thu, 29 Oct 2026 16:12:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Promotion

S. M. Wilson & Co. has appointed Will Perkins as K-12 Education Construction Consultant, a strategic move to enhance its engagement with school districts across Missouri. Perkins, with 31 years of experience in public education and a strong background in school administration and superintendency, will focus on introducing the company and assisting districts with their capital project planning. His expertise in budgeting, bond issues, and community campaigns for school projects is expected to be a significant asset.
