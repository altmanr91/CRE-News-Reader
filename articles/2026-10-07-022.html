Soaring Yields Strand REITs In M&A No-Man’s-Land

Bisnow Latest News Feed — Tue, 06 Oct 2026 19:52:09 -0400

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

Rising 10-Year Treasury yields, now exceeding 5%, are creating significant challenges for REITs in the M&A market. This surge in yields is necessitating real-time repricing of deals, effectively stalling large-scale transactions. The current economic environment has placed many potential acquisitions on hold as REITs navigate the increased cost of capital.
