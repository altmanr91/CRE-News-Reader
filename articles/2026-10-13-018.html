Kolter Urban Reveals Plans for 325-Unit St. Pete Condo Project

DEVELOPMENT

Connect CRE — Tue, 13 Oct 2026 14:54:48 +0000

SUMMARY

Kolter Urban has submitted plans for a significant condominium project in St. Petersburg, proposing two 44-story towers with 325 condo units, nearly 18,000 square feet of commercial space, and a 519-space parking garage. This $325 million redevelopment will replace the existing Hilton St. Petersburg Bayfront hotel, which Kolter acquired earlier this year for $96 million. The project underscores continued luxury residential development in the market.
