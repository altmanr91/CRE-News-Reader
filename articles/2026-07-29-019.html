CBRE Arranges Full-Building Industrial Lease in Irvine

LEASE

Connect CRE — Tue, 28 Jul 2026 21:05:26 +0000

SUMMARY

CBRE has arranged a full-building industrial lease for 136,612 square feet at Irvine Commerce Center in Irvine, CA. The Class A industrial property, developed and managed by Marq Logistics on behalf of an Ares Real Estate fund, was leased to an undisclosed tenant. The transaction underscores the sustained demand for high-quality industrial space in Orange County, particularly within the resilient Airport Area industrial market.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Property Name: Irvine Commerce Center

Address: 18582 Teller Ave, Irvine, CA

Size: 136,612 SF

Notable Features: Includes 13,275 square feet of high-end office space with upgraded finishes and modern industrial features, located along Jamboree Road with immediate access to Interstate 405 freeway.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

LANDLORD: Ares Real Estate

DEVELOPER/SPONSOR: Marq Logistics

LEASING AGENT: CBRE — Ross Bourne, EVP; Ross Fippinger, EVP; Joe Czech, Senior Associate
