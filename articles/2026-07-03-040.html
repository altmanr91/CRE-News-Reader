MCA Realty Acquires 138K-SF Seattle-Area Industrial Portfolio

ACQUISITION

Connect CRE — Thu, 02 Jul 2026 19:47:14 +0000

SUMMARY

MCA Realty has acquired the West Valley Distribution Center, a three-building, 138,296-square-foot industrial portfolio in Kent, Washington, from Tamarisk West Valley LLC. The acquisition, made through MCA Realty Industrial Growth Fund II, expands MCA Realty's industrial holdings in the Seattle metropolitan area. The new owner plans to implement significant exterior and interior improvements, including reconfiguring suites, to capitalize on the strong tenant demand and infill location of the Kent Valley market.
