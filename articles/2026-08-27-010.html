Cross Street, Mavrek Begin Leasing 46-Unit Apartment Building in Chicago

LEASE

REBusinessOnline — Thu, 27 Aug 2026 14:44:48 +0000

SUMMARY

Cross Street and Mavrek have commenced leasing for Belmora, a 46-unit apartment building in Chicago's Lakeview neighborhood. The property also features ground-floor retail space. A second, identical building is slated for completion later this year, adding another 46 apartments and retail space to the development.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Belmora

Address: 919 W. Belmont Ave, Chicago, IL

Units: 46

Notable Features: Includes 5,700 square feet of ground-floor retail space; amenities include a fitness center, coworking space, package room, bicycle storage, and a furnished lobby lounge.

Project Notes: A second building at 917 W. Belmont Ave. will be completed later in 2026, adding 46 apartments and 5,300 square feet of ground-floor retail.
