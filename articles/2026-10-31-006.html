Queens’ Tallest Tower Reaches 90% Leased, Adds First Retail Tenants

LEASE

Connect CRE — Fri, 30 Oct 2026 22:04:09 +0000

SUMMARY

The Orchard, a 70-story residential tower in Long Island City, Queens, has reached over 90% leased for its 824 residential units within nine months of launching. The development also secured its first three retail tenants, totaling more than 7,468 square feet, which are expected to open in 2027. This rapid leasing success underscores strong demand for the mixed-use project, which aims to serve both residents and the wider Long Island City community.
