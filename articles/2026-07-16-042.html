Switch's Planned IPO Extends Data Center Sector's Shift To Wall Street

Bisnow News Feed — Wed, 15 Jul 2026 16:43:17 -0400

SUMMARY

Market Research / Capital Markets

Data center developer and operator Switch is planning an initial public offering (IPO) that could raise up to $10 billion, valuing the company at nearly $80 billion. This move signals a broader industry trend of digital infrastructure companies returning to public markets for growth capital, following a period where many were taken private. Switch, known for its large-scale, renewable energy-powered data center campuses across multiple U.S. markets, previously went public in 2017 before being acquired by DigitalBridge and IFM Investors in 2022. The current shift back to public offerings is driven by the increasing demand for data centers, particularly for AI infrastructure, and the need for firms to access additional capital beyond private funding sources.
