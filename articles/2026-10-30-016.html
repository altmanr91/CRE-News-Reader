Walmart Advancing $400M San Antonio-Area Distribution Center

CONSTRUCTION

Connect CRE — Fri, 30 Oct 2026 14:45:11 +0000

SUMMARY

Walmart is proceeding with a $400 million distribution center in Cibolo, a suburb of San Antonio, Texas. The 744,000-square-foot facility is expected to break ground early next year and be completed by the third quarter of 2029. The city of Cibolo has approved significant tax abatements and rebates, totaling approximately $32.5 million, to support the project.
