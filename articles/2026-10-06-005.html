Gravity Fitness Signs 23K-SF Lease at Queens’ Flushing Plaza

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Mon, 05 Oct 2026 19:14:46 +0000

SUMMARY

Gravity Fitness has signed a long-term lease for 23,000 square feet at Flushing Plaza in Queens, New York. The athletic club will occupy the ground floor of the two-story office and retail complex, with plans to open in mid-2027. This lease underscores the demand for health and wellness options in the thriving Downtown Flushing community, which features a diverse mix of commercial and retail offerings.
