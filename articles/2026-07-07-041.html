45-Unit Garden Apartment Property Trades in LA’s Palms Neighborhood

SALE

Connect CRE — Mon, 06 Jul 2026 21:13:47 +0000

SUMMARY

A 45-unit garden apartment community in Los Angeles's Palms neighborhood, 3717 Cardiff Ave., has been sold for $13.125 million. The property, which had been family-owned since the mid-1970s, was acquired by a local private investor. The sale represented a rare opportunity in a submarket where properties of this size are infrequently available, attracting significant buyer interest and multiple offers.
