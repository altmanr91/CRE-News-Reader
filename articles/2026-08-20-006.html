CBRE Arranges $45.8 Million Loan for Refinancing of Scottsdale Towne Center

REFINANCE

REBusinessOnline — Wed, 19 Aug 2026 14:40:00 +0000

SUMMARY

CBRE has secured a $45.8 million, five-year, fixed-rate, full-term interest-only loan for the refinancing of Scottsdale Towne Center, a nearly fully leased power center in Scottsdale, Arizona. The owner acquired the property in 2021.
