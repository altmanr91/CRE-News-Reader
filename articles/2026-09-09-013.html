Newmark closes sale of 1,027-unit multifamily portfolio in North Austin

SALE

REJournals — Wed, 09 Sep 2026 13:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Newmark has successfully brokered the sale of The Silicon Hills Trio, a three-property multifamily portfolio totaling 1,027 units in North Austin, to Knightvest Capital. The portfolio, built between 2001 and 2004, offers a significant value-add opportunity through renovations and operational improvements. Its strategic location in Austin's technology corridor provides residents with access to major employers, retail, dining, and entertainment.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: The Silicon Hills Trio

Address: 10015 Lake Creek Pkwy, Austin, TX*

Units: 1,027

Notable Features: Located in North Austin's technology corridor, approximately five miles north of The Domain and near Lakeline Mall, with convenient access to major thoroughfares.

Project Notes: The portfolio comprises The Enclave at La Frontera (411 units), Lakeside at La Frontera (366 units), and Legends Lake Creek (250 units). Built between 2001 and 2004, the properties offer value-add potential through interior renovations and community enhancements.

* Address sourced separately, not in original article.
