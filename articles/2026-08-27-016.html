Trademark Acquires 116K-SF Austin Shopping Center

ACQUISITION

Connect CRE — Thu, 27 Aug 2026 18:32:53 +0000

SUMMARY

Trademark Property Company, in partnership with Cohen & Steers, has acquired Oak Hill Plaza, a 116,000-square-foot neighborhood shopping center in Austin, TX, from Partners Capital. Trademark will oversee property management, leasing, and a planned upgrade to enhance the tenant mix and modernize the center. The acquisition is strategically timed with the near completion of the $674 million Oak Hill Parkway Project, which is expected to significantly improve accessibility to the property.
