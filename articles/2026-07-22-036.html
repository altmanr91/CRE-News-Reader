Sasha Bass steps down as CEO of Fort Worth’s Sundance Square amid ongoing criticism of her management

The Real Deal — Texas — Tue, 21 Jul 2026 17:09:43 +0000

SUMMARY

Promotion

Sasha Bass is stepping down as CEO of Sundance Square, a prominent 37-block mixed-use district in downtown Fort Worth, after a five-year tenure marked by efforts to expand the company's operational scope and increase foot traffic through live events. Former Fort Worth Police Chief Ed Kraus, who joined Sundance Square in 2019 and became vice president in 2020, will succeed her as president and CEO on September 1. Bass's leadership faced criticism regarding high rents and a perceived lack of effective retail and restaurant revitalization post-pandemic, leading to significant storefront vacancies. In a strategic shift, Sundance Square recently engaged JLL to manage retail leasing and LanCarte Commercial for office properties, moving away from its previous in-house leasing approach.
