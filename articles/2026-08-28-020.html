Advertising Agency Havas Health Expands to 250K-Plus SF in Grand Central District

LEASE

Connect CRE — Fri, 28 Aug 2026 19:59:59 +0000

SUMMARY

Havas Health, an advertising agency, has significantly expanded its headquarters at 200 Madison Avenue in New York City's Grand Central district. The firm added 64,657 square feet, bringing its total footprint to 254,118 square feet across five floors. This expansion is coupled with a 15-year lease extension, securing Havas Health's presence in the building through 2041. The landlord noted that the long-term commitment reflects the appeal of the tower's amenity-rich environment.
