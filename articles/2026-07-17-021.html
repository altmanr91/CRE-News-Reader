Jed Walentas, Tom Brady’s ex-manager abruptly settle legal battle over Bahamas mansion

The Real Deal — New York — Thu, 16 Jul 2026 20:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

CRE Legal / Regulatory

A legal dispute between Jed Walentas, CEO of Two Trees Management, and Will McDonough, Tom Brady's former manager, over a hurricane-damaged luxury rental property in the Bahamas has been abruptly settled. Walentas had been attempting to collect a $300,000 arbitration award from McDonough, who allegedly refused to pay his share of repair costs for the jointly developed mansion. The settlement was reached shortly after a Manhattan Supreme Court Justice authorized Walentas to serve McDonough via email, marking a resolution to the year-long collection effort.
