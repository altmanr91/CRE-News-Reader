This article profiles Ian Slater, co-founder of Trove Partners, a residential real estate team affiliated with Compass. Slater, a top broker with over $1 billion in sales, discusses his career highlights, including a $112.5 million sale in Southampton and two $29 million sales in New York City. He also shares insights into building Trove Partners into a leading team in New York state.
