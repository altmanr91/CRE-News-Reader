Comstock Obtains $299.1M Refinancing for Office Towers at Reston Station in Northern Virginia

REFINANCE

REBusinessOnline — Mon, 02 Nov 2026 15:47:25 +0000

SUMMARY

Comstock Holding Cos. Inc. has secured a $299.1 million refinancing package for three office towers at its Reston Station mixed-use development in Northern Virginia. The financing, arranged by Eastdil Secured Savills, was provided by Bank of America and used to repay an existing loan from Blackstone Real Estate Debt Strategies. The office towers, which are part of a 90-acre transit-oriented development, were fully leased at the time of the refinancing.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Property Name: 1900, 1902 and 1906 Reston Metro Plaza

Loan Amount: $299.1M

Occupancy at Close: 100.0%

Notable Features: Part of a 90-acre mixed-use development surrounding the Wiehle-Reston East Metro Station.

Project Notes: Refinancing used to fully repay a $292.9 million loan provided by Blackstone Real Estate Debt Strategies.
