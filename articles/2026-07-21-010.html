Wellpointe to Develop $2B Affordable Seniors Housing Campus in Los Angeles

DEVELOPMENT

REBusinessOnline — Mon, 20 Jul 2026 14:30:50 +0000

SUMMARY

Wellpointe is developing Viva L.A. Warner Center, a $2 billion affordable seniors housing campus within the Warner Center master-planned district in Los Angeles. The project, which will be developed in four phases, will feature four high-rise towers totaling 3,192 units and 2.2 million square feet of space, including non-residential components. This development is noted as the largest affordable housing project under Mayor Karen Bass' Executive Directive 1, which aims to accelerate affordable housing development.
