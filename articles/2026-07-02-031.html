Stripe set to more than double office space to 222K sf in River North high-rise

LEASE

The Real Deal — Chicago — Wed, 01 Jul 2026 21:15:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Stripe, an online payments solutions company, is significantly expanding its Chicago presence by leasing over 222,000 square feet at 350 North Orleans Street in River North. This new lease more than doubles their previous footprint in the building and will be occupied in phases as the company adds new hires. The expansion provides a boost to the property, which has faced challenges including a loan default by its previous owner.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Address: 350 North Orleans Street, Chicago, IL

Size: 222,000 SF

Current Occupancy: 59.9%

Project Notes: Stripe's new lease more than doubles its previous 89,000 square feet in the building. The property's previous owner, Blackstone, defaulted on a $310 million loan.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

The office building at 350 North Orleans Street was just under 60 percent occupied last week. Bids for the building are in the $90 million to $100 million range, substantially less than the $378 million Blackstone paid in 2015. Another nearby property at 500 West Monroe Street sold for a 76 percent discount from its previous sale price.
