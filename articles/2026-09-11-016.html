Consumer Prices Rise 0.4% in August as Gasoline Costs Accelerate

Connect CRE — Fri, 11 Sep 2026 13:03:19 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Labor Report

U.S. consumer prices increased by 0.4% in August, driven primarily by a significant rise in gasoline and other energy costs. The Consumer Price Index (CPI) saw a 3.4% annual increase through August, consistent with July's pace. While core CPI, excluding food and energy, rose 0.3% for the month and 2.4% annually, the acceleration in energy prices was the main factor contributing to the overall headline inflation pressure. Shelter costs, a substantial component of the CPI, also saw a 0.3% increase for the month.
