The Plan: Stark Office Suites’ 717 Fifth Avenue Is Open to a Long-Term Relationship

Commercial Observer — Wed, 28 Oct 2026 10:00:25 +0000

SUMMARY

Development

Flexible workspace provider Stark Office Suites has opened its newest location, Excelsior by Stark, on the 14th floor of 717 Fifth Avenue in New York City. The 17,291-square-foot space, which features 27 private offices and a custom staircase, is designed for longer-term client relationships seeking flexibility rather than traditional coworking. The CEO emphasized a focus on private office space for individual clients, with arrangements typically spanning one to five years. The new facility, completed in August and already 60 percent leased, offers high-end finishes, natural light, and access to the building's 8,000-square-foot amenity floor, including a conference center and cafe.
