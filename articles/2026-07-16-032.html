Los Altos Hills estate sells for less than half of its original $88M list price

SALE

The Real Deal — San Francisco — Wed, 15 Jul 2026 23:23:39 +0000

SUMMARY

A Hong Kong-based buyer, Evergrowth Holdings, has acquired a sprawling 21,000-square-foot estate in Los Altos Hills, California, for $30 million. The seller was tech entrepreneur Kumar Malavalli and his wife, Vijaya Malavalli, who had originally listed the property for $88 million in 2015. The sale price, significantly below its initial asking, reflects a recalibration in the Silicon Valley luxury market, though the listing agent believes it could signal renewed momentum driven by anticipated tech IPOs.
