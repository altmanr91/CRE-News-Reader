Helping to meet the need: Sherman Associates project to bring mixed-income development to Rochester’s Destination Medical Center

DEVELOPMENT

REJournals — Tue, 08 Sep 2026 13:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Sherman Associates has closed on financing for a $136.5 million mixed-income multifamily development in downtown Rochester, MN. The project, located on the former Rochester Civic Center North Lot, will feature 349 new homes across two buildings: a 273-unit market-rate tower and a 76-unit affordable senior housing building. This development aims to address Rochester's significant housing shortage, particularly for the growing workforce associated with the Mayo Clinic's expansion and the Destination Medical Center initiative. The project is a public-private partnership with the City of Rochester and Destination Medical Center, and it will also include a publicly accessible walking path along the Zumbro River.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Address: 217 E. Center St, Rochester, MN

Units: 349

Total Project Cost: $136.5M

$/Unit: $391,117/unit (calculated)

Completion: spring 2028

Notable Features: Located along the Zumbro River and next to the Mayo Clinic campus, with a publicly accessible walking path along the river and proximity to downtown amenities and the Civic Center Link Bus Rapid Transit station.

Project Notes: The development includes 221 traditional market-rate apartments and 52 fully furnished short-term residences, alongside 76 affordable senior apartments. It is a public-private partnership with the City of Rochester and Destination Medical Center.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Rochester's housing market is struggling to keep pace with demand, with multifamily vacancy rates in the low 3% range, indicating a significant shortage of housing units. The Mayo Clinic's ongoing $5 billion campus expansion is expected to generate additional workforce growth and further demand for housing.
