JLL Arranges Loan for Refinancing of 300-Unit Apartment Community in Northwest Dallas

REFINANCE

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 09 Oct 2026 16:39:13 +0000

SUMMARY

JLL has arranged refinancing for Standard at Royal Lane, a 300-unit apartment community in northwest Dallas. The newly constructed property, developed by LDG Development, features a mix of one- to four-bedroom units, some with income restrictions. The loan was provided by TPG Real Estate Credit.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Standard at Royal Lane

Units: 300

Site Area: 12.4 acres

Notable Features: Amenities include two pools, dual fitness centers, a business center, and a theater room. Some units are subject to income restrictions.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

SPONSOR: LDG Development

LENDER: TPG Real Estate Credit

MORTGAGE BROKER: JLL — Kyle Spencer; Dan Kearns; Christian Johnston; Aidan Flanagan
