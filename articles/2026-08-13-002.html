Blackstone Snaps Up Ken Griffin’s 125 Worth Avenue in Palm Beach for $86M

ACQUISITION

Commercial Observer — Wed, 12 Aug 2026 20:30:15 +0000

SUMMARY

Blackstone Real Estate has acquired 125 Worth Avenue, a three-story office and retail building in Palm Beach, from billionaire Ken Griffin for $86 million in an all-cash transaction. The property, which Griffin had owned since 2023, spans approximately 50,000 square feet. Blackstone plans to renovate the building's retail and office components to enhance its alignment with the luxury retail market of Worth Avenue, a strategy consistent with the firm's global focus on high-street retail assets.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Worth Avenue in Palm Beach is characterized by extreme supply scarcity and high demand from affluent residents, leading to soaring property valuations. Acadia Realty Trust acquired 225 Worth Avenue for $43 million, or over $4,329 per square foot, in March 2026.
