University Retirement Communities Make the Grade

REBusinessOnline — Thu, 02 Jul 2026 15:45:24 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

University Retirement Communities (URCs) are gaining significant traction across the U.S., with over 80 currently operating, according to industry expert Andrew Carle. These communities, which integrate senior living with university campuses, are seen as a unique and economically sound model within seniors housing. Despite the initial perception of an "odd couple" pairing, developers, designers, and operators are enthusiastic about the growth potential, citing strong demand and positive resident experiences. The intergenerational aspect is highlighted as a key draw, offering health benefits for seniors and a vibrant environment for visiting families.
