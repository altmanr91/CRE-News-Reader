Lightstone Capital Provides Loan for Refinancing of Metro Houston Industrial Building

REFINANCE

REBusinessOnline — Wed, 09 Sep 2026 15:22:14 +0000

SUMMARY

Lightstone Capital, the debt financing arm of Lightstone Group, has provided a loan for the refinancing of an industrial building located in Channelview, an eastern suburb of Houston. The transaction was part of a larger debt origination package that included two multifamily properties in Southern California, totaling $70 million across all three loans. The specific loan amount for the industrial property was not disclosed.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Address: 300 S. Sheldon Road, Channelview, TX

Size: 180,000 SF

Year Built: 2003
