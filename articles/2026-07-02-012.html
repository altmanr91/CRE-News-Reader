Renewed growth in apartment rents … but not everywhere

REJournals — Thu, 02 Jul 2026 10:01:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Rent Report

National apartment rents are showing signs of renewed growth, with median asking rents for U.S. one-bedroom apartments rising 0.5% in June from May to $1,526 a month, and two-bedroom rents increasing 0.1% to $1,905 monthly. This marks the first annual increase in one-bedroom rents in over a year, up 0.4% from June of last year, indicating a stabilization after a period of softening. The report highlights that supply remains the primary driver of rent performance, with markets delivering new units experiencing softer rents, while those with limited inventory see stronger growth. San Francisco exemplifies this trend with record-high rents and leading annual growth, contrasting with markets in Arizona that continue to report annual declines due to new supply.
