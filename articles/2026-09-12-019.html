OPEN Impact Arranges Coney Island Ground Lease for Charter School

LEASE

Connect CRE — Fri, 11 Sep 2026 20:46:13 +0000

SUMMARY

OPEN Impact Real Estate has arranged a 39-year leasehold condominium for Coney Island Preparatory Public Charter School, enabling the development of a new 45,190-square-foot high school at 773 Neptune Ave. in Brooklyn. This build-to-suit facility, constructed by Rybak Development, will allow the charter school to expand its high school enrollment to over 500 students and create new specialty instructional spaces. Occupancy is anticipated in summer 2027.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: school

Address: 773 Neptune Ave, Brooklyn, NY

Size: 45,190 SF

Completion: Summer 2027

Notable Features: Ground-up, build-to-suit facility for exclusive occupancy by the charter school.

Project Notes: The new campus will allow the school to grow to serve more than 500 high school students, relocating its existing high school from 294 Avenue T in Gravesend.
