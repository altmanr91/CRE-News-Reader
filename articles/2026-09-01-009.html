Freestone Capital Acquires 106-Unit Multifamily Property in Seattle for $32.2M

ACQUISITION

REBusinessOnline — Tue, 01 Sep 2026 13:49:51 +0000

SUMMARY

Freestone Capital has acquired Aperture on Fifth, a 106-unit multifamily property in Seattle's Denny Triangle neighborhood, from PrivatePortfolio Group for $32.2 million. The transaction was brokered by Institutional Property Advisors, representing both the seller and the buyer, both of whom are based in Seattle.

DATA POINTS

Property Name: Aperture on Fifth

Address: 206 5th Ave N, Seattle, WA*

Units: 106

Sale Price: $32.2M

$/Unit: $303,774/unit (calculated)

Year Built: 2014

Notable Features: Features a central atrium courtyard, rooftop deck with a bocce ball court, fitness center, bike storage, package lockers, pet wash area, and underground parking garage. Some units offer views of the Space Needle and downtown Seattle skyline.
