Starwood Lands $483M CMBS Loan to Refi Single-Family Rental Portfolio

REFINANCE

Commercial Observer — Mon, 17 Aug 2026 21:35:36 +0000

SUMMARY

Starwood Asset Management has secured a $482.5 million CMBS loan to refinance a portfolio of 1,749 single-family rental homes spread across 10 states. The floating-rate, interest-only financing was originated by Nomura Securities and is scheduled to mature in August 2028, with options for three 12-month extensions. The portfolio, which Starwood assembled between 2021 and 2022 for $674 million, was over 96% leased in April.
