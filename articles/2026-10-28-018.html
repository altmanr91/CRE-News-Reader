Newmark Arranges $108M Sale of 310-Unit Virginia Multifamily Community

SALE

Connect CRE — Wed, 28 Oct 2026 14:19:23 +0000

SUMMARY

Newmark has facilitated the $108 million sale of Bainbridge Midlothian, a 310-unit multifamily community in Midlothian, Virginia. Frontier Group of Companies acquired the property from a joint venture between The Bainbridge Companies and BVT National Partners, marking Frontier's entry into the Virginia multifamily market. The transaction highlights the continued strong demand and premium valuations for high-quality, well-operated multifamily assets, even in the current capital markets climate.
