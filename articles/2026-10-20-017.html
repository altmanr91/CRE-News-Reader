Amazon Leases 91K-SF Warehouse in Everett

LEASE

Connect CRE — Mon, 19 Oct 2026 21:32:09 +0000

SUMMARY

Amazon has signed a new lease for a 90,970-square-foot warehouse in Everett, WA, to support its Leo satellite division. This marks Amazon's return to a facility it previously occupied, which had been vacant since 2022. The property's proximity to Amazon's existing 167,000-square-foot logistics hub for its orbital satellite division makes it a strategic location for the company's expanding low Earth orbit satellite broadband network.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Address: 6611 Associated Blvd, Everett, WA

Size: 90,970 SF

Notable Features: Located near Amazon's 167,000-square-foot logistics hub for its orbital satellite division.

Project Notes: Amazon previously occupied this facility from 2018 until 2024.
