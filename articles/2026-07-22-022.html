Braemar sells Key West resort amid Ashford split

SALE

The Real Deal — Miami — Tue, 21 Jul 2026 23:35:15 +0000

SUMMARY

Braemar Hotels & Resorts has agreed to sell the oceanfront Pier House Resort & Spa in Key West for $190 million, or $1.3 million per key. The 142-key hotel, which Braemar acquired in 2014 for $93 million, is being sold to an undisclosed buyer as part of a broader asset disposition strategy. This sale, along with other luxury hotel dispositions, is intended to fund a $480 million termination fee associated with Braemar's separation from its long-time manager, Ashford Incorporated, as the REIT transitions to a self-managed structure.
