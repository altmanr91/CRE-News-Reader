Air taxi operator grows in Silicon Valley with 501K sf Morgan Hill lease

LEASE

The Real Deal — San Francisco — Tue, 14 Jul 2026 23:03:22 +0000

SUMMARY

Archer Aviation, an air taxi manufacturer, has significantly expanded its Silicon Valley footprint with a 501,000-square-foot industrial lease at the Cochrane Technology Center in Morgan Hill. This move supports the company's plans to ramp up production for its first passenger flights later this year and its expansion into military aircraft. The developer, Trammell Crow Company, completed the five-building complex in 2024, anticipating increased demand for industrial space in south Santa Clara County.
