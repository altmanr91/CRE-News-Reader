Ashkenazy defaults on $40M loan tied to Union Square retail

The Real Deal — San Francisco — Wed, 22 Jul 2026 20:30:00 +0000

SUMMARY

REO / Foreclosure

Ashkenazy Acquisitions is facing potential foreclosure on two Union Square retail properties in San Francisco due to a default on a $40 million loan. The properties, 1 Grant Avenue and 156 Geary Street, are struggling with vacancies and upcoming tenant departures, exacerbating the challenges of expensive debt in a recovering but still difficult retail market. The firm has 90 days to repay the outstanding debt of $41.1 million, though a loan modification or other resolution is still possible.

Union Square has faced challenges with major retail departures post-pandemic, but is showing signs of recovery with new experiential concepts and recent investment, including a $48.5 million deal at 180 Post Street.
