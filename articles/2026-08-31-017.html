Developer Team Begins Work on Mansfield Midrise

CONSTRUCTION

Connect CRE — Mon, 31 Aug 2026 15:35:10 +0000

SUMMARY

Admiral Legacy Investments and Foundry Commercial have commenced construction on the Mansfield Midrise, a five-story, 152,894-square-foot office building in Mansfield, TX. This $57.5 million initial phase is part of the larger Mansfield Innovation Community, an 18-acre mixed-use campus. The project is expected to be completed in October 2027, with Keystone National Group providing $45.3 million in financing and Admiral Legacy Investments contributing $6.2 million in equity.
