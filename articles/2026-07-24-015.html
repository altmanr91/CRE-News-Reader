Norton Rose Fulbright expands by 20,000 square feet in downtown Houston office building

LEASE

REJournals — Thu, 23 Jul 2026 15:02:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Norton Rose Fulbright has expanded its presence at the Norton Rose Fulbright Tower in downtown Houston, adding approximately 20,000 square feet to its existing footprint, bringing its total to 139,000 square feet. This expansion reflects the firm's commitment to client service and talent recruitment, providing enhanced collaborative workspaces and amenities. The building's developer noted that the expansion reinforces the demand for thoughtfully designed, amenity-rich office environments in downtown Houston, with the tower now 63% occupied and over 70% leased.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Property Name: Norton Rose Fulbright Tower

Address: 1550 Lamar St, Houston, TX

Current Occupancy: 63.0%

Notable Features: LEED Platinum, WELL Platinum, and WiredScore Platinum certifications; features hospitality-inspired amenities including a coffee shop, restaurant, fitness facilities, and a rooftop venue called The Summit with landscaped gardens.
