Agora Starting Last Phase of $375M N. Las Vegas Development

CONSTRUCTION

Connect CRE — Mon, 05 Oct 2026 16:15:48 +0000

SUMMARY

Agora Realty has commenced construction on the third and final phase of Hylo Park, a 73-acre master-planned mixed-use community in North Las Vegas. This $375 million, 25-acre phase will create a youth sports and sports tourism-focused entertainment district, featuring a multi-sport indoor field house, an existing arena, a sports academy, retail, restaurants, and a 177-key Hilton Embassy Suites. The project is slated for completion in late 2027.
