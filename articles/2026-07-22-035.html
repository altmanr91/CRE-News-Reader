Foxconn to pour $72M into Apple AI server manufacturing facility in Houston

CONSTRUCTION

The Real Deal — Texas — Tue, 21 Jul 2026 17:30:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Foxconn, a Taiwanese electronics manufacturer, is investing $71.9 million to expand its AI server manufacturing facility in Houston, TX. The project, located at 8718 Fairbanks North Houston Road, will add over 74,000 square feet of production area, offices, and a break room, with completion expected in early 2027. This expansion builds on Foxconn's 2025 acquisition of the buildings for $142 million, which are leased to its subsidiary, Q-Edge, for the production of AI servers and motherboards for Apple data centers.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Texas is becoming a hub for AI manufacturing and data centers, with a report suggesting it could have the most data centers in the nation by 2030. Major investments include Elon Musk's projects in Austin and Central Texas, Meta's $10 billion data center in El Paso, and Project Big Pine in Texarkana. Governor Greg Abbott has recently advocated for state-level regulations and a ban on data center developments in rural communities, a shift from his previous pro-development stance.
