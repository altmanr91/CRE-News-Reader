Marcus & Millichap closes sale of Fogo de Chao restaurant in Katy

SALE

REJournals — Fri, 21 Aug 2026 14:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Marcus & Millichap has brokered the sale of a net-leased Fogo de Chao restaurant in Katy, Texas, for $7.5 million. The property, built in 2017, was acquired by a California-based investor as part of an all-cash 1031 exchange, with the buyer intending to hold it as a long-term investment. The seller was a developer.
