Planet Fitness to Open at Sky Three Residences in West Brighton Beach

LEASE

Connect CRE — Tue, 25 Aug 2026 21:45:10 +0000

SUMMARY

Planet Fitness has secured a 14,520-square-foot retail lease at the base of Tower 3 within the Sky Three Residences in Brooklyn's West Brighton Beach. The new fitness center, expected to open in early 2027, will enhance the retail offerings along the Neptune Avenue corridor, complementing a diverse mix of existing tenants.
