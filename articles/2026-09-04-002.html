Reuben Brothers’ Jordana Yechiel On the Firm’s Big Bet on Century City

Commercial Observer — Thu, 03 Sep 2026 20:25:42 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

Reuben Brothers is making a significant investment in Century City, Los Angeles, transforming the area around the Fairmont Century Plaza hotel and Park Elm condominiums into a luxury-driven, mixed-use destination. The firm's strategy involves layering in residential, experiential, and luxury offerings, including restaurants, galleries, wellness facilities, and a revamped public plaza. This approach is driven by a belief that the way people want to live in Los Angeles has shifted, with a desire for more integrated, round-the-clock environments, drawing inspiration from successful transformations like Dubai's International Financial Centre. The firm is focused on curating tenants and amenities to create a holistic destination rather than simply maximizing occupancy or rent.
