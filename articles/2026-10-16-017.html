Culver City Mixed-Use Secures $133M Bank OZK Loan

LOAN

Connect CRE — Thu, 15 Oct 2026 20:27:09 +0000

SUMMARY

Lincoln Property Company and McCourt Partners have secured $132.7 million in construction financing from Bank OZK for ElevenOneEleven, a new mixed-use development in Culver City. The project will feature 344 residences, including a significant portion of affordable units, alongside retail space and publicly accessible outdoor areas. This financing is a crucial step in advancing the development, reflecting the partners' confidence in the project's contribution to the Culver City community.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: mixed-use

Property Name: ElevenOneEleven

Address: 11111 Jefferson Blvd., Culver City, CA

Units: 344

Loan Amount: $132.7M

Loan Per Unit: $385,756/unit (calculated)

Notable Features: Includes 52 affordable units and 17,520 square feet of publicly accessible outdoor space.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

SPONSOR: McCourt Partners
