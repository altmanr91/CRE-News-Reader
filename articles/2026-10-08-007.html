BWE Provides $28.3 Million Permanent Loan for Columbus-Area Senior Living Community

LOAN

REBusinessOnline — Thu, 08 Oct 2026 15:41:55 +0000

SUMMARY

BWE has provided a $28.3 million permanent loan for Grove City Senior Living, a 124-unit senior living community in Grove City, Ohio. The financing, originated through Fannie Mae, will be used to retire existing construction debt and provide an equity return to the Ohio-based developer. The property, which opened in May 2023 and has achieved full occupancy, secured a 10-year term with three years of interest-only payments and a 100 percent loan-to-cost ratio, reflecting its strong market fundamentals.
