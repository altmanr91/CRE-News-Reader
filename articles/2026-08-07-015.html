Marcus & Millichap closes $3.55 million sale of retail property in Sussex

SALE

REJournals — Thu, 06 Aug 2026 15:49:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Marcus & Millichap has brokered the sale of Sussex Gateway Retail Center, a nine-suite retail property in Sussex, Wisconsin, for $3.55 million. The property consists of two multi-tenant retail buildings totaling approximately 24,834 square feet. The center benefits from high traffic counts and excellent visibility due to its location across from major retailers.
