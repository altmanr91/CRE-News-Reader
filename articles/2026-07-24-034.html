Viceroy signs onto Austin’s new $235M luxury condo project with branding rights

DEVELOPMENT

The Real Deal — Texas — Thu, 23 Jul 2026 21:06:10 +0000

SUMMARY

Pearlstone Partners has secured Viceroy Residences to brand its 146-unit luxury condominium project in Austin, marking the city's first branded condo complex not attached to a hotel. The $235 million development, which broke ground in March 2025, will offer a range of wellness-focused amenities. The project aims to attract buyers seeking larger, high-end units in a walkable downtown-adjacent location, with sales expected to launch in September.
