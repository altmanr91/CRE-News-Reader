Newmark Arranges $125M Sale of 352K-SF Deer Park Town Center

SALE

Connect CRE — Wed, 30 Sep 2026 13:54:33 +0000

SUMMARY

Newmark has arranged the $125 million sale of Deer Park Town Center, a 352,257-square-foot open-air lifestyle center in Deer Park, Illinois. The property, built in 2000, was approximately 84.6% occupied at the time of sale. This transaction represented a rare opportunity to acquire a dominant open-air lifestyle asset in a highly desirable retail trade area.
