80-Unit Seniors Housing Development Opens in Suburban Chicago

DEVELOPMENT

Connect CRE — Fri, 30 Oct 2026 21:37:36 +0000

SUMMARY

Leyden Senior Apartments, an 80-unit independent senior living development, has opened in Franklin Park, IL. Developed by Evergreen Real Estate Group, the four-story building offers one- and two-bedroom residences and is complemented by the adjacent Carl Fiorito Senior Center. The project was designed with accessible floor plans and energy-efficient features, with Evergreen also managing leasing and property management.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: senior housing

Property Name: Leyden Senior Apartments

Address: 2506 N. Mannheim Road, Franklin Park, IL

Units: 80

Notable Features: Adjacent to the newly constructed Carl Fiorito Senior Center, accessible floor plans, energy-efficient appliances, on-site amenities.

Project Notes: The Carl Fiorito Senior Center will be available to the entire community.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

EQUITY/FINANCING: Bank of America

EQUITY/FINANCING: KeyBanc Capital Markets

EQUITY/FINANCING: Hudson Housing Capital

EQUITY/FINANCING: Cook County Department of Planning and Development

EQUITY/FINANCING: Mesirow Financial

FINANCING

Funding partners include Illinois Housing Development Authority, Bank of America, KeyBanc Capital Markets, Hudson Housing Capital, Leyden Township, and Cook County Department of Planning and Development. Mesirow Financial participated in the underwriting syndicate for IHDA financing and served as lead underwriter for Leyden Township’s general obligation bond offering.
