ARCTRUST, Edison Equity Teaming Up on 175-Unit Austin-Area 55+ Community

CONSTRUCTION

Connect CRE — Fri, 30 Oct 2026 14:28:57 +0000

SUMMARY

ARCTRUST Private Capital and Edison Equity Residential have broken ground on Serenova at Dripping Springs, a 175-unit, age-restricted community in Dripping Springs, TX. The project secured a construction loan from North River Partners in August. The community will feature a mix of apartment homes and private villas, with first residents anticipated in Fall 2028.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Serenova at Dripping Springs

Address: 201 Headwaters Blvd, Dripping Springs, TX*

Units: 175

Completion: Fall 2028

Notable Features: 55+ age-restricted community, located adjacent to the Headwaters master-planned community, offering concierge services and amenities including a chef-curated restaurant, fitness centers, pool, and creative arts studio.

Project Notes: Construction loan closed in August.
