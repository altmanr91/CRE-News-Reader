Prudential splits Dallas presence with leases at Trammell Crow Center, 23Springs

LEASE

The Real Deal — Texas — Mon, 06 Jul 2026 19:45:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Prudential Financial is expanding its Dallas presence by signing new office leases at two prominent locations: the Trammell Crow Center in Downtown Dallas and the 23Springs office high-rise in Uptown. The company will occupy the 34th floor of the Trammell Crow Center, encompassing 25,000 square feet, and a 12,900-square-foot space at 23Springs. These moves represent a strategic relocation from its existing offices on Ross Avenue, with significant retrofits planned for both new spaces.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Property Name: Trammell Crow Center

Address: 2001 Ross Avenue, Dallas, TX

Size: 25,000 SF

Current Occupancy: 92.0%

Notable Features: Trammell Crow Center is a 50-story, 1.2 million-square-foot tower in downtown's Arts District. 23Springs is a 26-story, 625,000-square-foot tower in Uptown.

Project Notes: Prudential is undertaking a $4.2 million retrofit at Trammell Crow Center and a $2.6 million upgrade at 23Springs, with the latter expected to be completed in January 2027.
