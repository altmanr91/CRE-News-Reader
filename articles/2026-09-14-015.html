Rich Properties Snags $38.5M Refi for West Palm Beach Rental Asset

REFINANCE

Connect CRE — Mon, 14 Sep 2026 14:44:03 +0000

SUMMARY

Rich Properties has secured a $38.55 million refinancing package for its Tennis Towers Apartments, a 306-unit multifamily property in West Palm Beach, FL. The financing, arranged by Berkadia, is a seven-year, fixed-rate loan backed by Freddie Mac, featuring four years of interest-only payments. Rich Properties has owned and operated the asset for over 35 years.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Tennis Towers Apartments

Address: 2882 Tennis Club Drive, West Palm Beach, FL

Units: 306

Loan Amount: $38.5M

Loan Per Unit: $125,980/unit (calculated)

Year Built: 1990

Notable Features: Community amenities include a tropical swimming pool, sundeck, heated spa, 24-hour fitness center, three tennis courts, BBQ and picnic areas, and a car wash area. It is located near Okeechobee Boulevard and I-95, providing access to downtown West Palm Beach, CityPlace, Clematis Street, and the Brightline station, and is approximately 11 minutes from Palm Beach International Airport.

Project Notes: Freddie Mac-backed, seven-year, fixed-rate loan with four years of interest-only payments.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

SPONSOR: Rich Properties

LENDER: Berkadia — Mitch Sinberg; Scott Wadler; Matthew Robbins; Brad Williamson; Jared Hill
