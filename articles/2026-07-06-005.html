PSRS Arranges $6.5M in Refinancing for Shopping Center in Dana Point, California

REFINANCE

REBusinessOnline — Mon, 06 Jul 2026 12:30:09 +0000

SUMMARY

PSRS has secured $6.5 million in cash-out refinancing for a 31,801-square-foot shopping center in Dana Point, California. The non-recourse loan, obtained through a bank relationship, features a sub-30 percent loan-to-value ratio and a three-year, full-term interest-only structure.
