OCVibe Adds Five Dining Concepts to $5B SoCal District

Commercial Observer — Tue, 25 Aug 2026 21:15:15 +0000

SUMMARY

Development

The Samueli family has added five new dining concepts to Katella Commons, a 50,000-square-foot market hall within the $5 billion OCVibe mixed-use development in Anaheim, CA. The market hall, which will feature 21 kitchens, is expected to open in early 2027. OCVibe is taking an unconventional approach to leasing by fronting the initial investment for each stall, averaging $300,000, and providing operational support, aiming to reduce the financial burden on independent chefs and foster a more sustainable tenant environment. This strategy is part of a broader effort to establish OCVibe as a year-round destination, leveraging its diverse residential, office, hotel, and entertainment components to ensure consistent customer traffic.
