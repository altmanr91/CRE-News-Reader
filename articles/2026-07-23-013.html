Holt Lunsford Commercial Investments acquires 26.5-acre site in North Fort Worth for industrial park development

ACQUISITION

REJournals — Wed, 22 Jul 2026 17:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Holt Lunsford Commercial Investments (HLCI) has acquired a 26.5-acre site in North Fort Worth, TX, for the development of Meacham 820 Crossing, a two-building Class-A industrial park. The project will total approximately 238,000 square feet and is strategically located with direct frontage on I-820, offering efficient access to major transportation arteries and the Fort Worth Meacham International Airport. Construction is slated to begin in Q4 2026, with delivery anticipated in Q4 2027.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Property Name: Meacham 820 Crossing

Size: 238,000 SF

Completion: Q4 2027

Site Area: 26.5 acres

Notable Features: Two Class A industrial buildings with 32-foot clear heights, dock-high loading, deep truck courts, and generous car parking, with accommodations for trailer parking and outside storage.

Project Notes: Construction is expected to begin in Q4 2026.
