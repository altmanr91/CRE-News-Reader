Avison Young adds principal, market leader to Houston office

REJournals — Fri, 24 Jul 2026 10:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Promotion

Avison Young has appointed Matthew Seliger as Principal and Market Leader for its Houston office. In this role, Seliger will spearhead strategic growth initiatives, aiming to expand the firm's service capabilities across brokerage, property management, project management, and valuation, while also focusing on recruiting new talent. His mandate includes growing the Houston platform, strategically expanding the brokerage business, and fostering a collaborative culture for long-term success.
