Fisher Island mansion asking $32M leads luxury contracts in Miami

SALE

The Real Deal — Miami — Mon, 20 Jul 2026 17:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

A planned 14,800-square-foot mansion on Fisher Island, asking $32 million, led Miami-Dade County's luxury residential contracts last week. The property, currently under construction, is expected to be completed next year. Additionally, a 5,450-square-foot townhouse in Miami Beach's Portofino Tower, asking $9.5 million, was the priciest condo to go under contract.
