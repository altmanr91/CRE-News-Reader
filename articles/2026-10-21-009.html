Baker Katz Negotiates Five Leases for Einstein Bros. Bagels Totaling 9,548 SF in Greater Houston

LEASE

REBusinessOnline — Tue, 20 Oct 2026 15:09:59 +0000

SUMMARY

Baker Katz has successfully negotiated five new leases for Einstein Bros. Bagels across the greater Houston area, totaling 9,548 square feet. These new locations are strategically situated in Houston's Garden Oaks district and the suburban communities of Tomball, Spring, Baytown, and Manvel, expanding the bagel chain's presence in the region.
