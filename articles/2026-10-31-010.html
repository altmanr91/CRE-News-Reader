312-Unit Multifamily in Indiana Changes Hands

SALE

Connect CRE — Fri, 30 Oct 2026 21:27:39 +0000

SUMMARY

The Bend Apartments, a 312-unit garden apartment community in Clarksville, Indiana, was sold to a private investor. Northmarq's Investment Sales team represented the regional multifamily developer in the transaction. The property's recent construction, scale, and strategic location near major transportation arteries and employment centers contributed to strong investor interest, reflecting robust demand for institutional-quality multifamily assets in the Louisville metropolitan area.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: The Bend Apartments

Address: 1909 Greentree Blvd., Clarksville, IN

Units: 312

Year Built: 2023

Site Area: 13.22 acres

Notable Features: Conveniently located near shopping, national retailers, Green Tree Mall, and Interstates 65 and 265, with proximity to River Ridge Commerce Center and downtown Louisville.
