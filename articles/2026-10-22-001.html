Real Estate Tech Startup MARC Raises $5M Seed Round

Commercial Observer — Thu, 22 Oct 2026 13:00:07 +0000

SUMMARY

Non-CRE / Business News

MARC, an AI platform designed to help institutional real estate owners optimize operating costs, has successfully closed an oversubscribed $5 million seed funding round. This latest investment, led by Work-Bench with participation from MXV Capital and several prominent founders, brings the company's total funding to $6 million. The Manhattan-based startup plans to leverage the capital to accelerate its expansion across the U.S. and Canada, as well as grow its team in New York to meet increasing demand for its product.
