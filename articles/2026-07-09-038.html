Local investors buy another piece of Pleasanton mall for $11M

ACQUISITION

The Real Deal — San Francisco — Wed, 08 Jul 2026 23:00:27 +0000

SUMMARY

A group of local investors, led by Jerry Hunt of 300 Venture Group and Christopher George of CMG Group, has acquired an 8.4-acre parcel at Stoneridge Shopping Center in Pleasanton, CA, for $10.7 million. The site includes a former Nordstrom store and a surface parking lot. This acquisition expands the group's holdings at the retail complex to 18.2 acres, following a previous purchase in 2022 of a 9.8-acre parcel that included a former JC Penney store. The investors are exploring various options for the site, including re-tenanting the retail buildings and developing a higher-density project on the parking areas, citing advantageous pricing for both parcels.
