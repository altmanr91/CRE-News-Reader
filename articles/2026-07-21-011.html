Northern Builders, Affinius Capital close lease agreement for 1.2-million-square-foot industrial facility in Joliet

LEASE

REJournals — Tue, 21 Jul 2026 10:04:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Northern Builders and Affinius Capital have secured a long-term lease for a 1.2-million-square-foot industrial facility within Cherry Hill Business Park in Joliet, Illinois. The state-of-the-art development, initially an 800,000-square-foot speculative shell, will be expanded to include 400,000 square feet of convertible freezer-cooler space. This transaction highlights the strong demand for large, specialized industrial assets, particularly in the cold storage, manufacturing, and processing sectors within the I-80 corridor.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Property Name: Cherry Hill Business Park

Size: 1,200,000 SF

Notable Features: Includes 400,000 square feet of convertible freezer-cooler space, 21,000 square feet of office space, 140 equipped docks, 316 off-building trailer stalls, 6 drive-in doors, 464 car parking stalls, and a 40-foot clear height.

Project Notes: Originally delivered as an 800,000-square-foot speculative shell in December.
