Jared Solomon wants out of Brooklyn detention center

The Real Deal — New York — Wed, 01 Jul 2026 18:30:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Non-CRE / Political & Crime News

Jared Solomon, a convicted fraudster formerly with Vornado Realty Trust, is seeking release from the Metropolitan Detention Center in Brooklyn, citing brutal conditions and inadequate medical care. However, the U.S. Attorney's Office opposes his release, arguing that Solomon has violated prison rules regarding phone use and is "unsupervisable." The judge has not yet ruled on the bail application.
