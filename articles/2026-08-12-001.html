The Plan: Extell’s 70-Unit 50 West 66th Street Leans Into Luxury Via Hospitality

Commercial Observer — Wed, 12 Aug 2026 10:00:21 +0000

SUMMARY

Development

Extell Development Company's 50 West 66th Street, a 70-story luxury condominium building in New York City, is leaning into a hospitality-driven experience with its extensive amenities and high-end finishes. The project, which began resident move-ins last year, features 121 units across its 'house' and 'tower' sections, with prices ranging from $8 million to $85 million. Many buyers purchased units based on renderings, drawn by the panoramic views and resort-quality amenities, which span 50,000 square feet and include multiple pools, sports courts, and lounges.
