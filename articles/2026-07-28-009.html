Marcus & Millichap Brokers $9.9M Sale of Cincinnati Medical Office Building

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Mon, 27 Jul 2026 14:13:10 +0000

SUMMARY

Marcus & Millichap has brokered the sale of a 26,107-square-foot medical office building in Cincinnati for $9.9 million. The property is fully leased to a medical tenant under a double-net lease with approximately five years remaining. The brokerage team represented a private investor group as the seller and also procured a national REIT as the buyer.
