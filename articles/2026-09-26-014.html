Distyl AI Doubles Footprint at 135 Madison Ave.

LEASE

Connect CRE — Fri, 25 Sep 2026 21:45:29 +0000

SUMMARY

Distyl AI, a developer of AI-native operational systems, has doubled its office footprint at 135 Madison Avenue in New York City, expanding its lease by 15,038 square feet. This expansion brings the company's total occupancy to 30,076 square feet across two contiguous floors, reflecting its rapid growth and commitment to enterprise innovation in Manhattan. The landlord's leasing agent noted the company's rapidly growing operations necessitated the additional space.
