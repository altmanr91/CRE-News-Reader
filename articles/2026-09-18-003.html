Beer-Importing Holterbosch Family Offloads LIC Warehouse for $95M

SALE

Commercial Observer — Thu, 17 Sep 2026 20:06:49 +0000

SUMMARY

The Holterbosch family, former owners of a German beer importing and distribution business, has sold a warehouse property in Long Island City, Queens, for $95 million. The buyer is Ground Lease REIT, an affiliate of Montgomery Street Partners. The family had owned the property since 1968, and the sale comes a decade after the passing of the family patriarch.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Address: 10-01 45th Road, Long Island City, NY

Sale Price: $95.0M

Year Built: 1968

Notable Features: Located on the Long Island City waterfront, near the Eleventh Street Basin inlet of the East River, offering potential for future development.

Project Notes: The property was held by the Holterbosch family since approximately 1968.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: Ground Lease REIT

BUYER: Montgomery Street Partners

SELLER: Holterbosch family — Heidi Holterbosch

SELLER: Hans Holterbosch Inc.
