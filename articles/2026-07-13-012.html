Net Lease Cap Rates Tick Upward in Q2 2026

Connect CRE — Mon, 13 Jul 2026 09:55:56 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Net Lease Report

Overall single-tenant net lease cap rates saw a slight increase in the second quarter of 2026, rising two basis points to 6.82%, according to The Boulder Group. This uptick was primarily driven by retail and industrial sectors, which experienced increases of five and ten basis points respectively, while office cap rates held steady. The shift is attributed to the Federal Reserve's revised interest rate projections, which removed an anticipated rate cut and introduced the possibility of a hike later in the year, influencing investor calculations for the latter half of 2026. Despite these changes, transaction volume is expected to remain stable, with continued investor interest in the fundamental appeal of net lease assets, potentially bolstered by corporate sale-leaseback activity.
