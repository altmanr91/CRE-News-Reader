Mohr Capital closes sale of 1.05-million-square-foot industrial facility in Whiteland

SALE

REJournals — Mon, 05 Oct 2026 16:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Mohr Capital has completed the sale of Lot 6, a 1.05-million-square-foot industrial facility located within Mohr Logistics Park in Whiteland, Indiana. The property, which was rebuilt after sustaining tornado damage in 2023, offers modern bulk warehouse space with 40-foot clear heights. The buyer and transaction terms were not disclosed, but Mohr Capital will retain ownership of the remaining portions of the logistics park.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Property Name: Lot 6, Mohr Logistics Park

Address: 5741 North Graham Road, Whiteland, IN

Size: 1,057,350 SF

Year Built: 2022

Notable Features: Modern bulk warehouse space with 40-foot clear heights, located within a 475-acre master-planned industrial development along the I-65 corridor.

Project Notes: The facility was originally completed in 2022 and rebuilt on its original foundation after sustaining significant damage from an EF-3 tornado in April 2023.
