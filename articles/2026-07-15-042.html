Revealed: The Banks Powering Private Credit's CRE Lending Boom

Bisnow News Feed — Tue, 14 Jul 2026 11:45:41 -0400

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

This article analyzes the growing trend of traditional banks providing 'back leverage' to private credit firms, enabling them to expand their real estate lending activities. As regulatory constraints and past downturns have shifted banks away from direct lending, they are increasingly partnering with private credit funds through structures like warehouse lines, subscription facilities, and repurchase agreements. This symbiotic relationship allows banks to reduce their direct risk while still profiting from real estate debt, and it enables private credit firms to magnify their lending capabilities and lower their borrowing costs. The article highlights how major U.S. banks, including Goldman Sachs, Bank of America, JPMorgan Chase, and Citibank, dominate this space, each with distinct strategies, while smaller and digital banks like Axos Financial and Stifel Bank also play significant roles, often forming exclusive relationships with private lenders.
