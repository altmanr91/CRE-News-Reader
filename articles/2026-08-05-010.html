Walker & Dunlop Arranges $137.5M Loan for Refinancing of Brooklyn Apartment Building

REFINANCE

REBusinessOnline — Tue, 04 Aug 2026 15:05:46 +0000

SUMMARY

Walker & Dunlop has arranged a $137.5 million floating-rate loan for the refinancing of 12 Halsey, a 240-unit apartment building in Brooklyn's Bedford-Stuyvesant neighborhood. The property, completed last fall, includes 30 percent affordable housing units and features a strong amenity package. The loan was secured through AllianceBernstein for the ownership partnership of EJS Group and Hope Street Capital.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: 12 Halsey

Address: 12 Halsey, Brooklyn, NY

Units: 240

Loan Amount: $137.5M

Loan Per Unit: $572,917/unit (calculated)

Notable Features: 30 percent of units are reserved as affordable housing. Amenities include a pool, fitness center, rooftop terraces, coworking space, and outdoor grilling and dining stations. The building also includes 2,400 square feet of ground-floor retail space.
