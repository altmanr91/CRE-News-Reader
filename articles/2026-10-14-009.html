CanTex Capital Acquires 18,535 SF Industrial Building in New Braunfels, Texas

ACQUISITION

REBusinessOnline — Wed, 14 Oct 2026 13:57:07 +0000

SUMMARY

CanTex Capital, a Dallas-based investment firm, has acquired an 18,535-square-foot industrial building in New Braunfels, Texas. The single-tenant property, completed in 2023, also offers industrial outdoor storage capabilities. The seller and sale price were not disclosed.
