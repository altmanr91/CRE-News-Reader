Cohen Brothers Ink $400M Loan for 23-Story West Palm Beach Office Tower

LOAN

Connect CRE — Thu, 29 Oct 2026 14:52:44 +0000

SUMMARY

Cohen Brothers Realty Corp. has secured a $400 million loan for the development of West Palm Point, a 23-story Class AA+ office tower currently under construction in West Palm Beach. The project, which will feature 438,000 square feet of office space, ground-floor retail, and extensive amenities, is expected to be delivered in the first quarter of 2029. The financing was arranged by Cooper Horowitz.

Notable Features: Class AA+ office tower with ground-floor retail, a 30-foot-tall lobby, 1,200-space parking structure, one-acre private landscaped rooftop park with putting green, dog park, café, water feature, fitness center, and conference center. Located near the Palm Beach County Convention Center.
