Sunday Summary: The Best Office Market in Years

Commercial Observer — Sun, 04 Oct 2026 13:00:41 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

This article provides an optimistic overview of the commercial real estate market, particularly in Manhattan office and South Florida, despite recent disappointing national employment figures and rising Treasury rates. The author highlights that Manhattan office availability is at its lowest since 2020, with strong leasing activity in Q3 2026, exceeding the 10-year average. Significant office leases by various investment firms and a law firm underscore this trend. The retail sector also shows strength with a major REIT acquisition and new store openings. South Florida continues to be a robust market, evidenced by substantial financing for luxury condo and affordable multifamily projects, as well as a record-breaking land acquisition for a new university campus.
