How AI Is Transforming Utility Real Estate and Workplace Portfolios

Connect CRE — Fri, 24 Jul 2026 05:01:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / AI in CRE

A JLL report highlights how artificial intelligence is transforming utility companies' real estate and workplace portfolios beyond just grid management. By centralizing facility and asset data, AI can identify inefficiencies, recommend improvements, and enable predictive maintenance, leading to significant cost savings and enhanced sustainability. The technology also aids in strategic real estate planning by analyzing lease data and occupancy trends, allowing utilities to optimize office space utilization and reduce operational expenses, particularly for costs not recoverable through utility rates.
