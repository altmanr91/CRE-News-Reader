Stroller Brand Uppababy Relocates Store to 156 Montague Street in Brooklyn Heights

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Fri, 18 Sep 2026 16:03:03 +0000

SUMMARY

Uppababy, a high-end baby gear brand, is relocating its Brooklyn retail and service hub to a larger 3,780-square-foot space at 156 Montague Street in Brooklyn Heights. The move from its previous Cobble Hill location will allow the company to enhance its product experience and personalized service for local families, capitalizing on the strong community of young families in Brooklyn Heights. The new location offers improved visibility and accessibility, which are key for family-focused retailers.
