Mark Cuban takes Adelson to court on Mavs plans for new arena

The Real Deal — Texas — Sat, 11 Jul 2026 14:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Multi-Transaction Report

This article reports on several distinct commercial real estate developments and financial distress in Texas. Mark Cuban is suing Las Vegas Sands over his alleged exclusion from decisions regarding the Dallas Mavericks' new arena at the Valley View Mall site, which is contingent on casino gambling legalization in Texas. Separately, S2 Capital's fund dissolution accounts for a significant portion of Texas CRE loans flagged for foreclosure in July. Additionally, Caterpillar plans a $36 million headquarters expansion in Irving, and Naterra International is developing a new 442,000-square-foot headquarters in Coppell for $34 million. Finally, Toyota is committing to a $3.6 billion expansion of its manufacturing campus in San Antonio, adding a second assembly line and 2.5 million square feet.
