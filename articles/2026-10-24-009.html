Virtu Signs 55,000 SF Office Lease at The Republic in Downtown Austin

LEASE

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 23 Oct 2026 14:22:41 +0000

SUMMARY

Virtu, a global financial services firm, has signed a 55,000-square-foot office lease at The Republic, a 48-story office tower in downtown Austin. The firm will occupy two high-rise floors in the building. The lease signifies continued demand for prime office space in the Austin market.
