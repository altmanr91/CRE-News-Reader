Prysmian to Invest $1B for Expansion of Cable Manufacturing Facility in Metro Charlotte

CONSTRUCTION

REBusinessOnline — Thu, 13 Aug 2026 14:18:41 +0000

SUMMARY

Prysmian North America is investing $1 billion to expand its fiber optic and glass cable manufacturing facility in Claremont, a suburb of Charlotte, NC. The expansion will add 975,000 square feet to the existing plant and create 385 new jobs. This significant investment is supported by a $1 million performance-based grant from the One North Carolina Fund, highlighting the state's commitment to fostering economic growth and advanced manufacturing.
