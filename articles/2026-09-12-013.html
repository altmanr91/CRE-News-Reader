Crescent Communities planning 313-unit multifamily community for West Nashville

DEVELOPMENT

REJournals — Fri, 11 Sep 2026 15:22:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Crescent Communities is planning a new 313-unit multifamily community named NOVEL oneC1TY in West Nashville. The project, envisioned as "Nashville's Modern Social Club," will be part of the larger oneC1TY mixed-use development and is designed with a focus on healthy living and community, featuring ample green space and direct access to existing retail, dining, and office destinations. The development will replace the C1TYBLOX retail and sand volleyball village, with plans to incorporate beloved elements of C1TYBLOX into the new community's ground-floor retail and public plaza.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: NOVEL oneC1TY

Units: 313

Completion: 2029

Notable Features: Designed as "Nashville's Modern Social Club" with a focus on health and wellness, featuring a resort-style rooftop pool deck, bar with sand volleyball, fitness center, courtyard with fire pits, and a Sky Lounge with Nashville skyline views. It will include 5,000 square feet of ground-floor retail and a public plaza.

Project Notes: The project site currently houses C1TYBLOX, a retail and sand volleyball village constructed from repurposed shipping containers, which will continue to operate through the end of the year before redevelopment begins. The development is part of the larger oneC1TY mixed-use destination.
