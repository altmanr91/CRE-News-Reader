New York Life Provides $386M Loan for Refinancing of Midtown Manhattan Office Building

REFINANCE

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 25 Sep 2026 14:42:34 +0000

SUMMARY

New York Life Insurance has provided a $386 million refinancing loan for 200 Madison Avenue, a 750,000-square-foot office building in Midtown Manhattan. The five-year, floating-rate loan was arranged by Estreich & Co. and Newmark on behalf of the borrower, George Comfort & Sons, which owns the property in partnership with Loeb Partners Realty and Jamestown. The building recently secured a significant lease extension and expansion with its anchor tenant, contributing to its stable occupancy.
