Cushman & Wakefield adds three to Chicago office leasing and capital markets teams

REJournals — Fri, 16 Oct 2026 09:48:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Promotion

Cushman & Wakefield has expanded its Chicago office leasing and capital markets teams with three strategic hires, including two Executive Managing Directors and a Transaction Manager. These additions are expected to enhance the firm's capabilities in serving investors and owners in the suburban office market, particularly in lease-up strategies, office capital markets, and investment sales. The new team members bring extensive experience in commercial real estate, including a strong background in leasing, investment sales, acquisitions, and asset management, which will bolster the firm's ability to help clients achieve their real estate objectives.
