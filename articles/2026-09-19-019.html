Rhone Signs 40K-SF Headquarters Lease with RFR in Stamford

LEASE

Connect CRE — Fri, 18 Sep 2026 21:33:34 +0000

SUMMARY

Performance apparel brand Rhone has signed a 40,000-square-foot headquarters lease at 300 Atlantic St. in Stamford, CT, owned by RFR. The transaction will accommodate the brand's continued growth and is expected to house over 150 employees. The new headquarters will occupy the top two floors of the building, reflecting Rhone's expansion since its founding in 2014.
