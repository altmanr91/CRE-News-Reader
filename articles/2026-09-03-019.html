Northmarq Arranges Refi for Van Nuys Warehouse Portfolio

REFINANCE

Connect CRE — Wed, 02 Sep 2026 20:38:33 +0000

SUMMARY

Northmarq's Los Angeles Debt + Equity team successfully arranged $6.1 million in permanent financing for a portfolio of three flex/showroom industrial properties in Van Nuys, CA. The ten-year term loan was secured through a life insurance company on behalf of the borrower. The properties are strategically located in the San Fernando Valley, a well-established infill industrial market in Southern California.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Loan Amount: $6.1M

Notable Features: Portfolio of three flex/showroom industrial properties.

Project Notes: The portfolio includes 14943 Califa St. (built 1955, 0.33-acre site), 15044 Keswick St. (built 1969, 1.48-acre site), and 14932 Oxnard St. (0.65-acre site).

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

MORTGAGE BROKER: Northmarq — Zalmi Klyne; Stanley Chu

LENDER: Life Insurance Company
