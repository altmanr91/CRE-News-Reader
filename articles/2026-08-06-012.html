CBL Properties, Greystar celebrate groundbreaking of mixed-use development in Franklin

DEVELOPMENT

REJournals — Thu, 06 Aug 2026 09:40:00 +0000

SUMMARY

CBL Properties and Greystar have broken ground on a new mixed-use development at CoolSprings Galleria in Franklin, TN. The project will add 361 residential units and approximately 15,000 square feet of new retail and restaurant space to the existing campus. This development is part of the ongoing evolution of CoolSprings Galleria, aiming to create a vibrant mixed-use environment that complements the area's character and supports the growth of Franklin and Williamson County.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: mixed-use

Property Name: CoolSprings Galleria

Address: 1800 Galleria Blvd, Franklin, TN*

Size: 15,000 SF

Units: 361

Completion: 2028

Notable Features: Located within the CoolSprings Galleria campus, designed to reflect the architectural heritage of Franklin.

Project Notes: The 5.35-acre parcel of underutilized parking was previously sold by CBL Properties to Greystar in June 2026 for this development.
