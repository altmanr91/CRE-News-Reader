FTC’s challenge to Zillow-Redfin partnership headed to trial

The Real Deal — National — Wed, 08 Jul 2026 18:43:54 +0000

SUMMARY

CRE Legal / Regulatory

The Federal Trade Commission's antitrust challenge against a $100 million multifamily listing syndication deal between Zillow and Redfin is proceeding to trial. A federal judge denied the FTC's request for an immediate injunction, citing numerous disputes that necessitate a full trial. The FTC alleges the agreement, which makes Zillow the exclusive provider of multifamily listings for Redfin and its platforms, reduces competition in the online apartment listings market and violates antitrust laws.
