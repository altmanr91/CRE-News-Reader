Mid-America Real Estate Corporation brokers sale of 70,144-square-foot retail property in Park Ridge

SALE

REJournals — Wed, 01 Jul 2026 10:01:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Mid-America Real Estate Corporation's Investment Sales Group recently brokered the sale of The Shops of Uptown, a 70,144-square-foot grocery-anchored retail property in Park Ridge, Illinois. The property, anchored by a popular grocery retailer, was sold by Phillips Edison Company to an institutional investment manager. The transaction highlights the strong demand for well-located retail assets in affluent suburban communities with robust demographics and consumer spending power.
