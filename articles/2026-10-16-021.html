TruAmerica Buys Dunwoody Apartments At Discount: The Atlanta Deal Sheet

ACQUISITION

Bisnow Latest News Feed — Thu, 15 Oct 2026 20:05:37 -0400

SUMMARY

TruAmerica Multifamily has acquired a 322-unit apartment community in Dunwoody, GA, from Clark Ventures. The transaction was part of a larger trend of institutional investors seeking value in the current market, with TruAmerica noting the property was purchased at a significant discount to replacement cost. The acquisition expands TruAmerica's footprint in the Atlanta metropolitan area, a market they view as having strong long-term fundamentals.
