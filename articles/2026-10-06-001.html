Fifth Wall’s Brendan Wallace On Spotting Real Estate’s Next Hot Tech Early

Commercial Observer — Tue, 06 Oct 2026 13:00:14 +0000

SUMMARY

Feature / Profile

This article profiles Brendan Wallace, founder of Fifth Wall, a prominent investment firm focused on real estate technology. Wallace discusses his lifelong connection to real estate, influenced by his father's experiences as a New York City landowner, including a notable eminent domain case involving the site of the New York Times Building. He emphasizes his belief in land as a scarce, anti-inflationary asset that consistently appreciates in value. Wallace also reflects on his career path, from working in real estate groups at Goldman Sachs and Blackstone, where he learned from Jon Gray during major transactions like the Equity Office Properties Trust and Hilton Hotels buyouts, to ultimately founding Fifth Wall to invest in technology for the built environment.
