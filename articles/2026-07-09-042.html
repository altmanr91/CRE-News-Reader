MMCC Arranges Financing for Northridge Offices

LOAN

Connect CRE — Wed, 08 Jul 2026 21:09:42 +0000

SUMMARY

Marcus & Millichap Capital Corporation (MMCC) arranged $5.7 million in financing for a 48,954-square-foot office property in the Northridge neighborhood of Los Angeles. The three-year loan, secured through a regional bank, features a 65% loan-to-value, a 6% interest rate, and a 25-year amortization schedule. This financing demonstrates continued lender confidence in well-located Los Angeles office assets that benefit from stable tenancy and robust local demand drivers.
