Sterling Bay plans to offload Fulton Market site to Range Group for 109-unit mid-rise project

ACQUISITION

The Real Deal — Chicago — Thu, 16 Jul 2026 18:30:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Range Group is under contract to acquire a 27,714-square-foot site in Chicago's Fulton Market from Sterling Bay, with plans to develop a 109-unit mid-rise apartment building. The proposed five-story project at 113 West Kinzie Street includes 15% affordable units, with a fee-in-lieu for the remaining 5% to meet the city's 20% affordability requirement. This development reflects a trend of smaller residential projects emerging in the resilient Fulton Market district.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Address: 113 West Kinzie Street, Chicago, IL

Units: 109

Site Area: 0.636 acres

Notable Features: Proposed five-story mid-rise apartment building with 15% affordable units and a fee-in-lieu for the remaining 5% to meet city requirements.

Project Notes: Site is one of three properties Sterling Bay is offloading for residential redevelopment.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: Range Group

SELLER: Sterling Bay

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Fulton Market has shown strong resilience to pandemic-era property value declines in downtown Chicago. Smaller residential projects are increasing in the district due to a slowed large-scale project pipeline and efforts to balance infrastructure strain with historic preservation. Average rents at a comparable Tishman Speyer building in the area are $3,133 per unit, with its 12,436-square-foot retail area fully leased.
