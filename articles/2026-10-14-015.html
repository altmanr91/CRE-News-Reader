Draper and Kramer Incorporated purchases 268-unit multifamily building in Evanston

ACQUISITION

REJournals — Wed, 14 Oct 2026 09:07:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Draper and Kramer, Incorporated has acquired Albion Evanston, a 268-unit luxury multifamily building in Evanston, Illinois. The 15-story property, which was 98.5% leased at the time of acquisition, will be rebranded as Eva and managed by Draper and Kramer. The acquisition provides the buyer with a well-occupied asset in a diverse market, appealing to both university affiliates and young professionals due to its amenities and transit access.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Albion Evanston

Address: 1500 Sherman Ave., Evanston, IL

Units: 268

Occupancy at Sale: 98.0%

Notable Features: The 15-story building features custom European-style cabinetry, quartz countertops, hardwood-style flooring, 9- to 11-foot concrete ceilings, floor-to-ceiling windows, in-unit laundry, and a range of hospitality-inspired amenities including a rooftop pool, fitness center, and coworking space. It also includes 7,090 square feet of fully leased first-floor retail space and 198 parking spaces.

Project Notes: The property was 98.5% leased at the time of acquisition and will be rebranded as Eva under Draper and Kramer's ownership.
