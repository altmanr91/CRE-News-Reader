The Pain In NYC's Biotech Market Is Hitting Harlem Hardest

Bisnow Latest News Feed — Sun, 30 Aug 2026 17:04:48 -0400

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

Harlem is disproportionately affected by the downturn in New York City's biotech market, holding a significant share of the city's vacant lab space. This situation is attributed to a combination of reduced research funding and an abundance of speculative development projects that have not yet secured tenants.
