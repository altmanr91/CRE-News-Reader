Waterfront Gables Estates lot, once home to UMiami presidents, trades for $42M

SALE

The Real Deal — Miami — Mon, 06 Jul 2026 23:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

A waterfront lot in the exclusive Gables Estates community of Coral Gables, previously home to University of Miami presidents, was sold for $41.6 million. The 4.6-acre property at 8565 Old Cutler Road was acquired by Miami Real 1 LLC from 8565 Old Cutler LLC. The seller had purchased the property, which included a teardown home, in 2022 for $38 million and had filed for demolition last year, suggesting the transaction likely resulted in a break-even or loss after accounting for taxes, commissions, and demolition costs.
