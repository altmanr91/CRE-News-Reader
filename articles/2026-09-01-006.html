Connell Co. Begins Leasing 179-Unit Apartment Building in Berkeley Heights, New Jersey

REBusinessOnline — Tue, 01 Sep 2026 13:58:05 +0000

SUMMARY

Development

The Connell Co. has commenced leasing at RT500, a new 179-unit apartment building in Berkeley Heights, New Jersey. This 11-story residential property is part of The Park, a larger 185-acre mixed-use development. The building offers a variety of floor plans and a comprehensive suite of amenities, with construction having topped out last summer.
