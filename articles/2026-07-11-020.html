Miami green lights Coconut Grove Playhouse revival after two decades of battles

DEVELOPMENT

The Real Deal — Miami — Fri, 10 Jul 2026 14:45:31 +0000

SUMMARY

Miami city commissioners have approved Miami-Dade County's long-standing plan to redevelop the Coconut Grove Playhouse, clearing the way for a scaled-down performance venue, retail, offices, and a parking garage. The approval, which included five zoning exceptions and four waivers, allows for the restoration of the playhouse's 1926 façade and the construction of a new 310-seat theater. The project aims to reconnect the historically Black West Coconut Grove neighborhood through an open-air plaza, despite concerns from opponents about over-commercialization and increased traffic.

Project Notes: The project budget includes $28.5 million from 2004 Miami-Dade general obligation bonds, $13.4 million from the countywide infrastructure investment program, $9.1 million in 2005 special obligation bonds, $5.4 million in parking revenues, and a $2 million grant from the John S. and James L. Knight Foundation. The State of Florida acquired the property in 1980, and Miami-Dade County leased it from the state in 2013.
