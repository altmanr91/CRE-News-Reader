Opportunity Zone 2.0: Beefing Up an Investment Tool

Connect CRE — Fri, 10 Jul 2026 05:01:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

The article discusses the evolution of the Opportunity Zone program, highlighting the transition from the original 2017 framework to the new "Opportunity Zone 2.0" introduced as part of the One Big Beautiful Bill Act. This updated legislation aims to enhance the program's attractiveness to investors by making tax benefits permanent, offering a rolling five-year deferral period for capital gains, and providing specific incentives for rural development. Commercial real estate attorneys note that these changes, particularly the elimination of fixed deadlines and the reduced "substantial improvement" requirement for rural properties, are expected to drive an influx of new investments into Qualified Opportunity Funds (QOFs). While the new framework offers more clarity and consistency, potential complications related to the transition period and the redesignation of Opportunity Zones are anticipated, with further IRS guidance expected.

DATA POINTS

Original QOZ program creation: Tax Cuts and Jobs Act of 2017

Original QOZ benefits expiration: December 31, 2028

Original QOZ capital gains deferral deadline: December 31, 2026

New QOZ 2.0 deferral period: Five years from investment date

Rural Opportunity Fund gain exclusion: 30% after 5-year hold

Rural property substantial improvement requirement: 50% improvement

State QOZ redesignation frequency: Every ten years on a rolling basis, starting July 1, 2026
