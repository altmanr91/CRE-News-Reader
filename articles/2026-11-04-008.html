Weitzman Buys 51,933 SF Shopping Center in Helotes, Texas

ACQUISITION

REBusinessOnline — Wed, 04 Nov 2026 16:20:49 +0000

SUMMARY

A limited partnership managed by Weitzman has acquired Helotes Plaza, a 51,933-square-foot shopping center in Helotes, Texas. The property was fully leased at the time of sale, featuring a strong mix of national and local tenants. Weitzman's acquisition of this fully occupied asset underscores its strategy to invest in well-located, stable retail properties.
