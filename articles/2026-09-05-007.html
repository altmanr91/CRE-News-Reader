Marcus & Millichap Brokers $11.4M Sale of Lower Manhattan Apartment Building

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 04 Sep 2026 13:50:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Marcus & Millichap has brokered the sale of 196 Bowery, a seven-unit apartment building in Lower Manhattan's NoLita neighborhood, for $11.4 million. The property includes ground-floor retail space. Marcus & Millichap represented the seller, Bowery Square Corp., and procured the buyer, Javeri Capital.
