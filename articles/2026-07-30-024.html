CBRE Posts 16% Revenue Growth In Q2 As Data Center Business Shines

Bisnow News Feed — Wed, 29 Jul 2026 12:38:54 -0400

SUMMARY

Market Research / Earnings Report

CBRE reported a strong second quarter, with core earnings per share rising 30% and revenue increasing 16%, driven largely by its infrastructure and data center services businesses. The company raised its 2026 core earnings per share guidance, anticipating 23% growth at the midpoint. The CEO highlighted the exceptional performance of data center services, which saw nearly 30% revenue growth, and projected sustained annual growth of 25% for the next five years despite challenges like NIMBYism, water/power issues, and supply chain constraints. This positive earnings report is seen by analysts as a favorable indicator for the broader commercial real estate brokerage sector.
