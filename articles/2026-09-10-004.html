Law Firm Thompson Relocates to 4K SF at Cohen Brothers’ 622 Third Avenue

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Wed, 09 Sep 2026 20:56:30 +0000

SUMMARY

Thompson, a law firm specializing in M&A, tax, and intellectual property, is relocating its New York City offices to 622 Third Avenue, also known as Grand Central Plaza. The firm signed a five-year lease for 4,000 square feet on the 16th floor of the Midtown East office tower. The move from its current Financial District location is expected to occur in the fall.
