Zenith IOS Buys 7.6-Acre Facility in Tulsa, Oklahoma

ACQUISITION

REBusinessOnline — Thu, 13 Aug 2026 14:59:58 +0000

SUMMARY

Zenith IOS has acquired a 7.6-acre industrial outdoor storage facility in Tulsa, Oklahoma. The property includes a 26,900-square-foot building with six drive-through bays. This acquisition is part of a larger portfolio transaction that also involved properties in Duncanville and Blue Mound, Texas. The seller and sale price were not disclosed.
