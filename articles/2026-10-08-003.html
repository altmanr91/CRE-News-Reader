Toll Brothers Buys West Chelsea Development Site for $45M

ACQUISITION

Commercial Observer — Thu, 08 Oct 2026 15:40:48 +0000

SUMMARY

Toll Brothers, through its City Living condo development division, has acquired a development site in West Chelsea, Manhattan, for $45 million from the Nanis family. This acquisition, comprising 126-128 10th Avenue and 456 West 18th Street, expands on a previous purchase of an adjacent vacant lot at 118 10th Avenue for $53 million in February. The combined sites are intended for a significant luxury condominium development, with initial plans filed for a 240,000-square-foot property featuring 105 units and commercial space at the 118 10th Avenue parcel.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Address: 126-128 10th Avenue and 456 West 18th Street, New York City, NY

Units: 105

Sale Price: $45.0M

Notable Features: Planned amenities include a pool, sauna, cold plunge, and playroom, designed by CookFox.

Project Notes: The 8,738-square-foot assembled properties adjoin 118 10th Avenue, which Toll Brothers previously acquired in February for $53 million. The sites currently hold low-rise buildings, including a diner, which are slated for demolition.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: Toll Brothers

SELLER: Nanis family

SELLER BROKER: Adirondack Capital Partners — Michael Hunter Coghill, Founder and Managing Partner; Chad Sinsheimer; Brittany Feinberg

ARCHITECT: CookFox
