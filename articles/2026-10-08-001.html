TJ Maxx Signs 29K-SF Deal to Replace H&M at 150 East 86th Street

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Thu, 08 Oct 2026 15:52:06 +0000

SUMMARY

T.J. Maxx has signed a significant lease for nearly 29,000 square feet at Extell Development's retail condominium unit at 150 East 86th Street in Manhattan's Upper East Side. The discount department store will replace H&M in the space, which includes ground-floor and second-floor retail, with an anticipated opening in late 2027. This transaction represents the largest retail lease signed in Manhattan during the third quarter of 2026.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: retail

Address: 150 East 86th Street, New York City, NY

Size: 28,837 SF

Notable Features: The space includes approximately 4,000 square feet on the ground floor and 25,000 square feet on the second floor.

Project Notes: Extell Development previously acquired the retail condo unit from Vornado Realty Trust for $64 million in July 2025.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Retail rents on the Upper East Side averaged $259 per square foot during the third quarter of 2026. Other significant retail leases in Manhattan during Q3 2026 included Five Iron Golf's 28,288-square-foot deal at 32 Old Slip and Crunch Fitness' 28,100-square-foot lease at 2192 Broadway.
