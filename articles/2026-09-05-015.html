Global Holdings Lands $382M Refi for Midtown Tower

REFINANCE

Connect CRE — Fri, 04 Sep 2026 22:57:19 +0000

SUMMARY

Global Holdings has secured a $382.4 million refinancing loan for its 120 Park Ave. office tower in Midtown Manhattan. The new financing, provided by Wells Fargo and LBBW, replaces an existing $335 million loan from HSBC. This recapitalization follows a significant lease renewal by a major tenant, which occupies approximately 80% of the building, and will support a planned capital improvement program for the Class A asset.
