Colliers Secures $8.5M Sale of Industrial Building in Denver

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Tue, 03 Nov 2026 16:18:13 +0000

SUMMARY

Colliers Denver Investment Services Group has facilitated the $8.5 million sale of a multi-tenant industrial building located at 4001 Holly Street in Denver. DMS Real Estate sold the 53,740-square-foot asset to Watumull Properties Corp. The property was fully leased to three tenants at the time of sale, offering a strong income stream to the new owner.
