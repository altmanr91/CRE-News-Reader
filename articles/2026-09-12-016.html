Hudson Pacific Extends Maturity on $1.1B Hollywood Media Loan

REFINANCE

Connect CRE — Fri, 11 Sep 2026 21:25:37 +0000

SUMMARY

Hudson Pacific Properties and its joint venture partner have successfully extended the maturity of their $1.1 billion CMBS loan for the Hollywood Media Portfolio to November 9, 2027. The extension maintains the existing interest rate and does not require any principal paydown at closing. This move provides the owners with additional time and flexibility to advance their leasing strategy across the portfolio and proactively manage their debt maturity schedule.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: mixed-use

Property Name: Hollywood Media Portfolio

Size: 2,200,000 SF

Loan Amount: $1.10B

Notable Features: Includes Sunset Gower Studios, Sunset Las Palmas Studios, Sunset Bronson Studios, and five Class A office properties (ICON, EPIC, Harlow, 6040 Sunset, CUE).

Project Notes: The loan's maturity was extended to November 9, 2027, with no change to the interest rate and no principal paydown required at closing. The portfolio includes rights to build an additional 1.1 million square feet of office and production space.
