Construction crews break ground on Alpine Senior Apartments in Grand Rapids

CONSTRUCTION

REJournals — Fri, 25 Sep 2026 10:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Construction has commenced on Alpine Senior Apartments, a 52-unit affordable housing community for seniors aged 55 and older in Grand Rapids, MI. The $16 million development, a collaboration between Volker Development Inc., Little River Holdings, LLC, and Equity First Community Development LLC, will offer a mix of one- and two-bedroom units for residents earning 30% to 70% of the Area Median Income. Ten units will benefit from project-based rental assistance, enhancing affordability for lower-income seniors. The project aims to provide independent living options with various amenities and green building features, while reactivating a previously underused commercial site.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Alpine Senior Apartments

Address: 1625 Alpine Ave. NW, Grand Rapids, MI

Units: 52

Total Project Cost: $16.0M

$/Unit: $307,692/unit (calculated)

Notable Features: Affordable housing for seniors 55+ with in-unit laundry, fitness center, community room, enhanced security, bicycle parking, and EV charging stations. Designed with green building features to reduce utility costs and environmental footprint. Located along the Alpine Avenue corridor with access to transit, groceries, pharmacies, and medical care.

Project Notes: Offers 28 one-bedroom and 24 two-bedroom apartments. Ten apartments will be supported by project-based rental assistance vouchers administered by the Grand Rapids Housing Commission. The development is on the former Twisters Ice Cream property.
