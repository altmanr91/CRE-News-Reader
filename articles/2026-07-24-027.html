Ken Griffin wins city approval to buy historic cottage near Citadel’s HQ for $3M

ACQUISITION

The Real Deal — Miami — Thu, 23 Jul 2026 17:21:51 +0000

SUMMARY

Ken Griffin has received city approval to purchase a historic cottage in Miami for $2.98 million, located near Citadel's future headquarters in Brickell. The 121-year-old Dr. James M. Jackson Medical Office, currently home to Dade Heritage Trust, will be relocated to Coconut Grove, with Griffin funding the move and preservation. This transaction is part of Griffin's broader development strategy in Brickell, where he is assembling land for a new 54-story office tower for Citadel and Citadel Securities, along with a 300-unit apartment building and parking garage.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Property Name: Dr. James M. Jackson Medical Office

Address: 190 Southeast 12th Terrace, Miami, FL

Sale Price: $3.0M

Year Built: 1905

Notable Features: Historic cottage, former office and surgical center of Miami's first resident physician.

Project Notes: The property will be relocated to Coconut Grove, with funding for relocation and preservation provided by the buyer. The buyer previously acquired a 2.5-acre site for $363 million in 2022 for the main headquarters development.
