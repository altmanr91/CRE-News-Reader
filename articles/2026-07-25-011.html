Principle Construction completes truck maintenance, warehouse facility in Elgin

CONSTRUCTION

REJournals — Fri, 24 Jul 2026 16:39:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Principle Construction Corp. has completed a 55,468-square-foot build-to-suit truck maintenance and warehouse facility in Elgin, Illinois, for Orozco Trucking. The new headquarters, designed by Harris Architects, provides the national climate-controlled and dry freight specialist with a permanent operational base, replacing a leased site. The facility offers a strategic mix of office, maintenance, and warehouse space, with features like 26-foot clear heights and multiple dock and drive-in doors, and is situated on undeveloped land with direct access to I-90, allowing for future expansion.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Address: 2580 Mason Road, Elgin, IL

Size: 55,468 SF

Notable Features: Features a two-story office space, maintenance space, and warehouse space with 26-foot clear height, four dock doors, and four drive-in doors. Located on previously undeveloped land with immediate access to I-90.

Project Notes: Build-to-suit project for Orozco Trucking, relocating from a leased site in Elk Grove Village.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

GENERAL CONTRACTOR: Principle Construction Corp. — Darrin Dehmlow, Project Manager; Doug Brucato, General Superintendent

OWNER: Orozco Trucking

ARCHITECT: Harris Architects
