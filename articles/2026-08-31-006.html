Knighthead Funding Provides $32M Loan for Refinancing of Metro Chicago Apartment Community

REFINANCE

REBusinessOnline — Mon, 31 Aug 2026 15:15:01 +0000

SUMMARY

Knighthead Funding has provided a $32 million loan for the refinancing of Cerca, a newly constructed 62-unit multifamily community in Glenview, Illinois. The loan replaces a prior construction facility and repays the property's preferred equity investor. The Drake Group is the borrower in this transaction.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Cerca

Address: 1850 Glenview Rd, Glenview, IL*

Units: 62

Loan Amount: $32.0M

Loan Per Unit: $516,129/unit (calculated)

Notable Features: Amenities include fitness rooms, a private dining area, conference rooms, an amenity deck, dog spa, and grilling stations. The property also includes ground-floor retail space.

* Address sourced separately, not in original article.
