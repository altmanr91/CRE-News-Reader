PCCP Provides $41M Refinancing for Multifamily Community in San Diego

REFINANCE

REBusinessOnline — Tue, 20 Oct 2026 13:56:57 +0000

SUMMARY

PCCP has provided a $41 million senior loan to Foulger-Pratt for the refinancing of Tenney North Park, a 125-unit multifamily community in San Diego's North Park submarket. The loan refinances an existing construction loan for the recently built property, which features a mix of market-rate and affordable units, ground-floor retail, and a comprehensive amenity package.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Tenney North Park

Address: 2260 El Cajon Blvd, San Diego, CA

Units: 125

Loan Amount: $41.0M

Loan Per Unit: $328,000/unit (calculated)

Year Built: 2025

Notable Features: Eight-story property with 115 market-rate and 10 affordable units, averaging 727 square feet, with 3,116 square feet of ground-floor retail and 146 parking spaces. Amenities include multiple clubhouses, coworking spaces, a rooftop lounge, swimming pool, fitness areas, social courtyards, a pet spa, and EV charging stations.

Project Notes: Refinanced an existing construction loan.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

LENDER: PCCP

SPONSOR: Foulger-Pratt
