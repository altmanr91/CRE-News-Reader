Related Group’s Hollywood Live Local project draws suit from residents, nonprofit

The Real Deal — Miami — Tue, 07 Jul 2026 22:15:00 +0000

SUMMARY

CRE Legal / Regulatory

Hollywood residents and a newly formed nonprofit have filed a lawsuit against the City of Hollywood and an affiliate of Related Group, seeking to block a proposed beachfront luxury condo and community center project. The lawsuit claims the development violates a deed restriction requiring voter approval for long-term leases of public recreational land east of the Intracoastal Waterway and that the land must be used for public or municipal purposes. The developer plans to utilize Florida's Live Local Act to bypass rezoning, incorporating workforce housing units into the project.
