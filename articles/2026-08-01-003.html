CW Realty Closes Deal to Buy 55 Smith Street in Downtown Brooklyn for $58M

ACQUISITION

Commercial Observer — Fri, 31 Jul 2026 17:28:51 +0000

SUMMARY

CW Realty Group has acquired a significant development site at 55 Smith Street in Downtown Brooklyn from Edison Properties for $58 million. The 13,707-square-foot parcel, also known as 160 Livingston Street, is considered one of the largest residential development opportunities in the area. The site offers substantial development potential, allowing for up to 137,000 square feet as-of-right or 164,000 square feet under the Universal Affordability Preference program, with no height or parking restrictions for a potential housing development. Brokers noted the strong demand for well-located development sites in New York City, particularly in neighborhoods with robust residential fundamentals and supportive zoning.
