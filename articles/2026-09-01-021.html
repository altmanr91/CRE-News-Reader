Chicago's Slow Apartment Pipeline Fueling Deals And Adaptive Reuse

Bisnow Latest News Feed — Mon, 31 Aug 2026 19:15:14 -0400

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

Chicago's apartment market is experiencing a significant supply shortage, leading to increased activity in creative deal structures and the adaptive reuse of office buildings for residential purposes. This trend is driven by a slow pipeline of new apartment construction, which is failing to keep pace with demand.
