MCA Realty Acquires Three Multi-Tenant Industrial Buildings in Salt Lake City

ACQUISITION

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 21 Aug 2026 15:12:08 +0000

SUMMARY

MCA Realty has acquired three multi-tenant industrial buildings in Salt Lake City from a private investor in an off-market transaction. The properties, totaling 77,944 square feet, were built between 1998 and 2005 and feature 20-foot clear heights and 24 grade-level doors. MCA Realty plans to implement a capital improvement program to refresh the properties.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Address: 515, 537 and 539 Pickett Circle, Salt Lake City, UT

Size: 77,944 SF

Year Built: 1998

Notable Features: Properties feature 20-foot clear heights and a combined 24 grade-level doors.

Project Notes: MCA Realty plans to implement a capital improvement program including refreshed paint, upgraded landscaping, enhanced parking areas, and modernized signage.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: MCA Realty

SELLER: private investor

BUYER BROKER: Colliers — Travis Healey; Tom Freeman
