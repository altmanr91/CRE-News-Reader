Grubb Properties forms $1.9B apartment REIT after recapitalization

LOAN

The Real Deal — National — Wed, 22 Jul 2026 18:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Grubb Properties has formed Link Apartments REIT, consolidating several of its funds into a private, non-traded multifamily REIT valued at approximately $1.9 billion. The new entity comprises 45 properties and over 5,600 units, aiming for a larger balance sheet and increased financing capabilities. Concurrently, Grubb Properties secured $617 million in financing, including a $377 million construction loan for its 8 Carlisle project in Manhattan and a $240 million credit facility for both the new REIT and its existing Opportunity Zone REIT.
