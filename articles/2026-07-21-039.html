Raleigh Developer to Start Work on 269-Unit Rental Community

CONSTRUCTION

Connect CRE — Mon, 20 Jul 2026 23:02:10 +0000

SUMMARY

Blue Heron Asset Management and design firm Cline are set to commence construction on Rosewood, a 269-unit apartment community in Raleigh. The project will feature two buildings totaling 399,000 square feet, a central public plaza, and 5,500 square feet of ground-floor retail space. The development is expected to be completed in 2028.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Rosewood

Address: 611 West South Street, Raleigh, NC

Size: 399,000 SF

Units: 269

Completion: 2028

Notable Features: Units range from 499 to 1,531 square feet, with amenities including a custom sauna and cold plunge suite, private coworking areas, a rooftop lounge and terrace, and a concealed speakeasy.

Project Notes: The 5,500 square feet of retail space will be located along South Street. The land was previously acquired for $12.85 million earlier this year.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

DEVELOPER/SPONSOR: Blue Heron Asset Management

ARCHITECT: Cline

GENERAL CONTRACTOR: W.M. Jordan Company
