Get to Know the Broker: Lauren Muss of Douglas Elliman

Commercial Observer — Thu, 24 Sep 2026 11:15:50 +0000

SUMMARY

Feature / Profile

This article profiles Lauren Muss, a prominent residential real estate broker at Douglas Elliman, highlighting her career trajectory and approach to the business. Muss, a trained gemologist, transitioned into real estate in 1994 and has since achieved over $7 billion in sales in Manhattan's high-end residential market. She emphasizes her team-based approach, where each member operates with full ownership, fostering a collaborative environment rather than a hierarchical one.
