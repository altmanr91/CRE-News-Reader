Saber Hall Investment Management acquires two-building industrial portfolio in Eden Prairie

ACQUISITION

REJournals — Wed, 29 Jul 2026 20:37:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Saber Hall Investment Management, in partnership with SARA Investment Real Estate and Quality Trusted Commercial Construction & Roofing, has acquired a two-building industrial portfolio in Eden Prairie, Minnesota, for $28 million. The portfolio, comprising Prairieview II and Shady View Business Center, totals 275,152 square feet and was 97% occupied at the time of closing. This acquisition marks Saber Hall's largest to date and significantly expands its footprint in the southwest Twin Cities industrial market, which is noted for its strength.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Property Name: Prairieview II and Shady View Business Center

Address: 9901 W 74th St / 7100-7148 Shady Oak Rd, Eden Prairie, MN

Size: 275,152 SF

Sale Price: $28.0M

Occupancy at Sale: 97.0%

Notable Features: Located in the Golden Triangle, bounded by Hwy 169, Hwy 212 and I-494. Prairieview II features 18-26 foot clear heights, 22 dock doors, and two drive-in doors. Shady View Business Center features 14-foot clear heights, 10 dock doors, nine drive-in doors, and 356 parking stalls.
