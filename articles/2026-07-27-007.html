Berkadia brokers sale of 450-bed student housing property in Huntsville

SALE

REJournals — Mon, 27 Jul 2026 09:48:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Berkadia has brokered the sale of The Forum at Sam Houston, a 450-bed student housing property in Huntsville, Texas. The garden-style asset was sold by Preiss to CrestMarc, a Texas-based firm. Berkadia also arranged the financing for the acquisition.
