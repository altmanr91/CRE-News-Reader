Corpay’s Cross-Border Business Leases Prebuilt at Rudin’s 560 Lexington

LEASE

Connect CRE — Mon, 17 Aug 2026 22:27:53 +0000

SUMMARY

Corpay's Cross-Border business has signed an 11-year office lease for 17,778 square feet at 560 Lexington Avenue, a 22-story office tower in New York City's Plaza District owned by Rudin. The company, which specializes in foreign exchange and global payments, will relocate its headquarters to the entire 17th floor this fall. The deal highlights the appeal of the Plaza District and Rudin's successful prebuilt and amenity programs, which allowed for a quick move-in with minimal modifications to the space.
