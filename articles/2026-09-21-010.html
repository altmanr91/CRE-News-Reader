Walbridge launches Chicago-based data center firm

REJournals — Mon, 21 Sep 2026 14:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Non-CRE / Business News

Walbridge, a Detroit-based construction firm, has launched L5 Mission Critical, a new Chicago-headquartered company specializing in comprehensive data center services. The new entity will offer services from commissioning and site readiness to critical operations, retrofits, and project management. This strategic move aims to address the rapidly expanding data center market, driven by AI and cloud growth, and provide integrated solutions for both third-party clients and Walbridge's construction projects.
