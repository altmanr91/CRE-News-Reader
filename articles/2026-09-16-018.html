Ritz-Carlton Residences Coming to Brooklyn Heights

DEVELOPMENT

Connect CRE — Tue, 15 Sep 2026 21:45:54 +0000

SUMMARY

SomeraRoad and Marriott International are partnering to redevelop the historic Hotel Bossert at 98 Montague St. in Brooklyn Heights into The Ritz-Carlton Residences, Brooklyn Heights. This project marks Brooklyn's first branded residential offering and will feature a ground-floor restaurant by Union Square Hospitality Group, which will be its first full-service eatery in the borough. The development aims to thoughtfully restore the iconic Renaissance Revival building while creating a luxury residential experience that respects its legacy and the appeal of the neighborhood.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: condo

Property Name: The Ritz-Carlton Residences, Brooklyn Heights

Address: 98 Montague St., Brooklyn, NY

Project Notes: Redevelopment of the former 14-story Renaissance Revival Hotel Bossert.
