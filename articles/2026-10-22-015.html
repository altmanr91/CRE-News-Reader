Private-Label CMBS Accounts for Half of Delinquencies in Securitized Multifamily Loans

Connect CRE — Thu, 22 Oct 2026 02:03:43 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Delinquency Report

A recent report by Trepp highlights that private-label CMBS now accounts for half of all delinquent securitized multifamily loans, despite representing only 12% of the total outstanding balance. This marks a significant shift from two years prior when CRE CLOs held the largest share of delinquent multifamily debt. The high delinquency rate in private-label CMBS, at 7.69%, contrasts sharply with Freddie Mac's 0.80% delinquency rate, suggesting a concentration of distress within the private-label segment. The report also notes that CRE CLO managers' ability to remove credit-impaired loans from pools might understate the true level of distress in that sector.
