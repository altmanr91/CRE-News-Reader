R2 scoops West Loop office building at 44% discount

ACQUISITION

The Real Deal — Chicago — Mon, 06 Jul 2026 16:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

R2, in a joint venture, acquired the nine-story office building at 600 West Fulton Street in Chicago's West Loop for $17.6 million. This transaction represents a 44% discount from its 2006 sale price. The buyer plans to renovate the lobby and amenities, including adding a coffee shop, with the expectation of quickly increasing the 58% occupancy rate, attributing past vacancies to insufficient leasing capital rather than a lack of demand.
