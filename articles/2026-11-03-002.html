Affinius Capital Provides $226M for Downtown Brooklyn Multifamily Building

REFINANCE

Commercial Observer — Tue, 03 Nov 2026 15:54:57 +0000

SUMMARY

A joint venture between Lonicera Partners and Rabina has secured $226 million in bridge debt from Affinius Capital to refinance 55 Willoughby, a 43-story multifamily complex in Downtown Brooklyn. The financing will support the 295-unit property through its lease-up and stabilization phase, which is expected to conclude early next year. The property is described as a high-quality asset in a supply-constrained submarket, benefiting from strong lease-up momentum despite a volatile market.
