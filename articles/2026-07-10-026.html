TPG lists Payton Place apartments near site of Chicago Bears’ prior stadium plans

The Real Deal — Chicago — Fri, 10 Jul 2026 12:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Property Listing / For Sale

TPG Real Estate Finance Trust is marketing the 263-unit Payton Place apartment complex for sale in Arlington Heights, IL, following its acquisition through an $80 million foreclosure from developer CA Ventures in 2024. The property, which boasts a 97% occupancy rate and 4% annual rent growth, is situated near the former Arlington International Racecourse, a site the Chicago Bears had considered for a new stadium before recent relocation discussions. Despite the uncertainty surrounding the Bears' plans, the property's strong fundamentals and the robust suburban Chicago multifamily market are expected to attract investor interest.
