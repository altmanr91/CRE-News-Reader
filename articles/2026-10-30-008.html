JLL Negotiates $32.5M Sale of Power Retail Center in Birmingham

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 30 Oct 2026 14:56:48 +0000

SUMMARY

JLL has brokered the $32.5 million sale of the second phase of Village at Lee Branch, a power retail center in Birmingham, AL. Atlanta-based Cygnus Capital sold the 83 percent leased property to Richmond-based Hackney Real Estate Partners. The center is strategically located along U.S. Highway 280 in the Meadowbrook submarket.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: retail

Property Name: Village at Lee Branch, Phase II

Address: 1201 Doug Baker Blvd., Birmingham, AL

Sale Price: $32.5M

Occupancy at Sale: 83.0%

Notable Features: Located in the Meadowbrook submarket along U.S. Highway 280; adjacent to the first phase of Village at Lee Branch, which is anchored by Publix.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: Hackney Real Estate Partners
