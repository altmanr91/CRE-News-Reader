ARCO Breaks Ground on 355K-SF Advanced Manufacturing Facility in Maryland

CONSTRUCTION

Connect CRE — Thu, 05 Nov 2026 16:20:35 +0000

SUMMARY

ARCO Design/Build has broken ground on a 355,000-square-foot advanced manufacturing facility in Hagerstown, Maryland, for Jamison Door Company and its parent company, BMP Group. This project represents the initial phase of a larger 58-acre North American manufacturing campus, aiming to enhance production capacity and integrate U.S.-based manufacturing for European brands. The first phase is slated for completion in Q1 2028, with further expansion already being planned.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Size: 355,000 SF

Completion: Q1 2028

Site Area: 58 acres

Notable Features: Advanced manufacturing facility designed to bolster growth and add U.S.-based manufacturing for European brands.

Project Notes: This is the first phase of a planned 58-acre North American manufacturing campus. Planning for a second phase is underway.
