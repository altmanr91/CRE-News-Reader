“Have people lost their minds?”: Frustrated would-be SF homebuyers set sights on Marin

The Real Deal — San Francisco — Wed, 15 Jul 2026 17:42:42 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

San Francisco's housing market is experiencing an unprecedented surge in prices and bidding wars, driven by the artificial intelligence industry boom and extremely limited inventory. This intense competition is pushing frustrated homebuyers, even those willing to pay millions over asking, to seek alternatives in Marin County. While Marin County has historically attracted families seeking more space and better schools, its current market activity is a direct spillover effect of San Francisco's overheated conditions, with median home prices and price per square foot seeing significant year-over-year increases. Real estate agents note that Marin is becoming a preferred second option for those priced out of San Francisco, despite its less direct public transit links to Silicon Valley compared to the East Bay.
