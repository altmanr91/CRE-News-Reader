Shopoff Secures Approval for Buena Park Residential Development

DEVELOPMENT

Connect CRE — Fri, 07 Aug 2026 21:19:36 +0000

SUMMARY

Shopoff Realty Investments has received unanimous approval from the Buena Park City Council for a 13.76-acre residential development at 5600 Beach Blvd. The project will transform an industrial site, currently occupied by Amway Corporation under a sale-leaseback agreement until late 2026, into a mixed-housing community. The developer emphasized the project's role in addressing the need for attainable and diverse housing options for first-time homebuyers and the senior population in Buena Park.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Address: 5600 Beach Blvd, Buena Park, CA

Units: 281

Site Area: 13.76 acres

Notable Features: The development will include 231 three-story townhomes and duplex units, and 50 for-rent senior affordable housing units.

Project Notes: The site is currently occupied by Amway Corporation with a corporate sale-leaseback in place until the end of 2026. Homes are expected to be delivered in 2029-2030.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

DEVELOPER/SPONSOR: Shopoff Realty Investments — Bill Shopoff, President and CEO
