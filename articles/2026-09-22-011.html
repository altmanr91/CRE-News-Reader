Newmark 180,000 square feet of new office leasing activity in Spring property

LEASE

REJournals — Tue, 22 Sep 2026 13:59:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Newmark has announced approximately 180,000 square feet of new office leasing activity at 10000 Energy Drive in Spring, TX, over the past 16 months. This significant leasing momentum, which includes six headquarters commitments, has brought the Class AAA, 10-story office property to nearly 90% occupancy. The leasing team successfully positioned the asset as a premier headquarters location by highlighting its fundamentals, such as access to talent, regional connectivity, infrastructure reliability, and high-quality space, which resonated with corporate users seeking operational efficiency and a high-quality workplace environment.

Located along Interstate 45, providing direct access to George Bush Intercontinental Airport.
