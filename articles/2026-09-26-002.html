Construction Firm Brasfield & Gorrie Inks 19K-SF Lease in Broward County

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Fri, 25 Sep 2026 20:55:34 +0000

SUMMARY

Construction firm Brasfield & Gorrie has signed a 19,330-square-foot office lease at Weston Pointe I in Weston, FL, relocating from Coral Gables. The move, which will triple the company's footprint, is driven by rapid growth and the need for additional space, with Weston offering a strategic location and connectivity. The general contractor is expected to occupy most of the third floor of the building by the second quarter of 2027.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Property Name: Weston Pointe I

Address: 2400 North Commerce Parkway, Weston, FL

Size: 19,330 SF

Completion: Q2 2027

Notable Features: Part of the two-building Weston Pointe campus, located just west of I-75.

Project Notes: The landlord acquired the 99,752-square-foot asset for $114.8 million in 2016.
