Venture One Real Estate breaks ground on 1.2-million-square-foot distribution facility in Crown Point

CONSTRUCTION

REJournals — Fri, 25 Sep 2026 13:27:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Venture One Real Estate has commenced construction on a 1.2-million-square-foot build-to-suit distribution facility for Amazon at VenturePark 65 in Crown Point, Indiana. This project marks Venture One's second development in Indiana, expanding its industrial footprint from the Chicago area. The facility is anticipated to create approximately 500 jobs and will bolster Amazon's distribution capabilities across the Chicagoland and Northwest Indiana regions.
