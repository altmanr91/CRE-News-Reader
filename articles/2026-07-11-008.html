Versal Arranges Sale of 279-Unit U-Haul Storage Facility in Corpus Christi

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 10 Jul 2026 14:41:44 +0000

SUMMARY

Versal has facilitated the sale of Action Storage U-Haul, a 279-unit self-storage facility in Corpus Christi, Texas. The property, encompassing 51,164 net rentable square feet, was sold by Mission Lake Estates Inc. to Dallas-based Hinze Capital.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: self-storage

Address: 3209 Rodd Field Rd, Corpus Christi, TX*
