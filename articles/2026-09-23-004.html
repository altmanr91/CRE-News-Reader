CIP Real Estate Buys San Gabriel Valley Industrial Park for $61M

ACQUISITION

Commercial Observer — Tue, 22 Sep 2026 20:55:36 +0000

SUMMARY

CIP Real Estate has acquired the Walnut Tech Business Center, an 11-building industrial and flex complex in Walnut, CA, for $60.7 million from an affiliate of AEW. The property, which is 92% leased, comprises 109 suites and is strategically located near the 60 Freeway, serving the supply-constrained City of Industry area. CIP plans a $3.5 million capital improvement program for the asset, recognizing the scarcity of quality small bay industrial parks in this Southern California submarket.
