DC Partners Tops Out 210-Room Hotel in Fredericksburg, Texas

CONSTRUCTION

REBusinessOnline — Mon, 03 Aug 2026 15:04:37 +0000

SUMMARY

DC Partners has topped out a 210-room Kimpton-branded hotel in Fredericksburg, Texas. The hotel, which began construction last summer and is slated to open in 2027, will be part of The Meuse, a 22-acre mixed-use development. It will feature a full-service restaurant, multiple bars, a large outdoor pool, fitness center, café, and extensive meeting and event space.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: hotel

Property Name: Kimpton Fredericksburg

Keys: 210

Completion: 2027

Notable Features: Located within The Meuse, a 22-acre mixed-use development; features a full-service restaurant, multiple bars, 10,000-square-foot outdoor pool and deck, fitness center, café, and 18,500 square feet of indoor/outdoor meeting and event space.

Project Notes: Construction began in summer 2025.
