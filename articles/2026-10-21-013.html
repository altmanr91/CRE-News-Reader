Brixton Capital acquires 120,522-square-foot grocery-anchored shopping center in Sachse

ACQUISITION

REJournals — Wed, 21 Oct 2026 13:22:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Brixton Capital has acquired Oak Grove Plaza, a 120,552-square-foot, grocery-anchored shopping center in Sachse, Texas, from Nuveen. This acquisition marks Brixton Capital's entry into the Texas retail market, complementing its existing multifamily portfolio in the state. The property, anchored by a high-performing Kroger, offers strong patronage for its diverse mix of daily-needs services and medical providers, with potential for value-add development through an additional pad site.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: retail

Property Name: Oak Grove Plaza

Address: 5190-5250 Highway 78, Sachse, TX

Size: 120,552 SF

Site Area: 15 acres

Notable Features: Class A property anchored by a Kroger that has operated on-site for 23 years and performs substantially above chain average. Includes a Kroger gas pad.

Project Notes: Brixton Capital has the opportunity to build out an additional pad on the property.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

The DFW retail market is strong, with an average household income of $128,741 and nearly 17% of the population earning $200,000 or more. The Firewheel trade area, where Oak Grove Plaza is located, is a dominant retail concentration in the northeast quadrant of the metroplex, benefiting from an affluent workforce pushing east from the Plano corporate corridor.
