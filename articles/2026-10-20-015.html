Urban Outfitters–Leased Retail Condo in Wicker Park Changes Hands

SALE

Connect CRE — Mon, 19 Oct 2026 22:48:12 +0000

SUMMARY

Greenstone Partners has facilitated the sale of a 10,600-square-foot retail condominium in Chicago's Wicker Park neighborhood for $4.125 million. The property, fully leased to a national apparel retailer, was sold by Newcastle Investors. The retailer has been a long-standing presence in the area, having occupied the space since 2005.

DATA POINTS

Address: 1521 N. Milwaukee Ave., Chicago, IL

Size: 10,600 SF

$/SF: $389/SF (calculated)

Occupancy at Sale: 100.0%

Notable Features: Two-level retail condominium with an additional 2,676-square-foot lower level and approximately 50 feet of frontage on Milwaukee Avenue.

Project Notes: The seller, Newcastle Investors, acquired the property in 2013. The tenant has occupied the property since 2005.
