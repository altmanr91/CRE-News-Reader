PwC Commits to 350K SF at Canary Wharf Complex

LEASE

Connect CRE — Tue, 07 Jul 2026 11:21:57 +0000

SUMMARY

PwC has committed to a significant lease of approximately 350,000 square feet at One Eden, a redeveloped office property within the Canary Wharf mixed-use complex in London. This strategic move addresses PwC's immediate space needs as it plans for the refurbishment of its Embankment Place offices in 2030 and the expiration of its More London lease in 2035. The firm's decision underscores the growing demand for high-quality, sustainable office space that fosters collaboration and innovation, according to the landlord.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Property Name: One Eden

Address: 33 Canada Square, London, UK

Size: 350,000 SF

Notable Features: Redevelopment of an existing office property; located within the Canary Wharf mixed-use complex.

Project Notes: The space is currently occupied by Citigroup's EMEA headquarters and will be redeveloped prior to PwC's arrival. The lease helps PwC manage its future office needs, including a refurbishment of its Embankment Place offices in 2030 and the expiration of its More London lease in 2035.
