Cushman & Wakefield Arranges $33.7M Sale of Office Building in Ontario, California

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Wed, 23 Sep 2026 13:46:58 +0000

SUMMARY

MGR Real Estate has acquired 901 Via Piemonte, a Class A office building in Ontario, California, from Greenlaw Partners LLC for $33.7 million. The transaction was brokered by Cushman & Wakefield, which represented the seller, while MGR Real Estate's team represented the buyer.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Property Name: 901 Via Piemonte

Address: 901 Via Piemonte, Ontario, CA

Size: 122,602 SF

Sale Price: $33.7M

$/SF: $275/SF (calculated)

Year Built: 2008

Notable Features: The five-story building includes 1,000 onsite parking spaces, advanced security systems, and energy-efficient infrastructure.
