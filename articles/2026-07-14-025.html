After years of buyer pursuit, family sells Medley industrial portfolio for $39M

SALE

The Real Deal — Miami — Mon, 13 Jul 2026 20:53:44 +0000

SUMMARY

The Artiles family has sold a four-building industrial portfolio in Medley, FL, to Boston-based Longpoint for $38.8 million. The off-market transaction, which closed after years of unsolicited offers, reflects the continued strong investor interest in South Florida's industrial market despite recent softening in demand for larger spaces and an uptick in vacancy rates. The seller's broker noted that Longpoint was chosen due to its competitive offer and ability to close the deal efficiently.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Miami-Dade's overall industrial vacancy rate reached 7.1 percent in Q2, up from 6.3 percent year-over-year. The average county rent for industrial properties hit $17.22 per square foot, triple net, in Q2, representing a 4 percent increase year-over-year. J.P. Morgan Investment Management recently paid $65 million for an industrial complex used as a printing press for the South Florida Sun-Sentinel, sold by Blackstone's Link Logistics.
