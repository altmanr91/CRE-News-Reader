Greystone Refis Hollywood, Fla., Apartments With $109M Loan

REFINANCE

Commercial Observer — Fri, 30 Oct 2026 15:19:52 +0000

SUMMARY

A joint venture between bti partners and Bridge Investment Group secured a $109 million Freddie Mac-backed loan from Greystone to refinance The Bread Building, a 362-unit multifamily property in Hollywood, FL. The sponsors acquired the former office building in 2021 for $11 million and completed its conversion to apartments in 2025. The property achieved full occupancy in September 2026, demonstrating strong market fundamentals and making it an excellent candidate for the cash-out refinance with full-term interest-only and 35-year amortization.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Address: 1770 South Young Circle, Hollywood, FL

Units: 362

Loan Amount: $109.0M

Loan Per Unit: $301,105/unit (calculated)

Occupancy at Close: 100.0%

Completion: 2025

Notable Features: The 25-story property includes approximately 17,000 square feet of retail space, 478 parking spaces, a swimming pool with cabanas, a sun deck, an outdoor kitchen, and a fitness center. It is located just south of ArtsPark at Young Circle, a 10-acre recreational green space.

Project Notes: The property was previously the headquarters for Hollywood Bread Company and was acquired in 2021 for $11 million for redevelopment into apartments. The loan utilized Freddie Mac's Lease-Up Loan program.
