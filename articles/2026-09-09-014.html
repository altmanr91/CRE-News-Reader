Updates to the Wisconsin State Historic Tax Credit Program

REJournals — Wed, 09 Sep 2026 10:02:00 +0000

SUMMARY

CRE Legal / Regulatory

Wisconsin Governor Tony Evers signed Act 238 into law, significantly modifying the Wisconsin State Historic Tax Credit Program to enhance accessibility for historic rehabilitation projects. The new legislation removes the adjusted basis test for minimum expenditure, requiring only a $50,000 spend, which is expected to benefit smaller projects, especially in rural areas. Additionally, projects that previously received credits can now reapply after 15 years, incentivizing long-term investment in historic properties by providing a renewed financing source for subsequent repairs.

DATA POINTS

Federal Historic Tax Credit Program: 20% tax credit against federal income tax liability for qualified rehabilitation expenditures

Wisconsin State Historic Tax Credit Program (prior to Act 238): 20% tax credit against state income tax liability, minimum project expenditure of $50,000 and adjusted basis, $3.5 million maximum credit per project life

Wisconsin State Historic Tax Credit Program (after Act 238): Minimum project expenditure of $50,000 (adjusted basis test removed), $3.5 million maximum credit per project life, reapplication allowed after 15 years
