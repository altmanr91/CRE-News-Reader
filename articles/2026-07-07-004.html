Law Firm Loeb & Loeb Expands to 179K SF at Rudin’s 345 Park Avenue

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Mon, 06 Jul 2026 20:18:56 +0000

SUMMARY

A global law firm has expanded its New York City headquarters at Rudin's 345 Park Avenue, signing a 16-year lease extension for its existing space and adding nearly 19,000 square feet. The firm, which has been a tenant in the building since 1993, now occupies a total of 178,959 square feet. The landlord noted that ongoing investments in the property to maintain its status as a premier corporate destination were instrumental in securing the expanded commitment.
