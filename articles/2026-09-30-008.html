Cadence McShane Completes 75,000 SF Industrial Project in Kyle, Texas

CONSTRUCTION

REBusinessOnline — Wed, 30 Sep 2026 14:19:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Cadence McShane has completed the first phase of Kyle Technology Park, a 75,000-square-foot industrial project in Kyle, Texas. This initial phase consists of three 25,000-square-foot buildings designed for multiple users with 35-foot clear heights. The project is part of a larger 41-acre development by TIG Real Estate Services, which is ultimately planned to include 11 buildings totaling approximately 300,000 square feet.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Property Name: Kyle Technology Park

Size: 75,000 SF

Phase I: 75,000 square feet across three buildings

Total Project (All Phases): 11 buildings totaling approximately 300,000 square feet on 41 acres

Site Area: 41 acres

Notable Features: Buildings feature 35-foot clear heights and are designed to accommodate multiple users.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

GENERAL CONTRACTOR: Cadence McShane

ARCHITECT: Studio 8 Architects

DEVELOPER/SPONSOR: TIG Real Estate Services
