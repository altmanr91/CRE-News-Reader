Roanoke Helming Hotel/Convention Center Project

CONSTRUCTION

Connect CRE — Thu, 08 Oct 2026 15:42:51 +0000

SUMMARY

Garfield Public/Private has broken ground on The Cade Roanoke, a lifestyle-branded hotel and convention center in Roanoke, TX. The 205-key hotel and 20,000-square-foot convention center will address the town's increasing demand for tourism and event space. The project is expected to take approximately 23 months to complete.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: hotel

Property Name: The Cade Roanoke

Address: 600 S. Oak St., Roanoke, TX

Size: 169,152 SF

Keys: 205

Completion: 23 months from groundbreaking

Notable Features: Includes approximately 20,000 square feet of meeting space, a signature restaurant, and a 300-space parking garage. Hotel amenities include a fitness center, internal courtyard with pool and pool bar, lobby bar and lounge with outdoor patio, and a full-service coffee bar.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

DEVELOPER/SPONSOR: Garfield Public/Private

OWNER: Town of Roanoke

ARCHITECT: BOKA Powell

GENERAL CONTRACTOR: Brasfield & Gorrie
