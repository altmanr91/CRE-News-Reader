Interra Realty brokers $3.56 million sale of apartment building in Chicago’s West Ridge neighborhood

SALE

REJournals — Mon, 12 Oct 2026 16:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Interra Realty brokered the $3.56 million sale of a 13-unit courtyard apartment building in Chicago's West Ridge neighborhood. The fully occupied property, featuring updated two-bedroom units, was sold by an affiliate of Root Property Group to Paul Fortman. The transaction highlights continued investor interest in well-maintained multifamily assets in established Chicago neighborhoods.
