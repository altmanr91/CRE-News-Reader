Mermaid Inn Restaurateurs Ink 2K SF at 61 West 74th Street for New Eatery

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Thu, 06 Aug 2026 20:22:28 +0000

SUMMARY

Restaurateurs Danny Abrams and Cindy Smith, co-founders of Mermaid Inn, have signed a 2,300-square-foot lease for a new, as-yet-unnamed restaurant concept at 61 West 74th Street in New York City. The space, which includes a basement, was secured at an annual rental rate of $130 per square foot. This new venture expands their presence in the Upper West Side, building on the success of their nearby Mermaid Inn location.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: retail

Address: 61 West 74th Street, New York City, NY

Size: 2,300 SF

Rental Rate: $130.00/SF/yr

Project Notes: The site previously housed Patsy's Pizzeria, which closed in January 2026.
