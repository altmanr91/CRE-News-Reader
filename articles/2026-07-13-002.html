More Commercial Real Estate Owners See Value in Special Servicing Platforms

Commercial Observer — Mon, 13 Jul 2026 11:02:50 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

The article discusses the growing trend of commercial real estate owners launching special servicing platforms, leveraging their deep market expertise to resolve troubled assets. RXR is the latest firm to enter this space with its REX Loan Services platform, approved by Fitch Ratings and Morningstar. The move is seen as a way to complement RXR's lending business and utilize its extensive experience in the office sector, which still faces significant distress. While concerns about potential conflicts of interest exist, particularly when a competitor acts as a special servicer, proponents argue that owner-operators bring unique insights that can expedite solutions for defaulted CMBS loans.
