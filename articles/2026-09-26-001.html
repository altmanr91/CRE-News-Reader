U.S. Government Pays $285M for Defense Health Agency HQ in Northern Virginia

ACQUISITION

Commercial Observer — Fri, 25 Sep 2026 21:50:16 +0000

SUMMARY

The U.S. federal government has acquired the Defense Health Agency's headquarters campus in Falls Church, VA, for $285 million. The Naval Facilities Engineering Systems Command purchased the 44-acre property, which includes three Class B office buildings totaling approximately 686,000 square feet, from GBA Associates. This acquisition is part of a broader federal strategy to reduce lease obligations and is expected to generate significant annual savings compared to the previous 15-year, $370 million lease signed in 2010.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Address: 7700 Arlington Boulevard, Falls Church, VA

Size: 686,000 SF

Sale Price: $285.0M

Site Area: 44 acres

Notable Features: Campus includes three Class B office buildings and a separate parcel with 2,000 parking spaces.

Project Notes: Buildings constructed between 1954 and 1984. Plans filed in 2018 for an additional 179,200-square-foot office building and more parking did not proceed.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

The federal government has made several large acquisitions of previously leased properties in the Washington region, including the CIA's $247 million purchase of an office in Chantilly, VA, in 2025; the $531 million acquisition of Liberty Crossing I and II in McLean, VA, in 2023; and the GSA's $760 million purchase of the Department of Transportation headquarters near the Washington Navy Yard prior to the pandemic.
