Equus Capital Partners Acquires 300,075 SF Industrial Development in Shrewsbury, Massachusetts

ACQUISITION

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 09 Oct 2026 16:21:10 +0000

SUMMARY

Equus Capital Partners has acquired Centech Park North, a newly constructed 300,075-square-foot industrial development in Shrewsbury, Massachusetts. Developed by NorthBridge Partners and completed in 2023, the two-building park was fully leased at the time of sale to a diverse mix of tenants in life sciences, advanced manufacturing, and commercial services.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Property Name: Centech Park North

Address: 384-386 South St, Shrewsbury, MA*

Size: 300,075 SF

Occupancy at Sale: 100.0%

Year Built: 2023

Notable Features: Features 36-foot clear heights, 64 dock-high doors, and four drive-in doors.
