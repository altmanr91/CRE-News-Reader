Empire State Building Observation Deck Hemorrhaging Visitors, Value

Bisnow News Feed — Thu, 30 Jul 2026 14:31:41 -0400

SUMMARY

Market Research / Earnings Report

Empire State Realty Trust (ESRT) recorded a $166 million write-down on the value of its Empire State Building observation deck, leading to a net loss of 15 cents per share in Q2 2026. The observation deck's net operating income (NOI) plummeted by half year-over-year, from $24 million to $12.4 million, primarily due to a significant decline in international tourism and a 45% drop in visitors using discounted sightseeing passes. Despite the World Cup bringing over a million people to the region, ESRT's CEO Tony Malkin noted that the event did not translate into increased observatory visits, as attendees were primarily focused on the sporting event. The REIT is re-evaluating its business model, adjusting advertising strategies, and shifting focus to AI searches to attract more domestic visitors, as competition from newer observation decks like The Edge and Summit One Vanderbilt intensifies.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Manhattan's observation deck market has seen increased competition with the opening of The Edge in Hudson Yards in 2020 and Summit One Vanderbilt in 2021. Summit One Vanderbilt reported stable Q2 2026 revenue of $31.5 million, slightly up from $31 million in Q2 2025, and does not participate in discounted sightseeing pass programs.
