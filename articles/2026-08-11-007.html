Eldridge Capital Provides $278M Loan for Refinancing of Midtown Atlanta Office Tower

REFINANCE

REBusinessOnline — Mon, 10 Aug 2026 13:57:26 +0000

SUMMARY

Eldridge Capital Management has provided a $278 million refinancing loan for Ten Twenty Spring, a 540,000-square-foot office tower in Midtown Atlanta. The 25-story building, owned by Portman and Blackstone Real Estate, was delivered in 2024 and serves as an anchor for the larger Spring Quarter mixed-use development. The tower boasts a strong tenant mix, including a financial institution establishing its corporate headquarters there, and offers extensive amenities.
