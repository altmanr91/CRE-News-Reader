Henley Capital Underway on Hotel Redevelopment Project Near Downtown Dallas

DEVELOPMENT

REBusinessOnline — Wed, 28 Oct 2026 15:00:11 +0000

SUMMARY

Henley Capital is redeveloping the former City Inn & Suites hotel site near downtown Dallas into a mixed-use property featuring multifamily and retail components. Demolition of the existing hotel began in mid-August, with Susser Bank providing financing for the project.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: mixed-use

Property Name: former City Inn & Suites hotel site

Address: 223 S. Ewing Ave., Dallas, TX

Notable Features: Located adjacent to Halperin Park and approximately four miles south of downtown Dallas.

Project Notes: Demolition of the existing hotel began in mid-August.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

DEVELOPER/SPONSOR: Henley Capital

LENDER: Susser Bank
