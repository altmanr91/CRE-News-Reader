Nir Meir fails to reach plea deal, will head to trial for fraud

The Real Deal — New York — Wed, 22 Jul 2026 21:13:45 +0000

SUMMARY

CRE Legal / Regulatory

Nir Meir, former executive at HFZ Capital, has not reached a plea deal in his alleged $86 million fraud case related to the XI condo project. Despite his attorney's efforts to secure an agreement, Meir has pleaded not guilty and is set to proceed to trial in October. He is the only one of nine co-defendants who has not accepted a plea deal, with others receiving no jail time and minimal fines.
