Midtown Capital Inks Refi for 109K-SF Atlanta-Area Office Building

REFINANCE

Connect CRE — Mon, 19 Oct 2026 14:40:09 +0000

SUMMARY

Midtown Capital Partners has secured a $17.1 million refinancing loan for The Crawford Global HQ, a 109,171-square-foot office building in Peachtree Corners, GA. The financing, provided by City National Bank, was arranged by CBRE's Debt & Structured Finance team. The transaction highlights continued lender confidence in office properties with strong cash flow and tenants demonstrating long-term commitment to their space.
