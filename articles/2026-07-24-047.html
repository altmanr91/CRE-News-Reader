The World Cup Filled Stadiums. Hotel Rooms Were A Different Story

Bisnow News Feed — Thu, 23 Jul 2026 15:31:32 -0400

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

The 2026 World Cup, while setting attendance records, did not fully meet the hospitality industry's expectations for a hotel bonanza. Hotels saw strong revenue per available room (RevPAR) growth and average daily rate (ADR) increases on match days, particularly around marquee games. However, this was offset by fans booking last-minute, staying for shorter durations than projected, and a significant drop in convention and business travel in host cities, which largely displaced traditional demand. Performance varied widely by market, with some cities experiencing substantial gains while others saw overall occupancy dip below normal summer levels.

DATA POINTS

World Cup attendance: More than 6.8 million fans

RevPAR increase on match days (Round of 16 and quarterfinals): More than 40%

ADR increase on match days (Round of 16 and quarterfinals): Nearly 35%

Occupancy decrease on non-match days: Nearly 4%
