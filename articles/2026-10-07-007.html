Affinius Capital Provides s $94.8M Construction Loan for Student Housing Project Near Oregon State University

LOAN

REBusinessOnline — Wed, 07 Oct 2026 15:22:19 +0000

SUMMARY

Affinius Capital has provided a $94.8 million construction loan for Zev Corvallis, a 13-story student housing development near Oregon State University. The project, developed by Fields Holdings, will offer 667 beds across 262 units and is slated for completion in fall 2028. The property will feature a comprehensive amenity package and 4,500 square feet of retail space.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: student housing

Property Name: Zev Corvallis

Units: 262

Beds: 667

Loan Amount: $94.8M

Completion: fall 2028

Notable Features: 13-story building with a pool, rooftop deck, indoor basketball court, sauna, theater, fitness center, yoga room, study and resident lounges, and 4,500 square feet of retail space.

Project Notes: Residences will be furnished with quartz countertops, stainless steel appliances, modern cabinetry, in-unit washers/dryers and smart-home technology.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

LENDER: Affinius Capital

SPONSOR: Fields Holdings
