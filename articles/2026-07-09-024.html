Engineering failure likely behind Pfizer project scare

The Real Deal — New York — Wed, 08 Jul 2026 18:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

CRE Legal / Regulatory

An engineering failure is the likely cause of structural issues at Metro Loft and David Werner Real Estate Investments' office-to-residential conversion of the former Pfizer headquarters on East 42nd Street. Support columns buckled and several upper floors sagged on an 11-story cantilevered addition to the original building. Structural engineering experts indicate that the incident was likely due to an oversight in reinforcing existing steel i-beam columns for the added weight, rather than the office-to-residential conversion project type itself. The project, which will transform two connected office buildings into over 1,600 apartments, involves recladding the 33-story tower at 235 East 42nd Street and adding 19 stories to the nine-story building at 219 East 42nd Street.
