Lee & Associates Negotiates 28,212 SF Industrial Lease in McKinney, Texas

LEASE

REBusinessOnline — Wed, 16 Sep 2026 14:03:23 +0000

SUMMARY

Lee & Associates has facilitated a 28,212-square-foot industrial lease at 320 Wilmeth Road in McKinney, Texas. The transaction involved Blount Fine Foods as the tenant and ML Realty Partners as the landlord. The property is part of the larger McKinney Trade Center development.
