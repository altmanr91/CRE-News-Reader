Eliot Spitzer Gains State Approval to Redevelop 985 Fifth Avenue After Legal Bout

DEVELOPMENT

Commercial Observer — Tue, 25 Aug 2026 21:23:47 +0000

SUMMARY

Former New York Governor Eliot Spitzer has received state approval to proceed with the redevelopment of 985 Fifth Avenue, following a successful lawsuit against the state's Division of Homes and Community Renewal (HCR). This approval allows Spitzer to not renew rent-stabilized leases, a critical step for the demolition of the existing 25-story rental property and the construction of luxury condominiums. The project, designed by SLCE Architects, is slated to feature 25 condo units.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: condo

Property Name: 985 Fifth Avenue

Address: 985 Fifth Avenue, New York City, NY

Units: 25

Notable Features: Redevelopment of a 25-story rental property into luxury condominiums.

Project Notes: Approval to not renew rent-stabilized leases was granted after a two-year request and a lawsuit against the state. The New York City Landmarks Preservation Commission approved demolition in 2023.
