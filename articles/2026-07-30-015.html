U.S. Apartment Rents Post Eighth Consecutive Month of Growth

Connect CRE — Wed, 29 Jul 2026 21:46:05 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Rent Report

U.S. apartment rents experienced their eighth consecutive month of growth in July, with the national average increasing by 0.03% to $1,747. This modest rise follows a period of flat to declining performance in late 2025. Despite the typical summer slowdown, July's gains were particularly subdued, indicating a notable deceleration in monthly pricing momentum. While annual rent growth is gradually improving, elevated supply levels continue to temper national pricing.

DATA POINTS

National average apartment rent (July): $1,747

Month-over-month rent growth (July): +0.03%

Annual rent growth (July): +1.0%

Annual rent growth (June): +0.9%

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

U.S. apartment rents were essentially flat in July, rising +0.03% to $1,747. This marks the eighth consecutive month of positive rent increases. Annual rent growth accelerated to +1.0% in July 2026 from +0.9% in June. Elevated supply levels continue to restrain pricing momentum nationally.
