SparrowHawk acquires EQT portfolio of 20 industrial assets across Midwest

ACQUISITION

REJournals — Fri, 14 Aug 2026 13:16:19 +0000

SUMMARY

SparrowHawk, an industrial real estate investment and management firm, completed its largest transaction to date by acquiring a 20-property industrial portfolio totaling 4.4 million square feet across six Midwest logistics markets for just under $400 million. The acquisition from EQT expands SparrowHawk's presence in key regional growth markets including St. Louis, Cincinnati, Cleveland, Columbus, Dayton, and Louisville. The portfolio features a diverse tenant mix and is expected to provide significant rent growth opportunities, aligning with SparrowHawk's strategy for capital deployment and platform expansion.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Portfolio acquisitions in the $250 million to $750 million range have quadrupled in volume. The capital markets are highly liquid, with abundant sources of capital from insurance companies, banks, and credit and debt funds.
