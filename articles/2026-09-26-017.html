New-Construction Car Wash Properties Sell in Murrieta, Riverside

SALE

Connect CRE — Fri, 25 Sep 2026 21:03:38 +0000

SUMMARY

RealSource Group has facilitated the sale of two newly constructed Quick Quack Car Wash properties in Murrieta and Riverside, California, for a combined total of $8.63 million. Both properties are secured by new 20-year absolute triple-net ground leases. The transactions generated significant buyer interest, with the ground lease structure requiring extensive education for investors accustomed to fee-simple deals, but ultimately attracted local buyers with strong regional investment strategies.
