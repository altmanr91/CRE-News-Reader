Charney Acquires 143 Roebling in Williamsburg

ACQUISITION

Connect CRE — Wed, 01 Jul 2026 20:38:04 +0000

SUMMARY

Charney Companies has acquired 143 Roebling St., a 100,000-square-foot mixed-use building in Williamsburg, Brooklyn, for $20 million. The company plans to restore and modernize the vacant property, which was originally built in 1907 and previously housed residential and retail spaces. Financing for the acquisition was provided by BH3 Fund Advisors.
