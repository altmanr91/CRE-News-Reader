Trademark Completes 321-Unit Vickery Apartments in Downtown Fort Worth

CONSTRUCTION

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 14 Aug 2026 15:08:29 +0000

SUMMARY

Trademark Property Co. has completed The Vickery, a 321-unit multifamily project in downtown Fort Worth. Developed in partnership with SCOA Real Estate Partners and designed by GFF, the project includes 307 apartments, 14 townhomes, and a 5,300-square-foot restaurant with a second-story lounge. Construction on the development began in February 2024 and was topped out in January 2025, with residential leasing commencing in fall 2025.
