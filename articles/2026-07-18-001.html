Apartment Complex Sells for $51M in Los Angeles’ Miracle Mile

ACQUISITION

Commercial Observer — Fri, 17 Jul 2026 19:25:57 +0000

SUMMARY

Prime Residential has acquired the 132-unit Palm Court Apartments in Los Angeles' Miracle Mile neighborhood for $51.3 million from an LLC tied to Harrison Properties. The transaction, valued at approximately $388,000 per unit, reflects a pickup in multifamily investment activity in Los Angeles, despite the impact of the Measure ULA transfer tax on larger deals. The seller recently completed extensive upgrades to the property, which features a strong amenity package and a desirable location near major attractions and new transit infrastructure.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Multifamily investment sales in Los Angeles increased approximately 25 percent in the 12 months ending March, with the city accounting for over 20 percent of all apartment trades in primary U.S. markets. Transactions above $10 million were down 50 percent compared to the year before Measure ULA took effect in 2023. Los Angeles' multifamily vacancy rate was 4.8 percent at the start of 2026, with average effective rents increasing 1.4 percent year-over-year to $2,853 per month. Vacancy is projected to reach 5 percent by year-end 2026 with 8,500 new units delivered.
