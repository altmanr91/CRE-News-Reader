Colliers Arranges $44.5M Construction Loan for Multifamily Project in Norwood, Massachusetts

LOAN

REBusinessOnline — Thu, 10 Sep 2026 13:26:49 +0000

SUMMARY

Colliers has secured a $44.5 million construction loan from Beacon Bank for Tremont Asset Management's 145-unit multifamily development in Norwood, Massachusetts. The five-story project will feature studio, one-, and two-bedroom apartments, with 20% designated as affordable housing. Amenities will include an outdoor pool, fitness center, golf simulator, and coworking spaces.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Address: 259 Lenox St, Norwood, MA

Units: 145

Loan Amount: $44.5M

Loan Per Unit: $306,897/unit (calculated)

Notable Features: 20% of units will be affordable housing; amenities include an outdoor pool, fitness center, golf simulator, coworking spaces, and grilling/dining stations.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

DEVELOPER/SPONSOR: Tremont Asset Management
