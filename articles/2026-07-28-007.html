Foss Secures Tax Credits for 25-Story Office Tower Conversion in Des Moines

LOAN

REBusinessOnline — Mon, 27 Jul 2026 14:15:10 +0000

SUMMARY

Foss & Co., a tax credit syndicator, has secured financing for the conversion of the Financial Center, a 25-story office tower in downtown Des Moines, into a mixed-use property. The project, developed by Lawmark Capital, will create 209 market-rate apartment units and 152,000 square feet of commercial space. The financing includes approximately $14.6 million in 2026 federal Historic Tax Credits, supporting the transformation of this historic building.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: mixed-use

Property Name: Financial Center

Address: 666 Walnut St, Des Moines, IA*

Units: 209

Year Built: 1973

Notable Features: The building was the tallest in Iowa upon its completion and will feature a golf simulator, fitness center, rooftop deck with a pool, and direct access to the downtown skywalk system.

Project Notes: The project involves the conversion of floors one through three (partially) and floors four through 18 into residential units. The financing includes federal Historic Tax Credits.

* Address sourced separately, not in original article.
