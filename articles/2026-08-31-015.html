McShane Construction Company to build 52-unit affordable housing community in Portage

CONSTRUCTION

REJournals — Mon, 31 Aug 2026 13:47:00 +0000

SUMMARY

McShane Construction Company has been selected by Impact Seven and Harmony Housing Partners to construct Rivers Crossing, a 52-unit affordable housing community in Portage, Wisconsin. The development will feature a podium-style building with three stories of wood frame construction over an underground parking garage, offering a mix of one, two, and three-bedroom units. The project is designed to achieve Wisconsin Green Built Homes Zero LEED Low-rise Equivalence certification, with completion anticipated in October 2027.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Rivers Crossing

Address: 2941 Hunters Trail, Portage, WI

Units: 52

Site Area: 2.12 acres

Notable Features: Podium-style building with underground parking; designed to achieve Wisconsin Green Built Homes Zero LEED Low-rise Equivalence certification; includes units for adults with disabilities.
