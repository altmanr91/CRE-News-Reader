Joby Aviation establishes hub at Perot Field Fort Worth Alliance Airport

LEASE

REJournals — Mon, 10 Aug 2026 10:01:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Joby Aviation, a developer of electric vertical takeoff and landing (eVTOL) aircraft, has leased 45,000 square feet at Alliance Air Trade Center in Haslet, Texas. This marks the company's first significant presence in North Texas and positions it within Hillwood's 27,000-acre AllianceTexas development, a major aviation and logistics hub. The move is expected to strengthen the region's role in advanced air mobility, leveraging the area's multimodal transportation infrastructure and skilled workforce.

Size: 45,000 SF
