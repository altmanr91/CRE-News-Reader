Dorsey Development Adds Gulf Breeze Retail Center to Gulf Coast Holdings

ACQUISITION

Connect CRE — Wed, 04 Nov 2026 14:29:11 +0000

SUMMARY

Dorsey Development has acquired the Live Oak Shopping Center in Gulf Breeze, FL, from Exposition Properties for $14 million. The 107,065-square-foot retail center is strategically located near Pensacola Bay and the Live Oak Nature Preserve. The property is anchored by a dual-branded retailer occupying a significant portion of the space at below-market rents, presenting a strong opportunity for future rental growth.
