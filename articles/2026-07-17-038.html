Life Sciences Employers Consider Areas Beyond Established Biopharm Hubs

Connect CRE — Fri, 17 Jul 2026 05:01:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Labor Report

A new report from Cushman & Wakefield indicates that while established life sciences hubs like Boston-Cambridge and the San Francisco Bay Area continue to dominate in R&D and pharmaceutical manufacturing employment, secondary markets are gaining competitiveness. The report highlights that employers are increasingly considering factors beyond traditional biotech clusters, such as the cost of living, which favors Midwest and Southeast markets offering more cost-efficient labor environments. Education pipelines are also a critical differentiator, with Texas markets like Austin, Dallas, and Houston showing significant growth in life-science related degree completions, strengthening their talent pools. Despite some hiring bumps in 2023-2025, life sciences employment has steadied in the first half of 2026, with a modest but notable shift towards earlier-career hires, though challenges remain in filling specialized roles in AI and data science.
