CBRE brokers $15.6 million sale of 7-Eleven truck store, convenience store in New Albany

ACQUISITION

REJournals — Wed, 28 Oct 2026 13:07:00 +0000

SUMMARY

A private Southern California investor has acquired a newly developed 7-Eleven (Speedway) truck stop and convenience store in New Albany, Ohio, for $15.6 million. The 4,816-square-foot property, situated on a 7.38-acre site, benefits from a new 15-year triple-net lease with the investment-grade tenant. The acquisition highlights the strong investment appeal of Central Ohio's rapidly expanding commercial corridors, driven by significant corporate presence and ongoing economic development.
