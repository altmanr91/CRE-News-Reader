Riviera Beach ponies up nearly $3M for workforce housing project

DEVELOPMENT

The Real Deal — Miami — Fri, 10 Jul 2026 13:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

O'Connor Capital Partners secured approval for $2.8 million in assistance from the Riviera Beach City Council for the Marina Annex, a 175-unit workforce housing project. These funds will primarily cover water, sewer, and impact fees for the development, which is slated to begin construction by the end of the year. The $75 million project, developed in partnership with Urban Farmers, the Riviera Beach Housing Authority, and the Alpha Phi Alpha Foundation, will offer one-, two-, and three-bedroom units for families earning 80% to 120% of the area median income, alongside 4,000 square feet of retail space for local entrepreneurs. The project also received significant funding from HUD and Palm Beach County.
