Law Firm Seward & Kissel Signs 171K-SF Renewal at One Battery Park Plaza

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Fri, 09 Oct 2026 13:50:58 +0000

SUMMARY

Seward & Kissel, a financial services law firm, has renewed its 171,112-square-foot office lease at One Battery Park Plaza in the Financial District for an 11-year term. The building is jointly owned by Rudin Management and Allianz Real Estate. This significant renewal underscores the continued appeal of Lower Manhattan for established firms.
