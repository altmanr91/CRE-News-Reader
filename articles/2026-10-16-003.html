Related Urban Secures $129M Financing for Downtown Miami Project

LOAN

Commercial Observer — Thu, 15 Oct 2026 21:00:26 +0000

SUMMARY

Related Urban Development Group has secured $129 million in financing for Gallery Lummus Parc, a 257-unit affordable housing development in Downtown Miami. The project, located on a 0.7-acre site next to Lummus Park and near a major transit station, will feature 27 stories of residential units and a five-level parking garage. Over 80% of the apartments will be income-restricted, catering to a wide range of income levels, including workforce and market-rate units. Construction is slated to begin this month and is expected to be completed in two years.
