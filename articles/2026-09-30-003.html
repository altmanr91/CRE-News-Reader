Värde Partners Supplies $66M Refi for Phoenix Single-Family Rental Property

REFINANCE

Commercial Observer — Wed, 30 Sep 2026 13:00:10 +0000

SUMMARY

Empire Group secured a $65.5 million refinancing loan from Värde Partners for its newly completed 240-unit single-family rental community, Village at Sonoran Vista, in Phoenix. The three-year floating-rate loan includes two one-year extension options. The property's location near Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company's Arizona campus is expected to drive demand for rental housing, especially with rising mortgage rates making homeownership less accessible.
