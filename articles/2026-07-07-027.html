PGIM cashes out of SoNo East Apartments for $125M

SALE

The Real Deal — Chicago — Mon, 06 Jul 2026 18:15:00 +0000

SUMMARY

PGIM, the asset management arm of Prudential Financial, sold the 324-unit SoNo East Apartments in Chicago's Lincoln Park for $125.2 million to RPM Living. This transaction represents a significant gain for PGIM, which acquired the property in 2012 for $105.3 million. The sale reflects RPM Living's continued expansion in the Chicago market, which has seen strong apartment performance due to limited new supply and robust rent growth.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Chicago's apartment market has outperformed other U.S. markets due to a historic dip in new supply, with less than 4,000 new units expected in 2026. Rent growth in Chicago led all major U.S. metros in fall 2025, with typical asking rents up 6% year-over-year.
