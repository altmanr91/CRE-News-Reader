The Plan: 360 Lexington Avenue Has Been ‘Completely Reinvented’

Commercial Observer — Wed, 09 Sep 2026 10:00:40 +0000

SUMMARY

Development

AmTrustRE has completed a significant renovation of 360 Lexington Avenue, a boutique office building in New York City, which it acquired in 2024 for $65.5 million. The comprehensive overhaul included a redesigned facade, a reinvented lobby entrance with an underlit marquee, and the resolution of a persistent ventilation issue. A key feature of the renovation is a new hospitality-inspired amenity center on the eighth floor, offering a lounge, pantry, bar, and a large conference room, with a gym still under construction. The developer aimed to create a compelling reason for employees to return to the office by providing a high-quality, tenant-centric experience, making the building stand out in the Grand Central market.
