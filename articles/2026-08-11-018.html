MMCC Arranges Financing for Riverside County Self-Storage Property

LOAN

Connect CRE — Mon, 10 Aug 2026 21:32:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Marcus & Millichap Capital Corporation (MMCC) has arranged $7.38 million in financing for Beaumont RV and Self Storage, a 49,958-square-foot self-storage facility in Beaumont, CA. The 10-year loan, secured through a national federal credit union, features a 5.75% interest rate and a 30-year amortization period. This financing reflects the continued strong demand for self-storage, driven by its durable operating fundamentals and the need for flexible storage solutions for both household and recreational uses.
