Tratt Starts Work on 1.35M-SF Winter Haven Spec Warehouse

CONSTRUCTION

Connect CRE — Tue, 27 Oct 2026 14:36:31 +0000

SUMMARY

Tratt Properties is commencing construction on a 1.35 million-square-foot speculative warehouse in Winter Haven, Florida. This $40 million project is part of the larger 8.1 million-square-foot Central Florida Integrated Logistics Park. The site for the warehouse was acquired in 2021 for $4.14 million, and the park offers crucial rail access.
