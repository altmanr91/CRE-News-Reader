Gantry provides $48.3 million loan for 248-unit apartment community in Maple Grove

LOAN

REJournals — Fri, 11 Sep 2026 13:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Gantry has secured a $48.3 million permanent loan for The Edison at Maple Grove, a 248-unit apartment community in Maple Grove, Minnesota. The financing, provided by Freddie Mac, will be used to retire the existing construction loan for the Class A, podium-style property. The loan features a 10-year, fixed-rate, non-recourse, full-term interest-only structure.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: The Edison at Maple Grove

Address: 9820 Garland Lane North, Maple Grove, MN

Units: 248

Loan Amount: $48.3M

Loan Per Unit: $194,758/unit (calculated)

Notable Features: Class A, podium-style building with modern kitchens, stainless steel appliances, premium plank flooring, and amenities including a resort-style pool, rooftop clubhouse, fitness center, dog park, and playground.

Project Notes: Loan secured to retire construction financing.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

MORTGAGE BROKER: Gantry — Joe Monteleone, Principal; Bonnie Monteleone, Senior Associate

SPONSOR: private real estate investor
