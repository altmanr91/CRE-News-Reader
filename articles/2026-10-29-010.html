PCCP Provides $130M Construction Loan for Newark Multifamily Project

LOAN

REBusinessOnline — Thu, 29 Oct 2026 14:47:08 +0000

SUMMARY

PCCP LLC has provided a $130 million senior construction loan for the second phase of the Ballantine multifamily project in Newark, NJ. This phase will feature a seven-story building with 501 residential units, including 101 affordable housing units, and a 57,500-square-foot grocery store. The joint venture developing the project anticipates completion in late 2028.
