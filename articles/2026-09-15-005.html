New York City Selects It’s Electric to Install Curbside EV Charging Ports

DEVELOPMENT

Commercial Observer — Tue, 15 Sep 2026 12:45:40 +0000

SUMMARY

New York City has awarded It's Electric an exclusive contract to develop and install a permanent network of 700 curbside electric vehicle (EV) charging ports across all five boroughs. This initiative, managed by the city's Department of Transportation and the New York Power Authority, aims to replace existing pilot infrastructure and significantly expand EV charging access, particularly in neighborhoods with high concentrations of rideshare drivers and limited off-street parking. The project is expected to begin installation in the first quarter of 2027, following a review by the Public Design Commission, and will support the city's climate goals by accelerating EV adoption and creating local manufacturing jobs.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: infrastructure

Notable Features: The project involves replacing 88 existing pilot ports and installing over 600 new chargers, with all chargers manufactured in Queens, NY.

Project Notes: The contract is for a minimum five-year operational period. The city will retain revenue from the charging stations and set pricing.
