Colliers closes sale of 292,594-square-foot industrial property in Gahanna

SALE

REJournals — Mon, 06 Jul 2026 16:28:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Colliers has brokered the sale of Eastgate Logistics Center, a 292,594-square-foot industrial property in Gahanna, Ohio. The fully leased, two-tenant facility was sold by a joint venture between Scannell Properties and Manulife Investment Management to Dogwood Industrial Properties, a TPG Real Estate platform. The property's strategic location near major interstates, the Columbus International Airport, and the Intel semiconductor campus makes it a key asset in the Columbus East submarket.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Property Name: Eastgate Logistics Center

Address: 1804–1808 Deffenbaugh Court, Gahanna, OH

Size: 292,594 SF

Notable Features: Features 32-foot clear heights and 30 dock doors, located less than 10 minutes from Interstate 270 with direct access to Interstates 70 and 71, near John Glenn Columbus International Airport and the Intel semiconductor manufacturing campus.
