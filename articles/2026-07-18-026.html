Amazon nearing deal for first Chicago vertical warehouse

ACQUISITION

The Real Deal — Chicago — Fri, 17 Jul 2026 19:19:58 +0000

SUMMARY

Amazon is reportedly nearing a deal to acquire the vacant vertical warehouse at 1237 West Division Street in Chicago, a property that has sat empty since its completion in 2024. The e-commerce giant is expected to purchase the 1.2 million-square-foot facility at a significant discount to its construction cost, covering the outstanding balance of the developer's $150 million construction loan. This acquisition marks Amazon's entry into vertical warehousing in the Midwest, a building type more common in coastal U.S. markets, Europe, and Asia.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Address: 1237 West Division Street, Chicago, IL

Size: 1,200,000 SF

Total Project Cost: $150.0M

$/SF: $125/SF (calculated)

Year Built: 2024

Site Area: 11.5 acres

Notable Features: First vertical warehouse in the Midwest, designed for enhanced distribution efficiency with parking for 1,590 cars or 763 vans, located near Goose Island and the Kennedy Expressway.

Project Notes: The 11.5-acre site was previously acquired for $55 million. The property has been vacant since its completion in 2024 due to a fall-off in warehouse demand and higher alleged rent for this style of warehouse.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: Amazon

SELLER: Logistics Property

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Industrial vacancies in Chicago have seen record lows since the pandemic, but developing this vertical warehouse took longer than typical single-story facilities, coinciding with a decline in warehouse demand upon its completion.
