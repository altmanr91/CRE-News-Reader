Dean Adler Looks To Sell 'The Best Property We Own'

Bisnow Latest News Feed — Mon, 19 Oct 2026 18:52:14 -0400

SUMMARY

Property Listing / For Sale

Lubert-Adler Partners has engaged CBRE to market 2400 Market St. for sale, a trophy office property in Philadelphia. The building is currently occupied by a strong mix of tenants, including a prominent club, a major financial institution, and a global food service company. Dean Adler, co-founder of Lubert-Adler, indicated that this asset represents the firm's highest quality holding.
