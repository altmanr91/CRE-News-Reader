NY Dirt: Mamdani touts largest transfer of assistance project

CONSTRUCTION

The Real Deal — New York — Thu, 02 Jul 2026 11:30:00 +0000

SUMMARY

The Mamdani administration has closed on the Sol on Park project, a $214 million affordable senior housing development in the Morrisania neighborhood of the Bronx. This project will create 229 affordable homes, with 80 units reserved for NYCHA residents and 68 units for formerly homeless seniors, making it the city's largest use of the "transfer of assistance" program. Developers NRP Group, Selfhelp Realty Group, and Foxy Management are targeting an early 2029 completion, demonstrating the administration's reliance on public-private partnerships to address the housing crisis.

Notable Features: 80 units reserved for NYCHA residents, 68 units for formerly homeless seniors, and 80 units for low- and middle-income seniors. The project will include social services, a health and wellness center, and career training opportunities.

Project Notes: The project is the city's largest use of its "transfer of assistance" program, which taps the federal Housing and Urban Development Department’s Rental Assistance Demonstration (RAD) program. It is being developed on the same site as the Morris Houses.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Developers have utilized a loophole in the city's 485x tax incentive program, resulting in 538 fewer affordable housing units than required. The program allows buildings with 99 units or fewer to reserve 20% of apartments for low- and middle-income tenants with a worker wage floor of $17/hour, while developments over 99 units require a 25% affordable housing minimum and a $63/hour worker wage floor. A federal appeals court has upheld New York's statewide mandate for all-electric buildings, banning gas hookups in new buildings under seven stories and applying to all sizes after January 1, 2029.
