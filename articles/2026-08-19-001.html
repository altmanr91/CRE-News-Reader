OCVibe’s Scott Frick On Building a $5B District Around Anaheim’s Honda Center

DEVELOPMENT

Commercial Observer — Tue, 18 Aug 2026 20:45:27 +0000

SUMMARY

OCVibe, a 100-acre master-planned district surrounding Anaheim's Honda Center, is being privately financed by Henry and Susan Samueli, owners of the Anaheim Ducks, with a $5 billion investment. The development will transform surface parking lots into a dense mixed-use environment featuring 170,000 square feet of office space, over 2,000 apartments, two hotels, and more than 35 dining concepts, alongside 20 acres of parks. The project aims to create a year-round destination, addressing a gap in Orange County for cultural centers and high-quality urban living, while also enhancing the pre- and post-event experience for arena visitors. The first major public-facing components, including a market hall, a 5,000-capacity concert venue, and a mass-timber office building, are slated for completion in 2027, with overall project delivery expected through 2030-2031.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Orange County is described as very suburban with high rents and supply constraints, with inventory growth slowing to less than 1% annually. Anaheim attracts 30 million visitors a year due to attractions like Disneyland and the convention center. Office construction is currently dry, with a 'flight to quality' trend towards Class A-plus office spaces.
