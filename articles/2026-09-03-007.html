Accesso Signs Piedmont Health Services to 24,000 SF Office Lease in Durham

LEASE

REBusinessOnline — Thu, 03 Sep 2026 13:22:20 +0000

SUMMARY

Accesso has secured a significant 24,000-square-foot office lease with Piedmont Health Services, a nonprofit healthcare system, at Meridian Business Park in Durham, NC. The nearly 11-year lease will serve as Piedmont Health's long-term administrative headquarters and provide flex space for equipment warehousing. The landlord recently obtained a loan modification that includes capital reserves for property improvements, renovations, and spec suite development, indicating a commitment to enhancing the business park.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Property Name: Meridian Business Park

Size: 24,000 SF

Notable Features: The tenant will occupy two spaces within two different buildings at the 10-building office park.

Project Notes: The landlord recently secured a loan modification for its mortgage at Meridian Business Park, which includes capital reserves to fund property improvements, renovations, and spec suite development.
