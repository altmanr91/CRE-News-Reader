Chipmaker Nvidia Leases Office Space In Downtown D.C.

LEASE

Bisnow News Feed — Wed, 08 Jul 2026 14:04:45 -0400

SUMMARY

Nvidia, a leading artificial intelligence chipmaker, has secured a 27,600 SF sublease for a new office in the historic Woodies Building in downtown Washington, D.C. This move marks the company's expansion in the nation's capital, adding to its existing presence in Herndon, Virginia. The transaction is part of a broader trend of technology companies establishing or expanding their footprints in downtown D.C., contributing significantly to the area's leasing activity.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Property Name: Woodies Building

Address: 1025 F St. NW, Washington, D.C.

Size: 27,600 SF

Year Built: 1880

Notable Features: Historic 1880s-era building, formerly a Woodward & Lothrop department store, located at Metro Center.

Project Notes: The FBI, a major tenant occupying 200,000 SF, reportedly vacated in November 2025.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

TENANT: Nvidia

LANDLORD: Douglas Development Corp.

TENANT REP: Cushman & Wakefield

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

The tech sector accounted for 10% of leasing in downtown D.C. in the first half of 2026, following law firms (33%) and financial services (17%). These three sectors are expected to drive overall demand in the second half of 2026.
