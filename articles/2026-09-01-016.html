Gatsby Florida Inks $118.6M Construction Loan for Palm Beach Gardens Offices, Retail

LOAN

Connect CRE — Tue, 01 Sep 2026 13:30:09 +0000

SUMMARY

Gatsby Florida has secured an $118.6 million construction loan for The Palm, a new mixed-use development in Palm Beach Gardens, FL. The project will feature 200,000 square feet of Class A office space and 30,000 square feet of ground-floor retail and restaurant space. Construction is slated to begin shortly, with completion anticipated in 2028.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: mixed-use

Property Name: The Palm, Palm Beach Gardens

Address: 11200 RCA Center Drive, Palm Beach Gardens, FL

Size: 230,000 SF

Loan Amount: $118.6M

Loan Per SF: $516/SF (calculated)

Completion: 2028

Notable Features: Eight-story Class A office and retail development with an 838-space structured parking garage, EV charging stations, a rooftop terrace, conference facilities, and a fitness center.

Project Notes: Three-year, floating-rate, interest-only construction loan.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE
