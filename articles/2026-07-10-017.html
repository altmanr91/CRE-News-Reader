Pendry Nashville enlists Douglas Elliman, New York broker to woo New York condo buyers

The Real Deal — National — Thu, 09 Jul 2026 16:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Development

Redeavor, the exclusive sales and marketing team for the Pendry Residences Nashville, has partnered with Douglas Elliman and broker Greg Williamson to attract New York City buyers to the high-end condo project. The strategy involves leveraging Williamson's network in the entertainment and philanthropy sectors, including sponsoring his Soho Sessions concert series in Nashville. The 30-story tower, developed by SomeraRoad, is part of the larger Paseo South Gulch mixed-use district and is expected to complete construction by late 2027, with the hotel opening in early 2028. The project has already secured $26 million in condo pre-sale contracts, including a $7 million penthouse.

SPONSOR PIPELINE

Paseo South Gulch: Mixed-use district in Nashville's Gulch neighborhood, including redevelopment of two historic buildings (84,000 SF retail/office) and a 16-story, 278-apartment complex that opened in 2024.
