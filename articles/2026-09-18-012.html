CenterPoint Commercial Companies launches capital markets division based in Dallas

REJournals — Fri, 18 Sep 2026 09:49:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Non-CRE / Business News

CenterPoint Commercial Companies has launched CenterPoint Commercial Capital, a new capital markets division based in Dallas, focused on nationwide commercial real estate debt and equity placement. The firm recruited seasoned finance professionals Ari Raskas, founder of ARZ Realty Capital, and Matthew Sheard, formerly of Marcus & Millichap Capital Corporation, to lead the new platform. This expansion enhances CenterPoint's capabilities by offering comprehensive financing advisory and placement services for various transaction types, including acquisitions, refinancings, and construction, across all property types and capital structures.
