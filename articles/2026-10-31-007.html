Immersive Museum Will Make U.S. Debut at OCVIBE

Connect CRE — Fri, 30 Oct 2026 21:54:54 +0000

SUMMARY

Development

An immersive art museum by the art collective teamLab will establish its first permanent U.S. location within OCVIBE, a 100-acre sports, entertainment, and arts district currently under construction in Anaheim. The purpose-built museum will span approximately 110,000 square feet and is designed to integrate art as a central experience within the master-planned district, rather than just an amenity. This addition is expected to enhance OCVIBE's appeal as a cultural destination.
