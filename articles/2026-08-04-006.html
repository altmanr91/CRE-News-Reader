JLL Negotiates Sale of 767,520 SF Industrial Building in Brookshire, Texas

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Mon, 03 Aug 2026 15:14:25 +0000

SUMMARY

JLL has brokered the sale of Twinwood Distribution Center III, a 767,520-square-foot industrial building in Brookshire, Texas. The newly constructed facility, completed in 2024, was fully leased to a logistics services provider at the time of sale. Clay Development & Construction Inc. sold the asset to Bridge Logistics Properties.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Property Name: Twinwood Distribution Center III

Size: 767,520 SF

Occupancy at Sale: 100.0%

Year Built: 2024

Notable Features: Features 40-foot clear heights, 179 dock-high doors, 185-foot truck court depths, and approximately 2,600 square feet of office space.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: Bridge Logistics Properties

SELLER: Clay Development & Construction Inc.

SELLER BROKER: JLL — Trent Agnew; Charlie Strauss; Lance Young; Brooke Petzold; Dawson Hastings
