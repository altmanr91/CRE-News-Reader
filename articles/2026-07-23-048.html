What Charlotte Wants To Learn During Its 150-Day Moratorium On New Data Centers

Bisnow News Feed — Wed, 22 Jul 2026 09:38:32 -0400

SUMMARY

Infrastructure Investment / Government Policy

Charlotte has implemented a 150-day moratorium on new data center development, which began in June, to address growing concerns over the environmental impact and resource demands of these facilities. City officials are researching best practices from other jurisdictions and engaging with stakeholders to formulate new policies, with a potential extension of the pause. This move follows heated debate, particularly concerning a proposed project by American Tower Corp. near a nature preserve, and reflects a broader national trend of local governments re-evaluating data center growth due to significant water and power consumption.

DATA POINTS

Number of operating data centers in Charlotte: 40

Number of active data centers in North Carolina: 95

Duke Energy data center demand in development pipeline (Carolinas): 6 gigawatts

Duke Energy proposed electric rate increase (initial): 18%

Duke Energy proposed electric rate increase (revised): 9.5%

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

More than 100 local governments across the U.S. are grappling with data center development issues. Davie County, NC, passed a one-year data center moratorium. New York became the first state to impose a yearlong ban on data center construction.
