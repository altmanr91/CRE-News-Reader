Arizona Biltmore Owners Buy Wrigley Mansion

ACQUISITION

Connect CRE — Mon, 05 Oct 2026 16:02:33 +0000

SUMMARY

A joint venture led by Henderson Park, in partnership with Western & Southern Financial Group, Hamilton Lane, South Street Partners, and Pyramid Global Hospitality, has acquired the historic Wrigley Mansion in Phoenix, AZ, for $17 million. The property was sold by the Hormel family, who had owned it for 34 years. This acquisition brings the Wrigley Mansion under the same ownership as the nearby Arizona Biltmore, which the Henderson Park-led venture purchased in 2024.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: hospitality

Property Name: Wrigley Mansion

Address: 2501 E Telawa Trail, Phoenix, AZ

Sale Price: $17.0M

Year Built: 1931

Notable Features: Perched atop a 100-foot knoll, offering sweeping 360-degree views of the Valley.

Project Notes: Commissioned by William Wrigley Jr. as a 50th wedding anniversary gift for his wife, Ada.
