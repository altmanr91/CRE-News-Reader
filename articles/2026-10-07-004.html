John Meko of WiredScore: 5 Questions

Commercial Observer — Wed, 07 Oct 2026 13:00:42 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

WiredScore's latest report, "Fit for lease: How digital connectivity became the new test for Class A offices," reveals that 81% of tenants prefer office suites with pre-installed connectivity. The report highlights a significant gap between tenant expectations and reality, as only one in four tenants has operational connectivity on their lease start date. John Meko, WiredScore's VP for North America, notes that tenants increasingly view connectivity as a fundamental utility and are willing to sign longer leases for superior service, with 89% stating they would not renew due to poor connectivity. WiredScore, acquired by Meter in February 2026, is addressing this by speculatively installing internet infrastructure in office buildings to provide a turnkey solution for tenants and streamline the move-in process for landlords.
