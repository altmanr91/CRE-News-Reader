Kolter and Rockpoint Land $108M Loan for Palm Beach Gardens Rental

LOAN

Commercial Observer — Wed, 08 Jul 2026 21:30:52 +0000

SUMMARY

Kolter Group and Rockpoint have secured a $107.8 million construction loan from PNC Bank for The Sutton, a 432-unit garden-style multifamily project in Palm Beach Gardens, FL. The joint venture recently acquired the 17.6-acre site for $30 million, with construction anticipated to conclude in 2028. The developers are targeting submarkets with high demand and limited supply for new apartment living.
