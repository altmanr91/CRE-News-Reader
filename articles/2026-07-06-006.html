Target to Open 128,000 SF Store at Firestone City Centre in Colorado

LEASE

REBusinessOnline — Mon, 06 Jul 2026 12:29:32 +0000

SUMMARY

Target is set to open a 128,000-square-foot store at Firestone City Centre in Firestone, Colorado, this month. The Staenberg Group is the developer of the mixed-use project, which includes extensive retail and restaurant space, alongside a multifamily community.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: retail

Property Name: Firestone City Centre

Size: 128,000 SF

Notable Features: Part of a larger mixed-use development featuring over 600,000 square feet of retail and restaurant space, and a 300-unit multifamily community.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

LANDLORD: The Staenberg Group

TENANT: Target

Target
