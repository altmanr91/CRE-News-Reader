Walmart to Open 40,344 SF Grocery Store in Metro Miami

LEASE

REBusinessOnline — Tue, 06 Oct 2026 15:35:01 +0000

SUMMARY

Walmart has signed a 40,344-square-foot retail lease at Doral Atrium, a 78,000-square-foot lifestyle retail development in Doral, Florida. The retailer plans to open a new neighborhood-format grocery store, with construction slated to begin this fall and an opening anticipated in 2027. The project is attracting a strong tenant mix, indicating robust retail demand in the area.
