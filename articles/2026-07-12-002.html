Why this Brooklyn project is nowhere to be seen

The Real Deal — New York — Sat, 11 Jul 2026 13:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

This opinion piece argues that a proposed 250-unit multifamily development in Brooklyn, approved 17 months ago, remains stalled due to a confluence of economic and political factors. The author contends that elevated interest rates, the high percentage of affordable units promised, and a high wage scale mandate for large projects have made financing challenging for the developer. The article connects these local development hurdles to broader macroeconomic conditions, including presidential policies, international conflicts, and persistent inflation, which have kept interest rates high and deterred lenders.
