Marcus & Millichap closes $3.2 million sale of Austintown Walgreens

SALE

REJournals — Mon, 21 Sep 2026 13:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Marcus & Millichap has brokered the sale of a net-leased Walgreens property in Austintown, Ohio, for $3.25 million. The buyer was a private local investor. The property has been operating as a Walgreens for over two decades and is situated in a prominent retail corridor.
