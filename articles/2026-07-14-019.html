Michael Rubin scoops up Hamptons beachfront home that asked $43M

ACQUISITION

The Real Deal — New York — Mon, 13 Jul 2026 21:01:16 +0000

SUMMARY

Billionaire Michael Rubin, CEO of Fanatics, has acquired a 4,200-square-foot beachfront home at 167 Dune Road in Bridgehampton, NY. The five-bedroom, six-bathroom property was listed for $42.5 million by a family foundation tied to real estate mogul Donald Zucker. The final sale price was not disclosed. This acquisition marks Rubin's second beachfront property on Dune Road, located adjacent to his existing mansion where he hosts an annual A-list party.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: single family

Address: 167 Dune Road, Bridgehampton, NY

Size: 4,200 SF

Year Built: 1994

Site Area: 3.14 acres

Notable Features: Designed by Francis Fleetwood, the home features 300 feet of oceanfront along a private beach, an open chef's kitchen, living room with a large fireplace, ocean and bay views, a primary bedroom with a spa-like bathroom, four guest bedrooms with ensuite bathrooms, a pool, summer kitchen, and a pool house.
