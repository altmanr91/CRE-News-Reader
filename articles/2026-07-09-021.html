NYC’s top deals: Trio of Bronx apartment buildings trade for $17M

ACQUISITION

The Real Deal — New York — Thu, 09 Jul 2026 11:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Bronx GS Properties LLC acquired three apartment buildings in the Bronx for $16.7 million from entities associated with Chaim Eli Bleeman. The portfolio comprises 252 residential units across the three properties. Additionally, a mixed-use rental building in Flushing, spanning approximately 38,000 square feet, was sold for $6.5 million by 144-14 38th LLC to 38th Avenue Flushing LLC.
