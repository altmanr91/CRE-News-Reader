Crystal Lake Connects Growth and Investment to Build a Complete Community

REJournals — Mon, 26 Oct 2026 10:09:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Community Development

The city of Crystal Lake, Illinois, is strategically connecting residential and commercial growth with public spaces and transportation to foster a complete community. This approach is exemplified by the Water's Edge mixed-use redevelopment, which transformed a former shopping center into a vibrant destination with apartments, townhomes, retail, and public amenities adjacent to a major recreation area. The city is also revitalizing its downtown, focusing on small businesses and community activities, including the redevelopment of Depot Park. These efforts aim to enhance the resident and visitor experience by integrating new and existing assets, ensuring that housing, businesses, recreation, and transportation collectively strengthen the community's long-term economic vitality.

DATA POINTS

Population: 42,000+ residents

Water's Edge Apartments: 240 units

Water's Edge Townhomes: 20 units

Water's Edge Land Area: 23 acres
