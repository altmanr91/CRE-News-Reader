CrownPoint Arranges $9.2M Sale-Leaseback of Dental Portfolio in Michigan

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 09 Oct 2026 14:59:34 +0000

SUMMARY

CrownPoint Partners has arranged the $9.2 million sale-leaseback of a five-property dental portfolio across lower Michigan. The transaction involved new 15-year absolute-net leases with 2 percent annual rent increases, providing the buyer with long-term contractual income and no landlord responsibilities. The portfolio totals 35,212 square feet and is strategically located in established healthcare and retail corridors, benefiting from strong regional connectivity and proximity to major healthcare systems.
