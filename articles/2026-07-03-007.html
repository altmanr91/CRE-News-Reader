Landmark Properties, CrossHarbor Capital Break Ground on 272-Unit Student Housing Property in Eugene, Oregon

CONSTRUCTION

REBusinessOnline — Thu, 02 Jul 2026 14:39:59 +0000

SUMMARY

Landmark Properties and CrossHarbor Capital have commenced construction on The Mark Eugene, a 272-unit student housing development in Eugene, Oregon. This project, located near the University of Oregon, will offer 1,002 beds and extensive amenities, including a resort-style pool, fitness center, and study lounges. The development also incorporates ground-floor retail space and represents the first phase of a larger plan to deliver over 2,200 student housing beds to the market.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: student housing

Property Name: The Mark Eugene

Address: 1290 Alder St, Eugene, OR

Size: 37,000 SF

Units: 272

Beds: 1,002

Completion: 2028-2029 academic year

Phase I: 272 units / 1,002 beds

Total Project (All Phases): 2,275 beds

Site Area: 2.6 acres

Notable Features: Located less than one mile from the University of Oregon, featuring 37,000 square feet of amenity space and 5,785 square feet of ground-floor retail.

Project Notes: The 5,785 square feet of ground-floor retail space within a parking deck at 13th and Hilyard was included as part of the property acquisition. This is the first of a two-phase development, with the second phase to be developed separately by Landmark Properties.
