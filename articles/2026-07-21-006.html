Provident Industrial Buys 1.5 MSF Industrial Park in Hutchins, Texas

ACQUISITION

REBusinessOnline — Mon, 20 Jul 2026 15:12:08 +0000

SUMMARY

Provident Industrial, a division of Dallas-based investment firm Provident, has acquired Commerce 45, a two-building industrial park spanning 1.5 million square feet in Hutchins, a southeastern suburb of Dallas. The seller and sales price were not disclosed. The property was developed in 2015 on an 81-acre site.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Property Name: Commerce 45

Size: 1,500,000 SF

Year Built: 2015

Site Area: 81 acres

Notable Features: Buildings feature 32-foot clear heights, 185-foot truck court depths, and parking for 102 cars and 290 trailers.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: Provident Industrial

SELLER: Brookfield Properties
