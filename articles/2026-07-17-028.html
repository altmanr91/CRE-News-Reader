$1M rent, $100 steaks: Inside Coconut Grove’s booming restaurant scene

The Real Deal — Miami — Thu, 16 Jul 2026 15:56:41 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

Coconut Grove, once a bohemian enclave, has transformed into a premier dining destination in Miami, driven by a surge in retail rents. Average direct asking retail rents in the second quarter exceeded $110 per square foot, more than double the rates from a decade ago. This rapid appreciation has created a challenging environment for independent restaurateurs, with some being priced out, while also enabling landlords to curate a high-end tenant mix due to limited inventory and affluent clientele. The market's strong demand and limited supply are expected to sustain high rents, with no correction anticipated.
