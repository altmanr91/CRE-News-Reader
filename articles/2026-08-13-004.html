Peachtree Recaps Ritz-Carlton Portland With $141M C-PACE Loan

LOAN

Commercial Observer — Wed, 12 Aug 2026 18:13:46 +0000

SUMMARY

Ready Capital, which took full ownership of The Ritz-Carlton Portland mixed-use project after a July 2025 foreclosure, has secured a $141 million C-PACE loan from Peachtree Group for the recapitalization of the 35-story tower. The 2023-completed development includes a 251-key hotel, 131 condominium units, and 159,000 square feet of office space. The C-PACE financing was suitable due to the building's recent construction under stringent codes, making much of the project eligible for retroactive financing.
