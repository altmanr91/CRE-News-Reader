Newmark Brokers Sale of 1 MSF Colonnade Office Campus in Addison, Texas

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Wed, 04 Nov 2026 16:34:46 +0000

SUMMARY

Newmark has brokered the sale of The Colonnade, a 1 million-square-foot office campus in Addison, Texas. The property, comprising three buildings on 12.4 acres, was sold through a receivership process. A partnership between Cross Ocean Partners and Lincoln Property Co. acquired the asset and plans to invest $100 million in repositioning the campus, with the first phase of upgrades anticipated to conclude in 2027.
