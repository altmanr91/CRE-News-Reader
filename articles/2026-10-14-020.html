JLL Arranges $20M Sale of Fully Leased 107K-SF Retail Center in Ohio

SALE

Connect CRE — Tue, 13 Oct 2026 20:17:31 +0000

SUMMARY

JLL Capital Markets has arranged the $20.55 million sale of Hilliard Rome Commons, a fully leased power center in Hilliard, Ohio. The property, which features a strong mix of national retailers and a separately parceled outparcel, was acquired by CASTO from seller RCG Ventures, LLC. Investors were attracted to the center's full occupancy, nationally recognized tenant lineup, and the flexibility offered by the outparcel.
