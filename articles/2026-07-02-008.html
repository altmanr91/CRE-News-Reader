Lee & Associates Negotiates Two Industrial Leases Totaling 131,417 SF in Chicago’s Western Suburbs

LEASE

REBusinessOnline — Wed, 01 Jul 2026 13:46:38 +0000

SUMMARY

Lee & Associates of Illinois has brokered two industrial leases in Chicago's western suburbs, totaling 131,417 square feet. Nuvomed Inc. leased 54,087 square feet in Hillside, with Clear Height Properties as the landlord. Separately, Recyclops Inc. secured 77,330 square feet in Forest Park, where ML Realty Partners is the landlord.
