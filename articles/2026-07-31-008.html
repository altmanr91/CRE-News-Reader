Modern Home Concepts Signs 57,361 SF Industrial Lease in Carrollton, Texas

LEASE

REBusinessOnline — Thu, 30 Jul 2026 14:46:46 +0000

SUMMARY

Modern Home Concepts, a residential flooring products provider, has signed a 57,361-square-foot industrial lease in Carrollton, Texas. The property, located within Valwood Business Park, offers dedicated truck parking and potential outdoor storage, which were key features for the tenant's operational needs. This transaction highlights continued demand for well-located industrial space in the northern Dallas metro.
