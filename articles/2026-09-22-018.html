Hoag Hospital Opens $1B Expansion in Irvine

CONSTRUCTION

Connect CRE — Mon, 21 Sep 2026 21:47:05 +0000

SUMMARY

Hoag Hospital has opened its $1 billion Hoag Hospital Irvine Sun Family Campus, a significant expansion that integrates specialized hospitals, outpatient facilities, and advanced surgical services onto a single campus in Irvine. The project adds six new buildings, 155 inpatient beds, and 120,000 square feet of ambulatory facilities, including Orange County's first dedicated Cancer Urgent Care. This expansion reflects Hoag's commitment to meeting the growing healthcare needs of Orange County by providing a comprehensive and patient-centered healthcare village.
