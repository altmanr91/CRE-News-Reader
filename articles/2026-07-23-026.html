Summermaxxing: Wealth boom brings heat to South Florida with pricey off-season sales

The Real Deal — Miami — Wed, 22 Jul 2026 22:47:40 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

South Florida's luxury and ultra-luxury residential markets are experiencing a robust off-season, with significant sales activity driven by a continuous influx of wealthy buyers. Brokers report a busier year than last, with single-family home and condo sales, particularly in the $1 million-plus range, showing substantial year-over-year increases across Miami-Dade, Broward, and Palm Beach counties. This trend is fueled by affluent transplants and a growing practice of "landmaxxing," where buyers acquire adjacent properties to expand their compounds, seeking greater space, privacy, and amenities. The surge in high-end demand has exacerbated the region's cost of living, pushing it past New York City and making South Florida the second most expensive area in the nation.

DATA POINTS

June single-family home and condo sales: rose substantially year-over-year in Miami-Dade, Broward, and Palm Beach counties

Miami's cost of living: surpassed New York City's

South Florida's national cost of living ranking: second most expensive, behind San Francisco-Oakland-Fremont
