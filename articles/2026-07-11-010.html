Partnership Receives $217M in Construction Financing for Brooklyn Affordable Housing Project

LOAN

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 10 Jul 2026 14:32:37 +0000

SUMMARY

A joint venture between Apex Building Group and L+M Development Partners has secured $217 million in construction financing for Phase III of the Alafia affordable housing project in Brooklyn. This phase will deliver 273 units designated for households earning 70 percent or less of the area median income, along with a one-acre public park. Redstone Bank provided the construction loan, complemented by federal and state tax credit equity and other subsidies, with completion anticipated in 2029.

Project Notes: Phase III of a larger 27-acre redevelopment of the former Brooklyn Developmental Center site.
