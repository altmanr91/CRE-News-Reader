Aquarian Real Estate Partners, 3650 Capital Loan $72M on Chicago-Area Acquisition

LOAN

Commercial Observer — Fri, 25 Sep 2026 13:29:21 +0000

SUMMARY

Rhino Investments Group has secured $72.3 million in acquisition financing for Randhurst Village, a 929,899-square-foot open-air shopping center in Mount Prospect, IL. The financing, provided by Aquarian Real Estate Partners and 3650 Capital, supports Rhino's value-add strategy for the property, which includes leasing vacant spaces and selling outparcel pieces. The property, acquired from DLC Management Corp. for $95 million, is considered a dominant retail asset with strong traffic and significant upside potential in an affluent suburban Chicago market.
