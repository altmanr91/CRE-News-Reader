Transamerica complex nets 113K sf of new leases after $725M sale

LEASE

The Real Deal — San Francisco — Fri, 17 Jul 2026 17:59:56 +0000

SUMMARY

The Transamerica Pyramid Center in San Francisco has secured seven new leases totaling 113,000 square feet since its acquisition by Yoda PLC in March for $725 million. The new owner is continuing the repositioning efforts initiated by the previous owner, focusing on upgrades at Two Transamerica and Three Transamerica to attract premium tenants. This leasing activity reflects a broader market trend of flight-to-quality, where trophy office buildings with enhanced amenities are outperforming in San Francisco's elevated vacancy environment, particularly appealing to fast-growing tech and AI firms seeking ready-to-occupy spaces.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Property Name: Transamerica Pyramid Center

Address: 600 Montgomery St, San Francisco, CA

Size: 113,000 SF

Notable Features: Iconic mixed-use complex with office, retail, and public plaza; includes Transamerica Pyramid, Two Transamerica, and Three Transamerica buildings.

Project Notes: The complex was previously acquired for $725 million in March. The new owner plans further investment in spec suites, amenity centers, and workplace upgrades at Two Transamerica, and repositioning ground-floor restaurant spaces at Three Transamerica.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

OWNER: Yoda PLC

SELLER: Michael Shvo

LEASING AGENT: JLL — Chris Roeder, Vice Chairman

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

San Francisco office vacancy fell to approximately 29.7 percent in Q2, down from 34.7 percent a year earlier. Trophy buildings with upgraded amenities are outperforming in the market.
