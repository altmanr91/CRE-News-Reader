Knighthead Funding provides $32 million refinance loan for 62-unit multifamily community in Glenview

REFINANCE

REJournals — Wed, 02 Sep 2026 09:51:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Knighthead Funding has provided a $32 million refinancing loan to The Drake Group for Cerca, a newly constructed 62-unit Class-A multifamily community in downtown Glenview, IL. The loan refinances a previous construction facility and provides proceeds to pay off the property's preferred equity investor. This transaction highlights the continued demand for high-quality multifamily assets in supply-constrained markets with strong fundamentals, particularly in affluent suburban areas with excellent transit access.
