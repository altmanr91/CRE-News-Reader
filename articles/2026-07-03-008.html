Diversified Partners Advances 163-Acre Retail Center Development in Mesa, Arizona

DEVELOPMENT

REBusinessOnline — Thu, 02 Jul 2026 14:38:44 +0000

SUMMARY

Diversified Partners is moving forward with Destination at Gateway, a 163-acre retail center in Mesa, Arizona. The development will feature an 80-acre auto mall, 24 Auto Row, and a power center with a strong tenant mix. Off-site improvements on one side of Signal Butte Road are complete, with site work on the other side expected to finish by the third quarter of 2026.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: retail

Property Name: Destination at Gateway

Site Area: 163 acres

Notable Features: Includes an 80-acre purpose-built auto mall called 24 Auto Row and an adjacent power center.

Project Notes: Off-site improvements on the east side of Signal Butte Road are complete; site work on the west side is underway with completion expected by Q3 2026.
