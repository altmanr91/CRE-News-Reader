Barry Sternlicht raises $10B for fund eyeing data center push

The Real Deal — National — Wed, 01 Jul 2026 20:00:17 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Fund Raise

Starwood Capital Group has successfully raised $10.2 billion for its latest opportunistic commercial property fund, marking the largest such fund in the firm's history. Over a third of this capital is earmarked for data center investments, significantly increasing its allocation to the sector compared to previous funds. The fund, which secured commitments from over 300 investors, including a substantial contribution from Starwood itself, has already committed more than $3 billion across 20 investments, including a stake in a data center company, residential development land in Texas, and industrial assets in Northern Italy. Starwood plans to mitigate financing risk by seeking co-investors and extending development capital deployment over a longer timeframe, focusing on emerging rent growth markets in the Sun Belt for its rental housing investments.
