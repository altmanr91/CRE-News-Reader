Miami is now more expensive than New York City

The Real Deal — Miami — Mon, 20 Jul 2026 22:07:09 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Cost of Living Report

A new report indicates that the cost of living in the Miami metropolitan area has surpassed that of New York City for the first time on record, driven by significant increases in insurance costs, private school tuition, and restaurant spending. Since 2019, South Florida's consumer price index has surged by 36 percent, making it the second most expensive metro area nationally on the regional price parity index. While the absence of state income tax in Florida might seem appealing, other rising costs, particularly property taxes and the nation's highest home insurance premiums due to hurricane and flood risks, are eroding any savings for residents. This trend is occurring despite Miami's average household income being lower than the national median, creating a challenging environment for many residents.
