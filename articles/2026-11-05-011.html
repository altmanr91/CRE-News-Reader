Big-D Midwest wraps construction of second phase of affordable-housing community in Woodbury

CONSTRUCTION

REJournals — Thu, 05 Nov 2026 16:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Big-D Midwest has completed the second phase of Settler's Ridge, an affordable housing community in Woodbury, Minnesota. This phase adds 126 residential units, mirroring the design and amenities of the first phase. The units are offered at various Area Median Income (AMI) levels to ensure affordability.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Settler's Ridge

Units: 126

Completion: November 2026

Notable Features: Units feature balconies, granite countertops, stainless steel appliances, walk-in closets, and in-unit washer/dryer. Community amenities include a fitness center, club room, playground, dog park, outdoor grill stations, heated underground parking, and EV charging stations.

Project Notes: Units are offered at 50%, 60%, and 70% of the Area Median Income (AMI).

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

GENERAL CONTRACTOR: Big-D Midwest
