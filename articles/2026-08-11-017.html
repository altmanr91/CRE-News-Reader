PE Firm OceanSound Expands Presence at SL Green’s 450 Park Ave.

LEASE

Connect CRE — Mon, 10 Aug 2026 21:49:00 +0000

SUMMARY

OceanSound, LP, a private equity firm, has signed a new five-year lease for 32,032 square feet across three floors at 450 Park Avenue in New York City. This agreement expands their existing presence in the building, where they previously subleased 21,640 square feet. The landlord, SL Green Realty Corp, highlighted recent building upgrades that have enhanced 450 Park Avenue's appeal as a premier boutique office destination with luxury amenities.

DATA POINTS
