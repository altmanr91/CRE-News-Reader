Related Ross appointment fires up West Palm Beach development debate

The Real Deal — Miami — Fri, 10 Jul 2026 17:00:33 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

West Palm Beach residents are expressing significant opposition to the city commission's appointment of a Related Ross executive to the Downtown Development Authority, intensifying an ongoing debate about rapid development in the city. Critics, including the "Save West Palm Beach" group, argue that the city is prioritizing developer interests over community input and moving too quickly with high-rise construction, aiming to transform the area into a financial hub. The appointment of a second Related Ross executive to the DDA has fueled accusations of favoritism towards developers. In response to the backlash, Mayor Keith James announced a pause on the downtown master plan and the formation of a resident-led coalition to increase community involvement in future development decisions.
