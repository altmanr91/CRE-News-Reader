Century 21 Redevelopment Adds Burlington, National Grocer to Tenant Roster

LEASE

Connect CRE — Wed, 19 Aug 2026 21:41:24 +0000

SUMMARY

MCB Real Estate and Osiris Ventures have secured two major retail leases at Century Marketplace, a $130 million mixed-use redevelopment in Brooklyn's Bay Ridge neighborhood. A national grocery anchor and Burlington have signed leases totaling 60,112 square feet, bringing the 120,000-square-foot project to 65% pre-leased. The project aims to revitalize a key commercial corridor by introducing high-quality retail options to the dense Southwest Brooklyn market.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: retail

Property Name: Century Marketplace

Address: 458 86th St, Brooklyn, NY

Size: 60,112 SF

Total Project Cost: $130.0M

Current Occupancy: 65.0%

Notable Features: First national grocer to serve the immediate Southwest Brooklyn trade area.

Project Notes: Redevelopment of the 14-lot Century 21 department store assemblage.
