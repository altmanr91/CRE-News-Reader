Prologis scoops up vacant industrial site in San Jose

ACQUISITION

The Real Deal — San Francisco — Wed, 01 Jul 2026 21:13:44 +0000

SUMMARY

Prologis has acquired a 14.2-acre vacant industrial site at 455 Piercy Road in San Jose for $14 million. The property was previously slated for a light industrial development by the former owner, InSite Property Group, but those plans appear to be abandoned. This acquisition further solidifies Prologis's position as a dominant industrial property owner in Silicon Valley.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Address: 455 Piercy Road, San Jose, CA

Site Area: 14.2 acres

Project Notes: The 14.2-acre site was previously acquired in 2021 for nearly $4.8 million. Former owner InSite Property Group had proposed a 116,580-square-foot warehouse with 5,000 square feet of office space, but the development permit is set to expire by the end of 2026.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: Prologis

SELLER: InSite Property Group

SPONSOR PIPELINE

Crocker Industrial Park: 11-building, nearly 1-million-square-foot portfolio acquisition in Brisbane for $314.5 million in fall 2025.

Caltrain railyards along Townsend Street: Proposed redevelopment of a 20-acre site in San Francisco's South of Market neighborhood with approximately 2,500 homes and 4 million square feet of commercial space, including office, retail, and potentially a hotel.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

The south San Jose area has nearly 24 million square feet of industrial space.
