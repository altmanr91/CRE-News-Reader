Nashville Retail Portfolio Trades for $54.3M

SALE

Connect CRE — Wed, 23 Sep 2026 13:52:06 +0000

SUMMARY

LBX Investments has acquired the Infill Nashville Portfolio, a two-property retail center package totaling 134,000 square feet, from Vintage South for $54.3 million. The portfolio includes Breeze Block and Stateline, both recently developed and located in Nashville's active Charlotte Park and The Nations corridor. The properties are 91% occupied by a diverse mix of retail, fitness, wellness, and service-oriented businesses.
