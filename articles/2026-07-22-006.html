Rosewood Begins Leasing 359-Unit Multifamily Project in San Antonio

DEVELOPMENT

REBusinessOnline — Tue, 21 Jul 2026 15:06:33 +0000

SUMMARY

Rosewood Property Co. has commenced leasing for a 359-unit multifamily project in San Antonio, marking Phase III of the Tobin Estates development. The project offers a mix of traditional apartments and units with private garages, featuring a range of floor plans and amenities such as a pool, fitness centers, and a rooftop deck. The development began construction in May 2024.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Tobin Estates Phase III

Address: 3310 Oakwell Ct, San Antonio, TX*

Units: 359

Notable Features: Units include one-, two-, and three-bedroom floor plans, with amenities such as a pool, indoor and outdoor fitness centers, a resident lounge, and a rooftop deck. 94 units feature private garages.

Project Notes: Construction began in May 2024. This project is Phase III of the larger Tobin Estates development.
