Marcus & Millichap Brokers Sale of 218,689 SF Office Campus in Northwest Houston

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Mon, 24 Aug 2026 15:46:01 +0000

SUMMARY

Marcus & Millichap has brokered the sale of The Preserve at North Loop, a 218,689-square-foot office campus in northwest Houston. The 12.7-acre property, built in 1970, was approximately 65 percent leased at the time of sale. An Atlanta-based investor acquired the campus from an entity doing business as Silver Star CRE LLC.
