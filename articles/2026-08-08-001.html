Longfellow Sells Hatch Life Sciences Building in LIC for $87M

SALE

Commercial Observer — Fri, 07 Aug 2026 18:55:44 +0000

SUMMARY

Longfellow Real Estate Partners, in partnership with Sculptor Real Estate, has sold its Hatch Life Sciences Building in Long Island City, Queens, for $86.9 million to an entity associated with Pearl Realty Management. This transaction marks a discount from the $92.5 million Longfellow and Sculptor paid for the property in 2021, where they had planned a $120 million conversion of the former manufacturing plant into a life sciences and office facility. The sale concludes Longfellow's first project in New York City.
