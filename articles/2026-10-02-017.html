AI Office Demand Rises 27% Year-Over-Year

Connect CRE — Fri, 02 Oct 2026 14:20:43 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Office Demand

A new report by VTS indicates that demand for office space from artificial intelligence companies has increased by 27% year-over-year, with 820 new leasing requirements. The majority of this demand, totaling 16.9 million square feet, is concentrated in San Francisco, New York, and Silicon Valley, which together account for 64% of active AI office demand. New York has surpassed Silicon Valley to become the second-largest market for AI office demand, while other markets like Austin and Dallas-Fort Worth also show significant activity. Growth in smaller markets is varied, with some experiencing substantial increases and others seeing declines.
