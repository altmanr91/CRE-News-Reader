Midwood Buys Mixed-Use Building Across From Former Downtown Brooklyn Macy’s

ACQUISITION

Commercial Observer — Mon, 13 Jul 2026 20:56:16 +0000

SUMMARY

Midwood Investment & Development has acquired a four-story mixed-use building at 423 Fulton Street in Downtown Brooklyn for $9.5 million. The property, which includes ground-floor retail and office space, is located directly across from the former Macy's department store, which is slated for a significant redevelopment into a retail and amusement destination. This transaction reflects the ongoing retail revitalization in Downtown Brooklyn.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: mixed-use

Address: 423 Fulton Street, New York City, NY

Sale Price: $9.5M

Notable Features: Located across from the former Macy's department store, which is being redeveloped into a large-format retail and amusement destination.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Downtown Brooklyn has 125 available storefronts, but the area is seeing success with retail that caters to its population's needs, including three new supermarkets on Fulton Street.
