Financial Tech Platform Intuit Takes 4K SF at the Orchard in LIC

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Thu, 29 Oct 2026 19:56:44 +0000

SUMMARY

BLDG Management has secured three new retail leases totaling 7,468 square feet at The Orchard, Queens' tallest residential tower. Financial services platform Intuit signed a 3,546-square-foot lease, Japanese tea brand Mollytea took 1,671 square feet, and Korean beauty supplier Tokimo leased 2,251 square feet. The property, which is 90 percent leased residentially, aims to serve both its residents and the broader Long Island City neighborhood with its diverse offerings.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: retail

Address: 42-06 Orchard Street, Long Island City, NY

Rental Rate: $175.00/SF/yr

Current Occupancy: 90.0%

Notable Features: Queens' tallest residential tower with a 60,000-square-foot outdoor deck featuring a swimming pool, dog park, barbeque areas, and a running track.
