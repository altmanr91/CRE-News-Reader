Data Center Development Has Domestic Materials Manufacturers Working Overtime

Commercial Observer — Thu, 20 Aug 2026 18:49:25 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Construction Costs

A new report from Skanska indicates that while overall U.S. development saw uneven progress in the first half of 2026, specific sectors like data centers, power infrastructure, advanced manufacturing, and pharmaceutical production are experiencing significant growth. This surge is largely due to investors prioritizing projects with the highest returns amidst rising material costs and ongoing tariff policies. The strong demand in these sectors is causing unprecedented pressure on mechanical, electrical, plumbing, and steel supply chains, leading to increased lead times and higher prices for domestic materials.

DATA POINTS

Private nonresidential construction spending increase (2019-2025): 1.7% to 5.4%

Material delivery lead times increase: 24-36 weeks to 40-50 weeks
