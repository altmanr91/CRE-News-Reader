McGrath Real Estate Completes Lease-Up of 736-Bed Student Housing Project in Lubbock, Texas

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 23 Oct 2026 14:37:06 +0000

SUMMARY

Lease

McGrath Real Estate has successfully completed the lease-up of The Wyatt, a 736-bed student housing community in Lubbock, TX, serving Texas Tech University students. The property achieved approximately 98 percent occupancy shortly after its 2025 completion and was fully preleased for the 2026 academic year by May. Strong leasing momentum continues, with over 30 percent of beds already preleased for the fall 2027 semester.
