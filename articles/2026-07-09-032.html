LG Development pivots to $125M refi for Fulton Market high-rise

REFINANCE

The Real Deal — Chicago — Wed, 08 Jul 2026 16:30:00 +0000

SUMMARY

LG Development Group has secured a $124.6 million refinancing loan from Pacific Life Insurance Company for its 18-story, 363-unit luxury apartment building, Arthur on Aberdeen, in Chicago's Fulton Market. The three-year floating-rate loan replaces a 2022 construction loan from Santander Bank and comes after the developer opted to retain the property rather than sell it, leveraging its rapid lease-up to 92% occupancy since its 2024 completion. The refinancing provides financial stability for the developer amidst a challenging capital market and ongoing litigation related to other projects, while the property benefits from a 30-year property tax abatement due to its affordable housing component.
