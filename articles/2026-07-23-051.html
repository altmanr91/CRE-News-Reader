Bank OZK Continues To Cut Real Estate Portfolio

Bisnow News Feed — Wed, 22 Jul 2026 15:11:55 -0400

SUMMARY

Market Research / Earnings Report

Bank OZK is continuing to reduce its exposure to real estate, with its real estate loan book falling to 47% of its total portfolio in Q2 2026, down from 52% in Q1. This strategic shift, aimed at cutting nonperforming assets, resulted in the slowest second quarter for real estate loan originations in five years, totaling $1 billion. The bank is facing headwinds from sponsors struggling to raise equity for new construction due to macro uncertainty and high material prices, though data center projects are a notable exception in the broader construction pipeline. Bank OZK anticipates its real estate exposure will eventually align with its corporate and institutional banking segment, which currently represents 22.2% of its loan portfolio, a target it hopes to achieve next year.
