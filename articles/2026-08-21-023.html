Developers Line Up To Convert Boston Offices To Housing, But Most Still Stuck At Starting Gate

Bisnow Latest News Feed — Thu, 20 Aug 2026 18:02:24 -0400

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

Despite a strong push to convert underperforming office buildings into residential housing in Boston, most proposed projects are stalled due to escalating construction costs and complex policy challenges. Developers are finding it difficult to secure financing and navigate regulatory frameworks, hindering the progress of these adaptive reuse initiatives. This situation reflects a broader market trend where the economic viability of such conversions is being tested by current financial and logistical headwinds.
