Marcus & Millichap brokers sale of 44,068-square-foot shopping center in Temple

SALE

REJournals — Wed, 07 Oct 2026 13:29:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Marcus & Millichap has brokered the sale of Legends Plaza, a 44,068-square-foot shopping center in Temple, Texas. Both the buyer and seller were private investors. The buyer intends to continue operating the property as a multi-tenant retail center.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: retail

Property Name: Legends Plaza

Address: 2327 S. 57th St, Temple, TX

Size: 44,068 SF

Year Built: 1974

Site Area: 3.05 acres

Notable Features: Located along Loop 363; formerly a grocery store redeveloped into a multi-tenant shopping center.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

SELLER BROKER: Marcus & Millichap — Scott Jones; Gus Lagos; Alex Wolansky

BUYER: Private Investor
