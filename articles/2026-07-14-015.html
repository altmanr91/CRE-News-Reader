Wall Street landlords find wiggle room in new US housing law

The Real Deal — National — Mon, 13 Jul 2026 18:30:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

A new national housing law, the 21st Century Road to Housing Act, aims to restrict institutional investors owning 350 or more homes from purchasing single-family houses, but includes significant exceptions. Large landlords can still acquire properties if they undertake substantial renovations, offer tenants a purchase option, engage in institutional-to-institutional transactions, or buy homes specifically developed for rent (built-to-rent). While the law imposes penalties for violations and may encourage stricter state-level bans, it provides enough flexibility for institutional players to remain active in the housing market, particularly through built-to-rent strategies. However, the uncertainty surrounding the law has already impacted investment, with capital providers expected to take a year or two to regain comfort with new acquisitions.
