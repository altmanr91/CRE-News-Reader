Indecor Home Signs Full-Floor Lease in Garment District

LEASE

Connect CRE — Thu, 22 Oct 2026 23:51:40 +0000

SUMMARY

Indecor Home, a New York-based designer and manufacturer of home décor products, has signed a 10-year lease for a full floor at 247 W. 37th St. in Manhattan's Garment District. The company will utilize the 16,806-square-foot space for its executive, sales, and design offices, as well as a home products showroom. The landlord noted that the building continues to attract businesses seeking a central Manhattan location and adaptable space.
