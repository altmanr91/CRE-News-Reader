Westlight Capital Founder Doug Faron On Going Long in Multifamily

Commercial Observer — Tue, 29 Sep 2026 11:15:09 +0000

SUMMARY

Feature / Profile

Doug Faron, founder of Westlight Capital, discusses his career trajectory from investment banking to leading two entrepreneurial real estate investment firms. He highlights his experience at CIM Group, where he oversaw $10 billion in real estate investments, and the strategic shift from Shoreham Capital's build-to-rent focus to Westlight Capital's broader multifamily mandate. Faron emphasizes the importance of relationships in fundraising and adapting investment strategies to changing market conditions, such as the move from developing build-to-rent projects to acquiring existing multifamily assets when cap rates shifted.
