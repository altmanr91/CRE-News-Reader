Investment Firm HarbourVest Partners Takes 20K SF at 9 West 57th Street

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Mon, 20 Jul 2026 11:00:09 +0000

SUMMARY

HarbourVest Partners, a global private markets investment firm, has signed a 10-year lease for its inaugural New York City office. The firm will occupy nearly 20,000 square feet on the 49th floor of 9 West 57th Street, a prestigious Midtown office tower. The move reflects a strategic focus on attracting and retaining top talent by securing a high-caliber office space that aligns with the firm's business and provides an exceptional experience for clients and employees.
