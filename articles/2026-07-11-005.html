Twain Capital Supplies $65M C-PACE Loan for Colorado Hotel Project

LOAN

Commercial Observer — Fri, 10 Jul 2026 16:18:42 +0000

SUMMARY

Twain Capital Partners has provided a $65 million C-PACE loan to Nathaniel Kelly Foote for the Foote's Rest mixed-use development in Frisco, CO. The project, anchored by an 80-key Marriott Tribute Hotel, will also feature an entertainment venue, ballroom, event space, conference center, restaurants, and a rooftop bar. The financing leverages Colorado's robust C-PACE program, which allows property owners to fund 100 percent of sustainability upgrades, and addresses the strong hospitality demand and limited hotel supply in the Frisco market.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

The C-PACE market has seen significant growth, with average transaction sizes increasing from $5 million-$10 million to over $25 million, and several $100 million-plus C-PACE financings closing in the last 12 months, indicating its increasing role in capital stacks.
