Colliers Brokers Sale of Two-Property Self-Storage Portfolio in Wisconsin

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Tue, 20 Oct 2026 14:14:04 +0000

SUMMARY

Colliers has facilitated the sale of a two-property self-storage portfolio in Wisconsin, totaling 171,825 square feet and 1,486 units. Inland Real Estate acquired the portfolio from Bruno Self Storage LLC. The properties, located in Pleasant Prairie and West Bend, are managed by Extra Space and were completed in 2020 and 2019, respectively.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: self-storage

Size: 171,825 SF

Notable Features: Both properties feature climate-controlled units.

Project Notes: The Pleasant Prairie facility was completed in 2020, and the West Bend facility was completed in 2019.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: Inland Real Estate

SELLER: Bruno Self Storage LLC

SELLER BROKER: Colliers — Dan Lynch
