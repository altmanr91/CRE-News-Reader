Prosper Property, HM Group USA nab Tribeca lot for $24M

ACQUISITION

The Real Deal — New York — Mon, 13 Jul 2026 17:39:57 +0000

SUMMARY

Prosper Property Group and HM Group USA have acquired a long-undeveloped lot at 65 West Broadway in Tribeca for $24 million. The joint venture plans to construct a 23-unit condominium building with ground-floor retail, securing a $68 million construction loan from Kriss Capital and KRE Capital Management. This acquisition marks a new chapter for the site, which has a history of stalled development.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: condo

Address: 65 West Broadway, New York City, NY

Units: 23

Sale Price: $24.0M

Loan Amount: $68.0M

Loan Per Unit: $2,956,522/unit (calculated)

Notable Features: Condominiums will range from one- to three-bedroom units above ground-floor retail.

Project Notes: The site was previously assembled in 2015 for $50 million by Cape Advisors and Forum Absolute Equity Partners, with a prior stalled 23-unit condo project receiving a $52 million construction loan in 2017.
