JV Offloads 254K-SF Hollywood MOB

SALE

Connect CRE — Mon, 14 Sep 2026 14:15:30 +0000

SUMMARY

A joint venture comprising Fox Ridge Capital, Jax Capital Investments, and TKF Real Estate Investment has sold the Venture Center, a 254,015-square-foot professional and medical office complex in Hollywood, Florida, for $69 million. The buyer, South Broward Hospital District, acquired the campus-style property which is strategically located near major highways.
