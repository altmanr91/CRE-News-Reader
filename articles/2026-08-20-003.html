Developer Iadisernia Lands Mezz Loan to Finish Hallandale, Fla., Condo Tower

LOAN

Commercial Observer — Wed, 19 Aug 2026 21:45:27 +0000

SUMMARY

Developer Giuseppe Iadisernia has secured a $50 million mezzanine loan from Cottonwood Group to complete a 250-unit condominium tower in Hallandale Beach, FL. This financing follows a $112 million construction loan provided by S3 Capital last year for the 25-story tower, which is part of the larger 10-acre Oasis mixed-use development. The project, expected to be completed next year, also includes commercial office and retail space.
