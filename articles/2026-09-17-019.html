Private Investor Snags Brea Offices for $28M

SALE

Connect CRE — Wed, 16 Sep 2026 21:39:54 +0000

SUMMARY

A private investor, Whittier Comstock, LLC, has acquired Fairway Center II, a 135,308-square-foot office property in Brea, CA, for $27.8 million. The institutional seller was represented by CBRE, which also secured financing for the buyer. The property was 70.5% leased at the time of sale, anchored by a long-term lease with the County of Orange.
