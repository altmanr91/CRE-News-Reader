JBL Asset Management Buys Retail Condo Near Miami Design District

ACQUISITION

Commercial Observer — Fri, 21 Aug 2026 17:00:32 +0000

SUMMARY

JBL Asset Management acquired a 25,504-square-foot retail condo, located on the ground floor of the Quadro building near the Miami Design District, from Alta Developers for $17.3 million. The property, completed in 2019, is situated at 3900 Biscayne Boulevard and is currently marketing its last remaining retail suite. This transaction occurs amidst a significant expansion of the nearby luxury Miami Design District.
