JLL Arranges $154.1M in Financing for Six-Property Retail Portfolio Spanning Three States

LOAN

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 18 Sep 2026 13:19:19 +0000

SUMMARY

JLL Capital Markets has arranged $154.1 million in financing for a six-property retail portfolio totaling 1.3 million square feet across California, Arizona, and Georgia. The financing was secured for Kimco Realty Corp. and an institutional joint venture partner through institutional investors advised by J.P. Morgan Asset Management. The portfolio boasts a 99% occupancy rate and a strong tenant mix, including several grocery anchors and national retailers, with a significant portion of investment-grade credit tenants.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: retail

Size: 1,300,000 SF

Loan Amount: $154.1M

Occupancy at Close: 99.0%

Notable Features: The portfolio is 99% occupied with a 4.6-year weighted average lease term and features grocery anchors. National tenants occupy approximately 76% of the portfolio's gross leasable area, with 46% of the tenant roster comprising investment-grade credit.
