T&E Development Buys West Chelsea Site for $34M From PPHE Hotel Group

ACQUISITION

Commercial Observer — Thu, 20 Aug 2026 23:29:17 +0000

SUMMARY

T&E Development has acquired a 9,875-square-foot development site in West Chelsea, Manhattan, from PPHE Hotel Group for $33.5 million. The vacant lot, previously slated for a hotel and luxury condominiums, offers 74,063 square feet of development potential under current zoning and is now expected to be redeveloped into a residential project. The seller cited significant changes in New York's regulatory landscape for hotel development as a reason for divesting the property, while the buyer is poised to deliver new residential units into a market segment currently underserved by new construction.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

West Chelsea is experiencing a shortage of new condo construction, with only one building currently under construction between West 20th and West 30th streets. Nearby condo developments have achieved average sales of approximately $2,884 per square foot, and Chelsea pricing has increased by 44 percent since 2020, marking the largest five-year increase among all Manhattan submarkets. The market for buyers in the $2,000 to $2,700 per square foot range is currently underserved.
