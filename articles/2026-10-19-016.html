Dermody Adding 1.2M-SF to El Mirage Industrial Venture

CONSTRUCTION

Connect CRE — Mon, 19 Oct 2026 15:26:11 +0000

SUMMARY

Dermody Properties is expanding its LogistiCenter at Copperwing industrial park in El Mirage, Arizona, with the groundbreaking of two new buildings totaling over 1.2 million square feet. These facilities, designed for flexibility and potential combination into a larger single structure, are expected to be delivered in the third quarter of 2027. This second phase of development follows the successful full lease-up of the project's initial building, underscoring strong demand for industrial space in the region.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Property Name: LogistiCenter at Copperwing, Buildings 3 and 4

Size: 1,213,930 SF

Completion: Q3 2027

Phase I: Building 6 (284,421 SF) fully leased

Total Project (All Phases): More than 7 million square feet

Notable Features: Located approximately 27 minutes from TSMC’s Phoenix-area campus and within an approved Foreign Trade Zone.

Project Notes: This represents the second phase of the 961-acre master-planned industrial park.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

DEVELOPER/SPONSOR: Dermody Properties

LEASING AGENT: CBRE — Pat Feeney; Dan Calihan; Tyler Vowels
