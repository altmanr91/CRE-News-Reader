Marcus & Millichap Brokers $3.1M Sale of The Center at Evergreen Office Property in Colorado

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Mon, 12 Oct 2026 14:57:57 +0000

SUMMARY

Marcus & Millichap has brokered the $3.1 million sale of The Center at Evergreen, an office property in Evergreen, Colorado. Good Signature sold the 45,729-square-foot asset to Divine Homes. The property was 80 percent occupied at the time of sale.
