Personal Training Studio LIV Method Takes 12K SF in Meatpacking District

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Tue, 20 Oct 2026 21:30:04 +0000

SUMMARY

A luxury personal training and wellness studio, The LIV Method, has signed a 12,028-square-foot lease at 2 Gansevoort Street in Manhattan's Meatpacking District. The new location, set to open in January, will be the studio's third in Manhattan, offering a highly personalized and hospitality-driven experience with fitness, sauna, cold plunge, massage, and IV wellness treatments. The landlord expressed that the studio aligns with their vision for the property, enhancing its appeal as a dynamic destination within the neighborhood.
