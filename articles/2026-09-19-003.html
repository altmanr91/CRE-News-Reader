Decron Properties Buys 163-Unit Property in L.A.’s Miracle Mile for $114M

ACQUISITION

Commercial Observer — Fri, 18 Sep 2026 18:30:32 +0000

SUMMARY

Decron Properties has acquired a 163-unit mixed-use property in Los Angeles' Miracle Mile for $114 million, marking the firm's first acquisition in the city in nearly two years. The property, developed in 2010, includes residential units and fully leased ground-floor retail space. Decron's CEO noted that while the firm has focused on Sun Belt markets, Los Angeles remains a key target due to its undersupplied housing market and high barriers to new construction.
