Trilogy Obtains Construction Loan for 172-Unit Build-to-Rent Project in Huntsville

LOAN

REBusinessOnline — Thu, 17 Sep 2026 13:29:07 +0000

SUMMARY

Trilogy Investment Co. has secured a construction loan for REV3 at The Celeste, a 172-unit build-to-rent townhome community in Huntsville, AL. This project marks Trilogy's second build-to-rent development within an opportunity zone fund in partnership with Pinnacle Partners. The community will offer three-bedroom townhomes with attached garages and a range of amenities, with initial deliveries expected in early 2027.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: REV3 at The Celeste

Units: 172

Notable Features: Features three-bedroom townhomes with attached garages, a resort-style pool, clubhouse, fitness center, and outdoor gathering spaces.

Project Notes: Second build-to-rent opportunity zone fund development for Trilogy Investment Co. with Pinnacle Partners.
