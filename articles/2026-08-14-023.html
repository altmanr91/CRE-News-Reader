'Not In The Business Of Losing Our Customers': Renewals Dominate Boston Office Market

Bisnow Latest News Feed — Thu, 13 Aug 2026 19:49:09 -0400

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

Boston's office market is experiencing a surge in lease renewals, as tenants prioritize securing favorable terms on existing spaces rather than incurring the significant expenses associated with new office build-outs. This trend reflects a strategic decision by companies to retain their current locations and avoid the high costs and disruptions of relocation, indicating a tenant-friendly environment for renewals.
