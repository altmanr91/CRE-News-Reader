Alterra Acquires 23.5-Acre Industrial Outdoor Storage Portfolio in San Antonio

ACQUISITION

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 04 Sep 2026 12:32:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Alterra IOS has acquired a portfolio of six industrial outdoor storage (IOS) facilities, totaling 23.5 acres, in the greater San Antonio area. The portfolio includes five properties within San Antonio and one in the suburb of Schertz. These facilities offer a combined 172,141 square feet of warehouse space and are fully leased to a diverse tenant mix across various industrial sectors.

DATA POINTS

Site Area: 23.5 acres
