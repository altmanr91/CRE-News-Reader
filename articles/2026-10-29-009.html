HRSS CPAs Signs 19,569 SF Office Lease in Houston’s Westchase District

LEASE

REBusinessOnline — Thu, 29 Oct 2026 14:50:35 +0000

SUMMARY

HRSS CPAs has signed a 19,569-square-foot office lease for a full floor within Millennium Tower in Houston's Westchase district. The accounting firm will relocate from its current location at 6671 Southwest Freeway. Brokers from Stream Realty Partners represented the tenant, while Cushman & Wakefield represented the landlord.
