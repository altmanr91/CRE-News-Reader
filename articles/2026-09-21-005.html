Newmark Brokers Sale of Four-Building Industrial Portfolio in Silicon Valley

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Mon, 21 Sep 2026 14:19:51 +0000

SUMMARY

Newmark has brokered the sale of The Cadillac Court Industrial Portfolio, a four-building industrial campus in Milpitas, California. Galvanize Real Estate, the sustainable real estate arm of Galvanize, acquired the 302,447-square-foot property from an undisclosed seller. The campus is approximately 95 percent leased and features a strong tenant mix across various industries.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Property Name: The Cadillac Court Industrial Portfolio

Size: 302,447 SF

Occupancy at Sale: 95.0%

Notable Features: Adjacent to Interstate 880 with immediate access to State Route 237, featuring 61 dock-high doors, 16 grade-level doors, and approximately 8,000 amps of power.

Project Notes: Buildings were constructed between 1991 and 1994.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: Galvanize Real Estate

SELLER BROKER: Newmark — Steve Golubchik; Edmund Najera; Darren Hollak; Brendan Raney
