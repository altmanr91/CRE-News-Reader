Gravesend’s new condo record is courtesy of a home-grown developer

SALE

The Real Deal — New York — Thu, 02 Jul 2026 19:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

A new penthouse at The Opal Residences in Brooklyn's Gravesend neighborhood has set a new condo price record for the area, selling for $7.5 million. This transaction surpasses a previous record set by another unit in the same 12-unit development, which sold for $7 million in November. The developer, Redhoek+Partners, acquired the site in 2022 for $8.5 million and secured over $22 million in financing for the project, which has a projected sellout of $68.5 million.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: condo

Notable Features: The building offers amenities such as a doorman, full-time valet, and on-site superintendent, which are rare in this predominantly single-family neighborhood.

Project Notes: The developer previously acquired the former funeral home parking lot site for $8.5 million in 2022. The project had a projected sellout of $68.5 million for 12 units and 20 parking spaces when the condo filing plan was accepted in 2024. All units are reportedly spoken for, with seven units having closed at an average discount of almost 12 percent from the original offering plan.
