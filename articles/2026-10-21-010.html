Partnership Completes Multifamily Project, Public Park in Dover, New Hampshire

DEVELOPMENT

REBusinessOnline — Tue, 20 Oct 2026 14:55:53 +0000

SUMMARY

A partnership between Cathartes and the City of Dover has completed The Truette, a 220-unit multifamily project, and Nebi Park, a 3.5-acre public park, in Dover, New Hampshire's waterfront district. The apartment complex offers a variety of unit types and amenities including a rooftop terrace and coworking spaces, while the park features art installations and a pavilion.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: The Truette

Address: Parking lot, 9 River St, Dover, NH*

Units: 220

Site Area: 3.5 acres

Notable Features: Includes studio, one-, two-, and three-bedroom units, as well as 2,200-square-foot townhomes. Amenities include a rooftop terrace, coworking spaces, fitness areas, pet-friendly facilities, and an onsite restaurant. Nebi Park features art installations and a 2,300-square-foot pavilion.

* Address sourced separately, not in original article.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

DEVELOPER/SPONSOR: Cathartes

DEVELOPER/SPONSOR: City of Dover
