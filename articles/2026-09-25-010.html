Onyx+East celebrates opening of build-to-rent community in Arlington market

REJournals — Fri, 25 Sep 2026 14:04:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Development

Onyx+East has officially opened Haven, a new build-to-rent community in Upper Arlington, Texas, featuring 23 detached single-family rental homes. The community offers the privacy of a traditional home with professional management and maintenance-free living, catering to the growing demand for single-family rentals in the U.S. and Central Ohio.
