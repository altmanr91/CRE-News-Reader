Hudson Bay Capital Refis Microsoft-Anchored SoHo Building With $85M Loan

REFINANCE

Commercial Observer — Wed, 23 Sep 2026 20:30:26 +0000

SUMMARY

InterVest Capital Partners has secured an $85 million refinancing loan from Hudson Bay Capital for its mixed-use office and retail property at 300 Lafayette Street in Manhattan's SoHo neighborhood. The seven-story building, completed in 2019, is largely anchored by a major technology company occupying the entire office component. The strong tenancy and quality of the boutique property attracted competitive lender interest, reflecting its value in the Lower Manhattan market.
