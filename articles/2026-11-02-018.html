Refinancing Multifamily Borrowers Face 41% Increase in Interest Rates

Connect CRE — Mon, 02 Nov 2026 18:27:49 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Interest Rates

Multifamily borrowers are encountering a substantial 41% increase in interest rates when refinancing maturing loans, according to a Newmark report. The average interest rate for new multifamily loans has risen to 5.69%, up from 4.04% for loans maturing in Q3 2026. This creates a negative leverage scenario, as current cap rates average 5.5%. While widespread distress has not yet materialized, property sales prices are showing pressure, with 39% of multifamily properties selling below their inflation-adjusted prior purchase price, a significant jump from 6% in late 2021.

DATA POINTS

Increase in interest costs for refinancing multifamily borrowers: 41%

Average interest rate for new multifamily loans: 5.69%

Average interest rate for loans maturing in Q3 2026: 4.04%

Average cap rates: 5.5%

Percentage of multifamily properties sold below previous inflation-adjusted purchase price: 39%

Percentage of multifamily properties sold below previous inflation-adjusted purchase price (end of 2021): 6%

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Multifamily borrowers face limited protection against further increases in benchmark rates due to already narrow loan spreads. The share of properties trading below their previous inflation-adjusted purchase price is expected to remain elevated as borrowers refinance.
