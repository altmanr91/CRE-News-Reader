Colliers Brokers Sale of Two-Property Multifamily Portfolio in Fallbrook, California for $37M

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 16 Oct 2026 13:34:48 +0000

SUMMARY

Colliers has brokered the sale of a two-property multifamily portfolio in Fallbrook, California, for a combined total of approximately $37 million. The portfolio included Amberwood Apartments, a 133-unit community, and Rosewood Apartments, a 73-unit community. The transactions involved two separate sellers, Amber Creek Associates LTD and Rosewood Associates LTD.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

SELLER: Rosewood Associates LTD

SELLER BROKER: Colliers — Shane Shafer; Brian Anderson
