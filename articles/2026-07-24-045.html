SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

This article compiles insights from various commercial real estate leaders, moving beyond common discussions of remote work and interest rates to explore deeper, unasked questions shaping the office market. Experts from brokerage, investment, debt placement, and research weigh in on topics ranging from the impact of AI on building readiness and corporate geographic footprints to the importance of workplace friendships and neighborhood amenities. The consensus suggests a need for a more nuanced evaluation of office assets, considering factors like Class B potential, the alignment of financing structures with lender expectations, and the long-term rental upside for top-tier properties.
