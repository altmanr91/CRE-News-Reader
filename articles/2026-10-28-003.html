KKR Buys 49% Stake in Alexandria’s San Diego ‘Megacampus’

ACQUISITION

Commercial Observer — Wed, 28 Oct 2026 13:49:08 +0000

SUMMARY

KKR has acquired a 49% non-controlling equity interest and a 28.5% indirect ownership interest in Alexandria Real Estate Equities' Campus Point life sciences megacampus in San Diego. The $475 million investment provides Alexandria with crucial capital amidst a challenging life sciences market and upcoming lease expirations. Alexandria will retain control and consolidate the joint venture, which features a waterfall distribution model designed to provide KKR with at least a 5.5% return on its initial contribution in the first year.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: life sciences

Property Name: Campus Point

Address: 10300 Campus Point Dr, San Diego, CA*

Sale Price: $475.0M

Notable Features: Research center leased to a strong mix of life sciences and institutional tenants.

Project Notes: KKR holds a 49% non-controlling equity interest and a 28.5% indirect ownership interest. The joint venture includes a waterfall distribution model with a minimum 5.5% distribution to KKR in Year 1.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

The life sciences market has experienced saturation since 2021 and faced federal funding cuts since 2025. Alexandria Real Estate Equities is facing 1.4 million square feet in lease expirations in 2027 and has sold or is under contract to sell $242 million of non-core and land holdings as of Q3 to maintain liquidity.
