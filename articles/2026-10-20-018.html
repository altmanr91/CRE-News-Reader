Hanley Investment Group Arranges Sale of Milwaukee Shopping Center

SALE

Connect CRE — Mon, 19 Oct 2026 21:20:03 +0000

SUMMARY

Hanley Investment Group Real Estate Advisors facilitated the sale of Cornerstone Shopping Center, a fully occupied 13,308-square-foot retail center in Glendale, Wisconsin. The property, situated in the North Shore trade area across from the Bayshore mixed-use development, was acquired by SW Neighborhood Centers IV, an affiliate of Shannon Waltchack, from a private investor. The transaction generated multiple competitive offers and closed with an all-cash institutional buyer within 45 days.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: retail

Address: 635-695 West Silver Spring Drive and 5575-5595 North Port Washington Road, Glendale, WI
