Related Affiliate Obtains $167M Loan Package for Miami Apartment Community

LOAN

Connect CRE — Wed, 30 Sep 2026 14:04:45 +0000

SUMMARY

Related Urban Development Group, an affiliate of The Related Group, secured a $167 million financing package for the development of Gallery at Lummus Parc, a 257-unit apartment community in Miami. Greystone provided the comprehensive financing, which includes construction funding, Low-Income Housing Tax Credit (LIHTC) equity, and a Freddie Mac forward commitment for a permanent loan. The project is currently under construction and is anticipated to be completed in late 2028, with a significant portion of its units designated as affordable housing.
