A varied economy? It’s the secret to success of Lansing’s CRE market

REJournals — Fri, 03 Jul 2026 10:13:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Economic Report

Lansing, Michigan's commercial real estate market is being sustained by a diversified economy, despite some sectors experiencing a slight slowdown, according to Colliers' latest report. The region's strong economic fundamentals, including its ranking among the top U.S. mid-sized metros for economic growth and workforce education levels, are supporting demand across industrial, office, and retail sectors. While industrial and office markets show signs of softening with increased vacancies and negative absorption, retail and multifamily sectors remain healthy with stable rents and occupancy rates, indicating a resilient market overall.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Greater Lansing ranks among the top 10 U.S. mid-sized metros for economic growth and among the top 15 regions nationally for workforce education levels. Industrial vacancy rates increased slightly to an average of 2.9% during the first half of 2026, remaining below the national industrial vacancy rate. Office vacancy rate increased to 11.4% in the first half of 2026. Retail vacancy increased to 8.1% during the first half of 2026. Multifamily occupancy rate was 93.7% in Q2 2026.
