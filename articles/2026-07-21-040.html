Self-Storage Developer Closes On Loan for 1,500-Unit Expansion

LOAN

Connect CRE — Mon, 20 Jul 2026 22:41:55 +0000

SUMMARY

UTEX Storage Partners secured a $42.5 million construction loan from Affinius Capital and Axonic Capital for a two-property self-storage portfolio in the Miami metropolitan area. The financing will fund the development of a new 1,097-unit facility in Pembroke Pines and a 540-unit expansion of an existing facility in Coral Gables, bringing its total to 1,500 units. Upon completion, the portfolio will offer a combined 2,597 climate-controlled units.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: self-storage

Units: 2,597

Loan Amount: $42.5M

Notable Features: All units will be climate-controlled with interior and drive-up access and modern loading features.
