Monroe Developer Advancing 275-Unit Midrise

CONSTRUCTION

Connect CRE — Wed, 28 Oct 2026 14:08:55 +0000

SUMMARY

Switzenbaum & Associates has commenced construction on an $80 million, 275-unit midrise apartment development in Monroe, NC. This project, located at the intersection of U.S. 74 and Morgan Mill Road, marks the city's first four-story apartment building and is anticipated to open in Spring 2027. The development will feature a range of amenities including a natural pond, walking paths, a pool, pickleball courts, a putting green, and a fitness center.
