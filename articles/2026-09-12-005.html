Longpoint Buys Miami-Dade Warehouse Portfolio for $195M

ACQUISITION

Commercial Observer — Fri, 11 Sep 2026 18:10:40 +0000

SUMMARY

Longpoint Partners has acquired a 10-building industrial portfolio in Miami-Dade County for $195 million. The portfolio, comprising small-bay properties, totals 729,901 square feet across 41 acres and is 90 percent leased to a diverse tenant base. This acquisition aligns with Longpoint's strategy of securing functional, well-located industrial assets in supply-constrained markets.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Size: 729,901 SF

Sale Price: $195.0M

Occupancy at Sale: 90.0%

Site Area: 41 acres

Notable Features: Small-bay properties with clear heights ranging from 17 to 25 feet.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: Longpoint Partners — Dwight Angelini, Co-founder and Managing Partner
