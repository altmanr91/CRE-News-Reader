Worth & Associates Acquires 108,116 SF Office Complex in San Antonio

ACQUISITION

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 18 Sep 2026 14:40:46 +0000

SUMMARY

Worth & Associates has acquired The Commons at Concord Park, a 108,116-square-foot office complex in San Antonio. The locally based owner-operator plans to implement capital improvements to the property, which consists of four buildings with a range of suite sizes. Cushman & Wakefield represented the seller in the transaction.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Property Name: The Commons at Concord Park

Address: 300 E Sonterra Blvd, San Antonio, TX*

Size: 108,116 SF

Notable Features: Comprises four buildings with suites ranging from 1,254 to 9,790 square feet.

* Address sourced separately, not in original article.
