Colliers Facilitates Sale of Greater Cincinnati Residential Portfolio

SALE

Connect CRE — Wed, 09 Sep 2026 21:26:11 +0000

SUMMARY

Colliers facilitated the sale of an 80-property residential portfolio comprising 125 units across the Greater Cincinnati region. The portfolio, consisting of single-family homes, duplexes, small multifamily properties, a mixed-use property, and a land parcel, was acquired by Three Little Pigs Investment Services, LLC. The buyer plans to renovate the properties and return many of the homes to individual ownership, recognizing the value-add potential in the Cincinnati market.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Units: 125

Notable Features: Portfolio includes 58 single-family rental homes, eight duplexes, 12 small multifamily properties with six units or fewer, one mixed-use property, and one land parcel.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: Three Little Pigs Investment Services, LLC

SELLER: private entity

SELLER BROKER: Colliers — Marianne Taylor, vice president; Virginia Cunningham, associate
