RFR Buys Prada-Anchored Retail Condo at 841 Madison Avenue for $57M

ACQUISITION

Commercial Observer — Thu, 10 Sep 2026 20:19:35 +0000

SUMMARY

RFR Holding has acquired a 18,000-square-foot retail condominium at 841 Madison Avenue in New York City for $57 million from JSRE Acquisitions. The property is anchored by a luxury fashion house, which has occupied the space since 1996. Brokers involved in the transaction noted that this sale underscores Madison Avenue's resurgence as a prime luxury retail destination, with high-caliber tenancy driving up rents and quality in the corridor over the past 18 months.
