Chicago’s top construction permits: Week ending July 8, 2026

The Real Deal — Chicago — Thu, 09 Jul 2026 21:51:32 +0000

SUMMARY

Construction / Permit Report

Northwestern Memorial Hospital is undertaking an $81.3 million renovation of its Olson Pavilion lab and office building in Streeterville, Chicago. The project involves extensive alterations across multiple floors and a mechanical penthouse addition. Additionally, GI Partners and Legacy Investing secured approval for a $51.1 million infrastructure upgrade at 400 South La Salle Street, converting an office building into a data center. A joint venture between The Prime Group and Capri Investment Group is also pursuing a $28 million modification to the Thompson Center, an iconic office and retail building in the Loop.
