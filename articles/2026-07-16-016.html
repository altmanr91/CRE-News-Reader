BKM drops $86M for more industrial buildings in Seattle area

ACQUISITION

The Real Deal — National — Wed, 15 Jul 2026 17:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

BKM Capital Partners has expanded its industrial holdings in the Puget Sound region with the acquisition of a five-building portfolio in Kent and Tukwila for $85.7 million. The portfolio, totaling 413,840 square feet across two business parks, was sold by Blackstone's Link Logistics. This acquisition follows BKM's recent joint venture with Kayne Anderson Real Estate, which aims to invest $1.5 billion in industrial real estate nationwide, reflecting continued investor interest in the industrial sector despite rising vacancy rates in the local market.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Size: 413,840 SF

Sale Price: $85.7M

Project Notes: The portfolio's combined assessed value is roughly $77 million. Three buildings are in Southcenter West Business Park, Tukwila, and last sold in 2015 for $30.2 million. Two buildings are in Upland Corporate Park, Kent, and last traded in 2012 for $4.3 million and 2017 for $8.2 million.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: BKM Capital Partners

SELLER: Link Logistics

EQUITY/FINANCING: Blackstone

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Industrial vacancy rates in the Puget Sound region rose from 2.9 percent in late 2021 to 10.3 percent earlier this year, as new supply outpaces leasing activity.
