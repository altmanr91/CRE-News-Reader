George Comfort & Sons Refinances 200 Madison for $386M

REFINANCE

Connect CRE — Wed, 23 Sep 2026 22:00:46 +0000

SUMMARY

George Comfort & Sons, in partnership with Loeb Partners Realty and Jamestown, has secured a $386 million refinancing for 200 Madison Avenue, a 750,000-square-foot office tower in New York City's Grand Central district. The five-year floating-rate loan, provided by New York Life, will be used to retire existing debt and support ongoing leasing efforts. This refinancing reflects the lending community's confidence in well-located, amenity-rich office properties with strong sponsorship, particularly following a significant lease extension and expansion by a major tenant.
