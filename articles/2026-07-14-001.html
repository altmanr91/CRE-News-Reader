Information Is Becoming Cheaper. Judgment Is Becoming Priceless.

Commercial Observer — Tue, 14 Jul 2026 10:45:53 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Editorial

This opinion piece argues that while artificial intelligence is rapidly commoditizing information, human judgment is becoming an increasingly invaluable competitive advantage in the commercial real estate industry. The author contends that true expertise now lies in the ability to interpret readily available information, discern critical facts, assess hidden risks, and make superior decisions. Judgment, unlike information, cannot be downloaded or replicated by AI; it must be earned through accumulated pattern recognition, intuition, and contextual understanding derived from continuous learning from successes and failures. The author emphasizes that experience is measured by lessons extracted, not just tenure, and that relentless curiosity and self-analysis will be key for professionals to thrive in the AI era.
