Massachusetts Awards $15M in Tax Credits to Support 856 New Homes

Connect CRE — Fri, 28 Aug 2026 21:21:13 +0000

SUMMARY

Infrastructure Investment / Government Policy

The Healey-Driscoll Administration has allocated $15.3 million in Commercial Conversion Tax Credits to five projects across Massachusetts, facilitating the transformation of vacant or underutilized commercial properties into 856 new rental homes. This initiative, part of the Affordable Homes Act signed in 2024, aims to revitalize downtowns and commercial districts by increasing housing supply and bringing new residents and economic activity to these areas. The projects include the adaptive reuse of a historic office building in Boston, a former retail building in Worcester, an obsolete hotel in Westford, historic buildings in Springfield, and commercial buildings in Dedham.

DATA POINTS

Total Tax Credits Awarded: $15.3 million

Number of New Rental Homes: 856

First Round Tax Credits Awarded: $8.4 million

First Round New Homes: 339
