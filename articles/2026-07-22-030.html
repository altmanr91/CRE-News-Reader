From distressed debt to apartments: Envoi locks in $64M Fortress loan for LaSalle office to resi conversion

LOAN

The Real Deal — Chicago — Tue, 21 Jul 2026 15:49:06 +0000

SUMMARY

Envoi Partners secured a $64 million construction loan from Fortress Investment Group for the adaptive reuse of a 16-story office building at 19 South LaSalle Street in downtown Chicago. The financing package, which includes senior debt, federal historic tax credits, and private capital, will facilitate the conversion of 207,000 square feet of office space into 207 apartments and 6,811 square feet of ground-floor retail. This project, expected to be completed in 15 months, is a significant step in the revitalization of the LaSalle Street corridor, transforming a historic building into modern residential and retail space.

Project Notes: Envoi Partners acquired the building's loan note for $4.2 million in October 2024 and assumed ownership for the apartment rebuild. The project received a fee waiver for its permit.
