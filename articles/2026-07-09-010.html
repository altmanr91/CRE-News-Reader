Reports: Midtown Manhattan Office Building ‘Stable’ Following Yesterday’s Evacuation

REBusinessOnline — Wed, 08 Jul 2026 14:33:32 +0000

SUMMARY

Construction / Incident Report

A Midtown Manhattan office building undergoing a residential conversion was declared "stable" by New York City Department of Buildings Commissioner Ahmed Tigani following an evacuation due to buckling columns and sagging floors. Emergency stabilization measures, including the installation of jacks and new steel, have been implemented. City officials are confident in these measures but have not ruled out a localized collapse as additional stabilization practices are planned.
