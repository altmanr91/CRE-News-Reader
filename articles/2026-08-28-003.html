Dwight Supplies $70M Refi on Newark Multifamily Tower

REFINANCE

Commercial Observer — Fri, 28 Aug 2026 18:30:20 +0000

SUMMARY

New Jersey developer Yisroel Berger has secured a $70 million nonrecourse, interest-only loan from Dwight Investment Management to refinance the newly redeveloped Cosmo 440, a 216-unit multifamily tower in Newark, NJ. The 25-story building, which was gut-renovated after sitting vacant for a decade, has been transformed into an amenity-rich community with 26 units designated as affordable housing. The refinancing marks the project's transition from its construction phase and lease-up to a stabilized asset, strategically located near transit and Newark Liberty International Airport.
