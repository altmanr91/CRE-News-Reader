Gensler’s Adam Griff Joins JLL’s Nonprofit, Education and Government Practice

Commercial Observer — Tue, 03 Nov 2026 16:49:59 +0000

SUMMARY

Promotion

Adam Griff, a veteran in architecture and strategic planning, has joined JLL as a managing director within its nonprofit, education, and government practice. Based in New York, Griff will focus on advising educational institutions and other mission-driven organizations on real estate and institutional strategies. This move comes as higher education faces significant restructuring, prompting institutions to re-evaluate how their campuses align with academic missions and financial sustainability. Griff's expertise is expected to enhance JLL's capacity to guide clients through these complex decisions, helping them identify new opportunities and maximize real estate asset value for long-term success.
