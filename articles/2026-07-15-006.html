LITEON to Invest $919M in Metro Dallas Manufacturing Campus, Create 600 Jobs

ACQUISITION

REBusinessOnline — Tue, 14 Jul 2026 15:28:55 +0000

SUMMARY

LITEON Technology Corp., a provider of power management and AI infrastructure solutions, has acquired over 650,000 square feet of existing industrial space within Core5 Logistics Center in McKinney, Texas. The company plans to invest $919 million in capital improvements to establish a new manufacturing campus, which is expected to create approximately 600 new jobs. This significant investment was facilitated through collaboration with the City of McKinney and the McKinney Economic Development Corp., and is anticipated to bolster the city's high-tech employer base and tax revenue.
