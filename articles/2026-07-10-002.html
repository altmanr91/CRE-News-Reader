Nvidia Signs Downtown D.C. Office Lease as Trophy Buildings Outperform

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Thu, 09 Jul 2026 20:30:48 +0000

SUMMARY

NVIDIA, a leading artificial intelligence chipmaker, has signed a 27,600-square-foot sublease in the Woodies Building at 1025 F Street NW in Washington, D.C. The space is being subleased from Morning Consult. This transaction highlights the ongoing trend of technology companies expanding their footprint in Downtown D.C., particularly within premier office buildings, as the market sees a flight to quality.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Property Name: Woodies Building

Address: 1025 F Street NW, Washington, D.C.

Size: 27,600 SF

Project Notes: The space was previously occupied by the FBI, whose lease expired last year. Morning Consult had leased the entire 50,000-square-foot eighth floor in 2020.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Trophy office vacancy in Washington, D.C. finished Q2 at 13.4%, while overall vacancy climbed to 23.3%. Gross leasing totaled 1.6 million square feet during Q2, and 2.9 million square feet during the first half of the year, representing a 12% decrease from the same period last year.
