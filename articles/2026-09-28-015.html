Equus Capital Partners acquires 226-unit apartment community in Geneva

ACQUISITION

REJournals — Mon, 28 Sep 2026 13:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Equus Capital Partners, on behalf of a programmatic joint venture with a U.S.-based public pension plan, has acquired Ashford at Geneva, a 226-unit garden-style apartment community in Geneva, Illinois. The property was approximately 97% occupied at the time of acquisition. Madison Apartment Group, Equus's multifamily operating affiliate, will manage the property and implement a capital improvement program for apartment interiors, common areas, and select exterior enhancements.
