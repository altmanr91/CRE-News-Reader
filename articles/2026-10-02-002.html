Inside Ken Griffin’s $3B Bet on Miami’s Wynwood

ACQUISITION

Commercial Observer — Thu, 01 Oct 2026 21:25:14 +0000

SUMMARY

Billionaire Ken Griffin has acquired a 35-acre portfolio in Miami's Wynwood neighborhood for $1.1 billion in an all-cash transaction, marking one of the largest commercial real estate deals in U.S. history. The acquisition is part of a larger $3 billion commitment by Griffin, with $2 billion dedicated to establishing Carnegie Mellon University Miami, which will be housed on the newly acquired Wynwood site. The new campus aims to address Miami's need for top-tier universities to support its growing tech and finance sectors, with construction slated to begin next year and enrollment starting in 2028.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: mixed-use

Beds: 3,500

Sale Price: $1.10B

Completion: 2028 (graduate students), 2032 (undergraduate students)

Site Area: 35 acres

Notable Features: The campus is expected to house over 3,500 students and nearly 300 faculty members, focusing on computer science, medical research, national security, climate resilience, manufacturing, and industrial innovation.

Project Notes: The acquisition was an all-cash deal without outside debt or partners. The site's special area plan (SAP) will need to be amended to include educational uses. The $1.1 billion real estate value is part of a larger $2 billion gift to launch CMU Miami, with an additional $1 billion supporting Carnegie Mellon University's Pittsburgh campus.
