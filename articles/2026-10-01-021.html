NYC Unveils The Places Where Council Members Can't Block Housing Anymore

Bisnow Latest News Feed — Thu, 01 Oct 2026 00:05:02 -0400

SUMMARY

Infrastructure Investment / Government Policy

New York City has identified 12 specific areas where the process for approving affordable housing developments will be significantly streamlined, reducing the rezoning review period to just 90 days. This initiative aims to accelerate the production of much-needed affordable housing by limiting the ability of individual City Council members to block projects in these designated zones. The move is part of a broader strategy to address the city's housing crisis and increase the supply of affordable units more efficiently.

DATA POINTS

Number of areas designated for accelerated affordable housing production: 12

Reduced rezoning wait time in designated areas: 90 days
