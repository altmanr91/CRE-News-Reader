Cyber Risk Management Firm Signs Five-Year Renewal of Midtown Offices

LEASE

Connect CRE — Thu, 20 Aug 2026 22:11:00 +0000

SUMMARY

A cyber risk management firm has renewed its 8,475-square-foot headquarters lease for five years at 1140 Ave. of the Americas in Midtown Manhattan. The company, a tenant since 2023, will continue to occupy a full floor that includes a private outdoor terrace and collaborative workspace. The building's premier Midtown location, transit access, and modern amenities were cited as key factors in the decision to renew, supporting the firm's growth and talent attraction efforts.
