SF office market continues recovery with another AI-powered lease in South Beach

LEASE

The Real Deal — San Francisco — Thu, 23 Jul 2026 23:18:27 +0000

SUMMARY

Fintech firm Brex, recently acquired by Capital One, has significantly expanded its San Francisco footprint by signing a full-building, 200,000-square-foot lease at 270 Brannan Street in the South Beach neighborhood. This move more than triples the company's previous office space and reflects a growing trend of AI-backed firms driving office demand and positive net absorption in the city. The company's president noted the momentum around AI as a key factor in their decision to commit to a larger physical presence in San Francisco.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Address: 270 Brannan Street, San Francisco, CA

Size: 200,000 SF

Notable Features: Located in the South Beach neighborhood, near OpenAI's headquarters. The building is Class A.

Project Notes: Brex previously leased approximately 100,000 square feet in the same building last year after returning to San Francisco.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

San Francisco experienced its seventh consecutive quarter of positive net absorption in Q2 2026. AI startups accounted for approximately one-third of all leasing activity in the city, totaling nearly 1 million square feet out of 3 million square feet of overall leasing activity. Mission Bay had the lowest office vacancy at 17%, while Yerba Buena had the highest at 62.7%. Class A buildings comprised 77% of leasing activity in Q2 2026.
