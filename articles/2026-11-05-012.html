Cushman & Wakefield brokers sale of former public works facility in O’Fallon

SALE

REJournals — Thu, 05 Nov 2026 15:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Cushman & Wakefield brokered the sale of the former City of O'Fallon Public Works facility, a 37,002-square-foot industrial property on 5.57 acres, to Earthworks Inc. for $4,400,500. The property, comprising two industrial buildings with warehouse, office, and service space, along with secured outdoor storage, is strategically located in the growing St. Charles County industrial market. The sale occurred after the City of O'Fallon relocated its operations, allowing the new owner to reposition the asset for continued industrial or contractor-oriented uses.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Size: 37,002 SF

Sale Price: $4.4M

Site Area: 5.57 acres

Project Notes: The property previously served as the City of O'Fallon's public works operations center before the city relocated to a new facility.
