Opus begins construction on new high school for Des Moines Christian School

CONSTRUCTION

REJournals — Fri, 31 Jul 2026 14:35:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Opus has commenced construction on a new 133,670-square-foot high school for Des Moines Christian School in Urbandale, Iowa. The facility, located on a 104-acre site acquired by the school in 2024, is a key component of the school's 'Build Today | Plan for Tomorrow' campaign, designed to enhance the student experience. The new high school will feature a two-story academic wing with 24 classrooms and three labs, a collegiate-sized gymnasium, and dedicated spaces for wellness, fine arts, and student activities.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: educational

Property Name: Des Moines Christian School High School

Address: 4300 136th St, Urbandale, IA

Size: 133,670 SF

Site Area: 104 acres

Notable Features: Features a two-story academic wing with 24 classrooms and three labs, including STEM spaces, and a gymnasium with seating for over 2,000 fans.

Project Notes: The 104-acre site was purchased in 2024. The project is part of the school's 'Build Today | Plan for Tomorrow' campaign.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

GENERAL CONTRACTOR: Opus

OWNER: Des Moines Christian School

ARCHITECT: SVPA Architects
