Principle Construction completes renovation of historic industrial site in Forest View

REJournals — Tue, 28 Jul 2026 10:03:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Construction

Principle Construction and Crosswest Capital have completed the renovation of a 31,200-square-foot industrial facility in Forest View, Illinois, transforming it into a modern cross-dock building optimized for industrial outdoor storage and fleet maintenance. The project involved extensive upgrades including new dock doors, three new repair bays, and the demolition of 9,200 square feet to create a dedicated maneuverability court. The modernized facility is now being marketed for lease by Crosswest Capital and NAI Hiffman.
