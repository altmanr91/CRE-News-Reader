Retail Veterans Joshua Strauss and Scott Zinovoy Join JLL

Commercial Observer — Mon, 20 Jul 2026 20:41:14 +0000

SUMMARY

Promotion

Joshua Strauss and Scott Zinovoy, co-founders of Dreamscape, have joined JLL to bolster the firm's experiential retail, entertainment, and mixed-use leasing initiatives. Strauss will serve as executive managing director, and Zinovoy as senior vice president. Their expertise in creating compelling retail destinations and navigating the evolving retail landscape is expected to enhance JLL's project leasing platforms in New York and nationally.
