Buyers at Rishi Kapoor’s condo projects could get share of $4M in receivership funds

The Real Deal — Miami — Mon, 20 Jul 2026 22:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

CRE Legal / Regulatory

A court-appointed receiver is seeking authorization to distribute approximately $3.5 million from receivership funds to buyers and creditors who lost money in convicted developer Rishi Kapoor's real estate schemes. The funds are intended to satisfy claims related to the Villa Valencia condominium in Coral Gables and the failed Urbin Miami Beach co-living condo development. This distribution follows Kapoor's guilty plea to money laundering and payroll tax evasion, where he admitted to misappropriating $85 million from investors and unlawfully using $10.6 million in buyer deposits.
