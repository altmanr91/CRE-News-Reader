CBRE Arranges $109.5M Permanent Loan for Jersey City Apartment Building

LOAN

REBusinessOnline — Wed, 12 Aug 2026 14:31:59 +0000

SUMMARY

CBRE has secured a $109.5 million permanent loan for 425 Summit, a recently completed 27-story apartment building in Jersey City's Journal Square. The financing was provided by Northwestern Mutual to a joint venture between Spitzer Enterprises and Arden Group, who own the property.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: 425 Summit

Address: 425 Summit, Jersey City, NJ

Units: 390

Loan Amount: $109.5M

Loan Per Unit: $280,769/unit (calculated)

Year Built: 2024

Notable Features: Includes ground-floor retail space.
