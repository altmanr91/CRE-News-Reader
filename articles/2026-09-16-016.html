AI Legal Platform Orbital Signs Full-Floor Lease in Flatiron District

LEASE

Connect CRE — Tue, 15 Sep 2026 22:00:12 +0000

SUMMARY

Orbital, an AI legal platform specializing in real estate, has signed an 11,185-square-foot full-floor office lease at 40 W. 25th St. in Manhattan's Flatiron District. This new space will serve as the company's New York City headquarters, facilitating its continued U.S. expansion and collaboration with clients and partners in the real estate transaction ecosystem. The move from a temporary shared office underscores Orbital's growth, supported by a recent $60 million Series B funding round.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Address: 40 W. 25th St., New York City, NY

Size: 11,185 SF

Notable Features: Full-floor occupancy on the sixth floor of a 12-story building.
