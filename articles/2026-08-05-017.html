Development Site with Uncommonly Long Frontage Sells in Brooklyn

SALE

Connect CRE — Tue, 04 Aug 2026 21:40:07 +0000

SUMMARY

A residential development site with significant frontage in Brooklyn's Bedford-Stuyvesant neighborhood has been sold for $23.5 million. The 26,730-square-foot corner parcel, located at 1150 Broadway, was acquired by YS Developers and Rabsky Group from A-PLUS International Realty. The site offers substantial development potential, with zoning for a mixed-use building up to 133,917 square feet, and benefits from its location within a Qualified Opportunity Zone and eligibility for the 485-x tax abatement program. The property's extensive wraparound frontage and direct subway access were key factors in attracting strong developer interest.
