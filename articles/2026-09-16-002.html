Mesa West Lends $27M on Seattle-Area Apartments Acquisition

LOAN

Commercial Observer — Wed, 16 Sep 2026 11:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Mesa West Capital has provided $27 million in acquisition financing to Timberlane Partners for the purchase and renovation of Vista Ridge, a 91-unit garden-style apartment community in Issaquah, WA. Timberlane Partners acquired the property for $37 million in an off-market transaction, with plans to execute a value-add repositioning given the property's original condition. The five-year, nonrecourse, first-mortgage loan was originated by Mesa West Capital, which cited Timberlane's strong track record in the Seattle multifamily market.
