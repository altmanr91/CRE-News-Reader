JLL negotiates law firm lease at Chicago’s 155 N. Wacker Drive

LEASE

REJournals — Mon, 12 Oct 2026 15:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Blanchet LLP, a newly formed boutique litigation firm, has leased 10,647 square feet at 155 North Wacker Drive in Chicago's West Loop. The firm, which launched in late summer 2026, selected the trophy office tower for its Chicago office, citing the building's amenities and location among other leading law firms as crucial for attracting and retaining top legal talent. This lease reflects a broader trend in Chicago's downtown office market, where Class A and Trophy properties are experiencing positive net absorption, while lower-quality buildings are seeing tenant departures, highlighting a growing demand for premium office environments.
