First Industrial Leases 708,000 SF Building in York, Pennsylvania

LEASE

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 23 Oct 2026 14:01:34 +0000

SUMMARY

First Industrial Realty Trust has fully leased its 708,000-square-foot industrial building, First Logistics Center @ I-83, in York, Pennsylvania. The tenant was not disclosed. This significant lease underscores the strong demand for large-scale logistics facilities in Central Pennsylvania, a region known for its strategic access to major transportation arteries.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Property Name: First Logistics Center @ I-83

Address: 20 Leo Ln, York, PA*

Size: 708,000 SF

Current Occupancy: 100.0%

Notable Features: Proximity to Interstates 76, 81, and 78, as well as Harrisburg International Airport.

* Address sourced separately, not in original article.
