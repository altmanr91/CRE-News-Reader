MMG Real Estate Advisors Arranges Sale of 192-Unit Apartment Complex in Bryan, Texas

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Mon, 05 Oct 2026 14:21:59 +0000

SUMMARY

MMG Real Estate Advisors has facilitated the sale of The Element at University Park, a 192-unit apartment complex in Bryan, Texas. Houston-based Lumen Capital sold the property to Hayden Properties. The transaction involved a regional brokerage firm, highlighting continued investment activity in Central Texas multifamily assets.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: The Element at University Park

Address: 4475 Carter Creek Pkwy, Bryan, TX*

Units: 192

Year Built: 2000

Notable Features: Offers one- and two-bedroom units, sports courts (basketball, volleyball, pickleball), and a business center.

* Address sourced separately, not in original article.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

SELLER: Lumen Capital

BUYER: Hayden Properties

SELLER BROKER: MMG Real Estate Advisors — Harris Weber; Michael Moffitt; Michael Watson; Michael Miller; Thomas Skevington
