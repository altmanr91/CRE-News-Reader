Morehouse, Spelman Debut $147M Student Housing Project

CONSTRUCTION

Connect CRE — Wed, 21 Oct 2026 13:47:04 +0000

SUMMARY

Radnor Property Group, in partnership with Madrone Community Development Foundation, has broken ground on The Den at Eight50 West, a $147 million student housing development in Atlanta's West End. This project, developed in collaboration with Morehouse College and Spelman College, will provide 793 beds across 305 apartment-style units. The development is expected to be ready for occupancy by Fall 2028, offering a blend of community-oriented and independent living spaces with a range of amenities.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: student housing

Property Name: The Den at Eight50 West

Address: 850 West End Avenue, Atlanta, GA

Units: 305

Beds: 793

Total Project Cost: $147.0M

Completion: Fall 2028

Notable Features: Amenities include a 24/7 staffed lobby, outdoor recreation space, fitness room, community lounges, bike storage, dog run, and study rooms.

Project Notes: Construction commenced in July 2026.
