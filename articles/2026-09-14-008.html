CBRE Negotiates Sale of Two Industrial Buildings Leased to Tesla in Fort Worth Area

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Mon, 14 Sep 2026 14:41:27 +0000

SUMMARY

CBRE has facilitated the sale of two newly constructed industrial buildings in the Fort Worth area, both fully leased to a major electric vehicle manufacturer. The properties, located in Fort Worth and Flower Mound, represent a significant investment in the region's industrial market, reflecting strong demand for modern logistics and distribution facilities.
