Eli Karp sues attorney Leo Jacobs for malpractice

The Real Deal — New York — Wed, 15 Jul 2026 21:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

CRE Legal / Regulatory

Developer Eli Karp has filed a malpractice lawsuit against his former attorney, Leo Jacobs, alleging that Jacobs failed to prevent a $16 million judgment against him related to the foreclosure of a 55-unit apartment building at 271 Lenox Road. Karp claims Jacobs did not submit necessary motions or pursue lawsuits that could have reversed the judgment, despite charging high hourly rates. Karp is seeking a refund of legal fees and damages, asserting that the judgment has severely impacted his ability to conduct real estate business.
