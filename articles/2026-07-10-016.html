Dream Finders Homes keeps chasing rival acquisition

The Real Deal — National — Thu, 09 Jul 2026 16:30:13 +0000

SUMMARY

Non-CRE / Business News

Dream Finders Homes has increased its bid to $32 per share, or $875 million, in an attempt to acquire Atlanta-based homebuilder Beazer Homes USA. This marks at least the third offer from Dream Finders, which previously made public bids of $25.75 and $29.25 per share. Beazer Homes has rejected the offers, citing an undervaluation and expressing interest from other potential transaction parties, while also requesting a confidentiality and standstill agreement which Dream Finders declined.
