Brooklyn Village site in Charlotte changes hands twice in two month span

ACQUISITION

The Real Deal — National — Tue, 07 Jul 2026 12:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

A key portion of the Brooklyn Village redevelopment in Charlotte, comprising 5.8 acres at 700 and 800 East Brooklyn Village Avenue, has been acquired by First and Early Partners for $24.3 million. This marks the second transaction for the property in two months, as Peachtree Group had previously acquired it from Peebles Corporation in May. The site was originally intended for the first phase of a $700 million redevelopment, including multifamily units and retail space, and its new owners have not yet disclosed their plans.

Project Notes: The initial phase of the Brooklyn Village project was planned to include 496 market-rate multifamily units, 56 affordable units, and 20,000 square feet of retail space. Peachtree Group had greenlit $23.8 million in funds to Peebles Corporation in 2023 to secure the location and fund construction, and filed for foreclosure in early May before dismissing the action and transferring the deed.
