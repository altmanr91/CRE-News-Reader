Former professional golf coach Hank Haney selling home in Glencoe

The Real Deal — Chicago — Tue, 07 Jul 2026 17:45:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Celebrity Residential / Non-CRE

Former professional golf coach Hank Haney has listed his Glencoe residence for $4.3 million. Haney and his wife purchased the 8,400-square-foot home in January 2024 for approximately $3.8 million. The property, built in 2022, features a cabana house, outdoor kitchen, and a pool, and is situated on just under a half-acre.
