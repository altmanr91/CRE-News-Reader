Blackburn lands $47M bridge loan to refinance Leander apartments as Austin rents continue to fall

REFINANCE

The Real Deal — Texas — Fri, 17 Jul 2026 12:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Blackburn Communities has secured a $46.5 million bridge loan to refinance its River Junction Apartments in Leander, a suburb of Austin, TX. The financing, arranged by Walker & Dunlop and provided by Bridge Investment Group, comes as the Austin multifamily market continues to experience declining rents due to significant new supply. Despite current concessions offered at the property, market experts anticipate a rebound in Austin area rental rates by 2027 as the development pipeline slows.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Austin's multifamily market has absorbed over 100,000 new apartment units in the last six years, with Leander, Pflugerville, and East Austin receiving the most new supply. Rents for new leases in Austin were down 3.9 percent year over year in June, an improvement from the 7.5 percent decline in March. Austin rent growth is projected to turn positive in 2027.
