Local Owner-Operator Snags Pico-Robertson Apartment Property

SALE

Connect CRE — Fri, 21 Aug 2026 21:32:09 +0000

SUMMARY

A 16-unit multifamily property in Los Angeles' Pico-Robertson neighborhood has been sold for $5.3 million. The asset, which had been under the same ownership for 23 years, attracted significant buyer interest due to its desirable location and large floor plans. The new owner-operator plans to implement renovations as units turn over, indicating a long-term investment strategy.
