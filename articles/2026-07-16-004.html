Data Intelligence Firm Curinos Takes 14K SF at Marx Realty’s 10 Grand Central

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Wed, 15 Jul 2026 19:06:56 +0000

SUMMARY

Data intelligence firm Curinos is relocating its offices to Marx Realty's 10 Grand Central in New York City, signing a 14,000-square-foot lease. The move from its current location two blocks away underscores the appeal of the building's hospitality-infused design and amenities, which include a house car service, a seventh-floor lounge and cafe, an outdoor terrace, and a speakeasy-style restaurant. The transaction brings the building to 98 percent leased, reflecting strong demand for high-quality office experiences in the Grand Central market.
