Big Banks Shake Off CRE Jitters, Cut Loss Reserves And Raise AI Hopes

Bisnow News Feed — Wed, 15 Jul 2026 16:41:13 -0400

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

Major banks are increasingly optimistic about the commercial real estate market, reducing their loan loss provisions and shifting focus towards the burgeoning artificial intelligence and data center sectors. Despite lingering concerns over pandemic-era loan delinquencies, institutions like JPMorgan Chase, Goldman Sachs, Bank of America, Citigroup, and Wells Fargo reported strong second-quarter earnings, largely driven by significant investments and opportunities in AI and data center development. This pivot reflects a broader economic reordering and a resilient real estate sales landscape, with transaction volumes rebounding and banks expressing confidence in the long-term growth potential of these tech-driven asset classes.
