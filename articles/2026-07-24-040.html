Shorenstein pays $1K psf premium for Redwood City offices

ACQUISITION

The Real Deal — San Francisco — Thu, 23 Jul 2026 19:30:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Shorenstein has acquired a six-story, 76,700-square-foot office building at 550 Allerton Street in Redwood City for $78 million, or $1,018 per square foot. The property, developed in 2018 by Premia Capital, is 81 percent leased with a venture capital law firm as its anchor tenant. This transaction reflects a premium price for the Redwood City market, indicating strong demand for high-quality downtown office assets in the Peninsula and Silicon Valley.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Redwood City office buildings command an average per-square-foot asking price of $868, with listings maxing out at $1,346 per square foot. The Silicon Valley office market closed Q2 with a 15.2% vacancy rate, with Class A properties at 14.1% vacancy. Leasing activity was up 15.7% quarter over quarter, bolstered by a 9.8% increase in Class A leasing.
