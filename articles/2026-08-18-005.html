PGIM Sells Delray Beach Assisted Living Community for at Least $140M

ACQUISITION

Commercial Observer — Mon, 17 Aug 2026 17:59:10 +0000

SUMMARY

Artemis Real Estate Partners has acquired The Arbor at Delray, a 207-unit assisted living facility in Delray Beach, Florida, from PGIM Real Estate for at least $140 million. Completed in 2023, the 224,951-square-foot community includes a recreational clubhouse and a nursing home. This transaction represents one of the largest retirement home sales in South Florida recently, reflecting strong demand in Palm Beach County where occupancy rates for assisted living facilities are nearing 90 percent.
