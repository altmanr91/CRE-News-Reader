Adam America, JW Capital Land $180M Refi for Florida International Student Housing

REFINANCE

Commercial Observer — Tue, 22 Sep 2026 11:05:30 +0000

SUMMARY

A joint venture between Adam America Real Estate and JW Capital Management has secured $180 million in refinancing for Terrazul, a 1,201-bed student housing property adjacent to Florida International University in Sweetwater, FL. The loan, provided by Corebridge Financial, will retire the existing construction financing and provide cash-out proceeds to the owners. The 22-story property, completed in 2024, is currently 98 percent occupied, demonstrating the strength of the FIU student housing market.
