Day Care Operator Inks 21K-SF Lease in Brooklyn’s Gravesend

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Thu, 29 Oct 2026 14:18:46 +0000

SUMMARY

Ukrainian New Generation Kids Daycare has signed a 21,000-square-foot lease for the entire commercial building at 1940 Shore Parkway in Brooklyn's Gravesend neighborhood. The new day care center, which will accommodate 300 children, is expected to open in the second quarter of 2027. Brokers noted that securing a tenant for a space of this size with specialized requirements presented challenges, but a collaborative approach helped finalize the deal.
