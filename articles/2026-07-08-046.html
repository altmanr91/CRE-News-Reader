Blackstone Eyes £6B Indurent Exit As Logistics M&A Heats Up

Bisnow News Feed — Tue, 07 Jul 2026 10:44:32 -0400

SUMMARY

Property Listing / For Sale

Blackstone is exploring a potential sale or London listing for Indurent, its UK industrial and logistics platform, which could be valued at approximately £6 billion. Indurent was formed in February 2024 through the merger of Industrials REIT and St Modwen Logistics, and has since expanded with £2 billion in acquisitions and developments. Separately, Prologis is attempting a £12.6 billion all-stock takeover of Segro, the UK's largest publicly traded property company, an offer that Segro's board has rejected as undervalued, citing market dislocation and the company's embedded growth potential. Segro has also recently formed a joint venture with PSP to develop three major UK logistics parks.
