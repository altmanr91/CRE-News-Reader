Flexday Opens Flexible Office Space in Chicago’s Loop

LEASE

Connect CRE — Fri, 09 Oct 2026 21:36:43 +0000

SUMMARY

Flexday, a national provider of flexible office solutions, has launched four fractional office suites totaling over 25,000 square feet at PNC Centre in Chicago's Loop. This initiative, a partnership with building owner MetLife Investment Management, offers companies flexible, on-demand office space for teams of 10 to 70, providing a Class A environment without a long-term lease commitment. The launch is part of a broader capital investment by MetLife Investment Management in PNC Centre, which includes a newly renovated lobby and upgraded tenant amenities.
