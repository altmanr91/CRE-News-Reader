Elon Musk’s Austin area empire triples land holdings

ACQUISITION

The Real Deal — Texas — Tue, 14 Jul 2026 16:27:35 +0000

SUMMARY

Entities associated with Elon Musk have significantly expanded their land holdings in Bastrop County, Texas, tripling their acreage to approximately 2,000 acres since May. The acquisitions, totaling about 1,360 acres in the last three months, are concentrated along FM 969, a corridor between Bastrop and Austin. While the specific plans for the newly acquired land remain undisclosed, the area is already home to Musk's Gigasat solar factory, and his various companies have established a substantial presence in the Austin region since 2020.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: land

Site Area: 1360 acres

Notable Features: Land holdings are concentrated along FM 969, a rural roadway between Bastrop and Austin.

Project Notes: Total land holdings in Bastrop County for Musk-tied entities now total approximately 2,000 acres, up from 700 acres earlier in the year.

BUYER: Elon Musk affiliates
