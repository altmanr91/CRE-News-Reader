Infinity, KB Realty Partners Bag 75 Kenmare Street’s Retail Condo for $12M

ACQUISITION

Commercial Observer — Mon, 20 Jul 2026 19:55:09 +0000

SUMMARY

A joint venture between Infinity Real Estate and KB Realty Partners has acquired the 10,000-square-foot retail condominium at 75 Kenmare Street in Manhattan's Nolita neighborhood for $11.75 million. The property, which is fully leased to a strong tenant mix, represents a strategic return to Infinity's core focus on Manhattan retail and expands KB Realty's presence in the area. The buyers secured acquisition financing from ConnectOne Bank for the transaction.
