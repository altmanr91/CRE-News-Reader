Northmarq Provides $68.8M Agency Loan for Refinancing of Texas Multifamily Portfolio

REFINANCE

REBusinessOnline — Wed, 07 Oct 2026 15:13:58 +0000

SUMMARY

Northmarq has provided a $68.8 million Freddie Mac loan for the refinancing of a three-property multifamily portfolio in Texas. The portfolio consists of 940 units across two properties in Houston, Holden Heights and Estancia San Miguel, and one property in San Antonio, The Montecristo. The fixed-rate loan was originated on behalf of Francis Property Management.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Units: 940

Loan Amount: $68.8M

Project Notes: The portfolio includes Holden Heights (282 units, built 2015), Estancia San Miguel (300 units, built 2006), and The Montecristo (358 units, built 2005).

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

LENDER: Northmarq — Taylor Francis; David Blum; Joe Giordani; Charlie Buckingham; Matt Radich

SPONSOR: Francis Property Management
