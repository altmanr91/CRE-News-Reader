Prism Partners Trades Two-Building Edison Lofts for $131M

SALE

Connect CRE — Tue, 18 Aug 2026 21:23:01 +0000

SUMMARY

Prism Partners and its capital partners have sold Edison Lofts, a 334-unit luxury multifamily community in West Orange, NJ, to Sym Investments for $131 million. The property, which includes nearly 19,000 square feet of retail space, attracted significant investor interest due to its blend of historic conversion and modern amenities, strong occupancy, and favorable tax pilot program. Cushman & Wakefield also arranged a $91.7 million acquisition loan from New York Life for the buyer.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Edison Lofts

Address: 33 Ashland Ave., West Orange, NJ

Units: 334

Sale Price: $131.0M

Loan Amount: $91.7M

Notable Features: The community includes a 300-unit conversion of the historic Thomas Edison Battery Building and a 34-unit ground-up residential building.

Project Notes: The acquisition loan features a full-term interest-only structure.
