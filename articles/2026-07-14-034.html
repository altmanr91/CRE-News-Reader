Blake Poston jumps from the gridiron to the high-rise with plans for a $100M Uptown Dallas apartment project

DEVELOPMENT

The Real Deal — Texas — Mon, 13 Jul 2026 17:35:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Cover2 Capital, led by Blake Poston, is developing The Savannah, a 20-story, 100-unit luxury apartment high-rise in Uptown Dallas. The $100 million project, located at 5220 McKinney Avenue, is currently in the design and city development review phases, with construction anticipated to begin in late 2027. The boutique community will feature larger-than-average units and three penthouse apartments, with rents expected to align with nearby luxury properties. Cover2 Capital is under contract to acquire the development site from Halebian Investments.
