Remembering RXR’s Lauren Maturo, Who Passed Away at Age 32

Commercial Observer — Thu, 29 Oct 2026 15:20:47 +0000

SUMMARY

Non-CRE / Obituary

Lauren Maturo, Vice President of Marketing at RXR and daughter of RXR President Mike Maturo, passed away at age 32 in a car accident on October 24. Colleagues and industry leaders remembered her as a bright, authentic, and inspiring individual who championed women in real estate and brought creativity and warmth to her work and relationships. She was deeply involved in RXR's marketing efforts, mentored younger professionals, and was pursuing a master's degree at NYU's Schack Institute of Real Estate.
