BKM Pays $103M for Orlando Industrial Portfolio

ACQUISITION

Connect CRE — Tue, 06 Oct 2026 13:43:18 +0000

SUMMARY

BKM Capital Partners and Kayne Anderson Real Estate have acquired a four-property, 543,000-square-foot light industrial portfolio in Central Orlando for $103 million. This acquisition expands BKM's Central Florida industrial holdings to over one million square feet across 13 properties. The portfolio, which is 95% occupied, will undergo a significant capital improvement plan to revitalize the assets.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Size: 543,000 SF

Sale Price: $103.0M

Occupancy at Sale: 95.0%

Notable Features: The portfolio comprises seven buildings and 22 units, offering clear heights of 16 to 28 feet and a 1:4,000-square-foot loading ratio.

Project Notes: Two properties will join BKM's Gridline Orlando portfolio, while the other two will operate as standalone assets. The portfolio has a 4.3-year weighted average lease term (WALT).

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: BKM Capital Partners — Brett Turner; Max Stone

BUYER: Kayne Anderson Real Estate

SELLER BROKER: Cushman & Wakefield — Rick Brugge
