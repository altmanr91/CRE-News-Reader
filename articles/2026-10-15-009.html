Lincoln Property Co., McCourt Partners Receive $132.7M Construction Loan for Culver City Multifamily Project

LOAN

REBusinessOnline — Thu, 15 Oct 2026 13:38:20 +0000

SUMMARY

Lincoln Property Co. and McCourt Partners have secured a $132.7 million construction loan from Bank OZK for ElevenOneEleven, a 344-unit multifamily development in Culver City. The project will include 52 affordable units, 2,000 square feet of retail, and extensive public outdoor space. Construction is expected to begin in the fourth quarter of 2026.
