Bridges Development lands retail-office building in Brooklyn for $31M

ACQUISITION

The Real Deal — New York — Thu, 16 Jul 2026 20:57:55 +0000

SUMMARY

Bridges Development Group has acquired the Kingswood Center, a three-story retail and office complex in Midwood, Brooklyn, for $31 million. The 226,130-square-foot property was sold by Midland Loan Services, a unit of PNC Bank, and represents a significant discount from its previous sale price six years ago. The buyer secured $35 million in financing to execute its business plan, which includes leasing the substantial vacant space within the building.
