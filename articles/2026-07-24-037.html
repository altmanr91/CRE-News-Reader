Invesco takes $100M-plus hit on sale of Houston’s Williams Tower

ACQUISITION

The Real Deal — Texas — Thu, 23 Jul 2026 18:30:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Williams Companies has acquired the 64-story Williams Tower in Houston, TX, for $300 million from Invesco Real Estate. The transaction, which includes a parking garage, represents a significant loss for Invesco, which purchased the property in 2013 for $412 million. The energy company, which previously occupied space in the tower, aims to accommodate future growth in the Houston market through this purchase. The tower, completed in 1983, maintains an 83 percent occupancy rate, with several major tenants expanding or relocating their headquarters there recently.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

The Galleria submarket recorded 200,000 square feet of positive absorption in 2025. While prestigious, highly-amenitized towers in Houston's downtown areas are performing well, other office properties are struggling, with some facing demolition or being purchased by large tenants at lower values.
