Texas Icon Raymond J. Kane: Structuring real estate to endure beyond the closing

REJournals — Mon, 27 Jul 2026 10:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Feature / Profile

This article profiles Raymond J. Kane, a founding director at Kane Russell Coleman Logan PC, highlighting his four-decade career in commercial real estate law. Kane is recognized for his expertise in structuring projects, relationships, and institutions to ensure their long-term endurance through market cycles and generational changes. His practice focuses on real estate, lending, corporate, and federal taxation matters, with a particular emphasis on leasing and construction across various commercial development phases. Kane's approach is rooted in principles learned from navigating past economic disruptions, emphasizing adherence to legal frameworks, fulfilling commitments, and nurturing professional relationships.
