Concrete mogul sells waterfront home in Miami’s Bay Point for record price to West Coast buyer

SALE

The Real Deal — Miami — Wed, 15 Jul 2026 19:34:25 +0000

SUMMARY

Luis Garcia, chairman and CEO of Adonel Concrete, sold his waterfront home in Miami's exclusive Bay Point neighborhood for a record-setting $27.3 million. The sale of the newly built 6,400-square-foot residence at 790 Lake Road reflects the dramatic appreciation in Miami's luxury housing market, particularly for bespoke waterfront properties, since the pandemic. The buyer, represented by Douglas Elliman, financed the purchase with an $18 million mortgage from JPMorgan Chase.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: single family

Address: 790 Lake Road, Miami, FL

Size: 6,400 SF

Sale Price: $27.3M

$/SF: $4,266/SF (calculated)

Notable Features: The six-bedroom, seven-and-a-half-bathroom house includes a nearly 1,800-square-foot rooftop terrace with panoramic views, an outdoor kitchen, pool, jacuzzi, private dock, chef's kitchen, and a 400-bottle wine cellar. Furnishings were included in the sale.

Project Notes: The property was purchased by the seller in 2012 for $2 million. The sale price of nearly $4,300 per square foot marks a record for canal-fronting homes in Bay Point and is the priciest sale in the neighborhood this year.
