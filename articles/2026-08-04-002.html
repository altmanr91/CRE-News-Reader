University of California Buys 16 Westwood Village Properties for $50M

ACQUISITION

Commercial Observer — Mon, 03 Aug 2026 21:00:09 +0000

SUMMARY

The University of California Regents has acquired a 16-property portfolio in Westwood Village, Los Angeles, for $49.8 million. The portfolio, totaling 181,855 square feet across 2.6 acres, includes retail, office, and open space, primarily along Westwood Boulevard. The acquisition from Anderson Real Estate expands the university's commercial holdings in the Westside, a challenging market for land assembly, and follows several other significant real estate investments by UCLA for expansion and new facilities.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: mixed-use

Size: 181,855 SF

Sale Price: $49.8M

Site Area: 2.6 acres

Notable Features: Portfolio includes the historic Kinross Cornerstone, built in 1930 and renovated.

Project Notes: No specific plan for the properties has been publicly announced.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: University of California Regents

SELLER: Anderson Real Estate
