Now is the time to dream out loud: Expanded Negro Leagues Baseball Museum set to bring new life to Kansas City’s famed 18th & Vine Jazz District

DEVELOPMENT

REJournals — Thu, 15 Oct 2026 09:49:00 +0000

SUMMARY

The Negro Leagues Baseball Museum is undertaking a $35 million expansion in Kansas City's 18th & Vine Jazz District, which will include a new 35,000-square-foot museum building and a 144-room Pennant Hotel by Marriott. The project aims to significantly increase exhibit space and provide ancillary event areas, with a target completion by 2029. This development is expected to revitalize the historic district, bringing increased commercial activity and celebrating the legacy of Black baseball, according to the museum's president.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: museum / hotel

Property Name: Negro Leagues Baseball Museum Expansion / Pennant Hotel

Address: 1824 The Paseo, Kansas City, MO

Size: 35,000 SF

Keys: 144

Total Project Cost: $35.0M

Completion: 2029

Notable Features: New museum campus will feature interactive exhibits and is located just north of the historic Paseo YMCA, where the National Negro League was founded.

Project Notes: The Negro Leagues Baseball Museum owns the Paseo YMCA building, which has been transformed into the Buck O’Neil Education and Research Center. Construction is expected to break ground in Q1 2027 and take 18-24 months.
