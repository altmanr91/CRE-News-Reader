BGO, Yukon Real Estate Partners Sell 291K-SF Kansas Cold Storage Facility

SALE

Connect CRE — Wed, 29 Jul 2026 21:19:53 +0000

SUMMARY

BGO and Yukon Real Estate Partners have sold the New Century Cold Storage facility, a newly constructed 291,000-square-foot cold storage warehouse in New Century, Kansas, to a client of MetLife Investment Management. The facility was purpose-built as a plant-attached, rail-served build-to-suit for CJ Logistics America, highlighting its strategic importance for food supply chain resilience.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Property Name: New Century Cold Storage

Address: 27302 W 159th St, New Century, KS*

Size: 291,000 SF

Notable Features: Plant-attached, rail-served facility with a direct rail spur to the building and access to the BNSF Railway transcontinental intermodal facility, located within the Johnson County Airport Commission’s New Century AirCenter industrial park.

Project Notes: Developed as a build-to-suit for CJ Logistics America.

* Address sourced separately, not in original article.
