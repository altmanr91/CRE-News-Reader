The Barry Company marketing 155,000-square-foot landmark office/industrial property in Menomonee

REJournals — Thu, 02 Jul 2026 10:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Property Listing / For Sale

The Barry Company has been selected to market a 155,000-square-foot office and industrial property in Menomonee Valley, Wisconsin, following the previous occupant's relocation to a larger headquarters. The landmark facility offers a combination of Class-A office space and high-clearance warehouse/production areas, with features suitable for single or multi-tenant occupancy.
