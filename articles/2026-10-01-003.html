Podcast: Why Industrial Leasing Is Broken & How to Fix It, Warehub CEO James Holbrook

Commercial Observer — Thu, 01 Oct 2026 14:43:27 +0000

SUMMARY

Non-CRE / Business News

This article features an interview with James Holbrook, CEO of Warehub, who argues that industrial leasing is currently inefficient and ripe for technological disruption. He highlights the surging demand for short-term warehouse space driven by tariffs, geopolitical uncertainty, and the need for supply chain flexibility. Holbrook explains how Warehub, an AI-enabled platform, aims to streamline the discovery, leasing, and activation of warehouse space, reducing transaction times from weeks to days. He also discusses the evolving role of brokers in an AI-driven market and emerging trends like the inland shift of logistics hubs and the increasing importance of power availability in site selection.
