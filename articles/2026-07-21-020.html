Jack Welch’s widow sells Upper East Side townhouse for $23M

SALE

The Real Deal — New York — Mon, 20 Jul 2026 17:46:07 +0000

SUMMARY

Celebrity Residential / Non-CRE

Suzy Welch, widow of former General Electric CEO Jack Welch, has sold her Upper East Side townhouse at 160 East 81st Street for $23 million. This transaction follows her recent acquisition of a Park Avenue condo for $24.2 million, as she downsizes her residential portfolio within the neighborhood. The property, which she purchased in 2021 for a similar price, features seven bedrooms, seven bathrooms, a private garden, and a garage.
