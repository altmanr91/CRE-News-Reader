Bath & Body Works to Open 9K-SF Store in Flatiron District

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Tue, 13 Oct 2026 20:52:41 +0000

SUMMARY

Bath & Body Works has signed a lease for a nearly 9,000-square-foot retail space at 129 Fifth Avenue in Manhattan's Flatiron District. The new store is expected to open in the fall of 2027, taking over a two-level retail unit previously occupied by another fashion retailer. This transaction marks a significant retail presence for the tenant in a prime New York City shopping corridor.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: retail

Address: 129 Fifth Avenue, New York City, NY

Size: 9,000 SF

Notable Features: Two-level retail unit in an eight-story building with 30 residential co-ops on upper floors.

Project Notes: The tenant previously sued a landlord at another New York City location in 2020 to exit a lease due to COVID-19 related closures.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE
