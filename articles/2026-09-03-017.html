LaSalle IM, Camber Pick Up Long Island Industrial Portfolio

ACQUISITION

Connect CRE — Wed, 02 Sep 2026 21:20:56 +0000

SUMMARY

A joint venture between LaSalle Investment Management and Camber Real Estate Partners has acquired a three-property industrial portfolio on Long Island for an undisclosed price. The portfolio, which is fully leased, includes facilities in the JFK Airport area and Suffolk County. The acquisition provides a strong balance of stable income and long-term asset strength in a supply-constrained market, according to Camber's managing principal.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Size: 270,730 SF

Occupancy at Sale: 100.0%

Notable Features: The portfolio includes a 46,000-square-foot facility in the JFK Airport area and two buildings of 100,230 square feet and 124,500 square feet in the South Shore area of Suffolk County.

Project Notes: This was an off-market transaction.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: LaSalle Investment Management

BUYER: Camber Real Estate Partners — Christopher Bellapianta, Managing Principal

SELLER BROKER: JLL Capital Markets
