Terreno Realty Acquires Red Hook Warehouse for $26M

ACQUISITION

Commercial Observer — Mon, 17 Aug 2026 20:27:53 +0000

SUMMARY

Terreno Realty has acquired a 28,000-square-foot industrial warehouse property in Red Hook, Brooklyn, for $26.3 million from LBA Properties. The property, located on the East River waterfront, includes parking for 70 cars and two loading bays, and is currently leased to a maritime goods and services provider. This acquisition follows strong leasing fundamentals in the industrial sector, with significant year-over-year increases in leasing activity and declining vacancies.
