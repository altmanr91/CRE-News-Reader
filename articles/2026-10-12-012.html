JLL Arranges Sale of 10-Story Office Building in Tysons

SALE

Connect CRE — Mon, 12 Oct 2026 17:59:01 +0000

SUMMARY

JLL has facilitated the sale of 8270 Greensboro Drive, a 10-story office building in Tysons, Virginia. Nuveen sold the 166,393-square-foot asset to Onward Investors. The property, which underwent a comprehensive renovation in 2021, is 96% leased to a diverse tenant base, reflecting Tysons' strong appeal for high-quality, well-located office space.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Address: 8270 Greensboro Drive, Tysons, VA

Size: 166,393 SF

Occupancy at Sale: 96.0%

Year Built: 1999

Notable Features: 10-story building comprehensively renovated in 2021.
