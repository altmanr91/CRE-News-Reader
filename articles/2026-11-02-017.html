Harrison Street Asset Management, Harbert Management Corporation Form Seniors Housing Partnership

DEVELOPMENT

Connect CRE — Mon, 02 Nov 2026 18:46:51 +0000

SUMMARY

Harrison Street Asset Management and Harbert Management Corporation have formed a joint venture to develop two senior housing communities, The Palmer in Pasadena, California, and The Belle Mare in Potomac, Maryland. These projects will add 80 and 94 units respectively, contributing to Harbert South Bay's active construction portfolio of over 800 units. PNC Bank is providing construction financing for both communities, which are designed to meet demand in attractive, supply-constrained markets.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

DEVELOPER/SPONSOR: Harrison Street Asset Management

DEVELOPER/SPONSOR: Harbert Management Corporation

LENDER: PNC Bank

DEVELOPER/SPONSOR: Momentum Senior Living
