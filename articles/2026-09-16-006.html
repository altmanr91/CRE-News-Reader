Marcus & Millichap Brokers Sale of 50,000 SF Industrial Complex in Northwest Houston

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Wed, 16 Sep 2026 14:07:58 +0000

SUMMARY

Marcus & Millichap has brokered the sale of The Workshops at Breen, a 50,000-square-foot industrial complex in northwest Houston. The property, developed in 2021, features eight buildings with 28 suites. Harbor Capital sold the asset to Harbor Hill.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Property Name: The Workshops at Breen

Address: 5801 Breen Drive, Houston, TX

Size: 50,000 SF

Year Built: 2021

Site Area: 3.8 acres

Notable Features: Shallow-bay property with 28 suites, 40 grade-level doors, and 18-foot clear heights.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

SELLER: Harbor Capital

SELLER BROKER: Marcus & Millichap — Grant Roosma; Adam Abushagur; Tyler Ranft
