Stockdale Capital Partners, Hamilton Lane Acquire Shoppes at Chino Hills in California for $157M

ACQUISITION

REBusinessOnline — Tue, 15 Sep 2026 13:31:57 +0000

SUMMARY

Stockdale Capital Partners, in partnership with funds managed by Hamilton Lane, has acquired The Shoppes at Chino Hills, an open-air lifestyle center in Chino Hills, California, for $157 million. The new ownership plans to rename the property The Shops at Chino Hills and implement a rebranding, remerchandising, and repositioning strategy. The center, originally developed in 2008, features a strong tenant mix including a popular grocery store and bookstore, serving a significant local customer base.
