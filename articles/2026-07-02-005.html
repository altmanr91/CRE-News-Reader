Miami’s Office Leasing Jumps Nearly 45% in Q2

Commercial Observer — Wed, 01 Jul 2026 16:15:06 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Office Report

Miami-Dade County's office market experienced a significant surge in leasing activity during Q2 2026, with a nearly 45% year-over-year increase, pushing average asking rents to a record high of $67.12 per square foot. This robust demand, driven by an influx of family offices, financial firms, and technology companies, has led to a tightening vacancy rate, which fell to 14.9%. Class A buildings, particularly new, amenity-rich properties, saw rents climb 6% year-over-year to $80.85 per square foot, reflecting strong competition for premium space.
