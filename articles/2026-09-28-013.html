Subtext opens 498-bed student housing community in Knoxville

DEVELOPMENT

REJournals — Mon, 28 Sep 2026 15:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Subtext has opened EVER Knoxville, a 14-story student housing community with 498 beds near the University of Tennessee. The development, a partnership with FrontRange Capital and TPG Real Estate Partners, features 136 units and a wide array of amenities focused on student wellness and academic success. This project marks an expansion of Subtext's student housing portfolio in Tennessee.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: student housing

Property Name: EVER Knoxville

Address: 1919 Lake Ave, Knoxville, TN

Size: 329,294 SF

Units: 136

Beds: 498

Notable Features: 14-story building with a sky lounge, resort-style pool courtyard, 24/7 fitness center, wellness suite, study lounges, lobby café, pet spa, dog run, and integrated parking garage with EV charging.
