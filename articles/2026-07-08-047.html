Nscale Secures $900M For Data Center Expansion

LOAN

Bisnow News Feed — Tue, 07 Jul 2026 14:11:40 -0400

SUMMARY

Nscale, an AI infrastructure firm, has secured a $900 million line of credit to accelerate the construction of its global data center portfolio across the U.S., Europe, and Asia. This financing will provide the necessary liquidity for the company's rapid expansion, reflecting strong investor confidence in its strategic direction and growth prospects within the burgeoning AI infrastructure market. The company's CEO highlighted that this facility enhances their flexibility to build the essential infrastructure required by major technology companies for AI development and scaling.
