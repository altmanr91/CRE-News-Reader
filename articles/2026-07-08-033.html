Toyota commits to San Antonio plant expansion with $4 billion plan

DEVELOPMENT

The Real Deal — Texas — Tue, 07 Jul 2026 16:30:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Toyota is investing $3.6 billion to significantly expand its manufacturing presence in San Antonio, TX, by 2030. The plan includes a second 2.5 million-square-foot vehicle assembly line for Tacoma pickup truck production, consolidating operations from a previous location. This expansion, supported by $178 million in local tax incentives and a $20 million state grant, is projected to generate a $3.2 billion economic impact during construction and create approximately 6,000 jobs.

Project Notes: The 2.5 million-square-foot facility will be built on Toyota's existing 2,000-acre campus. San Antonio City Council and Bexar County commissioners approved $178 million in tax incentives. The Texas Enterprise Fund provided a $20 million grant. Toyota is also planning a 32,000-square-foot office building at the campus, adding $18.2 million to its investment.
