Big Banks Take Bigger CRE Losses As They Clear Out Problem Loans

Bisnow Latest News Feed — Mon, 19 Oct 2026 19:10:32 -0400

SUMMARY

Market Research / Loan Performance

Major banks are actively working to reduce their exposure to troubled commercial real estate loans across various property sectors. This strategic effort is leading to a decline in both nonaccrual and nonperforming loan balances, indicating a concerted push to clear out problematic assets from their balance sheets.
