VanTrust Real Estate starts construction 121 Commerce Park in McKinney

CONSTRUCTION

REJournals — Thu, 13 Aug 2026 17:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

VanTrust Real Estate has commenced construction on the first phase of 121 Commerce Park, a new industrial development in McKinney, Texas. This initial phase will deliver over 512,000 square feet of Class A industrial space across two speculative buildings, designed for flexible single or multi-tenant use. The project is strategically located with excellent access to major highways and is expected to be completed in the second quarter of 2027, addressing the growing demand for high-quality industrial facilities in the rapidly expanding North Texas market.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Property Name: 121 Commerce Park

Address: 4820 Tina Dr, McKinney, TX*

Size: 512,000 SF

Completion: Q2 2027

Phase I: 512,000 SF across two buildings

Total Project (All Phases): Phase 2 will add 250,000 SF of build-to-suit industrial space

Site Area: 42 acres

Notable Features: Class A industrial space with flexible single- or multi-tenant configurations, located at the northwest corner of Tina Drive and SH-121 with direct access to major highways and proximity to McKinney National Airport.

Project Notes: VanTrust will extend Collin McKinney Parkway and construct a new trail connection to the regional Cottonwood Creek Trail as part of the project.
