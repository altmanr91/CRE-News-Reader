NYC Stops Construction On Another Office-To-Residential Conversion As Inspectors Descend On Job Sites

Bisnow News Feed — Mon, 27 Jul 2026 18:06:23 -0400

SUMMARY

Construction

New York City officials have issued a stop-work order at GFP Real Estate's office-to-residential conversion project at 222 Broadway in Lower Manhattan, following a contractor's failure to report cracks in newly installed beams. This incident is part of a broader citywide inspection initiative targeting office-to-residential conversions after a near-collapse at the former Pfizer headquarters in Midtown. The Wrey project, which is set to deliver 788 luxury rental apartments and 40,000 SF of commercial space, had already begun move-ins in June, and the developer anticipates no impact on the project's timeline.
