After 5 Years Of Gains, Commercial Real Estate's C-Suites Just Got Less Diverse

Bisnow Latest News Feed — Mon, 07 Sep 2026 19:13:23 -0400

SUMMARY

Market Research / Diversity Report

For the first time since 2020, the commercial real estate industry's C-suite diversity has declined, reversing a five-year trend of increasing representation. This shift indicates that despite previous gains, the industry is experiencing a setback in its efforts to foster a more diverse leadership landscape, with some advocates noting that professionals from underrepresented groups are departing C-suite roles.
