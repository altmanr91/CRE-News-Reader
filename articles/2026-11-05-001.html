Catal Capital Lends $38M on Margaritaville-Branded RV Resort in Georgia

LOAN

Commercial Observer — Thu, 05 Nov 2026 16:37:14 +0000

SUMMARY

A joint venture between South Street Partners and Brighton Real Estate Ventures secured $38 million in construction financing from Catal Capital for the development of a Margaritaville-branded RV resort in Brunswick, GA. The project, Camp Margaritaville Resort Golden Isles RV, will feature 341 spaces and is expected to be completed in early 2028. The lender noted the growing institutional maturity of outdoor hospitality, driven by demand for experience-driven stays, and expressed confidence in the sponsors' vision.

Notable Features: The resort will feature 56 casitas and 285 RV pads, along with amenities including a swimming pool, restaurant, bar, clubhouse with retail, fitness center, pickleball courts, dog park, music pavilion, and boathouse.
