Cushman & Wakefield Fortifies Balance Sheet With Strong Q3 Earnings

Commercial Observer — Wed, 04 Nov 2026 15:21:31 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Earnings Report

Cushman & Wakefield reported strong third-quarter 2026 earnings, with revenue reaching $2.8 billion and net income at $52.1 million, demonstrating growth across its leasing and capital markets segments. The firm has significantly strengthened its balance sheet by paying off $800 million in debt since 2024, leading to improved credit ratings and increased financial flexibility. Despite macroeconomic headwinds like rising interest rates and construction slowdowns, the company is adapting by expanding in-house services to meet client demand for cost savings.
