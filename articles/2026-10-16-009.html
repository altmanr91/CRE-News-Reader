National City Investment Group Breaks Ground on 120-Unit Apartment Community in California

CONSTRUCTION

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 16 Oct 2026 13:38:27 +0000

SUMMARY

The National City Investment Group has commenced construction on Market View, a 120-unit workforce apartment community located at 303 Highland Ave. in National City, CA. The development will transform a former parking lot into three residential buildings offering studio, one-, and two-bedroom layouts, with completion anticipated in early 2028. The project emphasizes community and wellness, featuring shared outdoor spaces, a rooftop deck, coworking facilities, and a wellness center.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Market View

Address: 303 Highland Ave, National City, CA

Units: 120

Completion: first-quarter 2028

Notable Features: Adjacent to Manolo Farmers Market, features shared rooftop deck, coworking area, and wellness center with outdoor yoga deck.

Project Notes: Transforms an underutilized parking lot.
