Northmarq negotiates sale of Robert Browning Apartments in Kansas City

ACQUISITION

REJournals — Tue, 03 Nov 2026 15:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Bird Enterprises LLC has acquired The Robert Browning Apartments, a 64-unit mid-rise multifamily community in Kansas City, Missouri, from Bellada LLC. Northmarq's Kansas City Investment Sales team brokered the transaction for the seller. The property's historic architecture and prime location near the Country Club Plaza's ongoing redevelopment made it an attractive investment.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: The Robert Browning Apartments

Address: 4809 Roanoke Pkwy, Kansas City, MO

Units: 64

Year Built: 1929

Notable Features: Renovated studio apartment homes with historic charm, modern amenities, natural light, and Juliet balconies; designed by Nelle E. Peters; part of the Poet's District.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: Bird Enterprises LLC

SELLER: Bellada LLC

SELLER BROKER: Northmarq — David Belpedio; Jeff Lamott; Casey Wilhm
