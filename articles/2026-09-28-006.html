Marcus & Millichap Brokers Sale of 214-Unit Apartment Complex in Killeen, Texas

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Mon, 28 Sep 2026 14:18:42 +0000

SUMMARY

Marcus & Millichap has facilitated the sale of Copper Mountain, a 214-unit apartment complex in Killeen, Texas. A California-based investor sold the property to a New York-based family office, which acquired it as part of a 1031 exchange.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Copper Mountain

Address: 2501 Bacon Ranch Rd, Killeen, TX*

Units: 214

Year Built: 1985

Site Area: 8.7 acres

Notable Features: Amenities include a pool, fitness center, business center, playground, basketball and volleyball courts, a pet park, and outdoor grilling and dining stations.

Project Notes: Buyer utilized a 1031 exchange for the acquisition.

* Address sourced separately, not in original article.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

SELLER BROKER: Marcus & Millichap — Alec Schenk; Nick Fluellen; Bard Hoover; Peter Flis; John McGregor

SELLER: California-based investor

BUYER: New York-based family office
