NAI Robert Lynn closes pair of industrial leases totaling 150,000 square feet in San Antonio

LEASE

REJournals — Wed, 19 Aug 2026 15:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

NAI Robert Lynn represented two tenants, M Padel and Socceroof, in securing a pair of industrial leases totaling 150,000 square feet in Northeast San Antonio. The two former distribution buildings will be converted into a combined sports and recreation destination, marking the first Texas location for both brands and a significant expansion of their offerings.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Address: 6558 E. Campus Drive, San Antonio, TX

Size: 150,000 SF

Notable Features: Two former distribution buildings to be converted into a sports and recreation destination offering tennis, padel, and indoor soccer.
