Elliman launches AI-powered data company, tech upgrade

The Real Deal — National — Wed, 08 Jul 2026 17:49:33 +0000

SUMMARY

Non-CRE / Business News

Douglas Elliman Inc. has launched Elius, a new AI-powered data company developed with Google Cloud, as part of a broader technology infrastructure revamp. The initiative aims to automate and streamline operations, leading to significant cost reductions and an anticipated headcount reduction. Elius will leverage Elliman's proprietary brokerage and new development data to create new products and services, which the firm plans to eventually market to external consumers. This move is seen as an effort to monetize data that has historically been exploited by third-party firms.
