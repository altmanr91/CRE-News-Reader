Citi Prices Largest Single Bank-Contributed, Multi-Only CMBS Conduit Deal Since GFC

LOAN

Commercial Observer — Thu, 16 Jul 2026 22:31:53 +0000

SUMMARY

Citigroup has priced the largest multifamily-only conduit CMBS transaction originated by a single bank since the Global Financial Crisis, totaling $816.9 million. The Citigroup Commercial Mortgage Trust 2026-MFAM1 comprises 27 five-year, interest-only loans on 27 multifamily properties, with all loans contributed by Citi Real Estate Funding. The deal's AAA bonds priced at swaps plus 80 basis points, tighter than a comparable multifamily CMBS transaction in May, reflecting strong market appetite for medium- to high-leverage loans on quality multifamily assets. The transaction offers geographic and sponsor diversification across core markets, including New York, Los Angeles, and Florida, appealing to bond buyers seeking diversification in a market dominated by single-asset, single-borrower deals.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

The market shows a strong appetite for medium- to high-leverage loans on high-quality multifamily product. The lending landscape is very competitive, evidenced by a noticeable drop in amortizing loans in agency pools. Multifamily is considered an 'eternal darling' of investment portfolios due to structural undersupply of housing in the U.S., providing a strong floor to valuations.
