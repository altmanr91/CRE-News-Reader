Mill Creek takes Bay Area’s Safeway-to-housing redevelopment craze to Marin County

The Real Deal — San Francisco — Thu, 02 Jul 2026 23:41:11 +0000

SUMMARY

Development

Mill Creek Residential has proposed to redevelop a Safeway grocery store site in downtown San Rafael, Marin County, into a 331-unit apartment building. The project, submitted under Senate Bill 330 for streamlined approval, would involve demolishing the existing grocery store to construct an eight-story, nearly 377,000-square-foot residential building. Unlike many other Safeway redevelopments in the Bay Area, this proposal does not include a replacement grocery store, which has raised concerns among local residents about the loss of an important community amenity, especially with thousands of new apartments planned or under construction in the downtown area.

SPONSOR PIPELINE

930 Irwin Street: 210-unit project in San Rafael

Trader Joe's in Oakland: Redevelopment project without a replacement grocery store

Safeway in San Mateo: Redevelopment project

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Downtown San Rafael has approximately 2,000 approved apartment units and 360 units under construction. The city has a state-mandated housing planning goal of 3,220 new units by 2031. Across the Bay Area, seven known Safeway redevelopment proposals could deliver a total of 4,623 housing units.
