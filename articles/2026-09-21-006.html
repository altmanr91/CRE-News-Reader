Tesseract Capital Sells Apartment Property in Modesto, California for $26.6M

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Mon, 21 Sep 2026 14:18:49 +0000

SUMMARY

Tesseract Capital Group has sold The Marc at 1600, a 100-unit apartment property in Modesto, California, for $26.6 million. The buyer was an undisclosed family office. The Mogharebi Group represented the seller in this transaction.
