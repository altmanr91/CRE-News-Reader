Bascom Buys 183-Unit Orange County, Calif., Complex for $53M

ACQUISITION

Commercial Observer — Wed, 22 Jul 2026 19:30:49 +0000

SUMMARY

The Bascom Group has acquired Castlewood Park Apartments, a 183-unit multifamily complex in Buena Park, CA, for $53.1 million. This acquisition expands Bascom's multifamily portfolio in the Western U.S., with plans to renovate apartment interiors, upgrade amenities, and enhance on-site management. The property represents a significant transaction in a supply-constrained Orange County submarket.

DATA POINTS

Project Notes: The property was renovated multiple times between 2010 and 2023. Bascom plans further interior renovations and amenity upgrades.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Buena Park is identified as one of the most supply-constrained submarkets in Orange County. This transaction is the largest multifamily sale in Buena Park and one of only eight multifamily sales over $50 million in north Orange County in the last 24 months.
