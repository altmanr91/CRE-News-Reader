Pearlmark Provides $81M for Apartment Project in Coral Gables

LOAN

Commercial Observer — Fri, 16 Oct 2026 20:25:44 +0000

SUMMARY

Empira Group has secured an $81 million construction loan from Pearlmark for the development of Aurelian, a 12-story, 160-unit multifamily project in Coral Gables, FL. The project, designed by Behar Font & Partners, will feature extensive amenities including a swimming pool, fitness center, coworking space, and a public park. Empira Group previously acquired the 1.2-acre site in 2025 for $18 million and recently broke ground on the development, which is slated for completion in 2028.
