Stream Realty Partners takes on leasing assignment for 11-property industrial portfolio across Chicago’s O’Hare submarket

REJournals — Fri, 04 Sep 2026 14:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Leasing Assignment

Stream Realty Partners has been selected to manage the leasing for an 11-property industrial portfolio in Chicago's O'Hare submarket. The portfolio encompasses approximately 1.1 million square feet across Franklin Park, Des Plaines, and Elk Grove Village. This assignment highlights the continued demand for well-located industrial assets in the O'Hare submarket, which benefits from its proximity to a major air cargo hub, extensive highway access, and a robust labor market, making it a strategic location for industrial users.
