Chicago’s The Goldie Initiative announces 34 new Goldie Scholars for 2026-2027 academic year

REJournals — Wed, 19 Aug 2026 09:38:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Association / Leadership News

The Goldie Initiative, a non-profit organization, has announced 34 new Goldie Scholars for the 2026-2027 academic year, bringing the total cohort to 45 women pursuing graduate degrees in commercial real estate. This year saw a record 145 applications, a 51% increase from the previous year, making it the most competitive selection cycle in the program's history. The expansion of the cohort was made possible by the support of charitable foundations and individual donors, including new partnerships with the Sue Ling Gin Foundation Trust and continued collaboration with the Debbie Frank Legacy Fund, CREW Chicago, and the Annexstad Family Foundation. The incoming scholars represent 23 universities nationwide, with several institutions participating for the first time, reflecting the initiative's growing reach and impact in identifying future female leaders in the industry.
