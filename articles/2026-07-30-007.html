Shorenstein Investment Advisers Acquires 273,000 SF Office Property in Plano

ACQUISITION

REBusinessOnline — Wed, 29 Jul 2026 15:10:31 +0000

SUMMARY

Shorenstein Investment Advisers has acquired The Tennyson, a 273,000-square-foot office property in Plano, Texas. The two-building complex, located in the Legacy submarket, was fully leased at the time of sale. The property was originally developed in 2012 as a build-to-suit and recently underwent renovations.
