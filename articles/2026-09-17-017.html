1031 Deadline Means Fast Closing on Lynwood Retail Center

ACQUISITION

Connect CRE — Wed, 16 Sep 2026 21:58:11 +0000

SUMMARY

A Los Angeles-based retail real estate investment firm has acquired Atlantic Crossing, a 39,146-square-foot retail center in Lynwood, for $15 million. The transaction was completed in 20 days to meet the buyer's 1031 exchange deadline, highlighting a disciplined due diligence process and efficient collaboration among all parties involved. The center is anchored by a national pharmacy chain and features a strong mix of necessity-based tenants.
