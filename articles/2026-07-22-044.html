Want To Weigh In On The Next Opportunity Zone Maps? You May Be Too Late

Bisnow News Feed — Tue, 21 Jul 2026 14:38:57 -0400

SUMMARY

Infrastructure Investment / Government Policy

The second iteration of the Opportunity Zone program, established by the One Big Beautiful Bill Act, is nearing its final mapping phase, with governors preparing to submit their proposed census tracts to the Treasury Department by September 28. This new version, OZ 2.0, aims to narrow the focus of tax incentives to lower-income and rural areas, reducing the number of eligible zones from 8,764 to an estimated 6,544. While many states have already completed their public input processes for nominations, the uneven rollout across states may disadvantage smaller communities lacking resources to propose their tracts. Experts emphasize the importance of state leaders actively educating and engaging rural areas to ensure their participation in the program.
