New York’s luxury new development market is on fire

The Real Deal — New York — Mon, 06 Jul 2026 10:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / New Development Report

New York City's luxury new development condominium market is experiencing a boom, with contracts signed for units priced at $10 million or more nearly doubling in Q2 compared to the previous year. This high-end segment now accounts for half of all new development deals in the city, setting a record for contracts signed this decade. Despite potential headwinds like a new pied-à-terre tax, high mortgage rates, and global economic concerns, the luxury market remains robust. Overall contract activity in Manhattan saw a slight decline, but average sale prices increased due to the shift towards luxury homes. Dwindling inventory, particularly for lower-priced units, is cited as a key factor limiting overall market activity, suggesting strong demand for new, well-priced condos.
