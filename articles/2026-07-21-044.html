How Merritt Properties' New CEO Plans To Deploy $750M Investment

LOAN

Bisnow News Feed — Mon, 20 Jul 2026 13:12:36 -0400

SUMMARY

Merritt Properties, an industrial developer, secured a $750 million capital injection from longtime partner Almanac Realty Investors and new investor Centerbridge Partners. This investment, which gives the investors a 35% stake in the firm, will primarily fund new construction of shallow-bay industrial spaces, a segment of the market characterized by strong demand and undersupply. The company plans to focus its expansion in the Southeast, targeting markets with robust business growth, and will prioritize projects designed for tenants seeking smaller footprints.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Loan Amount: $750.0M

Notable Features: Focus on shallow-bay industrial spaces, typically 90-100 feet deep, for tenants seeking 9,000 SF or less.

Project Notes: The initial investment was $500 million, with Centerbridge providing 73% and Almanac 27%. An additional $250 million is committed for future growth.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Shallow-bay industrial vacancy nationwide was 4.8% last quarter, compared to 6.9% for industrial overall. Overall industrial vacancy nationwide saw a 20-basis-point decline year-over-year in Q2. Vacancy for warehouses 750,000 SF and larger fell from 8.3% to 7.3% in the year leading up to Q2 2026.
