San Antonio closes on three key Project Marvel parcels for $30M

ACQUISITION

The Real Deal — Texas — Wed, 08 Jul 2026 20:36:14 +0000

SUMMARY

San Antonio has officially acquired three federal properties downtown for $30 million, marking a significant step in the development of Project Marvel, a proposed sports and entertainment district. The purchase, funded by the San Antonio Spurs, includes an office building and two parking lots, and is intended to jumpstart the planning phase for the district. Project Marvel is envisioned to include a new arena for the Spurs, an expanded convention center, mixed-use development, and a large hotel, with an estimated total cost between $1.2 billion and $1.5 billion.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Address: 727 East César Chávez Boulevard, San Antonio, TX

Sale Price: $30.0M

Project Notes: The purchase was funded by the San Antonio Spurs. The acquired parcels are part of the larger Project Marvel development, which is expected to cost between $1.2 billion and $1.5 billion. Bexar County voters approved $311 million in financing for the project in November 2025.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: City of San Antonio — Erik Walsh, City Manager

SELLER: General Services Administration

EQUITY/FINANCING: San Antonio Spurs

EQUITY/FINANCING: Bexar County
