Kraus-Anderson wraps construction of highway facility in Hudson

CONSTRUCTION

REJournals — Fri, 10 Jul 2026 16:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Kraus-Anderson has completed the $5.8 million expansion of the St. Croix County Highway Department-Hudson Shop, a 27,000-square-foot highway facility in Hudson, Wisconsin. The project, designed by Cedar Corporation, adds new office space, shop space, and a wash bay to the county's existing maintenance campus, enhancing highway operations and service capacity for the eastern portion of St. Croix County.
