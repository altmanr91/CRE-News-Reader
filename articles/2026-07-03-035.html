Cheers: Napa Valley’s former “Two-Buck Chuck” estate auctioned off for nearly 30% of initial list price

SALE

The Real Deal — San Francisco — Thu, 02 Jul 2026 21:02:50 +0000

SUMMARY

The former 42-acre Napa Valley estate of vintner Charles Shaw, known for the "Two-Buck Chuck" brand, was auctioned for nearly $10.2 million. The St. Helena property, which includes 29 acres of vineyards, a tasting room, and residential homes, sold for significantly less than its initial 2024 asking price of $35 million after multiple price reductions and a shift to auction marketing. The sale reflects a trend of buyers seeking deep discounts for vineyard properties in the region amid slowing home sales and declining wine consumption.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: vineyard

Property Name: Benessere Vineyards

Address: 1010 Big Tree Road, St. Helena, CA

Site Area: 42 acres

Notable Features: Includes 29 acres of vineyards, a public tasting room, and primary and guest homes with a combined eight bedrooms and nine bathrooms.

Project Notes: The property was originally listed for $35 million in 2024, then reduced to $28 million in 2025, and $19 million in 2026 before going to auction. It was previously owned by Charles Shaw, then by John and Ellen Benish who transformed it into Benessere Vineyards.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

SELLER: Benish family

SELLER BROKER: Sotheby's International Realty — Jamie Spratling; Kevin McDonald

SELLER BROKER: Concierge Auctions

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Home sales in Napa and Sonoma counties have slowed from pandemic-era highs, with vineyard estates proving particularly difficult to sell due to a niche buyer pool and declining wine consumption among Gen Z consumers.
