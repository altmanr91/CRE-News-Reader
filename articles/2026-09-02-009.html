Ram Realty Delivers 310-Unit Apartment Community in Durham

CONSTRUCTION

REBusinessOnline — Wed, 02 Sep 2026 12:21:47 +0000

SUMMARY

Ram Realty Advisors has completed Wye Junction, a 310-unit apartment community in Durham, NC. The project involved the adaptive reuse of the former Erwin Oil site, incorporating historical elements like repurposed oil tankers and train trestles into its design. The community also includes over 7,000 square feet of retail space and various amenities.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Wye Junction

Address: 701 Washington St, Durham, NC

Size: 7,000 SF

Units: 310

Site Area: 5.2 acres

Notable Features: Adaptive reuse of a former oil distribution facility, incorporating repurposed oil tankers and train trestles; includes over 7,000 square feet of retail space, a pedestrian plaza, and rooftop amenities.

Project Notes: Former Erwin Oil site dating back to 1937.
