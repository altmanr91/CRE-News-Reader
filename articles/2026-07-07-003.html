PGIM Sells Coral Gables Office Complex for $98M

ACQUISITION

Commercial Observer — Mon, 06 Jul 2026 20:25:48 +0000

SUMMARY

A partnership including Intalex, Itero, Greenwall Capital Management, and the family office of Carl DeSantis has acquired the 717,805-square-foot Ponce office complex in Coral Gables, FL, from PGIM for $97.8 million. The buyers secured a $105 million acquisition loan from Acore Capital and plan to invest approximately $30 million in renovations, with completion anticipated next year. The managing partner of Greenwall noted strong pre-leasing activity, indicating the project is ahead of schedule in its business plan.

Notable Features: The complex includes four structures: two office buildings and two parking garages. It was built between 1966 and 2004.

Project Notes: The partnership plans to spend about $30 million renovating the complex. Hines, the original developer and prior owner, will continue to manage the asset.
