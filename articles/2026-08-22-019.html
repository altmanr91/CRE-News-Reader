Grocery-Anchored Queens Retail Secures $32M Financing

LOAN

Connect CRE — Fri, 21 Aug 2026 21:27:44 +0000

SUMMARY

Northmarq's New York Metro Debt + Equity team secured $32 million in financing for Springnex Plaza, a fully occupied, grocery-anchored retail center in Queens. The loan was arranged on behalf of the owner, Mattone Group Springnex LLC, through Principal Financial Group. The property, developed in 2000, has maintained strong occupancy, which continues to attract significant lender interest for supermarket-anchored shopping centers in the current market.
