Faris Lee Brokers $37.3M Sale of 97,319 SF Retail Space in Lakewood, California

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Mon, 02 Nov 2026 17:12:51 +0000

SUMMARY

Faris Lee Investments has brokered the sale of a 97,319-square-foot retail portion of Lakewood Center in Lakewood, California. A consortium of multiple owners sold the asset for $37.3 million to an undisclosed buyer. This transaction marks the second significant portion of Lakewood Center sold by Faris Lee this year, bringing their total sales for the center to $76 million.
