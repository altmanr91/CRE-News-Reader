Berkadia negotiates sale of 155,000-square-foot hospital in Cincinnati market

SALE

REJournals — Thu, 08 Oct 2026 14:43:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Berkadia has brokered the sale of SUN Behavioral Health, a 155,000-square-foot behavioral health hospital in Erlanger, Kentucky, part of the Cincinnati metropolitan area. Woodbury Corporation sold the class-A facility, which was constructed in 2018 and is fully leased to a national provider of acute mental health and substance use disorder treatment. The property serves as a flagship campus for the operator and partners with a local healthcare system to provide integrated care.
