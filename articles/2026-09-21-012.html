BOMA study: Commercial real estate remains a boon to the U.S. economy

REJournals — Mon, 21 Sep 2026 09:51:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Economic Impact

A new study by the Building Owners and Managers Association International (BOMA) highlights the significant economic impact of commercial real estate across the U.S. The report, which covers 35.4 billion square feet of commercial property in 79 markets, found that the operation and maintenance of office, retail, and industrial assets generate $609.9 billion in annual economic output and support 3.9 million jobs. The study also notes that for every dollar spent on building operations, $2.22 in total economic output is generated, underscoring the broad network of businesses and workers supported by the sector. Additionally, the research points to the growing influence of data centers on office construction statistics and sustained high levels of industrial construction.

DATA POINTS

Annual economic output from CRE operations and maintenance: $609.9 billion

Jobs supported by CRE operations and maintenance: 3.9 million

Total commercial real estate square footage covered: 35.4 billion SF

Annual operating expenditures: $274.9 billion

Contribution to U.S. gross domestic product (GDP): $344.4 billion

Personal earnings generated: $219.4 billion

Economic output generated per $1 spent on building operations: $2.22

Data center share of private office construction in 2025: 46% ($41.2 billion out of >$90 billion)

Traditional office construction (excluding data centers) in 2025: $48.4 billion

Industrial construction (warehouse and manufacturing) in 2025: $274.2 billion

Retail construction in 2025: $47.1 billion
