Cross Harbor Scores $78.5M Financing for Tempe Rental Community

LOAN

Connect CRE — Thu, 05 Nov 2026 15:47:31 +0000

SUMMARY

CrossHarbor Capital Partners secured $78.5 million in construction take-out financing for The Alyssa, a 335-unit Class A multifamily community in Tempe, Arizona. The property, delivered in 2024, achieved stabilization within 18 months, demonstrating strong market performance. The financing was arranged by JLL's Capital Markets Debt Advisory team.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: The Alyssa

Address: 98 S River Dr, Tempe, AZ*

Units: 335

Loan Amount: $78.5M

Loan Per Unit: $234,328/unit (calculated)

Year Built: 2024

Notable Features: Class A community with studio, one-, two-, and three-bedroom floor plans; amenities include a swimming pool, outdoor lounge, Peloton-equipped fitness center, co-working lounge, and controlled-access parking garage with EV charging stations.

Project Notes: Achieved stabilization 18 months after delivery.
