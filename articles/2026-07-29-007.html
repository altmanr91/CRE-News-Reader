Core Spaces Breaks Ground on 1,656-Bed Student Housing Development Near University of Georgia

CONSTRUCTION

REBusinessOnline — Tue, 28 Jul 2026 14:36:58 +0000

SUMMARY

Core Spaces has commenced construction on Hub Athens Dougherty, a substantial 1,656-bed student housing development near the University of Georgia. The project, slated for completion in 2028, will feature 536 units and a range of amenities. This development includes significant public benefit initiatives in partnership with Athens-Clarke County, such as a public parking deck, retail space, infrastructure upgrades to support affordable housing, and the preservation of historic buildings.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: student housing

Property Name: Hub Athens Dougherty

Units: 536

Beds: 1,656

Completion: 2028

Notable Features: Includes 400-space public parking deck, 16,000 square feet of retail space, water/sewer infrastructure for affordable housing, enhanced streetscapes, preservation of historic Foundry building, relocation of historic Hoyt House, and a new public plaza.
