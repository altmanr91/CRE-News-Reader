CanTex Capital Buys 14,860 SF Warehouse in Haltom City, Texas

ACQUISITION

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 25 Sep 2026 14:52:11 +0000

SUMMARY

CanTex Capital has acquired a 14,860-square-foot warehouse in Haltom City, Texas, from Roadmaster Industries Inc. The investment firm intends to reposition the vacant 1978-built property for industrial outdoor storage use, capitalizing on the growing demand for such facilities in the market.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Address: 2504 Minnis Drive, Haltom City, TX

Size: 14,860 SF

Year Built: 1978

Notable Features: Vacant at the time of sale, slated for repositioning to industrial outdoor storage use.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: CanTex Capital

SELLER: Roadmaster Industries Inc.

BUYER BROKER: Bradford Commercial Real Estate Services — Luke Clardy
