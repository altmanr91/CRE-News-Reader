Tapas Concept Fat of the Land Joining Restaurant Collection at Centerview Office Complex

LEASE

Connect CRE — Tue, 30 Jun 2026 19:20:32 +0000

SUMMARY

EMMES Realty Services and Oaktree Capital Management are expanding the dining options at their Centerview office complex in Irvine, CA, with the addition of Fat of the Land, a tapas restaurant. This new ground-floor establishment aims to enhance the hospitality-focused environment, serving both office tenants and the broader community. The addition is part of a strategy to create a vibrant sense of place through diverse dining experiences.
