Realterm Buys 40-Acre Industrial Outdoor Storage Facility in Laredo, Texas

ACQUISITION

REBusinessOnline — Wed, 22 Jul 2026 15:12:07 +0000

SUMMARY

Realterm, an investment manager specializing in transit-oriented properties, has acquired a 40-acre industrial outdoor storage (IOS) facility in Laredo, Texas. The property includes 25.3 acres of concrete paving, a check-in station, an office, and a wash bay. At the time of sale, a portion of the facility was leased to a transit firm.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Address: 14610 Mines Road, Laredo, TX

Size: 32,050 SF

Site Area: 40 acres

Notable Features: Features 25.3 concrete-paved acres, a 22,250-square-foot check-in station, a 6,800-square-foot office, and a 3,000-square-foot wash bay.

Project Notes: A portion of the facility was leased to Heartland Express at the time of sale.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: Realterm

TENANT: Heartland Express

Heartland Express
