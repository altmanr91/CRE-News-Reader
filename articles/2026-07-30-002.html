Data Center Giant Equinix Ups Its 2026 Forecast Behind Robust Q2

Commercial Observer — Wed, 29 Jul 2026 23:15:23 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Earnings Report

Data center REIT Equinix significantly increased its 2026 financial forecasts following a robust second quarter, driven by strong demand for artificial intelligence workloads and accelerated data center construction. The company reported record second-quarter revenue of $2.63 billion and net income of $477 million, with Funds From Operations (FFO) rising to $854 million. Equinix's CEO noted that the revised outlook reflects a materially improved market opportunity over the past year, with the vast majority of large deals stemming from AI workloads. The company has 52 projects underway across 33 markets, with capital expenditures increasing to $1.58 billion, primarily for capacity expansion.
