Freehill Breaks Ground on 809,220 SF Build-to-Suit Industrial Project in Northwest Houston

CONSTRUCTION

REBusinessOnline — Tue, 03 Nov 2026 16:40:25 +0000

SUMMARY

Freehill Co. has commenced construction on an 809,220-square-foot industrial project in northwest Houston, representing Phase I of the larger Fairbanks Landing development. This initial phase is a build-to-suit facility for Power Electronics, a manufacturer of solar inverters and EV chargers, and is expected to be completed in the third quarter of next year. Phase II will add two speculative buildings totaling 834,720 square feet, slated for delivery in late 2027.
