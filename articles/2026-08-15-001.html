Nuveen Green Capital Closes $281M C-PACE Loan for Boston Condos

LOAN

Commercial Observer — Fri, 14 Aug 2026 20:25:36 +0000

SUMMARY

Millennium Partners has secured a $281 million C-PACE loan for energy efficiency upgrades at the 62-story Winthrop Center in Downtown Boston. Nuveen Green Capital originated the fixed-rate loan, which represents the largest C-PACE financing in the New England region to date. This C-PACE debt closed concurrently with a $575 million CMBS loan for the office and retail components, forming a combined $856 million debt package that retires a previous construction loan.

Project Notes: C-PACE loan secured under the PACE Massachusetts program. A $575 million CMBS loan was also secured for the office and retail portions. The combined debt package of $856 million takes out a prior construction loan from Cale Street originated in October 2020. The PACE Massachusetts program was updated in June 2026 by MassDevelopment and the Massachusetts Department of Energy Resources.
