JLL Negotiates Sale of 757,325 SF Industrial Park in North Houston

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Mon, 26 Oct 2026 14:28:20 +0000

SUMMARY

JLL has negotiated the sale of Park 845 Crossing, a 757,325-square-foot industrial park in North Houston. The property, comprising five buildings, was sold by an affiliate of Equus Capital Partners to INDUS Realty Trust. The industrial park is strategically located at the interchange of I-45 and Beltway 8.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Property Name: Park 845 Crossing

Address: 11710 North Fwy, Houston, TX*

Size: 757,325 SF

Completion: 2014

Site Area: 38.9 acres

Notable Features: Five buildings with rear-load and cross-dock configurations, 18- to 30-foot clear heights.

Project Notes: Built in phases between 2008 and 2014.

* Address sourced separately, not in original article.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

SELLER: Equus Capital Partners

BUYER: INDUS Realty Trust

SELLER BROKER: JLL — Trent Agnew; Charlie Strauss; Lance Young; Brooke Petzold; Max Myers
