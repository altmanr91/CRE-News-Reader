Brazilian soccer legend Kaká buys Bal Harbour condo

SALE

The Real Deal — Miami — Thu, 09 Jul 2026 17:49:52 +0000

SUMMARY

Celebrity Residential / Non-CRE

Brazilian soccer legend Kaká, whose full name is Ricardo Izecson dos Santos Leite, has purchased a luxury condominium unit at Oceana Bal Harbour in Bal Harbour, Florida. The 3,350-square-foot residence was acquired for $8.7 million from Gaston Siciliano, who had bought the unit in 2022 for $6.5 million. The transaction highlights South Florida's appeal to high-profile athletes, with other soccer stars also owning properties in the region.
