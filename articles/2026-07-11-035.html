Data Center Pipeline Slows As Canceled Projects Pile Up

Bisnow News Feed — Fri, 10 Jul 2026 14:04:25 -0400

SUMMARY

Market Research / Data Center Report

The pipeline for new data center projects is showing signs of moderation, despite continued high demand for AI capacity and record overall U.S. construction levels. While data center construction spending reached a new high of $50 billion in April, the number of planned projects dipped in June, contributing to an overall decline in commercial project planning. This slowdown is attributed to increased local opposition and regulatory hurdles, with several large-scale projects recently canceled or rejected in Virginia and North Carolina, and numerous jurisdictions across 14 states considering bans on new digital infrastructure developments.
