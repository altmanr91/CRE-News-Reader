Newmark Brokers Sale of 115,512 SF Shopping Center in West Austin

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Mon, 26 Oct 2026 14:26:09 +0000

SUMMARY

Newmark has brokered the sale of Oak Hill Plaza, a 115,512-square-foot shopping center in West Austin. The property, situated on an 11.3-acre site, was 92.5 percent leased at the time of sale. Partners Real Estate, which had acquired the property in 2021, sold the asset to a joint venture between Trademark Property Co. and Cohen & Steers.
