Harborcrest Industrial Signs 74,421 SF Lease in East Dallas

LEASE

REBusinessOnline — Wed, 04 Nov 2026 16:22:58 +0000

SUMMARY

Harborcrest Industrial has signed a 74,421-square-foot lease for a single-tenant industrial building in East Dallas. The property, owned by Stonelake Capital Partners, offers significant clear heights and multiple loading doors, making it suitable for industrial operations. Brokers from Lee & Associates and Mercer Co. facilitated the transaction.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Address: 8721 Forney Road, Dallas, TX

Size: 74,421 SF

Year Built: 2001

Site Area: 5.8 acres

Notable Features: Features 20-foot clear heights, 18 dock-high loading doors, and two grade-level loading doors.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

TENANT: Harborcrest Industrial

LANDLORD: Stonelake Capital Partners

LEASING AGENT: Lee & Associates — Taylor Stell; Brett Lewis

TENANT REP: Mercer Co. — Thomas Mercer
