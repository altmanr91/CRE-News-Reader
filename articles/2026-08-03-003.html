Invesco Real Estate’s Chase Bolding Talks Market Opportunity

Commercial Observer — Mon, 03 Aug 2026 10:30:47 +0000

SUMMARY

Feature / Profile

Chase Bolding, CIO and Head of North American Real Estate at Invesco Real Estate, discusses his career trajectory and investment philosophy, emphasizing his expertise in distressed assets. He highlights his experience navigating the Global Financial Crisis (GFC) and its impact on his risk-based mindset, which he applies across Invesco's institutional and private wealth portfolios. Bolding details Invesco's post-GFC strategy, which involved acquiring loan portfolios across all commercial real estate sectors in the U.S. and later in Europe, leveraging the optionality of credit investments to either modify loans or pursue remedies to acquire real estate.
