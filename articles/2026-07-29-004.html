U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs Takes 5K SF at 1733 Eastchester Road in the Bronx

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Tue, 28 Jul 2026 19:54:44 +0000

SUMMARY

Simone Development Companies has achieved full occupancy at its 27,275-square-foot medical and office building at 1733 Eastchester Road in the Bronx, following two recent lease agreements. The U.S. General Services Administration (GSA) signed a long-term, 5,234-square-foot lease on behalf of the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs for a new Bronx Vet Center. Additionally, Downtown Brooklyn Physical Therapy leased 2,766 square feet for its first Bronx location. The full lease-up underscores the increasing demand for high-quality outpatient medical and social services space in the Bronx, according to the landlord.
