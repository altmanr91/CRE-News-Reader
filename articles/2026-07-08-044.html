Prince William County Board Rejects Plan For 1,940-Acre Data Center Hub

Bisnow News Feed — Tue, 07 Jul 2026 09:31:09 -0400

SUMMARY

Development / Regulatory Decision

The Prince William County Board of Supervisors unanimously rejected a comprehensive plan amendment for the proposed Dulles South Innovation Center, a 1,940-acre data center hub in Gainesville. The decision prevents the rezoning of land from agriculture, forestry, and mixed-use to industrial, which would have allowed for up to 42M SF of data center space. This rejection follows significant public debate over environmental concerns, infrastructure strain, and the preservation of the area's rural character, marking another setback for large-scale data center development in the county.
