Pistola Denim Takes Nearly 18K SF at ROW DTLA

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Thu, 24 Sep 2026 21:45:18 +0000

SUMMARY

Pistola Denim, a Los Angeles-based womenswear company, has secured a 17,922-square-foot office lease at ROW DTLA, a nearly 2 million-square-foot complex in Downtown Los Angeles. The move will relocate the brand's creative team from its current warehouse and distribution space in Vernon, further solidifying ROW DTLA's reputation as a hub for fashion companies due to its proximity to the city's Fashion District.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Address: 777 S Alameda St, Los Angeles, CA*

Notable Features: Seven former industrial buildings redeveloped and reopened in 2017, located on the edge of the Arts District, and includes over 35 retailers, restaurants, wellness operators, and event spaces.
