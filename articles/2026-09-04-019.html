Colliers Completes 67K-SF Industrial Lease in Maryland

LEASE

Connect CRE — Thu, 03 Sep 2026 21:06:47 +0000

SUMMARY

Colliers recently completed a 67,040-square-foot industrial lease at 4780 Winchester Boulevard in Frederick, Maryland. The landlord, Link Logistics, secured Senneca Holdings, Inc., a manufacturer of data center containment solutions, as the new tenant. The property's strategic location within a key Mid-Atlantic industrial corridor offers excellent connectivity to major East Coast population centers, which was a significant factor in attracting the new tenant.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Address: 4780 Winchester Boulevard, Frederick, MD

Size: 67,040 SF

Notable Features: Located within a strategically positioned industrial corridor with immediate access to Route 15, I-70, and I-270, providing connectivity to Washington, D.C., Baltimore, and Northern Virginia.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

TENANT: Senneca Holdings, Inc.
