Walker & Dunlop Arranges Refi for BellWorks Fort Monmouth Campus

REFINANCE

Connect CRE — Wed, 15 Jul 2026 20:41:36 +0000

SUMMARY

Walker & Dunlop arranged a $60 million bridge loan to refinance Bell Works Fort Monmouth, a mixed-use campus in Tinton Falls, NJ. The financing was secured on behalf of a joint venture between Inspired by Somerset and H.I.G. Realty Partners, with Hudson Bay Capital providing the loan. The campus, previously a corporate headquarters, has been redeveloped into a vibrant mixed-use destination with strong leasing momentum, reflecting its long-term value creation.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: mixed-use

Property Name: Bell Works Fort Monmouth

Address: 1 CommVault Way, Tinton Falls, NJ*

Loan Amount: $60.0M

Notable Features: Redeveloped into a mixed-use destination featuring flexible office spaces, retail, dining, coworking spaces, and community-focused amenities.

Project Notes: Previously home to the Commvault headquarters. Developed using Inspired by Somerset Development’s “Metroburb” model.
