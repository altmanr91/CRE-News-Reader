TruAmerica Pays $120M for Perimeter Center Rental Community

ACQUISITION

Connect CRE — Tue, 03 Nov 2026 14:34:33 +0000

SUMMARY

TruAmerica Multifamily has acquired Bricks at Perimeter Center, a 446-unit garden-style apartment community in Atlanta's Perimeter Center submarket, for $120 million from Clark Ventures. The new owner plans to implement a premium finish package across nearly all units, including quartz countertops, stainless steel appliances, and updated flooring and cabinetry. This acquisition expands TruAmerica's Atlanta portfolio and contributes to the firm's significant acquisition volume for the year.
