Hunt Midwest breaks ground on 227-unit luxury multifamily development in Overland Park

DEVELOPMENT

REJournals — Fri, 09 Oct 2026 16:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Hunt Midwest has broken ground on Canvas, a 227-unit luxury multifamily development in downtown Overland Park, Kansas. The project will feature one- and two-bedroom residences, over 5,000 square feet of ground-floor retail and restaurant space, and a range of luxury amenities. This infill development aims to integrate the Metcalf corridor with the downtown core, enhancing the pedestrian-focused area and contributing to the city's ongoing revitalization.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Canvas

Address: 8036 Metcalf Avenue, Overland Park, KS

Size: 5,000 SF

Units: 227

Completion: early 2029

Notable Features: Luxury amenities include a pool with grill stations, covered dog park, fitness center with yoga studio and sauna, coworking space, clubhouse, game room, and a four seasons garden room. The project also includes 49 public parking spaces.

Project Notes: The project is a public-private partnership with Overland Park, with Hunt Midwest contributing $5,000 annually to support the Floyd Street Apartments for individuals with disabilities. The development redevelops a vacant lot.
