Slatt Capital Arranges Acquisition Financing for Home2 Suites in Arkansas

LOAN

Connect CRE — Wed, 01 Jul 2026 21:15:27 +0000

SUMMARY

Slatt Capital successfully arranged a $9.49 million acquisition loan for the Home2 Suites, a 90-room hospitality property in Fort Smith, Arkansas. The financing, provided by Chambers Bank, features a three-year term with a 25-year amortization schedule and an 18-month interest-only period. The transaction highlights the property's strong performance, brand affiliation, and strategic location within the Fort Smith market, enabling the buyer to acquire this stabilized asset.
