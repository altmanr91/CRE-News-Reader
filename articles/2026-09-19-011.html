Berkadia closes sale of two multifamily properties in Houston’s Midtown neighborhood

SALE

REJournals — Fri, 18 Sep 2026 17:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Berkadia has brokered the sale of two multifamily properties, 220 and 230 W. Alabama, located in Houston's Midtown neighborhood. The 131-unit portfolio was sold by an affiliate of Realty Center Management, Inc. to Houston-based Fat Property. The transaction underscores the continued demand for urban core multifamily assets in Houston.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: 220 & 230 West Alabama

Address: 220 W. Alabama, Houston, TX

Units: 131

Year Built: 1960

Notable Features: Located in Midtown Houston, a vibrant and walkable urban district with convenient access to Downtown Houston and nearby amenities.

Project Notes: 230 W. Alabama was built in 1965.
