Alexander’s Signs Target to 15-Year Lease in Rego Park

LEASE

Connect CRE — Tue, 30 Jun 2026 19:39:57 +0000

SUMMARY

Alexander's Inc. has secured a 15-year lease with Target Corporation for 135,000 square feet at its Rego Park Shopping Center. This significant lease brings the multi-level, open-air shopping center to 99% occupancy, reinforcing its strong tenant mix and long-term stability. The transaction follows Alexander's recent sale of an adjacent property and a $175 million refinancing of the shopping center.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: retail

Property Name: Rego Park Shopping Center

Size: 135,000 SF

Current Occupancy: 99.0%

Notable Features: Multi-level, 600,000-square foot open-air shopping center.

Project Notes: The center has a weighted average lease term of approximately 9.3 years. The Target lease includes renewal options.
