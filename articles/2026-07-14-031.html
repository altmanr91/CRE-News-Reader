Mayor Johnson makes fresh bid to reassert power over the Chicago Housing Authority

The Real Deal — Chicago — Mon, 13 Jul 2026 18:48:24 +0000

SUMMARY

Non-CRE / Political & Crime News

Chicago Mayor Brandon Johnson is attempting to appoint three new board members to the Chicago Housing Authority (CHA) amidst an ongoing power struggle with the agency's former interim leader. The mayor's nominations, which include a former tenants' rights advocate, a former housing developer CEO, and an architect, aim to replace existing board members. This move is part of a broader effort by Johnson to assert control over the CHA, following disputes over board appointments and the current CEO's controversial selection.
