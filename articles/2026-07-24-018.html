Housing Notes: The Great Wealth Transfer is going to be underwhelming

The Real Deal — National — Thu, 23 Jul 2026 19:30:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

This article argues that the anticipated "Great Wealth Transfer" from Baby Boomers to their heirs will be underwhelming and will likely exacerbate the wealth gap rather than enrich the middle class. The author supports this by citing data indicating that only a small percentage of Americans receive substantial inheritances, with the vast majority receiving little to none. The piece also discusses the long-term trend of increasing unit sizes in Manhattan's co-op and condo market, driven by rising land values and developer economics, and links this to declining birth rates which may soften demand for larger homes in the future.
