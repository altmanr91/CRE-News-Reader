Walmart to Anchor 40-Acre Retail Development in St. Cloud, Florida

DEVELOPMENT

REBusinessOnline — Thu, 17 Sep 2026 13:31:38 +0000

SUMMARY

Walmart is set to anchor a new 40-acre retail development in St. Cloud, Florida, an Orlando suburb. The project, being developed by The Ferber Co., will feature a 187,000-square-foot Walmart Supercenter, a Walmart convenience store with a gas station, and seven to 10 additional outparcels. Site work and utility installation are currently underway, with the first tenants anticipated to open by early 2028.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: retail

Size: 187,000 SF

Site Area: 40 acres

Notable Features: Anchored by a Walmart Supercenter and includes a Walmart convenience store and gas station.

Project Notes: The project will include seven to 10 outparcels. First tenants are expected to open by early 2028.
