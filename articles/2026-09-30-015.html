Trammell Crow-Led Team Starts Work on Charlotte Warehouse Project

CONSTRUCTION

Connect CRE — Wed, 30 Sep 2026 14:16:18 +0000

SUMMARY

Trammell Crow Company, in partnership with a consortium of investors led by CBRE Japan and Mitsubishi Logistics Corporation, has broken ground on Caldwell Commerce Center, a two-building speculative industrial development in Charlotte, NC. The project will deliver 233,335 square feet of warehouse space by July 2027, featuring 32-foot clear heights and build-to-suit office options. Its strategic infill location provides excellent access to major interstates and a robust labor pool, making it attractive for future tenants.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Property Name: Caldwell Commerce Center

Address: 6122 & 6125 Caldwell Park Drive, Charlotte, NC

Size: 233,335 SF

Completion: July 2027

Site Area: 24 acres

Notable Features: Two rear-load buildings with 32-foot clear heights and build-to-suit office space, offering quick access to I-485 and I-85.
