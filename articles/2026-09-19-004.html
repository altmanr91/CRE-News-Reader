Corebridge Financial Refis Meatpacking Office Property With $293M Loan

REFINANCE

Commercial Observer — Fri, 18 Sep 2026 18:03:46 +0000

SUMMARY

A joint venture between Aurora Capital Associates and William Gottlieb Real Estate has secured a $293 million fixed-rate loan from Corebridge Financial to refinance their mixed-use property at 40 10th Avenue in Manhattan's Meatpacking District. The 10-story building, completed in 2019, features a fully leased office component and a strong retail presence, reflecting sustained demand for high-quality, well-located workplaces in New York City's supply-constrained Meatpacking District. Walker & Dunlop negotiated the financing.
