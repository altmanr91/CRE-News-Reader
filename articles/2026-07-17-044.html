How One Firm Is Leaning Into A Growing Secondaries Market

Bisnow News Feed — Thu, 16 Jul 2026 11:47:16 -0400

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

Partners Group, a UK-based firm, is actively leveraging the growing real estate secondaries market, having invested $6 billion across over 120 transactions since 2008. The firm is currently targeting a $1.5 billion fundraise for its fifth real estate secondaries program, which has already secured over $650 million in commitments. The secondaries market has evolved from a simple liquidity tool to a more strategic mechanism for managers to execute business plans and for investors to rebalance portfolios, especially in a market characterized by reduced distributions and slower fundraising cycles. Partners Group focuses on thematic investments, primarily targeting living and industrial asset classes, while largely avoiding office properties.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Global real estate secondaries transaction volume reached $25.1 billion in 2025, a 3% increase from $24.3 billion in 2024. GP-led secondaries constituted 64% of this volume at $16.1 billion, while LP-led secondaries totaled $8.8 billion, up 15% from the previous year. Industrial transactions led the market at $6.7 billion in 2025, followed by multifamily at $5 billion. Other living sectors, including student and senior housing, saw significant growth in transaction volumes, increasing by 67% and 92% respectively.
