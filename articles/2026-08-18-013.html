Marcus & Millichap brokers sale of 7-11 property in Cleburne

SALE

REJournals — Mon, 17 Aug 2026 16:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Marcus & Millichap has brokered the sale of a 4,088-square-foot net-leased 7-Eleven property in Cleburne, TX. The property, built in 2021, is situated on 1.12 acres and is secured by a lease with nearly 10 years remaining. The asset is noted for its strong performance, ranking among the top 2.5% of 7-Eleven locations nationwide and the top 2% in Texas.
