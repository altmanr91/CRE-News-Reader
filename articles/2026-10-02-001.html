Stockbridge Buys Maryland Industrial Property for $57M

ACQUISITION

Commercial Observer — Thu, 01 Oct 2026 22:00:39 +0000

SUMMARY

Stockbridge has acquired a 203,000-square-foot Class A industrial property in Upper Marlboro, MD, for $56.6 million. The facility, which was built in 2008, is fully leased to a mechanical contractor serving the data center industry. The transaction highlights the strong demand for industrial space in the Greater Washington market, driven by the expanding data center sector.

Project Notes: Seller TA Realty previously acquired the asset in 2022 for $56.2 million from an affiliate of Sonepar.
