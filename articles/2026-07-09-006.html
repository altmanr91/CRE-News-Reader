MG Properties Buys 458-Unit Apartment Community in Richardson, Texas

ACQUISITION

REBusinessOnline — Wed, 08 Jul 2026 15:25:26 +0000

SUMMARY

MG Properties has acquired Reserve at Spring Creek, a 458-unit apartment community in Richardson, Texas. Built in 2019, the property offers a variety of unit sizes and a comprehensive amenity package. Institutional Property Advisors (IPA) represented the seller, an affiliate of Lone Star Funds, and also arranged the acquisition financing for the buyer.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Reserve at Spring Creek

Address: 2710 Routh Creek Pkwy, Richardson, TX*

Units: 458

Year Built: 2019

Site Area: 17 acres

Notable Features: Amenities include a pool, fitness center, outdoor grilling and dining stations, coworking lounge, coffee bar, and a social lounge.

* Address sourced separately, not in original article.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE
