Woods Partners Building 372-Unit Charlotte Rental Community

DEVELOPMENT

Connect CRE — Wed, 28 Oct 2026 14:24:52 +0000

SUMMARY

Wood Partners and Sarofim Realty Advisors are developing Village Park, a 372-unit multifamily community in Charlotte, NC, with financing provided by J.P. Morgan. The project, located in the Highland Creek and Prosperity Church area, is expected to open in May 2028. This development marks Wood Partners' third community in the immediate vicinity, building on their previous work in the area.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Village Park

Units: 372

Completion: May 2028

Notable Features: Includes one-, two-, and three-bedroom apartments, with six carriage-house units. Five percent of units are designated as affordable housing for households earning up to 80% of the area median income. Amenities include a fitness center, coworking spaces, a coffee bar, a clubroom, a pool with cabanas, a dog park and spa, a fire pit, and grilling areas.

Project Notes: Located next to the previously developed Prosperity Village community and a Publix grocery store.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

DEVELOPER/SPONSOR: Wood Partners

DEVELOPER/SPONSOR: Sarofim Realty Advisors

LENDER: J.P. Morgan

SPONSOR PIPELINE

Prosperity Village: Previously developed community in the Prosperity Church area.
