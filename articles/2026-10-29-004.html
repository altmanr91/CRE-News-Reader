Robely, U.S. Legwork Each Ink 5K-SF Deals at Rosen’s 37 West 37th Street

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Thu, 29 Oct 2026 13:17:11 +0000

SUMMARY

Two wholesale distributors, U.S. Legwork and Robely, have each signed full-floor leases for 5,119 square feet at 37 West 37th Street in Midtown South. Both tenants committed to 10-year, 10-month terms, with U.S. Legwork taking the fourth floor and Robely occupying the fifth. The transactions highlight the building's appeal for businesses seeking well-located office space in Manhattan.
