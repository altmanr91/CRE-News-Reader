BlackRock, MGX investing $5B into Aligned Data Centers to supercharge growth

ACQUISITION

The Real Deal — Texas — Tue, 21 Jul 2026 21:56:23 +0000

SUMMARY

A joint venture between BlackRock's Global Infrastructure Partners and Abu Dhabi-based MGX is investing $5 billion into Aligned Data Centers, a Plano-based firm they recently acquired from Macquarie Asset Management for $40 billion. This significant capital injection is intended to fuel Aligned's expansion of its data center capacity across the Americas, including key markets like Dallas and Atlanta. The acquisition and subsequent investment highlight the intense demand for data center infrastructure driven by the power requirements of generative AI and other artificial intelligence technologies, prompting a race among global tech companies, sovereign wealth funds, and investment firms to expand or acquire existing data center properties.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

The power requirements of generative AI and other artificial intelligence ventures are rapidly increasing the valuations of existing data center properties. Communities are concerned about rising electric bills and environmental impacts like wastewater usage due to data center developments. In Texas, there are concerns about the Electric Reliability Council of Texas (ERCOT) grid capacity, leading Governor Greg Abbott to call for a ban on data center developments in rural areas and recommend statewide regulations.
