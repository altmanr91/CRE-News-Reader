AI-Assisted Construction-Bid Startup Fields $4M Seed Investment

Bisnow News Feed — Tue, 21 Jul 2026 15:33:04 -0400

SUMMARY

Non-CRE / Business News

Guthrie AI, a Philadelphia-based startup leveraging AI and a global workforce to streamline construction bidding, has successfully closed a $4 million seed funding round. The company's platform, which is currently 10% automated, aims to help specialty contractors increase their bidding capacity by up to 70% per person. The investment will primarily be used to expand its engineering team in the U.S. and further develop its proprietary AI platform, which currently relies on human input for complex tasks like interpreting construction documents.
