Arc Capital Lands $29M Refi for Chapman Market in L.A.’s Koreatown

REFINANCE

Commercial Observer — Fri, 11 Sep 2026 20:15:40 +0000

SUMMARY

Arc Capital Partners has secured a $29.3 million refinancing loan for Chapman Market, a historic retail and dining property in Los Angeles' Koreatown. Voya Investment Management provided the financing for the 41,241-square-foot asset, which is a prominent multi-tenant center known for its vibrant nightlife and Korean food concepts. The property, originally built in 1928, was designated a Los Angeles Historic-Cultural Monument and was acquired by Arc Capital a decade ago.
