Chinese Camera Company Insta360 to Open Store in Times Square

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Wed, 08 Jul 2026 19:11:26 +0000

SUMMARY

Insta360, a China-based global optics and camera company, has secured its first U.S. retail lease for a new store at 1515 Broadway in Manhattan's Times Square. The company plans to open the 1,100-square-foot shop in late summer, offering immersive demo zones and product education. The two-year lease, with an asking rent of $1,000 per square foot, was structured for a shorter term at the landlord's request to maintain flexibility for potential future redevelopment plans for the property.
