Construction work starts on 118-unit Upton apartment project in Clayton

CONSTRUCTION

REJournals — Fri, 16 Oct 2026 16:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Construction has commenced on The Upton, a $55 million adaptive reuse project transforming the former 7UP world headquarters in Clayton, MO, into a 118-unit Class A residential development. Led by Revive Capital Development, with KAI Build as general contractor and HDA as architect of record, the project will preserve the Mid-Century Modern building's historic exterior while creating modern studio, one-bedroom, and two-bedroom apartments. The development aims to revitalize a long-vacant landmark and introduce extensive amenities to downtown Clayton.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: The Upton

Address: 121 S. Meramec Avenue, Clayton, MO

Units: 118

Total Project Cost: $55.0M

$/Unit: $466,102/unit (calculated)

Year Built: 1964

Notable Features: Historic rehabilitation of the 11-story Mid-Century Modern former 7UP world headquarters, featuring an elevated outdoor plaza, fitness center, club room, heated outdoor spa, theater, game room, private speakeasy, outdoor kitchens, fire pits, and a private dog park.

Project Notes: The project represents a significant shift from earlier redevelopment concepts that called for replacing the existing building with a new high-rise, prioritizing historic preservation and adaptive reuse instead.
