Late German prince’s Palm Beach home trades for $30M

SALE

The Real Deal — Miami — Mon, 13 Jul 2026 22:26:59 +0000

SUMMARY

The Palm Beach estate of the late German Prince Albrecht zu Oettingen-Spielberg has sold for $30.3 million. The 4,700-square-foot home, situated on a 1.2-acre lot with 100 feet of beach frontage, was marketed as a renovation opportunity or a tear-down. The buyer, an LLC represented by attorney Leonard Ackerman, acquired the property which had been owned by the prince since 1987.
