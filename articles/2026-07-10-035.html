Digging into the Labor Outlook and What it Means for CRE

Connect CRE — Fri, 10 Jul 2026 05:01:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Labor Report

A recent Marcus & Millichap brief highlights the volatility and unreliability of preliminary Bureau of Labor Statistics employment numbers, noting significant downward revisions to prior months' job gains. The report suggests that a softer hiring environment and a thinner labor pool could lead to increased focus on productivity, influencing commercial real estate demand. This trend is expected to favor renewals and higher-quality spaces in the office sector, while necessity-based retail leasing may see stronger performance compared to large-ticket item retail. Additionally, stronger pay gains for job switchers could support in-migration, household formation, and apartment demand, particularly in areas close to employment centers.

DATA POINTS

June preliminary job additions: 57,000

April and May job revisions: downward by 74,000

Average monthly job gains (past six months): 92,000
