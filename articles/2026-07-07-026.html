Chicago homes are flying off the market as buyers bid above ask

The Real Deal — Chicago — Mon, 06 Jul 2026 19:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Residential Sales

The Chicago-area residential market is experiencing exceptionally fast home sales, with properties spending an average of 27 days on the market in May, significantly quicker than the national average of 52 days. This rapid pace is driven by a severe lack of inventory, leading to multiple aggressive offers and buyers frequently paying above the asking price. Real estate agents are advising buyers to be prepared to act quickly, waive contingencies, and offer over asking to compete effectively in this tight market.

DATA POINTS

Average days on market (Chicago-area, May): 27 days

Nationwide average days on market: 52 days

St. Charles home listing price: $599,900

St. Charles home closing price: $750,000

Tinley Park home asking price: $605,000

Glenview home premium over asking: 9 percent
