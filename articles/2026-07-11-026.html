SF approves another condo project for fast-densifying Market Street corner

The Real Deal — San Francisco — Fri, 10 Jul 2026 18:08:54 +0000

SUMMARY

Property Listing / For Sale

San Francisco officials have granted final approval for a 23-story, 201-unit condominium tower at 1965 Market Street, a site that has been entitled for development for over a decade. The project, owned by Keller Grover, incorporates a historic Mission Revival building and includes 28 affordable units. Brokers anticipate the newly approved site will be listed for sale and could set a new precedent for development land values under California's updated housing laws, particularly Senate Bill 423, which streamlines housing approvals.
