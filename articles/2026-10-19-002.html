Camera Brand Fujifilm Takes 13K SF at 210 Lafayette Street in SoHo

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Mon, 19 Oct 2026 15:29:15 +0000

SUMMARY

Fujifilm, a Japanese camera manufacturer, has signed a lease for 12,700 square feet at 210 Lafayette Street in Manhattan's SoHo neighborhood. The new three-level space is anticipated to become a retail location, similar to other camera and electronics stores. This transaction reflects the continued strength in SoHo's retail leasing market, which commands some of the highest asking rents in North America.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: retail

Address: 210 Lafayette Street, New York City, NY

Size: 12,700 SF

Notable Features: Three-level retail space located between Kenmare and Spring streets in SoHo.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

Fujifilm

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

SoHo retail asking rents range between $896 and $1,409 per square foot, significantly higher than the Manhattan average of $707 per square foot, which saw a 5% year-over-year increase in Q3.
