Ogilvie Partners Sells Prospect Innovation Campus in Fort Collins to ZS Capital Partners for $42M

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Mon, 31 Aug 2026 14:40:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Ogilvie Partners has sold the Prospect Innovation Campus, a 226,000-square-foot industrial flex and research-and-development campus in Fort Collins, CO, to ZS Capital Partners for $42 million. The campus consists of 11 buildings across four distinct developments, offering a mix of small-bay industrial, flex, and R&D space with flexible suite configurations. CBRE represented the seller in this transaction.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Property Name: Prospect Innovation Campus

Size: 226,000 SF

Sale Price: $42.0M

Notable Features: Comprises 11 buildings across four developments: Midpoint 3, River Center, Spring Creek and One Prospect. Offers a mix of small-bay industrial, flex and R&D space, with flexible suite configurations and a blend of office and warehouse components. Provides convenient access to I-25.

Project Notes: Constructed between the late 1980s and early 2000s.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

SELLER: Ogilvie Partners

BUYER: ZS Capital Partners

SELLER BROKER: CBRE — Jeremy Ballenger; Tyler Carner; Patrick Devereaux; James Brady; Campbell Davis
