Avatar Furnishes Bridge Loan on Fully Leased Santa Ana Warehouse

LOAN

Connect CRE — Wed, 23 Sep 2026 22:06:22 +0000

SUMMARY

Avatar Financial Group LLC provided a $4.75 million bridge loan for a fully leased warehouse and distribution building in Santa Ana, CA. The two-year, first-lien loan was originated at approximately 60% loan-to-value, replacing the property's existing first mortgage and a junior loan. The sponsor plans to secure long-term financing after approximately six months of rental income, a strategy supported by the tenant's significant investment in its build-out and the property's strong location.
