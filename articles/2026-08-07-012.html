Buffalo Grove’s PREMIER Design + Build adds executive vice president

REJournals — Fri, 07 Aug 2026 09:20:32 +0000

SUMMARY

Promotion

PREMIER Design + Build Group, a Buffalo Grove, Illinois-based firm, has appointed Ed Pitula as Executive Vice President. In this role, Pitula will be responsible for overseeing operations and strategic growth within PREMIER's Central Region, focusing on strengthening client relationships and expanding the company's market presence. He brings over 30 years of experience in development and construction, having delivered more than 12.7 million square feet of commercial real estate across various sectors.
