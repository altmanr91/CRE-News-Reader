Tesla leases 683K sf speculative industrial building amid Central Texas expansion spree

LEASE

The Real Deal — Texas — Tue, 07 Jul 2026 20:05:24 +0000

SUMMARY

Tesla has leased a 682,000-square-foot speculative industrial building within the Austin Hills Commerce Center, a project being developed by Sansone Group and Principal Asset Management. This lease represents the second phase of the development, which is expected to be completed by January 2027. The transaction underscores the significant demand for large industrial spaces in the Austin market, as Tesla continues its rapid expansion in Central Texas.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

There are at least a dozen speculative industrial buildings spanning upwards of 400,000 square feet in various stages of development across the Austin area. Tesla currently leases 2.2 million square feet and owns over 10 million square feet of space around Austin.
