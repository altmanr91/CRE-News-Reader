Berkadia brokers sale of 139-unit student housing property in Lawrence

SALE

REJournals — Mon, 02 Nov 2026 16:39:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Berkadia has brokered the sale of Orchard Corners & Suites, a 139-unit student housing property in Lawrence, Kansas. The garden-style community, which serves The University of Kansas, was sold by Utah-based Redstone Residential. The transaction was facilitated by Berkadia's student housing and Kansas City investment sales teams.
