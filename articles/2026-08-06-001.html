New York State Gets One More Shot at Opportunity Zones

Commercial Observer — Thu, 06 Aug 2026 11:13:32 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

This article discusses the revised Opportunity Zone (OZ) program, highlighting its resilience in attracting capital for ground-up development even when conventional equity retreated. The author argues that OZ capital's mandatory 10-year investment horizon and tax-advantaged pricing make it suitable for emerging neighborhoods. With Congress having rewritten the program, New York State has a 60-day window to designate new OZ tracts, which now require a median family income at or below 70% of AMI and eliminate the contiguous tract provision. The author, an equity investments head at a major real estate firm, advises local leaders to identify eligible tracts, assemble investable projects, and demonstrate predictable approval processes to effectively direct this capital to areas most in need.

DATA POINTS

National OZ investment through 2024: $112 billion

National OZ investment through 2020: $44 billion

New York State OZ investment: $8.2 billion

New York State original OZ designations (2018): 514 census tracts

New York State eligible tracts under new rules: 1,702 census tracts

Maximum tracts New York State can nominate: 426
