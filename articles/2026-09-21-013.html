Colliers negotiates sale of 31,000-square-foot manufacturing facility in Stafford

SALE

REJournals — Mon, 21 Sep 2026 09:45:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Colliers recently brokered the sale of a 31,000-square-foot manufacturing facility located at 10302 Mula Road in Stafford, Texas. The property, situated on 1.89 acres, features a versatile layout including office, assembly, fulfillment, shop, and showroom areas. Both the seller and the buyer were represented by Colliers brokers in the transaction.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Address: 10302 Mula Road, Stafford, TX

Size: 31,000 SF

Site Area: 1.89 acres

Notable Features: Crane-served manufacturing facility with two 5-ton cranes, 16-foot hook height, three-phase 600-amp 480-volt electrical service, sprinkler system, and T5 lighting. Includes primary office, assembly, fulfillment, shop, and showroom space, plus a 3,000-square-foot mezzanine.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

SELLER BROKER: Colliers — Jason Scholtz; David Falcon
