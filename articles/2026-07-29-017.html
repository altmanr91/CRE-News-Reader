Paragon Acquires Grocery-Anchored Shopping Center from Long-Term Owners

ACQUISITION

Connect CRE — Tue, 28 Jul 2026 21:27:53 +0000

SUMMARY

Paragon Commercial Group has acquired a 46,055-square-foot grocery-anchored shopping center in the Tujunga neighborhood of Los Angeles. The off-market transaction involved purchasing the property from the family of its original developer, who had owned and operated it for over six decades. Paragon plans to implement upgrades to the center's physical attributes, including common areas, building facades, lighting, and signage, while preserving its long-standing anchor tenants.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: retail

Size: 46,055 SF

Site Area: 2.7 acres

Notable Features: Grocery-anchored center with long-term anchor tenants, located along the foothills of the San Gabriel mountains.

Project Notes: Acquired off-market from the family of the original developer who owned the property for over six decades.
