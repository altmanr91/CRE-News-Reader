Alexander brothers swept into contraband probe at Brooklyn detention center

The Real Deal — New York — Sat, 04 Jul 2026 12:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Non-CRE / Political & Crime News

This article reports on a contraband cellphone probe at a Brooklyn detention center involving the Alexander brothers, formerly prominent residential real estate brokers. Federal prosecutors charged another inmate with possessing an illegal phone, which was allegedly used by Oren Alexander and others. The report also revealed that all three Alexander brothers were found to have used contraband cellphones, though they have not been charged with related crimes. The article also touches on Tal Alexander's divorce proceedings, which revealed communications from him while in detention, and allegations that he orchestrated an early lease termination for the apartment he shared with his estranged wife at 432 Park Avenue.
