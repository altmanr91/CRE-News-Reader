Nalcorp, Clipper Equity Buy Coney Island Oceanfront Development Site for $20M

ACQUISITION

Commercial Observer — Fri, 14 Aug 2026 18:21:10 +0000

SUMMARY

Nalcorp and Clipper Equity have acquired an oceanfront development site in Coney Island, Brooklyn, for $20 million from Bath Site LLC. The 69,530-square-foot parcel, which includes 247 feet of Boardwalk frontage, is part of a larger development where permits were filed in 2024 for a 23-story, 202-unit mixed-use building. This transaction represents a significant development deal in a rapidly transforming waterfront market, where opportunities for premier development sites are becoming increasingly scarce due to ongoing public and private investment.
