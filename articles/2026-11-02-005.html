PCCP Refis Long Island Apartments With $154M Loan

REFINANCE

Commercial Observer — Mon, 02 Nov 2026 17:49:01 +0000

SUMMARY

Rechler Equity Partners secured a $154.25 million loan from PCCP to refinance Greybarn Amityville, a 335-unit multifamily property in Amityville, NY. The property, developed in three phases between 2016 and 2020, includes both market-rate and workforce housing units, along with ground-floor retail. The refinancing was facilitated by JLL, with the lender noting the property's strong performance, robust amenity package, and the owner's management capabilities in a competitive lending environment. The Long Island rental market's low new supply and high incomes are expected to support the property's success.
