Therme Dallas pivots, adds skybridge to $800M wellness resort buildout plans

The Real Deal — Texas — Mon, 20 Jul 2026 19:45:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Development

Therme Dallas is seeking city approval for modifications to its planned $800 million wellness resort, including the addition of a pedestrian skybridge and new non-residential zoning. The Austrian wellness company aims to break ground in 2027 on the 24-acre, 500,000-square-foot development, which will feature thermal baths, swim-up bars, saunas, and restaurants under a retractable glass roof. Despite past controversies regarding the company's development experience, the project is moving forward with these updated plans.
