Carlson Partners helps bring Salvation Army store to Edina

LEASE

REJournals — Fri, 31 Jul 2026 14:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

The Salvation Army has secured a new 27,495-square-foot retail lease in Edina, Minnesota, for a new Family Store. This transaction follows a 13-month effort to amend a city code that previously restricted the size of secondhand retail stores, which was unanimously approved by the Edina City Council. The new store will convert an underutilized industrial and flex building into retail and donation processing space, creating approximately 36 jobs.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: retail

Address: 4200 W. 76th St., Edina, MN

Size: 27,495 SF

Notable Features: Converts an underutilized industrial and flex building; includes 18,425 SF of retail and 9,434 SF for donation intake and processing.

Project Notes: Required a 13-month effort to amend a city code prohibiting secondhand retail stores larger than 2,500 SF.
