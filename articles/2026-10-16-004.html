Union Investment Sells Meatpacking District Retail Condo for $27M

SALE

Commercial Observer — Thu, 15 Oct 2026 20:23:08 +0000

SUMMARY

Union Investment Real Estate, a German investment manager, has sold a 15,000-square-foot retail condominium unit at 415 West 13th Street in Manhattan's Meatpacking District for $26.5 million. The buyer was West Gotham Re Projects Corporation. Union Investment had originally acquired the condo in 2012 for $18 million.
