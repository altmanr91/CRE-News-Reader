Trammell Crow-Led JV Sells Houston Warehouse for $26.8M

SALE

Connect CRE — Mon, 05 Oct 2026 16:25:18 +0000

SUMMARY

A joint venture led by Trammell Crow Company and Daiwa House Texas Inc. has sold Building 3 of the Blue Ridge Commerce Center in Houston, Texas, to Applied Optoelectronics for $26.8 million. The 153,928-square-foot warehouse was fully leased by the buyer in February 2026, prior to the sale. This transaction marks the fourth building sold to an owner-occupier within the first phase of the commerce center, highlighting strong demand for industrial properties in the area.
