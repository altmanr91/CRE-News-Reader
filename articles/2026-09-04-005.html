Sagehall Picks Up Chetrit Organization’s 428 Broadway for $47M

SALE

Commercial Observer — Thu, 03 Sep 2026 18:52:43 +0000

SUMMARY

Sagehall, a New York City-based private real estate investment firm, has acquired the six-story office building at 428 Broadway in Manhattan's SoHo neighborhood for $47 million. The seller was a partnership between Chetrit Organization and American Industrial. The property faced distress after its anchor tenant, WeWork, filed for bankruptcy in 2023, leaving the building largely vacant. The sale follows a foreclosure process initiated by LoanCore Capital Credit, with the mortgage debt later transferred to an entity of the selling partners, who then took ownership through a foreclosure auction to facilitate the sale.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Address: 428 Broadway, New York City, NY

Sale Price: $47.0M

Project Notes: The Chetrit Group originally purchased the property in 2005 for $22.5 million. The building became 96% vacant after WeWork's bankruptcy in 2023. LoanCore Capital Credit initiated foreclosure actions in 2024 on approximately $200 million in loans, which included this property and 1 Whitehall Street. The mortgage debt on 428 Broadway was transferred to C&AI Soho Holding, an entity of the Chetrit Organization and Donics, in late 2025, which then took ownership via foreclosure auction.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: Sagehall — Jason Levine, managing director

SELLER: Chetrit Organization — Michael Chetrit

SELLER: American Industrial — Roget Lerner; Jean-Marc Donics

LENDER: LoanCore Capital Credit
