Inflation Could Stay Higher: What it Means for CRE

Connect CRE — Fri, 24 Jul 2026 05:01:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

This article analyzes the potential for sustained inflation and its implications for commercial real estate, despite a recent dip in the Consumer Price Index. The author argues that ongoing geopolitical tensions and persistent inflation in key sectors like energy, food, housing, and medical care suggest that elevated prices may continue, potentially leading to further federal interest rate hikes. However, the author concludes that commercial real estate remains a resilient investment, with the risk of rate increases largely priced into current lending rates, and all major property types demonstrating positive space demand and stable fundamentals.

DATA POINTS

June Consumer Price Index: 3.5%

Food, housing, and medical care inflation: 2% to mid-3% range

Office properties net absorption: Ninth consecutive quarter of positive net absorption in Q2
