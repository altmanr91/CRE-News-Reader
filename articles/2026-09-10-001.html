Get to Know the Broker: Teresa Minnick of Christie’s International Real Estate Group

Commercial Observer — Thu, 10 Sep 2026 11:15:53 +0000

SUMMARY

Feature / Profile

This article profiles Teresa Minnick, a leading residential broker with Christie's International Real Estate Group in New Jersey, highlighting her 25-year career and over $800 million in sales. Minnick discusses her most significant projects, including the Atlantic Club Residences, a 132-residence luxury oceanfront development in Long Branch, and her involvement in the revitalization of Asbury Park. She emphasizes her passion for the Jersey Shore's history, architecture, and charm, and credits her mentors for shaping her approach to real estate.
