Phoenix Investors’ Affiliate Acquires 1.8 MSF Industrial Complex in Saginaw, Michigan

ACQUISITION

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 28 Aug 2026 15:05:00 +0000

SUMMARY

An affiliate of Phoenix Investors has acquired a 1.8 million-square-foot industrial complex in Buena Vista Charter Township, bordering Saginaw, Michigan, from international auto supplier Nexteer Automotive. The complex comprises three buildings, with Nexteer Automotive continuing to occupy nearly 1.3 million square feet in two of the facilities. Approximately 552,000 square feet in the third building is available for lease, aligning with Phoenix Investors' strategy of revitalizing former manufacturing sites.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Property Name: Plants 5, 6/1 and 7

Size: 1,800,000 SF

Project Notes: Nexteer Automotive will continue to occupy nearly 1.3 million square feet across Plants 6/1 and 7. Approximately 552,000 square feet in Plant 5 is currently available for lease.
