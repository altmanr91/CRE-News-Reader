Entre Commercial Realty closes 20,500-square-foot industrial lease in Wauconda

LEASE

REJournals — Mon, 17 Aug 2026 17:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Entre Commercial Realty has successfully brokered a 20,500-square-foot industrial lease in Wauconda, Illinois. The transaction involved a freestanding industrial facility, which was brought to full occupancy following its recent acquisition by an investment purchaser. This lease completes the investor's strategy for the property.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Address: 1275 Kyle Court, Wauconda, IL

Size: 20,500 SF

Notable Features: Freestanding industrial facility with 20-foot clear heights, two exterior loading docks, and one drive-in door.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

LANDLORD: an investment purchaser

LEASING AGENT: Entre Commercial Realty — Brian Bocci, SIOR; Jack Steineke

TENANT REP: Cawley Commercial Real Estate
