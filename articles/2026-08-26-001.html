Sheppard Takes 107K SF at In-Rel’s D.C. Redevelopment

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Tue, 25 Aug 2026 21:25:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Law firm Sheppard has signed an anchor lease for 107,224 square feet at In-Rel Properties' 2033 K Street NW, a planned trophy office redevelopment in Washington, D.C. The firm will occupy space in the building, which is undergoing a significant expansion and modernization, upon its anticipated completion in July 2029. This lease highlights the ongoing trend in D.C.'s office market where demand for large, high-quality trophy spaces remains strong despite overall market distress, particularly from prominent law firms seeking modern and efficient workplaces.
