Greystar Accused By Watchdog Of Discriminating Against Section 8 Voucher Users

Bisnow News Feed — Tue, 14 Jul 2026 12:11:56 -0400

SUMMARY

CRE Legal / Regulatory

A housing watchdog group has accused Greystar, a major apartment owner, of widespread discrimination against Section 8 voucher holders across seven jurisdictions. The Housing Rights Initiative filed 114 complaints with state agencies and attorneys general, alleging that Greystar employees unlawfully refused to accept public assistance payments or imposed illegal conditions on their use. This marks the latest in a series of legal challenges for Greystar, which has faced similar allegations in the past and recently settled claims related to rent price-fixing and illegal fees for service members.

DATA POINTS

Number of alleged violations: 114

Households aided by Section 8 program: 2.2 million

Public housing authorities funded by Section 8: 2,500

Landlords failing to meet voucher holders (2018 HUD study): 11%

Greystar settlement with DOJ (June 2025): $1.4 million
