Energy Tech Company Novele Raises $17M Series A

Commercial Observer — Wed, 30 Sep 2026 12:00:24 +0000

SUMMARY

Non-CRE / Business News

Energy technology company Novele, based in Stamford, CT, has successfully raised $17 million in a Series A funding round. The capital will be used to expand its national presence beyond its current Mid-Atlantic customer base, grow its engineering and commercial teams, and scale installations of its autonomous energy platform. The platform combines AI-powered software, BoardOS, with distributed energy storage hardware, EnergyBoard, to help commercial buildings achieve net-zero emissions by optimizing energy consumption and providing real-time monitoring.
