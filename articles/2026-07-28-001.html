AI Company Antares Labs Launches With $7M Seed Round

Commercial Observer — Tue, 28 Jul 2026 11:00:45 +0000

SUMMARY

Non-CRE / Business News

Antares Labs, a Chicago-based company specializing in custom artificial intelligence solutions for real estate operations, has officially launched with a $7.25 million seed funding round. The company aims to bridge the gap between the real estate industry's extensive data and its adoption of AI technologies to leverage this data effectively. Antares Labs develops tailor-made AI systems that integrate with clients' institutional knowledge, proprietary data, and workflows, offering solutions such as identifying new submarkets and land parcels for developers, optimizing tenant turnover for REITs, and providing inventory insights for industrial landlords. The company emphasizes a consultancy model, focusing on enterprise-wide AI strategy rather than just providing specific tools.
