Lee & Associates negotiates purchase of 455,858-square-foot industrial building in Lansing

ACQUISITION

REJournals — Tue, 14 Jul 2026 10:02:27 +0000

SUMMARY

RBT Industries (The Great Escape) has acquired a 455,858-square-foot industrial building in Lansing, Illinois, from institutional seller Plymouth Industrial REIT, Inc. RBT Industries will occupy a significant portion of the facility, with the remaining space leased to an existing tenant. The acquisition was facilitated by Lee & Associates, representing the buyer, and CBRE, representing the seller.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Address: 16801 Exchange Avenue, Lansing, IL

Size: 455,858 SF

Notable Features: Heavily powered building with close proximity to I-80 and I-94.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

SELLER: Plymouth Industrial REIT, Inc.
