Hochul orders 1-year ban on large data center construction

The Real Deal — New York — Tue, 14 Jul 2026 16:02:44 +0000

SUMMARY

Infrastructure Investment / Government Policy

New York Governor Kathy Hochul has issued a one-year moratorium on the construction of large data centers exceeding 50 megawatts that have not yet received permits. This executive order aims to allow the state to develop environmental assessments, establish consistent standards for data center operations, and create rules for their energy provision. The move comes amid public concern over the environmental and financial impacts of hyperscale facilities, with the governor emphasizing the need to address potential utility bill hikes and resource depletion. The state is also exploring ways to extract more benefits from data center development, including a potential fund for grid investment and a review of sales tax exemptions.
