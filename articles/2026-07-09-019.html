Georgia bans real estate firm accused of deceptive business practices

The Real Deal — National — Wed, 08 Jul 2026 14:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

CRE Legal / Regulatory

The state of Georgia has permanently banned Florida-based MV Realty and secured a $1 million settlement to compensate hundreds of homeowners who were allegedly trapped in predatory 40-year contracts. The company's "homeowner benefit program" was accused of deceptively requiring homeowners to use MV Realty's agents for future property sales or transfers, or face a 3% termination fee, effectively placing a lien on their properties and hindering refinancing. This action follows a 2024 lawsuit filed by the Georgia Attorney General, with similar legal challenges and legislative efforts against MV Realty occurring in numerous other states.
