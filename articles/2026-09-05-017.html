Northern New Jersey Shopping Center Changes Hands

ACQUISITION

Connect CRE — Fri, 04 Sep 2026 21:42:47 +0000

SUMMARY

Adoni Property Group has acquired Highlands Village, a 60,614-square-foot shopping center in Basking Ridge, NJ, from Kushner Real Estate Group. JLL Capital Markets facilitated the sale and secured $12.2 million in acquisition financing for the buyer from an undisclosed life insurance firm. The fully leased, necessity-based retail center, anchored by Walgreens and the United States Postal Service, benefits from its location within The Hills at Basking Ridge master-planned community and recent leasing momentum.
