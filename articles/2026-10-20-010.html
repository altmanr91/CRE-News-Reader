DAUM Negotiates $10.6M Sale of Industrial Building in Phoenix

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Tue, 20 Oct 2026 13:52:37 +0000

SUMMARY

DAUM Commercial Real Estate Services has facilitated the $10.6 million sale of a 43,338-square-foot industrial building in Phoenix's Deer Valley submarket. A private Taiwanese investment company acquired the asset from a privately held California company in an off-market transaction. The property features single-tenant industrial space with 6,500 square feet of office, fully air-conditioned office and manufacturing areas, 18-foot clear heights, and multiple loading doors.
