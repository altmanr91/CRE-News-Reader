Mission District apartment project moving forward after height, unit count reduction

The Real Deal — San Francisco — Wed, 01 Jul 2026 22:54:06 +0000

SUMMARY

Development

Wong Logan Architects is moving forward with a revised plan for Trigona, a six-story, 20-unit multifamily infill project in San Francisco's Mission District. The updated proposal reduces the building's height and nearly halves its residential capacity compared to previous plans, now including two deed-restricted affordable housing units. The project will also feature ground-floor commercial space and bicycle parking, situated on a triangular parcel one block from the 24th Street BART Station.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

More than 20,000 housing units in San Francisco have been approved but are stalled in the development pipeline due to rising construction costs, city fees, material shortages, and federal tariffs. The City of San Francisco is mandated by the state to entitle 82,069 new housing units by 2030.
