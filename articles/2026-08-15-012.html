MDH Partners acquires 1-million-square-foot industrial building in Greenfield

ACQUISITION

REJournals — Fri, 14 Aug 2026 13:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

MDH Partners has acquired 70 Connect III, a 1-million-square-foot Class A industrial building in Greenfield, Indiana, located within the Indianapolis metropolitan area. The speculative cross-docked facility, developed by Lauth, offers 40-foot clear heights and extensive dock and trailer parking. MDH plans immediate capital improvements to enhance the property for a large industrial user, including doubling trailer parking and expanding office space. The acquisition aligns with MDH's strategy of investing its $1.2 billion Fund III, having closed over $1 billion in assets since early 2025.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Property Name: 70 Connect III

Size: 1,005,776 SF

Notable Features: Cross-docked building with 40-foot clear heights, 108 expandable to 216 dock doors, four drive-in doors, ESFR sprinkler system, and eight-inch slabs. Located less than three miles from Interstate 70.

Project Notes: Developed by Lauth on a speculative basis. MDH plans to double trailer parking from 115 to approximately 230 positions, expand speculative office space, and complete interior/exterior repainting.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Indianapolis metro's overall industrial vacancy fell to 5.9% in Q2 2026, with 3.1 million square feet absorbed during the quarter and nearly 19 million square feet over the past 12 months. The East submarket, where 70 Connect III is located, saw vacancy drop from over 18% to 11% in the past year, remaining the highest vacancy rate in Indianapolis despite leading in leasing activity.
