Mid-America Real Estate Corporation closes sale of grocery-anchored shopping center in Wheaton

SALE

REJournals — Wed, 21 Oct 2026 14:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Mid-America Real Estate Corporation's Investment Sales Group has facilitated the sale of Rice Lake Square, a 244,924-square-foot grocery-anchored shopping center in Wheaton, IL. The property, anchored by a high-performing Pete's Fresh Market, was sold by Core Acquisitions to Phillips Edison Company (PECO). The center benefits from its strategic location in an affluent western Chicago suburb, drawing significant traffic from surrounding communities.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: retail

Address: 303 W Wesley St, Wheaton, IL*
