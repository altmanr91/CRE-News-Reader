SUMMARY

ZAR Property NY has acquired a 12-story, 89,400-square-foot office building in New York City's Flatiron District for $28 million from GFP Real Estate. Additionally, two commercial properties in Jamaica, Queens, totaling 91,700 square feet of retail and office space, were sold for $17.3 million to 16101 Jamaica Avenue LLC by entities led by Leonard Zangas. These transactions highlight continued activity in the commercial real estate market across different boroughs of New York City.
