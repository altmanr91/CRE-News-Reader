Shorenstein, Portman Agree to Terms on Nashville Office Sale

ACQUISITION

Connect CRE — Mon, 06 Jul 2026 22:47:27 +0000

SUMMARY

Shorenstein Investment Advisers has acquired the Moore Building, a 245,826-square-foot office property in Nashville, from Portman Holdings. This transaction marks Shorenstein's fourth office investment in the Nashville market. The building, delivered in 2023, features a strong tenant mix including a major social media company.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Property Name: The Moore Building

Address: 827 19th Ave. S., Nashville, TN

Size: 245,826 SF

Year Built: 2023

Notable Features: Located within Music Row, the 16-story building includes a fitness center, conference rooms, indoor and outdoor lounges, and 8,500 square feet of retail space.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: Shorenstein Investment Advisers

SELLER: Portman Holdings

DEVELOPER/SPONSOR: Creed Investment Co.

TENANTS AT PROPERTY

TikTok

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

The last significant office sale in Nashville occurred in May, when Highwoods Properties sold the 522,000-square-foot Bridgestone Tower for $255 million, or $488.5 per square foot.
