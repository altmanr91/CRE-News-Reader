Matthews Brokers Sale of Magnolia Marketplace Shopping Center in New Orleans

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 30 Oct 2026 14:55:43 +0000

SUMMARY

Matthews has brokered the sale of Magnolia Marketplace, a 115,281-square-foot shopping center in New Orleans. Stirling Properties sold the fully leased asset to Agree Realty. While the sales price was not disclosed, the transaction represents the highest retail sales price in New Orleans' Mid-City/Central City submarket in the last decade, according to CoStar Group data.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: retail

Property Name: Magnolia Marketplace

Address: 2900 S. Claiborne Ave., New Orleans, LA

Size: 115,281 SF

Occupancy at Sale: 100.0%

Year Built: 2015

Project Notes: Stirling Properties is retaining ownership of an outparcel at the center.
