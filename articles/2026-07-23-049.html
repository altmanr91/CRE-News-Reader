REPORT: Google Quadruples Miami Office After Founders' Mansion Buys

LEASE

Bisnow News Feed — Wed, 22 Jul 2026 15:34:30 -0400

SUMMARY

Google has significantly expanded its office footprint in Miami, quadrupling its lease at 1450 Brickell Avenue from 10,000 SF to 45,000 SF. This expansion follows recent high-profile residential real estate purchases in the area by the company's founders. The move reflects a broader trend of out-of-state companies increasing their presence in South Florida, often after their leadership relocates to the region.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Property Name: 1450 Brickell Ave.

Address: 1450 Brickell Ave, Miami, FL

Size: 45,000 SF

Notable Features: 35-story skyscraper with 10,000 SF of ground-floor retail.

Project Notes: Google initially established its presence in the building approximately a decade ago with a 10,000 SF office lease.
