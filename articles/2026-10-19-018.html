Phillips Edison Pays $69.1M for Orlando-Area Retail Center

ACQUISITION

Connect CRE — Mon, 19 Oct 2026 14:23:57 +0000

SUMMARY

Phillips Edison has acquired Alafaya Square, a 168,532-square-foot grocery-anchored retail center in Oviedo, FL, for $69.1 million. This transaction, facilitated by JLL, marks the largest single-asset retail sale in Seminole County's history. The property boasts a strong tenant mix and high occupancy, underscoring its appeal in the Orlando metropolitan area.
