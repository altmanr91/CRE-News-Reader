Republic Clothing Commits to 81K SF at 1411 Broadway

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Mon, 31 Aug 2026 16:08:43 +0000

SUMMARY

Republic Clothing, an international apparel manufacturer and wholesaler, has signed a 15-year direct lease for 81,000 square feet at 1411 Broadway in Midtown Manhattan, converting its previous sublease into a full tenancy. This transaction is part of a larger leasing momentum at the property, which also saw new leases from Zeta Global for 50,522 square feet and Amorepacific for 50,522 square feet. The building's co-owners attribute the strong tenant interest to a $100 million repositioning project that modernized the property's infrastructure and amenities, including a new rooftop amenity slated for early 2028.
