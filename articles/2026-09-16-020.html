Related Launches Leasing for Tuxedo Luxury Rentals

Connect CRE — Tue, 15 Sep 2026 21:07:53 +0000

SUMMARY

Lease

Related Companies has commenced the initial leasing phase for 336 luxury rental apartments at The Village at Tuxedo Reserve, a mixed-use town center within the 1,200-acre Tuxedo Reserve community in Tuxedo, NY. This development, located 30 miles from Manhattan, integrates residential units with retail, dining, and public gathering spaces, aiming to create a vibrant community hub. An executive from Related Companies expressed anticipation for the community's growth and its role in serving residents, local businesses, and visitors.
