Rockspring Underway on 254-Acre Mixed-Use Development in Marble Falls, Texas

DEVELOPMENT

REBusinessOnline — Tue, 28 Jul 2026 14:44:12 +0000

SUMMARY

Rockspring, a Houston-based firm, is developing The Highlands, a 254-acre mixed-use project in Marble Falls, Texas. The master-planned community will feature single-family homes, apartments, townhomes, commercial space, and parks. The developer has secured a $28.2 million construction loan and is finalizing a public infrastructure development bond with the City of Marble Falls to fund predevelopment and sitework, which are expected to cost over $70 million.
