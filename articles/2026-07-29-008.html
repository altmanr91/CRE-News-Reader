JLL Arranges Sale of 2.4 MSF Industrial Portfolio in Florida, Georgia

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Tue, 28 Jul 2026 14:33:45 +0000

SUMMARY

JLL has facilitated the sale of a three-property industrial portfolio totaling 2.4 million square feet across Florida and Georgia. EQT Real Estate acquired the fully leased portfolio, which features modern facilities built around 2015 with an average clear height of 34 feet. The transaction involved properties in Lakeland, FL; Jacksonville, FL; and Pooler, GA.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Size: 2,400,000 SF

Year Built: 2015

Notable Features: Fully leased properties with 34-foot average clear heights and various dock configurations.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE
