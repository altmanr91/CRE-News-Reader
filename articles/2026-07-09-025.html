South Florida’s top deals: Miami medical facility trades for $32M

SALE

The Real Deal — Miami — Thu, 09 Jul 2026 12:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

An affiliate of Amdur Management sold an off-campus emergency center in Miami for $32 million to an LLC managed by Supermarket Management. The facility, located on a nearly 8-acre lot, was previously owned by the seller for several decades. This transaction represents the top commercial sale recorded in South Florida for the period.
