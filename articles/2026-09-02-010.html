Corvant Signs 113,865 SF Industrial Lease in Douglasville, Georgia

LEASE

REBusinessOnline — Wed, 02 Sep 2026 12:19:19 +0000

SUMMARY

Corvant, an HVAC and mechanical plumbing manufacturer, has signed a full-building lease for 113,865 square feet at LogistiCenter at Bright Star in Douglasville, GA. The industrial facility, recently delivered by landlord Dermody, offers modern features including 32-foot clear heights and ESFR fire protection. The lease was facilitated by Hughes Commercial Real Estate representing the landlord and The Realty Group representing the tenant.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Property Name: LogistiCenter at Bright Star

Size: 113,865 SF

Notable Features: Rear-load facility with 32-foot clear heights, a 60-foot speed bay, 28 dock-high doors, two drive-in doors, approximately 90 automobile parking spaces, and ESFR fire protection.
