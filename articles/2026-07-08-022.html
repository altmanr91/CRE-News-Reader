Meet the developers behind the Pfizer building conversion

DEVELOPMENT

The Real Deal — New York — Tue, 07 Jul 2026 21:05:51 +0000

SUMMARY

Metro Loft Management and David Werner are converting the former Pfizer headquarters at 235 East 42nd Street in Midtown East into approximately 1,500 rental units, making it the largest office-to-residential conversion in New York City. The project, which includes both luxury and affordable housing, recently secured a record $700 million construction loan from Madison Realty Capital. The development has faced several construction safety violations and incidents, including a recent fatality or injury notification failure and a worker fall.
