Red Rock to Develop 519,792 SF Industrial Facility Near Columbia, South Carolina

DEVELOPMENT

REBusinessOnline — Tue, 21 Jul 2026 14:14:12 +0000

SUMMARY

Red Rock Developments is set to construct a 519,792-square-foot speculative industrial facility within the Sandy Run Industrial Park in Gaston, a suburb of Columbia, South Carolina. The cross-dock facility will feature 40-foot clear heights and 98 dock-high doors, with groundbreaking anticipated this quarter. Colliers has been retained to handle the marketing and leasing of the property.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Size: 519,792 SF

Site Area: 54 acres

Notable Features: Cross-dock facility with 40-foot clear heights, 185-foot truck courts, 98 dock-high doors, four drive-in doors, ESFR sprinkler system, and expandable parking.

Project Notes: The facility will be built on a speculative basis within the 760-acre Sandy Run Industrial Park.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

DEVELOPER/SPONSOR: Red Rock Developments

LEASING AGENT: Colliers — Chuck Salley; Thomas Beard; John Peebles
