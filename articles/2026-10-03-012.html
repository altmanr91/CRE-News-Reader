Mojo Coffee Officially Opens in Chicago’s Loop

LEASE

Connect CRE — Fri, 02 Oct 2026 21:33:06 +0000

SUMMARY

Mojo Coffee has officially opened a new 1,576-square-foot cafe at 70 W. Madison in Chicago's Loop, occupying a ground-floor corner of the office tower. The New Zealand-born specialty roaster's opening follows significant leasing activity and ongoing investments in building upgrades by owners Namdar Realty Group and Mason Asset Management, who acquired the property in December 2024.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: retail

Address: 70 W. Madison, Chicago, IL

Size: 1,576 SF

Notable Features: Ground-floor corner retail space with street and direct lobby access.

Project Notes: The building was acquired in December 2024 for $85 million. The space was previously occupied by a Starbucks.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

LANDLORD: Namdar Realty Group
