Riverside Wharf JV Lands $145M Construction Loan for Dream Hotel

LOAN

Connect CRE — Mon, 26 Oct 2026 14:15:30 +0000

SUMMARY

A joint venture between Driftwood Capital, MV Real Estate Holdings, and Merrimac Ventures secured a $145 million construction loan from Centennial Bank for the Riverside Wharf project in Miami. This financing will facilitate the vertical construction of the $350 million hospitality-driven complex, which will feature a 167-key Dream Hotel, a large nightclub, event space, and signature restaurants. The development is expected to be completed in fall 2028.

Project Notes: The development also includes the rebirth and expansion of The Wharf Miami and a pop-up location for Garcia’s Fish Market.
