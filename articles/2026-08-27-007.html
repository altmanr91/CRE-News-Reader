JVM Realty Buys 280-Unit Apartment Community in Oswego, Illinois

ACQUISITION

REBusinessOnline — Thu, 27 Aug 2026 14:48:36 +0000

SUMMARY

JVM Realty Corp. has acquired Springs at Oswego, a 280-unit apartment community in Oswego, Illinois, which has been rebranded as Trillium at Oswego. The property, featuring townhome-style units and a range of amenities, was sold by Continental Properties. Cushman & Wakefield facilitated the transaction.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Trillium at Oswego

Address: 801 Fifth St., Oswego, IL

Units: 280

Year Built: 2019

Notable Features: Gated community with townhome-style units, heated pool, 24-hour fitness center, clubhouse, outdoor grilling stations, fire pit, car wash, and two dog parks.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: JVM Realty Corp.

SELLER: Continental Properties

SELLER BROKER: Cushman & Wakefield
