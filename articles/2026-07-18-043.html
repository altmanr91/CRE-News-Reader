Balbec Raises $930M For Fund To Target Commercial, Residential Debt

LOAN

Bisnow News Feed — Fri, 17 Jul 2026 12:48:03 -0400

SUMMARY

Alternative asset manager Balbec Capital LP has secured $930 million in its initial closing for the IGCF-VII fund, which will focus on commercial and residential mortgage debt across the U.S. and Western Europe. This fund aims to capitalize on opportunities arising from traditional lenders pulling back on new deals due to pandemic-era balance sheet issues, a trend that has fueled the growth of private credit in real estate. The fund's strategy includes investments in performing and nonperforming residential mortgage loans, mortgage servicing rights, and commercial mortgage and bridge loans.

DATA POINTS

Loan Amount: $930.0M

Project Notes: This represents the first closing for the IGCF-VII fund, which is anticipated to be larger than previous funds. Balbec has raised five prior funds, with the most recent bringing in $1.47 billion.

In June 2026, Balbec acquired London-based Funding 365 Lending, expanding its European real estate exposure. Balbec has invested in more than 20 countries and manages roughly $8 billion in assets.
