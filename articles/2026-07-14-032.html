Silver Star continues sell-off with Houston office deal

SALE

The Real Deal — Texas — Mon, 13 Jul 2026 20:45:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Silver Star CRE, a subsidiary of Silver Star REIT, sold The Preserve at North Loop, a seven-building Class-B office campus in Houston, to an undisclosed Atlanta-based investor. The sale is part of Silver Star's Chapter 11 bankruptcy proceedings and its strategy to divest office assets and pivot to self-storage. The property, spanning 219,000 square feet, was approximately 65 percent occupied at the time of sale, down from 92 percent when Silver Star acquired it in 2018. The buyer intends to modernize the office park while maintaining current operations.
