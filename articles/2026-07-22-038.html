Amazon grabs Sunnyvale offices once occupied by Google at Jay Paul Co. campus

LEASE

The Real Deal — San Francisco — Tue, 21 Jul 2026 21:42:47 +0000

SUMMARY

Amazon has expanded its presence at the Moffett Towers complex in Sunnyvale, signing a lease for the entire 317,000-square-foot building at 1000 Enterprise Way. This space was previously occupied by Google, which had been gradually vacating its offices at the campus. The deal reflects a potential shift in the Silicon Valley office market, with large tech companies showing renewed confidence and increasing their office footprints.

DATA POINTS

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

The first half of 2026 was the strongest first-six-months period for office leasing in Silicon Valley since 2018. Sizable leases over 100,000 square feet have doubled from the 2025 average. AI firms have leased 2.4 million square feet of offices in Silicon Valley and the Peninsula this year. New office construction has dropped to its lowest level in over two decades.
