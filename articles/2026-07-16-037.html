CPC Mortgage Company Secures $42M to Rehabilitate Michigan Housing Property

LOAN

Connect CRE — Wed, 15 Jul 2026 21:05:13 +0000

SUMMARY

CPC Mortgage Company has secured a $42.1 million loan for the preservation and rehabilitation of Parkside Estates and Demby Terraces, a 315-unit affordable housing community in Inkster, Michigan. The financing, provided through a Freddie Mac Targeted Affordable Housing forward commitment, will facilitate a full rehabilitation and conversion of the properties under HUD's Rental Assistance Demonstration program, transitioning them from public housing to Section 8. The project is a collaboration between Good Housing Partnership and the Inkster Housing Commission.
