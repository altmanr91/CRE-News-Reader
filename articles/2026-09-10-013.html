Smaller-format retail thriving in Memphis market. Larger stores? Not as much

REJournals — Thu, 10 Sep 2026 09:36:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Retail Report

The Memphis retail market is experiencing a significant divergence in demand, with smaller-format properties (under 20,000 square feet) thriving while larger retail spaces face rising vacancy rates. This trend is driven by strong leasing activity from daily-needs and discount retailers, leading to a two-year high in small-format leasing during Q2 2026 and vacancy rates below 3% in June. Conversely, larger properties saw their vacancy rate climb to 6% in June due to big-box and furniture store closures. Investors are actively pursuing small-format retail, particularly strip centers and properties in Southeast Memphis, where limited new construction and potential for rent growth are attractive.

DATA POINTS

Small-format retail vacancy (under 20,000 SF): Below 3% in June 2026

Large-format retail vacancy (over 20,000 SF): Approximately 6% in June 2026

Increase in large-format retail vacancy year-over-year: 50 basis points

Small-format retail leasing activity Q2 2026: Two-year high

Retail transaction activity in Southeast Memphis: Nearly doubled over the past year
