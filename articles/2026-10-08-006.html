Boulder Group Arranges Sale of Four-Property Net Leased Dollar General Portfolio in Michigan

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Thu, 08 Oct 2026 16:02:15 +0000

SUMMARY

The Boulder Group has facilitated the sale of a four-property net-leased Dollar General portfolio across Northern and Central Michigan for $8.3 million. The newly constructed stores, located in Gaylord, Reed City, Honor, and Vermontville, are leased to Dollar General on absolute triple-net terms with corporate guarantees and feature 5% rental increases every five years. The portfolio offers geographic diversification and serves as a primary source of essentials in rural trade areas, with several locations benefiting from tourism traffic.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: retail

Size: 42,560 SF

Sale Price: $8.3M

Notable Features: Three of the four properties are the 10,640-square-foot Dollar General Plus prototype, offering an expanded assortment of fresh and refrigerated products. Each property is leased on an absolute triple-net basis with corporate guarantees from Dollar General Corp. (BBB credit rating).

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

SELLER BROKER: The Boulder Group — Randy Blankstein; Jimmy Goodman; John Feeney

SELLER: Midwest-based developer

BUYER: Northeast based investment fund

TENANTS AT PROPERTY

Dollar General
