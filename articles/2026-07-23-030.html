Jiazhao “Frankie” Chen roped into another foreclosure suit as Chicago loan defaults near $100M

FORECLOSURE

The Real Deal — Chicago — Wed, 22 Jul 2026 21:55:25 +0000

SUMMARY

A subsidiary of CoreVest Finance has filed a foreclosure lawsuit against Jiazhao "Frank" Chen and his entity Best Credit Investment LLC, seeking to recover nearly $820,000 on a defaulted loan. The lawsuit targets three multi-residential properties in Chicago's Bridgeport neighborhood, which are reportedly under construction and vacant. This action adds to Chen's growing legal challenges, with his total alleged real estate loan defaults in Chicago now approaching $100 million across a portfolio of over 55 properties.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Address: 2628, 2634 and 2636 South Wells Street, Chicago, IL

Loan Amount: $819K

Notable Features: Multi-residential buildings, currently under construction and vacant.

Project Notes: The lawsuit is over a defaulted loan payment of nearly $820,000 against Best Credit Investment LLC, for which Chen is the registered agent and individual guarantor. The original authorized debt under the revolving credit contract was $8.5 million.
