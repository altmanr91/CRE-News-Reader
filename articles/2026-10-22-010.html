Edge Realty Partners Brokers Sale of 38,880 SF Shopping Center in Richmond, Texas

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Thu, 22 Oct 2026 14:03:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Edge Realty Partners has brokered the sale of Long Meadow Farms, a 38,880-square-foot shopping center in Richmond, Texas. The property features a service-oriented tenant mix. The buyer and sale price were not disclosed.
