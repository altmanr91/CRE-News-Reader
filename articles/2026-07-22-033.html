McShane secures $79M loan to pay off construction debt on East Dallas apartments

LOAN

The Real Deal — Texas — Tue, 21 Jul 2026 19:30:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Conor Commercial Real Estate, a subsidiary of The McShane Companies, has secured a $78.7 million construction takeout loan for its recently completed multifamily project, The Flynn at Live Oak, in East Dallas. The financing will be used to pay off the original construction debt on the 327-unit apartment building. The project, which faced challenges in a market with rising interest rates and increased supply, is offering significant concessions to new renters.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Multifamily developers in North Texas have faced challenges stabilizing apartment buildings due to rising interest rates and a significant increase in new apartment supply, which has led to downward pressure on rents. Old East Dallas, a neighborhood approximately 3 miles from downtown, has seen a surge in multifamily development interest, contrasting with its past reputation as a high-crime area.
