Bridge Logistics Properties Targets Northern California Assets

ACQUISITION

Connect CRE — Mon, 14 Sep 2026 21:36:49 +0000

SUMMARY

Bridge Logistics Properties (BLP) is actively pursuing industrial acquisitions across Northern California, adapting its strategy to evolving market dynamics. The firm is targeting well-located, functional, second-generation assets along the I-880 corridor, particularly in Fremont and San Jose, where Class A supply is tightening due to demand from AI, advanced manufacturing, and technology sectors. Concurrently, BLP is investing in Central Valley markets like Stockton, capitalizing on logistics demand migrating eastward. This dual-market approach reflects BLP's view of an interconnected Northern California industrial market, aiming to capture demand where it is created, displaced, and where supply is constrained.
