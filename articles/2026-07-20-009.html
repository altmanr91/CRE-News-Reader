Summer camp sales mark the next chapter in Simad’s collapse

SALE

The Real Deal — National — Sun, 19 Jul 2026 13:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

The Shabsels brothers' summer camp empire, Simad, is being dismantled through bankruptcy proceedings, with two camps finding buyers. Warner Bros. Discovery CEO David Zaslav made a $68 million stalking horse bid for Mohawk Day Camp in Westchester, while Camp Achim in the Catskills sold for $7 million to its long-term operator. These sales are part of the ongoing liquidation of a portfolio that was once valued at $466 million but became extraordinarily leveraged through Israeli bond market financing and high-cost merchant cash advance loans, leading to a liquidity crisis and default.
