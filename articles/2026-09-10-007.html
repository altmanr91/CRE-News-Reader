Marcus & Millichap Brokers $12M Sale of Holiday Inn Hotel in Danbury, Connecticut

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Thu, 10 Sep 2026 13:20:54 +0000

SUMMARY

Marcus & Millichap has facilitated the $12 million sale of the Holiday Inn Express & Suites in Danbury, CT. The 79-room hotel, built in 2001 and recently renovated, was acquired by a private investor. Another private investor was the seller in this transaction.
