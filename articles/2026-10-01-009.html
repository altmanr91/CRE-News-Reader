Joby Aviation Signs 45,000 SF Industrial Lease in Haslet, Texas

LEASE

REBusinessOnline — Thu, 01 Oct 2026 14:54:17 +0000

SUMMARY

Joby Aviation, a manufacturer of electric vertical take-off and landing aircraft, has secured a 45,000-square-foot industrial lease in Haslet, Texas. The facility is situated within the Alliance Air Trade Center, which is part of the larger Perot Field Fort Worth Alliance Airport complex. This lease supports Joby Aviation's operational expansion in the region.
