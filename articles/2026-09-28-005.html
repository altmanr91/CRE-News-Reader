Mainstay Raises $18M and Acquires Truelist

Commercial Observer — Mon, 28 Sep 2026 14:51:53 +0000

SUMMARY

Non-CRE / Business News

Mainstay, a residential real estate system of record, has successfully raised $18 million in new funding and simultaneously acquired Truelist, an AI-powered listing platform. This strategic move aims to enhance Mainstay's proprietary data, pricing intelligence, and agentic automation capabilities, replacing fragmented systems in residential real estate transactions. The capital infusion will support Mainstay's expansion into serving brokerage firms and smaller investors, leveraging the combined expertise of both companies to improve transparency and accuracy in home valuation.
