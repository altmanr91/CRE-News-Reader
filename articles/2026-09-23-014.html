Before you deploy AI, ask if your data is ready

REJournals — Wed, 23 Sep 2026 09:11:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

This article argues that the success of AI deployment in facilities management hinges on the quality and structure of an organization's operational data, rather than the speed of tool implementation. The author contends that facilities leaders often make mistakes by selecting tools before assessing data readiness, deploying narrow AI solutions instead of integrated systems, and neglecting data governance. For AI initiatives to yield tangible results, data must be clean, connected, and consistently structured across various systems, enabling AI to draw reliable conclusions and provide actionable insights for maintenance, space utilization, and lease management. The core message is that a unified operational platform with well-governed data is the essential foundation for any AI tool to deliver real value.
