Best First Half For NYC Offices Since 2002 Puts Landlords Back In Control

Bisnow News Feed — Wed, 01 Jul 2026 18:44:40 -0400

SUMMARY

Market Research / Office Report

New York City's office market experienced its strongest first half for leasing activity since 2002, with 22.8 million square feet leased through June, putting it on track for the busiest year since 2000. This surge in demand, driven by law firms and AI companies, combined with a reduction in available supply due to office-to-residential conversions, has significantly tightened the market. As a result, the citywide availability rate dropped to 13%, the lowest since October 2020, and average asking rents increased by 5.7% year-over-year to $78.03 per square foot, indicating a shift in leverage back to landlords.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

The New York City office market is experiencing a significant tightening, with availability rates at their lowest since October 2020. This is driven by strong leasing activity, particularly from law firms and AI companies, and a reduction in supply due to office-to-residential conversions. The conversions not only reduce available space but also generate additional demand as displaced tenants seek new offices. Average asking rents have seen their sharpest midyear growth since 2016, and the spread between taking rents and asking rents is narrowing, indicating a shift in market power towards landlords.
