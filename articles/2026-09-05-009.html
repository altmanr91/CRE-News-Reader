HMF Americana Acquires Land from Harris Teeter in Metro Charlotte, Plans Build-to-Rent Development

ACQUISITION

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 04 Sep 2026 13:45:46 +0000

SUMMARY

HMF Americana has acquired a development site in metro Charlotte from Harris Teeter, with plans to develop a 221-unit build-to-rent residential community. The project will feature cottage-style rental homes, including a portion reserved for residents aged 55 and older, and approximately 10,000 square feet of multi-tenant commercial space. Site work is slated to begin in October 2026, with vertical construction following in the first quarter of 2027 and initial move-ins expected in the third quarter of 2027.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Units: 221

Completion: Q3 2027

Notable Features: Includes cottage-style rental homes, with a portion reserved for residents age 55 and older. Additional land is available for future expansion.

Project Notes: Site work is expected to begin in October 2026, followed by vertical construction in Q1 2027.
