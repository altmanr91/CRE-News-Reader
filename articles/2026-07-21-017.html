Balbec raises more than $900M for flagship fund

The Real Deal — National — Mon, 20 Jul 2026 19:30:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Fund Close

Balbec Capital, a New York-based alternative asset manager, has successfully closed its latest flagship fund with $930 million in commitments. This capital will be deployed to acquire residential and commercial mortgage debt, including performing and non-performing loans, mortgage servicing rights, and bridge loans, across the United States and Western Europe. The fund's strategy aims to capitalize on opportunities in the private credit market, where non-bank lenders are increasingly active.

DATA POINTS

Fund Commitments: $930 million

Private Credit Loans (H2 last year): $1.4 trillion

Assets Under Management: ~$8 billion

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Private credit loans totaled $1.4 trillion in the second half of last year, indicating a significant market for non-bank lending.
