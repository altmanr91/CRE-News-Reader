NYC’s Development Site Market Is Changing. So Is the Way We Create Value.

Commercial Observer — Tue, 20 Oct 2026 11:07:52 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

The New York City development site market is experiencing a significant resurgence, with anticipated sales volume increasing over 30 percent this year compared to 2025. This growth is driven by expanding buyer demand, intensifying competition for well-positioned sites, and increasing opportunities to create value through zoning changes. The article highlights that the Department of City Planning's receptiveness to rezonings is a key factor, leading some brokers to advise owners to pursue rezoning before selling to maximize value, despite the inherent risks. The market is also seeing an expanding universe of purchasers for smaller Manhattan sites, including established developers from other boroughs, which further intensifies competition and expands the pool of potential buyers.

DATA POINTS

Anticipated development site sales volume increase (2026 vs. 2025): more than 30 percent

Offers received for a Chelsea development site: more than 80

Pricing above original high-end expectations for Chelsea site: approximately 15 percent

Difference in land values for sites with vs. without affordable housing requirements: approaching 50 percent in certain neighborhoods
