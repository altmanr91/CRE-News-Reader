Law Firm O’Melveny Renews 100K SF at 1625 Eye Street in D.C.

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Wed, 09 Sep 2026 20:40:47 +0000

SUMMARY

Law firm O'Melveny & Myers has renewed its 100,000-square-foot lease for 15 years at 1625 Eye Street NW in Washington, D.C. The renewal comes as the building's new ownership group, which acquired the property in 2025, plans a significant renovation to reposition the asset as a higher-end workplace. The ownership believes in the building's quality, irreplaceable location, and long-term potential, aiming to enhance its appeal in a market increasingly favoring premium office spaces.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Property Name: 1625 Eye Street

Address: 1625 Eye Street NW, Washington, D.C.

Size: 100,000 SF

Notable Features: Building features a nine-story atrium and 425-space parking garage. Planned renovations include a redesigned lobby, upgraded security, retail from LDV Hospitality, a rooftop with indoor and outdoor gathering areas, a conference and event facility, and an expanded tenant fitness center.

Project Notes: The building was acquired in 2025 by the current ownership group. O'Melveny & Myers has occupied space in the building since 2003.
