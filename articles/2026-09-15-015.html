Buffalo Grove’s PREMIER Design + Build Group adds project executive

REJournals — Tue, 15 Sep 2026 09:31:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Promotion

PREMIER Design + Build Group, based in Buffalo Grove, Illinois, has appointed Charles Worrell as Project Executive. In this role, Worrell will focus on expanding the company's market presence and strengthening client relationships, particularly within its Connecticut team. He brings over two decades of experience in construction and development, having successfully delivered significant projects across various sectors, including residential, mixed-use, commercial, and hospitality.
