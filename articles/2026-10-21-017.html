Newmark Affiliate Arranges $369M Financing for 11 Florida Affordable Housing Properties

LOAN

Connect CRE — Wed, 21 Oct 2026 14:01:54 +0000

SUMMARY

Newmark arranged a $369 million Fannie Mae credit facility for April Housing, a Blackstone portfolio company, to support the long-term preservation of an 11-property affordable housing portfolio across Florida. The financing included both fixed-rate and floating-rate draws, structured as an interest-only facility. The transaction involved navigating the complexities of 24 separate regulatory agreements for the properties.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Loan Amount: $369.0M

Notable Features: 11-property affordable housing portfolio

Project Notes: Financing structured as an interest-only Fannie Mae credit facility, consisting of a $221.4 million fixed-rate draw and a $147.6 million floating-rate draw. A buydown on the fixed-rate portion helped maximize proceeds.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

LENDER: Fannie Mae

MORTGAGE BROKER: Newmark Group — Evan Williams; Ian McIntire, Director; Seante Vang; Nicole Phillips

SPONSOR: April Housing

OWNER: Blackstone
