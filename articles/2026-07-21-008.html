Koontz Corp. Sells Two San Antonio Industrial Buildings Totaling 187,200 SF

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Mon, 20 Jul 2026 14:51:46 +0000

SUMMARY

Koontz Corp. has sold two industrial buildings, comprising Phase I of Westport Industrial Park in San Antonio, to Nuveen Real Estate. The 187,200-square-foot properties, built in 2023, were 79 percent leased at the time of sale. JLL brokered the transaction for the seller.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Property Name: Westport Industrial Park, Phase I

Address: 11725 Whisper Valley St, San Antonio, TX*

Size: 187,200 SF

Occupancy at Sale: 79.0%

Year Built: 2023

Site Area: 17 acres

Notable Features: Rear-load industrial buildings located on the city's northwest side.

Project Notes: The buildings comprise Phase I of a larger 374,400-square-foot development.
