Centerspace takes 48% hit on Denver apartment sale to Four Peaks

SALE

The Real Deal — National — Tue, 07 Jul 2026 16:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Centerspace Homes sold the Civic Lofts apartment building in Denver's Golden Triangle for $30 million to an LLC linked to Four Peaks Multifamily Partners. This sale represents a 48% discount from its 2021 purchase price of $63 million and is part of Centerspace's strategy to divest $240-$245 million in properties to reduce debt. The acquisition marks Four Peaks Multifamily Partners' entry into the Denver city market.
