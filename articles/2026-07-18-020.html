European hedge fund Brevan Howard leases Miami office amid flock of newcomers

LEASE

The Real Deal — Miami — Fri, 17 Jul 2026 22:55:40 +0000

SUMMARY

European hedge fund Brevan Howard has leased nearly 7,000 square feet at The Related Group's Coconut Grove office building in Miami, marking its expansion into the South Florida market. This transaction is part of a broader trend of new-to-market firms establishing a presence in Miami, including a Swiss packaging firm and a U.S. consultancy. Despite the influx of new companies, many are initially taking smaller spaces, and the overall office market still faces a significant vacancy rate.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Miami-Dade's Class A office vacancy rate was 15.7% in Q2, a 10 basis point drop quarter-over-quarter and 130 basis points year-over-year. Class B vacancy was 14%, a decrease of 90 basis points quarter-over-quarter and 250 basis points year-over-year. Miami had the highest office asking rent nationwide at $59.66 per square foot, surpassing New York's $50.56 per square foot.
