Midtown Office Tower Scores Leases Totaling 182K SF

LEASE

Connect CRE — Tue, 01 Sep 2026 22:22:09 +0000

SUMMARY

Three companies have signed new leases totaling over 182,000 square feet at 1411 Broadway, a Midtown office tower owned by The Swig Company. The transactions include a software company doubling its footprint and relocating its headquarters, a South Korean beauty group moving its headquarters, and an existing subtenant converting to a direct lease. These deals follow a significant $100 million renovation of the property, which is expected to boost occupancy to 90% by year-end.
