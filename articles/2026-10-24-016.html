Lewis Group Opens Second NorCal Rental-Home Community in West Sacramento

DEVELOPMENT

Connect CRE — Fri, 23 Oct 2026 21:10:34 +0000

SUMMARY

Lewis Apartments has opened Independence in West Sacramento, a new gated community featuring 208 townhome-style rental homes. This marks the developer's second such community in Northern California, following a successful launch in Dixon. The project aims to provide residents with the spaciousness and privacy of a single-family home, coupled with the flexibility and reduced responsibilities of renting.

DATA POINTS

Property Name: Independence in West Sacramento
