JLL Negotiates Sale of 323,949 SF Office Campus in North San Antonio

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Tue, 11 Aug 2026 14:49:23 +0000

SUMMARY

JLL has brokered the sale of Union Square, a two-building office campus totaling 323,949 square feet in North San Antonio. A partnership led by SynerMark Properties and including Tryperion Holdings acquired the property from a joint venture led by Worth & Associates. The campus was approximately 95 percent leased at the time of the transaction.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Property Name: Union Square

Address: 10101 Reunion Pl, San Antonio, TX*

Size: 323,949 SF

Occupancy at Sale: 95.0%

Notable Features: Two-building campus with one building constructed in 1986 (10 floors, 192,763 SF) and the other in 2006 (6 floors, 131,186 SF).

* Address sourced separately, not in original article.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE
