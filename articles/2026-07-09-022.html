Affinius ramping up lending prominence in Garden State

REFINANCE

The Real Deal — New York — Wed, 08 Jul 2026 21:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

A joint venture between PCCP, Claremont Development, and Stanbery Development Group secured a $188 million refinancing loan from Affinius Capital for the District 15Fifteen complex in Parsippany, New Jersey. The loan will support the lease-up and stabilization of the recently opened mixed-use property, which features 498 multifamily units and 59,000 square feet of retail space. An Affinius Capital executive noted the property's thoughtful design and robust amenities in a supply-constrained, transit-oriented submarket as key factors in their financing decision.
