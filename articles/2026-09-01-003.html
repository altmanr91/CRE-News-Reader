Butlr’s New Thermal Sensor Products Aim at Occupancy and Restrooms

Commercial Observer — Tue, 01 Sep 2026 14:00:11 +0000

SUMMARY

Non-CRE / Business News

Butlr, a physical artificial intelligence technology company, has launched two new products, Ask Butlr and its Smart Cleaning module, aimed at optimizing building operations. Ask Butlr is an AI-powered analyst tool that provides building operators with data-backed insights on occupancy, dwell time, foot traffic, and energy consumption, facilitating strategic space planning and capital real estate investments. The Smart Cleaning module, integrated into Butlr's spatial intelligence platform, utilizes anonymized usage data to reduce restroom cleaning costs by directing staff to areas that require attention, moving away from fixed maintenance schedules. These solutions leverage thermal body heat sensing technology and machine learning to infer human behavior without compromising privacy, addressing a growing need for accurate first-party data in buildings.
