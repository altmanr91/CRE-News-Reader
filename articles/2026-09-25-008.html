Stockman Development Receives $482.5M in Financing for Ski Resort in Steamboat Springs, Colorado

LOAN

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 25 Sep 2026 13:32:59 +0000

SUMMARY

Stockman Development, led by Marquee Development, has secured $482.5 million in financing from GoldenTree Asset Management for The Stockman, Auberge Collection, a luxury ski resort and residential project in Steamboat Springs. The project, which recently broke ground, has already seen strong early residential demand, with over 25 percent of its residences under contract since sales launched earlier this summer. This financing will advance the development of the ski-in, ski-out property, which is slated for a 2030 opening.
