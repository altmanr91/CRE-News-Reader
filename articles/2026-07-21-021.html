New dev deals sink as Manhattan luxury contends with shrinking pipeline

The Real Deal — New York — Mon, 20 Jul 2026 16:40:47 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Luxury Residential

Manhattan's luxury new development market is experiencing a significant slowdown in contract signings, with only 12 deals for units asking $4 million or more inked in the last four weeks, a substantial decrease from the decade average of 28 for the same period. This decline is attributed to a shrinking new development pipeline, which has led to a 62 percent reduction in new construction inventory over the past year. Despite the drop in new development supply, the broader Manhattan luxury market still saw 27 signed deals for homes asking $4 million or more in mid-July, totaling $205 million, with an average price of $7.6 million.
