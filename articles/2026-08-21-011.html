Pullman Gateway mixed-use development reaches full occupancy in Chicago’s Pullman neighborhood

REJournals — Fri, 21 Aug 2026 09:13:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Development

The Pullman Gateway mixed-use development in Chicago's Pullman neighborhood has reached full occupancy, marking a significant milestone in the area's economic revitalization. The eight-acre project, developed by Chicago Neighborhood Initiatives and Hope Center Foundation, features four national restaurant brands and offices for the Chicago Transit Authority's Red Line Extension. This completion coincides with the announcement of the new Pullman Park Retail Center, which will bring additional dining options to the community, further expanding amenities and job creation in the rapidly growing commercial district.
