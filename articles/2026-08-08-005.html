Selldorf Architects Renews 14K-SF 860 Broadway Lease for 10 Years

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Fri, 07 Aug 2026 17:36:42 +0000

SUMMARY

Selldorf Architects has renewed its 13,815-square-foot office lease at 860 Broadway in Manhattan's Flatiron District for another 10 years. The architecture firm has occupied space in the building for two decades, a testament to the property's enduring appeal and the strength of the Midtown South market, according to the landlord's broker. The landlord emphasized their long-term ownership strategy and commitment to creating an environment where companies choose to remain and expand.
