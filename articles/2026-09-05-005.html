Developer Ailanthus Files Plans to Build 400 Apartments at 424 Hoyt Street in Gowanus

DEVELOPMENT

Commercial Observer — Fri, 04 Sep 2026 15:30:15 +0000

SUMMARY

Developer Ailanthus has filed a rezoning application to construct a 17-story, mixed-use residential development at 424 Hoyt Street in Gowanus, Brooklyn. The project is planned to include 400 apartment units, with 100 to 120 designated as affordable, alongside a 46,000-square-foot public school and 14,000 square feet of commercial retail space. This development aligns with New York City's broader housing goals to address the significant need for new housing units, particularly affordable homes, in areas like Gowanus.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: mixed-use

Address: 424 Hoyt Street, New York City, NY

Units: 400

Notable Features: Proposed 17-story, 175-foot-tall building with a public school and commercial retail space.

Project Notes: The 2.1-acre site was previously acquired by Two Trees Management in July 2008 for $7.7 million. Ailanthus has a ground lease at the site from Two Trees Management. The property currently has a one-story automotive repair building and vehicle storage facility.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

DEVELOPER/SPONSOR: Ailanthus — Ofer Cohen; Vivian Liao; Tucker Reed

OWNER: Two Trees Management
