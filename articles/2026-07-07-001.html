Development Firm InvestMates Buys Greenpoint Retail Building for $26M

ACQUISITION

Commercial Observer — Tue, 07 Jul 2026 11:00:26 +0000

SUMMARY

InvestMates has acquired a single-story retail building in Greenpoint, Brooklyn, for approximately $26 million from Green Art Holdings. The development firm plans to demolish the existing structure and construct a 73-unit condominium building with ground-floor retail. The acquisition was financed with a $21 million loan from Northwind Group.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: retail

Address: 150 Green Street, New York City, NY

Sale Price: $26.0M

Loan Amount: $21.0M

Notable Features: Plans to redevelop into a 73-unit condominium building with ground-floor retail.

Project Notes: The 2.6-acre site was previously acquired for $18 million in 2017.
