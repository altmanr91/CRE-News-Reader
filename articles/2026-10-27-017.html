Embrey, Southstar Close on $71M Construction Loan for Nashville-Area Apartment Project

LOAN

Connect CRE — Tue, 27 Oct 2026 14:09:20 +0000

SUMMARY

A joint venture between Embrey Partners and Southstar Development secured a $71 million senior construction loan from PCCP for the development of Crossings Boulevard, a 360-unit apartment community in Spring Hill, TN. The Class A, garden-style project is part of a larger 213-acre master-planned community and is expected to be completed in Q1 2028. The development will offer a strong amenity package, including a fitness studio, pool, and pet-friendly features, catering to the growing demand in the Nashville-area market.
