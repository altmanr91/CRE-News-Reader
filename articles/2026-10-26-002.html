Dolce & Gabbana Inks 21K-SF Office Lease at Feil’s 551 Fifth Avenue

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Mon, 26 Oct 2026 15:44:57 +0000

SUMMARY

Luxury Italian fashion brand Dolce & Gabbana is relocating its New York City office headquarters to 551 Fifth Avenue in Midtown Manhattan, signing a long-term lease for 21,000 square feet. The move places the fashion house in a premier Midtown location, reflecting the landlord's strategy to attract a diverse mix of leading companies to the property. The building continues to evolve, drawing dynamic tenants seeking a prime Fifth Avenue address.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Property Name: 551 Fifth Avenue

Address: 551 Fifth Avenue, New York City, NY

Size: 21,000 SF

Rental Rate: $65.00/SF/yr

Notable Features: Located at the corner of Fifth Avenue and 45th Street, three blocks north of the New York Public Library.
