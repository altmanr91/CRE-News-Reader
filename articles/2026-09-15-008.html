MMCC Arranges $53M Refinancing for Build-to-Rent Property in Rogers, Arkansas

REFINANCE

REBusinessOnline — Tue, 15 Sep 2026 13:35:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Marcus & Millichap Capital Corp. (MMCC) has secured a $53 million nonrecourse refinancing loan for The Grove, a 235-unit build-to-rent residential community in Rogers, Arkansas. The three-year loan was arranged for a joint venture between Brittenum Group and Realty Capital Partners. The property, built in 2024, was 94.5 percent occupied at the time of the financing.
