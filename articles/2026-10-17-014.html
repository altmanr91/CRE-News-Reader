Rich Development Trades Newly Built Shopping Center

SALE

Connect CRE — Fri, 16 Oct 2026 21:49:23 +0000

SUMMARY

Rich Development Enterprises has sold the newly constructed Apple Valley Towne Center, an 87,042-square-foot regional shopping center in Apple Valley, CA. The property, completed in 2026, was fully leased to three national junior box tenants at the time of sale. The marketing strategy, which began before tenants opened, generated significant interest from a diverse pool of investors by offering the assets individually and as a package.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: retail

Property Name: Apple Valley Towne Center

Address: 19390 Bear Valley Rd., Apple Valley, CA

Size: 87,042 SF

Occupancy at Sale: 100.0%

Year Built: 2026

Notable Features: Shadow anchored by Stater Bros. Markets, Lowe’s Home Improvement, and Walgreens.

Project Notes: Features new long-term triple-net leases with scheduled rental increases every five years.
