Congressional committee eyes Compass partnership with Chicago MLS

The Real Deal — National — Wed, 22 Jul 2026 22:03:22 +0000

SUMMARY

CRE Legal / Regulatory

A Congressional subcommittee has requested a briefing from Compass CEO Robert Reffkin and Midwest Real Estate Data (MRED) CEO Rebecca Jensen regarding MRED's business practices and its partnership with Compass to implement its Private Listings Network. The subcommittee is investigating allegations of anticompetitive practices that could harm consumers by incentivizing brokers to push sellers towards private listings, potentially impacting housing affordability and creating conflicts of interest. This inquiry follows a federal antitrust lawsuit filed by Zillow against MRED and Compass, alleging a conspiracy to harm Zillow and force it to display Compass's private listings, which led to MRED cutting off Zillow's data feed in the Chicago area.
