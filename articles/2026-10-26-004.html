SASBs Dominate Conduits 3 to 1 in 2026 CMBS Market

Commercial Observer — Mon, 26 Oct 2026 12:55:53 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / CMBS Report

Single-asset, single-borrower (SASB) deals have dominated the private-label commercial mortgage-backed securities (CMBS) market in 2026, accounting for 76% of issuance through September. This trend reflects a shift in capital allocation, with money rotating away from office and retail properties towards apartments, hotels, and data centers. While conduits continue to finance a broad range of smaller, everyday collateral, SASB deals are increasingly focused on trophy assets and large portfolios, with seven major banks originating the majority of this volume.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

SASB deals accounted for 76% of 2026 private-label CMBS issuance through September, totaling $66 billion, compared to $21.2 billion for conduits. Combined volume of $87.2 billion is up 2% from the same period in 2025. SASB office volume fell to $12.5 billion from $22.4 billion a year earlier, and SASB retail halved to $5.1 billion. SASB multifamily rose to $10 billion (from $2.9 billion) and hotels to $11.3 billion (from $8.8 billion). Data centers reached $6.2 billion, comprising 9% of the SASB balance. Across both channels, multifamily's share more than doubled to 18% (from 8%), while retail fell to 9% (from 16%).
