You Better Hope Suri Kasirer Takes Your Call

Commercial Observer — Wed, 14 Oct 2026 10:00:17 +0000

SUMMARY

Feature / Profile

This article profiles Suri Kasirer, founder of Kasirer, a prominent government relations firm in New York City. The firm has been the highest-grossing lobbying firm in the city for 17 of the past 20 years, playing a critical role in numerous significant real estate developments and rezonings, including One Vanderbilt and the 5 Times Square office-to-residential conversion. Beyond large-scale projects, Kasirer is deeply committed to working with nearly 50 nonprofits, driven by her personal history as the daughter of a Holocaust survivor and her experiences with financial uncertainty during her upbringing. Her philanthropic efforts include successfully advocating for legislation to ensure faster payments from city contracts to nonprofits.
