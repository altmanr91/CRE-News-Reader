Multi-Tenant Corona del Mar Retail Sells Above Asking Price

SALE

Connect CRE — Wed, 19 Aug 2026 21:34:26 +0000

SUMMARY

A multi-tenant retail property located at 2515 E. Coast Highway in Corona del Mar has been sold for $13.7 million, exceeding its asking price by $700,000. The transaction, arranged by Coastal Commercial and Marcus & Millichap, attracted significant interest and multiple competitive bids from high-net-worth private investors in Orange County. The property's strong appeal was attributed to its prime frontage along Pacific Coast Highway, valuable onsite parking, and a configuration of smaller commercial spaces that offer potential for future rent maximization.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: retail

Address: 2515 E. Coast Highway, Corona del Mar, CA

Sale Price: $13.7M

Notable Features: Features incredible frontage along Pacific Coast Highway and approximately 20 onsite parking spaces.
