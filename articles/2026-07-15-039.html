Garden-Style Apartments Go for $78M in North County San Diego

SALE

Connect CRE — Tue, 14 Jul 2026 20:57:04 +0000

SUMMARY

29th Street Capital has sold Sunterra, a 240-unit garden-style multifamily community in Oceanside, CA, for $77.5 million. The property, built in 1975, is situated on 14.24 acres and offers a mix of amenities, with approximately 70% of its units already renovated. The sale attracted significant investor interest due to its scale and coastal location, presenting an opportunity in a supply-constrained market with strong fundamentals and sustained renter demand.
