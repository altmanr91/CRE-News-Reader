Membership Platform Patreon Renews 45K-SF Sublease at 75 Varick Street

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Fri, 04 Sep 2026 15:35:42 +0000

SUMMARY

Patreon, a membership and monetization platform, has renewed its sublease for 44,661 square feet at 75 Varick Street in Manhattan's Hudson Square. The building, also known as One Hudson Square, is a key asset within the Hudson Square Properties portfolio, a joint venture between Trinity Church Wall Street and Norges Bank Investment Management. The renewal reflects a broader trend of increased leasing activity and rising rents in the Hudson Square neighborhood, driven by the city's AI sector growth.
