Partnership Acquires 828,784 SF Office Campus in Houston’s Energy Corridor

ACQUISITION

REBusinessOnline — Wed, 04 Nov 2026 16:29:13 +0000

SUMMARY

A partnership between Cross Ocean Partners and Fuller Realty has acquired Eldridge Place, an 828,784-square-foot office campus located in Houston's Energy Corridor. The campus, which was 94% occupied at the time of sale, is anchored by a major engineering and construction firm and features a strong tenant mix. The property offers various amenities including a fitness center, pickleball courts, a deli, and a conference facility.
