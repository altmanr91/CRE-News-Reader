Cawley Commercial Real Estate brokers sale of 29,600-square-foot industrial property in Carol Stream

SALE

REJournals — Tue, 27 Oct 2026 09:56:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Cawley Commercial Real Estate recently brokered the direct sale of a 29,600-square-foot industrial property in Carol Stream, Illinois. High Street Logistics Properties acquired the Class B facility, which features a mix of warehouse, office, and specialized service space. The property's strategic location near Interstate 355 and its robust infrastructure, including multiple loading docks and drive-in doors, make it a valuable asset for industrial users in the western suburban market.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Address: 170 S. Gary Avenue, Carol Stream, IL

Size: 29,600 SF

Site Area: 1.9 acres

Notable Features: Class B industrial property with 24-foot clear ceiling heights, six interior loading docks with lifts, five interior drive-in doors, a mechanic repair bay, and a steel mezzanine. A full office renovation was completed in 2023.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: High Street Logistics Properties

SELLER BROKER: Cawley Commercial Real Estate — Mark Wilson; Daniel Cawley, SIOR; Jordan Kwiecinski
