Ad Agency Doubles Office HQ Space in Move to Hackman Campus in L.A.

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Fri, 07 Aug 2026 17:55:22 +0000

SUMMARY

An international advertising agency, Innocean USA, has signed a new headquarters lease for 101,000 square feet at Hackman Capital Partners' creative office campus in El Segundo, CA. The agency will relocate nearly 600 employees from its current 50,000-square-foot space in Huntington Beach once construction on the new facility is completed in 2027. The move represents a significant expansion and investment in creating an optimal environment for the company's future growth, with part of the space reportedly designated for commercial production.

Project Notes: Hackman Capital Partners acquired the former Northrop Grumman complex in 2016 and invested approximately $100 million in its conversion.
