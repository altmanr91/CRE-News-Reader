Cabot Cabot & Forbes Tops Off Construction at Woburn Innovation Center

CONSTRUCTION

Connect CRE — Mon, 14 Sep 2026 21:18:59 +0000

SUMMARY

Cabot, Cabot & Forbes (CC&F) has topped off construction at The Bolt, a 180,000-square-foot innovation center in Woburn, MA. This milestone marks significant progress in CC&F's $400 million redevelopment of a former Superfund site along the New Boston Corridor. The project is being built on spec and is expected to attract a diverse array of innovative companies to the region.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Property Name: The Bolt

Address: 216 New Boston St, Woburn, MA*
