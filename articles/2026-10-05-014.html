BGO acquires 11-property surgery center portfolio

ACQUISITION

REJournals — Mon, 05 Oct 2026 14:12:00 +0000

SUMMARY

BGO, on behalf of its U.S. Core Strategy, has acquired an 11-property portfolio of Class-A ambulatory surgery centers totaling approximately 223,000 square feet. The portfolio, which is 100% leased, was purchased from a joint venture between Flagship Healthcare Properties and AEW Capital Management. This acquisition expands BGO's healthcare real estate holdings with a geographically diversified collection of purpose-built outpatient surgical facilities across nine U.S. markets, reflecting BGO's confidence in the shift towards outpatient care delivery. Flagship Healthcare Properties will continue to provide property management services for the portfolio.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: medical office

Size: 223,000 SF

Notable Features: Purpose-built Class-A ambulatory surgery centers with specialized surgical infrastructure, established affiliations with major health systems, and relationships with national ASC operators. Clinical specialties include ophthalmology, orthopedics, gastroenterology, ENT, podiatry, plastic and reconstructive surgery, pain management, urology, and endoscopy.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE
