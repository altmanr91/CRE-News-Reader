Toys R Us Bets on Brick and Mortar With 120 New Stores

Commercial Observer — Mon, 28 Sep 2026 15:13:46 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Retail Expansion

WHP Global, the operator of Toys R Us, plans to open 120 temporary brick-and-mortar stores across the U.S. for the upcoming holiday season, in partnership with Go! Retail Group. These smaller-format locations, many with 12-month leases, aim to capitalize on a resurgence in U.S. toy sales and millennial/Gen Z nostalgia, offering experiential features like candy shops and event spaces. This expansion will bring the brand's total retail footprint to approximately 160 stores by year-end, supplementing its existing presence within Macy's department stores and a flagship location at American Dream Mall.

DATA POINTS

Total Toys R Us stores by year-end: Approximately 160

U.S. toy sales: Hit a six-year high earlier this year
