Azorim Secures $69M Construction Loan for Yonkers Development

LOAN

Commercial Observer — Thu, 02 Jul 2026 17:52:07 +0000

SUMMARY

Azorim has secured a $68.75 million construction loan for Miroza Tower 4, the final 14-story, 174-unit luxury apartment building within the Miroza at Ridge Hill master-planned development in Yonkers, NY. Western Alliance Bank provided the fixed-rate, interest-only financing, which was arranged by Walker & Dunlop. The project will complete the 520-unit residential complex, which is part of a larger live-work-play town square development.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Miroza Tower 4

Address: 599 Ridge Hill Boulevard, Yonkers, NY

Units: 174

Loan Amount: $68.8M

Loan Per Unit: $395,115/unit (calculated)

Notable Features: Part of the Miroza at Ridge Hill master-planned development, which includes a 25,000-square-foot amenity center with a swimming pool, basketball court, movie theater, and running track.

Project Notes: This is the fourth and final tower in a multiphase development. The first three towers opened in 2013, 2023, and 2024. The financing was optimized with a PILOT and market-rate bond structure.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

SPONSOR: Azorim

LENDER: Western Alliance Bank

MORTGAGE BROKER: Walker & Dunlop — Jonathan Zilber, Senior Managing Director of Capital Markets Real Estate Finance; Joel Chetner; Mackenzie Kerin

SPONSOR PIPELINE

Miroza Tower 2 and 3: Second and third towers of the Miroza development project, for which Walker & Dunlop arranged $145 million in financing in late 2025.
