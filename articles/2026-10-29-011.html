Pinecroft Realty acquires 40,268-square-foot medical office building in Bryan

ACQUISITION

REJournals — Thu, 29 Oct 2026 15:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Pinecroft Realty has acquired the Brazos Valley Medical Office Building, a 40,268-square-foot, 100% leased medical office property in Bryan, Texas, from a local investor. The two-story building, developed in 2015, is strategically located near popular neighborhoods, hospitals, and shopping, and is part of the Physicians Centre Hospital campus. This acquisition aligns with Pinecroft's strategy of partnering with local physicians and expands its presence in the growing Bryan market.
