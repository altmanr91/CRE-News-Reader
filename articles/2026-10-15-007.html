JLL Arranges $210M Refinancing for Grand Hyatt Indian Wells Resort & Villas in California

REFINANCE

REBusinessOnline — Thu, 15 Oct 2026 13:42:49 +0000

SUMMARY

JLL Capital Markets has arranged a $210 million refinancing package for the Grand Hyatt Indian Wells Resort & Villas in Indian Wells, California. The five-year, floating-rate SASB CMBS loan was secured through Citi for the borrower, Trinity Investments. The luxury resort, situated on 45 acres, recently underwent a $62 million renovation and conversion to the Grand Hyatt brand.
