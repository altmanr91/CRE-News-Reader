Irgens starts construction on 204-unit apartment community in Wauwatosa

CONSTRUCTION

REJournals — Tue, 04 Aug 2026 14:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Irgens has commenced construction on Viridia Apartments, a 204-unit market-rate apartment community in Wauwatosa, marking the company's entry into multifamily development. The four-story project is situated at a prominent address on West Wisconsin Avenue.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Viridia Apartments

Address: 10600 W. Wisconsin Ave., Wauwatosa, WI

Units: 204

Notable Features: Four-story market-rate apartment community.

Project Notes: This is Irgens' first multifamily development.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

DEVELOPER/SPONSOR: Irgens
