Ryan Companies celebrates opening of multifamily project on former 122-acre site of Ford assembly plant in Saint Paul

DEVELOPMENT

REJournals — Thu, 01 Oct 2026 16:05:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Ryan Companies US, Inc., in partnership with the Housing and Redevelopment Authority for the City of Saint Paul, celebrated the grand opening of The Albert, a 97-unit multifamily building with ground-floor retail, and the adjacent Civic Square at Highland Bridge in Saint Paul, MN. This mixed-use block, part of the larger 122-acre Highland Bridge development on the former Ford assembly plant site, also includes three standalone retail buildings and a daycare center. The project represents a significant step in the city's vision for a connected, sustainable urban community, integrating historic elements of the original Ford plant facade and frieze panels into its design.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: mixed-use

Property Name: The Albert and Civic Square at Highland Bridge

Size: 22,000 SF

Units: 97

Completion: September 2026

Site Area: 2.97 acres

Notable Features: Incorporates the preserved, historic 1920s Ford Motor Company assembly plant facade and frieze panels; designed by Albert Kahn; features LEED certification goals, solar-ready roofs, EV charging stations, and car-sharing accommodations.

Project Notes: Part of the 122-acre Highland Bridge master-planned development on the former Ford Motor Company Twin Cities assembly plant site; supported by Tax Increment Financing (TIF) from existing TIF district budget savings.
