La La Land Cafe Signs with Brixton Capital for First San Diego Location

LEASE

Connect CRE — Tue, 11 Aug 2026 21:26:44 +0000

SUMMARY

La La Land Cafe has signed a lease with Brixton Capital for its first San Diego location, taking 1,168 square feet of interior space with additional terrace seating at the Del Mar Plaza retail destination. This marks a significant expansion for the dynamic brand, which has grown rapidly since its founding in 2019, and is expected to become a popular social hub.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: retail

Property Name: Del Mar Plaza

Address: 1555 Camino Del Mar, San Diego, CA*

Size: 1,168 SF

Notable Features: Mezzanine terrace and café space with additional sitting and queueing space extending onto the terrace deck.

Project Notes: Space formerly occupied by Pacifica Breeze Café.

* Address sourced separately, not in original article.
