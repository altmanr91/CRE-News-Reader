'We Dodged A Bullet': Mass. Multifamily Market Starts To Thaw After Rent Control Ruling

Bisnow News Feed — Wed, 01 Jul 2026 16:17:46 -0400

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

Following a Massachusetts Supreme Judicial Court ruling that blocked a statewide rent control ballot measure, the multifamily investment market in Massachusetts is showing signs of recovery. Industry experts and brokers report an immediate uptick in deal conversations, signed contracts, and renewed investor confidence, which had been significantly dampened by the prospect of the restrictive policy. While the ruling has thawed the market, participants are proceeding with cautious optimism, acknowledging that rent control advocates are likely to reintroduce a revised measure in the future. The initial proposal would have capped rent increases at 5% or the CPI, whichever was lower, and its potential passage had led to a substantial decline in multifamily sales volume across the state.
