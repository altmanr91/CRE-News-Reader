Independence Realty Trust, Centerspace to Merge for $8.1B Apartment REIT

Connect CRE — Wed, 09 Sep 2026 11:21:26 +0000

SUMMARY

Non-CRE / Business News

Independence Realty Trust (IRT) and Centerspace have agreed to an all-stock merger, creating an $8.1 billion multifamily REIT focused on high-growth, non-gateway markets. The combined entity will own and operate 163 communities totaling 44,434 units across 17 states, with a significant portion of its net operating income derived from Sunbelt markets. This strategic combination aims to leverage the strengths of both portfolios, pairing IRT's Sunbelt assets with Centerspace's Midwest and Mountain West communities, to achieve greater operational efficiency and expand value-add renovation programs.
