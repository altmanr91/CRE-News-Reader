Colliers brokers sale of two industrial facilities in Columbus market

ACQUISITION

REJournals — Thu, 05 Nov 2026 17:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

A private investor has acquired two Class-A industrial facilities totaling 404,088 square feet in the Columbus, Ohio metropolitan area. The properties, located in Canal Winchester and Commercial Point, were developed by Tenby Partners in 2023 and were fully leased at the time of sale. The transactions underscore the sustained investor demand for modern industrial assets in Central Ohio, driven by robust transportation infrastructure and access to key distribution corridors.

DATA POINTS

Notable Features: Both properties benefit from strategic locations within Central Ohio's industrial corridors, offering connectivity to the regional interstate network and major freight infrastructure, including proximity to Rickenbacker International Airport, the Rickenbacker Inland Port, and the Norfolk Southern Intermodal Terminal.
