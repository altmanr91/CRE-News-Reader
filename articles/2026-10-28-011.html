Baldwin Supply tripling size of Sioux Falls branch

CONSTRUCTION

REJournals — Wed, 28 Oct 2026 15:13:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Baldwin Supply, an industrial components distributor, is significantly expanding its Sioux Falls, SD branch, tripling its size to over 50,000 square feet. This investment aims to enhance capacity for customers across South Dakota and neighboring states, strengthening a key hub in its Midwest network. The expansion will include additional warehouse and office space, a dedicated customer training center, and an indoor truck pull-through with an overhead crane system, improving operational efficiency and customer service.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Address: 908 E. Amidon Street, Sioux Falls, SD

Size: 50,000 SF

Completion: early 2027

Notable Features: Includes additional warehouse and office space, a dedicated customer training center, and a new indoor truck pull-through with an overhead crane system.

Project Notes: The expansion builds on 25 years of growth in the Sioux Falls market.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

OWNER: Baldwin Supply

GENERAL CONTRACTOR: Reynolds Construction Management
