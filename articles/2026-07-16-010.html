Automotive Fluid Distributor to Invest $170M for Metro Atlanta Distribution Center

LEASE

REBusinessOnline — Wed, 15 Jul 2026 14:27:58 +0000

SUMMARY

Highline Warren, a distributor of automotive and industrial fluids, is investing $170 million to establish a new 1.1 million-square-foot logistics hub in McDonough, GA. The company will occupy the former Zinus building and plans to commence operations by year-end, creating 160 new jobs. This expansion reflects the company's strategic growth and commitment to the metro Atlanta region.
