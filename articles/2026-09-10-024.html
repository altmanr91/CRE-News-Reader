Solar Projects In Georgia Meet Local Backlash As Data Centers Drive Demand For Power

Bisnow Latest News Feed — Wed, 09 Sep 2026 19:55:57 -0400

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

Solar energy projects in Georgia are encountering significant local resistance, primarily due to the increasing power demands from new data centers. This surge in demand for electricity is prompting the development of more solar farms, which in turn are raising concerns among local communities regarding their impact on land use and the environment.
