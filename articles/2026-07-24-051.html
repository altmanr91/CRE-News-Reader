Pennsylvania Data Center Boom Skips The Lehigh Valley

Bisnow News Feed — Thu, 23 Jul 2026 15:48:01 -0400

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

The Lehigh Valley, despite its robust power infrastructure inherited from a manufacturing past, is not emerging as a significant hub for data center development. This is primarily due to a scarcity of available development sites, elevated land valuations, and increasing community opposition during the entitlement process. While some smaller warehouse-to-data center conversions are occurring, the region's industrial market is instead attracting manufacturing-oriented tenants requiring substantial power for producing components that support data centers elsewhere.
