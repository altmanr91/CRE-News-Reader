JLL Arranges $86.7M Sale of 323,113 SF Shopping Center in Metro Raleigh

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Thu, 17 Sep 2026 13:33:07 +0000

SUMMARY

JLL has facilitated the $86.7 million sale of Knightdale Marketplace, a 323,113-square-foot super regional shopping center in Knightdale, North Carolina. Invesco Real Estate acquired the 43.5-acre property from a joint venture between affiliates of Bailard Inc. and M&J Wilkow Ltd. The center was fully leased at the time of sale, featuring a strong tenant mix and shadow-anchored by major retailers.
