StageLynk Launches AI Booking Platform for Film and TV Stages

Commercial Observer — Tue, 20 Oct 2026 13:00:50 +0000

SUMMARY

Non-CRE / Business News

StageLynk, a Los Angeles-based startup with an office in Chicago, has launched an AI-powered platform designed to streamline the booking and production planning process for film and television soundstages. The platform, which includes an AI production assistant called StageScout, connects studio owners with production companies by offering a searchable directory of nearly 17 million square feet across 1,200 soundstages in major production hubs. It helps production teams evaluate factors beyond just the stage, such as tax incentives, permitting, union considerations, and local amenities, while also serving as a marketing and lead-generation tool for studios to manage bookings and increase visibility in a competitive market.

DATA POINTS

Number of soundstages: 1,200
