New Apartment Building in Berkeley Heights Live-Work Campus Opens Doors

DEVELOPMENT

Connect CRE — Wed, 02 Sep 2026 22:02:12 +0000

SUMMARY

The Connell Company has opened RT500, a new 179-unit luxury apartment building within its 185-acre mixed-use campus known as The Park in Berkeley Heights, NJ. The 11-story residential property is part of a larger $500 million redevelopment of a former office campus. Greystar is handling the leasing and management for the community, which offers a variety of unit types and high-end amenities designed to integrate residents' home, work, wellness, and social lives.
