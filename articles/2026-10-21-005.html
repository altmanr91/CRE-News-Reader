Forbright Bank Provides $50M for Southern California R&D Campus

LOAN

Commercial Observer — Tue, 20 Oct 2026 20:25:24 +0000

SUMMARY

Alta West Partners has secured $49.8 million in bridge financing from Forbright Bank for the Lakeview Innovation Center, a fully leased research and development and office campus in Thousand Oaks, CA. The three-year, floating-rate loan follows Alta West's two-year repositioning of the asset, which resulted in significant net operating income growth through strategic leasing and capital improvements.

Project Notes: Alta West Partners acquired the campus in September 2024 and achieved over 170 percent growth in net operating income through leasing and capital improvements.
