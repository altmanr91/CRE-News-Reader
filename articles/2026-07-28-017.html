Northmarq Arranges Refi for Grocery-Anchored Queens Retail

REFINANCE

Connect CRE — Mon, 27 Jul 2026 21:57:31 +0000

SUMMARY

Northmarq's New York Metro Debt + Equity team has arranged a $15 million refinancing for the Atlantic Plaza Shopping Center, a 34,949-square-foot grocery-anchored retail center in Queens, NY. The financing was secured on behalf of owner Schuckman Realty Inc. through a commercial bank. The transaction highlights the continued strength and lender confidence in well-located, daily-needs retail properties within New York City's boroughs.
