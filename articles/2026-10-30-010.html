SEG Solar Acquires Two Manufacturing Buildings Totaling 1.1 MSF in Tomball, Texas

ACQUISITION

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 30 Oct 2026 14:32:04 +0000

SUMMARY

SEG Solar has acquired two manufacturing buildings totaling 1.1 million square feet within Northwest 99 Business Park in Tomball, a northwestern suburb of Houston. Building 2, spanning 450,320 square feet, is being converted into a solar module manufacturing facility, while the 704,080-square-foot Building 3 will support distribution operations. This acquisition expands SEG Solar's presence in the park, following its purchase of Building 1 in 2025.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Property Name: Northwest 99 Business Park (Buildings 2 & 3)

Size: 1,154,400 SF

Notable Features: Buildings 2 and 3 are under construction, with Building 2 planned for conversion into a solar module manufacturing facility and Building 3 for distribution operations.

Project Notes: SEG Solar previously acquired Building 1, a 425,360-square-foot facility, within the same business park in 2025.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: SEG Solar

OWNER: Clay Development

BUYER BROKER: Colliers — Jon Lindenberger; Barkley Peschel
