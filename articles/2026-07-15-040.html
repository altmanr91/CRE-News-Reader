Related Beal Plans Complete Upgrade, Renovation of Salem Affordable Housing

CONSTRUCTION

Connect CRE — Tue, 14 Jul 2026 20:26:19 +0000

SUMMARY

Related Beal, in partnership with Related Affordable, the Commonwealth of Massachusetts, and MassHousing, is undertaking a comprehensive $38 million renovation of the 250-unit Loring Towers affordable housing community in Salem, MA. The multi-phased project, set to begin in August and conclude by 2028, will extend the property's affordability through 2056, demonstrating a long-term commitment to the community and its residents. The renovation includes in-unit upgrades, common area improvements, and new amenities like a basketball court and children's playground.
