Adirondack Capital Partners Arranges $10.1M Sale of Office Complex in Albany, New York

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Thu, 03 Sep 2026 12:56:04 +0000

SUMMARY

Adirondack Capital Partners has arranged the $10.1 million sale of Omni Plaza, a 415,000-square-foot office complex in downtown Albany, NY. Omni Development sold the two-building property to Jankow Cos. The new owner plans to undertake capital improvements to one building and convert the other into a 120-unit apartment complex.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Property Name: Omni Plaza

Address: 30 S. Pearl St. / 54 State St., Albany, NY

Size: 415,000 SF

Sale Price: $10.1M

Notable Features: Consists of two interconnected buildings totaling 236,000 SF and 179,000 SF.

Project Notes: The buyer plans to convert one building into a 120-unit apartment complex and invest in capital improvements for the other.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

SELLER: Omni Development

BUYER: Jankow Cos.

SELLER BROKER: Adirondack Capital Partners — Michael Hunter Coghill; Jacob Brenner
