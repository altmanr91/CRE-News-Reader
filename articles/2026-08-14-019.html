NAI Capital Lists Owner-User Opportunity in Pasadena’s Playhouse Village District

Connect CRE — Thu, 13 Aug 2026 21:39:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Property Listing / For Sale

NAI Capital Commercial is marketing a 99,280-square-foot, eight-story office building in Pasadena's Playhouse Village District for sale. The property, built in 1924 and currently vacant, is being presented as an owner-user opportunity. Brokers note that the building's scale, parking, and transit access, combined with a central location on Colorado Boulevard, offer a significant long-term presence in a market where owner-user acquisitions have already tightened office inventory.
