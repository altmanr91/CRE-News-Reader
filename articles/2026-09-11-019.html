Diptyque Signs Full-Floor Lease in North American Headquarters Move

LEASE

Connect CRE — Thu, 10 Sep 2026 20:37:21 +0000

SUMMARY

Diptyque, a global luxury brand, has signed a 10-year, full-floor lease for 18,017 square feet at 16 E. 34th St. in Murray Hill, New York City, for its new North American headquarters. This move, expected in the first quarter of 2027, will more than double the company's current footprint. The building's recent renovations, including a reimagined lobby and roof deck, were cited as key attractions for the luxury brand.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Address: 16 E. 34th St., New York City, NY

Size: 18,017 SF

Notable Features: Newly renovated office and retail building with a reimagined lobby and roof deck.

Project Notes: Diptyque will relocate from 240-246 W. 35th St. and more than double its previous 8,097-square-foot footprint.
