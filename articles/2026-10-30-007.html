CBRE Arranges $81M Construction Loan for Multifamily Development in Charlotte

LOAN

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 30 Oct 2026 15:02:16 +0000

SUMMARY

CBRE's Debt & Structured Finance team has secured an $81 million construction loan from Western-Southern Life Insurance Co. for the development of Sutherland at Piper Glen, a 299-unit multifamily project in Charlotte, NC. The borrower is a consortium of investors led by RK Investors, who is also the developer. This project will be a key residential component of the larger Greenway at Piper Glen master-planned development.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Sutherland at Piper Glen

Address: 9621 Elm Lane, Charlotte, NC

Units: 299

Loan Amount: $81.0M

Loan Per Unit: $270,903/unit (calculated)

Completion: March 2028

Notable Features: Four five-story apartment buildings atop structured parking, with amenities including a fitness center, coworking spaces, golf simulator, swimming pool, and pet wash station.

Project Notes: The property is the multifamily component of the Greenway at Piper Glen master-planned development.
