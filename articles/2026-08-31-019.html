Longtime Holder Sells Doral Warehouse Portfolio for $86M

ACQUISITION

Connect CRE — Mon, 31 Aug 2026 15:03:59 +0000

SUMMARY

Midtown Capital Partners has acquired a 14-property industrial portfolio in Doral, FL, for $86 million. The portfolio, totaling 300,000 square feet, consists of warehouses ranging from 8,000 to 25,000 square feet, with the oldest building constructed in 1973. The sale reflects strong investor interest in small-bay industrial properties, driven by tightening supply and robust demand from local businesses, which has also contributed to rising rents in the market.

DATA POINTS

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Small-scale warehouses in the Doral market have vacancy rates near 4%, while larger warehouses have double that vacancy rate.
