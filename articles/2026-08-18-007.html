Haven Housing Acquires 264-Unit Apartment Complex in Pflugerville, Texas

ACQUISITION

REBusinessOnline — Mon, 17 Aug 2026 14:53:49 +0000

SUMMARY

California-based investment firm Haven Housing has acquired Wells Park Apartments, a 264-unit multifamily complex in Pflugerville, Texas. Built in 2018, the property features a strong amenity package including a pool, fitness center, and resident clubhouse. The transaction details, including the seller and sales price, were not disclosed.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Wells Park Apartments

Units: 264

Year Built: 2018

Notable Features: Amenities include a pool, fitness center, resident clubhouse, business center, and outdoor gathering spaces.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: Haven Housing

BUYER BROKER: Walker & Dunlop — Kevin Dufour; Matt Pohl; Spencer Roy; Forrest Bass
