A calmer U.S. industrial market? That’s what Colliers has found

REJournals — Tue, 21 Jul 2026 10:02:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Industrial Report

Colliers' latest U.S. industrial report indicates a stabilizing market where demand is now outpacing new supply, leading to a slight decrease in the national vacancy rate to 7.3% in Q2 2026. This marks a significant shift after two years of increasing vacancies, with 63% of tracked markets showing stable or declining rates. Leasing demand strengthened considerably, with net absorption nearly doubling year-over-year to 59 million square feet, predominantly in the South. New deliveries have slowed to their lowest quarterly total since 2016, helping to rebalance the market, though the construction pipeline is growing again, reaching 312 million square feet. Rental rates are also stabilizing, with average asking rents at $10.34 per square foot, reflecting modest corrections in some coastal markets.
