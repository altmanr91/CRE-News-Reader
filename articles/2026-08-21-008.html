Marcus & Millichap Brokers Sale of 689-Unit Self-Storage Facility in Decatur, Texas

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Thu, 20 Aug 2026 15:13:30 +0000

SUMMARY

Marcus & Millichap has brokered the sale of Decatur Self Storage, a 689-unit self-storage facility in Decatur, Texas. The property, which offers 121,650 net rentable square feet across 14 buildings, was approximately 94 percent occupied at the time of sale. SW Group, a Houston-based private investment firm, acquired the asset from an undisclosed family-owned operator.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: self-storage

Property Name: Decatur Self Storage

Size: 121,650 SF

Occupancy at Sale: 94.0%

Year Built: 1980

Site Area: 19.8 acres

Notable Features: Mix of climate-controlled spaces, drive-up storage units, covered parking, and specialty storage options.

Project Notes: Expanded in 2018.
