Blanca Signs 117,764 SF of Office Leases at Waterford Business District in Miami

LEASE

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 11 Sep 2026 13:13:13 +0000

SUMMARY

Blanca Commercial Real Estate has completed 15 office lease transactions totaling 117,764 square feet at the Waterford Business District in Miami. The deals include both new leases and renewals with a diverse group of tenants from various sectors, including life sciences, logistics, and professional services. The office campus, owned by a joint venture between Nuveen Real Estate and PIMCO Prime Real Estate, has recently undergone significant renovations to enhance its amenities and tenant experience.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Property Name: Waterford Business District

Address: 5200 Waterford District Dr, Miami, FL*

Size: 117,764 SF

Notable Features: 250-acre office campus adjacent to Miami International Airport, spanning over 3 million square feet across seven buildings. Recent renovations include redesigned lobbies, upgraded outdoor environments, a conference center, waterfront gathering spaces, fitness facilities, and collaborative tenant lounges.
