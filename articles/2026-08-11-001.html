Simon Raises 2026 Expectations as Leasing, Development Pipeline Accelerate

Commercial Observer — Mon, 10 Aug 2026 23:35:07 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Earnings Report

Simon Property Group, the nation's largest mall owner, reported strong second-quarter results for 2026, with climbing revenue, funds from operations (FFO), and tenant sales, leading the company to raise its full-year outlook. The REIT signed over 1,200 leases totaling more than 4.8 million square feet, with new deals increasing 20% year-over-year and initial base rents on new deals up 17%. Occupancy at its U.S. malls and premium outlets remained at 96%, while base minimum rent increased 6.3% to $62.42 per square foot. The company is also accelerating its development pipeline, with $1.07 billion in projects underway and an additional $600 million expected to start construction in the second half of the year, bringing the total pipeline to over $4 billion.
