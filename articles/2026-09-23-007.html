TruAmerica Buys 200-Unit Bridges at San Ramon Multifamily Complex in San Francisco Bay Area

ACQUISITION

REBusinessOnline — Wed, 23 Sep 2026 13:49:22 +0000

SUMMARY

TruAmerica Multifamily has acquired Bridges at San Ramon, a 200-unit multifamily community in San Ramon, California. This acquisition expands the Los Angeles-based firm's portfolio in the San Francisco Bay Area to 1,240 units. TruAmerica plans to implement a renovation program for the majority of the units, building on prior ownership's recent exterior and common-area improvements.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Bridges at San Ramon

Address: 309 Springfield Dr, San Ramon, CA*

Units: 200

Notable Features: Units average 739 square feet; amenities include a pool, spa, fitness center, resident lounge, courtyard, barbecue area, playground, pet spa, EV charging stations, and garage parking.

Project Notes: Prior ownership completed approximately $2.3 million in exterior and common-area improvements over the past five years.

* Address sourced separately, not in original article.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: TruAmerica Multifamily
