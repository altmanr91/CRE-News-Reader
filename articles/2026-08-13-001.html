TikTok Tells U.S. Employees to Return to Office 5 Days a Week

Commercial Observer — Wed, 12 Aug 2026 20:44:46 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Labor Report

TikTok has mandated a five-day-a-week return to office for its U.S. employees, impacting thousands across various locations including its Culver City headquarters and Manhattan offices. This move follows a December announcement to hybrid employees and aligns with a broader trend of companies increasing in-office presence, as evidenced by a 6% year-over-year rise in U.S. office visits in the first half of 2026. The company recently closed its Nashville office, two years after opening.

DATA POINTS

U.S. office visits year-over-year increase (H1 2026): 6%

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Return-to-office policies are gaining ground across industries, with U.S. office visits rising 6% year-over-year in the first half of 2026, achieving the narrowest gap from pre-pandemic levels. This trend has contributed to record-high office rents in cities like New York.
