Stanhope And Norges Go Hunting As Sovereign Fund Pivots Strategy

Bisnow News Feed — Thu, 09 Jul 2026 11:44:49 -0400

SUMMARY

Acquisition / Asset Management Mandate

Stanhope has secured a mandate to manage a £4 billion portfolio of office assets in London and Paris for Norges Bank Investment Management, the world's largest sovereign wealth fund. This partnership also includes a joint effort to identify new acquisition opportunities and enhance returns on the existing assets. The collaboration signifies a strategic shift for Norges, which is moving away from in-house management of its directly owned real estate to an outsourced model with sector specialists to optimize performance. The portfolio under Stanhope's management encompasses 2.4 million square feet across six standing assets and three refurbishment or redevelopment projects.
