For Southern California, Defense Tech Is the Best Real Estate Offense

Commercial Observer — Mon, 24 Aug 2026 22:00:35 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

Southern California, particularly the South Bay region, is experiencing an unprecedented boom in defense technology and advanced manufacturing, driven by increased defense spending and the demand for high-tech warfare solutions. This surge is transforming port-adjacent warehouse spaces into military manufacturing hubs, with Los Angeles County securing the most direct Department of Defense contracts in the U.S. in 2025. The region's unique talent pool and specialized manufacturing base are attracting startups and established defense contractors, leading to a frenzy for industrial space and significant leasing activity. Landlords are seeing premium rents for these specialized facilities, which require specific features like vibration control and high power capacity, despite challenges with utility infrastructure.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Demand for advanced manufacturing space in the South Bay is estimated to be four times the current supply. Industrial firms typically command a rent premium of 25 to 50 cents per square foot per month. The Los Angeles industrial market recorded 2.8 million square feet of net absorption in Q2 2026, nearly triple the previous quarter's rate.
