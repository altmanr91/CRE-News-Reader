Alta Housing Breaks Ground on 108-Unit Affordable Housing Community in Mountain View, California

CONSTRUCTION

REBusinessOnline — Mon, 12 Oct 2026 15:03:06 +0000

SUMMARY

Alta Housing has commenced construction on La Bella, a 108-unit affordable multifamily community in Mountain View, California. The project, located in the Terra Bella neighborhood, will offer a mix of studio, one-, two-, and three-bedroom apartments, with a significant portion reserved for households transitioning out of homelessness, Section 8 voucher holders, and individuals with developmental disabilities. The all-electric building is designed with various community amenities and is expected to be completed in the second quarter of 2028.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: La Bella

Address: 1020 Terra Bella Ave, Mountain View, CA

Units: 108

Completion: 2Q 2028

Notable Features: All-electric building with a ground-floor lobby, multipurpose community room, landscaped third-floor courtyard, children's play areas, raised garden beds, onsite laundry, and offices for property management and resident services. Income limits range from 30% to 60% of area median income.

Project Notes: 27 units reserved for households transitioning out of homelessness via Santa Clara County's Rapid Rehousing Program; 23 units include Section 8 Project-based vouchers; 10 units reserved for individuals with developmental disabilities.
