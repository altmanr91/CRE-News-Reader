Matawan Retail Trades to Private Investor for $15M

SALE

Connect CRE — Fri, 07 Aug 2026 20:58:35 +0000

SUMMARY

Park Plaza, a 61,750-square-foot retail center in Matawan, NJ, has been sold for $14.67 million to a private investor. The property, which features a diverse tenant mix and strong visibility, attracted significant investor interest due to its strategic location in a well-established retail corridor. This transaction highlights the sustained demand for quality neighborhood shopping centers with durable cash flow in the New Jersey market.
