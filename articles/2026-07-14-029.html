Resort-style Naperville home sells for city, county record $8.3M

SALE

The Real Deal — Chicago — Mon, 13 Jul 2026 19:45:00 +0000

SUMMARY

A resort-style mansion in Naperville, IL, sold for $8.3 million, setting a new city record and tying for the second-highest sale in the greater Chicago area in 2026. The previous owners, Rocco and Diana Salviola, extensively upgraded the 21,261-square-foot property, adding numerous recreational amenities and smart-home features. The home, originally built in 2014, sits on three acres and includes seven bedrooms and 11 full bathrooms.

Project Notes: The property was previously listed in August 2024 for $9.85 million and in April 2026 for $8.65 million. The previous Naperville record was $7.55 million in 2024.
