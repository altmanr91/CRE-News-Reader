Breese-based Poettker raises $50,000 for Big Brothers Big Sisters of Southwestern Illinois

REJournals — Mon, 14 Sep 2026 14:52:17 +0000

SUMMARY

Non-CRE / Business News

Poettker Construction and Poettker Industrial Services successfully raised $50,000 for Big Brothers Big Sisters of Southwestern Illinois and other local charities through their annual golf classic. This event, now in its 13th year, has collectively generated over $450,000 for charitable causes. The company emphasizes that giving back to organizations that support local children and families is a fundamental aspect of their family-owned business culture, aiming to build stronger communities.
