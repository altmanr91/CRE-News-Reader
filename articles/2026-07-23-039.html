Yellowstone seizes Parkmerced redevelopment sites through foreclosure

FORECLOSURE

The Real Deal — San Francisco — Wed, 22 Jul 2026 19:18:12 +0000

SUMMARY

Yellowstone Real Estate Investments has seized four parcels within the Parkmerced mega-development in San Francisco through foreclosure, stemming from a defaulted $101 million construction loan originated in 2022 for Maximus Real Estate. The debt had escalated to over $199 million with interest and fees, leading Yellowstone to acquire the properties via credit bids. These sites are entitled for nearly 1,700 residential units and represent the initial phases of a long-delayed redevelopment plan for the 152-acre community.

DATA POINTS

Units: 1,700

Notable Features: The sites are entitled for nearly 1,700 residential units and are located next to San Francisco State University.

Project Notes: The foreclosure stems from a construction loan originated in 2022. The redevelopment plan, approved in 2011, aimed to triple housing stock to over 5,600 units with new retail and transit stops, but never broke ground.
