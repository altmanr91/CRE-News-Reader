Roadway Moving, Contenur Take Combined 231K SF at Innovo’s 28-90 Review Avenue

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Thu, 10 Sep 2026 19:47:59 +0000

SUMMARY

Innovo Property Group has secured two significant industrial leases totaling 231,000 square feet at its 28-90 Review Avenue complex in Long Island City, Queens. Roadway Moving, a moving and storage company, committed to 117,000 square feet on the first floor, while Spanish manufacturer Contenur leased 114,000 square feet on the second floor. These transactions underscore the continued demand for industrial space in the Queens market, following other recent leases at the property.
