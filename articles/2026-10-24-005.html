Roof of Concept: Meet Jeff Thramann, a Data Center Disruptor

Commercial Observer — Fri, 23 Oct 2026 16:16:31 +0000

SUMMARY

Feature / Profile

This article profiles Jeff Thramann, CEO of LT350, a company he co-founded with former U.S. Secretary of Defense Mark Esper. LT350 is developing an innovative data center concept that involves installing mini data centers on the roofs of solar power canopies above parking lots at properties like malls, hospitals, and schools. This approach aims to provide low-latency inference capabilities by integrating solar panels with modular cartridges containing GPUs, battery storage, and inverters, effectively turning parking lot airspace into a data center without consuming additional land. The company is targeting healthcare, financial services, and government sectors due to their need for low latency and high data security, and plans to break ground on its first project at a Dallas hospital early next year.
