Mid-America brokers sale of 240,345-square-foot community center in Lombard

SALE

REJournals — Mon, 05 Oct 2026 14:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Mid-America Real Estate Corporation's Investment Sales Group facilitated the sale of High Point Centre, a 240,345-square-foot community center in Lombard, Illinois. The property, located in the affluent western DuPage County Chicago suburbs, was acquired by a private investment group from a publicly traded REIT. The center offers significant value-add potential, currently operating at 68% occupancy with a diverse tenant mix.
