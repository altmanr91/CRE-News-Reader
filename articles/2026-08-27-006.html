Concord Summit Capital Arranges $28M in Financing for Metro Houston Mixed-Use Project

LOAN

REBusinessOnline — Thu, 27 Aug 2026 14:50:47 +0000

SUMMARY

Concord Summit Capital has arranged $28 million in nonrecourse financing for The Albritton, a mixed-use development currently under construction in Friendswood, a suburb of Houston. The project, being developed by Tannos Development Group, will feature 111 apartments, retail, office space, and a parking garage. Completion is anticipated in 2028.

DATA POINTS

Property Name: The Albritton

Address: 408 S Friendswood Dr, Friendswood, TX*

Units: 111

Completion: 2028
