Newmark closes 157,642-square-foot office lease in Southfield

LEASE

REJournals — Fri, 02 Oct 2026 17:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Newmark recently facilitated a significant 157,642-square-foot office lease at Travelers Tower II in Southfield, Michigan. The transaction involved Time Equities, Inc. as the landlord and a tenant represented by JLL. This lease underscores the continued appeal of Class-A office space in the Southfield market, particularly in well-located and amenity-rich properties that have undergone substantial capital improvements.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Property Name: Travelers Tower II

Address: 26533 Evergreen Rd, Southfield, MI*

Size: 157,642 SF

Notable Features: Class-A office building with renovated lobbies, fitness center, café, restrooms, elevator modernization, HVAC upgrades, conference center, tenant lounge, and outdoor patios. Located with convenient access to major regional thoroughfares.

Project Notes: Travelers Towers complex comprises two interconnected office buildings totaling 810,460 square feet on a 21-acre site. Approximately 33,786 square feet remains available at Travelers Tower II following this lease.

* Address sourced separately, not in original article.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

TENANT REP: JLL — AJ Weiner; Dave MacDonald
