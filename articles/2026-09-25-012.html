Fairview’s IMPACT Strategies earns GCI President’s Circle Award

REJournals — Fri, 25 Sep 2026 13:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Non-CRE / Business News

IMPACT Strategies Inc., a Fairview, Illinois-based firm, has received the General Contractors Insurance, LTD (GCI) President’s Circle Award for the second consecutive year. This award recognizes companies for their exceptional commitment to safety, loss control, and risk management. IMPACT Strategies distinguished itself among approximately 250 participants through its robust safety programs, proactive loss control efforts, and continuous investment in continuing education for its team members.
