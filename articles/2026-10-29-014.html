CBRE brokers sale of two-property healthcare portfolio in Lubbock, Carbondale

SALE

REJournals — Thu, 29 Oct 2026 13:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

CBRE has brokered the sale of a two-property healthcare portfolio comprising single-tenant dialysis clinics in Carbondale, Illinois, and Lubbock, Texas. The portfolio, totaling 33,645 square feet, was acquired for $10.5 million. The clinics are fully occupied by a prominent healthcare provider.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: medical office

Address: 1425 E. Main St., Carbondale, IL; 2710 Ave. Q, Lubbock, TX

Size: 33,645 SF

Sale Price: $10.5M

Notable Features: Single-tenant dialysis clinics.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

SELLER BROKER: CBRE — Karly Iacono; Rachel Wilson
