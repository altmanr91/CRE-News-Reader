Colliers Arranges Sale of 302K-SF Industrial Building in Kentucky

SALE

Connect CRE — Fri, 17 Jul 2026 21:11:41 +0000

SUMMARY

Colliers has arranged the sale of Gray Industrial Center Building 2, a newly completed 302,400-square-foot industrial facility in Shelbyville, Kentucky. The build-to-suit distribution center, fully leased to a global logistics provider, was acquired by ElmTree Funds from Gray Development in a forward-funded transaction. This sale highlights the strong investor demand for modern, strategically located industrial assets with long-term tenancy in markets offering excellent logistical access.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Property Name: Gray Industrial Center Building 2

Address: 530 Logistics Drive, Shelbyville, KY

Size: 302,400 SF

Completion: newly completed

Site Area: 32.19 acres

Notable Features: Build-to-suit distribution facility located approximately one mile from Interstate 64, providing convenient access to Louisville International Airport and UPS Worldport.

Project Notes: Acquisition was a forward-funded deal that closed upon completion of the facility.
