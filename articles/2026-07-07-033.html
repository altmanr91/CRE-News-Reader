Texas distress report: $900M in CRE loans flagged for auction in July

FORECLOSURE

The Real Deal — Texas — Mon, 06 Jul 2026 16:33:42 +0000

SUMMARY

Texas is seeing a significant increase in commercial real estate loans flagged for foreclosure, with $913 million in loans scheduled for auction in July. This follows surges past $1 billion in May and June. A substantial portion of this distress, approximately one-third of the total value, is attributed to S2 Capital's multifamily portfolio, which is dissolving its first fund with no capital returns. Other notable properties facing foreclosure include multifamily assets in Houston and Dallas, a rehabilitation center in Austin, and office and retail properties in San Antonio.
