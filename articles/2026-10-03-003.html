Bank of America, JLL Provide $276M Refi for SoCal Senior Housing

REFINANCE

Commercial Observer — Fri, 02 Oct 2026 18:20:50 +0000

SUMMARY

An affiliate of Nexus Development secured $276 million in refinancing for two senior housing communities in Newport Beach, CA. Bank of America provided a $140 million loan for Vivante Newport Center, while JLL Real Estate Capital provided $136 million for Vivante Newport Mesa. The financing for Vivante Newport Center, at approximately $1.4 million per unit, represents one of the largest per-unit financings in the senior housing sector's history.
