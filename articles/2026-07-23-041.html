Billionaire Bill Koch finds buyer for Aspen compound at 75% off list price

SALE

The Real Deal — Denver — Wed, 22 Jul 2026 15:30:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Billionaire industrialist Bill Koch has found a buyer for his 52-acre Aspen compound, Elk Mountain Lodge, which sold for over $33.5 million through an auction. The sale price represents a significant discount from its previous asking price of $125 million. Koch, who acquired the property in 2007 for $26.5 million and expanded it, had been attempting to sell the estate for over a decade.

Notable Features: The compound includes an eight-bedroom main lodge and seven other cabins, one of which is a 3,100-square-foot gym. The main house features two offices, two hot tubs, and temperature-controlled wine storage.

Project Notes: The property was originally purchased in 2007 for $26.5 million and converted from an event venue to a private residence. A 31-acre parcel was added to the property, then sold in 2020 for $14.5 million. The estate was rented out in 2023 and 2024 for an asking rent of $300,000 per week.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Aspen's real estate market has experienced a post-Covid downturn, with a 44% decline in sales during the first half of the year compared to the same period last year. Transactions exceeding $10 million saw an even steeper drop, falling 48% year-over-year.
