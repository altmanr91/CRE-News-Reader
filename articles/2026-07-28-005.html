Grun Group Files Plans for Pair of 99-Unit Residential Buildings in the Bronx

DEVELOPMENT

Commercial Observer — Mon, 27 Jul 2026 18:31:08 +0000

SUMMARY

Grun Group has filed plans with the New York City Department of Buildings for two separate 99-unit residential buildings in the Woodlawn Heights neighborhood of the Bronx. The proposed developments, located at 4241 Webster Avenue and 4230 Peters Place, will each feature eight stories and replace existing parking lots. The developer likely structured the projects with 99 units to qualify for the city's 485-x tax abatement program, which offers tax benefits without mandating higher construction wages typically associated with projects of 100 units or more.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Size: 128,344 SF

Units: 198

Notable Features: Each building will have 99 units across eight floors, likely structured to qualify for the 485-x tax abatement program.

Project Notes: The two projects are located at 4241 Webster Avenue and 4230 Peters Place. Both sites are currently parking lots.
