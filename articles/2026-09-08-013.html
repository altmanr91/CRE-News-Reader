Green Street: Industrial still one of the leaders in commercial real estate recovery

REJournals — Tue, 08 Sep 2026 10:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Price Index

The industrial sector continues to lead the commercial real estate recovery, with its property values rising faster than most other major sectors, according to Green Street's September 2026 Commercial Property Price Index. Industrial values increased 0.6% in August and 7% over the past year, now standing just 8% below their 2022 peak. While the overall commercial property market also saw an increase of 0.8% in August and 5% over the past year, it remains 13% below its 2022 peak. However, rising Treasury yields and borrowing costs are expected to temper buyer enthusiasm, potentially slowing the recovery across all property types.
