Related Ross Buys Land for Major Mixed-Use Dev in Florida

ACQUISITION

Commercial Observer — Tue, 01 Sep 2026 18:50:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Related Ross, led by Stephen Ross, has acquired a 32-acre parcel in Wellington, FL, for $28.3 million to develop Village Landing, a significant mixed-use project. The development will feature a 180-room hotel and over 300,000 square feet of retail, office, and restaurant space, designed to enhance the local community with everyday amenities and experiences. Construction is slated to begin in the fall, with Starwood Property Trust providing a $29.1 million loan for the acquisition and development.
