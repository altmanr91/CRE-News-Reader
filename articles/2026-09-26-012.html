St. Louis’ ARCO Construction completes 57,300-square-foot warehouse and HQ improvements in St. Louis market

REJournals — Fri, 25 Sep 2026 16:13:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Construction

ARCO Construction has completed a 57,300-square-foot tenant improvement project for RJP Electric in Affton, Missouri, a suburb of St. Louis. The project involved transforming an existing concrete masonry facility into a combined corporate headquarters and warehouse, featuring 37,700 square feet of warehouse space and 19,600 square feet of office space. ARCO Construction provided design-build services for the renovation, which included demolition of the previous office and a new buildout.
