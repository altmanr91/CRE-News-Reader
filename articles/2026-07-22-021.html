BXP putting 7 Times Square ground lease on the market

The Real Deal — New York — Tue, 21 Jul 2026 16:15:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Property Listing / For Sale

BXP is marketing the ground lease for 7 Times Square, a 1.2 million-square-foot office tower in Manhattan, with an asking price between $700 million and $750 million. The offering, which could pertain to BXP's majority stake or the entire ground lease, represents one of the few single-property office deals at this price point in recent years. The property, developed in 2004 and currently 91% leased, includes recent amenity upgrades and generates additional revenue from electronic billboards.
