JLL provides $406 million in financing for Dallas’ Trammel Crow Center

LOAN

REJournals — Tue, 29 Sep 2026 14:11:00 +0000

SUMMARY

JLL's Capital Markets group has secured $406 million in five-year, fixed-rate financing for the Trammell Crow Center, a prominent 1.2 million-square-foot trophy office tower in Dallas' Arts District. The loan, provided by Wells Fargo and Morgan Stanley, was arranged on behalf of the borrower, Regent Properties, LLC. The 50-story property, which underwent a significant $182 million renovation in 2019, is 92% leased to a strong tenant mix and offers Class AA amenities and a prime location within the largest contiguous urban arts district in the United States.
