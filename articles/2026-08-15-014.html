After more than four decades in publishing, Mark Menzies retires from REjournals

REJournals — Fri, 14 Aug 2026 10:48:44 +0000

SUMMARY

Non-CRE / Business News

Mark Menzies, senior vice president and national publisher at REjournals, is retiring after more than four decades in the publishing industry. Menzies was instrumental in guiding REjournals through significant industry changes and economic shifts, including the 2008 recession, the transition to online publishing, the COVID-19 pandemic, and the emergence of AI. His career included 29 years with Law Bulletin Publishing before its real estate publications were acquired by REjournals, where he spent an additional seven years overseeing the growth of several key regional real estate journals.
