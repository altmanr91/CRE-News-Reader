Hugs Café Inc. Opens $10M SF Headquarters Office in McKinney, Texas

CONSTRUCTION

REBusinessOnline — Mon, 24 Aug 2026 15:38:52 +0000

SUMMARY

Hugs Café Inc., a nonprofit focused on training and employing individuals with intellectual and developmental disabilities, has opened its new $10 million headquarters in McKinney, Texas. The 13,500-square-foot facility includes a training academy, commercial kitchen, classrooms, and administrative offices, designed to support the organization's mission.
