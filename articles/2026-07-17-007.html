Robins & Morton Tops Out $563M BayCare Hospital Manatee in Palmetto, Florida

CONSTRUCTION

REBusinessOnline — Thu, 16 Jul 2026 15:14:07 +0000

SUMMARY

Robins & Morton has topped out the BayCare Hospital Manatee, a 450,000-square-foot, 154-bed hospital project in Palmetto, Florida. The $563 million facility, designed by Gresham Smith, is expected to open in 2028 and will be the 17th hospital for the BayCare healthcare system.
