KKR considers offloading real estate lending arm

The Real Deal — National — Thu, 23 Jul 2026 20:30:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Non-CRE / Business News

KKR Real Estate Finance Trust (KREF) is exploring strategic alternatives, including a potential sale of the company or disposition of assets, following a challenging second quarter. The real estate lending arm of KKR reported a $121.8 million loss on its $4.5 billion loan portfolio, which has decreased from $5.1 billion in the first quarter. Despite resolving two watchlist loans and repurchasing shares, KREF is still managing six watchlisted properties, including office and life sciences assets, as it continues its "year of transition."
