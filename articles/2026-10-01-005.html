BankUnited Lands Fifth Avenue Retail Flagship and Expands Offices at 450 Park Avenue

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Thu, 01 Oct 2026 13:42:12 +0000

SUMMARY

BankUnited has significantly expanded its presence in Manhattan with two major transactions. The Florida-based bank secured a new 10,068-square-foot retail flagship branch at 10 Bryant Park, located on the prominent corner of West 40th Street and Fifth Avenue, with an expected opening in 2027. Concurrently, BankUnited expanded its office footprint at 450 Park Avenue by adding an entire 10,392-square-foot floor and extending its existing lease, bringing its total office space to 27,392 square feet. These deals underscore BankUnited's long-term commitment to New York City, which its CEO described as integral to the bank's growth and crucial for building commercial relationships and core deposits.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: mixed-use

Property Name: 10 Bryant Park / 450 Park Avenue

Size: 37,460 SF

Completion: 2027 (retail branch)

Notable Features: The retail flagship is located in the historic portion of 10 Bryant Park, originally designed in the early 1900s, incorporating 1 West 39th Street and the former Kress department store.

Project Notes: BankUnited previously added 17,000 square feet at 450 Park Avenue in 2021 for executive offices. The owner of 10 Bryant Park, PBC, spent $100 million upgrading the property in recent years.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

TENANT REP: Mogull Realty — Kim Mogull, Founder and CEO

LANDLORD: SL Green Realty

LEASING AGENT: CBRE — Paul Amrich; Neil King; Alex D'Amario; Matthew Dichter; Brooke Dewing

LANDLORD: Property & Building Corporation (PBC)

OWNER: PBC USA — Eli Elefant, CEO

OWNER: Discount Investment Corporation

LEASING AGENT: JLL — Patrick A. Smith; Matt Ogle; Amanda Sujak

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

The asking rent for the lower office floor at 450 Park Avenue was $135 per square foot. The asking rent for the Fifth Avenue retail space at 10 Bryant Park was in the mid-$400s per square foot. PBC is currently marketing 10 Bryant Park and the adjacent 1 West 39th Street for sale, with bids expected to exceed $800 million, though the process has shifted to a recapitalization.
