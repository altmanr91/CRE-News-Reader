Brixton Capital closes sale of Dow Texas Innovation Center in Lake Jackson

SALE

REJournals — Tue, 13 Oct 2026 14:03:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Brixton Capital has sold the Dow Texas Innovation Center, a 664,100-square-foot research and development campus in Lake Jackson, Texas, to ExchangeRight Essential Income REIT. The 34-acre campus, comprising four buildings, is fully leased to The Dow Chemical Company under a single-tenant, triple-net lease. The transaction was structured as an UPREIT and debt-assumption deal.
