Newmark Arranges Sale of 452-Unit Apartment Community in East Fort Worth

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Thu, 30 Jul 2026 14:57:57 +0000

SUMMARY

Newmark has facilitated the sale of Centreport Lake, a 452-unit apartment community in East Fort Worth. The property, built in 2008, features a mix of one-, two-, and three-bedroom units across 24 acres. Newmark also arranged the acquisition financing and equity investments for the undisclosed buyer.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Centreport Lake

Address: 14301 Center Station Dr, Fort Worth, TX*

Units: 452

Year Built: 2008

Site Area: 24 acres

Notable Features: Amenities include two pools, a dog park, business center, and outdoor grilling and dining stations.

* Address sourced separately, not in original article.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

SELLER: Marlin Spring US Realty

SELLER BROKER: Newmark — Richard Furr; Brian Murphy; Brian O’Boyle Jr.
