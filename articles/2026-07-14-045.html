$30B Investment In Limbo After Attorneys General Sue To Stop Paramount, Warners Bros. Merger

Bisnow News Feed — Mon, 13 Jul 2026 15:04:39 -0400

SUMMARY

CRE Legal / Regulatory

Twelve attorneys general have filed a lawsuit to block the proposed $111 billion merger between Paramount and Warner Bros. Discovery, arguing that the union would violate antitrust laws by reducing competition in the entertainment industry. The lawsuit claims the merger would lead to higher prices, lower quality, and less content for consumers, impacting movie theaters and cable distributors. Paramount has countered that the merger is necessary to create a more competitive media company capable of challenging dominant players in the industry and that delaying the transaction would harm entertainment workers.
