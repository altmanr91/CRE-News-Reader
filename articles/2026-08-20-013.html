Apricus Realty Capital acquires 53,100-square-foot IOS property in San Antonio

ACQUISITION

REJournals — Wed, 19 Aug 2026 15:02:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Apricus Realty Capital, in partnership with ABR Capital Partners, has acquired an industrial outdoor storage (IOS) property in San Antonio, marking its first investment in this market and expanding its Texas IOS portfolio. The fully occupied 53,100-square-foot facility, located on 4.3 acres, is strategically positioned near San Antonio International Airport with excellent highway access. Apricus plans to implement capital improvements to enhance the property's functionality and institutional quality.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Address: 8799 Crownhill Blvd., San Antonio, TX

Size: 53,100 SF

Site Area: 4.3 acres

Notable Features: Located adjacent to San Antonio International Airport with direct access to major interstates and highways; features crane-served warehouse space, maintenance facilities, a concrete storage yard, and multiple drive-in loading positions.

Project Notes: Acquired in partnership with ABR Capital Partners as part of a programmatic joint venture focused on IOS and infill industrial aggregation.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: Apricus Realty Capital

EQUITY/FINANCING: ABR Capital Partners
