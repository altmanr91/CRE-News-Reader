HPI Breaks Ground on 396-Unit Multifamily Project in North Austin

CONSTRUCTION

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 16 Oct 2026 15:07:17 +0000

SUMMARY

HPI Real Estate Services & Investments has commenced construction on The Kent, a 396-unit multifamily development in North Austin, near The Domain. The seven-story apartment project is being developed in partnership with private investors and is expected to be completed by mid-2029.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: The Kent

Address: 2631 Kramer Ln, Austin, TX*

Units: 396

Completion: mid-2029

* Address sourced separately, not in original article.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

DEVELOPER/SPONSOR: HPI Real Estate Services & Investments
