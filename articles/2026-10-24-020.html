Hudson Pacific's Historic San Francisco Conversion Project Gets Approval

DEVELOPMENT

Bisnow Latest News Feed — Fri, 23 Oct 2026 17:41:50 -0400

SUMMARY

Hudson Pacific Properties has received approval to convert a historic 115-year-old building on Market Street in San Francisco into 136 residential units. This adaptive reuse project aims to contribute to the city's housing supply by transforming an existing structure into new residential inventory.

Units: 136
