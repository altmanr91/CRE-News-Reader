Logistics Plus Signs 744,452 SF Industrial Lease in Wilmer, Texas

LEASE

REBusinessOnline — Thu, 13 Aug 2026 15:21:55 +0000

SUMMARY

Logistics Plus, a Pennsylvania-based freight and fulfillment services provider, has signed a substantial 744,452-square-foot industrial lease in Wilmer, Texas. The company will fully occupy Building 2 within the Southport Logistics Center, a large industrial development. This lease underscores the continued demand for significant logistics space in the southern Dallas submarket.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Property Name: Southport Logistics Center, Building 2

Size: 744,452 SF

Notable Features: Building 2 features 123 dock doors, three drive-in doors, and 4,647 square feet of speculative office space.
