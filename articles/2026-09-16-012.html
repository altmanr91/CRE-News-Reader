Evergreen Management adds Ohio memory care communities to its portfolio

ACQUISITION

REJournals — Wed, 16 Sep 2026 13:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Evergreen Management has expanded its specialized memory care platform by adding two dedicated memory care communities in Ohio: The Auberge at River Oaks in Miamisburg and The Auberge at Greenfield Estates in Akron. These acquisitions increase Evergreen's national senior living portfolio from 66 to 68 communities and introduce The Auberge brand to the Ohio market. Evergreen will operate both communities, providing its established infrastructure in operations, clinical support, and resident-centered care.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: senior living

Property Name: The Auberge at River Oaks / The Auberge at Greenfield Estates

Address: 2961 W. Spring Valley Pike, Miamisburg, OH / 3522 Commercial Drive, Akron, OH

Notable Features: Both communities are dedicated memory care facilities for individuals with Alzheimer's and dementia, featuring secure outdoor areas and welcoming shared spaces.

Project Notes: These additions expand Evergreen's Auberge portfolio to 13 dedicated memory care communities, extending its presence beyond Texas, Illinois, and Wisconsin.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: Evergreen Management
