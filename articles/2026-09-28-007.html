JLL Arranges $66.8M Refinancing for Industrial Development in Stuart, Florida

REFINANCE

REBusinessOnline — Mon, 28 Sep 2026 14:17:21 +0000

SUMMARY

JLL Capital Markets has arranged a $66.8 million floating-rate refinancing loan for South Florida Gateway, a newly delivered industrial development in Stuart, Florida. The loan was secured on behalf of the borrowers, Foundry Commercial and Stockbridge Capital Group, for the nearly 1 million-square-foot property comprising two buildings on 65 acres.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Property Name: South Florida Gateway

Address: 2400-2450 Southwest Gateway Place, Stuart, FL

Size: 987,184 SF

Loan Amount: $66.8M

Loan Per SF: $68/SF (calculated)

Loan/Acre: $1,027,692/acre (calculated)

Loan/Land SF: $23.59/land SF (calculated)

Completion: newly delivered

Site Area: 65 acres

Notable Features: Comprises two buildings: Building One with 285,510 SF rear-load warehouse space, 32-foot clear heights, 60 dock doors, and two drive-in ramps; Building Two with 701,674 SF cross-dock distribution space, 36-foot clear heights, 120 dock doors, and four drive-in ramps.
