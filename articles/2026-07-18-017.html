Amazon eyes 4M sf warehouse on Long Island

The Real Deal — New York — Fri, 17 Jul 2026 17:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Development

Amazon is planning its largest Long Island development to date, proposing a 4.2 million-square-foot warehouse complex in Holbrook. The e-commerce giant has filed plans for two connected industrial buildings, including a 3.7 million-square-foot fulfillment center and a 450,000-square-foot storehouse, on a 140-acre site. The project, estimated at $1 billion, is contingent on a zoning change from the Town of Islip and securing tax breaks from the Islip Industrial Development Agency.
