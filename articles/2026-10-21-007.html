Marcus & Millichap Brokers $7.5M Sale of Restaurant Building in Katy, Texas

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Tue, 20 Oct 2026 15:21:21 +0000

SUMMARY

Marcus & Millichap has brokered the $7.5 million sale of an 8,753-square-foot restaurant building in Katy, Texas. The property, net-leased to a Brazilian restaurant chain, was sold by a developer to a California-based 1031 exchange investor in an all-cash transaction. The sale price equates to $874.84 per square foot.
