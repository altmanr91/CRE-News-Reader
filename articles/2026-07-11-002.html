Palm Beach County Pauses Data Center Applications

Commercial Observer — Fri, 10 Jul 2026 18:50:02 +0000

SUMMARY

Infrastructure Investment / Government Policy

The Palm Beach County Commission has implemented a temporary moratorium on new data center applications in unincorporated areas, citing concerns from residents regarding infrastructure, resource consumption, and long-term impacts. This pause, enacted through a 5-2 vote, allows the county to develop appropriate zoning regulations for this asset class. The moratorium is expected to last up to a year or until new zoning is adopted, though one large-scale project, Central Park Commerce Center, will proceed with its review as it was filed prior to the suspension.
