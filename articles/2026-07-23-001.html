Meal Delivery Service CookUnity to Open Flagship Storefront at NoMad Hotel

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Wed, 22 Jul 2026 20:55:51 +0000

SUMMARY

CookUnity, a subscription-based meal delivery service, has secured 4,721 square feet of ground-floor retail space at the Virgin Hotel New York City in Manhattan's NoMad neighborhood. This transaction marks the company's first flagship storefront in New York City, which will offer dining, retail, and community gathering spaces, signifying a strategic expansion beyond its delivery-only model. The new location is anticipated to open in the first half of 2027.
