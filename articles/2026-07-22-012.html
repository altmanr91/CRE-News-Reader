What to look for in a snow removal plan

REJournals — Wed, 22 Jul 2026 09:19:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

This article provides facility managers with key considerations for developing a comprehensive snow removal plan. It emphasizes the importance of defining desired outcomes and levels of service rather than focusing solely on equipment. The author highlights the need for plans to address varying weather conditions, manage meltwater and snow pile placement, ensure continuous accessibility routes, and clearly delineate decision-making authority. Additionally, the article stresses the importance of matching equipment to property limitations and implementing a strategic material application approach, advocating for detailed documentation and adherence to industry standards.
