SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

This article provides a comprehensive overview of the commercial real estate market, highlighting the explosive growth in data center investment, which has reached $1.3 trillion. It also notes a resurgence in the office sector, particularly in New York City, driven by AI firms, and continued strength in retail and multifamily markets across various regions. The piece draws on data from Atrium, Skanska, CBRE, Colliers, SitusAMC, Hoya Capital, and NAI Capital to support its observations on market trends and investment activity.
