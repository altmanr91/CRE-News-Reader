Nuveen Acquires $445M Retail Portfolio Across Three Western States

ACQUISITION

Commercial Observer — Mon, 02 Nov 2026 18:55:41 +0000

SUMMARY

Nuveen Real Estate has acquired a 13-property retail portfolio across California, Colorado, and Washington for $445.5 million. The portfolio, totaling 1.89 million square feet, is 93.5% leased and primarily features grocery-anchored shopping centers. This transaction represents one of the largest grocery-anchored retail deals in the Western U.S. in recent years, reflecting strong investor demand for well-located retail assets with future growth potential amid limited new construction and robust residential populations.

DATA POINTS

Sale Price: $445.5M

Occupancy at Sale: 93.5%

Notable Features: Portfolio includes six properties in California, four in Colorado, and three in Washington, primarily grocery-anchored shopping centers.

FINANCING

U.S. Bank provided $239 million in financing for 11 of the 13 properties, covering 54% of the total purchase price.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Investor demand for high-quality, well-located retail remains strong, particularly for grocery-anchored and open-air retail properties due to limited new construction and significant residential populations in the Western U.S.
