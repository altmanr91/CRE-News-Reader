Pickleball Concept Will Make California Debut with Lewis Retail Centers

CONSTRUCTION

Connect CRE — Wed, 09 Sep 2026 21:16:39 +0000

SUMMARY

Lewis Retail Centers has commenced construction on Electric Pickle, a new chef-driven restaurant, bar, and pickleball social house concept, marking its California debut at The Resort in Rancho Cucamonga. The 8,500-square-foot entertainment destination will anchor The Courtyard, a 31,800-square-foot urban village, and is expected to open in summer 2027. This project aligns with the developer's vision for the master-planned urban village, reflecting the growing popularity of pickleball as a lifestyle activity and a draw for social gatherings.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: retail

Property Name: Electric Pickle at The Courtyard

Address: 4th Street and Resort Parkway, Rancho Cucamonga, CA

Size: 8,500 SF

Completion: Summer 2027

Notable Features: Features 10 pickleball courts (seven outdoor, three covered), a lawn, and multiple bar and lounge areas.
