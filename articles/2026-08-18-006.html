Jackson-Shaw Breaks Ground on 908,730 SF Industrial Project in Denton, Texas

CONSTRUCTION

REBusinessOnline — Mon, 17 Aug 2026 15:03:04 +0000

SUMMARY

Jackson-Shaw has commenced construction on Horizon 35, a substantial industrial project spanning over 900,000 square feet in Denton, Texas. The initial phase will deliver three rear-load buildings totaling 543,550 square feet, featuring modern specifications such as high clear heights and extensive loading capabilities. This development aims to meet the growing demand for industrial space in the North Texas market.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Property Name: Horizon 35

Size: 908,730 SF

Completion: Q3 2027

Phase I: 543,550 square feet across three buildings

Notable Features: Phase I features 32- to 36-foot clear heights, dock-high loading doors, 18-by-20-foot drive-in doors, and ample car and trailer parking.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

DEVELOPER/SPONSOR: Jackson-Shaw

GENERAL CONTRACTOR: ARCO/Murray

LEASING AGENT: Stream Realty Partners
