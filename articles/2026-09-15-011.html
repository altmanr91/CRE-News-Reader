Marcus & Millichap closes sale of 72,551-square-foot shopping center in Granbury

SALE

REJournals — Tue, 15 Sep 2026 14:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Marcus & Millichap has brokered the off-market sale of Granbury Lakeside Center, a 72,551-square-foot shopping center in Granbury, Texas. The fully leased property, built in 2019, was acquired by a private buyer from a private seller. The buyer intends to maintain the center as a stabilized retail investment.
