Cortland, Pulte, INVH, Walker & Dunlop Execs Talk Changing Demographics, AI

Bisnow News Feed — Thu, 30 Jul 2026 12:28:48 -0400

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

This article summarizes a Walker & Dunlop webcast where housing industry leaders discussed demographic shifts and their impact on the U.S. housing market. Key takeaways include the country's declining replacement rate, the increasing number of young adults living with parents, and the rise of remote work, all of which are reshaping demand for housing types and locations. While some rapidly growing markets are experiencing a "fallout," more affordable, stable markets with strong job growth are performing well. The panelists also highlighted the strong performance and future potential of active adult communities, particularly as the baby boomer demographic ages and Generation X seeks similar lifestyles. The discussion concluded with an acknowledgment of the ongoing housing shortage, especially in the affordable segment, and the unknown long-term impact of artificial intelligence on commercial real estate.

DATA POINTS

Adults under 35 living with parents: One-third

U.S. fertility rate in 2024: Record low

U.S. households consisting of married couples: 47%

People working from home today vs. pre-2020: Four times as many
