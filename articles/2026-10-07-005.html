Health Care Investment Firm Lorient Inks 6K-SF lease at Durst’s 825 Third Avenue

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Wed, 07 Oct 2026 11:00:19 +0000

SUMMARY

Lorient, a healthcare investment firm, has signed a 5,556-square-foot lease for its inaugural New York City office at 825 Third Avenue in Midtown East. The Durst Organization, which owns the 40-story office tower, represented itself in the transaction, while Lee & Associates represented Lorient. The building recently underwent a $150 million renovation, enhancing its appeal with modern amenities and prebuilt, furnished suites for immediate occupancy.
