Hollywood talent agency takes 100K sf at Empire State Building

LEASE

The Real Deal — New York — Mon, 20 Jul 2026 14:23:44 +0000

SUMMARY

United Talent Agency has signed a 101,000-square-foot lease at the Empire State Building in New York City, spanning the 32nd through 35th floors. This move consolidates three of the agency's offices into a prominent new location, reflecting its continued growth and long-term commitment to New York as a cultural and business hub. The transaction is a significant win for Empire State Realty Trust, bringing the iconic property closer to full occupancy.
