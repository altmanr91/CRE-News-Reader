KAI leading repair efforts at historic Annie Malone Children and Family Services HQ in St. Louis

CONSTRUCTION

REJournals — Wed, 30 Sep 2026 15:10:41 +0000

SUMMARY

KAI has been selected as the general contractor to lead stabilization and repair efforts at the historic Annie Malone Children and Family Services headquarters in St. Louis, MO. The nearly century-old, 15,200-square-foot building, originally constructed in 1922, suffered damage from a tornado in May 2025 and subsequent pipe bursts due to a boiler system failure. The project, funded by a $1 million grant from the St. Louis Development Corporation, aims to preserve the building's historic integrity and ensure its continued service to the community.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Property Name: Annie Malone Children and Family Services headquarters

Address: 2612 Annie Malone Drive, St. Louis, MO

Size: 15,200 SF

Year Built: 1922

Notable Features: Historic building, symbol of Annie Malone's legacy of service to the St. Louis community.

Project Notes: Project received $1 million through the St. Louis Development Corporation’s North Corridor Community Enhancement Grant Program. Repairs are focused on stabilization, including emergency roof replacement, weatherproofing, structural stabilization, masonry restoration, chimney repairs, drainage improvements, and repairs from freeze-related water damage.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

GENERAL CONTRACTOR: KAI — Gyasi H.L. Haynes, Vice President

OWNER: Annie Malone Children and Family Services — Keisha Lee, chief executive officer

EQUITY/FINANCING: St. Louis Development Corporation
