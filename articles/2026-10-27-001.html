Mesa West Provides $62M Refi for Silicon Valley Office Property

REFINANCE

Commercial Observer — Tue, 27 Oct 2026 14:27:05 +0000

SUMMARY

LBA Realty has secured a $61.5 million refinancing loan for a 125,000-square-foot Class A office property in Sunnyvale, CA. Mesa West provided the debt, which was arranged by JLL Capital Markets. The property, built in 2021, benefits from a tight Class A office market in Silicon Valley, characterized by strong technology sector growth and limited new supply.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Address: 285 Sobrante Way, Sunnyvale, CA

Size: 125,000 SF

Loan Amount: $61.5M

Loan Per SF: $492/SF (calculated)

Year Built: 2021

Notable Features: Class A office property with on-site solar power generation.

Project Notes: Property was previously acquired by LBA Realty for $42.3 million in June 2025 after falling into receivership due to a default on construction debt by previous owners.

OWNER: Principal Real Estate Investors

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Class A office space availability in Sunnyvale declined to 11% in Q2 2026, marking the third consecutive quarter of declines. Sunnyvale has not seen new Class A office deliveries in recent years.
