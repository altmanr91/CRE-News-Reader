Andee Robb Joins Marcus & Millichap to Lead Retail Leasing & Advisory Services

Connect CRE — Tue, 30 Jun 2026 19:51:11 +0000

SUMMARY

Promotion

Andee Robb has been appointed as the head of Retail Leasing & Advisory Services at Marcus & Millichap, a newly created role aimed at expanding the firm's retail leasing business across North America. Robb, with over two decades of experience in retail real estate, will focus on identifying new opportunities, enhancing retailer engagement, and evolving the company's leasing platform. This strategic hire is part of Marcus & Millichap's broader initiative to strengthen its retail leadership, following the recent appointments of Michael Puline as senior managing director and national director of retail, and Thomas Gorman as senior managing director and national net lease division leader.
