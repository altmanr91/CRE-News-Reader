Greenstone Partners Closes Off-Market West Loop Land Sale

SALE

Connect CRE — Wed, 09 Sep 2026 21:26:55 +0000

SUMMARY

Greenstone Partners has facilitated the $4.35 million off-market sale of a 14,850-square-foot development site at 1276 W Washington Boulevard in Chicago's West Loop. The firm represented both the seller, a long-standing client, and the buyer, who plans to develop a boutique condominium project on the site. The property's as-of-right zoning was a significant factor, enabling the buyer to proceed with development without a lengthy rezoning process, which is particularly appealing in the current financing climate for smaller, more attainable projects.
