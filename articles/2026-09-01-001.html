Retail Location Intelligence Grows in Sophistication and Usage

Commercial Observer — Tue, 01 Sep 2026 14:00:35 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

This article highlights the increasing sophistication and essential role of location intelligence technology in retail real estate. Advanced platforms, leveraging AI and machine learning, can now predict retail success metrics like revenue and cash-on-cash returns for specific sites, considering hundreds of demographic, psychographic, and competitive factors. This technology allows retailers and brokers to make highly informed decisions, adapting to rapidly changing market conditions and consumer behaviors. Industry professionals emphasize that this data-driven approach has transformed brokerage into a consulting business, providing clients with the confidence needed for strategic site selection and expansion.
