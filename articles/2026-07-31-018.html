Beyond the Megawatts: How Data Centers Are Reshaping Industrial Markets

Connect CRE — Fri, 31 Jul 2026 05:01:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Data Center Impact

A new report by Cushman & Wakefield highlights the significant economic impact of data centers beyond their primary function of supporting cloud computing and artificial intelligence. The study, titled "From Megawatts to Multipliers: The Data Center Effect on Industrial Demand, Jobs and Local Revenue," analyzed six key markets and found that data center development spurs employment growth, regional economic development, and industrial real estate demand. Companies supporting data center operations accounted for a substantial portion of new industrial leases, reaching a record 14.4% of leasing activity in 2025, and generated tens of thousands of jobs and billions in economic output across the studied regions.
