Oxford Partners brokers sale of 57,396-square-foot building in Houston

SALE

REJournals — Wed, 04 Nov 2026 14:32:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Oxford Partners has brokered the sale of a 57,396-square-foot commercial building located at 1751-1765 Stebbins Drive in Houston, Texas. The transaction involved an undisclosed seller and a buyer represented by Outpost Partners and ICO Commercial.
