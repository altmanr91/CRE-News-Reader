Lincoln Property pays $61M for office campus in Boston suburb

ACQUISITION

The Real Deal — National — Tue, 07 Jul 2026 19:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Lincoln Property Company, in partnership with an investor group advised by JPMorgan Asset Management, has acquired The Edge office campus in Wakefield, MA, for $61 million. The seven-building, 962,000-square-foot complex was sold by Hobbs Brook Real Estate, which developed the campus on the former Pleasure Island amusement park site. Lincoln Property sees significant value-add and mixed-use redevelopment potential in the property, which is located approximately 12 miles north of Downtown Boston.

DATA POINTS

Address: 101 Edgewater Dr, Wakefield, MA*

Notable Features: Seven-building office campus located on the former Pleasure Island amusement park site, approximately 12 miles north of Downtown Boston.

Project Notes: Hobbs Brook Real Estate assembled and developed the office park between 2001 and 2011. Hobbs Brook announced capital improvements at 601 and 701 Edgewater Drive in 2024, including café renovation, fitness center improvements, and a new conference center.
