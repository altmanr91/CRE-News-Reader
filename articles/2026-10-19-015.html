JLL Capital Markets brokers sale of retail, multifamily portfolio in Dallas’ Bishop Arts District

ACQUISITION

REJournals — Mon, 19 Oct 2026 09:38:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Hines has acquired the Bishop Arts retail and multifamily portfolio in Dallas' Bishop Arts District from seller and developer Exxir, owned by the Nazerian family. The portfolio comprises four Class-A multifamily properties totaling 539 units and 40,747 square feet of premier retail space across 1.07 acres. This transaction represents a significant institutional-scale mixed-use deal in Texas, combining newly developed housing with curated street retail in a dynamic urban neighborhood.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: mixed-use

Property Name: Bishop Arts retail and multifamily portfolio

Size: 40,747 SF

Occupancy at Sale: 94.0%

Site Area: 1.07 acres

Notable Features: The retail component was built in 2018 and 2019, and the multifamily component has an average vintage of 2021, offering luxury amenities including resort-style pools, fitness centers, co-working spaces, courtyards, and dog parks.
