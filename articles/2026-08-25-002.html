Tavaco Properties Buys DMV Office for $29M

ACQUISITION

Commercial Observer — Mon, 24 Aug 2026 23:35:50 +0000

SUMMARY

Tavaco Properties has acquired a 272,300-square-foot office building in Falls Church, VA, for $28.5 million, marking its third discounted office acquisition in the Greater Washington area since late last year. The property, located at 3110 Fairview Park Drive, was sold by James Campbell Company, which had purchased it for $43.7 million in 2016. Tavaco Properties specializes in optimizing underperforming assets and anticipates that continued return-to-office trends will bolster demand for its well-managed properties.
