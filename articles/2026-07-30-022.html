Newmark Posts Record Q2 Revenue, Holds Guidance Flat

Bisnow News Feed — Wed, 29 Jul 2026 13:21:02 -0400

SUMMARY

Market Research / Earnings Report

Newmark reported record second-quarter revenue of $888.4 million, a 17% increase year-over-year, driven by strong performance in multifamily investment sales and office leasing, particularly in coastal markets. Despite these robust results, the brokerage maintained its full-year revenue guidance, citing anticipated tougher comparisons in the second half of 2025 and a desire for more market data given the current macro environment. The company's CEO highlighted its strategic focus on multifamily, especially senior and affordable housing, and its significant involvement in the growing data center market, anticipating continued strong activity in these sectors.
