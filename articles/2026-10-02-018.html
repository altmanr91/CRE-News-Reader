Publix Pays $83.5M for Miami Shopping Center

ACQUISITION

Connect CRE — Fri, 02 Oct 2026 14:11:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Publix Super Markets has acquired Airpark Plaza, a 204,000-square-foot shopping center in Miami, for $83.25 million from First Washington Realty. This acquisition aligns with Publix's strategy of purchasing properties where it is a key tenant, enabling greater control over property improvements and tenant curation. The shopping center, originally built in 1971, is strategically located just south of Miami International Airport.
