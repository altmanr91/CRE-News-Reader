Location, Timing, Connection Drove World Cup Gains in Host Cities

Connect CRE — Tue, 20 Oct 2026 00:06:15 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Event Impact

A new white paper by Placer.ai and Colliers analyzes the economic impact of the 2026 FIFA World Cup on its 11 U.S. host cities, revealing that while the tournament provided a substantial boost, the benefits were highly localized. Metro-wide retail and dining sectors saw only marginal to modest lifts; instead, the most significant gains accrued to businesses and properties strategically located near stadiums and fan zones. The report emphasizes that successful economic leverage from major events hinges on precise location, opportune timing, and a direct connection to the event's fan journey, with well-placed fan destinations capable of extending the economic footprint beyond immediate venues.
