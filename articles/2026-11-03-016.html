Duke Buys Discounted Durham Office Building

ACQUISITION

Connect CRE — Tue, 03 Nov 2026 14:59:27 +0000

SUMMARY

DUMAC, Duke University's investment arm, acquired Erwin Square Plaza, a 238,792-square-foot office building in Durham, NC, for $30 million. The property, which was last sold in 2015 for $57 million, is fully leased, with Duke University itself serving as the anchor tenant, occupying 80% of the space. The university recently extended its lease for 10 years, with renewal options, for various administrative and health system offices.
