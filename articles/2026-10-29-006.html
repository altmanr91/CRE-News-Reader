Carrier Global to Invest $600M in HVAC Manufacturing Facility in Fort Worth, Create 1,500 Jobs

DEVELOPMENT

REBusinessOnline — Thu, 29 Oct 2026 15:18:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Carrier Global Corp. is investing approximately $600 million to develop a new 1 million-square-foot HVAC manufacturing and engineering facility in Fort Worth, Texas. The project, located within Hillwood’s AllianceTexas master-planned community, is expected to create about 1,500 new jobs over the next three years. The facility will produce advanced thermal management systems, including commercial chillers, with operations anticipated to begin next year.
