Get to Know the Broker: Vickey Barron of Compass

Commercial Observer — Tue, 18 Aug 2026 17:30:58 +0000

SUMMARY

Feature / Profile

This article profiles Vickey Barron, an associate broker at Compass, highlighting her career achievements and philosophy. Barron emphasizes that her purpose in real estate is to serve people and help them find homes, rather than focusing solely on commissions or sales volume. She recounts her most significant deal as helping a 93-year-old woman negotiate a life estate to remain in her West Village townhouse after its sale, underscoring the human impact of her work.
