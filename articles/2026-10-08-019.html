Space Craft Building 4th Charlotte Optimist Park Rental Community

CONSTRUCTION

Connect CRE — Thu, 08 Oct 2026 13:57:46 +0000

SUMMARY

Space Craft is developing Mira, a $122 million mixed-use project in Charlotte's Optimist Park, which will feature 371 apartments and nearly 10,000 square feet of retail. This marks Space Craft's fourth community in the neighborhood, with construction slated to begin in fall 2026 and an opening anticipated in fall 2028. Anchor Loans is providing a $70.6 million senior construction loan for the development.

DATA POINTS

Completion: fall 2028

Notable Features: Eight-story development with 8,000 square feet of amenity space, including a rooftop terrace, coworking lounge, fitness and yoga spaces, and an outdoor spa area with a pool.

Project Notes: The community will occupy the site formerly home to the Textile Rubber & Chemical Company, which operated there from the 1950s through 2023.
