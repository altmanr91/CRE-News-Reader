Kayla Lee of Serhant: 5 Questions

Commercial Observer — Tue, 20 Oct 2026 12:00:02 +0000

SUMMARY

Feature / Profile

This article features an interview with Kayla Lee, a prominent broker at Serhant, who discusses her decade-long career in real estate, during which she has sold over $1 billion across more than 850 units. Lee specializes in new development projects, particularly in Brooklyn and Queens, where she leverages her hyper-local expertise. She highlights the appeal of the outer boroughs for new development due to their convenience to Manhattan, vibrant communities, and access to green spaces. Lee also notes evolving buyer preferences, with a growing demand for natural light, higher ceilings, better views, and intelligent design features in residential projects, reflecting a shift towards more time spent at home.
