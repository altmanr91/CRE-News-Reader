Alexandria Shows Positive Signs After Several Depressed Quarters: Earnings

Commercial Observer — Tue, 04 Aug 2026 19:34:10 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Earnings Report

Alexandria Real Estate Equities, a life sciences-focused REIT, reported signs of improvement in the second quarter of 2026, with increased leasing activity despite a decline in net income and funds from operations compared to the previous year. The company leased 1 million square feet of space, a significant increase from the prior quarter, driven by strong demand from life science product, service, and device sectors. Executives expressed optimism about the improving economic conditions in science sectors, anticipating further balance sheet stabilization by 2028.
