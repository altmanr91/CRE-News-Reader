Transwestern to manage more than 3 million-square-foot healthcare portfolio across 16 states

REJournals — Tue, 03 Nov 2026 16:35:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Promotion

Transwestern has been appointed by Spring Creek Healthcare Properties to manage a 3.1 million-square-foot healthcare real estate portfolio. The portfolio comprises 47 medical outpatient buildings located across 16 states, including key markets such as Southern California, Phoenix, Denver, Dallas-Fort Worth, Houston, San Antonio, Tampa Bay, and South Florida. Transwestern commenced its management responsibilities on October 1, 2026.
