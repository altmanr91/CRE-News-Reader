MMCC Arranges $19.6M in Financing for Travel Center in Mount Pleasant, Texas

LOAN

REBusinessOnline — Mon, 24 Aug 2026 15:40:05 +0000

SUMMARY

Marcus & Millichap Capital Corp. (MMCC) has secured $19.6 million in financing for an undisclosed 9,557-square-foot travel center in Mount Pleasant, Texas. The five-year loan, arranged through an undisclosed national bank, features a 6.75 percent interest rate. The property includes a fuel station, restaurant, and laundry facilities.
