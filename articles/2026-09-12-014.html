Berkadia closes sale of 160-unit seniors housing property in Evanston

SALE

REJournals — Fri, 11 Sep 2026 14:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Berkadia Seniors Housing & Healthcare has brokered the sale of Trulee Evanston, a 160-unit Class-A senior living community in Evanston, IL. AEW Capital Management sold the property to a national REIT, with Solera Senior Living continuing as the operator. The transaction highlights strong institutional investor interest in high-quality, stabilized seniors housing assets located in markets with limited supply and favorable demographics.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: senior housing

Property Name: Trulee Evanston

Address: 1815 Norwood Ct, Evanston, IL*

Units: 160

Year Built: 2021

Notable Features: Nine-story community offering 127 independent living/assisted living units and 33 memory care units, with amenities including chef-prepared dining, a ninth-floor Skyline Club, fitness and wellness spaces, an outdoor courtyard, theater, art studio, concierge services, local transportation and on-site salon services. Located near downtown Evanston, Northwestern University, healthcare providers, retail, dining and public transportation.

* Address sourced separately, not in original article.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

SELLER: AEW Capital Management

BUYER: large national REIT

SELLER BROKER: Berkadia Seniors Housing & Healthcare — Dave Fasano, Senior Managing Director; Ross Sanders, Senior Managing Director; Cody Tremper, Senior Managing Director; Mike Garbers, Senior Managing Director

OWNER: Solera Senior Living
