Andreessen Horowitz to Open West Palm Office for Defense-Tech Bets

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Fri, 11 Sep 2026 19:15:25 +0000

SUMMARY

Andreessen Horowitz, a prominent venture capital firm, has signed a lease for a new office in West Palm Beach, FL, at Related Ross' CityPlace Tower. This new regional headquarters will house the firm's $1.18 billion American Dynamism fund, focusing on defense-tech investments and is slated to open early next year. The transaction further solidifies West Palm Beach's growing reputation as a tech and finance hub, a transformation driven by Related Ross' significant development initiatives in the area.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Property Name: CityPlace Tower

Address: 525 Okeechobee Boulevard, West Palm Beach, FL

Year Built: 2008

Notable Features: 18-story building spanning 303,519 square feet.

Project Notes: The building was purchased by Stephen Ross for $175 million in 2021.
