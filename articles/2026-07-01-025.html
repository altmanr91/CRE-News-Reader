Digital Realty Pays Blackstone $3.5B For Stake In 3 Virginia Data Centers

ACQUISITION

Bisnow News Feed — Tue, 30 Jun 2026 14:01:17 -0400

SUMMARY

Digital Realty is acquiring Blackstone's majority stake in three Northern Virginia data centers for $3.5 billion in a cash-and-stock deal. The transaction involves Digital Realty taking full ownership of two 96 MW data centers in Manassas and a 96 MW data center in Sterling, which were developed through a 2023 joint venture between the two firms. The facilities are fully leased to investment-grade tenants, with two expected to be fully operational in early 2027 and the third in 2028. This acquisition allows Digital Realty to increase its ownership in high-quality hyperscale assets and expand its strategic private capital platform.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: data center

Sale Price: $3.50B

Occupancy at Sale: 100.0%

Notable Features: Three data centers totaling 288 MW of capacity, fully leased to investment-grade tenants.

Project Notes: The three data centers have a combined value of $7.8 billion, including debt and planned capital expenditures. Payment to Blackstone includes $1.2 billion in cash and $2.3 billion in Digital Realty stock. This transaction follows a 2023 joint venture between Digital Realty and Blackstone to develop hyperscale data centers in Virginia, France, and Germany, a $7 billion partnership where Blackstone held majority stakes and Digital Realty handled construction and operations.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: Digital Realty — Greg Wright, Chief Investment Officer

SELLER: Blackstone

SPONSOR PIPELINE

Northern Virginia data centers: Two preleased facilities totaling 48 MW of capacity, developed in partnership with Realty Income.

Dallas data centers: A pair of data centers developed in partnership with Mitsubishi Corp. in 2023.
