Ruben Cos. Says It Can't Sell, Finance Navy Yard Multifamily Project

Bisnow Latest News Feed — Thu, 17 Sep 2026 15:29:26 -0400

SUMMARY

Development

Ruben Cos. is seeking an extension from the D.C. Zoning Commission for its approved plans to construct a 263-unit multifamily building near Nationals Park. The developer indicated that current market conditions, specifically high interest rates and construction costs, are making it difficult to secure financing or sell the project, necessitating more time to proceed with the development.
