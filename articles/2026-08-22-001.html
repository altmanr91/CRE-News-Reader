CoStar Closes $800M Cash Acquisition of New-Home Platform Zonda

Commercial Observer — Fri, 21 Aug 2026 17:47:49 +0000

SUMMARY

Non-CRE / Business News

CoStar Group has completed its $800 million cash acquisition of Zonda, a platform specializing in new home-construction data, homebuilder software, and residential real estate marketplaces. This strategic move provides CoStar with a significant entry into the U.S. new-home sales market, leveraging Zonda's proprietary lot-level construction and development data, which serves over 3,000 homebuilders and related industry professionals. The acquisition also expands CoStar's consumer marketplace business with Zonda-owned platforms NewHomeSource.com and Livabl, and offers opportunities to integrate Zonda's visualization technology with CoStar's spatial technology for enhanced digital home-shopping experiences.
