Terra Lands $245M Financing for Newly Completed Upland Park Development

LOAN

Connect CRE — Tue, 01 Sep 2026 13:50:57 +0000

SUMMARY

Terra has secured $245 million in permanent financing for the first phase of Upland Park, a newly completed 578-unit multifamily community in West Miami-Dade County. The property, which is part of a larger $1 billion mixed-use development, is already over 60% leased within 90 days of opening. Construction on the second residential phase, comprising 484 additional apartments, is slated to commence later this year.
