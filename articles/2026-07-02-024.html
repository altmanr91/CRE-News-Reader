Moishe Mana buys more land in Miami’s Allapattah

ACQUISITION

The Real Deal — Miami — Wed, 01 Jul 2026 21:15:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Moishe Mana has expanded his extensive land holdings in Miami's Allapattah neighborhood, acquiring a 2.5-acre assemblage for $21.8 million from Allapattah East LLC. The 17 parcels, located between Northwest 20th and 22nd streets, will initially be used for parking, though Mana has not yet finalized long-term development plans. The seller indicated that the sale to a developer with a plan to activate the properties made strategic sense, given their own focus is not primarily on Miami at this time.

DATA POINTS

Address: 2055 Northwest Seventh Avenue, Miami, FL

Size: 108,900 SF
