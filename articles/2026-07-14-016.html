How Compass’ new size could spell new problems

The Real Deal — National — Mon, 13 Jul 2026 12:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

This article discusses the potential legal challenges facing Compass, a major real estate brokerage, following its significant expansion and acquisition of Anywhere Real Estate. A class-action lawsuit has been filed in Florida, alleging that Compass' nationwide transaction fee constitutes a deceptive business practice due to alleged lack of disclosure. While attorneys suggest the legality hinges on proper disclosure, the lawsuit highlights broader concerns about how Compass' increasing market share, which in some cities is projected to exceed 50%, could lead to new legal scrutiny regarding consumer choice, commission rates, and competitive practices. The article also notes other legal challenges, including an antitrust lawsuit filed by Zillow and an investigation by the New York Attorney General's Office into Compass' market share in New York.

DATA POINTS

Compass acquisition of Anywhere Real Estate: $1.6 billion

Projected market share in major cities post-acquisition: Exceed 50%

FTC and DOJ merger guideline threshold: 30%

Compass transaction fee: $495

Compass referral fee program: 10% fee if a sale closes within 24 months
