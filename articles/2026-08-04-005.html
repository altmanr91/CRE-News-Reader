Newmark Arranges Recapitalization of 1,546-Unit Seniors Housing Portfolio in Dallas, Houston

REFINANCE

REBusinessOnline — Mon, 03 Aug 2026 15:17:10 +0000

SUMMARY

Newmark has arranged the recapitalization of a five-property seniors housing portfolio totaling 1,546 units across the Dallas and Houston metropolitan areas. The transaction involved securing a joint venture equity investment from Kayne Anderson Real Estate for the owner, Tradition Senior Living, and also included new financing for the portfolio. The properties, which offer independent living, assisted living, and memory care services, boasted a strong combined occupancy rate of approximately 96 percent at the time of the deal.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: seniors housing

Units: 1,546

Occupancy at Close: 96.0%

Project Notes: The portfolio includes The Tradition-Clearfork in Fort Worth, The Tradition-Lovers Lane and The Tradition-Prestonwood in Dallas, and The Tradition-Woodway and The Tradition-Buffalo Speedway in Houston.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

OWNER: Tradition Senior Living

EQUITY/FINANCING: Kayne Anderson Real Estate
