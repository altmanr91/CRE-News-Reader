Manhattan Investment Sales Dip but Remain Ahead of 2025: Report

Commercial Observer — Thu, 22 Oct 2026 11:00:45 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Investment Sales Report

Manhattan's investment sales volume experienced a 27% decline in Q3 2026, reaching $2.5 billion, marking the second consecutive quarterly drop. Despite this, the year-to-date sales volume of $9.61 billion is up 4% from 2025, and the market is on track for a record year in terms of total transactions. The market continues to see strong underlying fundamentals, particularly in multifamily and retail sectors, which are attracting capital despite headwinds like higher interest rates and geopolitical uncertainty. The buyer pool has shifted, with private buyers leading and REITs significantly increasing their share of dollar volume.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Manhattan's investment sales market is experiencing a shift in buyer demographics, with private buyers taking the lead and REITs significantly increasing their market share from 7.3% in 2025 to 36% year-to-date in 2026. International and foreign buyers are pulling back. Multifamily and retail sectors show strong momentum, with multifamily dollar volume up 74% year-to-date and retail experiencing a 54% quarterly increase in dollar volume, driven by owner-user purchases. Development activity has slowed, with year-to-date dollar volume down 17% and average prices per square foot nearly halved from 2025 levels.
