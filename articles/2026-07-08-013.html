Marcus & Millichap closes sale of mixed-use property in Tinley Park

SALE

REJournals — Tue, 07 Jul 2026 15:20:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Marcus & Millichap has brokered the sale of Boulevard at Central Station, a recently built mixed-use property in Tinley Park, IL. The asset, featuring 66 multifamily units and five ground-floor retail spaces, was marketed exclusively by Marcus & Millichap, which also procured the buyer. The property's prime location across from Harmony Square and the Tinley Park Metra Station offers residents convenient access to downtown Chicago.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: mixed-use

Property Name: Boulevard at Central Station

Address: 6701-6723 South Street, Tinley Park, IL

Size: 73,216 SF

Year Built: 2021

Notable Features: Amenities include a clubhouse, fitness studio, yoga room, smart home technology, an outdoor lounge, and a dog wash. Located directly across from Harmony Square and the Tinley Park Metra Station.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

SELLER BROKER: Marcus & Millichap — Ryan Engle, Investment Specialist; Andrean Angelov, Investment Specialist
