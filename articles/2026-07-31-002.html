NYC Taxi and Limousine Commission Takes 39K SF at AmTrustRE’s 250 Broadway

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Thu, 30 Jul 2026 20:37:40 +0000

SUMMARY

The New York City Taxi and Limousine Commission (TLC) is relocating its headquarters to 39,000 square feet at AmTrustRE's 250 Broadway in Manhattan's Financial District. The agency will occupy the entire fourth floor and a portion of the fifth floor, with an expected move-in during the first quarter of 2027. This lease brings the 31-story office tower to 90 percent leased, reflecting strong demand for quality office environments in Downtown Manhattan.

Project Notes: The tenant is relocating its headquarters from 33 Beaver Street in the Financial District. The space includes private offices, conference rooms, and a hearing room.
