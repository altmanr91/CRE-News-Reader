New Tenants Bring DivcoWest’s Hudson Square AI Hub to Full Occupancy

LEASE

Connect CRE — Wed, 30 Sep 2026 22:06:06 +0000

SUMMARY

DivcoWest's 325 Hudson St., a multi-tenant AI tech hub in Hudson Square, has achieved full occupancy following recent leasing activity. The final available spaces in the 10-story building were leased by an AI startup, an AI partner for investment management, and a life sciences enterprise. A DivcoWest executive noted that New York City is a significant market for AI innovators, with substantial active demand for space.
