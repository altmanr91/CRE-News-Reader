Ross Tower hits the market as Downtown Dallas sale wave builds

The Real Deal — Texas — Thu, 02 Jul 2026 18:44:33 +0000

SUMMARY

Property Listing / For Sale

Ross Tower, a 45-story, 1.1 million-square-foot office tower in Downtown Dallas, is being marketed for sale to investors. The property, owned by a partnership including Bandera Ventures, HPI Real Estate, and Second City Real Estate, recently underwent a $14 million capital improvement program. Despite being 60 percent occupied, the tower is positioned as an attractive investment due to its Class A space, in-place rents that are 15 percent below market, and its proximity to the thriving Uptown Dallas financial district.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

The Dallas Central Appraisal District valued Ross Tower at over $99 million for tax purposes. In-place rents at Ross Tower are 15 percent below market rates. Other Downtown Dallas properties, such as Victory Commons One, are also being explored for sale, driven by the growth of Uptown Dallas.
