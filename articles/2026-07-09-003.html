Arlington, Va., Hotel-to-Resi Project Sells for $35M

ACQUISITION

Commercial Observer — Wed, 08 Jul 2026 20:35:45 +0000

SUMMARY

GoodHomes Communities has acquired the Arlington Court Suites, a 187-key extended-stay hotel in Arlington, VA, for $35 million. The buyer plans to convert the 219,000-square-foot property into a 180-unit apartment building, continuing its strategy of repositioning aging hospitality assets into multifamily housing. The transaction was financed with a $34.6 million loan from NewPoint Real Estate Capital, and the deal reflects a growing trend of adaptive reuse projects in the region.
