M&T RCC Provides $141M Bridge Loan to Newly Built Turtle Bay Apartments

LOAN

Connect CRE — Thu, 23 Jul 2026 21:52:31 +0000

SUMMARY

M&T Realty Capital Corporation provided a $141.4 million bridge loan to an ownership group led by Global Holdings, MAG Partners, and Safanad for Anagram Turtle Bay, a newly constructed 194-unit multifamily community in Manhattan's Turtle Bay neighborhood. The three-year loan refinances the property's construction financing and is intended to support the asset through its final lease-up and stabilization phase before securing long-term permanent financing. This bridge financing strategy is crucial for high-quality multifamily assets nearing stabilization in the current market, offering flexibility and execution certainty during the transition.
