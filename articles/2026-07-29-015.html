MAG Capital Partners closes sale of six-property industrial portfolio across Midwest

SALE

REJournals — Tue, 28 Jul 2026 17:07:08 +0000

SUMMARY

MAG Capital Partners has completed the sale of a six-property industrial portfolio totaling over 1.37 million square feet to Fundamental Income Properties, a subsidiary of Starwood Property Trust. The approximately $89 million transaction involved long-term, net-leased industrial manufacturing assets located across the Midwest.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Size: 1,370,000 SF

Sale Price: $89.0M

Notable Features: Triple-net-lease industrial manufacturing properties.

Project Notes: Assets primarily acquired through MAGCP Industrial Fund II, LP.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

SELLER: MAG Capital Partners — John Dehn, Senior Vice President; Eric Wood, Senior Vice President

BUYER: Fundamental Income Properties

OWNER: Starwood Property Trust
