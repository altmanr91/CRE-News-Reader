Terreno Adds to NYC Portfolio with Red Hook Acquisition

ACQUISITION

Connect CRE — Tue, 18 Aug 2026 21:43:24 +0000

SUMMARY

Terreno Realty Corporation, an industrial real estate owner and operator, has acquired an industrial property in Red Hook, Brooklyn, for approximately $26.3 million. The property, which includes a 28,000-square-foot distribution building on 1.5 acres, is fully leased to a maritime goods and services provider. This acquisition further expands Terreno's significant industrial portfolio in the New York City/Northern New Jersey market.
