Marcus & Millichap Arranges Sale of 516-Unit Self-Storage Facility in Windsor, Colorado

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Tue, 08 Sep 2026 13:05:50 +0000

SUMMARY

Marcus & Millichap has brokered the sale of Falcon Point Self Storage, a 516-unit self-storage facility in Windsor, Colorado. The property, originally built in 2019 and expanded in 2024, features 72,068 net rentable square feet across 14 single-story buildings on 6.8 acres. The transaction terms were not disclosed.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: self-storage

Property Name: Falcon Point Self Storage

Address: 600 Gyrfalcon Ct, Windsor, CO*

Size: 72,068 SF

Year Built: 2019

Site Area: 6.8 acres

Notable Features: The facility includes climate-controlled units, traditional drive-up access, dedicated vehicle storage, 100 surface parking spaces, LED lighting, gated entry with digital keypad access, 24/7 video surveillance, and an onsite management office.

Project Notes: The facility was expanded in 2024 with an additional 51,530 square feet.

* Address sourced separately, not in original article.
