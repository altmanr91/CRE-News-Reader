For Manhattan Class B Office, the Days of Optional Amenities Are Past

Commercial Observer — Tue, 08 Sep 2026 13:00:16 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

The Manhattan office market is experiencing a notable shift, with demand for Class B and C office space rising as Class A desirability slightly wanes. This trend is driven by less Class A availability, increasing Class A rents, and a competitive environment within the Class B sector that necessitates enhanced amenities. Data indicates that new leasing for Class B and C office space in the first half of 2026 surpassed pre-pandemic averages, with these classes now accounting for approximately 40% of new leasing activity. This suggests that the city's office recovery is broadening beyond just trophy buildings, with price-sensitive and mid-market demand returning, provided that Class B properties offer amenities previously common only in Class A spaces.
