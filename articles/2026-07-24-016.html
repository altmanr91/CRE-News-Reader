Boston Children’s Hospital sells campus for $62M in leaseback deal

SALE

The Real Deal — National — Thu, 23 Jul 2026 21:15:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Boston Children's Hospital has sold its 114,000-square-foot clinical campus in Peabody, MA, to an affiliate of Barings for $62 million in a sale-leaseback transaction. This strategic move provides the hospital with a significant cash infusion and relieves it of debt obligations, while ensuring its continued long-term presence and operations at the North Shore location, where it has been serving the community for three decades.
