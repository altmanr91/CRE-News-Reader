Irvine Company Secures $157M Multifamily Refi in L.A.’s Playa Vista

REFINANCE

Commercial Observer — Fri, 30 Oct 2026 19:05:55 +0000

SUMMARY

Irvine Company has secured a $157.2 million refinancing loan from Capital One for its 309-unit luxury apartment property, Villas at Playa Vista – Montecito, located in Los Angeles' Playa Vista neighborhood. This new Fannie Mae loan replaces a previous $136 million loan, increasing the financing by approximately $21 million. The property, which opened in 2016, offers one- and two-bedroom apartments and a range of amenities.

DATA POINTS

Notable Features: Luxury apartment community with a swimming pool, fitness center, coworking space, and conference rooms.

Project Notes: The 2.6-acre site was previously acquired by Irvine Company from Brookfield in 2015. The property is part of the larger 460-acre Playa Vista master-planned development.
