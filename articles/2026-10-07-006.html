Agora Realty Breaks Ground on Final Phase of $375M Mixed-Use Entertainment Project in North Las Vegas

CONSTRUCTION

REBusinessOnline — Wed, 07 Oct 2026 15:32:30 +0000

SUMMARY

Agora Realty has commenced the third and final phase of Hylo Park, a substantial mixed-use entertainment development in North Las Vegas. This $375 million phase, spanning 25 acres, will introduce a 250,000-square-foot indoor multi-sport field house, an existing multi-sport arena, a college preparatory sports academy, outdoor public spaces, retail, restaurants, a family entertainment center, and a 177-key Hilton Embassy Suites. The project is expected to be completed in late 2027, building on previous phases that included a grocery-anchored retail center and a residential neighborhood.
