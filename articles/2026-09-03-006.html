Holladay, Urban League Open East Nashville Affordable Housing Community

DEVELOPMENT

REBusinessOnline — Thu, 03 Sep 2026 13:27:25 +0000

SUMMARY

Holladay Ventures and the Urban League of Middle Tennessee have completed Park24, a 140-unit affordable housing community in East Nashville's McFerrin Park neighborhood. The development offers one-, two-, and three-bedroom apartments for households earning between 30% and 80% of the area median income. The project also includes an onsite empowerment center operated by the Urban League and ground-level commercial space.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Park24

Address: 660 Joseph Ave, Nashville, TN

Units: 140

Notable Features: Affordable housing for households earning 30-80% of AMI; includes an onsite empowerment center and ground-level commercial space.

Project Notes: Capital partners included Amazon Housing Fund, U.S. Bancorp Impact Finance, Cedar Rapids Bank and Trust, and a $2 million grant from Barnes Fund.
