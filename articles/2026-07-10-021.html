“Housing for billionaires” is not the problem

The Real Deal — New York — Thu, 09 Jul 2026 13:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

This opinion piece argues that opposition to "housing for billionaires" is a misguided political tactic that distracts from the real causes of New York City's housing affordability crisis. The author contends that wealthy buyers have a minimal impact on the overall housing supply and that the focus should instead be on increasing housing production of all types. The article criticizes past City Council members, including former Council member Ben Kallos, for failing to support broad housing development and for succumbing to constituent pressure against market-rate projects, particularly in affluent areas. The author highlights that districts with low housing production, like Kallos' former district, contribute significantly to the supply-demand imbalance, and suggests that new administrative rules aimed at accelerating projects in such areas are a necessary step.
