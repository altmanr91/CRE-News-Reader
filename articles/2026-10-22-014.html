Integrate Construction Partners wraps construction of fleet maintenance building for city of Clayton

CONSTRUCTION

REJournals — Thu, 22 Oct 2026 09:32:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Integrate Construction Partners has completed the first phase of the City of Clayton's $11.7 million Municipal Maintenance Facility project, delivering a new 5,750-square-foot fleet maintenance building. This initial phase, which began in October 2025, involved constructing a facility designed to maintain the city's public works fleet, featuring multiple service bays, offices, and specialized equipment. The project team successfully managed significant logistical challenges, including working near a Metro Transit easement and coordinating deliveries under a low overpass, while also addressing environmental remediation for contaminated soils and groundwater. The second phase, expected to finish in January 2027, will involve extensive renovations to an existing 17,000-square-foot building and the construction of additional structures like a truck wash bay and storage canopies.
