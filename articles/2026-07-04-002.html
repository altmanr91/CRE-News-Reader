Stephen Wendell of Mountain Shore Properties: 5 Questions

Commercial Observer — Fri, 03 Jul 2026 14:00:33 +0000

SUMMARY

Feature / Profile

This article is a Q&A interview with Stephen Wendell, CEO of Mountain Shore Properties, discussing the firm's history, diversified portfolio, and development strategy. Mountain Shore Properties, founded by Wendell's father in the 1980s, has grown into a national real estate development company with over $400 million in project value, focusing on hospitality, residential, and office properties across more than 25 cities in 11 states. Wendell emphasizes the firm's intentional diversification across asset classes and geographies, with a particular focus on hotel development, often leveraging existing market knowledge from previous projects to expand into other asset types.
