NYC Pushes To Use Opportunity Zone 2.0 For Mamdani's Housing Goals

Bisnow Latest News Feed — Wed, 16 Sep 2026 20:16:03 -0400

SUMMARY

Infrastructure Investment / Government Policy

New York City is exploring the use of an "Opportunity Zone 2.0" framework to support Council Member Mamdani's housing objectives. This initiative aims to facilitate both the development of new affordable housing units and the preservation of existing ones within the city.
