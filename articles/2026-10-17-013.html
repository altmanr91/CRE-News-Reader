Habitat breaks ground on redevelopment of LeClaire Courts public housing community in Chicago’s Garfield Ridge

CONSTRUCTION

REJournals — Fri, 16 Oct 2026 14:43:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Habitat and Cabrera Capital have broken ground on the first residential phase of the LeClaire Courts redevelopment, a multiphase project transforming a former public housing site in Chicago's Garfield Ridge neighborhood. This initial phase involves the construction of two six-story mixed-use buildings with 183 apartments, 90% of which will be affordable, along with a 14,000-square-foot early childhood education center and ground-floor retail. The comprehensive redevelopment aims to create a mixed-income, mixed-use community with up to 700 residential units and 440,000 square feet of commercial space, reconnecting the site with the surrounding area and providing essential services and economic opportunities.

FINANCING

Funding for the $119 million first phase includes a $27 million capital loan from the Chicago Housing Authority, $9.5 million in city multifamily program funds, $5.5 million in tax-increment financing, $33.7 million in Low-Income Housing Tax Credit equity, $1.25 million in Illinois Affordable Housing Tax Credit equity, and a $1.2 million deferred developer fee.
