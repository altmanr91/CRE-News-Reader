Sapien AI Signs 11K-SF Lease at Kaufman’s 48 West 25th Street

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Mon, 03 Aug 2026 11:00:18 +0000

SUMMARY

Sapien AI, a financial and operations analytics firm, has signed a lease for 10,500 square feet, encompassing the entire fifth floor, at 48 West 25th Street in Midtown South. This relocation from its previous offices at 184 Fifth Avenue provides the AI firm with a full-floor headquarters in the NoMad area. The deal reflects the continued expansion of New York's AI sector, with the new space designed to match the company's growth and creative energy.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Address: 48 West 25th Street, New York City, NY

Size: 10,500 SF

Rental Rate: $80.00/SF/yr

Notable Features: Full-floor headquarters in the NoMad area, located within a block of Madison Square Park.

Project Notes: The building was recently acquired by Kaufman Organization from Savanna for $125 million in June 2026, as part of a two-building portfolio including 24-28 West 25th Street.
