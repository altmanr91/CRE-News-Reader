Former Factory to Receive New Life as Affordable Homes in Upstate NY

DEVELOPMENT

Connect CRE — Tue, 01 Sep 2026 21:52:20 +0000

SUMMARY

A former factory in Tupper Lake, NY, is being redeveloped into an 80-unit affordable housing property with additional commercial space. The $46 million project, a collaboration between Housing Visions, Lahinch Group, and Braxton Capital, will transform the long-vacant Oval Wood Dish Factory into modern, all-electric apartments for households earning up to 90% of the Area Median Income. This development is part of Governor Hochul's broader $25 billion Housing Plan aimed at creating or preserving 100,000 affordable homes across New York.
