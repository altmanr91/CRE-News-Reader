Savills Inks 20K-SF Deal at Rudin’s 560 Lexington Avenue

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Thu, 27 Aug 2026 18:10:26 +0000

SUMMARY

Savills, a global real estate advisory firm, has expanded its New York City footprint by leasing 20,481 square feet at Rudin's 560 Lexington Avenue. The firm will occupy the entire third floor of the Midtown East tower for a five-year term, accommodating its program and project management group. This expansion follows Savills' recent acquisition and launch of Eastdil Secured Savills, signaling a strategic growth in its platform.
