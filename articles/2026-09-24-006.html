CBRE Arranges Sale of 384-Unit Apartment Community in Somerset, New Jersey

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Thu, 24 Sep 2026 13:48:28 +0000

SUMMARY

CBRE has facilitated the off-market sale of The Grove at Somerset, a 384-unit apartment community in Franklin, New Jersey. The property, developed in 2013, was acquired by Los Angeles-based owner-operator TruAmerica from Harbor Group International. The community offers a mix of studio, one-, two-, and three-bedroom units, with 58 designated for affordable housing.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: The Grove at Somerset

Address: 500 Bristol Blvd, Somerset, NJ*

Units: 384

Year Built: 2013

Site Area: 41 acres

Notable Features: Amenities include a pool with a sundeck, resident clubhouse with a lounge area, business center, fitness center, playground and a dog park. 58 units are designated as affordable housing.

* Address sourced separately, not in original article.
