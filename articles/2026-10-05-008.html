Sports Companies Sign Two Industrial Leases Totaling 150,000 SF in San Antonio

LEASE

REBusinessOnline — Mon, 05 Oct 2026 13:54:23 +0000

SUMMARY

Two sports-related companies, M Padel and Socceroof, have signed industrial leases totaling 150,000 square feet in San Antonio. The companies will occupy former distribution buildings on the city's northeast side, with one tenant taking 50,000 square feet and the other 150,000 square feet. The transactions reflect continued demand for large-format industrial spaces for experiential and recreational uses.

DATA POINTS

Address: 6558 E. Campus Drive, San Antonio, TX
