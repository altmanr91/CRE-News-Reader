Dwight Capital Provides $66M HUD-Insured Construction Loan for Abilene Multifamily Project

LOAN

REBusinessOnline — Thu, 23 Jul 2026 15:18:27 +0000

SUMMARY

Dwight Capital has provided a $66 million HUD-insured construction loan for The Lariat at Abilene, a 312-unit multifamily project in West Texas. The garden-style development will feature 13 three-story buildings with a mix of one- and two-bedroom units, along with a clubhouse and various amenities. The financing was originated through HUD's 221(d)(4) program on behalf of Martin Inderman Development.

Notable Features: Garden-style property with a pool, fitness center, pickleball court, and outdoor grilling and dining areas.
