Hindu nonprofit putting dent in Denver’s affordable housing shortage

DEVELOPMENT

The Real Deal — National — Fri, 17 Jul 2026 18:21:33 +0000

SUMMARY

Mounashram, a Hindu nonprofit, is planning to convert the former Highlands Lutheran Church building in Denver's Berkeley neighborhood into 10 income-restricted apartments for seniors. The nonprofit acquired the 70-year-old, 14,000-square-foot property in February 2024 for $1.5 million. This redevelopment project aims to address Denver's significant affordable housing shortage, with the nonprofit prioritizing community needs over maximizing profit by preserving the existing structure for residential use.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Denver's multifamily market had a deficit of at least 13,000 units in 2022, with some estimates indicating the crisis has worsened since then. The National Multifamily Housing Council predicts it could take a century for the market to catch up at the current rate.
