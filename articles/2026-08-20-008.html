Benderson Acquires Three Properties Totaling 400,000 SF in Florida

ACQUISITION

REBusinessOnline — Wed, 19 Aug 2026 14:32:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Benderson Development has expanded its portfolio with the acquisition of three retail properties totaling 400,000 square feet along Florida's west coast. The portfolio includes Granada Plaza, a Publix-anchored center in Dunedin; Venice Market Place, a shopping center in Venice; and a building in Estero occupied by a home improvement retailer.

DATA POINTS

Size: 400,000 SF
