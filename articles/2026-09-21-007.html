Livmark, Hillside to Develop 185-Unit Multifamily Community in Longmont, Colorado

CONSTRUCTION

REBusinessOnline — Mon, 21 Sep 2026 14:17:43 +0000

SUMMARY

Livmark Communities and Hillside Commercial Group have commenced construction on Fielder at Fox Hill, a 185-unit multifamily community in Longmont, Colorado. The project, which began site work in June, will feature a mix of apartments and townhomes with private yards and two-car garages. Greystar has been tapped to manage the property upon its anticipated completion in fall 2027.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Fielder at Fox Hill

Units: 185

Completion: fall 2027

Notable Features: Adjacent to Fox Hill Country Club golf course; includes townhomes with private yards and two-car garages; amenities include a clubhouse, indoor/outdoor fitness, pool, coworking space, sauna, and EV chargers in every garage.

Project Notes: Construction began in June 2026 with initial site work and infrastructure improvements.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

DEVELOPER/SPONSOR: Livmark Communities

DEVELOPER/SPONSOR: Hillside Commercial Group

GENERAL CONTRACTOR: Landmark Construction Solutions

OWNER: Greystar
