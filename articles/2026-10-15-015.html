RESTO Athletic establishes world headquarters RS Impact Exchange in Kansas City

LEASE

REJournals — Thu, 15 Oct 2026 09:42:00 +0000

SUMMARY

RESTO Athletic, an athletic apparel manufacturer, has leased the entire second floor of the historic RS Impact Exchange at 3110 Troost Avenue in Kansas City, MO, for its new world headquarters. This 8,189-square-foot lease represents a significant expansion for the company, which is relocating from a nearby facility. The RS Impact Exchange is a community-centered retail and social impact hub developed by the nonprofit Reconciliation Services, with all tenant lease proceeds reinvested into their client programs and the revitalization of the Troost corridor. The project, which includes a $1.3 million Jackson County investment for accessibility upgrades, is expected to open in early 2027.
