JBL Hopes to Convert Deerfield Beach Retail Center into 736 Apartments

Connect CRE — Mon, 31 Aug 2026 14:20:55 +0000

SUMMARY

Development

JBL is seeking to redevelop the Rivertowne Square retail center in Deerfield Beach, FL, into a mixed-use project featuring 736 apartments and 75,527 square feet of retail space. The company acquired the 13-acre site for $18.85 million and is currently awaiting rezoning approval from the city for its proposed two eight-story apartment buildings and two parking garages.
