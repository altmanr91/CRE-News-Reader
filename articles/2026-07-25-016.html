Governors Island Issues RFEI for Revitalization of Former YMCA, Theater

Connect CRE — Fri, 24 Jul 2026 21:18:53 +0000

SUMMARY

Property Listing / For Sale

The Trust for Governors Island has issued a Request for Expressions of Interest (RFEI) for the revitalization of two historic buildings, Building 324 and Building 330, located within the Governors Island Historic District. The RFEI seeks proposals for arts and cultural, recreational, or other public-facing uses to bring these former YMCA recreation center and movie theater buildings back into active, year-round use. The Trust is looking for visionary partners with bold ideas to transform these iconic structures into vibrant public spaces.
