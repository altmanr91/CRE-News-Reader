A big impact: Interstate Capital planning five industrial developments along Michigan’s Interstate-94 corridor

DEVELOPMENT

REJournals — Fri, 11 Sep 2026 09:47:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Interstate Capital is undertaking five significant industrial developments along Michigan's I-94 corridor, collectively spanning over 600 acres and projected to offer more than 5 million square feet of industrial space with an investment approaching $500 million. These projects include specialized cold storage and distribution facilities near Detroit Metropolitan Airport, two large industrial campuses near the Canadian border, and a Class-A logistics facility in Kalamazoo Township. The developments aim to expand modern industrial capacity, support various supply chains, and enhance logistics and distribution capabilities across the Great Lakes region and the U.S.-Canada trade corridor.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

DEVELOPER/SPONSOR: Interstate Capital

DEVELOPER/SPONSOR: CMAC Transportation

SPONSOR PIPELINE

DTW Romulus Cold Storage: A 257,950-square-foot temperature-controlled facility on 21 acres in Romulus, expected to break ground in Fall 2026.

Kalamazoo Commerce Park: A commerce park on 56 acres in Kalamazoo Township with two 242,000-square-foot Class A warehouse buildings, breaking ground in Fall 2026.

Port Huron Commerce Park: A heavy-industrial campus on 224 acres in Port Huron Township with an initial 252,000-square-foot building expandable to 399,000 square feet, and a separate 301,320-square-foot building, expected to break ground in Spring 2027.

Bluewater Commerce Park: A master-planned industrial campus on 295 acres in Kimball Township with capacity for up to 3.5 million square feet, expected to break ground in Spring-Summer 2027.
