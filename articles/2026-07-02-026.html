Mixed-use mania: Mapping mall redevelopments planned in South Florida

The Real Deal — Miami — Wed, 01 Jul 2026 17:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Development / Redevelopment

South Florida is experiencing a wave of mixed-use redevelopments at several shopping malls across Miami-Dade, Broward, and Palm Beach counties. These projects aim to transform traditional retail centers into vibrant districts by adding thousands of apartments, alongside new retail, restaurants, offices, hotels, and entertainment venues. This trend is driven by the region's strong housing demand and mall owners' efforts to revitalize properties that have faced declining foot traffic and department store closures. However, many of these ambitious plans have encountered significant hurdles, including political opposition and legal challenges, impacting their progress.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Miami International Mall's CMBS loan has been in special servicing since February 2024, with the property valued at $143 million in June 2025, a 60% decrease from its $391 million valuation over a decade prior. As of September, the mall was 85% occupied overall, while the collateral portion backing the loan was 75% occupied.
