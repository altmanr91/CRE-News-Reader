Baptist Health Spends $39M on Coral Gables Office Building

ACQUISITION

Connect CRE — Thu, 08 Oct 2026 14:35:03 +0000

SUMMARY

Baptist Health South Florida acquired a 124,168-square-foot office building in Coral Gables from City National Bank of Florida for $39 million. The nonprofit hospital operator has not yet disclosed its plans for the property, which includes 47,732 square feet of leasable office space and a 122-space parking garage. City National Bank sold the asset after relocating its headquarters in 2025.
