Hiffman National surpasses 100 million square feet of CRE under management

REJournals — Mon, 19 Oct 2026 17:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Company Growth

Hiffman National has surpassed 100 million square feet of commercial real estate under management, reaching over 104 million square feet across 665 properties nationwide as of September 2026. This milestone reflects significant organic growth driven by existing clients expanding into new markets, allowing the firm to extend its hands-on management approach across various property types including office, medical office, industrial, and retail. The firm's expansion includes new assignments in Arizona and a 167,000-square-foot office property in downtown Chicago, demonstrating its ability to grow alongside its client base while maintaining service quality.
