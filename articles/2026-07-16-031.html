Dallas-Fort Worth home starts down 12% from last year as interest rates remain elevated

The Real Deal — Texas — Wed, 15 Jul 2026 17:45:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Housing Report

Home starts in the Dallas-Fort Worth Metroplex declined by nearly 12% year-over-year through mid-2026, with 11,290 homes breaking ground in Q2 2026, a 6.5% decrease from the previous year. This slowdown is attributed to rising mortgage rates, which have climbed above 6.5%, and broader economic uncertainties including oil price volatility and inflation. Despite these challenges, homebuyers are still proceeding with purchases, leading to the lowest level of finished vacant new homes in two years, potentially aided by builder discounts. The rental sector has been particularly impacted, with new rental unit starts dropping significantly as profitability wanes.
