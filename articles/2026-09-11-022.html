SUMMARY

Starlight Investments, a Canadian real estate firm, has successfully closed a £680 million fund dedicated to developing 6,000 build-to-rent (BTR) homes across the UK. This significant capital raise is expected to provide a substantial boost to the UK's BTR sector, which has experienced slower growth compared to other residential segments.
