JLL Arranges $53M Refinancing for Apartment Community in Huntsville

REFINANCE

REBusinessOnline — Tue, 06 Oct 2026 15:29:55 +0000

SUMMARY

JLL has arranged a $53 million refinancing loan for Arcadia Huntsville, a 250-unit apartment community in Huntsville, AL. The property, developed by Bobo Development Group and completed in 2024, is located within the Cumming Research Park. Revitate is a limited partner in the project.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Arcadia Huntsville

Address: 4810 Bradford Drive, Huntsville, AL

Units: 250

Loan Amount: $53.0M

Loan Per Unit: $212,000/unit (calculated)

Year Built: 2024

Notable Features: Features 18,000 square feet of retail, a heated saltwater pool, clubhouse with demonstration kitchen, fitness center, coworking space, dog park, electric vehicle charging stations, and outdoor grilling stations and lounge areas with gas fire pits.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

SPONSOR: Bobo Development Group

LENDER: JLL — Gregg Shapiro; Anthony Sansone; Kelsey Bawcombe; Connor McCarthy; Jada Cooper

EQUITY/FINANCING: Revitate
