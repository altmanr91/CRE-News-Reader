NAI DiLeo-Bram Markets Piscataway Industrial in Key Supply Chain Corridor

Connect CRE — Mon, 27 Jul 2026 21:39:59 +0000

SUMMARY

Property Listing / For Sale

NAI DiLeo-Bram & Co. is marketing a 74,500-square-foot industrial building at 240 Circle Drive North in Piscataway, NJ. The property, which includes 6,000 square feet of office space and sits on five acres, is being offered to owner-users looking to capitalize on its strategic location within a critical Central New Jersey supply chain corridor. The brokerage team emphasized the rarity of such opportunities for user-buyers in the current market, highlighting the property's heavy power, functional logistics infrastructure, and excellent regional connectivity.
