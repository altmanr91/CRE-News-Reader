S3 Capital Lends $53M for Upper East Side Condo Tower

LOAN

Commercial Observer — Mon, 19 Oct 2026 15:49:09 +0000

SUMMARY

A joint venture between Grand Cru Real Estate and Visabe secured $52.5 million in construction financing from S3 Capital for a 20-story condominium tower on Manhattan's Upper East Side. The project, located at 1527 York Avenue, will feature 28 units ranging from one to five bedrooms, along with a suite of resident amenities. S3 Capital expressed confidence in the sponsorship's ability to execute, citing their successful track record in the submarket.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: condo

Address: 1527 York Avenue, New York City, NY

Units: 28

Loan Amount: $52.5M

Loan Per Unit: $1,875,000/unit (calculated)

Notable Features: 20-story tower with units ranging from one to five bedrooms; amenities include a full-time doorman, fitness center, recreation lounge, and children's playroom.

Project Notes: Construction is slated to begin in early 2027.
