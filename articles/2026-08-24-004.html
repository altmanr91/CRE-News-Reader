Continuing to bet on the Minneapolis-area industrial market

CONSTRUCTION

REJournals — Mon, 24 Aug 2026 09:07:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Oppidan Investment Company has broken ground on Rogers Gateway, a 117,000-square-foot speculative industrial project in Rogers, MN, with an estimated cost of $15 million. The development, scheduled for completion in November 2026, aims to provide flexible space for a variety of industrial users, including warehouse, distribution, manufacturing, showroom, and office components. The project's strategic location with direct visibility and access to Interstate 94 is a key selling point, capitalizing on the strong demand for new industrial construction in the growing northwest Twin Cities submarket.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

The Minneapolis-area industrial market continues to show strong demand for new construction, particularly in well-located areas with strong highway access, visibility, and flexibility for various industrial users. The northwest Twin Cities is identified as one of the strongest submarkets for industrial leasing activity, driven by access to I-94, population growth, and connectivity to broader regional markets.
