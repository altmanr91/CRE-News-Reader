Northmarq arranges $50 million in financing for 235-unit apartment community in Bloomington

LOAN

REJournals — Tue, 13 Oct 2026 15:21:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Northmarq's Minneapolis Debt + Equity team successfully arranged $50 million in bridge loan financing for Ardor on the Bluffs, a 235-unit luxury multifamily community in Bloomington, Minnesota. The financing, secured through a life insurance company, is structured as a three-year loan with extension options. The property, completed in December 2024, offers a strong amenity package and is strategically located near major transportation hubs and employment centers, underscoring the continued demand for high-quality multifamily assets in the Bloomington market.

DATA POINTS

Units: 235

Notable Features: Six-story luxury multifamily community with studio, one-, and two-bedroom homes, some with in-unit fireplaces, built-in desks, quartz countertops, and patios. Amenities include lounges, party rooms, a spa, pool, fitness center, underground parking, and river valley views. Located minutes from MSP International Airport and the Mall of America.

Project Notes: The loan is a three-year bridge loan with extensions.
