Developers score $368M in loans for South Florida projects

LOAN

The Real Deal — Miami — Thu, 09 Jul 2026 20:20:45 +0000

SUMMARY

South Florida developers recently secured $368 million in financing for a diverse range of commercial projects across the tri-county area. HA Emprendimientos obtained a $111 million construction loan for Sense22, a 328-unit luxury multifamily tower in Miami's Edgewater, with 40% of units designated as affordable housing under Florida's Live Local Act. Kolter Group and Rockpoint received a $107.8 million construction loan for The Sutton, a 432-unit garden-style apartment complex in Palm Beach Gardens, which includes the acquisition of a 17.6-acre site. Sina Companies secured a $64.8 million construction loan for The Health Park at Avenir, a medical campus in Palm Beach Gardens fully leased to Jupiter Medical Center. Additionally, Dependable Equities landed a $54 million pre-construction loan for two luxury residential towers in Fort Lauderdale, while Grover Corlew received $30 million in financing for the Hillsboro Center office complex in Deerfield Beach.
