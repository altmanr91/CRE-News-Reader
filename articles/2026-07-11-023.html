Acadia Realty shops North Side retail portfolio near Lincoln Park for $60M

The Real Deal — Chicago — Fri, 10 Jul 2026 17:30:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Property Listing / For Sale

Acadia Realty Trust is marketing a five-building retail portfolio near Lincoln Park and Lakeview in Chicago, with an expected sale price of approximately $60 million. The portfolio, totaling 103,526 square feet, is 95 percent occupied with a strong tenant mix including a grocery store anchor. Acadia assembled these properties between 2007 and 2012 for a total of $51.2 million.
