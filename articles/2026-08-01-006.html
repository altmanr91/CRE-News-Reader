Hanover, TIG Complete 213,425 SF Spec Industrial Project in South Houston

CONSTRUCTION

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 31 Jul 2026 14:24:04 +0000

SUMMARY

A partnership between Hanover Co. and Transwestern Investment Group (TIG) has completed Kirby 288, a 213,425-square-foot speculative industrial project in South Houston. The development, which began construction in August 2025, features a cross-dock configuration, 36-foot clear heights, and excess trailer storage. Colliers is handling the leasing for the new facility.
