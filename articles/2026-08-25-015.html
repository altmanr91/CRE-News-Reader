Transwestern Signs New Grocer Lease at 680 North Lake Shore Drive

LEASE

Connect CRE — Mon, 24 Aug 2026 22:00:04 +0000

SUMMARY

Transwestern announced that Golub & Company and TopMed Realty, owners of 680 N. Lake Shore Drive, signed a new lease with 680 Lakeside Market for a 5,512-square-foot grocery store. This addition is expected to enhance the building's reputation as a hub for wellness and convenience within Streeterville's Medical Research District, improving the daily experience for building occupants.
