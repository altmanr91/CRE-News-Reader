CIM defaults on Kaiser HQ tower as office distress in Oakland spreads

FORECLOSURE

The Real Deal — San Francisco — Tue, 21 Jul 2026 19:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

CIM Group is facing a potential foreclosure on its $97.1 million loan for 1 Kaiser Plaza, a 28-story office tower in Oakland, after the property's value plummeted to $53.5 million from $212 million in 2016. The loan entered special servicing in October 2024 following a significant downsizing by the largest tenant, Kaiser Permanente, which reduced its headquarters footprint by one-third. Despite CIM Group remaining current on interest payments, the lender has demanded immediate repayment and placed the property's income in trust, highlighting the widespread distress in the Oakland office market.
