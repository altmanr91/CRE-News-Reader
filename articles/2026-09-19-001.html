Asian-American Deli Café Hestia Signs 8K-SF Lease at 570 Lexington Avenue

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Fri, 18 Sep 2026 19:30:09 +0000

SUMMARY

Café Hestia, an Asian-American deli, has secured an 8,000-square-foot lease for 20 years at 570 Lexington Avenue in Manhattan, marking its fourth location in the borough. The space will serve as a new cafe and likely a catering facility. The landlord noted that the cafe's all-day dining concept and diverse offerings align with their strategy to bring lasting value to tenants and the surrounding neighborhood.
