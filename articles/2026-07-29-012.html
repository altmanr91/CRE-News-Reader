Scannell Properties developing master-planned industrial park in Chambers County

CONSTRUCTION

REJournals — Wed, 29 Jul 2026 09:46:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Scannell Properties has commenced construction on the first phase of Chambers99 Logistics Center, a master-planned industrial park in Chambers County, Texas. This initial phase includes two speculative industrial buildings totaling 491,400 square feet, with future phases potentially adding up to 1 million square feet. The development is strategically located along Highway 99, offering direct access to a key transportation corridor and proximity to Port Houston, making it attractive for bulk distribution, manufacturing, and packaging operations.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Property Name: Chambers99 Logistics Center

Size: 491,400 SF

Completion: spring 2027

Phase I: two speculative industrial buildings totaling 491,400 square feet

Total Project (All Phases): up to 1 million square feet of additional industrial space

Site Area: 112 acres

Notable Features: Located along Highway 99, several miles south of Interstate-10 East, providing direct access to a key transportation corridor and near Port Houston. Features include cross-dock and front-load configurations, 32-36 foot clear heights, ESFR sprinkler systems, and ample truck courts.

Project Notes: First phase includes two speculative buildings: a 386,880 SF cross-dock facility and a 104,520 SF front-load facility. Future phases will include build-to-suit facilities.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

DEVELOPER/SPONSOR: Scannell Properties — Ryan Lovell, development manager

ARCHITECT: Goree Architecture

ENGINEER: Langan Engineers

GENERAL CONTRACTOR: 6E Construction

SPONSOR PIPELINE

industrial park in north Houston: Planned along Aldine Westfield Road, adjacent to George Bush Intercontinental Airport, with groundbreaking expected later this year.
