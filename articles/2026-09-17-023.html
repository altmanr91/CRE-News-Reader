SUMMARY

The Cambridge Planning Board has approved the extensive Alewife development plan, a significant mixed-use project that will introduce a substantial amount of housing and lab space, alongside new infrastructure. This approval marks a major step forward for the multi-billion dollar project, which is expected to transform the Alewife area.
