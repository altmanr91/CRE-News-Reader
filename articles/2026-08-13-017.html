Institutional Investor Snares San Dimas Light Industrial Park

ACQUISITION

Connect CRE — Wed, 12 Aug 2026 21:43:39 +0000

SUMMARY

An institutional investor has acquired the San Dimas Commerce Center, a 19-building light industrial park in San Dimas, CA, for $41 million. The property, spanning 179,345 square feet across 10.54 acres, was 90% occupied at the time of sale with a diverse tenant mix of over 70 local and regional businesses. The sale highlights the strong demand from institutional investors for infill small-bay industrial product, which offers both diversified income and future flexibility to sell individual buildings to owner-users.
