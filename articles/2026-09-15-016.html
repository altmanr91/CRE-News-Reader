Marcus & Millichap’s Azzi Group Handles Sale of Section 8 Housing on Catalina Island

SALE

Connect CRE — Mon, 14 Sep 2026 21:53:19 +0000

SUMMARY

The Azzi Group of Marcus & Millichap has facilitated the sale of Tremont Street Apartments, a 62-unit affordable housing complex on Santa Catalina Island, for $8.64 million. The transaction involved navigating a complex structure including a ground lease, HUD approval, a Housing Assistance Payments (HAP) contract assignment, and a Freddie Mac loan assumption, ensuring the preservation of affordable housing in the Avalon community. The property was marketed on behalf of the seller, Black Rose Catalina L.P., and acquired by Tailwind Investment Group.
