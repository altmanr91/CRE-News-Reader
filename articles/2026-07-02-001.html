Greater L.A. Office Leasing Shows Signs of Life — Finally

Commercial Observer — Wed, 01 Jul 2026 21:10:57 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Office Leasing Report

Greater Los Angeles' office market is showing signs of recovery, with nearly 4 million square feet of office space leased in the second quarter, a 15% increase from the previous quarter and 8% year-over-year. This quarterly total is 22% above the five-year average, approaching 2019 levels. However, the recovery is primarily driven by renewals and expansions rather than new demand, and overall availability remains high at 26.6%. Sublease availability has declined for seven consecutive quarters, falling to 6.7 million square feet in Q2.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Landlords are still offering concession packages near historic highs to attract tenants, which keeps net effective rents below quoted rates. Distressed assets transitioning to new ownership, such as Capital Group's $210 million acquisition of Bank of America Plaza, are expected to help accelerate leasing activity.
