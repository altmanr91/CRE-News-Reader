Nadeem Meghji Exits Blackstone After Less Than a Year as Global Real Estate Head

Commercial Observer — Wed, 09 Sep 2026 13:07:40 +0000

SUMMARY

Non-CRE / Business News

Nadeem Meghji is departing from Blackstone after less than a year as the sole global head of real estate. His exit follows other executive departures, including Kathleen McCarthy, who previously served as global co-head of real estate alongside Meghji. David Levine and Giovanni Cutaia have been elevated to fill Meghji's former role, with Blackstone's president emphasizing the firm's continuity and talent despite the departure.
