Sephora Relocates Meatpacking District Store with Long-Term Lease

LEASE

Connect CRE — Wed, 23 Sep 2026 22:11:40 +0000

SUMMARY

Sephora has signed a long-term lease for 4,200 square feet of ground-floor retail space at 401 W. 14th St. in Manhattan's Meatpacking District, relocating its existing store in the neighborhood. The new location, expected to open in Q1 2027, is strategically positioned on a highly visible and trafficked retail corner, adjacent to a major tech flagship store, underscoring the importance of prime visibility for establishing a strong retail presence in this iconic shopping destination.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: retail

Address: 401 W. 14th St., New York City, NY

Size: 4,200 SF

Completion: Q1 2027

Notable Features: Located adjacent to Apple's Meatpacking District flagship store, at a prominent retail corner.
