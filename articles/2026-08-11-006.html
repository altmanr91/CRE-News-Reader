BLS: U.S. Economy Unexpectedly Loses 23,000 Jobs in July

REBusinessOnline — Mon, 10 Aug 2026 13:58:37 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Labor Report

The U.S. economy unexpectedly lost 23,000 nonfarm jobs in July, a significant deviation from the 83,000 gain forecasted by economists. This decline was compounded by downward revisions to May and June's job figures, totaling 103,000 fewer jobs. Despite the job losses, the unemployment rate slightly decreased to 4.1 percent, primarily due to a shrinking labor force. Sector-wise, healthcare continued to add jobs, albeit at a slower pace, while retail trade and financial activities experienced notable declines.

DATA POINTS

Total nonfarm employment change (July): -23,000 jobs

Dow Jones economist forecast: +83,000 jobs

May and June job report revisions: -103,000 jobs combined

Unemployment rate (July): 4.1%

Healthcare employment change (July): +22,000 jobs

Retail trade employment change (July): -19,000 jobs

Local government education employment change (July): -50,000 jobs

Financial activities employment change (July): -14,000 jobs
