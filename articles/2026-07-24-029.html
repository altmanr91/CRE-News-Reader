“It’s out of control”: Inside Chicago’s bidding war summer

The Real Deal — Chicago — Thu, 23 Jul 2026 22:13:29 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

Chicago's residential real estate market is experiencing an unusually hot summer, characterized by competitive bidding wars and rising prices, driven by scarce inventory. The Illinois median home price reached $345,000 in June, a 6.2% annual increase, while Chicago's city-specific median home price surpassed $400,000 in March and has remained above that mark. Brokers note that demand, particularly in neighborhoods like Lincoln Park, is "out of control," leading to homes selling quickly and often above asking price. This trend suggests that the typical post-July 4th slowdown may be shorter than usual, with expectations for continued price growth if interest rates remain below 7%.

DATA POINTS

Illinois median home price (June): $345,000

Annual increase in Illinois median home price: 6.2%

Chicago city-specific median home price (June): $427,500

Lincoln Park average median home price (3 months ending May): $490,000

Average days on market in Lincoln Park: 36 days

30-year, fixed-rate mortgage average (July 16): 6.55%
