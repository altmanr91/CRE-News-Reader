Costa Mesa Multifamily Changes Hands for Value-Add Repositioning

SALE

Connect CRE — Wed, 29 Jul 2026 21:36:05 +0000

SUMMARY

A 41-unit multifamily property in Costa Mesa, known as Sundance West Apartment Homes, has been sold for $16.9 million. The transaction represents a significant increase over its 2018 sale price, reflecting strong investor interest in low-density multifamily assets within Orange County's supply-constrained rental market. The buyer, a professional investor, plans a value-add repositioning, while the local family seller sought to reduce hands-on management by exchanging into an out-of-state asset.
