Greysteel closes $18.1 million sale of 142-unit apartment property in Cudahy

SALE

REJournals — Fri, 04 Sep 2026 14:02:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Greysteel has brokered the $18.1 million sale of Cudahy Commons, a 142-unit multifamily property in Cudahy, Wisconsin. A private investor sold the asset to Peak Capital. The transaction highlights strong investor interest in stabilized multifamily housing, particularly within the Midwest, where a significant majority of investors plan to increase their exposure.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Greysteel's Mid-Year 2026 Investor Sentiment Pulse Check found that 84% of respondents plan to increase their Midwest multifamily exposure over the next 12–18 months. Yield and cash flow were the dominant motivation among Midwest-focused respondents (approximately 71%). Active private investors most frequently targeted $5–20 million transactions.
