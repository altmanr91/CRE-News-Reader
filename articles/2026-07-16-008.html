Andover Properties Buys 134,500 SF Industrial Flex Building in San Antonio

ACQUISITION

REBusinessOnline — Wed, 15 Jul 2026 15:01:20 +0000

SUMMARY

Andover Properties has acquired Rail Drive Commerce Center, a 134,500-square-foot industrial flex building in northeast San Antonio. The property, completed in April and acquired by Andover in May, offers small-bay and flex spaces with move-in ready suites and 25-foot clear heights. Cushman & Wakefield has been appointed as the leasing agent for the property.

DATA POINTS
