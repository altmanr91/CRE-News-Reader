Little-known foreign entity and lender locked in $25M foreclosure of O’Hare hotels

FORECLOSURE

The Real Deal — Chicago — Thu, 23 Jul 2026 22:04:55 +0000

SUMMARY

A dual-branded hotel near O'Hare International Airport is facing foreclosure after its developer allegedly failed to repay a $25 million construction mortgage that matured in 2022. The unpaid balance on the note currently stands at $23 million. The lender has also requested the appointment of a receiver for the property, which was repurposed from an existing building into the Hyatt La Quinta hotel using the 2014 loan.
