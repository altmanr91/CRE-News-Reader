Agemark Acquires, Rebrands Seniors Housing Community in Omaha

ACQUISITION

REBusinessOnline — Thu, 27 Aug 2026 14:45:52 +0000

SUMMARY

Agemark Senior Living, in partnership with an undisclosed investment partner, has acquired Esprit Whispering Ridge, an assisted living and memory care community in Omaha, NE. The property has been rebranded as The Bellflower at Whispering Ridge, and the new owners plan to invest approximately $1.3 million in capital improvements. The community will expand its offerings to include independent living and "assisted living plus" units, bringing Agemark's Omaha portfolio to five communities with over 450 units.
