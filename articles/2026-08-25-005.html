Indian Restaurant Samudhra Takes 10K SF at 312 West 43rd Street

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Mon, 24 Aug 2026 18:47:08 +0000

SUMMARY

Indian restaurant Samudhra has signed a 10,000-square-foot lease for its second U.S. location at The Ellery, a mixed-use property in Hell's Kitchen. The new restaurant will occupy ground-level space at 312 West 43rd Street, enhancing the dining and social experience for both residents and the broader neighborhood. This lease contributes to the landlord's vision of creating a dynamic mixed-use destination and strengthens the property's retail and dining offerings.
