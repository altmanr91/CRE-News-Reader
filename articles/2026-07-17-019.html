David Zaslav seeks to buy Camp Mohawk for $68M

ACQUISITION

The Real Deal — New York — Thu, 16 Jul 2026 22:45:00 +0000

SUMMARY

David Zaslav, CEO of Warner Bros. Discovery, through his Grandview Ventures, has made a $68 million stalking horse bid to acquire Camp Mohawk in White Plains, NY. The summer camp's parent company, Simad Holdings, filed for bankruptcy in June after defaulting on payments to Israeli bondholders and facing allegations of diverted funds. Zaslav's bid sets a floor price for the property, which was appraised at $85.8 million in December 2025, with other bidders able to submit higher offers in an upcoming auction. The acquisition is described as a personal family investment for Zaslav, whose children previously attended the camp.
