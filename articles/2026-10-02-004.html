Helaba Provides $105M Construction Takeout Loan on Nashville Apartments

LOAN

Commercial Observer — Thu, 01 Oct 2026 19:52:52 +0000

SUMMARY

A joint venture between CrossHarbor Capital Partners and Mill Creek Residential secured a $105 million construction takeout loan from Helaba for their newly completed Modera Riverview mixed-use development in Nashville's Germantown neighborhood. The property, which opened in 2024, features 400 multifamily units and nearly 14,000 square feet of fully leased retail space. Helaba was attracted to the project due to the sponsors' experience and Nashville's strong market momentum, which continues to be a key focus for the bank's U.S. real estate platform.
