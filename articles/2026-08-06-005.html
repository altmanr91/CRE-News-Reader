Krea USA Nabs $62M Construction Loan for Lauderhill, Fla., Rental

LOAN

Commercial Observer — Wed, 05 Aug 2026 17:35:07 +0000

SUMMARY

Krea USA secured a $61.8 million construction loan from Abanca Bank for its 385-unit multifamily development, Le Parc at Lauderhill, in Lauderhill, FL. The project, which broke ground in March and is slated for completion next year, will feature three eight-story buildings on a 10-acre site. Although initially planned as workforce housing, the development will now have no rental restrictions, but will retain a $9.1 million tax abatement from the City of Lauderhill.
