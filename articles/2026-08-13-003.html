Fund Administrator Hanover Park Inks 19K-SF Deal at 387 Park Avenue South

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Wed, 12 Aug 2026 18:44:46 +0000

SUMMARY

Hanover Park, an AI-driven fund administrator, is relocating its headquarters to 387 Park Avenue South in NoMad, signing a 19,269-square-foot lease for the entire 10th floor. This marks the firm's fifth office move in two years, reflecting its rapid growth. The landlord noted strong tenant interest in the property, with existing tenants expanding and new companies choosing to locate there, indicating a preference for best-in-class buildings in the Midtown South market.
