Sports-Focused Investment Firm Patricof Takes 8K SF at Marx’s 545 Madison Avenue

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Tue, 29 Sep 2026 15:03:21 +0000

SUMMARY

Patricof, a private investment platform, has signed a 10-year lease for 7,500 square feet at Marx Realty's 545 Madison Avenue in Midtown Manhattan. The firm will occupy the entire 16th floor of the office building, relocating from its current address in the first quarter of 2027. Marx Realty's CEO noted that the building's distinctive design and hospitality-infused sensibility continue to attract high-quality tenants.
