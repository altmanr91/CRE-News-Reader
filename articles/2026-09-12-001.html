Azimuth Sells Bronx Educational Facility to Equality Charter School for $55M

SALE

Commercial Observer — Fri, 11 Sep 2026 20:19:44 +0000

SUMMARY

Azimuth Development Group has sold an educational facility at 801 Bartholdi Street in the East Bronx to Equality Charter School for $54.5 million. The 63,000-square-foot property, completed in 2025, will serve as an elementary school capable of accommodating 900 students. The facility includes two cafeterias, a gymnasium, and rooftop recreational space.
