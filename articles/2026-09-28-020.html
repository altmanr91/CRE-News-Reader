Greater Clarity, Less Speculation, But A Higher Bar: Dublin Data Centre Development Wakes Up

Bisnow Latest News Feed — Mon, 28 Sep 2026 10:50:32 -0400

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

New regulations are significantly impacting Dublin's data center sector, creating a more challenging environment for developers. The new rules aim to reduce the sector's energy consumption and grid impact, necessitating innovative power solutions. This shift is expected to raise the bar for new projects, requiring greater clarity and less speculative development to attract continued investor interest in the market.
