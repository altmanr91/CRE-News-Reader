Subtext Completes Two Student Housing Developments in Florida, Tennessee

CONSTRUCTION

REBusinessOnline — Tue, 06 Oct 2026 15:41:08 +0000

SUMMARY

Subtext has completed two student housing developments: VERVE Orlando, a 626-bed community near the University of Central Florida, and EVER Knoxville, a 498-bed property near the University of Tennessee. Both projects offer a wide array of amenities designed for student living, including fitness centers, study lounges, and various recreational spaces. VERVE Orlando was developed in partnership with FrontRange Capital Partners and Nuveen Real Estate, while EVER Knoxville was a collaboration with FrontRange Capital and TPG Real Estate Partners.
