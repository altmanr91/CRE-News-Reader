West Palm Beach Multifamily Property Converted From Condos Sells for $91M

ACQUISITION

Commercial Observer — Fri, 24 Jul 2026 20:04:23 +0000

SUMMARY

A joint venture between The Kamson Corporation and Scopus Property Management has acquired Ponte Verde at Palm Beach Lakes, a 400-unit multifamily property in West Palm Beach, for $90 million. The property, originally built as multifamily, was converted to condominiums and then restored to rental housing in August 2025. The acquisition was financed with an $81 million loan from Prospect Ridge and Blue Owl, reflecting the lenders' confidence in the buyer's experience and business plan for the well-located community.
