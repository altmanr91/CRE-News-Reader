Peachtree Lends $113M on Apartments’ Conversion to Margaritaville Hotel Savannah

LOAN

Commercial Observer — Mon, 27 Jul 2026 20:43:54 +0000

SUMMARY

Tidal Real Estate Partners secured $113 million in bridge debt from Peachtree Group to convert the recently built Ann Street Lofts multifamily property in Savannah, GA, into a 230-key Margaritaville Hotel. The three-year floating-rate loan will facilitate the conversion of the mass timber multifamily building into a hospitality asset, capitalizing on Savannah's robust tourism market and limited lodging supply in the Historic District. The project is expected to commence in late summer 2026 and debut in summer 2027.

Notable Features: Conversion of Georgia's first large-scale mass timber multifamily project into a hotel, featuring a pool deck, Jimmy Buffett’s Margaritaville restaurant, and LandShark Bar & Grill.

Project Notes: The property was previously a 232-unit multifamily building known as Ann Street Lofts. Tidal Real Estate Partners previously secured a $79 million loan from BGO in May 2024 to refinance Ann Street Lofts shortly after its completion.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Savannah experiences strong tourism with over 18 million annual visitors, coupled with a limited lodging supply within the city’s Historic District.
