NRP Group Breaks Ground on 336-Unit Affordable Housing Project in Southeast Austin

CONSTRUCTION

REBusinessOnline — Tue, 08 Sep 2026 15:24:25 +0000

SUMMARY

The NRP Group, in partnership with the Housing Authority of Travis County, has commenced construction on Catalina, a 336-unit affordable housing development in southeast Austin. The project will feature 12 three-story buildings offering one- to four-bedroom units, with residences reserved for households earning 30 to 70 percent of the area median income. Amenities will include a pool, fitness center, and various community programs, with initial units expected by fall 2027 and full completion by spring 2028.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Catalina

Address: 2702 Catalina Dr, Austin, TX*

Units: 336

Completion: spring 2028

Notable Features: Residences will be reserved for households earning between 30 and 70 percent of the area median income. Amenities include a pool, fitness center, business center, multipurpose community room, children's activity room, playground, green spaces, and outdoor seating areas. Residents will have access to afterschool care, financial literacy courses, ESL classes, and first-time homebuyer programs.
