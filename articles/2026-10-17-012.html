Cravey Real Estate Services brokers sale of former car wash property in Aransas Pass

ACQUISITION

REJournals — Fri, 16 Oct 2026 15:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

DAGER Properties, LLC acquired the former Bay Car Wash property at 952 S. Commercial St. in Aransas Pass, TX, from CCC Real Estate Holding Co., LLC. The 3,496-square-foot, seven-bay car wash facility, situated on 0.64 acres at a signalized corner, had ceased operations and was returned to the lender prior to the sale. Despite challenges including a lack of operational history and significant vandalism during the marketing period, the property attracted a buyer quickly, with the new owner planning to reactivate it as a car wash operation.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: retail

Address: 952 S. Commercial St, Aransas Pass, TX

Size: 3,496 SF

Site Area: 0.64 acres

Notable Features: Located at a signalized corner along a primary commercial corridor, benefiting from strong local visibility and proximity to a public boat ramp.

Project Notes: The property had ceased operations and was returned to the lender prior to listing. It experienced significant vandalism during the marketing period, with damages exceeding $30,000. The buyer intends to reactivate the property as a car wash operation.
