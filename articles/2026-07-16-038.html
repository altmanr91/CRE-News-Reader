Ice Cream Franchise Coming to Bleecker Street with First NYC Location

LEASE

Connect CRE — Wed, 15 Jul 2026 21:02:41 +0000

SUMMARY

Scoop N Scootery, an ice cream franchise, has signed a 15-year lease for its first New York City location at 162 Bleecker Street. The 1,325-square-foot retail space is expected to open in September 2026. The landlord, Acram Group, was represented by Lee & Associates NYC, while the tenant was represented by AMPS. The broker noted that Bleecker Street offers exceptional foot traffic and a strong mix of food and beverage operators, making it an ideal debut location for a growing retail brand.
