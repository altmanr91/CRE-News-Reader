Pye-Barker Fire & Safety Signs 13,776 SF Industrial Lease in Irving

LEASE

REBusinessOnline — Tue, 07 Jul 2026 15:20:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Pye-Barker Fire & Safety LLC has signed a 13,776-square-foot industrial lease at Skyway Corporate Center in Irving, Texas. The company, which provides alarm and monitoring systems, was represented by CBRE, while Bradford Commercial Real Estate Services represented the landlord.
