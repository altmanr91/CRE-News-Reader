ORIX Acquires Buckhead Apartments for $83M

ACQUISITION

Connect CRE — Fri, 23 Oct 2026 13:54:38 +0000

SUMMARY

ORIX Corporation has acquired AMLI Lenox, a 391-unit luxury multifamily community in Atlanta's Buckhead neighborhood, for $83 million. The property, which will be rebranded as Akari Buckhead, was sold by AMLI Residential. New York Life provided the acquisition financing, arranged by CBRE's Debt & Structured Finance team, while CBRE's Southeast multifamily team facilitated the sale.
