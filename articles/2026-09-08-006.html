MBK Rental Living, Haseko Sell 315-Unit Zia Multifamily Community in Anaheim

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Tue, 08 Sep 2026 13:25:58 +0000

SUMMARY

MBK Rental Living and Haseko Corp. have sold Zia, a 315-unit multifamily community in Anaheim, to an undisclosed institutional investor. The property, which opened in September 2024, features a variety of unit types and extensive amenities.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Zia

Address: 1600 W Lincoln Ave, Anaheim, CA*

Units: 315

Completion: September 2024

Notable Features: Five-story community with studios, one-, two- and three-bedroom residences, a 24-hour fitness center, pool with grill and bar areas, a dog park and spa, coworking spaces, courtyards, and a game lounge with a race car simulator.

* Address sourced separately, not in original article.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

SELLER: MBK Rental Living

SELLER: Haseko Corp.

BUYER: unnamed institutional investor
