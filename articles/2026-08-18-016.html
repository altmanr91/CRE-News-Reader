Northmarq Arranges $75M Acquisition Loan for Grocery-Anchored Queens Center

LOAN

Connect CRE — Mon, 17 Aug 2026 22:19:02 +0000

SUMMARY

Northmarq's New York Debt + Equity team secured a $75 million acquisition and redevelopment loan for the Whitestone Shopping Center in Queens, New York. The financing was arranged on behalf of BTF Whitestone LLC, which plans a significant transformation of the grocery-anchored retail center. The project aims to revitalize the property into a vibrant neighborhood retail destination, benefiting both existing tenants and the local community.
