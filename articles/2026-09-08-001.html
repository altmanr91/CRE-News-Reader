CRE CLO Distress Accelerates in August

Commercial Observer — Tue, 08 Sep 2026 13:24:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Distress Report

The distress rate for commercial real estate collateralized loan obligations (CRE CLOs) surged to 28% in August from 19% in July, marking the most significant one-month increase this year across all deal types. This acceleration, along with a stable 22% distress rate for single-asset, single-borrower (SASB) commercial mortgage-backed securities, is primarily driven by 2021 and 2022 vintage loans. The distress is concentrated in a few large deals, particularly in multifamily collateral for CRE CLOs in the Sun Belt and office/lab properties for SASBs in major coastal markets, reflecting challenges with refinancing and unmet rent growth projections.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Distress rates for conduit, Freddie Mac, and single-family rental loans have remained under 5% for eight months, indicating that the current double-digit distress is specific to CRE CLO and SASB categories, particularly for 2021 and 2022 vintage collateral. Office and mixed-use loans maturing in the next nine months face a refinancing gap of 170 to 180 basis points above their in-place notes, which is the widest gap among all property types.
