Is Scottsdale the nation’s best market for renters?

The Real Deal — National — Thu, 09 Jul 2026 16:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Rental Report

A recent report by Realtor.com, citing WalletHub data, identifies the Greater Phoenix area as the nation's leading market for renters, with four of the top ten cities located there. Scottsdale, Gilbert, Chandler, and Peoria were highlighted for their strong performance across various metrics. The study evaluated cities based on 21 factors, including tenant protections, job market, traffic, recreation, weather, and public schools, with the Phoenix submarkets excelling in quality of life and rental affordability compared to homeownership.

DATA POINTS

Scottsdale average home square footage: 2,179 square feet

Gilbert average home square footage: 2,100 square feet

Percentage of Phoenix metro rentals offering concessions: 54%

Chandler homes built between 2010 and 2024: 17%
