Joint Venture Acquires 1,035-Bed Student Housing Property Near San Jose State University for $204.7M

ACQUISITION

REBusinessOnline — Thu, 15 Oct 2026 13:40:50 +0000

SUMMARY

A joint venture between Coastal Ridge Real Estate and Brookfield has acquired The Grand San Jose, a 19-story student housing property near San Jose State University, for $204.7 million. The asset, completed in 2020, offers 1,035 beds across 206 units and was fully occupied at the time of sale for the second consecutive leasing season. Institutional Property Advisors represented both the buyer and seller in this transaction.
