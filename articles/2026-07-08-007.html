IPA Arranges Sale of 250-Unit Alta Firewheel Apartments in Garland, Texas

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Tue, 07 Jul 2026 15:27:12 +0000

SUMMARY

Institutional Property Advisors (IPA) has facilitated the sale of Alta Firewheel, a 250-unit apartment complex in Garland, Texas. Wood Partners, based in Atlanta, sold the property to Pardue Cos. The transaction involved a recently constructed asset, completed in 2024, offering a range of unit types and modern amenities.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Alta Firewheel

Address: Building 1, 3422 Firewheel Pkwy, Garland, TX*

Units: 250

Year Built: 2024

Notable Features: Amenities include a pool, fitness center, lounge, game room, coffee bar, and outdoor grilling and dining stations.

* Address sourced separately, not in original article.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: Pardue Cos.
