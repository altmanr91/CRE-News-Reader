Newmark brokers $95 million sale of Randhurst Village power center in Mount Prospect

SALE

REJournals — Wed, 30 Sep 2026 14:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Newmark brokered the $95 million sale of Randhurst Village, a 931,798-square-foot open-air retail power center in Mount Prospect, Illinois. The seller, DLC, successfully executed a comprehensive business plan to enhance the asset's positioning and value, driving significant leasing activity and strengthening the property's tenant mix. The buyer, Rhino Investment Group, acquired a dominant mixed-use retail destination that attracts approximately 8.6 million annual visitors and benefits from strong demographic fundamentals in Chicago's affluent northwest suburbs.
