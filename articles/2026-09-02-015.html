Dallas Multifamily 2026 Panelists Cautious, Hopeful About Industry Future

Connect CRE — Tue, 01 Sep 2026 22:24:24 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

A panel of multifamily experts at the Connect Texas Multifamily Dallas event discussed the competitive Dallas market, emphasizing the importance of operational execution and differentiation beyond standard amenities. While acknowledging near-term operational pressures due to a significant supply wave, panelists expressed optimism for improvement as the market works through overbuilding, with some developers exploring opportunities in secondary Texas cities.
