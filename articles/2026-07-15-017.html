Arizona price record: Paradise Valley estate sells for $40M

SALE

The Real Deal — National — Tue, 14 Jul 2026 15:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

A 20,900-square-foot estate in Paradise Valley, Arizona, sold for over $40.2 million in an all-cash transaction, setting a new record for the most expensive home sale in state history. The property, designed by Candelaria Design and built by Arcadia Custom Builders, features extensive luxury amenities. The sale highlights the resilient demand for high-end luxury homes in the Phoenix metro area, driven by out-of-state cash buyers, with prices for top-tier properties continuing to appreciate.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Home prices in the Phoenix metro's highest-priced quartile have appreciated 6.2 percent year over year, while lower-priced segments saw changes between 0 and 1.9 percent. The listing agent noted that $2,000 per square foot is becoming the new standard for top-tier homes.
