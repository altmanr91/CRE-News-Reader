OHT Partners Breaks Ground on 360-Unit Multifamily Project in West Houston

CONSTRUCTION

REBusinessOnline — Mon, 21 Sep 2026 13:58:21 +0000

SUMMARY

OHT Partners has commenced construction on Park Row, a 360-unit multifamily development situated on a 14-acre site in Houston's Energy Corridor. The project will offer a variety of one- and two-bedroom units and a comprehensive suite of amenities designed to enhance resident lifestyle.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Park Row

Address: 14192 Park Row Blvd., Houston, TX

Units: 360

Site Area: 14 acres

Notable Features: Located in the Energy Corridor, amenities include a pool, clubhouse, fitness room, coworking space, communal kitchen, package lockers, a dog park, pickleball court, and outdoor grilling and dining stations.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

DEVELOPER/SPONSOR: OHT Partners

ARCHITECT: Meeks + Partners
