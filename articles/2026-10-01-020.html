Galvanize Adds Orange County Industrial to Expanding Portfolio

ACQUISITION

Connect CRE — Wed, 30 Sep 2026 21:54:20 +0000

SUMMARY

Galvanize Real Estate (GRE) has expanded its industrial portfolio with the acquisition of three properties totaling 280,000 square feet in Orange County, California. This transaction represents GRE's second acquisition in California and fifth nationwide within the last year, bringing their total portfolio to 3.8 million square feet. The company plans to upgrade the properties with sustainable features including solar energy generation, battery storage, and electric vehicle charging infrastructure, aiming to generate approximately 475 MWh of clean electricity annually.
