JLL Arranges Construction Debt, Joint Venture Equity for Dallas Mixed-Use Project

CONSTRUCTION

REBusinessOnline — Wed, 22 Jul 2026 15:20:27 +0000

SUMMARY

JLL has arranged construction debt and joint venture equity for 8300 Douglas, a new mixed-use development in Dallas's Preston Center submarket. The project will feature a 12-story office building, a 17-story residential tower with 147 units, and significant retail space, along with a rooftop park. RAMROCK Real Estate is spearheading the development in collaboration with Lincoln Property Co. and Willow Bridge Property Co.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: mixed-use

Property Name: 8300 Douglas

Address: 8300 Douglas, Dallas, TX

Size: 359,000 SF

Units: 147

Notable Features: Includes a 35,000-square-foot rooftop park and will house the regional headquarters of Fifth Third Bank.
