Colliers Arranges Sale of 1.06 MSF Industrial Facility Near Indianapolis

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Thu, 08 Oct 2026 15:27:39 +0000

SUMMARY

Colliers has brokered the sale of Mohr Logistics Park Building 6, a 1.06 million-square-foot industrial facility in Whiteland, Indiana, south of Indianapolis. Mohr Capital sold the fully leased property, which features modern distribution specifications and a long-term tenancy through August 2036. The transaction highlights continued investor interest in large-scale industrial assets with strong tenancy in growing Midwest markets, particularly those with excellent transportation access.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Property Name: Mohr Logistics Park Building 6

Address: 5635 Graham Rd, Whiteland, IN*

Size: 1,060,000 SF

Occupancy at Sale: 100.0%

Notable Features: Features a 40-foot clear height, 120 dock doors, four drive-in doors, 652 auto parking spaces, 270 trailer parking spaces, and a 140-foot truck court. Located at the I-65 and Whiteland Road interchange, providing immediate access to Indianapolis' primary north-south distribution corridor and key regional transportation networks.

Project Notes: The facility is part of the 475-acre Mohr Logistics Park, a master-planned industrial development expected to total over 6 million square feet at full buildout.
