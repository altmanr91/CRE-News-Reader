Related Affiliate Nabs $167M From Greystone to Build Miami Resi Towers

LOAN

Commercial Observer — Tue, 29 Sep 2026 14:40:33 +0000

SUMMARY

Related Urban Development Group, an affiliate of The Related Group, secured $167 million in construction financing from Greystone for the development of Gallery at Lummus Parc, a two-tower multifamily project in Miami. The financing package includes an $80 million construction loan from Greystone Housing Impact Investors and BlackRock Impact Opportunities Fund, along with $27.4 million in 4 percent Low-Income Housing Tax Credits from Greystone Real Estate Capital. This comprehensive financing solution, which also involves a Freddie Mac forward tax-exempt loan, will support the creation of 257 affordable and workforce housing units in downtown Miami, with completion expected in late 2028.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Gallery at Lummus Parc

Address: 395 Northwest First Street, Miami, FL

Units: 257

Loan Amount: $167.0M

Loan Per Unit: $649,805/unit (calculated)

Completion: late 2028

Notable Features: Two interconnected towers of 30 and 27 stories, with approximately 83% of units designated as affordable housing ranging from 20% to 100% of area median income. Amenities include coworking spaces, a health club, a rooftop pool, grilling areas, a pickleball court, and an outdoor fitness area. The project benefits from close access to public transportation.

Project Notes: The financing includes an $80 million construction loan and $27.4 million in 4% Low-Income Housing Tax Credits (LIHTC). Greystone also obtained a Freddie Mac forward tax-exempt loan to repay construction financing costs with LIHTC equity, providing a clear path to long-term permanent financing.
