Investment Firm 26 North Takes 84K SF at LeFrak Tower

LEASE

Connect CRE — Wed, 09 Sep 2026 21:30:03 +0000

SUMMARY

Alternative investment management firm 26 North has signed an 84,000-square-foot, 13-year office lease at 40 W. 57th St., a tower owned by LeFrak. This transaction, along with a 20,750-square-foot lease by Mack Real Estate Group, brings the total new leasing activity to nearly 105,000 square feet. The building's appeal stems from its stable ownership, prime location in a desirable business district, and ongoing capital improvements, which continue to attract top-tier tenants seeking high-quality office space.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Property Name: LeFrak Tower

Address: 40 W. 57th St., New York City, NY

Size: 84,000 SF

Project Notes: 26 North will relocate from 600 Madison. Mack Real Estate Group will relocate from 60 Columbus Circle.
