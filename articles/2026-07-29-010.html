IPA Brokers $23.1M Sale of Publix-Anchored Shopping Center in Alexander City, Alabama

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Tue, 28 Jul 2026 14:28:35 +0000

SUMMARY

Institutional Property Advisors (IPA), a division of Marcus & Millichap, has brokered the $23.1 million sale of The Gateway at Lake Martin, a Publix-anchored shopping center in Alexander City, Alabama. The newly built 93,352-square-foot retail center was sold by a repeat developer of grocery-anchored properties to Calhoun Shores LLC. IPA Capital Markets also arranged acquisition financing for the buyer.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: retail

Property Name: The Gateway at Lake Martin

Size: 93,352 SF

Sale Price: $23.1M

Year Built: 2024

Site Area: 22 acres

Notable Features: Grocery-anchored shopping center.

Project Notes: Four outparcels were not part of the transaction.
