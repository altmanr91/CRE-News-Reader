JLL Negotiates Sale of 172,854 SF Office Building in Northwest Austin

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Mon, 05 Oct 2026 14:08:48 +0000

SUMMARY

JLL has facilitated the sale of Research Park Plaza V, a 172,854-square-foot office building located in northwest Austin. The four-story property, completed in 2015, was 85 percent leased to a strong tenant mix at the time of the transaction. The seller was Cousins Properties, an Atlanta-based REIT.
