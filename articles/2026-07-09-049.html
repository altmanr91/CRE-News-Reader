Shipping Giant Inks Greater Boston's Largest Industrial Lease In Years

LEASE

Bisnow News Feed — Wed, 08 Jul 2026 15:55:40 -0400

SUMMARY

Maersk, a global shipping company, has signed the largest new industrial lease in Greater Boston since 2020, taking a full 617,000-square-foot distribution center in Hopedale, MA. The facility was speculatively developed by GFI Partners, who acquired the 141-acre parcel in 2023. This significant transaction reflects renewed confidence among large occupiers and a resurgence in big-box leasing activity within the Greater Boston industrial market, as national and global companies continue to invest in strategic locations.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Address: 75 Plain St., Hopedale, MA

Size: 617,000 SF

Notable Features: Largest new industrial lease in Greater Boston since 2020.

Project Notes: The 141-acre site was previously acquired for $15M in 2023. The project is expected to create over 300 jobs and generate $1M in tax revenue for the town.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

TENANT: Maersk

LANDLORD: GFI Partners

LEASING AGENT: Cushman & Wakefield — Riley McMullan, Senior Research Manager; Brian Pinch, Vice Chair of Industrial and Logistics

SELLER: Boston Sand and Gravel

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Greater Boston's industrial sector saw 2M SF of leasing activity in Q2 2026, the second-highest quarter for demand in nearly four years. Industrial leasing volume more than doubled in H1 2026 compared to the same period last year, with 10 new leases for 100K SF or more. Demand is shifting towards new leasing activity, and a slowdown in new industrial construction has allowed demand to catch up with supply.
