CBRE facilitates sale of 435-unit multifamily property in Woodbury

ACQUISITION

REJournals — Thu, 08 Oct 2026 14:32:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Waterton has acquired City Walk, a 453-unit multifamily property in Woodbury, Minnesota, through a transaction facilitated by CBRE. The property, which has demonstrated strong operational growth, is poised for a value-add investment strategy by the new owner. Located at 10225 City Walk Drive, the asset offers convenient access to major interstates and significant retail amenities.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: City Walk

Address: 10225 City Walk Drive, Woodbury, MN

Units: 453

Year Built: 2005

Notable Features: Features a mix of one, two, and three-bedroom floor plans with an average unit size of 1,198 square feet, and amenities including a heated garage, fitness center, indoor pool, golf simulator, and business center. It is proximate to the Interstates 94 and 494 interchange and 2.2 million square feet of retail.

Project Notes: Property was expanded in 2011.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: Waterton

SELLER BROKER: CBRE — Keith Collins; Abe Appert; Ted Abramson
