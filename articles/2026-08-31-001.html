Gatsby Florida Lands $119M Loan to Build Spec Palm Beach Gardens Office

LOAN

Commercial Observer — Mon, 31 Aug 2026 16:59:06 +0000

SUMMARY

Gatsby Florida has secured a $118.6 million construction loan from Cirrus Real Estate Partners for The Palm, a speculative luxury office and retail complex in Palm Beach Gardens, FL. The project will feature 200,000 square feet of Class A office space and 30,000 square feet of ground-floor retail. Construction is slated to begin this month and complete in 2028, with the developer anticipating strong demand from financial services firms and family offices expanding into Palm Beach County.

DATA POINTS

Site Area: 5 acres

Notable Features: Eight-story development with 200,000 square feet of Class A office space, 30,000 square feet of ground-floor retail, and an 838-space parking garage. Located adjacent to State Road 811, between Interstate 95 and Legacy Place open-air mall.

Project Notes: The developer previously acquired the 5-acre parcel for $17.5 million in 2022. The loan is a three-year, floating-rate, interest-only debt.
