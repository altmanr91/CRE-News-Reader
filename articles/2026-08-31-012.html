Kraus-Anderson wraps construction on remodeling, expansion project at Cumberland high school

CONSTRUCTION

REJournals — Mon, 31 Aug 2026 15:56:59 +0000

SUMMARY

Kraus-Anderson has completed a significant remodeling and expansion project at Cumberland High School in Cumberland, Wisconsin. The $32.95 million project, backed by a voter-approved bond, involved both new additions and extensive renovations to the existing facility. The upgrades include new classrooms, a cafeteria, offices, locker rooms, and an outdoor learning space, enhancing the school's overall functionality and student experience.
