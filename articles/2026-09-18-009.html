BWE Arranges $48M Refinancing for 978,000 SF Industrial Property in DeKalb, Illinois

REFINANCE

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 18 Sep 2026 13:06:37 +0000

SUMMARY

BWE has arranged a $48 million fixed-rate refinancing for a 978,000-square-foot industrial logistics facility in DeKalb, Illinois. Griffin Capital secured the loan from Aegon, a life insurance company lender. The property is fully leased to a major tenant and features specialized chemical storage, aerosol rooms, cold storage, and containment floors.
