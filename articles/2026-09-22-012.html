Marcus & Millichap closes sale of 15-suite industrial office property in Grove City

SALE

REJournals — Tue, 22 Sep 2026 13:55:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Marcus & Millichap brokered the sale of a 15-suite industrial office property in Grove City, Ohio, for $2.5 million. The buyer, Buckeye Physical Medicine, had been a long-term tenant at the property. The asset, built in 1974, was 87% occupied at the time of sale and features a diverse tenant mix.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Address: 2228 Stringtown Road, Grove City, OH

Size: 41,050 SF

Sale Price: $2.5M

$/SF: $61/SF (calculated)

Occupancy at Sale: 87.0%

Year Built: 1974

Site Area: 3.43 acres

Notable Features: Steel-constructed buildings with eight drive-in doors, located west of I-71 and south of I-270.

Project Notes: Property consists of 15 rental suites across four buildings.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

SELLER: Keil Family Limited Partnership

BUYER: Buckeye Physical Medicine

SELLER BROKER: Marcus & Millichap — Nathan Pealer, Investment Specialist
