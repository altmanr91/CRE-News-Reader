South Korean Culture Is Inspiring The Next Wave Of Apartment Amenities

Bisnow News Feed — Wed, 01 Jul 2026 17:14:15 -0400

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

South Korean cultural influences, particularly in longevity-focused wellness, are increasingly shaping amenity design in U.S. apartment buildings. Developers are moving beyond traditional gyms and party rooms to incorporate holistic wellness features like hot-cold plunge pools, Himalayan salt rooms, and sensory showers, drawing inspiration from East Asian spa traditions. This shift reflects a growing demand among renters for amenities that support mind-body connection, stress relief, and recovery, with developers betting on residents' willingness to pay for services that promote a longer, healthier life.
