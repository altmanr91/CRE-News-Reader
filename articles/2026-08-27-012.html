The NRP Group breaks ground on 150-unit affordable-housing community in Columbus

CONSTRUCTION

REJournals — Thu, 27 Aug 2026 16:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

The NRP Group has broken ground on Stone Ridge at High Street, a 150-unit affordable housing community in Columbus, OH. Developed in partnership with the City of Columbus and the Ohio Housing Finance Agency, the project will provide housing for working professionals and families earning up to 60% of the Area Median Income. The community is scheduled for completion in April 2028.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Stone Ridge at High Street

Address: 45 W. Barthman Avenue, Columbus, OH

Units: 150

Completion: April 2028

Site Area: 4.5 acres

Notable Features: Located approximately one mile from downtown Columbus with ample public transit access and proximity to shopping, dining, job centers, and major employers.

Project Notes: Developed in partnership with the City of Columbus and the Ohio Housing Finance Agency to provide housing for families earning up to 60% of the Area Median Income.
