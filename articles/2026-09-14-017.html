Healthcare Realty Acquires $58M of Chapel Hill, Summerville Medical Assets

ACQUISITION

Connect CRE — Mon, 14 Sep 2026 13:57:46 +0000

SUMMARY

Healthcare Realty has acquired a portfolio of medical assets for $56 million from Hammes. The acquisition includes a fully leased medical office building and an ambulatory surgery center in Summerville, SC, along with a five-acre entitled development site in Chapel Hill, NC. The Summerville properties are 100% occupied with a weighted-average lease term of approximately seven years and fixed annual rent escalations, reflecting strong demand for medical office space.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: medical office

Property Name: Summerville Medical Campus / Charleston Surgery Center

Address: 5936 Farrington Road, Chapel Hill, NC

Size: 122,261 SF

Sale Price: $56.0M

Occupancy at Sale: 100.0%

Site Area: 5 acres

Notable Features: The Summerville assets are anchored by the Medical University of South Carolina and fully leased to a joint venture led by SCA Health. The Chapel Hill site is fully entitled for a six-story, 168,000-square-foot medical and office building with a structured parking garage.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: Healthcare Realty

SELLER: Hammes

SELLER BROKER: Newmark — Ben Appel; Jay Miele; Justin Shepherd; John Nero; Michael Greeley; Ron Ott
