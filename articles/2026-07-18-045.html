Manhattan Beach Studios Shopped As Possible Defense Tech Site

Bisnow News Feed — Fri, 17 Jul 2026 11:43:38 -0400

SUMMARY

Property Listing / For Sale

Lenders are marketing the $240 million mortgage on Manhattan Beach Studios, a 15-soundstage campus, highlighting the property's potential as an advanced manufacturing site for the burgeoning aerospace and defense technology sectors in the South Bay. The submarket has seen significant growth in this industry, with established and emerging companies expanding their presence. The property, acquired by Hackman Capital Partners in 2019 for $650 million, has faced challenges due to rising interest rates and entertainment industry disruptions, leading to a notice of default filed by lenders in June. Despite these challenges, the property is reportedly in lease negotiations with multiple prospective tenants, and lenders are also exploring financing options for the site.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Aerospace and defense technology companies accounted for 11% of new industrial real estate leases in the South Bay submarket since the start of 2025.
