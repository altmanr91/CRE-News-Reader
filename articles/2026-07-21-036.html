SF real estate, media mogul Clint Reilly dies at 79

The Real Deal — San Francisco — Mon, 20 Jul 2026 22:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Non-CRE / Obituary

Clint Reilly, a prominent San Francisco real estate mogul and philanthropist, has passed away at 79. Reilly, who transitioned from a career in political consulting to real estate, built a portfolio valued at $400 million, encompassing significant office properties like the Merchants Exchange Building and local media holdings. He remained an active participant in the real estate market, notably selling Financial District offices earlier this year at a premium price.
