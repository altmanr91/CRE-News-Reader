Prado, Presidio Bay jump ship on deal to buy long-distressed SF Centre mall

The Real Deal — San Francisco — Wed, 08 Jul 2026 20:15:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Property Listing / For Sale

Prado Group and Presidio Bay Ventures have withdrawn from their agreement to acquire the San Francisco Centre mall, a 1.5-million-square-foot retail complex. The deal's collapse was reportedly due to the buyers' inability to renegotiate an existing long-term lease with the San Francisco Unified School District for a portion of the property, as well as potential difficulties in securing financing for the high-risk investment. The mall, which was seized by lenders Goldman Sachs and JPMorgan Chase in a foreclosure auction last November, is now expected to be returned to the market.
