Progress Residential scores $385M refi for resi portfolio amid federal housing act

REFINANCE

The Real Deal — Miami — Tue, 14 Jul 2026 21:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Progress Residential, a major owner of single-family rental homes and a subsidiary of Pretium Partners, secured a $384.7 million refinancing for a multi-state residential portfolio. The loan, provided by Goldman Sachs Mortgage Company, was backed by properties valued at $462.1 million, including 64 homes in Broward County, Florida. This refinancing occurred shortly before the 21st Century ROAD to Housing Act became law, which aims to increase housing supply and limit large investors from acquiring additional existing single-family homes, though it does not restrict refinancing of current portfolios.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: single family

Loan Amount: $384.7M

Project Notes: The loan has an 83 percent loan-to-value ratio, with the total portfolio valued at $462.1 million. The Broward County portion of the portfolio includes 64 residential properties across 14 cities, valued at $71.7 million.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

SPONSOR: Progress Residential

OWNER: Pretium Partners

LENDER: Goldman Sachs Mortgage Company

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Broward County's median gross rent was $1,907 between 2020 and 2024. Florida is Progress Residential's largest market, with a presence in six regions. Invitation Homes generated $556 million in revenue from its Florida portfolio during the first nine months of last year, owning nearly 27,000 homes valued at about $4.2 billion. American Homes 4 Rent owns nearly 8,700 single-family homes in Florida with an estimated value of roughly $2.1 billion. Corporate ownership of Florida single-family homes was estimated at roughly 117,000 as of late 2024.
