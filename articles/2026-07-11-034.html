'Not Happy, But Comfortable': CREFC President On CRE Finding A New Normal

Bisnow News Feed — Fri, 10 Jul 2026 15:14:18 -0400

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

A recent survey by the CRE Finance Council (CREFC) indicates a cautiously optimistic sentiment within the commercial real estate industry, with a slight uptick in market sentiment compared to the previous quarter. While only a small percentage of respondents expressed negative views for 2027, the majority remained neutral, suggesting a period of stabilization rather than strong recovery. CREFC President and CEO Lisa Pendergast noted that despite the current challenges, the industry is adapting to elevated interest rates, finding a new equilibrium where it can manage within the existing financial landscape, even if not entirely content with it. She believes the industry's resilience, honed through past crises like the pandemic and the Global Financial Crisis, is enabling it to navigate the current environment.
