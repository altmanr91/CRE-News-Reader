Citi Provides $210M Refi for Grand Hyatt Resort in Southern California

REFINANCE

Commercial Observer — Fri, 16 Oct 2026 17:40:45 +0000

SUMMARY

Trinity Investments has secured $210 million in refinancing for the Grand Hyatt Indian Wells Resort & Villas, a 531-key luxury hotel in Southern California's Coachella Valley. Citi provided the five-year, floating-rate, single-asset, single-borrower CMBS loan, which follows a $62 million renovation and rebranding of the property in 2024. The successful refinancing highlights a growing lender appetite for recently repositioned hospitality assets with strong sponsorship in supply-constrained markets.
