Associated Bank Provides $50.1M Construction Loan for Multifamily Project in Flower Mound, Texas

LOAN

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 21 Aug 2026 15:07:31 +0000

SUMMARY

Associated Bank has provided a $50.1 million construction loan to Trinsic Residential Group for Aura Brookview, a 300-unit multifamily project in Flower Mound, Texas. The development will feature four four-story buildings on a 10-acre site within the larger Brookview master-planned community. The project is designed with modern amenities and unit finishes to attract residents in the northern-central part of the Dallas-Fort Worth metroplex.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Aura Brookview

Units: 300

Loan Amount: $50.1M

Loan Per Buildable Unit: $167,000/buildable unit (calculated)

Loan/Acre: $5,010,000/acre (calculated)

Loan/Land SF: $115.01/land SF (calculated)

Site Area: 10 acres

Notable Features: Located within the 240-acre Brookview master-planned community; units will feature stainless steel appliances, quartz countertops, and in-unit washers and dryers; amenities include a pool, fitness center, coworking space, dog park, and outdoor grilling/dining stations.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

LENDER: Associated Bank

SPONSOR: Trinsic Residential Group
