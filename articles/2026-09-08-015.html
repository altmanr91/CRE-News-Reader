Shoppers in 2026 Stay Close to Home, Keep Trips Short

Connect CRE — Tue, 08 Sep 2026 13:11:24 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Retail Consumer Report

A new white paper by Placer.ai, "The 2026 Retail Consumer in Five Charts," reveals that the typical retail shopper in 2026 prioritizes short, convenient trips close to home. The report highlights that most retail visits are under 30 minutes, with the largest segment being under 15 minutes, underscoring the importance of efficiency for retailers. Additionally, nearly 60% of retail visits originate within a five-mile radius, emphasizing the local nature of brick-and-mortar retail and its implications for site selection and tenant mix. The study also notes the continued importance of older shoppers as a core demographic and identifies opportunities for brands to tailor in-store strategies to attract affluent consumers.

DATA POINTS

Visits under 30 minutes: Vast majority of visits to Placer 100 Retail Index

Visits under 15 minutes: Largest dwell-time bucket

Visits originating within five miles: Nearly 60%
