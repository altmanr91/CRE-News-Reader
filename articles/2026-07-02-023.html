NYCHA paperwork failures spark eviction wave

The Real Deal — New York — Wed, 01 Jul 2026 18:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

CRE Legal / Regulatory

A significant administrative failure within the New York City Housing Authority (NYCHA) has led to an eviction crisis for hundreds of tenants in privately managed public housing developments under its PACT program. NYCHA's inability to process annual income recertifications resulted in the mistaken termination of Section 8 subsidies, causing private managers to bill tenants for full market rents. This error has led to a surge in eviction proceedings, particularly in East New York, despite tenants having paid their subsidized rent portions.

DATA POINTS

Section 8 terminations for failure to recertify (2024): 42

Section 8 terminations for failure to recertify (last year): 836

Increase in Section 8 terminations: nearly 2,000 percent

Eviction proceedings filed by Stanley Avenue Preservation LLC since 2023: more than 900
