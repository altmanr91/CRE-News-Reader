Starwood Advances 226K-SF Data Center in Fairfax County, Va.

DEVELOPMENT

Commercial Observer — Tue, 21 Jul 2026 21:35:49 +0000

SUMMARY

Starwood Capital Group is moving forward with the first phase of its Plaza 500 development in Fairfax County, Virginia, filing plans for a 226,469-square-foot data center. The initial construction phase for the building shell is estimated at $5 million. This project is part of a larger data center initiative by Starwood in Northern Virginia, which has faced community opposition regarding the proximity of a proposed electrical substation to residential areas.

DATA POINTS

Project Notes: The 34-acre site was previously acquired for $165 million in 2022. Starwood is also awaiting approval for a proposed electrical substation.
