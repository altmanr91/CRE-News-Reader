Harbor Associates Sells Office Campus in North L.A. County for $32M

ACQUISITION

Commercial Observer — Fri, 25 Sep 2026 18:45:50 +0000

SUMMARY

Strauss Investments has acquired Commons at Valencia, a two-building Class A office campus in Santa Clarita, CA, from Harbor Associates for $32 million. The 157,189-square-foot property, which underwent extensive renovations in 2020, is 79% leased to a diverse mix of professional services, insurance, financial services, and real estate tenants. The sale highlights the continued investor interest in well-located, recently improved assets within strong-performing suburban office markets like Valencia.
