PTM Partners Obtains $125M Construction Loan for Phase II of High-Rise Project in St. Petersburg, Florida

LOAN

REBusinessOnline — Mon, 05 Oct 2026 13:39:27 +0000

SUMMARY

PTM Partners has secured a $125 million construction loan for the second phase of its EDGE Collective development in downtown St. Petersburg. The financing, arranged by Berkadia through an affiliate of GID, will support the construction of a 20-story residential tower with ground-floor retail. This project builds on the success of the first phase, which included a hotel and adaptive reuse components.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: EDGE Collective II

Units: 330

Loan Amount: $125.0M

Loan Per Unit: $378,788/unit (calculated)

Notable Features: 20-story residential tower with ground-floor retail space.

Project Notes: Three-year, floating-rate loan.
