JLL Capital Markets arranges financing for two-building mixed-use portfolio in Fort Worth

REFINANCE

REJournals — Thu, 17 Sep 2026 13:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

JLL Capital Markets has arranged a five-year, fixed-rate, non-recourse loan with full-term interest-only payments for Magnolia Equity Partners to refinance a two-building mixed-use portfolio in Fort Worth, TX. The portfolio, consisting of Supreme Golf Building and Sawyer Grocery, offers a combined 37 residential units and 36,141 square feet of first-floor retail space. Both properties are located in Fort Worth's Near Southside neighborhood, a rapidly growing cultural hub with strong walkability to downtown and proximity to major employers.
