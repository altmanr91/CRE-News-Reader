Friedman’s Restaurant Expands with Upper West Side Location

ACQUISITION

Connect CRE — Fri, 11 Sep 2026 21:16:55 +0000

SUMMARY

The ownership group behind Friedman's Restaurant has acquired a 5,000-square-foot retail cooperative unit on Manhattan's Upper West Side for $4.135 million. The new owners plan to open a new Friedman's Restaurant location in an existing vacant space within the property, while two long-standing neighborhood businesses will continue to operate. This acquisition allows the restaurant group to expand its presence in a prime Upper West Side location.
