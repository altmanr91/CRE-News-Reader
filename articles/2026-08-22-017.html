Avison Young Recapitalization Provides “War Chest” for Further Growth

Connect CRE — Fri, 21 Aug 2026 21:40:45 +0000

SUMMARY

Non-CRE / Business News

Avison Young has announced a recapitalization expected to close in October, which will significantly deleverage the company's balance sheet by reducing debt and preferred equity by nearly 70%. This strategic move aims to provide a "war chest" for future growth, enabling the firm to pursue strategic acquisitions, broaden its service platform, and expand its talent pool through recruitment. The transaction involves a 70% debt-to-equity swap, converting financial stakeholders into equity holders and resulting in an approximately 50-50 ownership split between Avison Young principals and the former lenders, positioning the company for substantial upsizing and global expansion.
