Marcus & Millichap facilitates sale of 53,812-square-foot self-storage facility in Athens

SALE

REJournals — Tue, 14 Jul 2026 15:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Marcus & Millichap has facilitated the sale of North Loop Mini Warehouses, a 53,812-square-foot self-storage facility in Athens, TX. The property, which was inherited by a family, was sold to a California-based private investor looking to expand their existing self-storage portfolio in East Texas. The transaction highlights the continued demand for self-storage assets in growing markets and opportunities for operational improvements.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: self-storage

Property Name: North Loop Mini Warehouses

Address: 2202 N.E. Loop 7, Athens, TX

Size: 53,812 SF

Year Built: 1994

Site Area: 7.67 acres

Project Notes: The property was previously operated as a family-owned self-storage facility before ownership transitioned to relatives who inherited the asset.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: Private Investor (California-based)

SELLER BROKER: Marcus & Millichap — Danny Cunningham, Investment Specialist; Brandon Karr, Investment Specialist
