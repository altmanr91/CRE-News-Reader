Tishman Speyer, American Express Partner to Offer ZO Access to Non-Tenants

Connect CRE — Fri, 23 Oct 2026 21:58:49 +0000

SUMMARY

Non-CRE / Business News

Tishman Speyer has partnered with American Express to extend access to its ZO amenity network to eligible U.S. Business Platinum Card and Corporate Platinum Card holders. This collaboration marks the first time a major commercial real estate landlord has teamed up with a credit card issuer to offer such benefits to non-tenants. Cardholders will gain complimentary access to over 20 ZO Clubhouse and Studio coworking locations across six major cities, along with guest passes and annual statement credits for event space bookings.
