Fortress Refinances Dania Design Center With $45M After Foreclosure Fight

REFINANCE

Commercial Observer — Thu, 13 Aug 2026 17:40:19 +0000

SUMMARY

Fortress Investment Group has refinanced the Design Center of the Americas in Dania Beach, FL, with a $45 million loan from City National Bank of Florida. This refinancing follows Fortress gaining control of the 780,000-square-foot office and design showroom complex in 2024 through a $76 million credit bid, after a foreclosure battle with previous owner Charles Cohen. Fortress has invested $3 million in renovations over the past two years and plans an additional $3 million to $5 million in upgrades.
