Turret takes over debt on troubled Upper East Side condo buildings

ACQUISITION

The Real Deal — New York — Tue, 21 Jul 2026 18:47:17 +0000

SUMMARY

Turret Equities has acquired a distressed $19.9 million debt note secured by two condo buildings on the Upper East Side of Manhattan. The current owner, Continuum Analytics, had defaulted on its loans and was facing foreclosure by the original lender, Preferred Bank. This acquisition expands Turret's portfolio of distressed debt in the Upper East Side market.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: condo

Property Name: The Gianna / The Jacqueline

Address: 184 East 64th Street / 227 East 67th Street, New York City, NY

Loan Amount: $19.9M

Project Notes: The buildings were previously purchased by Preferred Bank at a bankruptcy auction in February 2019 for $32.5 million and sold to Continuum Analytics two months later for $38 million. Preferred Bank also provided Continuum with a $29 million loan and refinanced the properties with a nearly $20 million loan in 2023.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: Turret Equities — David E. Dweck

SELLER: Preferred Bank

OWNER: Continuum Analytics
