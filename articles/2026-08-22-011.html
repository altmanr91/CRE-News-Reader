Interra Realty closes $2 million sale of Chicago apartment building

SALE

REJournals — Fri, 21 Aug 2026 17:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Interra Realty brokered the $2.135 million sale of a 13-unit multifamily building in Chicago's Edgewater neighborhood. The property, which had not traded in nearly 50 years, was acquired by an undisclosed local buyer who plans a full gut renovation, including reconfiguring and adding units. The building is currently less than half occupied.
