Marcus & Millichap Closes $12.5M Sale of La Mesa Seniors Housing

SALE

Connect CRE — Mon, 27 Jul 2026 21:48:22 +0000

SUMMARY

Marcus & Millichap has brokered the $12.5 million sale of an 81-unit seniors affordable multifamily property in La Mesa, CA. The transaction highlights strong demand for well-maintained, age-restricted affordable housing in San Diego County, particularly for properties with long-term affordability protections. The property, built in 1986, is the only senior deed-restricted building in the submarket with an agreement in perpetuity, positioning the new owner to continue providing low-income senior housing for the foreseeable future.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Address: 5035-49 Guava Ave., La Mesa, CA

Units: 81

Sale Price: $12.5M

$/Unit: $154,321/unit (calculated)

Notable Features: Age-restricted community with long-term affordability protections and a deed restriction in perpetuity.

Project Notes: The sale is one of five transactions over 80 units in La Mesa and one of seven senior affordable transactions countywide in the past five years.
