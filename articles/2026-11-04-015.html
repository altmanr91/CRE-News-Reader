Industrial Employment Emerges as Key Demand Driver of CRE Demand

Connect CRE — Wed, 04 Nov 2026 16:02:07 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / CRE Demand Index

The National Association of Realtors' (NAR) third-quarter 2026 Commercial Real Estate (CRE) Demand Index highlights industrial employment growth as a significant driver of CRE demand in specific markets. While national industrial employment remained stable, robust job creation in manufacturing, transportation, and warehousing sectors boosted demand in cities like San Antonio, Seattle, and Nashville. The index, which tracks 306 metropolitan areas, found 143 markets with demand drivers above the national average, with St. George, UT, Huntsville, AL, and Fayetteville-Springdale-Rogers, AR, leading the rankings. This localized employment growth is reshaping the landscape of commercial real estate demand.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Industrial employment remained relatively flat nationally in Q3 2026, but stronger job growth in manufacturing, transportation, and warehousing sectors drove CRE demand in specific markets.
