What Will You Do With Your Turn?

Commercial Observer — Mon, 14 Sep 2026 14:51:04 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Editorial

This opinion piece reflects on the brevity of human life in the context of the September 11th tragedy, urging readers to live with intentionality and make the most of their limited time. The author, a commercial real estate veteran, emphasizes that while professional ambition and achievement are valuable, they should be balanced with a broader perspective on life's ultimate scorecard, focusing on maximizing potential, helping others, building relationships, and making a positive impact. The central argument is that recognizing life's shortness should inspire urgency, not anxiety, leading to a more purposeful and impactful existence in all dimensions.
