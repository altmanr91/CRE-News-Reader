Kansas City Royals select Mortenson|McCownGordon joint venture to build new ballpark

CONSTRUCTION

REJournals — Mon, 14 Sep 2026 15:19:08 +0000

SUMMARY

The Kansas City Royals have selected the Mortenson|McCownGordon joint venture as the Construction Manager at Risk for their new 1.25-million-square-foot open-air ballpark at Crown Center in downtown Kansas City, Missouri. This project is part of a larger $3 billion mixed-use redevelopment, with the ballpark and associated infrastructure representing a $1.9 billion investment. The new venue, featuring approximately 34,000 fixed seats, is expected to be completed by opening day 2030 and aims to serve as a long-term catalyst for investment and community connection in the downtown area.
