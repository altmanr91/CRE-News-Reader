Aimco Advancing Luxury Edgewater Apartments

DEVELOPMENT

Connect CRE — Tue, 13 Oct 2026 14:22:18 +0000

SUMMARY

Aimco is proceeding with the development of FIVESIXTY Waterfront Residences + Club, a luxury residential tower in Miami's Edgewater neighborhood. The project will feature 114 units with rents ranging from $15,000 to over $25,000, and is expected to achieve initial occupancy in spring 2027. The property will offer extensive amenities including a bayfront pool, private marina, fitness facilities, and executive work suites.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: FIVESIXTY Waterfront Residences + Club

Address: 560 NE 34th Street, Miami, FL

Units: 114

Completion: Spring 2027

Notable Features: Luxury waterfront property with a private marina accommodating vessels up to 70 feet, fitness and spa facilities, padel court, golf practice facilities, and private executive work suites. Residences range from 2,300 to 3,000 square feet. Located adjacent to the Miami Design District with easy access to Miami Beach, Downtown, Brickell, Wynwood, and Midtown.
