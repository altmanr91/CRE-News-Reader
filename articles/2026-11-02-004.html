Terra, Fortune International Land $220M for Waterfront Condo near Miami

LOAN

Commercial Observer — Mon, 02 Nov 2026 17:55:11 +0000

SUMMARY

Terra and Fortune International Group have secured a $220 million predevelopment loan for a 56-unit luxury condominium project on Key Biscayne, near Miami. The 13-story development, located at 301 Ocean Drive, will replace the former Silver Sands Beach Resort. The developers acquired the 3.8-acre waterfront site last year for $205 million and have obtained approvals for the project, which will include a subterranean parking garage. Sales for the new condo building are expected to commence later this year.
