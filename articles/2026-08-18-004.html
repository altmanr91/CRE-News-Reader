Aircraft Finance Firm Buys Florida HQ Complex For $118M

ACQUISITION

Commercial Observer — Mon, 17 Aug 2026 19:40:00 +0000

SUMMARY

An aircraft finance firm has acquired the Cocomar Business Park, a three-building, 375,000-square-foot campus under construction in Coconut Creek, Florida, for $118 million. The campus, which is expected to be completed in the third quarter of this year, will serve as the company's new corporate headquarters. The seller, an affiliate of a Charleston-based real estate firm, had previously acquired the 35-acre site for $30.5 million last year.
