Technology Platform Genius AI Expands by 14K SF at 838 Broadway

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Mon, 27 Jul 2026 12:00:23 +0000

SUMMARY

Genius AI, a technology platform, has expanded its headquarters at 838 Broadway in New York City by 13,754 square feet, bringing its total footprint to 27,373 square feet across the entire second and third floors. This expansion also brings the six-story building to full occupancy. The tenant's broker noted the company's strong momentum and recent fundraising success as drivers for the increased physical space.
