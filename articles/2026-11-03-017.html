Colliers Arranges $71M Sale of Chicago Educational Facility

SALE

Connect CRE — Tue, 03 Nov 2026 14:58:52 +0000

SUMMARY

Colliers facilitated the $70.6 million sale of a 102,000-square-foot educational facility in Chicago's Roosevelt Collection. The property, fully leased to the British International School of Chicago, South Loop, was acquired by W. P. Carey from EPR Properties. The transaction highlights strong investor interest in high-quality, mission-critical real estate with long-term lease structures and reputable operators.
