Oak Brook mansion relisted for $10M, could fetch highest village price if sold

The Real Deal — Chicago — Tue, 21 Jul 2026 21:42:47 +0000

SUMMARY

Property Listing / For Sale

A luxury mansion in Oak Brook, IL, has been relisted for just under $10 million. If sold near its asking price, the 15,820-square-foot residence at 404 Fox Trail Lane would set a new record for the highest home sale in the village, surpassing the previous record set in 2003. The property, built in 2009, features seven bedrooms, 13.5 bathrooms, and a range of high-end amenities.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

The wider Chicago area is experiencing a fast-moving housing market due to limited inventory, leading buyers to act more quickly and spend more.
