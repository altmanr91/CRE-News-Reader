Electronics Company Acquires Flex Industrial for Headquarters Use

ACQUISITION

Connect CRE — Mon, 14 Sep 2026 21:25:27 +0000

SUMMARY

A-V Services Realty LLC, an electronics and technology company, has acquired a 40,000-square-foot industrial flex property at 60 E. Rte. 46 in Fairfield, NJ, to establish its first owned headquarters. The acquisition allows the company to secure a long-term home within striking distance of New York City, eliminating the need for future lease negotiations. Renovations are scheduled to begin in November.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Address: 60 E. Rte. 46, Fairfield, NJ

Size: 40,000 SF

Notable Features: Two-story design allows for separation of office and warehouse operations, supporting office, light industrial, showroom, and distribution functions.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: A-V Services Realty LLC

BUYER BROKER: Avison Young — Ed English, Principal; Ron Ganter, Principal
