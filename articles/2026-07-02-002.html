Zar Property NY Buys 118 West 22nd Street for $28M

ACQUISITION

Commercial Observer — Wed, 01 Jul 2026 18:51:08 +0000

SUMMARY

Zar Property NY has acquired 118 West 22nd Street, a 12-story mixed-use building in Manhattan's Flatiron District, for $28 million. The seller was Adlie Associates, which had owned the property since 1986. The acquisition was supported by a $19.6 million financing package arranged by Filler Capital and provided by J.P. Morgan, marking a significant transaction for the buyer.
