Starwood’s Net Income Drops 95% Annually — and Then Its Stock Drops

Commercial Observer — Thu, 06 Aug 2026 17:52:39 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Earnings Report

Starwood Property Trust reported a significant 95% year-over-year decline in net income for Q2 2026, leading to a 4% drop in its stock price. Despite this, Chairman and CEO Barry Sternlicht expressed confidence in the firm's fundamentals, citing improving conditions across most U.S. and European asset classes, particularly in multifamily, self-storage, senior housing, and logistics. The firm's president, Jeffrey DiModica, highlighted a near-record $6.7 billion in year-to-date deployment and strong loan origination, though he acknowledged some multifamily assets in Sun Belt markets faced downgrades due to elevated supply and higher rates. Sternlicht remains optimistic about the firm's ability to restore earnings power and maximize shareholder value by addressing underperforming assets.
