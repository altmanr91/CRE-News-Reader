Constellation Breaks Ground on 492,624 SF Industrial Project in South Fort Worth

CONSTRUCTION

REBusinessOnline — Tue, 30 Jun 2026 14:33:19 +0000

SUMMARY

Constellation Real Estate Partners has commenced construction on Constellation Summit 35, a substantial 492,624-square-foot industrial development in South Fort Worth. The project, situated on a 44-acre site, is designed with modern industrial specifications including 40-foot clear heights and ample parking for both cars and trailers. Completion is anticipated in the third quarter of next year, with KBC Advisors handling the leasing efforts.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Property Name: Constellation Summit 35

Address: 5933 South Freeway, Fort Worth, TX

Size: 492,624 SF

Completion: Q3 2027

Site Area: 44 acres

Notable Features: Features 40-foot clear heights and parking for 296 cars and 194 trailers.
