What Trio of Things Would Our 2026 Lenders Take to a Desert Island?

Commercial Observer — Tue, 03 Nov 2026 15:00:37 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Editorial

This article presents a lighthearted thought experiment, asking several prominent commercial real estate lenders what three items they would bring if stranded on a desert island. The responses range from practical survival tools like a spear, knife, and fire starter, to comfort items such as a lounge chair, sparkling water, and a good book. Some lenders also highlighted their resourcefulness and even their continued focus on real estate, with one joking about financing the island itself and another envisioning building a new home to address the housing shortage.
