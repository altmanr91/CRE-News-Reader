Cross Street unveils branding for nine-story boutique apartment community under construction in Chicago’s River North

DEVELOPMENT

REJournals — Fri, 16 Oct 2026 13:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Cross Street, ZSD Corp., and Third Coast Real Estate have unveiled the branding and official website for 310 West, a nine-story boutique apartment community currently under construction in Chicago's River North neighborhood. The 149-unit development, which recently topped off, will offer a mix of studio to three-bedroom rental apartments, including 11 two-story duplex residences with private patios, along with approximately 3,800 square feet of ground-floor retail. Pre-leasing is slated to begin in February 2027, with initial move-ins expected in spring 2027.
