CBRE closes sale of redevelopment land sites in Chicago’s Fulton market neighborhood

SALE

REJournals — Fri, 18 Sep 2026 16:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

CBRE has facilitated the sale of two redevelopment land sites in Chicago's Fulton Market neighborhood. Range Group acquired 1133 W. Kinzie, while another Chicago-based developer/investor purchased 1201 W. Kinzie. Both parcels were sold by North Carroll, LLC, and are slated for multifamily developments, reflecting the strong investment momentum in the area.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: land

Address: 1133 W. Kinzie, Chicago, IL

Size: 41,714 SF

Notable Features: Located near the intersection of Racine Avenue and Kinzie Street, offering convenient access to the Morgan CTA station and downtown Chicago.

Project Notes: 1133 W. Kinzie is planned for a 109-unit multifamily development, and 1201 W. Kinzie is planned for a 66-unit multifamily development.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: Range Group

BUYER: Chicago-based developer/investor

SELLER: North Carroll, LLC

SELLER BROKER: CBRE — Tom Svoboda; Dominic Soltero; Larry Goldwasser

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Fulton Market is recognized as one of the nation's premier mixed-use urban neighborhoods and a highly desirable development destination in Chicago, with significant new developments like Related Midwest's Sidley Austin-anchored office project highlighting its continued transformation and investment activity.
