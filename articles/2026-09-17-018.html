Cord Meyer Breaks Ground on Rentals at Bay Terrace Shopping Center

CONSTRUCTION

Connect CRE — Wed, 16 Sep 2026 21:51:27 +0000

SUMMARY

Cord Meyer Development has broken ground on a new 145-unit multifamily rental community at its Bay Terrace Shopping Center in Bayside, Queens. The project, valued at over $100 million, will consist of two five-story buildings constructed on existing underutilized parking areas. This development aims to address the pressing need for additional housing options in the community, according to a company executive.
