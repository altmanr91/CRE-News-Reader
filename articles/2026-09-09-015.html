Falling in love with physical retail again? Shoppers seek freedom from the algorithm

REJournals — Wed, 09 Sep 2026 09:55:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Retail Report

JLL's latest report, "Resetting the Retail (Algo)Rhythm," indicates a growing consumer preference for physical retail as an antidote to digital exhaustion and algorithmic influence. Shoppers, particularly Gen Z and Millennials, are seeking discovery and a calmer experience that online platforms often lack. While convenience still drives online shopping, brick-and-mortar stores must offer unique atmospheres and curated experiences to avoid overwhelming consumers, as evidenced by 72% of shoppers leaving stores due to clutter or excessive noise. The report highlights that physical retail never truly lost its appeal, with over 80% of sales still occurring in stores, but successful retailers are those who adapt to provide a distinct, engaging, and less overstimulating environment.

DATA POINTS

Consumers enjoying finding products in stores they wouldn't search for digitally: 83%

Millennial and Gen Z consumers preferring browsing physical stores without algorithmic influence: 53%

Shoppers leaving a store early because it felt overwhelming: 72%

Shoppers influenced by a store's atmosphere to recommend it: 60%

Retail sales happening in brick-and-mortar locations: More than 80%

Abercrombie & Fitch positive results: 14 quarters
