Plenty of positive signs for North American big-box industrial market

REJournals — Thu, 29 Oct 2026 09:25:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Industrial Report

The North American big-box industrial market is demonstrating renewed vitality, with significant increases in leasing activity and net absorption during the first half of 2026. This surge in demand has led to a notable drop in vacancy rates, particularly for larger facilities, indicating a healthier market balance after a period of oversupply. Developers are responding with a more selective approach to new construction, focusing on build-to-suit projects and markets with tightening vacancy and strong tenant demand, while average rental rates are also on the rise.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

New leasing activity in North America reached 170.6 million square feet during the first half of 2026, a 17% increase from the second half of 2025. Net absorption climbed to 87.6 million square feet, more than double the level recorded a year earlier. Big-box vacancy fell to 9.4% from 11.1% a year earlier, with buildings over 750,000 square feet seeing a 7.1% vacancy rate. Construction pipeline increased to 142.6 million square feet from 113.1 million square feet at the end of last year. Average taking rents for big-box properties reached $8.87 per square foot on a triple-net basis, up 3.9% year over year. Chicago's big-box vacancy rate was 8.7% at midyear 2026, with 13.6 million square feet of new leasing and 4.2 million square feet of net absorption. Dallas-Fort Worth led all markets in new leasing during the first half with 26 million square feet.
