L.A. County Investment Sales Jump 28% in July Thanks to Multifamily

Commercial Observer — Thu, 20 Aug 2026 21:20:53 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Investment Sales Report

Commercial real estate investment sales in Los Angeles County saw a significant acceleration in July, with a 28.5% jump in dollar volume compared to the previous year, reaching $2.1 billion across 7.25 million square feet. Multifamily properties were the primary driver, accounting for 58% of all investment with $1.22 billion in sales, reflecting a 55% increase year-over-year. Retail also experienced substantial gains, while industrial sales volume increased but dollar volume remained flat, and the office sector was the only major property type to decline in both sales volume and square footage.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Los Angeles County commercial real estate investment sales in July saw a 28.5% increase in dollar volume year-over-year, reaching $2.1 billion. Multifamily sales volume increased 55% year-over-year, with average pricing up 28.7% to $376/SF. Retail dollar volume rose 40% from a year ago. Industrial dollar volume remained flat despite a 71% increase in square footage traded, with average pricing falling 3.6% to $273/SF. Office sales volume and square footage declined 9.7% and 23.4% respectively from July 2025, with average pricing dropping 23.5% to $250/SF.
