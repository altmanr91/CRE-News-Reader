Kraus-Anderson wraps expansion of health clinic in Pine City

CONSTRUCTION

REJournals — Tue, 03 Nov 2026 16:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Kraus-Anderson has completed a 36,383-square-foot expansion and renovation of the Welia Health Clinic in Pine City, Minnesota. The project, designed by DSGW Architecture, involved a 14,808-square-foot addition and a 21,485-square-foot renovation of the existing medical facility. Key enhancements include new exam rooms, expanded urgent care and rehabilitation services, an outdoor therapy space, and upgraded laboratory and imaging areas, all aimed at improving patient care and staff well-being.

DATA POINTS

Size: 36,383 SF
