Resolute Structured Capital arranges acquisition financing for 240,000-square-foot Chicago-area manufacturing HQ

LOAN

REJournals — Tue, 11 Aug 2026 14:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Resolute Structured Capital arranged acquisition financing for MAG Capital Partners' purchase of a 240,000-square-foot manufacturing and headquarters facility in Chicago Heights, Illinois. The loan, provided by a national bank, covered 75% of the acquisition's loan-to-value. The property serves as the headquarters for Applied Acoustics International, a Tier 1 supplier to the North American automotive industry.
