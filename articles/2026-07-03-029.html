Chicago’s top construction permits: Week ending June 30, 2026

The Real Deal — Chicago — Thu, 02 Jul 2026 18:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Construction / Permits Issued

This article provides a roundup of the top construction permits issued in Chicago for the week ending June 30, 2026. The permits cover a range of projects including a $4.4 million renovation of a group living community, a $3.5 million demolition for a new hospital development, and several office and residential construction and renovation projects. The reported costs for these projects range from $800,000 to $4.4 million, indicating ongoing development and modernization across various property types in the city.
