Industrial Consolidation Continues With European Giants Agreeing To $15B Merger

Bisnow News Feed — Fri, 24 Jul 2026 12:02:49 -0400

SUMMARY

Non-CRE / Business News

French industrial landlord Argan and Belgian firm Warehouses De Pauw (WDP) have announced an all-stock merger, creating a pan-European logistics platform valued at approximately $14.8B. The combined entity will operate across eight countries, leveraging Argan's 3.9M SF portfolio of 110 warehouses in France and WDP's 8M SF holdings. This strategic consolidation aims to optimize debt, enhance operating efficiencies, and generate an estimated $11.3M in synergies within the first year, subject to regulatory and shareholder approvals.

DATA POINTS

Argan Portfolio Size: 3.9M SF across 110 warehouses

WDP Portfolio Size: 8M SF
