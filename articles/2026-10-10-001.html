Charter School Inks 56K-SF Lease in Retail Center Near Miami Suburb

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Fri, 09 Oct 2026 20:50:00 +0000

SUMMARY

BridgePrep Academy, a charter school network, has signed a 56,000-square-foot lease at the California Club Shopping Center in Ives Estates, near Aventura, Florida. The space was previously occupied by another educational institution. The transaction highlights the significant demand for educational facilities in South Florida, particularly for private schools, which has intensified since the pandemic due to an influx of new companies and residents. Brokers noted the challenge of finding suitable properties for educational operators given specific coding and layout requirements, necessitating quick action on available spaces.
