Marcus & Millichap Brokers Sale of 51,542 SF Retail Property in Richardson, Texas

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 09 Oct 2026 16:33:52 +0000

SUMMARY

Marcus & Millichap has brokered the sale of Baybury Square Shopping Center, a 51,542-square-foot retail property in Richardson, Texas. The 11-acre property, comprising two multi-tenant centers and two freestanding buildings constructed in 1966, was 64 percent leased at the time of sale. The transaction involved an out-of-state investor as the seller and a local developer as the buyer, both of whom requested anonymity.
