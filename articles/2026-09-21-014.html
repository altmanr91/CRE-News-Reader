RIVANI Lands $114.3M Refi on Miami Beach Office/Retail Project

REFINANCE

Connect CRE — Mon, 21 Sep 2026 14:13:21 +0000

SUMMARY

RIVANI has secured a $114.3 million refinancing package for its recently completed office and retail development in Miami Beach. The financing, provided by BridgeInvest, will be used to retire existing debt on the 165,170-square-foot property, which is currently 83% leased. The project underwent a significant $38 million renovation after RIVANI acquired it in 2024 for $62.5 million.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: mixed-use

Address: 1691 Michigan Avenue, Miami Beach, FL

Size: 165,170 SF

Loan Amount: $114.3M

Loan Per SF: $692/SF (calculated)

Occupancy at Close: 83.0%

Notable Features: The property features a full-service concierge and valet, spa, fitness and wellness center, meditation room, podcast studio, and a Tokyo-inspired speakeasy.

Project Notes: RIVANI acquired the property for $62.5 million in 2024 and subsequently launched a $38 million renovation. The firm is seeking voter approval for an estimated $50 million expansion above the parking garage, adding approximately 47,000 square feet.
