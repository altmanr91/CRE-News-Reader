Principal Financial Group Loans $32M to Refi Queens Shopping Center

REFINANCE

Commercial Observer — Thu, 20 Aug 2026 18:53:50 +0000

SUMMARY

An affiliate of Mattone Investors secured a $32 million refinancing loan for Springnex Plaza, a 96,028-square-foot shopping center in Queens, NY. Principal Financial Group provided the debt for the fully occupied, supermarket-anchored property. The center was originally developed by Mattone Investors six years ago and has maintained strong occupancy, reflecting continued lender interest in well-maintained, grocery-anchored retail assets.
