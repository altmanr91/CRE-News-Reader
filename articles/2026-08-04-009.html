Savills Completes $1.1B Acquisition of Eastdil Secured

REBusinessOnline — Mon, 03 Aug 2026 14:43:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Non-CRE / Business News

Savills, a London-based real estate brokerage, has finalized its $1.1 billion acquisition of Eastdil Secured, a global advisory firm and real estate investment bank. The combined entity will be rebranded as Eastdil Secured Savills. Eastdil Secured will continue its operations as Savills Group's global real estate investment bank, maintaining its headquarters in New York City, Southern California, and London, while its 21 other offices will integrate into the broader Savills network.
