Cushman & Wakefield Arranges $72M Refinancing for Two Adjacent Shopping Centers in Sandy Springs, Georgia

REFINANCE

REBusinessOnline — Wed, 02 Sep 2026 12:25:39 +0000

SUMMARY

Cushman & Wakefield has arranged a $72 million floating-rate refinancing loan for Jamestown LP, covering two adjacent retail centers in Sandy Springs, Georgia. The properties, Parkside Shops and Hammond Exchange, total over 340,000 square feet and are anchored by a prominent grocery store. The financing was secured through accounts managed by KKR.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: retail

Property Name: Parkside Shops and Hammond Exchange

Size: 334,400 SF

Loan Amount: $72.0M

Site Area: 25 acres

Notable Features: Anchored by a 49,506-square-foot Whole Foods Market.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

SPONSOR: Jamestown LP

LENDER: KKR

MORTGAGE BROKER: Cushman & Wakefield — John Alascio; Alex Hernandez; Alex Lapidus; Mitch Rothstein; Nick Scibelli; Mark Gilbert
