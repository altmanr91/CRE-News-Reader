CBRE Arranges $11M Refinancing for Minnesota Office Property

REFINANCE

Connect CRE — Fri, 28 Aug 2026 20:31:49 +0000

SUMMARY

CBRE Capital Markets has arranged an $11 million refinancing package for Grandview Square, a fully occupied office property in Edina, Minnesota. The five-year, fixed-rate, interest-only loan was secured on behalf of Capital Partners, the property's owner. The property's strong fundamentals, including full occupancy, a diverse tenant mix, and consistent owner reinvestment, attracted competitive interest from lenders.
