Former religious buildings could be blessing for multifamily investors

REJournals — Wed, 14 Oct 2026 09:19:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

This article explores the growing trend of converting former religious buildings into multifamily housing, highlighting their unique appeal to investors and cities seeking to expand housing options. These properties often boast established locations in dense neighborhoods, distinctive architecture that offers a competitive advantage in leasing, and a positive community reception due to preservation efforts. While presenting challenges such as unusual unit configurations and higher utility costs, experienced teams can mitigate these issues. The conversions contribute to the broader adaptive reuse movement, offering a sustainable way to add housing and expand the tax base without requiring new land.
