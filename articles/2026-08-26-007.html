FRP, Woodfield Begin Vertical Construction on Mixed-Use Development in Southwest Florida

CONSTRUCTION

REBusinessOnline — Tue, 25 Aug 2026 14:08:27 +0000

SUMMARY

FRP Development Corp. and Woodfield Development have commenced vertical construction on the initial phase of a mixed-use project in Estero, Florida. This phase will deliver 296 apartments and 30,000 square feet of retail space, with tenant move-ins anticipated by the third quarter of 2027. The developers recently secured an $81.5 million construction loan from Santander Bank for the project.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: mixed-use

Address: 23281 Lyden Drive, Estero, FL

Units: 296

Loan Amount: $81.5M

Loan Per Unit: $275,338/unit (calculated)

Completion: Q3 2027

Phase I: 296 apartments, 30,000 SF retail

Total Project (All Phases): 600 apartments, 80,000 SF retail, 20,000 SF office, 190-room hotel

Notable Features: Amenities include a resort-style pool, gaming lounge, coworking facilities, fitness center, and a sky lounge.

Project Notes: The development will ultimately include 600 apartments, 80,000 square feet of retail, 20,000 square feet of offices, and a 190-room boutique hotel.
