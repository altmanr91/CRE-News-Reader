Waste Storage Manufacturer Citibin Triples Footprint to 15K SF at Industry City

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Mon, 26 Oct 2026 11:00:25 +0000

SUMMARY

Citibin, a manufacturer of waste storage enclosures, has significantly expanded its presence at Industry City in Brooklyn, tripling its warehouse and office footprint to 15,000 square feet. This five-year lease expansion was driven by increased demand for its products, largely due to New York City's new waste containerization policies. The company, which moved to Industry City in 2017, has consolidated its operations and grown its business to supply numerous business improvement districts and customers in other major cities.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Property Name: Industry City, Building 26

Address: 4014 First Avenue, Brooklyn, NY

Size: 15,000 SF

Notable Features: The space accommodates the manufacturer's inventory, installers, offices, and design teams under one roof.

Project Notes: Citibin moved its operations to Industry City in 2017, consolidating from two warehouses in New Jersey and Brooklyn's Red Hook. The expansion was largely due to New York City's recent policies requiring waste to be stored in bins.
