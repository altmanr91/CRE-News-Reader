Marcus & Millichap brokers sale of 42-unit multifamily property in Saint Johns

SALE

REJournals — Thu, 29 Oct 2026 16:09:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Marcus & Millichap brokered the sale of Sturgis Crossings, a 42-unit multifamily property in Saint Johns, Michigan. The firm also arranged acquisition financing for the buyer through its capital corporation subsidiary. The loan features a 10-year term, a 6.3% interest rate, and 25-year amortization.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Sturgis Crossings

Address: 900 E. Sturgis St., Saint Johns, MI

Units: 42

Year Built: 1984

Site Area: 2.8 acres

Notable Features: Convenient access to U.S. Highway 127.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

SELLER BROKER: Marcus & Millichap — Gilewski, Investment Specialist; Chris Futo, Investment Specialist

FINANCING

Acquisition financing with a 10-year term, 6.3% interest rate, 6 months of interest-only payments, and 25-year amortization.
