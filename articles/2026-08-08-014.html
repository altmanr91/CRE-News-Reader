Lawrence Group providing architecture services for health and manufacturing building in Rolla

CONSTRUCTION

REJournals — Fri, 07 Aug 2026 14:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Lawrence Group is providing architecture, interior design, and planning services for East Central College's new $11 million Allied Health and Advanced Manufacturing Building in Rolla, Missouri. The 107,000-square-foot renovation project will consolidate the college's academic programs, student services, and advanced manufacturing center into a single facility. This integration aims to enhance operational efficiency by eliminating the need for the college to lease two separate off-site locations, while also strengthening campus identity and fostering collaborative learning environments.
