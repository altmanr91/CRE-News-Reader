Colliers Arranges Sale of 1.5 MSF Industrial Portfolio in Indianapolis

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Tue, 08 Sep 2026 12:57:54 +0000

SUMMARY

Colliers has facilitated the sale of the Indianapolis West Infill Industrial Portfolio, a 1.5 million-square-foot collection of 29 light industrial buildings in Indianapolis. MLG Capital acquired the portfolio, which is 94 percent leased to a diverse tenant mix, on behalf of its MLG Legacy Fund and 1031 exchange investors. The properties are situated within the Park 100 and Park Fletcher industrial parks, spanning the city's Northwest and Southwest submarkets.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Property Name: Indianapolis West Infill Industrial Portfolio

Size: 1,500,000 SF

Occupancy at Sale: 94.0%

Notable Features: The portfolio consists of 29 buildings ranging from 16,000 to 157,000 square feet, featuring an average clear height of 15 feet with 194 dock doors and 155 drive-in doors.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: MLG Capital

SELLER BROKER: Colliers — Alex Cantu; Alex Davenport; Jeff Devine; Steve Disse; Tyler Ziebel; Tyler Wilson; Jason Speckman; Sydney Gabriel
