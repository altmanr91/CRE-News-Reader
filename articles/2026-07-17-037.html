Brookfield ups market-rate unit count, building heights at Pier 70 project

The Real Deal — San Francisco — Thu, 16 Jul 2026 18:45:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Development

Brookfield Properties is seeking to increase the residential unit count and building heights for its Pier 70 redevelopment in San Francisco's Dogpatch neighborhood. The proposed changes would raise the maximum residential count from 2,150 to 2,750 units and increase building heights from 70 feet to 90 feet. This adjustment aims to make the project more financially viable, allowing Brookfield to break ground on the first market-rate apartment building, a 350-unit project, in 2027. The overall first phase of development would now include 700 homes, up from 575 units, with the additional density helping the project pencil out amidst rising construction costs.
