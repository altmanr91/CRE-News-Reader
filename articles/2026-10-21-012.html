A major mixed-use success story in Sugar Land: Why Sugar Land Town Square continues to thrive

LEASE

REJournals — Wed, 21 Oct 2026 13:53:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Sugar Land Town Square, a prominent mixed-use development in Sugar Land, TX, is undergoing a $12.5 million revitalization to update its offerings and maintain competitiveness. The improvements, nearing completion, include upgrades to office buildings with new amenities like a café, shared meeting spaces, and a tenant lounge. This reinvestment has already spurred leasing activity, with a Fortune 500 firm signing a significant office lease and new restaurant, retail, and entertainment concepts being introduced. City officials emphasize that the revitalization is crucial for the district to evolve with modern market demands and continue its role as a vital economic and social hub.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Property Name: Town Square One

Size: 25,352 SF

Total Project Cost: $12.5M

Completion: 2026

Site Area: 32 acres

Notable Features: Mixed-use district with retail, office, and residential properties; includes a new fitness center and various dining and entertainment options.

Project Notes: Revitalization project for an existing mixed-use district, supported by an incentive program. The district was originally developed over 20 years ago as part of the City’s first public-private partnership.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Sugar Land Town Square generates the highest taxable value per acre of any property in Fort Bend County, according to an Urban3 study. The district has welcomed 70 million visitors over the past two decades.
