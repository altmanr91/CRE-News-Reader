Colliers negotiates sale of 105,675-square-foot industrial building in Irving

SALE

REJournals — Thu, 01 Oct 2026 13:40:00 +0000

SUMMARY

A joint venture between Hopewell Development and MBK Industrial Properties has sold Tristar Business Park Building 2, a 105,675-square-foot Class-A industrial building in Irving, Texas. The buyer, Blue Ocean TX Industrial, LLC, acquired the asset, which is strategically located near Dallas-Fort Worth International Airport and major highways. The property's modern specifications and prime location in a robust industrial market attracted significant investor interest.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Property Name: Tristar Business Park Building 2

Address: 7810 Jetstar Drive, Irving, TX

Size: 105,675 SF

Notable Features: Class-A industrial building with 32-foot clear heights, ESFR sprinkler systems, 10 dock-high doors, two grade-level doors, and 2,589 square feet of office space, located minutes from Dallas-Fort Worth International Airport with direct access to State Highway 114 and Belt Line Road.
