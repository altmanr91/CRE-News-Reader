Interra Realty closes $3.25 million sale of multifamily building in Chicago’s Rogers Park neighborhood

SALE

REJournals — Thu, 02 Jul 2026 14:41:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Interra Realty brokered the $3.25 million sale of a newly renovated 12-unit multifamily building in Chicago's Rogers Park neighborhood. The property, which was 92% occupied at the time of sale, features high-end finishes and is conveniently located near public transit and local amenities. A local family real estate operator acquired the asset from a local private developer.

Units: 12

Notable Features: Newly renovated units with high-end finishes including hardwood floors, stainless steel appliances, and granite countertops. Located near dining, shopping, and public transportation options including Metra and CTA stations.
