Houston’s office glut is fueling a demolition trend for mostly vacant properties

The Real Deal — Texas — Thu, 02 Jul 2026 21:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

Houston's office market is experiencing a demolition trend for mostly vacant properties due to record-high vacancy rates and a glut of obsolete buildings. While maintaining an empty lot is cheaper than an outdated building, many properties cannot be demolished due to existing debt and lenders unwilling to assume ownership. Owner-occupiers are increasingly purchasing vacant office space at low prices, with approximately 2 million square feet acquired in 2025, including a significant deal by Energy Transfer.

DATA POINTS

Houston office vacancy rate: 26.8%

Galleria area positive absorption (2025): 200,000 square feet

Owner-occupier purchases of vacant space (2025): 2 million square feet
