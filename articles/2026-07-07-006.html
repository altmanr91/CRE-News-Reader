IPA Negotiates Sale of 201-Unit Apartment Complex Near Downtown Dallas

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Mon, 06 Jul 2026 16:27:14 +0000

SUMMARY

Institutional Property Advisors (IPA) has facilitated the sale of Sylvan Thirty, a 201-unit apartment complex situated west of downtown Dallas. The transaction involved an undisclosed seller and California-based Archway Equities as the buyer. IPA also arranged acquisition financing for the deal through BrightSpire Capital.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Sylvan Thirty

Address: 1818 Sylvan Ave, Dallas, TX*

Units: 201

Year Built: 2015

Notable Features: Amenities include a pool, rooftop terrace, game room, business center, fitness center, and outdoor grilling and dining stations.

* Address sourced separately, not in original article.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: Archway Equities
