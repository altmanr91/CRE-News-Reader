Auto Parts Distributor Signs $42M Lease in Southern California

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Thu, 02 Jul 2026 19:25:14 +0000

SUMMARY

An auto parts distribution company has signed a lease valued at over $42 million for a 522,267-square-foot industrial building in Chino, CA. This transaction represents a significant expansion of the tenant's distribution footprint in Southern California, according to the tenant's broker. The deal underscores the tight market for large distribution space in the Inland Empire.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Property Name: Building 3 at the Chino South Industrial Center

Address: 16380 Euclid Avenue, Chino, CA

Size: 522,267 SF

Year Built: 2014

Notable Features: The property features 32-foot clear heights and dock-high loading bays.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Chino's direct industrial vacancy rate was 3.24 percent in the first quarter, indicating limited availability for large distribution space.
