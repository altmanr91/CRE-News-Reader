MADDD Equities Scoops Up Vacant West Farms Industrial

SALE

Connect CRE — Fri, 07 Aug 2026 21:29:58 +0000

SUMMARY

MADDD Equities has acquired a vacant 60,000-square-foot multi-story industrial property at 425 Devoe Ave. in the West Farms neighborhood of The Bronx for $10.75 million. The property, which also includes 30,000 square feet below grade, was sold by Abingdon Square Partners. The sale highlights the robust demand for large commercial sites with excellent regional transportation access.
