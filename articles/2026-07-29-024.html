Mamdani Adds Millions To Program Helping Retailers Negotiate With Landlords

Bisnow News Feed — Tue, 28 Jul 2026 17:30:35 -0400

SUMMARY

Infrastructure Investment / Government Policy

New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani has doubled the funding for a city program that provides legal assistance to commercial tenants in lease negotiations, increasing the allocation from $4 million to $8 million. The program, run by the Department of Small Business Services, helps small businesses navigate rising rents and competitive market conditions by offering support for new leases, renewals, terminations, and disputes. Industry professionals note that the program benefits both tenants by providing crucial guidance and landlords by ensuring more capable and informed negotiation partners, ultimately streamlining the leasing process in a tight retail market.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Average retail availability in New York City's nine prime corridors was 11.9% at the end of Q1, the lowest quarterly rate recorded by JLL since Q3 2017. This lack of available space is driving tenants to side streets. Average asking rents in JLL's prime submarkets increased from $585 per SF in Q1 to $592 per SF by the end of Q2, though still below the post-Covid peak of $608 per SF.
