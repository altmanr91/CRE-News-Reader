David Martin in talks to join Related, BH on Hollywood Beach Resort redevelopment

ACQUISITION

The Real Deal — Miami — Thu, 02 Jul 2026 21:30:00 +0000

SUMMARY

David Martin's Terra Group is in discussions to join Related Group and BH Group in the planned redevelopment of the Hollywood Beach Resort. Related and BH Group are under contract to acquire the 4.5-acre oceanfront property from The Chetrit Group for $100 million. The partnership intends to redevelop the 398-unit resort, which was originally built in 1925. The deal is significant as it brings together prominent South Florida developers on a major redevelopment project.

DATA POINTS

Project Notes: The Chetrit Group acquired the property through a bulk purchase of condos and timeshares, which concluded in 2023.
