Clay Development Breaks Ground on Two Industrial Buildings Totaling 552,240 SF in Brookshire, Texas

CONSTRUCTION

REBusinessOnline — Tue, 11 Aug 2026 14:52:59 +0000

SUMMARY

Clay Development & Construction has commenced construction on two new industrial buildings, Twinwood Distribution Center IV and V, totaling 552,240 square feet within the Uplands Twinwood Business Park in Brookshire, Texas. These facilities are part of the initial 200-acre phase of the business park and are expected to be completed in the first quarter of next year. KBC Advisors has been appointed to handle the leasing for both properties.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Property Name: Twinwood Distribution Center IV and V

Size: 552,240 SF

Completion: Q1 2027

Notable Features: Buildings feature 36-foot clear heights, 69 dock-high doors, four drive-in ramps, and 2,600 square feet of office space each, with a combined 342 car parking spaces and 120 trailer parking spaces.

Project Notes: These buildings are part of the 200-acre first phase of Uplands Twinwood Business Park.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

DEVELOPER/SPONSOR: Clay Development & Construction Inc.

LEASING AGENT: KBC Advisors
