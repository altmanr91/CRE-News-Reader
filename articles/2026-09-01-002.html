GreenBarn’s David Welsh and David Schonbraun Aren’t Afraid of a Little Complexity

Commercial Observer — Tue, 01 Sep 2026 14:00:31 +0000

SUMMARY

Feature / Profile

GreenBarn Investment Group, co-founded by industry veterans David Welsh and David Schonbraun, has rapidly grown to manage approximately $3.5 billion in investments since its rebranding in 2023. The firm specializes in complex, opportunistic credit investments across various sectors and capital structures, leveraging the partners' extensive experience in capital markets, lending, and real estate operations. GreenBarn's strategy involves actively pursuing challenging deals, including debt restructurings, recapitalizations, and mezzanine loans, often partnering with former competitors and relying on direct relationships for deal flow.
