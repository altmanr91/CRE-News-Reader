S2 Capital Buys 589,022 SF Industrial Park in Fort Worth’s Historic Southside District

ACQUISITION

REBusinessOnline — Thu, 20 Aug 2026 15:10:07 +0000

SUMMARY

S2 Capital's industrial investment arm has acquired a 589,022-square-foot industrial park in Fort Worth's Historic Southside district. The new owner plans to rebrand the 31-building development as Panther City Industrial Park and will invest in capital improvements to the suite interiors. The previous ownership had already completed upgrades to common areas and building facades.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Property Name: Panther City Industrial Park

Address: 5721 E. Rosedale St., Fort Worth, TX
