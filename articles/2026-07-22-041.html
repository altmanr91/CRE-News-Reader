Resorts World NYC Adds 1,400 Slot Machines as Part of $5.5B Expansion

DEVELOPMENT

Connect CRE — Tue, 21 Jul 2026 20:45:44 +0000

SUMMARY

Resorts World New York City has opened the first floor of its commercial casino, adding 1,400 slot machines and bringing the total onsite to 3,900. This expansion is part of a larger $5.5 billion development plan for the site, which is expected to significantly boost gross gaming revenue and tax contributions to New York State. The initial slot machines have already generated over $230 million in gross gaming revenue in 11 weeks, indicating strong public demand.
