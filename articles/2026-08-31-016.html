Pearl Developers Hoping for Success with Dittlinger Mill Redevelopment

Connect CRE — Mon, 31 Aug 2026 16:00:16 +0000

SUMMARY

Development

Oxbow, the developer behind the Pearl redevelopment, is planning a mixed-use project to revitalize the historic Dittlinger Mill in New Braunfels, TX. The project will incorporate residential, office, retail, and food-and-beverage components while preserving the site's existing historic mill buildings and industrial structures. The developer is seeking public funding from New Braunfels officials for necessary infrastructure improvements related to traffic, parking, drainage, and utility capacity to facilitate the project's anticipated 2028 start.
