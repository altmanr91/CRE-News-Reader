Overall CMBS Distress Hits a 2026 High

Commercial Observer — Mon, 03 Aug 2026 12:00:37 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / CMBS Report

The overall distress rate for commercial mortgage-backed securities (CMBS) reached 10.91% in July 2026, marking a three-month upward trend. This figure, which includes loans in special servicing or 30-plus days delinquent, is driven by a significant increase in special servicing referrals (10.38%), indicating proactive transfers due to upcoming maturities or borrower requests rather than just missed payments. Office properties are the primary outlier, with a distress rate of 16.65%, while industrial and self-storage sectors remain robust. The report emphasizes that a granular, loan-level view is crucial for understanding and addressing the uneven distribution of distress across property types and metro areas.

DATA POINTS

Overall CMBS distress rate (July 2026): 10.91%

Special servicing rate (July 2026): 10.38%

Delinquency rate (July 2026): 8.68%

Office property distress rate: 16.65%

Mixed-use property distress rate: 13.01%

Multifamily property distress rate: 11.21%

Industrial property distress rate: 2.35%

Self-storage property distress rate: 0.28%

Total CMBS universe: $600 billion-plus

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

The CMBS market is experiencing a widening spread between special servicing and delinquency rates, indicating proactive loan transfers rather than just missed payments. Conduit-sourced delinquent balances outpaced single-asset, single-borrower (SASB) loans in July. Distress is concentrated in specific property types, with office being a clear outlier, and unevenly distributed across metro areas, particularly on the West Coast and in the Midwest.
