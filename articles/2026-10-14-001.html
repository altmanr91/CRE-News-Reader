Podcast: Fire Prevention Tech to Protect Homes After LA Fires, CitroTech’s Wes Bolsen

Commercial Observer — Wed, 14 Oct 2026 14:18:25 +0000

SUMMARY

Feature / Profile

This article is a Q&A interview with Wes Bolsen, CEO of CitroTech, discussing the company's fire prevention technology and its implications for homebuilders and developers in fire-prone regions. Bolsen highlights the shift towards community-wide fire protection over individual home solutions, emphasizing the cost-effectiveness of prevention versus suppression. He also touches on how insurance companies are influencing construction practices and the potential for fire resilience to become a marketing differentiator for new homes, particularly in states like California, Colorado, Montana, and Idaho.
