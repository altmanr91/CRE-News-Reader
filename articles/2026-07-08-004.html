Manufacturing, Big-Box Leasing Fuel U.S. Industrial Reset in 2026

Commercial Observer — Tue, 07 Jul 2026 19:55:31 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Industrial Report

The U.S. industrial market is experiencing a significant reset in 2026, driven by robust leasing activity, particularly for large spaces, and a diversifying demand base fueled by a new wave of manufacturing investment. National asking rents have risen despite elevated vacancy, while new construction has moderated from pandemic-era highs. This shift is characterized by substantial manufacturing job announcements and capital investment, with a notable focus on aerospace, defense, AI infrastructure, energy equipment, and pharmaceuticals, rather than solely electric vehicles.

DATA POINTS

National asking rents year-over-year increase: 1.8%

National asking rent (triple-net): $9.74 per square foot per year

National vacancy rate Q2 2026: 8.2%

Industrial leasing H1 2026: 491 million square feet

Industrial leasing H1 2026 vs H1 2025 increase: 27%

Quarterly net absorption year-over-year increase: more than doubled to 53.3 million square feet

First-half absorption vs H1 2025 increase: 28%

National construction pipeline: 320 million square feet

Deliveries Q2 2026: 49.4 million square feet

Manufacturing jobs announced (trailing 12 months through May): nearly 66,000

Planned capital investment in manufacturing: almost $50 billion

Defense manufacturing share of announced jobs: roughly 40%

AI and energy infrastructure share of announced jobs: 26%

Life sciences share of announced jobs: 12%

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

National vacancy rate finished Q2 2026 at 8.2%, unchanged from Q1 and 10 basis points higher than a year ago. Industrial construction pipeline is 320 million square feet, less than half the 782 million-square-foot peak in late 2022. Deliveries dropped to 49.4 million square feet during Q2 2026 from over 82 million square feet at this point last year. Southern California remains the top market due to its port complex. North Carolina, Texas, and California led the nation in announced manufacturing jobs.
