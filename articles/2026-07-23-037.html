Clarion Partners offloads Sunnyvale offices to SFF Realty for $46M

SALE

The Real Deal — San Francisco — Wed, 22 Jul 2026 23:09:27 +0000

SUMMARY

Clarion Partners, through an affiliate, has sold a 104,000-square-foot research and development office building in Sunnyvale, CA, to an affiliate of SFF Realty Partners for $46 million. The property is fully leased to a German tech company, which uses it as an engineering hub. The transaction highlights the continued investor interest in Silicon Valley's office and R&D market, driven by the presence of tech and artificial intelligence tenants.
