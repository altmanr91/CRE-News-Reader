Investor Duo Acquires Two Tennessee LIHTC Apartment Assets

ACQUISITION

Connect CRE — Tue, 27 Oct 2026 14:08:35 +0000

SUMMARY

Western Wealth Capital (WWC) and FSO Capital Partners have acquired a two-property, 192-unit affordable housing portfolio in Tennessee for $20.7 million. The portfolio includes River City North Apartment Homes in Chattanooga and Meadow Creek Apartment Homes in Goodlettsville, north of Nashville. This acquisition leverages FSO Capital Partners' expertise in Low-Income Housing Tax Credit (LIHTC) investments, ensuring the properties continue to provide below-market rents under existing Section 42 LIHTC Land Use Restriction Agreements.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: River City North Apartment Homes / Meadow Creek Apartment Homes

Address: 919 S. Dickerson Pike, Goodlettsville, TN

Units: 192

Sale Price: $20.7M

Notable Features: Both communities operate under Section 42 LIHTC Land Use Restriction Agreements (LURAs), holding rents below market levels.

Project Notes: River City North was built in 2007 and Meadow Creek in 2003. River City North is financed with an $8.47 million loan at a 6.17% fixed rate with five years of interest-only payments. Meadow Creek's existing $7.79 million mortgage was assumed at a 4.60% blended rate.
