CBRE closes sale of 4.66-acre parcel in Fitchburg

ACQUISITION

REJournals — Wed, 04 Nov 2026 15:14:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Trilogy Real Estate Fitchburg LLC has acquired a 4.66-acre parcel at the intersection of County Highway MM and U.S. Highway 14 in Fitchburg, Wisconsin, for $2.23 million. This purchase marks Trilogy's third location in the Madison market, expanding its presence beyond Waunakee and Sun Prairie. The site is part of the larger Syncopation mixed-use development, which will include multifamily apartments, commercial space, and a senior living campus.
