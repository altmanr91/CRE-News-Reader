CEDARst Snags $80M Construction Loan for San Diego Apartments

LOAN

Connect CRE — Thu, 03 Sep 2026 21:10:25 +0000

SUMMARY

CEDARst Companies secured an $80 million construction loan for The Samuel, a 197-unit Class A multifamily development in San Diego's North Park neighborhood. The financing, arranged by JLL, will facilitate the construction of the eight-story building, which is expected to break ground in Q3 2026 and be completed by Q4 2028. The project will offer a mix of studio, one-, and two-bedroom units along with a comprehensive suite of amenities.
