JLL Negotiates Sale of 441,620 SF Retail Power Center in Cedar Park, Texas

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 24 Jul 2026 15:11:05 +0000

SUMMARY

JLL has negotiated the sale of 1890 Ranch, a 441,620-square-foot retail power center in Cedar Park, Texas. The property was 99 percent leased at the time of sale to a strong tenant mix. A joint venture between Sterling Organization and Cohen & Steers acquired the asset, with JLL also arranging acquisition financing for the buyer.
