Partners Land Fund I brokers sale of 16.7-acre land parcel at Waco Gateway Park

SALE

REJournals — Tue, 06 Oct 2026 15:02:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Partners Land Fund I, an investment vehicle of Partners Capital, has sold a 16.7-acre industrial parcel within Waco Gateway Park to Partners Development Company, the development arm of Partners Real Estate. This transaction, which closed in August, marks the first significant industrial sale from the 153-acre park, originally acquired by the fund in November 2023. Proceeds from the sale will be used to finance infrastructure development within the park, including the extension of Gateway Boulevard and the installation of municipal utilities.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Property Name: Waco Gateway Park

Site Area: 16.7 acres
