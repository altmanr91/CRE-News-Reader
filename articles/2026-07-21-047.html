Chicago Ranks No. 3 For AI Job Growth As Office Market Stands To Benefit

Bisnow News Feed — Mon, 20 Jul 2026 17:48:42 -0400

SUMMARY

Market Research / Labor Report

Chicago is emerging as a significant hub for artificial intelligence job growth, ranking third nationally behind New York City and San Francisco in year-over-year increases in AI-related job postings. This surge, with nearly 10,000 new postings, is driven by demand across diverse sectors including medical, finance, and education, not just traditional tech companies. While AI companies' national office footprint has grown to 9.2M SF, and 308K SF in Chicago, the direct impact of this hiring boom on office leasing trends in Chicago remains uncertain, as companies are not yet translating AI job growth into a one-to-one increase in office space demand.

DATA POINTS

Chicago AI job posting increase (year-over-year): just fewer than 10,000

National AI companies' office footprint (Q2): 9.2M SF

Chicago AI firms' office footprint (mid-2026): 308K SF

Building tours by AI-focused prospective tenants (through May): jumped 85%

AI-related job postings in Chicago (2024-2025): surged 80.6%

National office-using employment vs. 2019: 10% higher

Chicago office-using employment growth (Q2): 15% to 20% quarter-over-quarter
