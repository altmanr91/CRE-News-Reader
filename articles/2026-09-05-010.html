JLL Arranges Sale of 356,514 SF Office Building in Orlando

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 04 Sep 2026 13:44:39 +0000

SUMMARY

JLL Capital Markets has arranged the sale of Sea Harbor Office Center, a 356,514-square-foot office building in Orlando, Florida. Northridge Capital LLC sold the property to a joint venture between Duvalla Investments and Epoch Residential. The property's Planned Development zoning allows for future hotel or residential development, indicating potential for a significant repositioning or expansion of the asset.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Property Name: Sea Harbor Office Center

Address: 6277 Sea Harbor Dr, Orlando, FL*

Size: 356,514 SF

Year Built: 1984

Site Area: 14.2 acres

Notable Features: Located across from SeaWorld, features a full-service cafeteria, fitness center, two lobbies, and a five-story parking garage with 1,250 spaces, plus 380 surface spaces. Planned Development (PD) zoning allows for future hotel and/or residential development.

* Address sourced separately, not in original article.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: Duvalla Investments
