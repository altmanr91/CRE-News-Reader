Longpoint Partners Buys Miami-Dade Warehouses for $39M

ACQUISITION

Commercial Observer — Tue, 07 Jul 2026 22:20:56 +0000

SUMMARY

Longpoint Partners has acquired a three-warehouse industrial complex in Miami-Dade County for $38.8 million. The fully leased small-bay warehouses, totaling 121,579 square feet across two addresses in Medley, were sold by Martha and Ruben Artiles, who had owned the properties since the 1980s and 1990s. This acquisition continues Longpoint Partners' active investment strategy in the South Florida industrial market.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Address: 7200 Northwest 84th Avenue, Medley, FL

Size: 121,579 SF

Sale Price: $38.8M

Occupancy at Sale: 100.0%

Site Area: 5.2 acres

Notable Features: Small-bay warehouses located about half a mile west of the Palmetto Expressway.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: Longpoint Partners

SELLER: Martha and Ruben Artiles — Martha Artiles; Ruben Artiles
