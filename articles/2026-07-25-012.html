Berkadia arranges financing for 50-bed inpatient rehab facility in Bismarck

LOAN

REJournals — Fri, 24 Jul 2026 16:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Berkadia has arranged both equity placement and development financing for Vibra Hospital of the Central Dakotas, a new 50-bed inpatient rehabilitation facility and long-term acute care hospital in Bismarck, ND. The build-to-suit facility will be fully leased to Vibra Hospital of the Central Dakotas and will replace an existing 41-bed LTACH while introducing a new IRF to the market. The project aims to enhance patient access and care through its central location and all-private rooms.
