Loop Adjacent: Small-bay project fills a gap in East Pilsen

DEVELOPMENT

REJournals — Tue, 15 Sep 2026 09:59:55 +0000

SUMMARY

Podmajersky, Inc. is developing a 12,000-square-foot speculative industrial facility in Chicago's East Pilsen neighborhood, addressing a critical shortage of modern small-bay industrial space near the Loop. The project, scheduled for 2027 delivery, aims to attract food users, industrial distributors, and service companies that require quick access to the central business district and major highways. The developer, known for its work in the East Pilsen arts district, is designing the new building to blend with the area's historic architecture while offering modern features like 24-foot clear heights and flexible power capacity.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Address: 2100 S. Canalport Ave., Chicago, IL

Size: 12,000 SF

Completion: 2027

Notable Features: 24-foot clear heights, dock and drive-in loading, flexible power capacity, Enterprise Zone incentives, located 600 feet from I-90/I-55 interchange and 2.5 miles from downtown.

Project Notes: The developer has owned the parcel for years and shifted its vision for the site as industrial users were pushed out of Fulton Market. The design borrows from the adjacent 900 W. Cermak Road building, featuring Chicago common brick and a scale that fits historic commercial structures.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

DEVELOPER/SPONSOR: Podmajersky, Inc. — John Podmajersky III, President

LEASING AGENT: Avison Young — Steven Kohn, Principal

SPONSOR PIPELINE

900 W. Cermak Road: Chicago industrial loft housing Gustave Larson and Bento Inc., a sushi provider.

East Pilsen portfolio: Renovated, managed, and leased over 300,000 square feet of creative industrial, loft office, retail, art, and residential space.

Second phase nearby: Early planning for an additional project.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Avison Young's Q2 2026 Chicago Industrial report indicates no available Class-A industrial buildings between 10,000 and 20,000 square feet within a five-mile radius of 2100 S. Canalport Ave. The Pilsen market is experiencing growth across all property types, including office, retail, industrial, and residential.
