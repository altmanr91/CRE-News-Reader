Orlando developer in contract to buy out aging West Palm co-op for $200M

ACQUISITION

The Real Deal — Miami — Thu, 09 Jul 2026 22:30:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Orlando-based Unicorp National Developments, led by Chuck Whittall, is under contract to acquire La Fontana, a 140-unit waterfront co-op building in West Palm Beach, for approximately $200 million. The 65-year-old property, located at 2800 North Flagler Drive, was marketed for bulk sale by Serhant. This potential acquisition highlights a growing trend in South Florida where developers are buying out aging co-op and condo buildings due to limited undeveloped waterfront land and new state condo safety laws that have increased maintenance costs for unit owners.
