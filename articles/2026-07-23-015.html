Redline Property Partners acquires the 15 Building in downtown Minneapolis

ACQUISITION

REJournals — Wed, 22 Jul 2026 14:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Redline Property Partners has acquired The 15 Building, a 12-story, 131,460-square-foot boutique office building in downtown Minneapolis. The property, now rebranded as "Studio 15," is adjacent to Redline's existing Andrus office building and features Art Deco design, recent renovations, and a strategic location with skyway connectivity and transit access. Redline plans to continue the building's amenitization program and invest in system improvements.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Property Name: The 15 Building

Address: 15 S. 5th St, Minneapolis, MN

Size: 131,460 SF

Year Built: 1916

Notable Features: Art Deco design elements, Bob Dylan mural, skyway connection, transit-oriented location, adjacent to Redline's Andrus office building.

Project Notes: Building was previously renovated in 2018 with spec suites, bike room, and tenant lounge/conference center. Redline intends to continue amenitization and improve building systems.
