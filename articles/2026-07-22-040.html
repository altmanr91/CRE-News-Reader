Original Developer Trades Castro Valley Multifamily Duo

SALE

Connect CRE — Tue, 21 Jul 2026 21:02:58 +0000

SUMMARY

Berkadia has facilitated the sale and acquisition financing of two adjacent multifamily properties in Castro Valley, California, totaling 135 units. The Cedars, an 83-unit community, sold for $21.45 million, while the 52-unit Creekside Terrace fetched $14.1 million. The properties, which offer significant value-add potential, were sold by their original developer and longtime owner, Felson Companies, to Prime Residential. The transaction highlights strong investor demand for rare, long-held assets in the Bay Area, despite a complex market environment.
