The Next Office Cycle Will Be Defined by Scarcity, Not Oversupply

Connect CRE — Thu, 30 Jul 2026 22:30:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

The office sector is transitioning from a period of oversupply to one of scarcity, driven by a shrinking construction pipeline, improving leasing activity, and a sustained tenant preference for high-quality spaces. This shift is creating a more competitive market where tenants are vying for a limited supply of top-tier office buildings, leading to rising rents and falling vacancies. While completions are expected to increase in the coming months, significant preleasing activity may limit available space, potentially drawing more developers back into the market as the cost of capital decreases.
