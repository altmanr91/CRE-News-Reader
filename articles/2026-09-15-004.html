Property Operations Platform Visitt Teams With VendorPM

Commercial Observer — Tue, 15 Sep 2026 13:00:23 +0000

SUMMARY

Non-CRE / Business News

Visitt, an AI-native property operations platform, and VendorPM, a vendor lifecycle management company, have partnered to create a unified system for managing buildings and vendors. This collaboration aims to streamline daily operational workflows and ensure vendor compliance by integrating VendorPM's capabilities with Visitt's platform. The partnership addresses the industry trend of technology consolidation in commercial real estate, enabling property teams to work more efficiently and reduce complexity.
