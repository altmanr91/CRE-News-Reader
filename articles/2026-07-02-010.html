Gantry provides $32.5 million loan for multifamily community in Kansas City

LOAN

REJournals — Thu, 02 Jul 2026 10:03:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Gantry has secured a $32.5 million permanent loan for Edison at Tiffany Springs, a Class A multifamily community in Kansas City, MO. The financing serves as construction takeout for the 243-unit property, which was delivered in 2022. The 10-year, fixed-rate, non-recourse loan was provided through Freddie Mac, featuring partial interest-only payments before transitioning to a 35-year amortization schedule.
