Gilbane, Strada Development Break Ground on Affordable Tower in Castle Hill/Soundview

CONSTRUCTION

Connect CRE — Fri, 16 Oct 2026 21:25:21 +0000

SUMMARY

Gilbane and Strada Development LLC have broken ground on 1920 Turnbull, a $175 million affordable and supportive housing development in the Castle Hill/Soundview neighborhood of the Bronx. The 22-story building will feature 228 affordable rental apartments, with 137 units designated for formerly homeless individuals and families. This project represents the first phase of a larger development, with a second building planned to add another 207 affordable apartments.

Notable Features: Includes 137 permanent supportive housing units for formerly homeless individuals and families, with a 24/7 staffed lobby, outdoor recreation space, fitness room, community room, bike storage, and laundry facilities.

Project Notes: This is the first of two planned buildings; Phase 2 will add 207 additional affordable apartments, 18% of which will be supportive housing, with construction planned to begin within the next year.
