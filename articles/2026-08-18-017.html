SRS Arranges Sale of Newly Built Caliber Collision Property

SALE

Connect CRE — Mon, 17 Aug 2026 22:13:34 +0000

SUMMARY

SRS Real Estate Partners has completed the $5.24 million sale of a newly constructed Caliber Collision property in Chula Vista, CA. The 16,832-square-foot asset, part of a larger retail development, was acquired by a Southern California-based family trust from a Southern California developer. The property features a 15-year corporate-guaranteed absolute triple net lease, attracting significant investor interest and selling above its asking price, reflecting a 5.5% cap rate. Its strategic location near auto dealerships and a dense population base positions the tenant for strong business.
