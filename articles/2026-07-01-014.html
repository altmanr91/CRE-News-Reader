Essex Realty Group closes sale of three multifamily properties in Chicago

SALE

REJournals — Wed, 01 Jul 2026 10:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Essex Realty Group has facilitated the sale of three distinct multifamily properties across Chicago, totaling 20 units and approximately $9.6 million in transaction volume. The portfolio included a newly constructed luxury building in West Town, a value-add opportunity in Ukrainian Village, and a vacant repositioning asset in Uptown. These sales underscore sustained investor interest in diverse multifamily assets throughout the Chicago market, from luxury new builds to properties with significant upside potential.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Units: 20

Sale Price: $9.6M

Project Notes: The properties included 1049 N. Wolcott Avenue (6 units, sold for $1,405,000), 918 W. Eastwood Avenue (6 units, sold for $1,985,000), and 1620 W. Grand Avenue (8 units, sold for $6,225,000).

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

SELLER BROKER: Essex Realty Group, LLC — Matt Feo, Principal; Abe Eilian, Director
