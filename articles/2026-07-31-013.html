CRG adds student housing veteran to Chicago office

REJournals — Fri, 31 Jul 2026 09:51:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Promotion

CRG has appointed Carly Shepherd as Senior Vice President of Sales and Marketing, a strategic move to enhance the firm's student housing and residential platform. Based in CRG's Chicago office, Shepherd will lead sales and marketing strategy for the company's Chapter portfolio of student housing and multifamily communities across the nation. Her extensive experience in student housing operations, leasing, and marketing is expected to bolster CRG's vertically integrated approach to developing and leasing high-quality residential projects.
