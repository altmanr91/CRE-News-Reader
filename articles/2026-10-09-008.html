Northmarq Arranges $15M Loan for Refinancing of Queens Shopping Center

REFINANCE

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 09 Oct 2026 15:22:41 +0000

SUMMARY

Northmarq has secured a $15 million refinancing loan for the Atlantic Plaza Shopping Center, a 34,949-square-foot retail property in Queens, New York City. The loan was arranged through a commercial bank on behalf of the owner, Schuckman Realty Inc. The shopping center, built in 1984, features a strong tenant mix.
