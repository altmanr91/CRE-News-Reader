Dermatology Chain LaserAway Adds 15th NYC Location on the Upper East Side

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Tue, 01 Sep 2026 19:37:26 +0000

SUMMARY

LaserAway, a provider of dermatology and body wellness services, has signed a 10-year lease for 1,800 square feet at 242 East 86th Street in Manhattan's Upper East Side. This marks the company's 15th location in New York City, expanding its presence in a key urban market. The asking rent for the ground-floor space was $165 per square foot.
