Record Diesel Prices Fuel More Challenges for Retail Real Estate

Commercial Observer — Mon, 05 Oct 2026 16:01:36 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

Record-high diesel prices, surpassing $6 per gallon in September, are significantly impacting U.S. retail real estate development by increasing transportation and construction costs. Despite strong consumer purchasing power and aggressive expansion plans from retailers, particularly in the value sector, developers face challenges due to chronically flat retail rents and compressed profit margins. This dynamic is leading to historically strained retail construction nationwide, with limited new supply available for lease, creating a competitive environment for existing quality space and favoring landlords.
