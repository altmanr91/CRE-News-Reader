Lightstone Capital Provides $29.5M Loan for Orange, N.J., Multifamily Development

LOAN

Commercial Observer — Thu, 29 Oct 2026 20:54:47 +0000

SUMMARY

PEEK Capital, a division of PEEK Properties, has secured a $29.5 million bridge loan from Lightstone Capital for its recently completed 102-unit multifamily project, The Lincoln, in Orange, NJ. The financing will support the building's lease-up and stabilization, as well as refinance its previous construction loan. The property, which received its certificate of occupancy in September, is strategically located near public transit, offering convenient access to Newark and Midtown Manhattan.
