EōS Fitness to Open 40,000 SF Gym in Northwest Houston

LEASE

REBusinessOnline — Mon, 26 Oct 2026 14:17:22 +0000

SUMMARY

EōS Fitness will open a new 40,000-square-foot gym in northwest Houston, located at the intersection of Grand Parkway and State Highway 249. The new facility is expected to open next year, marking a significant expansion for the fitness chain in the area. NewQuest represented EōS Fitness in the lease negotiations.
