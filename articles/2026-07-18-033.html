Align ups unit count, lowers height for controversial Marina Safeway project

The Real Deal — San Francisco — Fri, 17 Jul 2026 23:44:37 +0000

SUMMARY

Development

Align Real Estate has revised its controversial Marina Safeway redevelopment project in San Francisco, reducing the height of its proposed towers while increasing the total number of residential units. The updated plans aim to address feedback from the Planning Department and improve project efficiency, despite continued opposition from local residents and the mayor's office. The project is being reviewed under Assembly Bill 2011, a state law designed to streamline housing approvals, though its eligibility is being contested.
