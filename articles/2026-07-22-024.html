Witkoff, Monroe Capital score $303M loan for huge Miami Worldcenter project

LOAN

The Real Deal — Miami — Tue, 21 Jul 2026 20:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Witkoff and Monroe Capital secured a $302.6 million construction loan for the first phase of their large-scale residential and retail project at Miami Worldcenter. The financing, provided by Athene Annuity and Life Company and J.P. Morgan Chase, includes $245.3 million in new construction funding and refinances $57.3 million in existing debt. The initial phase will feature an 894-unit residential tower, over 17,000 square feet of retail, and a nine-story parking garage, marking a significant step in the development of one of Miami Worldcenter's last major undeveloped parcels.

The project will ultimately include 2,346 residential units, 30,500 square feet of commercial space, and 2,078 parking spaces across four towers. The transaction refinanced $57.3 million in existing debt and provided $245.3 million in new construction financing.
