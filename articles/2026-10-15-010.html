Sierra Ridge Capital Buys 123,032 SF Industrial Building in Phoenix for $31M

ACQUISITION

REBusinessOnline — Thu, 15 Oct 2026 13:35:43 +0000

SUMMARY

Sierra Ridge Capital has acquired an industrial property located at 3137 East Elwood Street in Phoenix for $31 million. The 123,032-square-foot asset, built in 1995 and recently renovated, was fully leased to three tenants at the time of sale. Newmark represented the seller in this transaction.
