Japanese Firm Acquires Upper West Side Apartments for $25M

ACQUISITION

Connect CRE — Mon, 21 Sep 2026 21:56:28 +0000

SUMMARY

Phoenix Co. Ltd., a Japanese firm, has acquired a 36-unit residential elevator building on Manhattan's Upper West Side from Alchemy Ventures for $24.8 million. Alchemy Ventures had previously purchased the property in February 2024 for $11.2 million and conducted a gut renovation, leading to a substantial increase in value. This transaction aligns with Alchemy Ventures' strategy of acquiring, operating, and selectively monetizing residential and mixed-use properties across New York City.
