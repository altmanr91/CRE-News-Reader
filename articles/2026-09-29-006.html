Partnership to Develop 225,000 SF Office Project in Allen, Texas

DEVELOPMENT

REBusinessOnline — Tue, 29 Sep 2026 15:07:58 +0000

SUMMARY

KDC and Pillar Commercial have formed a partnership to develop One Bethany North, a 225,000-square-foot office project in Allen, Texas. The nine-story building will be part of the Watters Creek mixed-use development and will feature various amenities including pickleball courts, putting greens, and a restaurant.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Property Name: One Bethany North

Size: 225,000 SF

Notable Features: Located within the Watters Creek mixed-use development, with amenities including pickleball courts, putting greens, outdoor gathering spaces, and a 3,500-square-foot restaurant.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

DEVELOPER/SPONSOR: KDC

DEVELOPER/SPONSOR: Pillar Commercial
