UK Budget Risks Deterring Sovereign Wealth Funds From Real Estate

Bisnow Latest News Feed — Fri, 30 Oct 2026 19:02:13 -0400

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

The UK government's recent budget changes to tax rules for sovereign wealth funds could significantly deter these major global investors from allocating capital to the country's real estate market. Sovereign wealth funds have historically been a crucial source of foreign direct investment in UK property, and the altered tax landscape may reduce their appetite for future acquisitions, potentially impacting market liquidity and asset valuations.
