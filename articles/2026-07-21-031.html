Austin suburb selects Griffin Swinerton to revamp downtown

The Real Deal — Texas — Mon, 20 Jul 2026 20:30:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Development

The city of Liberty Hill, a rapidly growing Austin suburb, has selected Griffin Swinerton, a joint venture between Griffin Structures Incorporated and Swinerton Incorporated, to lead the revitalization of its downtown area. The joint venture will act as an extension of city staff, responsible for community engagement, evaluating infrastructure needs, and market financing. The project aims to create a walkable central gathering place, potentially including parking upgrades and industry-specific districts, with a target start date of 2028.
