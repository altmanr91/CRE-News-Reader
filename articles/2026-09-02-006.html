Altana Signs 62,309 SF Office Lease at The PENN District in Midtown Manhattan

LEASE

REBusinessOnline — Wed, 02 Sep 2026 13:28:53 +0000

SUMMARY

Altana, a provider of AI-driven trade and supply chain management solutions, has signed a 10-year lease for 62,309 square feet of office space at The PENN District in Midtown Manhattan. The company will establish its new headquarters on the entire 21st floor of PENN 2, a component of Vornado Realty Trust's 4.4 million-square-foot development.
