Housing Notes: Inman tells Compass to pound sand

The Real Deal — National — Fri, 03 Jul 2026 13:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Editorial

This opinion piece praises Inman News for resisting pressure from Compass to remove an op-ed by Bess Freedman, CEO of Brown Harris Stevens. Freedman's article criticized Compass's alleged practice of steering sellers into 90-day private listing periods, arguing that this boosts Compass's internal economics while limiting market exposure and potentially depressing sale prices. The author supports Freedman's claims, suggesting that such practices violate fiduciary and fair housing obligations and reduce transparency. The article highlights that New York's Attorney General has initiated an antitrust investigation into Compass, and proposed state legislation aims to restrict private listings to protect consumers.
