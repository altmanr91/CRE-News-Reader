Taco Bell’s Office Lease Largest in Orange County, Calif., in Five Years

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Fri, 09 Oct 2026 20:00:14 +0000

SUMMARY

Taco Bell signed the largest office lease in Orange County, CA, in five years, securing 254,162 square feet at Irvine Company's Alton HQ. This significant transaction contributed to an improved office market in the third quarter, with overall vacancy decreasing and average asking rents rising. The move reflects a trend of diverse corporate leasing activity in the region, despite a slight dip in net absorption for the quarter.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Property Name: Alton HQ

Size: 254,162 SF

Project Notes: Taco Bell plans to relocate from its current Irvine headquarters in 2028.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

LANDLORD: Irvine Company

TENANT: Taco Bell

TENANTS AT PROPERTY

Taco Bell

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Orange County's office vacancy improved to 15.4% in Q3, down from 17.1% a year ago. Average asking rents increased to $3.02 per square foot per month, up from $2.90 at the end of 2025. Net absorption for the year remained positive at 265,394 square feet. Approximately 1.55 million square feet of leases were signed during the quarter. No new office completions have been recorded this year, with only one project, a 168,137-square-foot building at the Anaheim Ducks' mixed-use development, currently under construction and 23% pre-leased.
