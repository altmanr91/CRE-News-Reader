IPA Negotiates Sale of 306-Unit Apartment Community in Red Oak, Texas

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 31 Jul 2026 14:27:54 +0000

SUMMARY

Institutional Property Advisors (IPA) has facilitated the sale of Emerson at Red Oak, a 306-unit apartment community located in Red Oak, a southern suburb of Dallas. The transaction involved CESM Real Estate as the seller and Professional Equity Management as the buyer. The property, built in 2023, features a mix of one-, two-, and three-bedroom units and a variety of amenities.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Emerson at Red Oak

Address: 351 Hickory Creek Dr, Red Oak, TX*

Units: 306

Year Built: 2023

Notable Features: Amenities include a pool, fitness center, picnic and grilling areas, dog park, game room, and a business center.
