Miami Design District Associates, BH3 Partner to Develop 20-Story Office Building

DEVELOPMENT

REBusinessOnline — Wed, 07 Oct 2026 15:15:25 +0000

SUMMARY

Miami Design District Associates and BH3 Management have formed a joint venture to develop The Ursa, a 20-story Class A office tower in Miami's Design District. The project will feature 250,000 square feet of office space, 15,000 square feet of ground-level retail, and onsite parking. This newly formed partnership has expanded the project's original vision by 40 percent, with construction slated to begin in 2027 and completion expected in 2029.

DATA POINTS
