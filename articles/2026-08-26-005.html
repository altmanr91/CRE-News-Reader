Fifth Third, CB&T TruAmerica Lend $124M on Bay Area Apartments Build

LOAN

Commercial Observer — Tue, 25 Aug 2026 18:31:02 +0000

SUMMARY

SummerHill Apartment Communities has secured $123.5 million in construction financing for a new 251-unit multifamily project in San Carlos, CA. The financing package includes a $96.5 million senior construction loan from Fifth Third Bank and California Bank & Trust, alongside a $27 million preferred equity investment from TruAmerica Multifamily. This development is TruAmerica Multifamily's inaugural preferred equity financing through its recently launched structured finance platform, reflecting the firm's expanded strategy as a residential investment management company. The project will include 38 affordable housing units and is strategically located to benefit from the Bay Area's robust technology and life science sectors, which are driving strong multifamily occupancy and rent growth.
