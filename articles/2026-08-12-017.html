Domain Lines Up $176M Financing for Mixed-Income Astoria Rentals

LOAN

Connect CRE — Tue, 11 Aug 2026 21:39:32 +0000

SUMMARY

The Domain Companies has secured $175.6 million in financing from Wells Fargo for Elara, a new mixed-income residential development in Astoria, Queens. The project will feature 429 apartments across two buildings, with 107 units designated as permanently affordable. Equity partners for the development include Canyon Partners Real Estate and BLDG Management, with JLL Capital Markets brokering the financing. The development aims to provide a high-quality living experience while reflecting the neighborhood's character.
