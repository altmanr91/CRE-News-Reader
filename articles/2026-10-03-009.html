Beyond the Headlines, CRE Is Thousands of Local Stories

Connect CRE — Fri, 02 Oct 2026 22:02:45 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

This article argues that the commercial real estate market is primarily driven by thousands of smaller, local transactions rather than the high-profile, institutional-scale deals that dominate headlines. The author, citing data from CoStar, Altus Group, and the Census Bureau, highlights that the majority of multifamily properties have fewer than 50 units, most retail leases are under 5,000 square feet, and the average commercial transaction is typically under $5 million. This disconnect between media coverage and market reality means that national data often fails to capture the nuances of local markets, where granular intelligence about specific properties, landlords, and zoning changes is crucial for successful deals. The article emphasizes that a distributed network, like REMAX Commercial, is better positioned to identify and execute these smaller, community-sustaining transactions.

DATA POINTS

Multifamily properties with fewer than 50 units: Two-thirds of the market

Retail leases under 5,000 square feet: Approximately 80% of retail leases

Average U.S. office requirement: About 3,500 square feet

Median per-square-foot pricing across major property types (Q1 2026): Reached new highs

Typical commercial transaction price (excluding hospitality): Under $2 million

REMAX Commercial brokers completed transactions (last year): More than 65,000

REMAX Commercial sales and leasing volume (last year): Over $18.5 billion
