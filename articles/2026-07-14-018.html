Shvo gets fraud claims tossed in Mandarin Oriental suit

The Real Deal — New York — Mon, 13 Jul 2026 21:13:57 +0000

SUMMARY

CRE Legal / Regulatory

A New York Supreme Court judge has dismissed fraud and shoddy construction claims brought against developer Michael Shvo and his companies by two condo owners at the Mandarin Oriental Residences. The judge ruled that the developer's omissions regarding a prior dispute with a former partner and marketing statements about "turnkey luxury" were not sufficient grounds for fraud claims. While most claims against Shvo were dismissed, the condo owners can still pursue an effort to unwind their purchase contract.
