PENN Entertainment to Invest $195M to Relocate Riverboat Casino in New Orleans

DEVELOPMENT

REBusinessOnline — Tue, 18 Aug 2026 15:06:02 +0000

SUMMARY

PENN Entertainment plans to invest $195 million to relocate its Boomtown Casino & Hotel New Orleans riverboat casino to an adjacent landside property, pending regulatory approval. The new development will span 140,000 square feet and feature a rebranded Hollywood Casino New Orleans, expected to open in 2029. This project marks PENN's fourth landside relocation of a riverboat casino, which the company intends to finance with cash on hand.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: casino

Property Name: Hollywood Casino New Orleans

Size: 140,000 SF

Total Project Cost: $195.0M

$/SF: $1,393/SF (calculated)

Completion: 2029

Notable Features: Will include approximately 875 slot machines, 45 table games, a retail sportsbook, and multiple dining options.

Project Notes: The existing riverboat casino will be vacated upon the new property's opening, but the existing hotel is expected to remain open. This is the fourth landside relocation of a riverboat casino for the developer.
