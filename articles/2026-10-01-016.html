Vintage South Trades Nashville Mixed-Use Portfolio for $54.3M

ACQUISITION

Connect CRE — Thu, 01 Oct 2026 14:29:17 +0000

SUMMARY

LBX Investments has acquired a 134,113-square-foot mixed-use and retail portfolio in Nashville from Vintage South Development for $54.3 million. The portfolio includes two properties, Breeze Block and Stateline, both of which are adaptive reuse projects that transformed former industrial facilities into vibrant commercial spaces. The properties are located within two miles of each other and feature a diverse tenant mix.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: mixed-use

Property Name: Breeze Block and Stateline Portfolio

Size: 134,113 SF

Sale Price: $54.3M

Notable Features: Breeze Block was converted from an industrial facility in 2023; Stateline is an adaptive reuse of an old warehouse.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

SELLER: Vintage South Development
