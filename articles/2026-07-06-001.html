RREAF Holdings CEO Kip Sowden Drives Southeast Real Estate

Commercial Observer — Mon, 06 Jul 2026 10:30:04 +0000

SUMMARY

Feature / Profile

This article profiles Kip Sowden, CEO of RREAF Holdings, a vertically integrated real estate firm with $4.8 billion in assets under management. Sowden discusses his 40-year career, from real estate investment banking and brokerage to founding RREAF in 2010. The firm operates across five main divisions: multifamily, beachfront hospitality and resorts, master-planned communities, extended-stay hospitality, and RV communities, primarily in the South and Southeastern United States. Sowden emphasizes RREAF's in-house capabilities, including property management, acquisitions, underwriting, due diligence, and construction, which allows them to manage 23,000 units across 90 assets.
