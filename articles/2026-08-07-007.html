Newmark Brokers Sale of 376,259 SF Office Campus in North Dallas

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Thu, 06 Aug 2026 14:53:15 +0000

SUMMARY

Newmark has brokered the sale of Tollway Plaza, a 376,259-square-foot office campus in North Dallas. The campus, consisting of two eight-story buildings, was 91 percent leased at the time of sale with a weighted average remaining lease term of 5.5 years. Newmark also arranged acquisition financing for the buyer, a partnership led by Tourmaline Capital Partners.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Property Name: Tollway Plaza

Address: 16000 Dallas Parkway, Dallas, TX

Site Area: 7.4 acres
