CRE’s ESG Retreat Masks Growing Spending On Climate Risk

Bisnow Latest News Feed — Thu, 06 Aug 2026 18:39:01 -0400

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

The commercial real estate industry is increasingly prioritizing climate risk data and predictive models, even as the broader focus on ESG investing appears to be diminishing. This shift reflects a growing recognition that understanding and mitigating climate-related physical and transition risks is crucial for asset valuation, investment decisions, and long-term portfolio resilience. The demand for sophisticated data tools to assess these risks is rising, indicating a strategic pivot towards tangible risk management rather than solely broad ESG mandates.
