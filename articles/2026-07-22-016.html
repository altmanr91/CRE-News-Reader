Lake Havasu new build sets local price record nearly two years after listing

SALE

The Real Deal — National — Tue, 21 Jul 2026 15:00:02 +0000

SUMMARY

A newly constructed lakeside estate in Lake Havasu City, Arizona, has sold for $4.5 million, establishing a new record for residential property sales in the area. The 6,000-square-foot luxury home, developed by Gaskin Design and Development, was initially listed for $6.7 million in December 2024 before its price reduction. The sale highlights the evolving dynamics of the luxury housing market in the region, even as the broader Phoenix metro area experiences significant home price reductions.
