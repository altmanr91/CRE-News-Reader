Wold Architects & Engineers acquires Minnesota-based architecture firm

REJournals — Thu, 17 Sep 2026 14:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Non-CRE / Business News

Wold Architects & Engineers, a Minneapolis-based firm, has acquired Klein McCarthy Architects, a Minnesota-based architecture firm specializing in government and justice facility design. This acquisition is Wold's third strategic partnership in 2026, reinforcing its government practice by integrating two teams with extensive experience serving public agencies across the Midwest. The move aims to provide Klein McCarthy's clients with enhanced in-house engineering capabilities and expanded technical resources while maintaining existing client relationships and local leadership.
