Berkadia brokers sale of 458-unit multifamily property in Mount Pleasant

SALE

REJournals — Tue, 13 Oct 2026 16:04:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Santefort Real Estate Group, LLC has acquired Tivoli Green, a 458-unit luxury multifamily property in Mount Pleasant, Wisconsin, from Wangard Partners, Inc. Berkadia facilitated both the sale and the acquisition financing through Freddie Mac. Wangard Partners will continue to manage the property, which is one of the largest multifamily assets built in Southeastern Wisconsin in the last 25 years and is strategically located in an area experiencing significant economic expansion and job growth.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Tivoli Green

Address: 9110 Megan’s Way, Mount Pleasant, WI

Units: 458

Site Area: 38 acres

Notable Features: Luxury multifamily property with 100% direct-access units and two-story townhomes, located in an area with significant economic expansion and job growth along the I-94 Corridor.

Project Notes: Delivered in three phases from 2021 to 2024, it was the first apartment community built in Racine County in over 20 years.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: Santefort Real Estate Group, LLC

SELLER: Wangard Partners, Inc.

SELLER BROKER: Berkadia Chicago — Pete Evans, Senior Managing Director

LENDER: Freddie Mac
