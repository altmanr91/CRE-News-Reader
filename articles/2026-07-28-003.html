AI Is Rewiring the Office-Leasing Playbook

Commercial Observer — Mon, 27 Jul 2026 19:56:06 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

This article argues that the future of office leasing will be shaped by where AI companies choose to invest and build, rather than by general return-to-office policies. The author, a real estate software executive, highlights a significant concentration of AI office demand in specific submarkets of San Francisco, San Jose, and New York City, which collectively account for nearly three-quarters of current AI square footage. This growth is characterized by financially sound companies scaling revenue faster than headcount, focusing on senior problem-solving talent. While initial demand is concentrated, the article predicts a second wave of AI-driven office demand emerging in markets like Seattle, Austin, Northern Virginia, Atlanta, and Chicago, as large firms like Blackstone and Brookfield integrate AI into their diverse portfolio companies. The author advises CRE executives to use forward-looking indicators of AI demand to inform capital allocation and asset positioning, emphasizing the importance of internal AI capability and data infrastructure for long-term advantage.
