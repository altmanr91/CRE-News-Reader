Decron Properties Acquires Apartment Property in Los Angeles for $114M

ACQUISITION

REBusinessOnline — Wed, 23 Sep 2026 13:50:55 +0000

SUMMARY

Decron Properties has acquired 5550 Wilshire, a 163-unit apartment community in Los Angeles' Miracle Mile area, from private equity real estate investor GID for $114 million. The property, developed in 2010, also includes 14,686 square feet of fully leased ground-floor retail space. This acquisition expands Decron's significant multifamily and retail portfolio across the Western U.S.
