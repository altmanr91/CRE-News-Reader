Oak Brook home lists for record price shy of $7M

The Real Deal — Chicago — Wed, 01 Jul 2026 17:45:02 +0000

SUMMARY

Property Listing / For Sale

A luxury home in Oak Brook, IL, has been listed for nearly $7 million, setting a new record for the western Chicago suburb's residential listings. The property, located in the Ginger Creek community, spans 12,221 square feet on almost an acre of land. The listing price significantly exceeds the village's recent record sale of $5.3 million in October, highlighting the ambitious pricing in an unpredictable luxury housing market.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

The Oak Brook luxury home market has seen few successful sales above $5 million since 2003, with a recent $6.7 million listing from December remaining unsold. The highest recorded home sale in Oak Brook was $7.95 million in 2003. Recent luxury sales include a 10,000-square-foot mansion for $5.3 million in October, an 11,000-square-foot mansion for $4 million in December 2024, and a 12,000-square-foot property for $4.8 million in 2023.
