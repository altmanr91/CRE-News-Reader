Knighthead Lends $63M or Doral, Fla., Industrial Portfolio Buy

LOAN

Commercial Observer — Thu, 17 Sep 2026 18:59:07 +0000

SUMMARY

Midtown Capital Partners secured $62.92 million in acquisition financing from Knighthead Funding for its $86 million purchase of a 13-property urban-infill industrial portfolio in Doral, FL. The portfolio, totaling 290,000 square feet across 13.33 acres, was acquired off-market from Wes Realty. The transaction highlights the strong demand and institutional interest in the small-bay industrial sector in South Florida, where vacancy rates are exceptionally low.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Size: 290,000 SF

Loan Amount: $62.9M

Total Project Cost: $86.0M

Site Area: 13.33 acres

Notable Features: Urban-infill, small-bay industrial portfolio located within a mile of Miami International Airport.

Project Notes: The 13 properties were developed and assembled over the last 30 years.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

MORTGAGE BROKER: Arrow Real Estate Advisors — Morris Betesh, Founder and Managing Partner; Morris Dabbah; Zach Strauss

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

South Florida's small-bay industrial market is experiencing strong demand with vacancy rates below 3 percent.
