Ideal Marketplace Renews 15K-SF Grocery Store at Chelsea’s 317 Ninth Avenue

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Wed, 08 Jul 2026 19:45:47 +0000

SUMMARY

Ideal Marketplace has renewed its 14,770-square-foot grocery store lease at 317 Ninth Avenue in Chelsea, Manhattan. The property, also known as Morgan South, is owned by the United States Postal Service (USPS), which operates a connected facility at 341 Ninth Avenue. The terms of the renewed lease were not disclosed, though asking rents in prime retail submarkets across the city have reportedly increased.
