The 'Zombie' Building Mirage: How Owners Who Can't Transact Are Stirring The Market

Bisnow Latest News Feed — Mon, 26 Oct 2026 20:11:51 -0400

SUMMARY

Market Research / Office Report

A new report by CBRE highlights a significant issue in the Washington D.C. office market, identifying 30 large blocks of office space, totaling 5.5 million square feet, where owners are currently unable to secure new leases. This phenomenon, dubbed 'zombie space,' indicates a substantial portion of the market that is effectively frozen, impacting overall transaction activity and market dynamics.

DATA POINTS

Number of large office blocks affected: 30
