Canyon Creek Real Estate acquires One Eleven Congress in downtown Austin

ACQUISITION

REJournals — Mon, 10 Aug 2026 10:03:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Canyon Creek Real Estate has acquired One Eleven Congress, a prominent 533,711-square-foot office tower in Downtown Austin, marking the firm's strategic entry into the Austin market. Stream Realty Partners will continue to handle leasing services for the 30-story building and will expand its role to include property management. The acquisition underscores the continued investment and momentum in the surrounding area, which benefits from its prime location between Austin's Central Business District and the waterfront.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Property Name: One Eleven Congress

Address: 111 Congress Ave., Austin, TX

Size: 533,711 SF

Notable Features: 30-story office tower with panoramic views of Downtown Austin, Lady Bird Lake, and the Texas Hill Country; features a food hall, conference center, fitness center, and 24-hour security.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: Canyon Creek Real Estate

LEASING AGENT: Stream Realty Partners — Rachel Coulter, Managing Director
