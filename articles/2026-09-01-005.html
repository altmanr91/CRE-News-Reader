Trepp’s Stephen Buschbom and Andy Boettcher On a Very Busy 2026 for CMBS

Commercial Observer — Tue, 01 Sep 2026 13:00:10 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

The commercial mortgage-backed securities (CMBS) market is poised to surpass its initial issuance projections for 2026, driven primarily by single-asset, single-borrower (SASB) transactions. Trepp analysts Stephen Buschbom and Andy Boettcher note that while overall CMBS volume is strong, the market is experiencing a significant shift in composition, with SASB deals accounting for a dominant share compared to pre-COVID levels. This trend is attributed to buyer preferences for more targeted portfolio management and banks' increased comfort in managing specific credit risks. Despite rising distress, particularly in the office sector, the market is showing signs of stabilization, with banks having largely reserved for their commercial real estate exposure and demonstrating healthy lending growth, which in turn supports liquidity for CMBS maturities and rollovers.
