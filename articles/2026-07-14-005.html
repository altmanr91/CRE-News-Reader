Fashion Platform Orly Inks 20K-SF Office Lease at 20 West 33rd Street

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Mon, 13 Jul 2026 20:09:49 +0000

SUMMARY

Fashion platform Orly has signed a 10-year office lease for 20,014 square feet at 20 West 33rd Street in Midtown South. The company, which manages over 20 brands, is relocating from a nearby address to accommodate its growth and expects to occupy the new space early next year. The landlord, JLA Home, secured an asking rent of $55 per square foot for the transaction.
