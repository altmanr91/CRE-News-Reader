Urban Edge Pays $90.3M for 140K-SF Boca Raton Retail Center

ACQUISITION

Connect CRE — Thu, 29 Oct 2026 14:19:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Urban Edge Properties has acquired Regency Court, a 140,000-square-foot grocery-anchored shopping center in Boca Raton, Florida, from Invesco for $90.3 million. The acquisition was underwritten with a focus on future rent growth, as the anchor tenants currently pay below-market rents with leases expiring in six years. Urban Edge anticipates an 8% to 9% unlevered internal rate of return from the asset, which benefits from its prime location with no competing grocery-anchored centers nearby and high annual visitor traffic.
