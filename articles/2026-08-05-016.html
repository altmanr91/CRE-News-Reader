Co-Living Complex Near UCLA Will Become Student Housing

SALE

Connect CRE — Tue, 04 Aug 2026 21:45:48 +0000

SUMMARY

A seven-story co-living complex in West Los Angeles, near UCLA, has been sold for $8.5 million to UCLA Housing Group, which plans to convert it into student housing. The property, built in 2023, features 16 residential units and 48 beds across 18,000 square feet. The transaction, completed through a court-appointed receivership, reflects a broader market trend in Los Angeles where investors are acquiring existing assets below replacement cost due to muted rent growth and increasing development challenges.

SELLER BROKER: Colliers — Kitty Wallace, Vice chair

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

The Los Angeles multifamily market has experienced muted rent growth post-pandemic, while household incomes, replacement costs, and home prices have increased. New development has become more challenging, leading investors to focus on acquiring well-located existing assets below replacement cost.
