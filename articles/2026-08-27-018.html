Bourn Cos. Acquires Oro Valley’s Innovation Park for $21.2M

ACQUISITION

Connect CRE — Thu, 27 Aug 2026 17:26:10 +0000

SUMMARY

Bourn Cos. has acquired Innovation Park, a 333-acre master-planned business park in Oro Valley, from Venture West Group for $21.2 million. The transaction involved approximately 30 parcels within the business park, which is home to a strong tenant mix. Bourn Cos. plans to transform the property into a mixed-use business park, aiming to attract major employers and provide modern amenities for the workforce.

DATA POINTS
