For Mall Giant Macerich, More Turnaround in Q3

Commercial Observer — Thu, 05 Nov 2026 13:40:26 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Earnings Report

Mall owner Macerich reported improved financial performance in the third quarter of 2026, with net operating income and revenue increasing year-over-year, signaling progress in its "Path Forward Plan." The REIT's net loss significantly narrowed compared to the previous year, and funds from operations also saw a notable rise. Leasing activity was robust, with 1.2 million square feet of leases secured and a 1.5% increase in portfolio occupancy, reaching 94.9%. The company continues to execute its disposition strategy, including the recent sale of Pacific View Mall, and anticipates a new mall acquisition by year-end.
