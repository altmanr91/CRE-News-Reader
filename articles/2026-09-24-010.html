First Washington Realty acquires grocery-anchored shopping centers in Minnesota, Missouri

ACQUISITION

REJournals — Thu, 24 Sep 2026 14:06:00 +0000

SUMMARY

First Washington Realty has expanded its portfolio with the acquisition of two grocery-anchored shopping centers, The Village of Blaine in Minnesota and Liberty Corners in Missouri, for a combined price exceeding $65 million. These acquisitions align with the buyer's strategy of investing in well-located neighborhood shopping centers that serve as essential community hubs for daily needs. The properties are expected to benefit from strong local demographics and continued investment in their respective areas.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: retail

Sale Price: $65.0M

Project Notes: The combined value of the two properties is in excess of $65 million.

BUYER: First Washington Realty
