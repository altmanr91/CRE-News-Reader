The Problem With Fake Buyers

Commercial Observer — Thu, 17 Sep 2026 11:00:12 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Editorial

This opinion piece by a residential real estate agent highlights the persistent challenge of 'fake buyers' in the New York City market. The author shares anecdotes of time-wasting inquiries and elaborate hoaxes, emphasizing the professional obligation to treat all leads seriously due to fair housing laws and brokerage policies. Despite the frustrations, the author recounts a rare instance where a seemingly fake buyer, initially using a pseudonym, turned out to be a high-profile public figure who became a loyal client, underscoring the unpredictable nature of the business and the importance of trusting one's instincts while remaining open to possibilities.
