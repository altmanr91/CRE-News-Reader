The Goldie Initiative celebrates 2026-2027 Goldie Scholars

REJournals — Wed, 23 Sep 2026 09:39:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Association / Leadership News

The Goldie Initiative, an organization dedicated to advancing women in commercial real estate, celebrated its 2026-2027 Goldie Scholars at its annual Goldie Gala in Chicago. The event, attended by over 1,100 people, contributed to the more than $1.6 million raised this year to support scholarships, mentorship, and professional development for women pursuing graduate-level real estate programs. The program saw a 50% increase in applications this year, expanding its reach to a record number of universities nationwide, including several new participating institutions. The organization also honored Lisa Konieczka of CBRE Chicago with its Shero Award for her significant contributions to supporting and advancing women in the industry, notably through establishing the CBRE Women's Network.
