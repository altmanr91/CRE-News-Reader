Proposition 45 Would Set Deadlines For Some Projects' CEQA Reviews

Bisnow Latest News Feed — Fri, 16 Oct 2026 18:16:42 -0400

SUMMARY

Infrastructure Investment / Government Policy

Proposition 45, a ballot initiative in California, aims to establish specific deadlines for California Environmental Quality Act (CEQA) reviews for certain essential projects. The proposition seeks to streamline the approval process, thereby reducing associated costs and project delays. This measure is intended to accelerate the development of critical infrastructure and other significant projects within the state.
