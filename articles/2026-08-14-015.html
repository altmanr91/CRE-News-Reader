JLL Capital Markets closes sale of 138,266-square-foot retail center in Plano

SALE

REJournals — Thu, 13 Aug 2026 16:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

JLL Capital Markets has facilitated the sale of Legacy Drive Village, a 138,266-square-foot grocery-anchored retail center in Plano, Texas. An institutional investor acquired an equity stake in the property from a joint venture managed by Weitzman. The center, built in 1994, is fully occupied and anchored by a high-performing grocer, boasting a strong tenant mix and exceptional tenant stability with an average occupancy of 15 years.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

The surrounding trade area has an average household income of $171,251 within three miles and has experienced 21.5% population growth within five miles since 2010. The anchor grocer ranks in the top 14% of most-visited grocery stores in Texas, attracting 848,000 visits annually.
