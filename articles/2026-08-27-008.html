Merritt Properties Breaks Ground on 150-Acre Industrial Park Near Raleigh-Durham Airport

CONSTRUCTION

REBusinessOnline — Thu, 27 Aug 2026 14:48:18 +0000

SUMMARY

Merritt Properties has commenced construction on the initial phase of RDU Business Park, a substantial 150-acre light industrial development in Morrisville, NC, strategically located near Raleigh-Durham International Airport. The first phase, encompassing 31 acres, will deliver three single-story buildings totaling 201,250 square feet by late 2027. Upon full build-out, the park is slated to feature 13 buildings across 790,527 square feet, offering modern industrial specifications designed to attract a diverse tenant base.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Property Name: RDU Business Park

Address: 2040-2060 Seagirt Ave, Morrisville, NC

Size: 201,250 SF

Completion: late 2027

Phase I: 31 acres, three buildings, 201,250 square feet

Total Project (All Phases): 13 single-story buildings spanning 790,527 square feet

Site Area: 150 acres

Notable Features: Buildings will include 18- or 24-foot clear heights, rear-loading docks, drive-in capabilities, 30- and 40-foot column spacing, and surface parking. Two buildings will share a 150-foot truck court, while the third will feature a dedicated 110-foot truck court.

Project Notes: The project is situated within the jurisdiction of the city of Cary, NC.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

DEVELOPER/SPONSOR: Merritt Properties
