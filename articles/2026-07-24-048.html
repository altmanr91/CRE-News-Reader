Former Arms Dealer Launches Takeover Of Heartland Apartment Portfolio

Bisnow News Feed — Thu, 23 Jul 2026 14:29:55 -0400

SUMMARY

CRE Legal / Regulatory

A legal battle has erupted over Vesta Capital, a multifamily firm, involving its founder Marc Kulick, his former business partners, and lender Efraim Diveroli. Kulick defaulted on high-interest loans from Diveroli's YSA Investments, leading to Diveroli's team taking over management of 10 properties in Tulsa, Oklahoma, and initiating foreclosure proceedings on at least nine properties. Kulick's former partners allege he committed fraud to fund a lavish lifestyle, while Kulick claims he is a victim of a hostile takeover and is fighting to regain control of over 30 assets. Fannie Mae has also initiated foreclosure proceedings on eight properties within the portfolio, and several holding companies under Vesta's structure have filed for bankruptcy.
