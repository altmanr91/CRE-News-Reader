More CIM-owned offices in Bay Area headed for foreclosure after loan default, receivership

FORECLOSURE

The Real Deal — San Francisco — Tue, 21 Jul 2026 23:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Lenders are initiating foreclosure proceedings on CIM Group's Central Tower in San Francisco, following a default on a $98 million loan. The historic two-building office property, located at 703 Market Street and 30 Third Street, was placed into receivership in May after its largest tenant vacated, leading to a significant drop in occupancy. This situation highlights the ongoing challenges in the Bay Area office market, with lenders now exploring options to take ownership or sell the asset to recover the outstanding debt.
