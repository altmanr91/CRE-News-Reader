Partnership Underway on 274-Unit Multifamily Project in Horsham, Pennsylvania

CONSTRUCTION

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 17 Jul 2026 14:19:05 +0000

SUMMARY

A partnership has commenced construction on AVE Horsham, a 274-unit multifamily project in Horsham, Pennsylvania. The development will feature studio, one-, and two-bedroom units along with a variety of amenities, with completion anticipated in early 2028.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: AVE Horsham

Address: 215 Cedar Ave, Horsham, PA*

Units: 274

Completion: early 2028

Notable Features: Amenities will include a pool, outdoor grilling and dining stations, coworking space, meeting rooms, game room, and a golf simulator.

* Address sourced separately, not in original article.
