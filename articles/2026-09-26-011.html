Opus beings construction of 314,684-square-foot spec industrial building in Joliet

CONSTRUCTION

REJournals — Fri, 25 Sep 2026 17:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Opus has commenced construction on Opus Intermodal at Joliet, a 314,684-square-foot speculative industrial building in Joliet, Illinois. The project is strategically located across from Union Pacific's Global IV intermodal terminal, capitalizing on Joliet's robust infrastructure, including major rail facilities and highway access. This development aims to meet the needs of industrial users seeking Class A quality and immediate access to extensive logistics infrastructure.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Property Name: Opus Intermodal at Joliet

Address: 891 E Laraway Rd, Joliet, IL*

Size: 314,684 SF

Site Area: 39 acres

Notable Features: 36-foot clear rear-load facility with 31 dock doors, two drive-in doors, 209 trailer stalls, parking for 214 cars, and clerestory windows. Located directly across from Union Pacific’s Global IV intermodal terminal.

Project Notes: The building can accommodate up to two users.

* Address sourced separately, not in original article.
