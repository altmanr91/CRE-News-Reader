Global Wellness Network Havas Health Expands to 254K SF at 200 Madison Avenue

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Thu, 27 Aug 2026 19:35:23 +0000

SUMMARY

Havas Health, a global health and wellness communications network, has expanded its Midtown headquarters at 200 Madison Avenue in a 15-year lease deal. The company renewed its existing 189,461 square feet and added an additional 64,657 square feet, bringing its total footprint to 254,118 square feet across five floors. The landlord noted that Havas Health's long-term commitment reflects the appeal of the building's hospitality-focused and amenity-rich environment.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Property Name: 200 Madison Avenue

Address: 200 Madison Avenue, New York City, NY

Size: 254,118 SF

Rental Rate: $70.00/SF/yr

Notable Features: 26-story office building located seven blocks south of Grand Central Terminal. Building features a recently renovated entrance and lobby, and a new 11,000-square-foot indoor-outdoor amenity center on the 10th floor with conference rooms, a game area, lounge, event spaces, and an outdoor garden.

Project Notes: Havas has been a tenant at the building since the late 1990s. The lease extends through at least 2041.
