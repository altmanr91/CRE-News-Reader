JLL Arranges $333.3M Refinancing for 1,184-Unit Multifamily Portfolio Near Denver

REFINANCE

REBusinessOnline — Tue, 27 Oct 2026 14:47:32 +0000

SUMMARY

JLL Capital Markets has arranged $333.3 million in bridge financing for Palomino Park, a 1,184-unit multifamily portfolio in Highlands Ranch, an unincorporated master-planned community south of Denver. Greystar provided the floating-rate loan to a real estate fund advised by Crow Holdings Capital. The financing will retire existing loans and fund ongoing value-add renovations across the three communities within the portfolio, building on significant capital improvements already made since the borrower acquired the assets in 2021.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Palomino Park

Address: 963 S Columbine St, Denver, CO*

Units: 1,184

Loan Amount: $333.3M

Notable Features: The communities are anchored by the Colorado Club, a 26,000-square-foot central amenity facility with a resort-style pool, gym, sports courts, and a full-service bar, surrounded by a 17-acre park.

Project Notes: The financing includes $325 million at initial funding and $8.3 million in future funding for unit renovations. The borrower has invested significant capital in interior and exterior improvements since acquiring the assets in 2021.
