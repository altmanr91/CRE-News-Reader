CIP Real Estate Buys Broward Campus for $99M

ACQUISITION

Commercial Observer — Mon, 03 Aug 2026 18:25:52 +0000

SUMMARY

CIP Real Estate has acquired Lyons Business Park, a 338,000-square-foot industrial and office campus in Coconut Creek, FL, for $99 million. The transaction was financed with a $66.9 million acquisition loan from BMO Bank. This purchase represents CIP Real Estate's second acquisition in South Florida within the last year, aligning with its strategy to expand its industrial footprint in the Southeast.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Property Name: Lyons Business Park

Address: 6601 Lyons Road, Coconut Creek, FL

Size: 338,000 SF

Sale Price: $99.0M

Loan Amount: $66.9M

Site Area: 26 acres

Notable Features: Campus includes approximately 10 buildings, located about a mile west of Florida's Turnpike.

Project Notes: Sellers owned the campus since the 1990s and early 2000s. The acquisition loan matures in 2029.
