LOCAL on Delmar Mixed-Use Community Opens in Suburban St. Louis

DEVELOPMENT

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 09 Oct 2026 15:54:46 +0000

SUMMARY

Subtext, in partnership with FrontRange Capital Partners and Larson Capital Management, has opened LOCAL on Delmar, a new mixed-use residential community in University City, Missouri. The development features 259 apartments and retail space, transforming a previously underutilized site near Washington University in St. Louis. The project offers a range of resident amenities and collaborative workspaces, with retail tenants including Commerce Bank and Misfit Society Coffee Club.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: mixed-use

Property Name: LOCAL on Delmar

Size: 398,225 SF

Units: 259

Notable Features: Located at the western edge of the Delmar Loop entertainment district and less than half a mile from Washington University in St. Louis, with a rooftop pool, wellness suite, and dedicated workspaces.
