Court rejects REBNY’s FARE Act appeal

The Real Deal — New York — Tue, 14 Jul 2026 17:36:00 +0000

SUMMARY

CRE Legal / Regulatory

A federal appeals court has rejected the Real Estate Board of New York's (REBNY) appeal to revive key claims against the city's Fairness in Apartment Rental Expenses (FARE) Act. The U.S. Court of Appeals for the Second Circuit affirmed a lower court's dismissal of REBNY's claims that the FARE Act violates the First Amendment and is preempted by state law. The court also upheld the denial of REBNY's request to prevent the law from taking effect during the lawsuit. REBNY expressed disappointment and plans to explore further legal challenges against the law, which shifts the responsibility for paying broker fees from tenants to landlords.
