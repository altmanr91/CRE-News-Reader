Summit RE brokers sale of retail center in Lewisville

SALE

REJournals — Thu, 27 Aug 2026 15:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Summit RE has brokered the sale of Sojourn Retail, a 7,871-square-foot retail condominium interest in Lewisville, TX. The property, located on Windhaven Parkway, was sold by a developer to a local Dallas-Fort Worth buyer. The transaction highlights the continued demand for retail assets in areas with strong demographics and traffic counts.

The property is located in an area with over 247,469 people within a 5-mile radius and an average household income exceeding $131,808. Windhaven Pkwy sees over 13,332 vehicles per day.
