Kiser Group negotiates sale of 11-unit mixed-use property in Chicago’s Wicker Park neighborhood

SALE

REJournals — Tue, 14 Jul 2026 14:43:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Kiser Group recently brokered the sale of an 11-unit mixed-use property in Chicago's Wicker Park neighborhood for $3.88 million. The fully occupied asset, comprising three commercial spaces and eight residential apartments, underwent a comprehensive gut renovation in 2020, featuring updated building systems and high-end finishes. Its prime location near the Milwaukee and Damen corridor, coupled with its stabilized occupancy and recent upgrades, attracted substantial investor interest.
