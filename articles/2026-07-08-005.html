Bravo Property Trust Lends $68M on New Jersey Waterfront Apartments

LOAN

Commercial Observer — Tue, 07 Jul 2026 18:25:19 +0000

SUMMARY

Skyrock Capital secured $68 million in construction financing from Bravo Property Trust for its 180-unit luxury waterfront multifamily project in Bayonne, NJ. The development, known as Bayonne Luxury Waterwalk, is situated on a 2.3-acre site along Newark Bay. A Bravo Property Trust executive noted that Bayonne's waterfront is drawing significant long-term investment, and the lender was pleased to provide the necessary financing certainty for the sponsor to proceed with construction.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Address: 219 West Fifth Street, Bayonne, NJ

Units: 180

Site Area: 2.3 acres

Notable Features: Luxury waterfront property along Newark Bay with a swimming pool and fitness center.

Project Notes: The 2.3-acre site was previously acquired for $4.55 million in 2019. Two floors of the six-story building will be used for parking.
