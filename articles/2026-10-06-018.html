Woodfield Acquires West Palm Beach Condos at Auction

ACQUISITION

Connect CRE — Tue, 06 Oct 2026 14:02:27 +0000

SUMMARY

Woodfield Development acquired an 84-unit, 13-building condominium community in West Palm Beach, FL, for $13.5 million through an auction. The property, located at 2800 Georgia Avenue, includes a 0.32-acre clubhouse and pool parcel. The sale was notable as the development had accumulated over $1.5 million in unpaid water bills, and the site offers significant redevelopment potential with existing MF-20 zoning and potential for increased density under affordable housing overlays.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Address: 2800 Georgia Avenue, West Palm Beach, FL

Units: 84

Site Area: 0.32 acres

Notable Features: Existing MF-20 zoning permits up to 20 dwelling units per acre, with potential for up to 40 dwelling units per acre under the city's Affordable and Workforce Housing Overlay.

Project Notes: The development was over $1.5 million behind in paying its water bills.
