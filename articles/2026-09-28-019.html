NMHC Survey Highlights Challenging Environment for Apartment Development

Connect CRE — Mon, 28 Sep 2026 14:49:13 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Development Survey

The National Multifamily Housing Council's (NMHC) Q3 2026 survey indicates a challenging landscape for apartment development, primarily due to escalating construction costs, economic uncertainty, and subdued rent growth. A significant portion of developers reported initiating fewer projects, citing financial infeasibility and economic concerns as key deterrents. Despite these headwinds, a notable segment of respondents maintains an optimistic outlook for overall construction conditions over the next 6-12 months, with expectations for improved debt and equity financing conditions.

DATA POINTS

Respondents starting fewer projects (Q3 2026): 29%

Respondents starting fewer projects (Q2 2026): 20%

Respondents starting more projects (Q3 2026): 24%

Fewer projects due to economic uncertainty/financial feasibility: 65%

Fewer projects due to low rent growth: 59%

Respondents expecting improved construction conditions (6-12 months, Q3 2026): 50%

Respondents expecting improved construction conditions (6-12 months, Q2 2026): 46%
