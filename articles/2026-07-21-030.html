Why an East Lake Shore Drive condo sold for $5M less than its 2015 price

SALE

The Real Deal — Chicago — Mon, 20 Jul 2026 16:45:00 +0000

SUMMARY

A trust benefiting Barbara Bluhm-Kaul sold a five-bedroom condo at 189 East Lake Shore Drive in Chicago for $5.2 million, representing a significant loss from its 2015 purchase price of $10.37 million. The 2015 acquisition was primarily to secure roof rights for an adjacent unit, which were not included in the recent sale. The price reduction is attributed to the exclusion of roof rights and garage spaces, as well as the premium paid in the original 2015 transaction.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: condo

Address: 189 East Lake Shore Drive, Chicago, IL

Sale Price: $5.2M

Notable Features: Five-bedroom condo. Sold without roof rights and a couple of garage spaces.

Project Notes: The property was originally purchased in 2015 for $10.37 million. It was also sold in 2006 for $8 million.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

SELLER: Trust benefiting Barbara Bluhm-Kaul

BUYER BROKER: Re/Max — Mera Kordic

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

The $5.2 million sale is the second-highest condo purchase in the East Lake Shore Drive area in the current decade. Last November, a condo in the area sold for $7.5 million.
