Sky Equity Goes Vertical on Tribeca Condos

CONSTRUCTION

Connect CRE — Tue, 28 Jul 2026 21:43:26 +0000

SUMMARY

Sky Equity Group has commenced vertical construction on 65 Franklin St., a 24-story luxury condominium tower in Manhattan's Tribeca neighborhood. The project, which broke ground in April, is expected to launch sales next year. The developer, Sky Equity Group, secured a $320 million construction loan in 2025 to facilitate the ground-up development, which is designed to complement Tribeca's historic architecture.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: condo

Property Name: 65 Franklin St.

Address: 65 Franklin St., New York City, NY

Notable Features: 24-story luxury condominium tower designed to complement Tribeca's distinctive architecture.

Project Notes: Construction loan of $320 million secured in 2025. Sky Equity Group acquired the neighboring site at 59 Franklin St. and air rights from 361 Broadway. Sky Equity became the sole developer after a restructuring and recapitalization with HAP Investments in 2025.
