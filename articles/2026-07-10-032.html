Adult entertainment mogul buys building once at center of FBI probe into city corruption

ACQUISITION

The Real Deal — San Francisco — Thu, 09 Jul 2026 20:36:44 +0000

SUMMARY

An entity tied to Peter Acworth, founder of Kink.com, has acquired the 95,000-square-foot office and industrial property at 1717 Mission Street in San Francisco for $14.9 million. This transaction marks Acworth's return to a familiar area of Mission Street and concludes the property's history with disgraced permit expediter Walter Wong, whose company previously headquartered there and was subject to an FBI corruption probe. The building traded at a significant discount from its initial 2020 asking price of $45 million, reflecting the broader post-pandemic commercial market adjustments in San Francisco.
