Investment Firm Garnett Station Partners Expands to 35K SF at 450 Park Avenue

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Thu, 15 Oct 2026 21:23:15 +0000

SUMMARY

Garnett Station Partners, a private equity investment firm, has expanded its office space at 450 Park Avenue in Midtown Manhattan, signing an 11.5-year lease for an additional 10,392 square feet. This expansion brings their total footprint in the building to 35,355 square feet. The firm has been a tenant in the SL Green Realty-owned building since 2023.

DATA POINTS

Property Name: 450 Park Avenue

Address: 450 Park Avenue, New York City, NY

Size: 10,392 SF

Rental Rate: $140.00/SF/yr

Project Notes: Garnett Station Partners previously occupied 24,963 square feet in the building. SL Green Realty acquired the building in 2022 for $445 million.
