Roselle Park Apartments Trade After Brokers Resolve Permit Issue

SALE

Connect CRE — Wed, 22 Jul 2026 21:30:48 +0000

SUMMARY

SAGE Investment Real Estate Advisors arranged the sale of Roselle Grande, a 119-unit garden-style apartment community in Roselle Park, NJ. The transaction, which involved an undisclosed price, was notable for the brokerage team's efforts in resolving a permit issue during the due diligence phase, demonstrating a proactive approach to deal-making in the current market. The property, built in 1970, features a strong tenant mix and historically high occupancy.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Roselle Grande

Address: 220-250 W. Sumner Ave., Roselle Park, NJ

Units: 119

Year Built: 1970

Notable Features: Garden-style apartment community with on-site parking and dedicated laundry facilities.

Project Notes: Brokers resolved an unresolved permit issue during the seller's deliverables phase.
