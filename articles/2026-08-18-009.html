Base Power Signs 32,375 SF Industrial Lease in Frisco

LEASE

REBusinessOnline — Mon, 17 Aug 2026 14:44:37 +0000

SUMMARY

Base Power Inc., a home battery provider, has signed a 32,375-square-foot industrial lease for space within Building 3 at Frisco Trade Center. Dalfen Industrial, the locally based landlord, was represented by Lee & Associates in the transaction.
