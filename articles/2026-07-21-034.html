Japanese semiconductor manufacturer triples Austin office presence

LEASE

The Real Deal — Texas — Mon, 20 Jul 2026 17:30:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Renesas Electronics Corporation, a Japanese semiconductor manufacturer, has significantly expanded its Austin presence by signing a 95,000-square-foot lease at 3700 North Capital of Texas Highway. This new lease nearly triples their previous footprint in the city, reflecting Austin's growing importance as a hub for tech hardware manufacturing, driven by the demand from AI companies and their infrastructure needs. The company cited Austin's proximity to its customer base as a key factor in this expansion.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Address: 3700 North Capital of Texas Highway, Austin, TX

Size: 95,000 SF

Project Notes: This lease represents a nearly threefold increase from the company's previous 34,203-square-foot lease at 900 South Capital of Texas Highway.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

TENANT: Renesas Electronics Corporation — Shea Petricek, Vice President of Product Marketing, Power Systems Engineering and Software

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Austin's 'Silicon Hills' reputation is experiencing a resurgence due to the boom in AI companies and their infrastructure requirements. Data center construction across Texas is projected to make the state the leader in active centers by 2030. However, there is growing local and state-level opposition to unregulated data center development, with Governor Greg Abbott calling for a ban in rural communities and advocating for regulation and rescinding tax breaks.
