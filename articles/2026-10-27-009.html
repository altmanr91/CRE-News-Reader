April Housing Underway on $19.3M Renovation of McKinney Affordable Housing Complex

CONSTRUCTION

REBusinessOnline — Tue, 27 Oct 2026 14:45:42 +0000

SUMMARY

April Housing, the affordable housing division of Blackstone Real Estate, has commenced a $19.3 million renovation of Skyway Villas, a 232-unit complex in McKinney, Texas. This extensive upgrade is part of a re-syndication effort designed to extend the property's affordability status for an additional 30 years. The project will introduce modern interior finishes, exterior improvements, and significant enhancements to building systems and residential amenities.
