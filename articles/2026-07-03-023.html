Blake Investment wins bid for $8B Tropicana Field redevelopment

DEVELOPMENT

The Real Deal — Miami — Thu, 02 Jul 2026 22:44:43 +0000

SUMMARY

A team led by Blake Investment Partners has been selected by St. Petersburg Mayor Ken Welch to redevelop the 86-acre Historic Gas Plant District, which includes Tropicana Field. The winning proposal, "The Burg Bid," involves purchasing 58 acres for $275 million and investing $8.1 billion into the project. The ambitious plan includes 3,600 income-restricted housing units, new jobs, parks, museums, and a new home for the Woodson African American Museum of Florida, with Tropicana Field slated for demolition and replacement. The project is expected to span two decades and will involve a panel of local organizations for input.
