mHub eyes more than $1B industrial expansion in Kinzie Corridor

The Real Deal — Chicago — Wed, 22 Jul 2026 16:33:12 +0000

SUMMARY

Development

The nonprofit advanced manufacturing incubator mHub is planning a significant industrial expansion in Chicago's Kinzie Corridor, aiming to develop a 72-acre industrial ecosystem called M+. The project, which could exceed $1 billion in total investment, will create a campus for startups, manufacturers, and researchers. The first phase is estimated at $250 million, with mHub seeking approximately $50 million in public funding from the city to support its development.
