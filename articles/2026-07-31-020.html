Law Firm Relocates Within GFP’s 520 Eighth Ave.

LEASE

Connect CRE — Thu, 30 Jul 2026 21:55:25 +0000

SUMMARY

Kelman Winston & Vallone PC, a New York-based law firm, has signed a five-year, four-month lease for 2,695 square feet at 520 Eighth Ave. The firm will relocate to newly improved office space on the 14th floor within the same building, allowing them to upgrade their premises while maintaining their central location. The building continues to attract a diverse mix of professional firms due to its efficient floor plates and ownership's commitment to tenant experience.
