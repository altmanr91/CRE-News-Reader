CBRE provides $380 million loan for refinance of multifamily portfolio in Texas, Colorado and Virginia

REFINANCE

REJournals — Fri, 10 Jul 2026 14:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

CBRE has secured a $380.2 million refinancing loan for a five-property, Class A multifamily portfolio totaling 1,549 units across Texas, Colorado, and Virginia. The financing was provided by AllianceBernstein on behalf of Crow Holdings. The portfolio consists of institutional-quality communities developed by Trammell Crow Residential in partnership with Crow Holdings, located in desirable infill submarkets near major employment and lifestyle centers.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Units: 1,549

Loan Amount: $380.2M

Notable Features: Class A multifamily portfolio located in highly desirable, infill submarkets near major employment and lifestyle centers.
