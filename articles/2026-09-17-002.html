Digital Financial Services Firm Zip Takes 13K SF at Rudin’s 41 Madison Avenue

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Thu, 17 Sep 2026 13:30:49 +0000

SUMMARY

Digital financial services firm Zip is relocating its office to 13,394 square feet on the entire 18th floor of 41 Madison Avenue in Manhattan's NoMad neighborhood. The 11-year lease brings the Rudin-owned property to 93 percent occupancy. The landlord noted that modern tenants are seeking highly amenitized and well-designed spaces, making the recently renovated 41 Madison Avenue an attractive option for companies like Zip.
