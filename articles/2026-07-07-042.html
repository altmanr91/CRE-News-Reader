Joint Efforts: Dearth Of Equity Investors Pushes NYC CRE's Biggest Players To Team Up

Bisnow News Feed — Mon, 06 Jul 2026 13:34:01 -0400

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

New York City's leading real estate owners are increasingly forming joint ventures and selling partial stakes in their marquee assets to recycle capital, driven by a scarcity of traditional equity sources and rising development costs. This shift allows developers to pursue new opportunities in a recovering market without fully divesting, as passive investors consolidate their capital with larger, diversified private equity funds. General partner-led recapitalizations now account for a significant majority of real estate secondaries transactions, reflecting the need for creative financing structures. Developers are finding that partnering with former competitors and accepting minority ownership stakes are necessary strategies to access high-quality assets and platforms, especially for large-scale, multi-billion dollar projects where the cost of equity has become more valuable.
