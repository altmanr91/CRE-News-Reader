Brookfield Reinforces Qatar Presence with New Doha Office

Connect CRE — Tue, 27 Oct 2026 01:57:49 +0000

SUMMARY

Non-CRE / Business News

Brookfield has established a new office in Doha, Qatar, to strengthen its presence and investment activities in the region. This expansion is anchored by its global credit platform, which includes the recently acquired Oaktree. The move builds on Brookfield's existing partnerships and significant investments in Qatar, notably a planned $20 billion joint venture with Qai, the artificial intelligence subsidiary of the Qatar Investment Authority, focused on developing AI infrastructure both locally and internationally. The firm aims to collaborate closely with local partners to identify new investment opportunities.
