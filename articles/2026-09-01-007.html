Canyon Partners Provides $74.4M Construction Loan for Inland Empire BTR Project

LOAN

REBusinessOnline — Tue, 01 Sep 2026 13:53:38 +0000

SUMMARY

Canyon Partners and J.P. Morgan have provided a $74.7 million construction loan to BCT Development, a joint venture between Bain Capital Real Estate and Cherry Tree Development. The financing will support the development of a 180-unit built-to-rent townhome community in Riverside's Mission Grove neighborhood. This represents Canyon Partners' second construction loan to BCT Development this year for a rental townhome project in Riverside.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Units: 180

Loan Amount: $74.7M

Notable Features: Community amenities will include a pool, fitness center, and clubhouse.
