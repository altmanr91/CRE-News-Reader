Mixed-Use Upper West Side Building Sells to Terra Developers for $28M

ACQUISITION

Commercial Observer — Mon, 03 Aug 2026 19:29:12 +0000

SUMMARY

Terra Developers has acquired 500 Columbus Avenue, a 35,300-square-foot mixed-use building on Manhattan's Upper West Side, for $28 million from CKMR Corporation. The property, which features ground-floor retail and a dozen long-vacant apartment units, is considered well-suited for conversion into condominiums, with potential for vertical expansion. The transaction was financed with a $25 million loan arranged by Newmark.
