Trademark Property Company purchases 116,000-square-foot shopping center in Austin

ACQUISITION

REJournals — Tue, 01 Sep 2026 13:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Trademark Property Company, in partnership with Cohen & Steers, has acquired Oak Hill Plaza, a 116,000-square-foot neighborhood shopping center in Austin, TX. Trademark will also manage and lease the property, with plans to upgrade the center and enhance its tenant mix. The acquisition is strategically timed with the near completion of the $674 million Oak Hill Parkway Project, which is expected to significantly improve access and mobility to the property.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: retail

Property Name: Oak Hill Plaza

Address: 7191 State Hwy 71, Austin, TX*

Size: 116,000 SF

Notable Features: Located at the northwest corner of US 290 and SH 71, benefiting from the recently completed Oak Hill Parkway Project.

Project Notes: The acquisition follows the substantial completion of TXDOT’s $674 million Oak Hill Parkway Project, which improves regional mobility and access to the property.
