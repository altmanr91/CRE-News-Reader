Partnership Begins Construction on 489-Acre Industrial Park in Gainesville, Texas

CONSTRUCTION

REBusinessOnline — Wed, 12 Aug 2026 15:11:13 +0000

SUMMARY

A partnership between B-29 Investments and Hughes Commercial has commenced construction on Camp Howze Industrial Rail Park, a 489-acre industrial development in Gainesville, Texas. The master-planned project, strategically located along I-35 and U.S. Highway 82, is designed to accommodate up to 7 million square feet of industrial space across 14 buildings, offering flexible options for users from 50,000 to 1 million square feet, including build-to-suit facilities. BNSF Railway is also developing a direct rail connection to the park, enhancing its logistical appeal.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Property Name: Camp Howze Industrial Rail Park

Size: 7,000,000 SF

Site Area: 489 acres

Notable Features: Located along I-35 and U.S. Highway 82; will feature direct rail service from BNSF Railway.

Project Notes: BNSF Railway is constructing a rail connection to the park, with service expected to commence in Q2 2027.
