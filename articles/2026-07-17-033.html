Fort Worth Stockyards will get $71M apartment project as $1B planned redevelopment hangs in balance

CONSTRUCTION

The Real Deal — Texas — Thu, 16 Jul 2026 19:21:36 +0000

SUMMARY

Oldham Goodwin Group and Fort Worth Housing Solutions have broken ground on The Exchange, a $71 million, 296-unit affordable apartment complex located north of Fort Worth's historic Stockyards. This project represents the residential component of a larger 14.6-acre mixed-use development, Stockyards North, which also plans for a hotel, retail, and dining options. The development aims to create a walkable and affordable district, marking Fort Worth Housing Solutions' initial venture into the area surrounding the historic district.
