AI Employee Management Platform Warp Triples Footprint at 156 Fifth Avenue

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Thu, 06 Aug 2026 17:48:18 +0000

SUMMARY

Warp, an AI-driven employee management platform, has significantly expanded its office footprint at 156 Fifth Avenue in New York City's Flatiron District. The company secured an additional 16,500 square feet, bringing its total occupancy to 24,500 square feet across the seventh and eighth floors. This expansion, less than a year after its initial lease in the building, reflects the rapid growth of AI companies and their substantial demand for office space in the city.
