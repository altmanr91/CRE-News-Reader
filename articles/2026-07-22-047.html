Grubb Properties Merges Funds To Create $1.9B Apartment REIT

REFINANCE

Bisnow News Feed — Tue, 21 Jul 2026 11:36:09 -0400

SUMMARY

Grubb Properties has consolidated several legacy funds into a newly formed, non-traded multifamily REIT called Link Apartments REIT, valued at approximately $1.9 billion. This strategic move, which includes a $617 million recapitalization of over 60 properties, aims to create a larger balance sheet for enhanced financing capabilities and improve operational simplicity and efficiency. The portfolio comprises more than 5,600 apartments, including the under-construction 8 Carlisle Street tower in Manhattan. Concurrently, Grubb secured a $240 million credit facility for its separate Link Apartments Opportunity Zone REIT and a $300 million senior construction loan along with a $77 million mezzanine loan to fully capitalize the 8 Carlisle project.
