The Langley multifamily development opens in Houston

REJournals — Mon, 13 Jul 2026 09:46:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Construction

The Langley, a 20-story luxury multifamily high-rise developed by StreetLights Residential and Hunt Real Estate, has completed construction in Houston. Located near Rice University, the building features 134 residences for lease, with two- and three-bedroom homes ranging from 2,165 to 3,396 square feet and monthly rents starting at $9,480. The development emphasizes timeless architectural principles, high-end finishes, and a boutique scale with only eight residences per floor to create an intimate and private living experience.
