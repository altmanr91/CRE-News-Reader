Colliers wins first Downtown Real Estate Softball League title since 2018 in Chicago

REJournals — Wed, 16 Sep 2026 09:40:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Non-CRE / Business News

Colliers secured its first Downtown Real Estate Softball League (DRESL) Championship since 2018, defeating Baird & Warner 2-0 in a dramatic extra-innings final. The victory concluded a strong playoff run for Colliers, which included an upset win over the league's second seed. Baird & Warner, the top seed, made their second consecutive championship appearance but ultimately fell short in a tightly contested defensive battle.
