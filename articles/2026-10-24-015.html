Tech Employment Training Group Signs Headquarters Lease in Long Island City

LEASE

Connect CRE — Fri, 23 Oct 2026 21:23:55 +0000

SUMMARY

Pursuit Transformation Company Inc., a non-profit focused on tech and AI employment training, has signed a long-term lease for approximately 41,000 square feet at The JACX in Long Island City. This new headquarters will occupy the entire 14th and 15th floors of the Class A office development, allowing Pursuit to expand its operations and continue its mission in a supportive environment.
