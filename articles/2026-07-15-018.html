Zeckondorf family accuses supermarket moguls of selling pilfered art

The Real Deal — New York — Tue, 14 Jul 2026 22:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Non-CRE / Political & Crime News

The Zeckendorf family, prominent New York office developers, has filed a lawsuit against the heirs of Robert and Patricia Weis, who control Weis Markets, alleging they sold a painting stolen from the Zeckendorf patriarch's estate. The abstract work by Pierre Soulages, "Peinture 161 x 200 cm, 14 novembre 1958," was auctioned at Christie's New York for $4.95 million. The Zeckendorfs are seeking the proceeds from the sale, claiming the painting was stolen around 1977 and that the Weis family's acquisition documents show inconsistencies.
