JLL Capital Markets provides $13.1 million in acquisition financing for Allen Corporate Center in Allen

LOAN

REJournals — Thu, 22 Oct 2026 16:03:00 +0000

SUMMARY

JLL Capital Markets secured $13.1 million in acquisition financing for Perceptive Real Estate Equities, Inc. for the Allen Corporate Center, an 82,834-square-foot office campus in Allen, Texas. The five-year, fixed-rate, non-recourse loan was provided by a correspondent life insurance company. The property is 97.5% leased and benefits from its strategic location with excellent connectivity to major highways and proximity to key amenities and employment centers.
