JLL Closes Sale of Boutique Hotel in New Hampshire Capital City

SALE

Connect CRE — Wed, 08 Jul 2026 20:39:13 +0000

SUMMARY

JLL's Hotels & Hospitality Group facilitated the sale of The Centennial Hotel, a 32-room boutique hotel in Concord, New Hampshire. Sparta Properties sold the Victorian-era asset to Lord Hotels. The property, built in 1898 and converted to a hotel in 1996, recently underwent comprehensive capital improvements and is strategically located within New Hampshire's primary business and medical district. The transaction reflects a trend of high-net-worth individuals and families seeking boutique hotel acquisitions with strong narratives and strategic locations, offering immediate upside through active management and food and beverage optimization.
