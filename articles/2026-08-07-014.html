NAI Ruhl Commercial Company closes sale of former Quad-City Times headquarters in Davenport

SALE

REJournals — Thu, 06 Aug 2026 16:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

The former Quad-City Times headquarters in Davenport, IA, a 122,800-square-foot landmark property, has been sold to The Geifman Group. The buyer plans extensive remodeling to convert the facility into a multi-tenant building. The property, which includes office and high-bay production space, was exclusively brokered by NAI Ruhl Commercial Company.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Property Name: former Quad-City Times headquarters

Address: 500 E. Third St., Davenport, IA

Size: 122,800 SF

Year Built: 1989

Site Area: 6.25 acres

Notable Features: Overlooks the Mississippi River; includes two-story office space and high-bay production/warehouse space.

Project Notes: Served as the regional headquarters for the Quad-City Times for over three decades.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: The Geifman Group

SELLER BROKER: NAI Ruhl Commercial Company — Rick Schaefer; Charlie Armstrong, SIOR
