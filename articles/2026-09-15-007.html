Piedmont Realty Trust Acquires Office Building in Arlington, Virginia for $56.3M

ACQUISITION

REBusinessOnline — Tue, 15 Sep 2026 13:37:18 +0000

SUMMARY

Piedmont Realty Trust Inc. has acquired a 188,014-square-foot office building in Arlington, VA, for $56.3 million. The property, located in the Ballston submarket, was sold by a partnership that had invested approximately $6 million in capital improvements, leading to significant new leasing activity. The building features a strong tenant mix, including office and ground-level retail occupants.
