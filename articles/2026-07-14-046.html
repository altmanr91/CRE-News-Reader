Denver Office Market Posts Best Absorption In 4 Years

Bisnow News Feed — Mon, 13 Jul 2026 16:34:44 -0400

SUMMARY

Market Research / Office Report

Denver's office market is showing signs of stabilization, posting its strongest net absorption in four years during Q2 2026, with tenants occupying approximately 119,700 SF more than they vacated. This positive trend, despite some tenant downsizing, indicates that the period of rapid deterioration has largely passed. Experts anticipate a gradual recovery, driven by declining sublease inventory, limited new supply, and sustained demand for high-quality office spaces, rather than a swift rebound.

DATA POINTS

Q2 2026 Net Absorption: 119,700 SF

Q1 2022 Net Absorption: 282,000 SF

National Q2 Vacancy Rate Change: Down 10 basis points

Denver Office Vacancy Rate Range: 26.6% to 28.7%

Downtown Denver Vacancy Rate: 38.6%

River North Vacancy Rate: 42.3%

Cherry Creek Vacancy Rate: 12.8%

Southeast Market Vacancy Rate (including Denver Tech Center): 26.4%

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Nationally, office vacancy rates dropped by 10 basis points in Q2 2026, with 49 out of 92 tracked markets showing a decline. In Denver, vacancy rates varied by submarket, with downtown and River North experiencing higher rates (38.6% and 42.3% respectively), while Cherry Creek posted a significantly lower rate of 12.8%.
