Healthcare development today is about thoughtful design and speed to market

REJournals — Thu, 13 Aug 2026 10:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

This article argues that successful healthcare real estate development in the current economic climate prioritizes flexibility, practicality, and speed over flashy architecture. The author, Dan Baumeister of Bremner Healthcare Real Estate, contends that soaring construction costs and shrinking operating margins for healthcare providers necessitate a shift in strategy. Key approaches include adaptive reuse of existing retail or office properties, designing facilities with future adaptability in mind to accommodate evolving services, and making disciplined material selections. The piece emphasizes that thoughtful design should focus on improving care and supporting long-term goals rather than unnecessary aesthetic complexity, ultimately aiming for faster project completion and financial viability.

DATA POINTS

Construction costs (old): $300-$400 per square foot

Construction costs (new): $500-$800 per square foot

Construction costs (high acuity facilities): Exceeding $1,000 per square foot

Existing medical office lease rates: Mid-$20s to low-$30s per square foot

New build-to-suit medical office lease rates: High $30s to well above $50 per square foot
