Classical Christian School Acquires Permanent Upper West Side Home

ACQUISITION

Connect CRE — Mon, 24 Aug 2026 21:42:31 +0000

SUMMARY

The Geneva School of Manhattan, an independent Classical Christian school, has acquired a permanent Upper West Side campus for $33.2 million. The six-story, 38,838-square-foot educational facility was approved by the United States Bankruptcy Court in July, securing a long-term home for the school's continued growth. The acquisition represents significant value, with the purchase price well below the cost of developing a comparable facility in Manhattan today.
