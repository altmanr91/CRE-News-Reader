Greenheck Group to Open 1.3 MSF Manufacturing Facility in Owasso, Oklahoma

ACQUISITION

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 16 Oct 2026 15:11:44 +0000

SUMMARY

Greenheck Group, a manufacturer of commercial air movement equipment, is establishing a 1.3 million-square-foot manufacturing facility in Owasso, Oklahoma. The company acquired a 72-acre former Macy's distribution campus for the project, which represents a $300 million capital investment and is expected to create approximately 700 new jobs over the next five years. Initial building improvements are slated for completion by spring 2027, with production commencing soon after.
