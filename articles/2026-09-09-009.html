Houston Bank & Trust Signs 19,600 SF Office, Retail Lease in Uptown

LEASE

REBusinessOnline — Tue, 08 Sep 2026 15:16:20 +0000

SUMMARY

Houston Bank & Trust has signed a 19,600-square-foot lease for its new banking headquarters, trust department, and flagship retail banking branch in Houston's Uptown area. The space is located at 2603 Augusta Drive. This move signifies a strategic expansion for the bank within a prominent commercial district.
