NRP Ground Debuts 115-Unit Affordable Housing Community in D.C.

DEVELOPMENT

REBusinessOnline — Mon, 28 Sep 2026 14:14:39 +0000

SUMMARY

The NRP Group, in collaboration with Marshall Heights Community Development Organization, has opened Emblem Apartments, a 115-unit affordable housing community in Washington, D.C. The 13-story building provides housing for individuals and families earning up to 30 and 50 percent of the area median income. This project marks NRP's initial affordable housing development in the D.C. market.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Emblem Apartments

Address: 301 Florida Ave NE, Washington, DC*

Units: 115

Notable Features: 13-story building reserved for individuals and families earning up to 30% and 50% of the area median income.

Project Notes: Community amenities include a toddler playroom, bike storage, and a multipurpose room. Residents will have access to supportive services such as financial education and entrepreneurship resources.

* Address sourced separately, not in original article.

EQUITY/FINANCING: DC Housing Finance Agency (DCHFA)
