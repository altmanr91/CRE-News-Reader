Transwestern Investment acquires 408,176-square-foot industrial facility in Chicago market

ACQUISITION

REJournals — Mon, 28 Sep 2026 15:31:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Transwestern Investments has acquired a 408,176-square-foot industrial facility in the Chicago I-88 Corridor on behalf of a separate account. The property, built in 2023, is fully leased and offers strong connectivity to major interstates and transportation hubs, reflecting the robust demand for industrial assets in the region.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Address: 320 Overland Drive, Chicago, IL

Size: 408,176 SF

Occupancy at Sale: 100.0%

Year Built: 2023

Site Area: 26.79 acres

Notable Features: Cross-dock configuration, 36-foot clear heights, 55 dock doors expandable to 86, four drive-in doors, and dedicated car and trailer parking. Located 1.7 miles from Interstate 88, providing connectivity to I-355, I-294, O'Hare International Airport, and downtown Chicago.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: Transwestern Investments

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

The I-88 Corridor in Chicago is currently 94% occupied and has maintained an average occupancy of approximately 95% over the past 15 years.
