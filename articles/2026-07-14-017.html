Lawsuit accuses ex-DCAS official Jesse Hamilton, Cushman broker Diana Boutross of $250K shakedown

The Real Deal — New York — Mon, 13 Jul 2026 21:49:24 +0000

SUMMARY

CRE Legal / Regulatory

A consultant is suing a former New York City official, a Cushman & Wakefield broker, the brokerage firm, and two landlords, alleging a $250,000 shakedown demand to facilitate two city leases. The lawsuit claims the former deputy commissioner of real estate services at DCAS and the broker demanded the payment to allow lease negotiations for the FDNY and Department of Homeless Services to proceed at 850 Third Avenue in Brooklyn. After the consultant refused, he was allegedly frozen out of the project, and his consulting contract was terminated, leading to a claim for $36 million in damages from the individuals and Cushman, and $8 million from the property owners.
