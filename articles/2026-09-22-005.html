Chelsea’s Rubin Museum of Himalayan Art Hits the Market

Commercial Observer — Tue, 22 Sep 2026 11:00:40 +0000

SUMMARY

Property Listing / For Sale

The Rubin Museum of Himalayan Art is selling its 80,000-square-foot, six-story building at 140–154 West 17th Street in Chelsea, New York City. The museum, which opened in 2004, is shifting its business model away from a single physical location to focus on traveling exhibitions and object loans. The property, originally built in the mid-19th century and previously a flagship store for Barneys New York, is being marketed by JLL and is considered suitable for various cultural, educational, commercial, or mixed-use applications.
