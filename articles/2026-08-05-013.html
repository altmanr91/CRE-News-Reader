SR Realty Trust acquires 183,000-square-foot distribution facility in Whitestown

ACQUISITION

REJournals — Tue, 04 Aug 2026 14:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

SR Realty Trust has acquired Westpark Logistics Center, a newly constructed 183,000-square-foot Class-A industrial distribution facility in Whitestown, Indiana. The property is fully leased to a regional coffee chain, serving as a critical distribution hub for over 300 locations. This acquisition marks SR Realty Trust's entry into the Indianapolis market, strategically expanding its Midwest portfolio.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Property Name: Westpark Logistics Center

Size: 183,000 SF

Occupancy at Sale: 100.0%

Notable Features: Class-A industrial distribution facility with immediate access to Interstate 65 and proximity to Interstate 465.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Whitestown, IN is one of the fastest-growing industrial submarkets in the Indianapolis metropolitan area, attracting national distribution, logistics, and manufacturing users due to its strategic location, skilled workforce, and business-friendly environment.
