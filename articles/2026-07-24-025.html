American Landmark expands South Florida apartment portfolio with Palm Beach County acquisition

ACQUISITION

The Real Deal — Miami — Thu, 23 Jul 2026 21:45:00 +0000

SUMMARY

American Landmark Apartments has expanded its South Florida multifamily portfolio with the acquisition of Savona Grand, a 214-unit apartment community located between Boynton Beach and Lake Worth Beach in Palm Beach County. The Tampa-based investment manager plans to invest in upgrades at the property, which was previously sold in 2019 for $47.8 million. The acquisition brings American Landmark's South Florida portfolio to four properties, reflecting the company's strategy to capitalize on Palm Beach County's strong resident and employer growth amidst constrained new supply.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Savona Grand

Address: 7132 Colony Club Drive, Palm Beach County, FL

Units: 214

Year Built: 2003

Site Area: 13 acres

Notable Features: Amenities include a pool, fitness center, clubhouse, basketball and tennis courts, pickleball court, dog park, and gated entry. The property is near Florida’s Turnpike, Interstate 95, HCA Florida JFK Hospital, and Palm Beach State College.

Project Notes: The property was previously acquired by Southstar Capital Group in 2019 for $47.8 million, with a $32 million mortgage from Hunt Mortgage Capital.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: American Landmark Apartments — Elizabeth Roy, Chief Investment Officer

SELLER: Southstar Capital Group
