Vernon Hills clears 1.2M sf industrial redevelopment on former American Hotel Register site

DEVELOPMENT

The Real Deal — Chicago — Thu, 09 Jul 2026 18:15:00 +0000

SUMMARY

A 70-acre former American Hotel Register site in Vernon Hills, IL, has received conditional approval for a 1.2 million-square-foot industrial redevelopment. The project will feature three speculative industrial buildings, an existing warehouse, and a dedicated site for a classroom supplies company. The developer revised its initial 2024 proposal to address local concerns regarding traffic and noise, leading to the condition of an extended sound wall.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Address: 100-230 South Milwaukee Avenue, Vernon Hills, IL

Size: 1,200,000 SF

Site Area: 70 acres

Notable Features: Redevelopment of a former American Hotel Register property.

Project Notes: Conditional approval was granted, requiring an extended sound wall to mitigate noise concerns. The project includes combining four existing facilities for a classroom supplies company into a new site within the development.
