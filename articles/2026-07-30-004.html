NYC Selects Slate Property Group, Hudson Companies to Build 980 Homes in LIC

DEVELOPMENT

Commercial Observer — Wed, 29 Jul 2026 19:24:05 +0000

SUMMARY

A development team comprising Slate Property Group, Hudson Companies, and Volunteers of America has been selected by the NYC Department of Housing Preservation and Development to construct a 980-unit mixed-use project in Long Island City, Queens. Named The Orion, the development will feature two-thirds affordable housing units, including supportive apartments, along with an affordable child care center, a workforce development center, a community pool, and ground-floor retail space. This project is part of the larger Hunter's Point South housing complex, with the developers emphasizing the importance of providing housing for all income levels in a high-opportunity neighborhood.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: mixed-use

Property Name: The Orion

Address: 54-42 Second Street, Long Island City, NY

Size: 6,000 SF

Units: 980

Notable Features: Two-thirds of the units will be affordable housing, including 150 supportive apartments. The mixed-use community will feature an affordable child care center, a workforce development center, and a community pool.

Project Notes: The project is located on Parcel E within the 30-acre Hunter's Point South housing complex, which is expected to feature approximately 5,000 new homes upon completion. Parcel E was subject to an RFP requiring more affordable homes than previous phases.
