E Ink Breaks Ground on Expansion of Billerica Innovation Center

CONSTRUCTION

Connect CRE — Fri, 24 Jul 2026 20:37:17 +0000

SUMMARY

E Ink, a leader in ePaper technology, has broken ground on a significant expansion of its U.S. headquarters and global R&D center in Billerica, MA. The project will enlarge the company's Billerica Innovation Center, which currently houses over 260 employees. This expansion is expected to enhance E Ink's capacity for technological innovation, bolster its global customer support, and contribute to the local economy and innovation ecosystem.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: R&D

Property Name: Billerica Innovation Center

Completion: March 2027

Notable Features: Global R&D center and U.S. headquarters for ePaper technology pioneer.

Project Notes: E Ink was spun out of the MIT Media Lab in 1997 and has been headquartered in Billerica since its inception.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

OWNER: E Ink — Paul Apen, Chief Business and Operations Officer

PLANNER: NV5 Planning & Design, Inc.

ARCHITECT: IF Design L.L.C.

ENGINEER: SPEC Process Engineering & Construction, Inc.
