Banyan Street, Independencia Refi South Florida Office With $54M

REFINANCE

Commercial Observer — Mon, 24 Aug 2026 19:50:12 +0000

SUMMARY

Banyan Street Capital and Independencia Asset Management secured a $53.5 million refinancing loan from Wells Fargo for the Doral Center, a 290,000-square-foot office complex in Doral, FL. The owners have invested $15.5 million in capital improvements since acquiring the property in 2020, transforming it into a premier workplace environment. This strategic repositioning has significantly enhanced the property's competitiveness and spurred a notable increase in leasing activity, reflecting the robust economic fundamentals of South Florida.
