Phoenix Commercial Advisors Directs $10.7M Sale of Retail Center in Surprise, Arizona

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Thu, 02 Jul 2026 14:36:41 +0000

SUMMARY

Phoenix Commercial Advisors has facilitated the $10.7 million sale of Grand Village Center, a necessity-based retail center in Surprise, Arizona. The property, built in 2005, was 97 percent leased at the time of sale, featuring an internet-resistant tenant mix anchored by a trampoline park. The transaction achieved a price of $203 per square foot.
