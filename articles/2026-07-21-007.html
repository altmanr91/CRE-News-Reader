JLL Arranges $40.9M Acquisition Loan for Irving Apartment Complex

LOAN

REBusinessOnline — Mon, 20 Jul 2026 14:57:31 +0000

SUMMARY

JLL has arranged a $40.9 million acquisition loan for Brixton Capital to purchase Allura Las Colinas, a 288-unit apartment complex in Irving, Texas. The nonrecourse, fixed-rate loan was secured through an undisclosed life insurance company. JLL also represented the undisclosed seller in the transaction.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Allura Las Colinas

Address: 6445 Love Dr, Irving, TX*

Units: 288

Loan Amount: $40.9M

Loan Per Unit: $142,014/unit (calculated)

Year Built: 2003

Notable Features: Amenities include a pool, fitness center, clubhouse, dog park, and outdoor grilling and dining stations.
