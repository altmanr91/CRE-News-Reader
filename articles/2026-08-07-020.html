Production Fabrication Firm Relocates to Rancho Dominguez

LEASE

Connect CRE — Thu, 06 Aug 2026 21:41:50 +0000

SUMMARY

Cactus Fabrication, a production fabrication firm for the entertainment industry, has signed a 10-year lease for a 35,333-square-foot industrial property in Rancho Dominguez, CA. The company is relocating from a smaller Los Angeles facility to accommodate its expanding operations. The property's large footprint, heavy electrical capacity, functional layout, and recent modernization were key factors in the relocation, providing a best-in-class asset suitable for attracting employees and impressing clients.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Address: 2945 East Maria St., Rancho Dominguez, CA

Size: 35,333 SF

Notable Features: Features heavy electrical capacity, a large secured side yard, and a separate front-loading area. The building has been recently modernized with superior office space.

Project Notes: Tenant is relocating from a smaller property in Los Angeles.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

LANDLORD: Crown Associates Realty, Inc.
