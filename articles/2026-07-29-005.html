Whole Foods Signs 8K-SF Lease to Open Daily Shop at 1524 Second Avenue

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Tue, 28 Jul 2026 19:31:41 +0000

SUMMARY

Whole Foods Market has signed an 8,121-square-foot lease for a new small-format store, likely a 'Daily Shop' concept focusing on prepared foods, at 1524 Second Avenue in Manhattan's Upper East Side. The transaction secures a highly visible corner location in a well-established retail corridor with significant residential density, making it an ideal fit for the grocer's neighborhood store format. This lease reflects Whole Foods' strategy to expand its smaller-scale concepts in New York City.
