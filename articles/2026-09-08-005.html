A Wave of New York Office Tower Listings Tests the Market’s Recovery

SALE

Commercial Observer — Tue, 08 Sep 2026 12:00:59 +0000

SUMMARY

New York City's office investment sales market is experiencing a significant resurgence, driven by strong leasing fundamentals and increasing investor confidence. Several Midtown office towers have recently hit the market, with some already finding buyers, indicating a robust appetite for well-leased, high-quality assets in prime submarkets. This uptick in activity, which saw office investments grow 30% to exceed $11 billion in 2025, is expected to continue as institutional players re-engage and debt markets become more accommodative. Brokers note that current leasing strength is unusually leading capital markets, signaling a shift where investors are acting on proven market performance rather than future speculation.

New York City office investment sales are on the rise, with activity in 2025 exceeding $11 billion, a 30% increase. Manhattan office sales in Q2 2026 totaled $2.3 billion, a 42% increase over Q2 2025 and on par with the five-year quarterly average.
