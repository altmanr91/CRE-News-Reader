New Opportunity Zones May Help Finish California Projects, Not Start Them

Bisnow Latest News Feed — Mon, 02 Nov 2026 20:10:11 -0500

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

This article suggests that Opportunity Zones are more effective in providing the final push for real estate projects nearing completion rather than initiating new developments. The author argues that while Opportunity Zones offer significant tax benefits, they are not sufficient to overcome major obstacles such as high construction costs, which often deter projects from breaking ground in the first place. The core argument is that these zones serve as a valuable accelerant for existing projects but do not fundamentally alter the economic viability of new, speculative ventures facing substantial upfront costs.
