Cooper Commercial closes sale of two-building retail center in Solon

SALE

REJournals — Fri, 21 Aug 2026 15:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

The Cooper Commercial Investment Group recently brokered the sale of Kruse Commons, a fully occupied, two-building retail center in Solon, Ohio, for $9.3 million. The property, anchored by a national bakery-cafe chain and a regional ice cream shop, was acquired by an undisclosed buyer at a 6.79% capitalization rate. This transaction marks the second time Cooper Commercial has facilitated the sale of this asset within 18 months, highlighting the firm's strong market relationships and the property's appeal as a stable, income-producing asset in a robust suburban market.
