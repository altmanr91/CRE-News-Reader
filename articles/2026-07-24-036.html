Jerry Jones sells 17 Cowboys Way to Hobbs Brook

SALE

The Real Deal — Texas — Thu, 23 Jul 2026 20:30:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Jerry Jones' real estate firm has sold an 11-story, 300,000-square-foot office building at 17 Cowboys Way in Frisco, TX, to Massachusetts-based Hobbs Brook Real Estate. The property is part of The Star, a mixed-use campus that serves as the Dallas Cowboys' corporate headquarters. This acquisition marks Hobbs Brook's entry into the Texas real estate market. The building is 98% leased, though one of its tenants recently announced layoffs.
