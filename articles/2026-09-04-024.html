Miami-Dade Overturns Mayor's Veto For 2.7M SF Kelly Tractor HQ Beyond Urban Development Boundary

Bisnow Latest News Feed — Thu, 03 Sep 2026 16:33:42 -0400

SUMMARY

Infrastructure Investment / Government Policy

Miami-Dade County has approved the development of a new 2.7 million-square-foot headquarters for Kelly Tractor, overriding a mayoral veto. This decision allows for the construction of the facility outside the established Urban Development Boundary, a move that has faced opposition regarding its environmental and planning implications.
