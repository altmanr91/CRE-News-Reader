Savanna, Deutsche Bank Pushing Off 521 Fifth Avenue’s Foreclosure Sale

Commercial Observer — Wed, 28 Oct 2026 15:29:33 +0000

SUMMARY

Foreclosure

Savanna and Deutsche Bank are jointly marketing the 39-story office building at 521 Fifth Avenue in New York City, following the default of a $242 million CMBS loan originated by Deutsche Bank in 2019. The property, which Savanna acquired for $381 million, has seen its occupancy decline significantly since 2020. Despite an initial marketing attempt at $265 million that fell through, the parties are seeking another 90-day extension on the foreclosure sale, indicating continued efforts to find a buyer for the asset, which was appraised at $270 million in June 2026.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Two Midtown properties less than a mile from 521 Fifth Avenue recently sold for less than their appraised values. The 303,000 SF Berkeley Building at 19 West 44th Street, 79% occupied, sold for $108 million ($356/SF) in June 2026. The 531,270 SF building at 260 Madison Avenue, 68% occupied, sold for $211.2 million ($398/SF) in December 2025.
