Retailers Charging Ahead With EV Stations Despite Policy Pullback

Bisnow Latest News Feed — Thu, 03 Sep 2026 19:23:49 -0400

SUMMARY

Market Research / EV Infrastructure

Despite a flattening in new electric vehicle (EV) sales in 2025, the used EV market is experiencing rapid growth, prompting retailers to continue investing in EV charging stations. This trend suggests that while initial adoption rates for new EVs may have slowed, the overall demand for EV infrastructure remains strong due to the expanding secondary market for these vehicles. Retailers are likely anticipating sustained long-term demand for charging solutions as more used EVs enter circulation.
