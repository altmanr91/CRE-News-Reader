Woodcliff Lake Retail Center Trades for $109M in Move from Legacy Ownership

SALE

Connect CRE — Fri, 30 Oct 2026 21:32:06 +0000

SUMMARY

Tice's Corner, a prominent lifestyle retail center in Woodcliff Lake, NJ, has been sold for $109.5 million. The transaction involved WS Development acquiring the property from WS Tice's Inc., marking one of New Jersey's largest single-asset retail sales in the last decade. The center, anchored by a major tech retailer, boasts a strong tenant mix and is situated in an affluent, supply-constrained market, presenting significant long-term growth potential for the new owner. The sale was a highly competitive process, concluding quickly without broad market exposure due to the asset's generational ownership.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: retail

Property Name: Tice’s Corner

Address: 441 Chestnut Ridge Rd., Woodcliff Lake, NJ

Sale Price: $109.5M

Project Notes: This was a generational retail asset held by the same legacy ownership for decades.
