NAI Hiffman closes 7,941-square-foot lease with speech therapy center in Arlington Heights

LEASE

REJournals — Thu, 22 Oct 2026 13:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

NAI Hiffman represented a private ownership group in a 7,941-square-foot lease with Chicago Speech Therapy for a new location in a two-building office campus at 3436 N. Kennicott Ave. in Arlington Heights, IL. The tenant, a provider of speech and language services for children, chose the property for its accessibility and convenience for families in the northwest suburbs. Brokers noted the long-term commitment reflects confidence in the property and its location, highlighting the appeal of well-located suburban office space to healthcare and service providers due to its financially stable position and proximity to a major hospital and retail corridor.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Address: 3436 N. Kennicott Ave., Arlington Heights, IL

Size: 7,941 SF
