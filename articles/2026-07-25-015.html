JLL Capital Markets arranges $78 million in construction financing for multifamily community in Dallas

LOAN

REJournals — Fri, 24 Jul 2026 13:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

JLL Capital Markets has arranged $78.732 million in construction takeout financing for The Flynn at Live Oak, a recently completed 327-unit luxury multifamily community in Dallas, TX. The loan, secured on behalf of Conor Commercial Real Estate and Globe Corporation, was provided by funds managed by Blue Owl Capital Inc. The property is strategically located in the East Dallas submarket, offering residents convenient access to major entertainment districts and employment centers in Uptown and Downtown Dallas.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Dallas is projected to add nearly 600,000 new residents by 2030 and ranks first among major metros for corporate headquarters relocations. East Dallas is a strong multifamily submarket, ranking third in the Dallas-Fort Worth area for occupancy and rent performance.
