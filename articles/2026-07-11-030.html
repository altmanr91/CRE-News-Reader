Colliers Facilitates Sale of 954K-SF Industrial Portfolio in Indianapolis

SALE

Connect CRE — Fri, 10 Jul 2026 21:49:03 +0000

SUMMARY

Colliers facilitated the sale of a six-building industrial portfolio totaling 954,072 square feet across the Indianapolis metropolitan area. The properties are strategically located in high-demand submarkets including Park 100, Fishers, and Plainfield, benefiting from Indianapolis's position along major Midwestern transportation corridors. The transaction highlights continued strong investor interest in Midwestern markets due to their economic momentum and strategic location.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Size: 954,072 SF

Notable Features: Portfolio includes properties in Park 100, Fishers, and Plainfield, which are high-demand user markets.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: MLG Capital
