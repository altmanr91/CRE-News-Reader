Vaja Group Acquires Astoria Development Site at 24-41 31st Street for $26M

ACQUISITION

Commercial Observer — Fri, 18 Sep 2026 11:00:15 +0000

SUMMARY

Vaja Group has acquired a 29,638-square-foot development site in Astoria, Queens, for $26.39 million. The site, formerly a retail building, offers nearly 150,000 buildable square feet and is suitable for a 108-unit mixed-use development with additional retail space and parking. This acquisition, along with a previous purchase, was facilitated by New York City's 2022 31st Street–Hoyt Avenue rezoning, which created a new mixed-use corridor.
