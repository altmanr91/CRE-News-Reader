Olmstead Signs Luckin Coffee to 10-Year Lease in Hudson Square

LEASE

Connect CRE — Mon, 27 Jul 2026 22:05:01 +0000

SUMMARY

Olmstead Properties has secured a 10-year retail lease with Luckin Coffee for 1,200 square feet at 180 Varick St. in Manhattan's Hudson Square. This transaction brings a rapidly expanding coffee brand to the base of a historic office building, replacing a long-standing neighborhood eatery. The landlord noted that Luckin Coffee is an ideal tenant, enhancing amenities for building occupants and serving the evolving Hudson Square neighborhood, which is becoming a dynamic office market.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: retail

Address: 180 Varick St., New York City, NY

Size: 1,200 SF

Year Built: 1920

Notable Features: Located in a historic office building in Hudson Square.

Project Notes: The building has been owned by the Rosenblatt family since its development in the 1920s.
