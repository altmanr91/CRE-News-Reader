ULI Emerging Trends Report: Manhattan, Jersey City, Among Top 10 Markets to Watch in 2027

REBusinessOnline — Thu, 05 Nov 2026 15:39:32 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Emerging Trends Report

The annual Emerging Trends in Real Estate 2027 report, a collaboration between the Urban Land Institute (ULI) and PwC, identifies Manhattan and Jersey City as top markets to watch, ranking them 7th and 8th respectively. Manhattan's significant improvement from its 2022 ranking of 42nd is attributed to diversified strengthening across office, retail, and tourism sectors, bolstered by a substantial infrastructure pipeline. The report highlights AI companies as a key driver of office absorption in Manhattan, alongside planned office-to-residential conversions. Jersey City continues to be a strong performer due to its robust multifamily construction and its appeal as a more affordable, well-connected alternative to Manhattan.
