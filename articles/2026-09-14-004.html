Embrey’s Garrett Karam Discusses Multifamily Investment Strategies

Commercial Observer — Mon, 14 Sep 2026 10:30:38 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

Garrett Karam, CIO at Embrey, a vertically integrated multifamily developer, discusses the current state of the multifamily market and the firm's investment strategies. Embrey focuses on the Sun Belt and Mountain West regions, emphasizing tactical site selection and creating value through community-focused management. Karam notes the challenges posed by rapid interest rate movements but views the recent spike in Treasury yields as a short-term issue rather than a fundamental shift. He identifies new development as a prime opportunity, citing an anticipated demand-outstripping-supply dynamic in the coming years and improved construction execution post-COVID.

DATA POINTS

Embrey portfolio size: 16,000 multifamily units

Development capital secured by Karam: $5 billion

National multifamily unit deliveries (past 3 years): 500,000+ units annually

Projected national multifamily unit deliveries (next few years): around 300,000 units annually

10-Year Treasury rate (pre-Iran War): 3.95%

10-Year Treasury rate (current): nearly 5%
