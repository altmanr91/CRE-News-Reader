Javits Center Expansion, Tech Upgrades Boosting NYC Hospitality Industry

Commercial Observer — Thu, 13 Aug 2026 19:37:03 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Hospitality

The Javits Center in New York City has completed a $5 million technology upgrade, installing Wi-Fi 7 to enhance its appeal for major conferences and events. This upgrade follows a $1.5 billion expansion completed in 2021, which added 1.2 million square feet, including a large ballroom and a green rooftop pavilion. The improvements aim to boost the city's hospitality industry by attracting more large-scale conventions, with the Javits Center estimating its bookings generated over 100,000 hotel stays in 2025. The increased capacity and technological capabilities are enabling the center to host a wider range of events, from medical conferences to large fan festivals, and are expected to drive further growth in the surrounding area, including new hotel developments.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

The Javits Center's convention bookings generated more than 100,000 hotel stays in the New York City region in 2025. A new 455-room Hilton-branded hotel is slated to open at 418 11th Avenue, and a 379-room Hotel Meta is planned for 450 11th Avenue, both near the Javits Center.
