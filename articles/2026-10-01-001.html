Law Firm Morgan, Lewis & Bockius Inks 205K SF at 1251 Avenue of the Americas

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Thu, 01 Oct 2026 16:01:03 +0000

SUMMARY

Global law firm Morgan, Lewis & Bockius has signed a significant lease for 205,000 square feet at 1251 Avenue of the Americas in Midtown Manhattan, relocating its New York City offices. The transaction was confirmed by third-quarter market reports from Colliers and Savills. The specific terms of the lease, including asking rent and duration, were not disclosed.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Property Name: 1251 Avenue of the Americas

Address: 1251 Avenue of the Americas, New York City, NY

Size: 205,000 SF

Notable Features: 54-story office building formerly known as the Exxon Building, located between West 49th and West 50th streets.

Project Notes: The law firm is relocating from its current office at 101 Park Avenue.
