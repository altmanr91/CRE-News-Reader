Abbott’s shift into reverse puts data center debate into overdrive

The Real Deal — Texas — Sun, 05 Jul 2026 14:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

Texas Governor Greg Abbott has reversed his previous pro-data center stance, now advocating for an outright ban on these developments in rural areas and calling for increased regulation statewide. This shift comes amid growing public opposition to data centers due to concerns over noise, water, and power consumption. The governor's proposed regulations include annual reporting, limiting incentives, and requiring closed-loop water systems. This change in policy, particularly given his history of restricting municipal power, sets the stage for a contentious legislative session.
