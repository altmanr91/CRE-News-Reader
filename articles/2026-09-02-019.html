Essex Realty Group Brokers Sale of 12-Unit MF in Tinley Park

SALE

Connect CRE — Tue, 01 Sep 2026 21:22:47 +0000

SUMMARY

Essex Realty Group brokered the sale of a 12-unit multifamily property in Tinley Park, Illinois, for $1.51 million. The property, which had been under the same ownership since 2001, was acquired by an out-of-state investor looking to expand their Chicago-area portfolio. The buyer plans to implement a value-add strategy by renovating the remaining ten units and increasing rents to market rates, capitalizing on the strong investor demand for such assets in Chicago's suburban multifamily market.

DATA POINTS

Units: 12

Sale Price: $1.5M

Project Notes: The property was owned by the same seller since 2001. The buyer plans to renovate the remaining units and push rents to market levels.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

There is strong investor appetite for value-add multifamily assets in Chicago's suburban markets.
