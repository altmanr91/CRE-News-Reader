HLCI acquires Houston site for development of 191,768-square-foot industrial facility

DEVELOPMENT

REJournals — Wed, 09 Sep 2026 13:03:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Holt Lunsford Commercial Investments (HLCI) has acquired a 14.4-acre site in Northwest Houston for the development of The Bend at 249, a 191,768-square-foot Class-AA industrial facility. The project will feature a single rear-load building designed for modern distribution, logistics, and light manufacturing, with groundbreaking imminent and delivery anticipated in mid-2027. This acquisition expands HLCI's presence in the Northwest Houston industrial corridor, a market characterized by strong tenant demand and limited supply.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Property Name: The Bend at 249

Size: 191,768 SF

Completion: mid-2027

Site Area: 14.4 acres

Notable Features: Class-AA industrial facility with 36-foot clear height, upsized power, 44 dock high doors, 61 trailer stalls, and direct frontage along SH-249.
