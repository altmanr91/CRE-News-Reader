Bascom Acquires Buena Park Apartments as Value-Add Deal

ACQUISITION

Connect CRE — Wed, 15 Jul 2026 20:53:23 +0000

SUMMARY

The Bascom Group has acquired Castlewood Park Apartments, a 183-unit multifamily community in Buena Park, for $53.125 million. The transaction was financed by Brightspire Capital, Inc., with IPA Capital Markets arranging the acquisition loan. The new owner plans to implement a value-add strategy, modernizing the 1963-vintage property while preserving its desirable features such as large floorplans and green spaces, aiming to enhance its appeal to North Orange County residents.
