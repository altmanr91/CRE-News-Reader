Partners Development Breaks Ground on 80,000 SF Industrial Project in Seguin, Texas

CONSTRUCTION

REBusinessOnline — Thu, 29 Oct 2026 15:12:52 +0000

SUMMARY

Partners Development, an affiliate of Partners Real Estate, has commenced construction on the first phase of Seguin Commerce Park, an 80,000-square-foot industrial project in Seguin, Texas. This initial phase will consist of two 40,000-square-foot buildings, with construction financing provided by Palladius Capital Management. The project is expected to be delivered in late spring/early summer 2027, and the buildings are being marketed for lease or sale. The overall development is planned to include eight buildings totaling 234,600 square feet.
