Marcus & Millichap Closes Suburban Chicago Multifamily Property Sale

SALE

Connect CRE — Tue, 08 Sep 2026 21:29:40 +0000

SUMMARY

Marcus & Millichap has facilitated the sale of Fox Run Apartments, a 220-unit multifamily property in St. Charles, Illinois. The fully renovated asset, located in a high-demand suburban Chicago submarket, was sold by a private investor to another private investor. The property's strategic location and recent upgrades position it for sustained long-term growth.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Fox Run Apartments

Address: 115 Walnut Drive, St. Charles, IL

Units: 220

Year Built: 1973

Site Area: 7.5 acres

Notable Features: Fully renovated asset with loft-style apartments, updated kitchens, and stainless-steel appliances. Accessible from Illinois Route 38 and Route 64, and within walking distance of public transportation.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

SELLER BROKER: Marcus & Millichap — Ryan D. Engle, Senior Managing Director of Investments; Andrean Angelov

SELLER: Private Investor
