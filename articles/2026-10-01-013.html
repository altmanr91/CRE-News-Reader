Freehill, JLL mark groundbreaking of 1.64-million-square-foot industrial development in Northwest Houston

CONSTRUCTION

REJournals — Thu, 01 Oct 2026 15:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Freehill and JLL have broken ground on Fairbanks Landing, a 1.64-million-square-foot Class-A industrial development in Northwest Houston. The project will be delivered in two phases, with Phase I, totaling 809,220 square feet, scheduled for completion in Q3 2027 as a build-to-suit for Power Electronics. Phase II, comprising 834,720 square feet across two buildings, is expected to be delivered in Q4 2027 and will feature cross-dock configurations, high clear heights, and ample parking.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Property Name: Fairbanks Landing

Address: 9220 Fairbanks North Houston Road, Houston, TX

Size: 1,643,940 SF

Completion: Q4 2027

Phase I: 809,220 SF, completion Q3 2027

Notable Features: Class-A industrial development with cross-dock configurations, 40-foot and 36-foot clear heights, and ESFR sprinkler systems. Convenient access to Beltway 8, U.S. Highway 290, and State Highway 249.

Project Notes: Phase I is a build-to-suit for Power Electronics. Phase II will include Building 3 (568,080 SF) and Building 4 (266,640 SF).
