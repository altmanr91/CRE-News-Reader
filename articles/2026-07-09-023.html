Anthropic finalizes Hudson Square 466K sf building lease as it grows in NYC

LEASE

The Real Deal — New York — Wed, 08 Jul 2026 20:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Anthropic, a prominent AI firm, has finalized a full-building lease for 466,000 square feet at 330 Hudson Street in Manhattan's Hudson Square. The company is expanding its New York City presence significantly, planning to double its local workforce to 1,000 people by year-end, with the new office capable of accommodating 1,700 workers. This transaction highlights the growing demand from AI companies for large office spaces in New York City, which has emerged as a key market for the industry.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Address: 330 Hudson Street, New York City, NY

Size: 466,000 SF

Notable Features: 16-story full-building lease.

Project Notes: Existing tenants will need to vacate before Anthropic moves in, which may slow the relocation process. Anthropic's previous NYC lease was for 15,000 square feet at 155 Sixth Avenue, expiring this year.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

AI companies are leasing office space in New York City at roughly twice last year's pace. AI tenants accounted for over a third of the tech sector's demand in Q1 2026 with 670,000 square feet leased, a significant increase from their 12% share in 2025. While San Francisco remains the primary hub for AI, New York City has emerged as the second-largest central market for the industry.
