JLL Negotiates $27M Sale of Apartment Complex in Haddam, Connecticut

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Tue, 22 Sep 2026 13:47:30 +0000

SUMMARY

JLL has brokered the $27 million sale of Blueway Commons, an 88-unit apartment complex in Haddam, Connecticut. The property, developed by Elm Tree Communities between 2023 and 2025, was acquired by RAK Realty. The transaction highlights continued investor interest in well-amenitized multifamily assets in suburban markets.
