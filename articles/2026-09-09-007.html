Walker & Dunlop Arranges $46.5M Loan for Refinancing of Metro Austin Apartment Community

REFINANCE

REBusinessOnline — Tue, 08 Sep 2026 15:21:35 +0000

SUMMARY

Walker & Dunlop has secured a $46.5 million bridge loan for the refinancing of River Junction, a 329-unit apartment community in Leander, Texas. The floating-rate, interest-only loan was provided by Bridge Investment Group on behalf of the undisclosed owner. The property, completed in 2024, features a mix of apartments and townhomes with various amenities.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: River Junction

Units: 329

Loan Amount: $46.5M

Loan Per Unit: $141,337/unit (calculated)

Year Built: 2024

Notable Features: Amenities include a pool, fitness center, clubhouse, coworking space, pet park, and outdoor grilling and dining stations.

Project Notes: The community comprises 15 buildings housing one-, two-, and three-bedroom apartments, as well as 17 townhomes.
