Barry Gosin to Step Down as Newmark CEO at Year’s End

REBusinessOnline — Tue, 11 Aug 2026 14:27:25 +0000

SUMMARY

Promotion

Barry Gosin, who has served as CEO of Newmark Group since 1979, will step down from his role at the end of 2026. He will transition to chairman of the operating company, and a new CEO is expected to be named by his departure. Gosin's tenure saw Newmark through its sale in 2011, a spin-off from BGC Partner Inc. in 2018, and an initial public offering in 2017, alongside over 55 company acquisitions and European expansion.
