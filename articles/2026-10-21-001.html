Northwind Group Closes $1B Debt Fund

Commercial Observer — Wed, 21 Oct 2026 12:00:36 +0000

SUMMARY

Loan

Northwind Group has successfully closed its third real estate credit fund, Northwind Debt Fund III (NDF III), with $1 billion in committed capital, significantly exceeding its initial $500 million target. The fund, which primarily focuses on senior loans, has already deployed $1.5 billion across multifamily, condominium, and office-to-residential projects in major U.S. gateway markets. Its investor base includes pension funds, insurance companies, banks, family offices, and ultra-high-net-worth individuals, many of whom are returning investors from Northwind's previous successful funds. The fund is nearly 60% deployed and is expected to reach 90% deployment by the end of 2026, with a strong emphasis on residential properties in supply-constrained markets like New York City.
