Walker & Dunlop Arranges Refi on Newly Built Bed-Stuy Multifamily

REFINANCE

Connect CRE — Mon, 03 Aug 2026 21:44:40 +0000

SUMMARY

Walker & Dunlop has arranged a $137.5 million refinancing package for 12 Halsey, a recently completed Class A mixed-use multifamily property in Brooklyn's Bedford-Stuyvesant neighborhood. The three-year, floating-rate financing was secured with AllianceBernstein for EJS Group and Hope Street Capital. The property's combination of high-quality construction, affordability, and transit-oriented location generated significant lender interest, reflecting strong demand for new multifamily assets in New York City.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: 12 Halsey

Address: 12 Halsey Street, Brooklyn, NY

Units: 240

Loan Amount: $137.5M

Loan Per Unit: $572,917/unit (calculated)

Completion: October 2025

Notable Features: 30% of residential units are designated as affordable. The property occupies a full cross-block site and has immediate access to the A and C subway lines.
