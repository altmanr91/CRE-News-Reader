Construction tops out at mixed-use development in Chicago’s Fulton Market neighborhood

CONSTRUCTION

REJournals — Thu, 09 Jul 2026 17:02:50 +0000

SUMMARY

Construction has topped out at 1221 W. Washington Blvd., a new mixed-use tower in Chicago's Fulton Market neighborhood. The 19-story luxury development, known as 1221, will feature 287 apartment units, ground-floor retail, and extensive amenities. The developer anticipates strong demand for the new apartments due to limited supply in the Fulton Market area, with first deliveries expected in January 2027.
