Machine Investment Group Closes Second Fund at Hard Cap of $350M

Connect CRE — Wed, 19 Aug 2026 21:59:56 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Fund Close

Machine Investment Group has successfully closed its second flagship investment vehicle, Machine Real Estate Fund II, at its hard cap of $350 million in primary commitments, with an additional $120 million raised in co-investments. The New York City-based real estate investment platform focuses on opportunistic, distressed, and special situations across the U.S. The fund garnered support from a diverse group of institutional investors, surpassing its debut fund which closed in 2022 with $246 million in primary commitments. The successful fundraising, particularly in a challenging environment for middle-market managers, underscores investor confidence in Machine Investment Group's diversified strategy.
