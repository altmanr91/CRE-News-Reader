BWE originates $69.13 million of loans for multifamily properties in Indiana, Alabama

LOAN

REJournals — Wed, 12 Aug 2026 17:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

BWE originated three loans totaling $69.13 million for a longtime client to finance the acquisition of two new multifamily properties and refinance an existing asset. The financing included two cash-out refinance loans on the borrower's existing Indiana properties, which generated equity for the new acquisitions, and one acquisition loan for a multifamily property in southern Alabama. The BWE team coordinated the complex, multi-faceted transaction, closing all three loans within 33 days to meet critical purchase contract deadlines and provide the borrower with flexible prepayment options.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Loan Amount: $69.1M

Project Notes: The loans included a $21.38 million acquisition loan for a multifamily property in southern Alabama, a $33.75 million cash-out refinance for a multifamily property in southern Indiana, and a $14 million cash-out refinance for a multifamily property in central Indiana.
