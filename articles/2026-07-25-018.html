Greystone Provides $92M in Fannie Mae Loans to Metropolitan Realty

LOAN

Connect CRE — Fri, 24 Jul 2026 20:57:07 +0000

SUMMARY

Greystone has provided $91.85 million in Fannie Mae loans to Metropolitan Realty Group for the refinancing and acquisition of three affordable housing communities in New York. The financing package includes three non-recourse, fixed-rate loans with 10-year terms and five years of interest-only payments. The properties, which include Pine Town Houses in Long Beach and Hudson View II and III in Northern Manhattan, are supported by Section 8 Housing Assistance Payments contracts and will undergo moderate rehabilitation.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Loan Amount: $91.9M

Year Built: 1975

Notable Features: All three properties are supported by long-term Section 8 Housing Assistance Payments (HAP) contracts and are subject to long-term affordability restrictions.

Project Notes: The financing includes three affordable multifamily communities that will each undergo moderate rehabilitation as part of the transaction.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

SPONSOR: Metropolitan Realty Group

SELLER BROKER: Ariel Property Advisors
