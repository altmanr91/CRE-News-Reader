America's War Machine Is Growing, Sparking A Defense Real Estate Boom

Bisnow News Feed — Wed, 29 Jul 2026 10:58:25 -0400

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

The U.S. defense sector is experiencing its most rapid expansion in decades, driven by a strategic shift to a permanent wartime posture and substantial government spending. This has led to a surge in multi-billion dollar contracts and a significant increase in demand for commercial real estate, particularly specialized office and manufacturing facilities. Defense contractors are expanding their footprints and breaking ground on new projects to meet the accelerated need for space, with experts anticipating a prolonged period of growth for defense-oriented real estate.
