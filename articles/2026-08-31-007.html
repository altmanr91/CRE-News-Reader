Davis Acquires Minnesota Outpatient Medical Portfolio for $31.3M

ACQUISITION

REBusinessOnline — Mon, 31 Aug 2026 15:14:37 +0000

SUMMARY

Davis, a Minneapolis-based healthcare real estate firm, has acquired a four-building outpatient medical portfolio totaling 114,346 square feet from Lakeview Clinic for $31.3 million. The transaction included properties across Waconia, Norwood, Chaska, and Watertown, with Lakeview Clinic simultaneously extending its leases at all locations. This acquisition strengthens Davis's presence in the healthcare real estate sector while providing Lakeview Clinic with continued operational stability.
