Kimco Buys Two Broward Retail Centers for $109M

ACQUISITION

Commercial Observer — Fri, 31 Jul 2026 17:25:17 +0000

SUMMARY

Kimco Realty, a prominent owner of grocery-anchored retail centers, has acquired two retail properties in Broward County, Florida, from KPR Centers for a total of $109 million. The acquisition includes Sunshine Plaza in Tamarac for $56 million and Pompano Marketplace in Pompano Beach for $53 million. Both properties are significant retail hubs anchored by major grocery and discount retailers, reinforcing Kimco's presence in the South Florida market.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: retail

Notable Features: Both properties are grocery-anchored retail centers.

Project Notes: KPR Centers previously acquired Sunshine Plaza for $28.5 million in 2014 and Pompano Marketplace for $46.8 million in 2015.
