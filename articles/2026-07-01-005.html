BridgeCity Lends $72M on Long Island City Apartments Build

LOAN

Commercial Observer — Tue, 30 Jun 2026 19:08:20 +0000

SUMMARY

Elmord Management secured $72.3 million in acquisition and construction financing from BridgeCity Capital for a new 161-unit multifamily development in Long Island City, Queens. The project will rise on a former industrial site at 44-68 Vernon Boulevard, which Elmord Management acquired for $28 million. The 16-story building will feature 142,697 square feet of residential and commercial space, transforming a site near a prominent former development proposal.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Address: 44-68 Vernon Boulevard, Long Island City, NY

Units: 161

Loan Amount: $72.3M

Loan Per Unit: $449,068/unit (calculated)

Year Built: 1953

Notable Features: The property is located steps from a warehouse site formerly planned as a major e-commerce headquarters.

Project Notes: The 2026 acquisition price for the site was $28 million. A warehouse-style structure built in 1953 is currently on the site.
