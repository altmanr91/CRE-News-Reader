Berkadia Arranges $19.1M in Acquisition Financing for Build-to-Rent Community in St. Cloud, Florida

LOAN

REBusinessOnline — Mon, 10 Aug 2026 13:53:41 +0000

SUMMARY

Berkadia has secured $19.1 million in acquisition financing for Ibis Park at Harmony West, a newly built, build-to-rent residential community in St. Cloud, Florida. The financing was arranged for Bayshore Investment Partners, which acquired the property from D.R. Horton for $29.4 million. The five-year, fixed-rate loan includes a full-term, interest-only structure.

DATA POINTS

Property Name: Ibis Park at Harmony West
