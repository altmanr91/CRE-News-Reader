The Plan: With a Big Vacancy Looming, Durst Goes Big on Renovating 114 West 47th

Commercial Observer — Wed, 29 Jul 2026 10:00:31 +0000

SUMMARY

Construction

The Durst Organization is undertaking a significant renovation of its 26-story office building at 114 West 47th Street in Midtown Manhattan. The project, prompted by a major tenant's lease expiration in early 2028, aims to modernize the 600,000-square-foot asset with a new lobby, enhanced tenant amenities, private terraces, and updated building systems. The renovation is expected to be completed by 2029, positioning the property as a world-class work environment for visionary companies seeking a new headquarters in a prime Midtown location.
