Crow Holdings secures $380M refi deal for 1,500-unit apartment portfolio

REFINANCE

The Real Deal — Texas — Tue, 14 Jul 2026 19:20:21 +0000

SUMMARY

Crow Holdings secured a $380.2 million refinancing package from AllianceBernstein for a five-property multifamily portfolio totaling 1,549 units across Texas, Colorado, and Virginia. The properties, developed in partnership with Trammell Crow Residential and delivered between 2019 and 2023, are part of the firm's extensive Alexan apartment portfolio. This refinancing comes as multifamily markets, particularly in Texas, show signs of stabilization after a period of significant new supply and rent declines.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Austin's rental rates for new leases were down 3.9 percent year over year at the end of June, an improvement from a 7.5 percent year-over-year drop in March.
