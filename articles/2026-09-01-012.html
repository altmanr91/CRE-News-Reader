Habitat reaches key milestones at two Chicago mixed-income developments

CONSTRUCTION

REJournals — Tue, 01 Sep 2026 13:46:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Habitat has reached significant milestones at two mixed-income developments in Chicago, underscoring the strong demand for attainable housing integrated with community services and retail. The first residential phase of the LeClaire Courts redevelopment in Garfield Ridge has achieved financial closing, initiating construction for 183 mixed-income apartments, 90% of which will be affordable. Concurrently, The Junction at OC Living, the second residential phase of the Ogden Commons development in North Lawndale, has reached full lease-up, demonstrating sustained demand for quality affordable housing in the area. These projects reflect Habitat's commitment to creating vibrant, community-centered developments that provide essential amenities like healthcare, childcare, and retail alongside housing.

Phase I: 183 apartments

Total Project (All Phases): up to 700 residential units

Site Area: 32 acres

Notable Features: The first phase includes two six-story mixed-use buildings with studio, one-, two-, and three-bedroom floor plans, and will feature a day care center operated by Chicago Commons.

Project Notes: The 32-acre site was formerly the LeClaire Courts public housing complex, demolished in 2011. Funding sources include a $27 million capital loan from the Chicago Housing Authority, $9.5 million in city multifamily program funds, $5.5 million in tax-increment financing, $33.7 million in Low-Income Housing Tax Credit equity, $1.25 million in Illinois Affordable Housing Tax Credit equity, and a $1.2 million deferred developer fee.
