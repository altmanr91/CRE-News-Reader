Rexford Plans Up to $2B in Sales After Posting $507M Loss in Q2

Commercial Observer — Fri, 24 Jul 2026 17:00:17 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Earnings Report

Rexford Industrial Realty, a Los Angeles-based REIT, is significantly increasing its disposition strategy, planning up to $2 billion in sales of "noncore assets" totaling approximately 8 million square feet. This move, a sharp increase from previous forecasts, aims to repay maturing debt, repurchase shares, and redeploy capital into higher-yielding investments, with the majority of sales expected to close by year-end. The company reported a $506.9 million net loss in Q2 2026 due to noncash impairment charges related to these dispositions, but core FFO rose to $141.4 million, and total rental income reached $243 million. Despite negative rent spreads on renewals, Rexford maintains strong occupancy and plans to start 1.2 million square feet of new projects this year.

DATA POINTS

Planned dispositions: $2 billion

Square footage of planned dispositions: 8 million square feet

Q2 2026 Net Loss: $506.9 million

Q2 2026 Core FFO: $141.4 million

Q2 2026 Total Rental Income: $243 million

Q2 2026 Leases Executed: 117 leases covering 2.1 million square feet

Q2 2026 Comparable Rents (net-effective): declined 2.8%

Q2 2026 Comparable Rents (cash basis): declined 11.3%

2026 Stock Repurchases: $300 million

New Stock Repurchase Program: $1 billion

Full-year core FFO guidance: $2.38 to $2.43 per share

Same-property NOI outlook: decline of 0.25% to 1.25%

SPONSOR PIPELINE

16425 Gale Avenue: New development in City of Industry, expected to be complete by late 2027.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Supply of industrial space under construction and expected to be delivered to the market is at multi-decade lows. Rexford expects continued pressure on rent spreads in 2027, but less severe than current declines.
