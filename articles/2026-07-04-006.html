Cravey Real Estate Services brokers sale of 8,750-square-foot retail center in Corpus Christi

SALE

REJournals — Fri, 03 Jul 2026 16:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Cravey Real Estate Services brokered the sale of King's Court, an 8,750-square-foot retail center in Corpus Christi, Texas. The property, built in 2005, was acquired from Vista Bella Investments, LP. The center benefits from its location adjacent to a desirable residential community and across from a popular cinema, serving a growing trade area with strong demographics and continued commercial investment.
