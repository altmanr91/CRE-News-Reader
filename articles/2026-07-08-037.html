Santa Clara County property values hit record high $760B

The Real Deal — San Francisco — Tue, 07 Jul 2026 18:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Property Values

Santa Clara County's taxable property values reached a record $760.1 billion for the 2026-27 fiscal year, marking a 4.7% increase year-over-year. This growth was primarily driven by changes in ownership, particularly residential sales, which contributed $16.9 billion, and inflation adjustments adding $14 billion. New construction also played a role, contributing $4.9 billion, though several major development projects remain delayed. The county faces challenges from a lagging office market, with vacancy rates near 20% and a significant volume of commercial property assessment appeals that could impact future tax revenues.

DATA POINTS

Total taxable property value (FY 2026-27): $760.1 billion

Year-over-year increase in assessment roll: $34.4 billion (4.7%)

Value added by ownership changes: $16.9 billion

Value added by inflation adjustments: $14 billion

Value added by new construction: $4.9 billion

Residential sales contribution to ownership transfers: 82%

Office vacancy rate: 20%

Assessed value under appeal: $153.6 billion

Commercial properties share of appeals: 98%

Properties in decline-in-value status (Proposition 8): 24,727
