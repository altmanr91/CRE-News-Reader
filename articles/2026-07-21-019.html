Buckling scare puts MetroLoft’s ‘scoop-and-stack’ playbook under microscope

The Real Deal — New York — Mon, 20 Jul 2026 18:30:59 +0000

SUMMARY

Development / Structural Incident

A structural failure at MetroLoft's conversion of Pfizer's former Midtown headquarters at 235 East 42nd Street in New York City has drawn attention to the developer's "scoop-and-stack" redevelopment technique. This method involves hollowing out lower floors to create lightwells and transferring development potential to new floors added at the top, addressing the challenge of deep floor plates in postwar office-to-residential conversions. While the incident is under investigation, the developer attributed the buckling to insufficiently reinforced columns rather than a flaw in the overall concept, with missing reinforcing steel plates identified as a potential cause.
