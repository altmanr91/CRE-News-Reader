MidPen floats 110-unit affordable housing project on former Wells Fargo site in Sunnyvale

The Real Deal — San Francisco — Wed, 15 Jul 2026 23:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Development

MidPen Housing, a Foster City-based nonprofit, has proposed a 110-unit affordable housing development at 295 South Mathilda Avenue in Sunnyvale, California. The project, planned for a former Wells Fargo bank site, will feature income-restricted units, with 25% designated for previously unhoused individuals. This development aims to address the city's housing needs, particularly for tech and AI workers, and aligns with Sunnyvale's state mandate to plan for nearly 12,000 new housing units by 2031.

SPONSOR PIPELINE

215 Bordeaux Drive: 265-unit residential building proposed by Beam Reach, replacing former Google-occupied offices.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Area median income for Santa Clara County is $143,850 for a one-person household. The City of Sunnyvale is mandated by the state to plan for 11,966 new housing units by 2031.
