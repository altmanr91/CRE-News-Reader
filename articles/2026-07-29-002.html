Bill Ackman Buys 125 West End Avenue for $260M Brain Research Institute

ACQUISITION

Commercial Observer — Tue, 28 Jul 2026 20:48:50 +0000

SUMMARY

The Pershing Square Foundation, the philanthropic arm of Bill Ackman's hedge fund, has acquired 125 West End Avenue for $188 million, with plans to establish a brain research institute. This acquisition is part of a larger $260 million initiative that includes an in-contract purchase of the neighboring 320 West 66th Street, aiming to create a 700,000-square-foot life sciences and office campus. The foundation is partnering with Mount Sinai Hospital System on the development of the research institute.

Notable Features: The building, also known as West End Labs, is largely vacant and was previously part of the 3.4-acre West End Campus.

Project Notes: The acquisition is part of a larger $260 million plan to establish the Ackman Oxman Institute, a brain research center. The overall plan includes an in-contract sale for the neighboring 320 West 66th Street for approximately $70 million, bringing the total campus to an estimated 700,000 square feet. The West End Campus was previously sold to Taconic by Silverstein Properties for $230 million in 2019 and repositioned as a research and laboratory complex.
