Citi Lends $45M on Luxury East Village Multifamily

LOAN

Connect CRE — Tue, 30 Jun 2026 20:01:19 +0000

SUMMARY

JLL Capital Markets arranged $44.5 million in financing from Citi for Benchmark Real Estate Group's luxury multifamily property at 194 E. 2nd St. in Manhattan's East Village. The loan supports the 61-unit building, which also includes ground-floor retail. Benchmark Real Estate Group acquired the property in 2024 and completed extensive renovations to the residential units, common areas, lobby, and amenity spaces, positioning it as a high-quality asset in a market with strong demand and limited new supply.
