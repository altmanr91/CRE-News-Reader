Buyer scores $2M judgment against Mandarin Oriental Boca developer

The Real Deal — Miami — Mon, 13 Jul 2026 23:45:11 +0000

SUMMARY

CRE Legal / Regulatory

A Palm Beach County Circuit Court judge has ruled in favor of Douglas and Debra Jacobs, awarding them a $2 million judgment against Penn-Florida affiliate Via Mizner Owner III. The lawsuit alleged that the developer failed to complete their condo unit in the Mandarin Oriental Boca Raton project by the contractual deadline of December 31, 2025. This ruling allows the buyers to terminate their purchase agreement and seek a refund of their deposit, with the judge also permitting them to demand payment from the title companies holding their escrow funds. The developer plans to file a motion for a rehearing, citing a conflicting ruling in a separate case.
