Marx Realty Secures Two Leases at 10 Grand Central

LEASE

Connect CRE — Tue, 13 Oct 2026 20:34:34 +0000

SUMMARY

Marx Realty has secured two new leases at 10 Grand Central, a hospitality-infused tower in the Grand Central neighborhood. Brenton Point will occupy 9,000 square feet on the 24th floor, and Cornerstone will take 5,000 square feet on the second floor. These leases underscore the building's appeal to a diverse range of companies seeking a high-quality, service-oriented environment with extensive amenities.
