Tishman Speyer Sells Manhattan Office Building for $132.5M

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Wed, 28 Oct 2026 14:12:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Tishman Speyer has sold 148 Lafayette Street, a 12-story office building in Manhattan's SoHo neighborhood, for $132.5 million. The 153,000-square-foot property, originally built in 1913 and renovated in 2017, was fully leased at the time of sale. Shorenstein Investment Advisers acquired the asset, which Tishman Speyer had purchased in June 2025 for $105.5 million.
