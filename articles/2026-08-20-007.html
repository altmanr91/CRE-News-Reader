CBL Properties Closes $50.6M Sale of York Town Center in Pennsylvania

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Wed, 19 Aug 2026 14:35:00 +0000

SUMMARY

CBL Properties and its joint venture partner have sold York Town Center, an open-air regional retail center in York, Pennsylvania, for $50.6 million. The transaction was completed at an 8.5 percent capitalization rate. The center features a strong tenant mix across 297,000 square feet.
