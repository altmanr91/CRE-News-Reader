Tyko Capital Lends $322M on Integra Investment’s West Palm Beach Marina Project

LOAN

Commercial Observer — Mon, 31 Aug 2026 12:00:51 +0000

SUMMARY

Tyko Capital has provided a $322 million acquisition and construction loan to Integra Investments for the Safe Harbor Rybovich marina project in West Palm Beach, FL. The financing will support the development of the 15-acre marina located at 4200 North Flagler Drive. This significant loan underscores the continued investment in South Florida's waterfront properties.
