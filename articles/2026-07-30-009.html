Matthews Negotiates Sale of 23,957 SF Retail Building in Bridgeport, Texas

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Wed, 29 Jul 2026 15:02:42 +0000

SUMMARY

Matthews, a Nashville-based brokerage firm, has facilitated the sale of a 23,957-square-foot retail building in Bridgeport, Texas. The recently constructed property is fully occupied by a national retailer under a 15-year lease. The buyer, a California-based investor, utilized a 1031 exchange for the acquisition.
