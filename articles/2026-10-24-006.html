Joint Venture Completes 873-Bed Student Housing Project in College Station

CONSTRUCTION

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 23 Oct 2026 14:44:32 +0000

SUMMARY

A joint venture comprising The Dinerstein Cos., Up Campus Student Living, and Harrison Street Asset Management has completed Aspire Reserve, an 873-bed student housing development in College Station, TX. The 19-story property is located near the Texas A&M University campus within the Northgate District. It offers a variety of unit types and extensive amenities designed for student residents.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: student housing

Property Name: Aspire Reserve

Address: 100 Church Ave, College Station, TX*

Beds: 873

Notable Features: Located within the Northgate District near Texas A&M University campus; amenities include two pools, study spaces, golf/gaming simulator, resident game lounges, beauty bar, retail market, and indoor/outdoor fitness center with yoga and spin studios.

* Address sourced separately, not in original article.
