Gillett Commercial to Develop Spec Office, Flex Building in Allen, Texas

DEVELOPMENT

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 24 Jul 2026 15:06:31 +0000

SUMMARY

Gillett Commercial, in partnership with the Allen Economic Development Corp., is developing Allen Place, a speculative office and flex building in Allen, Texas. The project is initially planned for 89,032 square feet, with an expandable mezzanine level that could bring the total to 109,032 square feet. Construction is slated to begin later this year and is expected to take approximately one year to complete.

DATA POINTS

Address: 6976 Allen Pl Dr, Fort Worth, TX*

Completion: late 2027
