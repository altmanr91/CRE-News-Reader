IPA Negotiates Sale of 188-Unit Apartment Complex in Arlington

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Wed, 12 Aug 2026 15:08:59 +0000

SUMMARY

Institutional Property Advisors (IPA) has negotiated the sale of Flintridge, a 188-unit apartment complex in Arlington, Texas. Apogee Capital acquired the garden-style property from an undisclosed lender. The transaction highlights continued investor interest in well-located multifamily assets in the Dallas-Fort Worth metroplex.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Flintridge

Address: 3106 Flintridge Dr, Arlington, TX*

Units: 188

Year Built: 1979

Notable Features: Garden-style apartment complex with a pool, clubhouse, business center, and onsite laundry facilities.

* Address sourced separately, not in original article.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: Apogee Capital

SELLER BROKER: Institutional Property Advisors (IPA) — Michael Ware; Drew Kile; Joey Tumminello; William Hubbard
