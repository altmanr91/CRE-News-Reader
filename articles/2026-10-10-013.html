Colliers Represents ClarkDietrich in Anchor Lease at Ohio Office Development

LEASE

Connect CRE — Fri, 09 Oct 2026 21:31:18 +0000

SUMMARY

Colliers represented ClarkDietrich in a 32,558-square-foot office lease, securing nearly three-quarters of a new office building at Montgomery Quarter, a mixed-use development in Cincinnati, Ohio. This transaction highlights the continued demand for well-located, high-quality office space in the Cincinnati market, attracting tenants making long-term commitments. The new office provides access to retail and dining amenities, structured parking, and a modern workplace designed for collaboration.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Address: 9260 Montgomery Road, Cincinnati, OH

Size: 32,558 SF

Completion: October 2027

Notable Features: Located along the Montgomery Road corridor in Cincinnati’s northeastern suburbs, offering access to retail and dining amenities, structured parking, and a modern workplace environment.
