Newmark Negotiates $30M Sale of Trophy Retail Property in Downtown Chicago

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Mon, 12 Oct 2026 14:56:19 +0000

SUMMARY

Newmark has brokered the $30 million sale of Block 37, a prominent 277,654-square-foot retail property in downtown Chicago. The five-story asset, anchored by a strong tenant mix and including a four-level underground parking garage, was sold by CIM Group. The property is a key component of the larger Block 37 mixed-use development.
