Lefrak Offloads Brooklyn Rent-Stabilized Assets to HF NYC for $38 million

SALE

Commercial Observer — Tue, 30 Jun 2026 21:55:57 +0000

SUMMARY

LeFrak has sold its Parkway Portfolio, comprising four rent-stabilized multifamily properties with over 300 units in Brooklyn's Bensonhurst and Midwood neighborhoods, to HF NYC for $38 million. J.P. Morgan Chase provided a $24.8 million acquisition loan for the purchase. The transaction comes shortly after the New York City Rent Guidelines Board voted to freeze rents on one- and two-year leases in rent-stabilized buildings.

DATA POINTS
