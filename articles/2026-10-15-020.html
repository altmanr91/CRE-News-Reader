AI Spurs Unprecedented Proptech Interest, Along with Questions (VIDEO)

Connect CRE — Wed, 14 Oct 2026 20:30:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

The emergence of artificial intelligence has ignited unprecedented interest in proptech, drawing more real estate companies to technology conferences than ever before, according to Michael Beckerman, CEO of CREtech. Despite this heightened engagement, there is significant uncertainty within the industry regarding the optimal path for AI adoption, with companies weighing whether to implement off-the-shelf solutions, develop proprietary tools using platforms like Claude or Gemini, or pursue a hybrid approach. This discussion highlights the current state of proptech investment and the long-term implications of AI for commercial real estate firms and their workforce.
