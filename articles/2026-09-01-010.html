Northmarq Arranges $23.5M Sale of Two-Property Multifamily Portfolio in Northern California

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Tue, 01 Sep 2026 13:48:49 +0000

SUMMARY

Northmarq has facilitated the $23.5 million sale of a two-property multifamily portfolio in Ukiah, California. Ridge Capital Investments sold the 121-unit portfolio to an undisclosed buyer. The properties, Sierra Sunset Village and Alderwood Apartments, offer a mix of two- and three-bedroom residences with various amenities.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Sierra Sunset Village and Alderwood Apartments

Address: 505-531 Capps Lane and 1416 and 1450 S. State St, Ukiah, CA

Units: 121

Sale Price: $23.5M

Notable Features: Sierra Sunset Village features a swimming pool, fitness center, and outdoor picnic area. Alderwood Apartments offers a resort-style swimming pool, covered garage parking, and outdoor grilling areas.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

SELLER: Ridge Capital Investments

SELLER BROKER: Northmarq — Zach LeBeouf; Anthony Pappageorge
