Real Estate Firm Yuco, AI Company Elite Each Take 7K SF at 757 Third Avenue

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Tue, 21 Jul 2026 21:24:38 +0000

SUMMARY

Yuco Management, a real estate firm, and Elite, an AI-powered legal software company, each signed leases for 6,960 square feet at 757 Third Avenue in Midtown East. Yuco Management will relocate its offices to the 27th floor, while Elite will establish its first New York City office on the 22nd floor. The leases reflect strong Midtown leasing fundamentals and the value tenants place on well-executed repositioning strategies, according to the landlord's broker.

Project Notes: New York Life Real Estate Investors acquired the property in October 2025 through a deed-in-lieu of foreclosure sale and is undertaking cosmetic renovations to the lobby and rolling out a new prebuilt office program.
