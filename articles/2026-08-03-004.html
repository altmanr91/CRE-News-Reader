MARKET INTELLIGENCE

The Department of Buildings halted construction at two prominent office-to-residential conversion projects in Manhattan (750 Third Avenue and 222 Broadway) last week, indicating heightened scrutiny in the conversion space. The state's 485-x development program for new housing is facing challenges due to inflation and rising costs, making margins slimmer. The Mamdani administration aims for 200,000 new housing units and the preservation of another 200,000 units. The Federal Reserve, chaired by Kevin Warsh, kept interest rates steady to hedge against inflation.
