Marcus & Millichap Brokers Sale of 14,000 SF Medical Office Building in West Houston

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 04 Sep 2026 12:30:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Marcus & Millichap has brokered the sale of a 14,000-square-foot medical office building in West Houston. The property, constructed in 2024, was approximately 40 percent leased at the time of sale. The undisclosed buyer intends to occupy the remaining vacant space.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: medical office

Address: 19304 Katy Freeway, Houston, TX

Size: 14,000 SF

Occupancy at Sale: 40.0%

Year Built: 2024

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

SELLER BROKER: Marcus & Millichap — Joseph Jaques; Alex Wolansky; Ron Hebert; Gus Lagos

TENANTS AT PROPERTY

Excel Urgent Care
