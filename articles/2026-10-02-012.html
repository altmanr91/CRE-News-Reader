Essex Realty Group brokers sale of 91-unit mixed-use property in Chicago’s Andersonville neighborhood

SALE

REJournals — Fri, 02 Oct 2026 14:56:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Essex Realty Group has brokered the sale of a 91-unit mixed-use property in Chicago's Andersonville neighborhood. The asset, which includes 81 residential units and 10 retail spaces, was held by the same owner for over 40 years. The property offers significant upside potential due to residential rents being substantially below market rates, with additional value-add opportunities in the retail component.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: mixed-use

Address: 5154 N. Clark Street, Chicago, IL

Size: 63,800 SF

Notable Features: Features recent capital improvements including new electric service, modernized elevator, newer windows, and a newer boiler. Residential rents are 40% to 60% below market.

Project Notes: Property held by the same ownership for over 40 years.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

SELLER BROKER: Essex Realty Group, LLC — Brian Karmowski, Principal
