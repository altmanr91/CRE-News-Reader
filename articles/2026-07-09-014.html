RAFA Racing Club, Maximo Capital, XSpace Group to develop five multi-use industrial condominium projects

DEVELOPMENT

REJournals — Wed, 08 Jul 2026 15:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

RAFA Racing Club, Maximo Capital, and XSpace Group have formed a joint venture to develop five luxury multi-use industrial condominium projects across the United States. These projects will feature XSpace's premium flex industrial condominiums on upper levels and a RAFA Racing Club Social & Performance Centre on the ground floor, offering a members-only clubhouse, event space, and automotive lifestyle hub. The first phase of development will target markets including Miami/Boca Raton, Charlotte/Mooresville, Dallas/Fort Worth, Scottsdale/Phoenix, and Los Angeles, with total projected sales revenue of $250 million.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial condominium

Total Project Cost: $250.0M

Notable Features: Each project will feature a RAFA Racing Club Social & Performance Centre on the ground floor and premium ownable flex condominiums on upper levels, designed for automotive collectors and enthusiasts.

Project Notes: The $250 million figure represents total projected sales revenue for the five-project first phase.
