CrestMarc, Midloch Acquire 294-Unit Apartment Complex in Huntsville, Texas

ACQUISITION

REBusinessOnline — Wed, 09 Sep 2026 15:35:07 +0000

SUMMARY

A joint venture between CrestMarc and Midloch Investment Partners has acquired The Forum at Sam Houston, a 294-unit apartment complex in Huntsville, Texas. The property, built in 2010, serves both general residents and students of Sam Houston State University. The transaction details, including the seller and sale price, were not disclosed.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: The Forum at Sam Houston

Address: 3019 Sam Houston Ave, Huntsville, TX*

Units: 294

Year Built: 2010

Notable Features: Amenities include a pool, sand volleyball court, basketball court, clubhouse, study lounge, fitness center, bark park, complimentary Starbucks Coffee bar, and private shuttle to the SHSU campus.

Project Notes: The property functions as student housing for Sam Houston State University.

* Address sourced separately, not in original article.
