Houston Industrial Market Stays Strong

Connect CRE — Mon, 05 Oct 2026 16:46:19 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Industrial Report

Houston's industrial market experienced a significant surge in the third quarter of 2026, marking its strongest leasing activity in over four years. This acceleration was driven by a notable increase in large transactions, with nineteen leases exceeding 250,000 square feet signed during the quarter. The market also saw robust net absorption, indicating strong demand for industrial space.
