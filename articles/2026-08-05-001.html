The Plan: Architecture Firm Corgan Redesigned Its Own Empire State Building Office

Commercial Observer — Wed, 05 Aug 2026 10:00:25 +0000

SUMMARY

Construction

Architecture firm Corgan has completed a significant renovation and expansion of its New York City office on the 54th floor of the Empire State Building. The firm increased its footprint from 18,714 square feet to 26,035 square feet, accommodating nearly 150 employees in a full-floor space. The redesign, conceptualized as "Echoes of Deco," integrates Art Deco themes and original building elements with modern amenities and a strong focus on sustainability and employee well-being.
