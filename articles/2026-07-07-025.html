Negroni restaurants seek bankruptcy with $330K-plus in rent claims

The Real Deal — Miami — Mon, 06 Jul 2026 16:57:50 +0000

SUMMARY

CRE Legal / Regulatory

Negroni Bistro & Sushi Bar's Brickell and Doral locations have filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy, citing over $330,000 in rent claims. The Brickell restaurant, which opened less than a year ago, owes its landlord $274,000, while the Doral location owes $57,000. The company's attorney stated the bankruptcy was initiated to restructure finances after taking out merchant cash advance loans post-pandemic, with a reorganization plan expected within months.

DATA POINTS

Brickell rent claims: $274,000

Doral rent claims: $57,000

Brickell asking rents Q1: $150-$200 per square foot

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Asking retail rents in Brickell ranged from $150 to $200 per square foot in the first quarter. Brickell has experienced several restaurant closures this year.
