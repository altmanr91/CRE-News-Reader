Opus starts construction of 65,000-square-foot industrial facility in Omaha

CONSTRUCTION

REJournals — Mon, 12 Oct 2026 14:10:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Opus has commenced construction on a 65,000-square-foot build-to-suit industrial facility in Omaha, Nebraska, which will serve as the new headquarters and operations base for Viking Industrial Painting. Opus is acting as both the developer and design-builder for the project, with completion anticipated in July 2027.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Address: 9225 N. 108th St., Omaha, NE

Size: 65,000 SF

Completion: July 2027

Notable Features: Located in Rainwood Industrial Park.

Project Notes: Build-to-suit facility for Viking Industrial Painting, which specializes in inspecting, painting, and maintaining municipal water towers.
