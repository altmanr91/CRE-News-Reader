Lee & Associates closes 10,500-square-foot industrial lease in Fort Worth

LEASE

REJournals — Tue, 29 Sep 2026 13:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Lee & Associates Dallas-Fort Worth has facilitated a new industrial lease for a 10,500-square-foot service facility in Fort Worth, Texas. The tenant, Paragon Pro Rentals, secured the space at IndustriParks820 Lot 1, with Lee & Associates representing both the tenant and the landlord, CanTex Capital.
