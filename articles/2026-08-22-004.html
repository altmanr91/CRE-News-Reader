Now Is the Time to Buy in Commercial Real Estate: Morgan Stanley

Commercial Observer — Fri, 21 Aug 2026 16:17:12 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

Morgan Stanley Real Estate Investing's mid-year 2026 outlook suggests that now is an opportune time to invest in U.S. commercial real estate, despite economic headwinds and high Treasury yields. The firm argues that a four-year downturn has led to repricing below replacement costs, creating attractive entry points for prime assets. This perspective is supported by dramatically reduced supply across all sectors and a strong macroeconomic backdrop, with U.S. GDP growth and increased transaction activity, capital raising, and debt originations in CRE. Morgan Stanley also highlights that real estate can perform well in sustained high-rate environments once cap rates have adjusted, and that elevated construction and financing costs will continue to constrain new development, supporting rent growth and value appreciation in existing assets. The report specifically identifies industrial, retail, and senior housing as sectors poised for strong performance due to megatrends like supply chain reorganization, AI-driven manufacturing, and aging demographics.
