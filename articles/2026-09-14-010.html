BridgeInvest Provides $87.9M Acquisition Loan for Industrial Facility in San Francisco

LOAN

REBusinessOnline — Mon, 14 Sep 2026 14:15:51 +0000

SUMMARY

BridgeInvest has provided an $87.9 million acquisition loan for 300 Kansas Street, a six-story Class A research and development property in San Francisco's Mission Bay submarket. The sponsors, Lincoln Property Co. and Sabal Investment Holdings, will continue to lease and stabilize the 152,000-square-foot facility, which was constructed in 2023.
