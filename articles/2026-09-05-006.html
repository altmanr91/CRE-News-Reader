Partnership Underway on Seniors Housing Project in East Brunswick, New Jersey

CONSTRUCTION

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 04 Sep 2026 14:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

A joint venture between National Development and an affiliate of Benchmark Senior Living has commenced construction on Benchmark at East Brunswick, a new seniors housing project in Central New Jersey. The community will offer 87 units and 100 beds for assisted living and memory care residents, featuring a range of amenities. JLL secured $35.1 million in construction financing for the project, which is slated for completion in 2028.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: seniors housing

Property Name: Benchmark at East Brunswick

Units: 87

Beds: 100

Completion: 2028

Notable Features: Amenities will include multiple dining venues, a theater, fitness center, salon and outdoor spaces.
