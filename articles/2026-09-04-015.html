JLL Arranges Refi for Maine Self-Storage Asset

REFINANCE

Connect CRE — Thu, 03 Sep 2026 22:18:06 +0000

SUMMARY

JLL Capital Markets Debt Advisory team has arranged a five-year refinancing loan for a recently constructed self-storage facility in South Portland, ME. The loan was secured through a regional bank on behalf of the borrower, The Ardent Companies. The property, completed in 2022, has achieved high occupancy, reflecting the strong demand in one of New England's most robust self-storage markets.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: self-storage

Property Name: Extra Space South Portland

Address: 193 Western Ave., South Portland, ME

Size: 90,000 SF

Year Built: 2022

Notable Features: Features climate-controlled units with 24/7 access and is located just south of Portland International Jetport.

Project Notes: Managed by Extra Space Storage Inc.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

SPONSOR: The Ardent Companies

MORTGAGE BROKER: JLL

LENDER: regional bank

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

The trade area for the property includes over 125,000 households within a 30-minute drive, with average household incomes exceeding $133,000 in the surrounding area.
