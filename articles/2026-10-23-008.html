JLL Brokers $56.9M Sale of Central New Jersey Distribution Facility

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 23 Oct 2026 13:45:19 +0000

SUMMARY

JLL has brokered the sale of a 223,977-square-foot distribution facility in Monmouth Junction, New Jersey, for $56.9 million. CenterPoint Properties sold the single-tenant property to Denver-based investment firm Sagard Real Estate. The facility offers robust logistics features, including 28 loading doors and ample truck and car parking.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Address: 27 Distribution Way, Monmouth Junction, NJ

Size: 223,977 SF

Sale Price: $56.9M

$/SF: $254/SF (calculated)

Notable Features: Clear height of 22 feet, 28 loading doors, 225-foot truck court depths, parking for 57 trailers and 126 cars.
