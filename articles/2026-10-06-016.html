CREFC Sentiment Index Reaches Lowest Level in Three Years

Connect CRE — Tue, 06 Oct 2026 14:49:30 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Sentiment Index

The CRE Finance Council's (CREFC) Third-Quarter 2026 Board of Governors Sentiment Index has dropped to its lowest point in three years, falling 17.5% to 83.3. This broad-based decline reflects significant concerns among members regarding the negative impact of mortgage and capitalization rates on commercial real estate finance businesses, with 92% of respondents anticipating adverse effects. The sentiment also indicates a growing pessimism about the U.S. economy's performance over the next 12 months, with 62% expecting a worsening outlook, driven by worries extending beyond immediate Federal Reserve decisions to broader issues like inflation, fiscal deficits, and long-term borrowing costs.
