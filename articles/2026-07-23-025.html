Historic cottage near Citadel’s Brickell HQ to move to Coconut Grove

ACQUISITION

The Real Deal — Miami — Wed, 22 Jul 2026 23:52:55 +0000

SUMMARY

Ken Griffin is set to acquire the historic Dr. Jackson Medical Office cottage in Brickell for $2.98 million, pending City of Miami commission approval. The 121-year-old property, currently leased by Dade Heritage Trust, will be relocated to Coconut Grove, with Griffin funding its preservation and move. This transaction is part of Griffin's broader development plans for his Citadel headquarters site in Brickell, where the cottage occupies a key parcel.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Property Name: Dr. Jackson Medical Office

Address: 190 Southeast 12th Terrace, Miami, FL

Sale Price: $3.0M

Year Built: 1905

Notable Features: Historic wood-frame cottage, former office and surgical center of Miami's first resident physician.

Project Notes: The property was originally located on Flagler Street in downtown Miami and moved to the Brickell Bay Drive area in 1917. The buyer previously arranged to move the William Jennings Bryan bayfront home from his Brickell Avenue estate to Vizcaya Museum & Gardens.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: Ken Griffin

SELLER: City of Miami

TENANT: Dade Heritage Trust — Christine Rupp, Executive Director
