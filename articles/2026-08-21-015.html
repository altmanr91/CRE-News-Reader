Inflation Is Down. But Consumers Aren’t Out of the Woods Yet

Connect CRE — Fri, 21 Aug 2026 05:01:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

A recent report from Marcus & Millichap indicates that while inflation is showing signs of moderation, consumers continue to face economic headwinds. The Consumer Price Index (CPI) and core CPI both saw slight month-over-month decreases in July, aligning with market expectations and potentially easing pressure on the Federal Reserve to raise interest rates. However, housing costs, particularly Owners' Equivalent Rent (OER) and apartment rents, remain significant contributors to inflation, and real average hourly earnings declined, suggesting ongoing pressure on consumer purchasing power. Additionally, rising food prices, mixed retail fundamentals, and a softening leisure and hospitality sector point to continued challenges for household budgets, despite a resilient consumer environment where spending remains selective.

DATA POINTS

CPI increase year-over-year (July): 3.4%

Core CPI increase year-over-year (July): 2.5%

Apartment rent increase year-over-year (July): 1.3%

Real average hourly earnings decline (July): 0.1%

Grocery price increase year-over-year (July): 2.7%

Restaurant price increase year-over-year (July): 3.4%

Retail vacancy vs. historical average: 60 basis points below

Leisure and Hospitality job losses (July): 40,000

Hotel occupancy decline (June): 20 basis points
