U.S. REITs Raise $20B in Q3 2026; IPO Market Cools

Connect CRE — Tue, 13 Oct 2026 15:47:20 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Capital Markets Report

U.S. REITs raised nearly $20 billion in the third quarter of 2026 through secondary debt and equity offerings, maintaining a similar pace to the previous quarter. Year-to-date capital raising reached $65.7 billion, comparable to the $67.9 billion raised through Q3 2025. The majority of Q3's capital came from debt offerings, while the REIT IPO market saw no activity after a busy first half of the year. Rising borrowing costs were noted, with the weighted average coupon for REIT debt increasing to 5.4% in Q3 from 4.7% in Q1.
