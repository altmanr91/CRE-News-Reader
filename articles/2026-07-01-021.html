The Housing Bill Sitting On Trump's Desk Isn't The Game-Changer Many Hoped — Or Feared

Bisnow News Feed — Tue, 30 Jun 2026 13:48:49 -0400

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

The 21st Century Road to Housing Act, currently awaiting presidential signature, is considered a significant housing policy, though experts believe it will not be a game-changer for the commercial real estate sector. While it includes provisions to streamline environmental reviews, offer grants for increased housing supply, and remove the permanent chassis mandate for manufactured housing to reduce costs, its impact on the broader multifamily market is expected to be limited. The bill also raises the public welfare investment cap for banks and reauthorizes HUD's HOME Program, which are seen as positive signals for affordable housing development. However, some industry leaders view these measures as incremental rather than transformative, with one CEO noting they are more of a "tangent" for multifamily clients. The legislation also includes a cap on institutional ownership of single-family homes, though experts suggest there are exemptions and workarounds, particularly for new build-to-rent construction.

DATA POINTS

Annual competitive grant program for municipalities: $200M

Reduction in manufactured housing construction costs: Up to $10K per unit

Increase in public welfare investment cap for banks: From 15% to 20%

Cap on single-family homes owned by institutional investors: 350 homes (with exemptions)
