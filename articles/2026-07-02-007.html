IRG, PREP Funds to Renovate 160,000 SF Industrial Property in Dayton, Ohio

ACQUISITION

REBusinessOnline — Wed, 01 Jul 2026 13:47:38 +0000

SUMMARY

Industrial Realty Group LLC (IRG) and PREP Funds have acquired an industrial property at 5870 Poe Ave. in Dayton, Ohio, with plans to redevelop it into the Dayton Commerce Center. The 160,000-square-foot asset includes a main building with office space, multiple docks and drive-in doors, and heavy crane infrastructure, along with a smaller standalone building. Construction upgrades are set to begin immediately, and the ownership is actively seeking tenants for the repositioned space.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Property Name: Dayton Commerce Center

Address: 5870 Poe Ave, Dayton, OH

Size: 160,000 SF

Notable Features: Main building features 40,000 square feet of office space, six docks, four drive-in doors, and heavy crane infrastructure. Both structures offer 18-foot clear heights.
