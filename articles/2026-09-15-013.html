Construction moving along on Seefried’s 502,000-square-foot industrial development in Des Plaines

CONSTRUCTION

REJournals — Tue, 15 Sep 2026 10:03:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Seefried Industrial Properties is progressing with the construction of a three-building, 520,000-square-foot Class-A industrial development in Des Plaines, IL, following the demolition of three obsolete office buildings. This speculative project, located in the O'Hare submarket, aims to provide modern logistics, distribution, and manufacturing space in a supply-constrained market. The development highlights Seefried's expertise in complex infill redevelopment, transforming existing sites into facilities designed for contemporary industrial users.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Size: 520,000 SF

Completion: Q2 2027

Site Area: 35 acres

Notable Features: Class-A industrial development designed for modern logistics, distribution, and manufacturing users, featuring efficient site circulation, modern loading configurations, and trailer parking.

Project Notes: Redevelopment of approximately 35 acres at the Northeast corner of Algonquin Road and Mount Prospect Road, transforming three functionally obsolete office buildings into a modern industrial park. The project is being developed on a speculative basis.
