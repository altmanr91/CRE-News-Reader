Benderson Expands to Hawaii, Acquires 130,000 SF Retail Asset in Honolulu

ACQUISITION

REBusinessOnline — Tue, 20 Oct 2026 13:55:26 +0000

SUMMARY

Benderson Development has expanded its national portfolio by acquiring a 130,000-square-foot retail property in Honolulu, marking its entry into the Hawaiian market. The asset includes a two-story parking structure with over 600 stalls. The terms of the transaction were not disclosed.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: retail

Address: 421 Alakawa St, Honolulu, HI

Size: 130,000 SF

Notable Features: Includes an adjoining two-story parking structure with more than 600 stalls.
