Oracle heir, “Boonville” author list Pac Heights compound for $65M as SF’s mansion shortage tightens

The Real Deal — San Francisco — Fri, 17 Jul 2026 21:54:06 +0000

SUMMARY

Property Listing / For Sale

Nicola Miner, daughter of Oracle co-founder Robert Miner, and her husband, writer and filmmaker Robert Mailer Anderson, have listed their 11,150-square-foot Pacific Heights estate for $65 million. The modern four-bedroom property, which includes a separate guest house, was built on a 0.4-acre site that the couple acquired in 1999 for $5 million. The listing agent noted that the asking price reflects the property's size, modern design, and proximity to the Presidio, aligning with recent comparable sales and the ongoing shortage of luxury homes in San Francisco's high-demand market.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

The priciest home sale in San Francisco history was Laurene Powell Jobs' purchase of a Pacific Heights mansion at 2840 Broadway for $71 million. In April, TelevisaUnivision CEO Daniel Alegre sold his 9,500-square-foot mansion at 2898 Vallejo Street for $56 million. San Francisco is experiencing a 'mansion shortage' due to high demand from tech and AI executives.
