CBRE Arranges $30.7M Loan for Refinancing of Apartment Building in West Caldwell, New Jersey

REFINANCE

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 31 Jul 2026 13:43:09 +0000

SUMMARY

CBRE has secured a $30.7 million refinancing loan for The Vail, a 92-unit apartment building in West Caldwell, New Jersey. The loan was provided by Principal Financial to the borrower, Accordia. The property offers a mix of studio, one-, and two-bedroom units, with 20% designated as affordable housing.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: The Vail

Address: 780 Passaic Ave, West Caldwell, NJ*

Units: 92

Loan Amount: $30.7M

Loan Per Unit: $333,696/unit (calculated)

Notable Features: 20% of units are reserved as affordable housing; units include washers/dryers and select units have private balconies. Amenities include a fitness center, community room, and coworking spaces.

* Address sourced separately, not in original article.
