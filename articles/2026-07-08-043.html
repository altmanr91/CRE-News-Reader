World Cup Delivers Assist For U.S. Hotels, But Not At Super Bowl Scale

Bisnow News Feed — Tue, 07 Jul 2026 16:02:00 -0400

SUMMARY

Market Research / Hospitality Report

The 2026 FIFA World Cup has provided a significant boost to the U.S. hotel industry, though not quite reaching the "Super Bowl-size" economic impact initially projected by tournament organizers. Early returns show strong but not exceptional revenue per available room (RevPAR) and average daily rate (ADR) increases, driven by shorter-term stays rather than the anticipated weeklong visits. While host markets experienced an 18.7% year-over-year RevPAR increase during the group stage, the overall U.S. hotel industry achieved record highs for RevPAR and ADR in late June, with match days showing a 25.8% RevPAR increase. Short-term rentals also saw rate increases, particularly on game days, and industry experts anticipate further premiums as the tournament progresses into later stages, especially with popular teams advancing.
