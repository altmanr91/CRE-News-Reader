Former Charles Schwab CEO sells SF condo after 10 years. How did he only make $18K?

SALE

The Real Deal — San Francisco — Thu, 23 Jul 2026 19:03:02 +0000

SUMMARY

The former CEO of Charles Schwab, David Pottruck, sold his three-bedroom, three-bathroom condo in San Francisco's Rincon Hill neighborhood for just under $6.7 million, realizing a modest profit of $18,300 after a decade of ownership. The transaction highlights the divergent trends in San Francisco's luxury housing market, where single-family homes are experiencing significant gains driven by the AI boom, while even high-end condominiums like those in the Lumina towers have struggled to appreciate, with many selling at or below their 2016 prices. The buyer, Ledion Bitincka, is the chief technology officer at an AI data firm, reflecting the new wave of wealth entering the market.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

San Francisco's luxury housing market shows a divergence between single-family homes, which are seeing increased demand and appreciation due to the AI boom, and luxury condominiums, which have not experienced similar gains. Median condo sales prices per square foot in San Francisco peaked at $1,200 in 2020, dropped to $950 in 2025, and saw a slight increase to $990 in early 2026, marking the first real boost since the pandemic. Over 75% of condos sold in the Lumina towers since last year went for at or below their 2016 prices.
