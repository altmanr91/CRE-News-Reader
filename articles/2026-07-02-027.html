CIM Group uses credit bid to take Goodtime Hotel for $100 in foreclosure auction

REO

The Real Deal — Miami — Wed, 01 Jul 2026 16:05:04 +0000

SUMMARY

CIM Group, through an affiliate, acquired the Goodtime Hotel in South Beach, Miami Beach, via a foreclosure auction with a credit bid of $100. This transaction follows a $204.7 million foreclosure judgment obtained by CIM Group against the hotel's owner, an entity tied to developers Eric Birnbaum and Michael Fascitelli. The hotel, which opened in 2021, faced financial distress and a default on a $164 million loan provided by CIM Group in 2021.
