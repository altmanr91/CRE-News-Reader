Capital Square Sells 2000 West Creek Apartments in Richmond for $115M

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Thu, 16 Jul 2026 15:11:59 +0000

SUMMARY

Capital Square has sold the 373-unit 2000 West Creek apartment community in Richmond, VA for $115 million. The company originally acquired the property in 2019 for $103 million through a Delaware statutory trust program, providing its 115 exchange investors with a 137 percent total return over a seven-year hold period. The sale generated over $17.5 million in distributions for the trust's investors.
