New Optima condo sales in Wilmette angling for North Shore sales records

The Real Deal — Chicago — Wed, 22 Jul 2026 20:16:54 +0000

SUMMARY

Property Listing / For Sale

Optima Real Estate is launching sales for Optima Lumina, a 133-unit luxury condominium building in Wilmette, with prices for some units reaching $4.6 million, potentially setting a new sales record for the North Shore. The developer designed the units to be modular, allowing for combinations up to 5,000 square feet to meet buyer demand. Construction is expected to begin later this year, with initial occupancy projected for summer 2028.
