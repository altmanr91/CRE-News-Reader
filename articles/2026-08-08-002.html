Barry Sternlicht’s Starwood Buys Two Miami Affordable Housing Rentals

ACQUISITION

Commercial Observer — Fri, 07 Aug 2026 17:55:49 +0000

SUMMARY

Starwood Asset Management, led by Barry Sternlicht, has acquired two affordable housing properties in Miami-Dade County from the Gatehouse Group for a combined $63.8 million. The portfolio includes the 150-unit Magnolia Landing Apartments in Naranja and the 160-unit Lafayette Square complex in Little River. This acquisition highlights continued investor interest in the affordable housing sector within the Miami market.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Magnolia Landing Apartments / Lafayette Square

Address: 25881 Southwest 143rd Court, Miami, FL / 150 Northeast 79th Street, Miami, FL

Units: 310

Sale Price: $63.8M

Year Built: 2011

Site Area: 6 acres

Notable Features: Magnolia Landing Apartments consists of 20 three-story buildings; Lafayette Square is a 19-story building.

Project Notes: The Gatehouse Group developed both properties, completing Lafayette Square in 2008 and Magnolia Landing in 2011. The seller appears to have retained ownership of an adjacent 136-unit property next to Lafayette Square.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: Starwood Asset Management — Barry Sternlicht

SELLER: Gatehouse Group

LENDER: CBRE Capital Partners
