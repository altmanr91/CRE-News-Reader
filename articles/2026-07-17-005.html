AI Firm Isara Laboratories Inks 6K-SF Lease at 515 West 20th Street

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Thu, 16 Jul 2026 20:04:57 +0000

SUMMARY

AI startup Isara Laboratories has signed a two-year lease for 6,009 square feet at 515 West 20th Street in Chelsea, New York City. The company, which develops AI software for finance and biotech, is relocating its commercial and customer teams to the boutique office building. The transaction reflects a trend of AI firms seeking downtown, hip office spaces in West Chelsea, which offers a more accessible price point compared to the SoHo market.
