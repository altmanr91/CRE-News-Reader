Greenstone Partners Brokers Two Chicago Multifamily Transactions

SALE

Connect CRE — Mon, 31 Aug 2026 22:11:48 +0000

SUMMARY

Greenstone Partners recently brokered two multifamily transactions in Chicago, totaling $3.27 million. The deals included the sale of an eight-unit property in Wicker Park for $2.245 million and a three-unit building with a coach house and an adjacent development lot in Bridgeport for $1.025 million. The Bridgeport property offers both existing rental income and future value-creation opportunities through potential rent growth, additional parking income, and development optionality.
