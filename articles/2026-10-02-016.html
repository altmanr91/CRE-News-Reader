Waterstone Acquires 660K-SF Matthews Retail Center

ACQUISITION

Connect CRE — Fri, 02 Oct 2026 14:26:59 +0000

SUMMARY

Waterstone Properties has acquired the 659,222-square-foot Windsor Square Shopping Center in Matthews, NC, for $87 million. The power center, which was 99% leased at the time of sale, was sold by Hackney Real Estate Partners. United Bank provided a $63.1 million acquisition loan for the transaction.
