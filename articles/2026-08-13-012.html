CBRE closes sale of 32-unit multifamily community in Mauston

SALE

REJournals — Thu, 13 Aug 2026 09:31:00 +0000

SUMMARY

CBRE has facilitated the sale of Riverwood Apartments, a 32-unit multifamily community in Mauston, Wisconsin. Tripp & Associates acquired the fully occupied property from Riverwood Apartments, LLC for $3.95 million. The community, built in 1994, features a mix of one-, two-, and three-bedroom units with renovated interiors and a pet-friendly environment.
