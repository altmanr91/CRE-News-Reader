Apricus, ABR Capital Buy 18.5-Acre Industrial Outdoor Storage Facility in Northwest Houston

ACQUISITION

REBusinessOnline — Tue, 18 Aug 2026 15:25:45 +0000

SUMMARY

A joint venture between Apricus Realty Capital and ABR Capital Partners has acquired an 18.5-acre industrial outdoor storage (IOS) facility in northwest Houston. The property, which includes 53,000 square feet of warehouse and service facilities, was fully leased at the time of sale. JLL arranged the acquisition financing for the transaction.
