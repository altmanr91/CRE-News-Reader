Peachtree Refis AJ Capital’s Graduate by Hilton Nashville Hotel With $63M Loan

REFINANCE

Commercial Observer — Mon, 31 Aug 2026 15:26:40 +0000

SUMMARY

AJ Capital Partners has secured a $62.5 million refinancing loan from Peachtree Group for its Graduate by Hilton Nashville hotel. The three-year loan, with two 12-month extension options, will support the 205-room hotel located across from Vanderbilt University. The hotel has shown strong performance, particularly since Hilton took over operations of the Graduate brand in 2024, expanding its customer base beyond the university.
