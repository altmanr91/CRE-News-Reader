Today’s shoppers? They prefer quick trips and local options

REJournals — Thu, 10 Sep 2026 09:45:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Retail Trends

A new report from Placer.ai, "The 2026 Retail Consumer in 5 Charts: Who’s Winning in 2026?", reveals evolving consumer shopping habits that emphasize quick trips and local options. The research indicates that while younger consumers are frequenting physical stores more often, the majority of retail visits are short, often under 30 minutes, suggesting a mission-driven approach to shopping. Furthermore, retail remains intensely local, with nearly 60% of visits originating within five miles of a store, highlighting the importance of understanding local demographics for site selection and marketing. The report also stresses the continued significance of older shoppers, who are overrepresented in physical store traffic, and notes that lower- and middle-income households account for a disproportionate share of brick-and-mortar visits. These trends underscore the need for retailers and commercial real estate professionals to prioritize convenience, localized strategies, and inclusive store designs to cater to a diverse consumer base.

DATA POINTS

Retail visits under 30 minutes: Vast majority

Retail visits under 15 minutes: Largest dwell-time category

Retail visits originating within five miles of store: Nearly 60%

Retail visits originating within three miles of store: About 44%

Age group overrepresented in captured market: Adults aged 55 and older

Household income accounting for disproportionate share of physical-store traffic: Less than $100,000 annually
