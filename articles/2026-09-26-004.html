Legion Investment Group, SMA Equities Pair on 300K-SF UES Resi Project

LOAN

Commercial Observer — Fri, 25 Sep 2026 18:59:10 +0000

SUMMARY

Legion Investment Group and SMA Equities have formed a partnership to develop a 37-story residential tower on Manhattan's Upper East Side. The joint venture secured a $99 million pre-development loan from BDT & MSD Partners and Deutsche Bank for the 300,000-square-foot project. The development, which will include approximately 120 condominium units and nearly 19,000 square feet of commercial space, is being built on an assemblage of parcels acquired over nearly two decades.
