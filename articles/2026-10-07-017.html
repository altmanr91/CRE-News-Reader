Kidder Mathews Arranges Sale of 58K-SF Tacoma Industrial Building

SALE

Connect CRE — Wed, 07 Oct 2026 15:46:27 +0000

SUMMARY

Kidder Mathews has facilitated the sale of the Smith-Western Building, a 58,255-square-foot industrial distribution facility in Tacoma, Washington. The property, fully leased to a long-term tenant through 2031, was sold amidst challenging market conditions characterized by high interest rates and elevated industrial vacancy. The transaction highlights the value of stabilized assets with established tenancy in the current market.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Property Name: Smith-Western Building

Address: 2223 S. 80th Street, Tacoma, WA

Size: 58,255 SF

Year Built: 1993

Site Area: 2.44 acres

Notable Features: Concrete industrial property with 24-foot clear height, nine dock-high doors, two grade-level doors, three exterior levelers, and a secure fenced yard with outside storage.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE
