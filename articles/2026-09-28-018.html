Longpoint Pays $195M for 10-Building Miami-Area Warehouse Portfolio

ACQUISITION

Connect CRE — Mon, 28 Sep 2026 14:59:39 +0000

SUMMARY

Longpoint Partners has acquired a 10-building industrial portfolio in Miami-Dade County from Brookfield Asset Management for $195 million. The portfolio spans 729,901 square feet across approximately 41.8 acres and was 90% occupied at the time of sale. This acquisition aligns with Longpoint's strategy to invest in functional industrial assets located in supply-constrained infill markets, capitalizing on Miami's strong demand drivers.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Size: 729,901 SF

Sale Price: $195.0M

Occupancy at Sale: 90.0%

Site Area: 41.8 acres

Notable Features: Clear heights ranging from 17 to 25 feet, 231 loading positions, front and rear loading configurations, and a 40% floor area ratio.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: Longpoint Partners

SELLER: Brookfield Asset Management

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Miami's population growth, proximity to major consumer markets, and critical transportation infrastructure continue to support demand for well-located industrial space.
