Mid-America Real Estate Corporation brokers sale of 37,615-square-foot retail asset in Chicago’s Lincoln Park neighborhood

SALE

REJournals — Wed, 29 Jul 2026 09:43:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Mid-America Real Estate Corporation's Investment Sales Group brokered the sale of Lincoln Park West, a 37,615-square-foot retail asset in Chicago's Lincoln Park neighborhood. The property, anchored by a strong tenant mix with a weighted average lease term of 7.0 years, was acquired by a private local investor from LLJ Ventures. The offering presented a significant value-add opportunity through the lease-up or adaptive reuse of its 19,531-square-foot second floor.
