Aviation Repair Company Pays Nearly $30M for Doral Warehouse

ACQUISITION

Connect CRE — Fri, 25 Sep 2026 14:09:37 +0000

SUMMARY

Jet Engine Technology Corporation acquired a 112,000-square-foot industrial warehouse in Doral, Florida, for $29.5 million. The property, located in America's Gateway Park near Miami International Airport, will serve as an expansion for the buyer's FAA Repair Station. The transaction was brokered by Transwestern, representing the buyer, and Casal Group, representing the seller, Century Properties.
