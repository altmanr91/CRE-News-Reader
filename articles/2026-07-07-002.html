Former Downtown Brooklyn Macy’s Slated for Dramatic Retail Makeover

DEVELOPMENT

Commercial Observer — Mon, 06 Jul 2026 20:28:06 +0000

SUMMARY

United American Land and the Jackson Group are transforming the former 440,000-square-foot Macy's department store at 422 Fulton Street in Downtown Brooklyn into BKX, a five-floor retail and amusement destination. Described as a "next-generation cultural and commercial hub," the project will feature a curated mix of entertainment, dining, and flagship retail tenants. The developers aim to create a synergistic environment by controlling the tenant mix within the large, single-owner property, a strategy that differentiates it from traditional retail districts.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: retail

Property Name: BKX

Address: 422 Fulton Street, Brooklyn, NY

Size: 440,000 SF

Notable Features: Features 60,000-70,000 SF floor plates and ceiling heights from 17.5 to 22.5 feet, with a central atrium at the entrance designed as a dramatic shared gathering space with LED screens.

Project Notes: The site was previously acquired in December 2024 by a consortium including United American Land, Crown Acquisitions, and the Jackson Group for $23 million. The upper 10 floors of office space at 422 Fulton Street are separately owned by Tishman Speyer.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

The acquisition price of $23 million for the former Macy's site, at approximately $52 per square foot, represented a significant discount compared to Brooklyn's average sales price of $617 per square foot for mixed-use and retail properties in the first half of 2024.
