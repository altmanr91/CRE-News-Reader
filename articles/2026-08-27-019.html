Work Begins on 60-Unit Denver Supportive Housing Community

CONSTRUCTION

Connect CRE — Thu, 27 Aug 2026 16:25:26 +0000

SUMMARY

Brothers Redevelopment, Inc. has commenced construction on Wagoner on the Creek, a 60-unit permanent supportive housing community in Aurora, CO. The nearly $31 million development will cater to justice-involved and unhoused residents in Arapahoe County, requiring future occupants to earn less than 60% of the area median income. The project is supported by a diverse capital stack, including contributions from seven government agencies, two banks, and Low-Income Housing Tax Credits, and will offer comprehensive on-site support services.

DATA POINTS

Project Notes: Serves justice-involved and unhoused residents earning less than 60% of area median income. On-site services include case management, behavioral health counseling, peer navigation, employment support, and medical services.
