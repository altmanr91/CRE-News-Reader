Ken Mattson has a trial date. Silicon Valley’s office recovery has momentum.

The Real Deal — San Francisco — Sun, 05 Jul 2026 15:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

CRE Legal / Regulatory

A federal jury trial has been scheduled for April 12 for Sonoma developer and investor Ken Mattson, who is accused of orchestrating a multi-million dollar Ponzi scheme. Mattson faces nine felony counts, including wire fraud and money laundering, after initially agreeing to a guilty plea but later reversing his decision. Meanwhile, the Silicon Valley office market is showing significant recovery, with vacancy rates falling to the mid-teens in 2026 from 20-23% at the end of 2024. This momentum is partly attributed to the artificial intelligence boom, which has also driven down San Francisco's office vacancy rate by five points year-over-year to 29.7% in 2026, with net absorption reaching 2.9 million square feet in the first half of the year.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Silicon Valley's office market is experiencing a recovery, with vacancy rates dropping from 20-23% at the end of 2024 to the mid-teens in 2026. Kidder Mathews reported a 16.6% vacancy rate in Q2 2026, a 1.7% decrease year-over-year. San Francisco's office vacancy rate also fell five points year-over-year, from 34.7% in 2025 to 29.7% in 2026, driven by the artificial intelligence boom. Net absorption in San Francisco reached 2.9 million square feet in the first half of 2026, surpassing the total for all of 2025, and tenant demand hit an all-time high of 8.9 million square feet.
