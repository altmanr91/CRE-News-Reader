Park Aerospace to Open $65M Manufacturing Facility in Tulsa, Create 100 Jobs

CONSTRUCTION

REBusinessOnline — Mon, 14 Sep 2026 14:49:01 +0000

SUMMARY

Park Aerospace Corp. is set to develop a new $65 million manufacturing facility in Tulsa, Oklahoma, dedicated to producing advanced composite materials for the defense and aerospace industries. The project, located on an 18-acre site near Tulsa International Airport, is expected to create approximately 100 new jobs. Construction is slated to commence shortly and conclude by 2028.
