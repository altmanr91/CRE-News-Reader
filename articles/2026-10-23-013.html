Chief financial officer for Draper and Kramer to serve on Illinois’ Teachers’ Retirement System Board of Trustees

REJournals — Fri, 23 Oct 2026 09:06:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Non-CRE / Business News

James Hayes, CFO of Draper and Kramer, has been appointed by Illinois Governor JB Pritzker to the state's Teachers' Retirement System Board of Trustees. Once confirmed, Hayes will contribute to the fiduciary oversight and strategic direction of the pension system, which manages over $85 billion in assets. His experience includes consolidating Draper and Kramer's real estate holdings into a $1.2 billion equity fund.
