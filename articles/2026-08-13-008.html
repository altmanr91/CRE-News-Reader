New Hope Housing Completes Affordable Seniors Housing Project in East Houston

CONSTRUCTION

REBusinessOnline — Wed, 12 Aug 2026 15:03:44 +0000

SUMMARY

New Hope Housing has completed Avenue C, an affordable seniors housing project located in Houston's Magnolia Park. This development is one of three new affordable seniors housing projects by New Hope Housing to open in Houston within the last two years, underscoring the organization's commitment to providing much-needed housing options for seniors in the area.
