A Buffett protege said to have bet nearly $49M on Union Square retail

ACQUISITION

The Real Deal — San Francisco — Tue, 14 Jul 2026 17:34:51 +0000

SUMMARY

A Delaware corporation, Silver Gate Property II LLC, an affiliate of investor Ian Jacobs, acquired the four-story retail and office building at 180 Post Street in San Francisco for $48.5 million. The 39,000-square-foot property, anchored by a long-term lease with Bulgari, was sold by Grosvenor Americas, which had owned the building for three decades. This acquisition is part of Jacobs' ongoing strategy to invest in distressed commercial properties in downtown San Francisco, particularly around Union Square, reflecting a vote of confidence in the city's luxury retail sector despite broader challenges.
