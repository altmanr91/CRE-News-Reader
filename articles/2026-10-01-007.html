Talonvest Capital Arranges $47.7M Permanent Loan for Texas Self-Storage Portfolio

LOAN

REBusinessOnline — Thu, 01 Oct 2026 15:07:11 +0000

SUMMARY

Talonvest Capital has arranged a $47.7 million permanent loan for a portfolio of six self-storage facilities across multiple Texas markets. The financing was secured for a partnership between The Jenkins Organization and Clark Investment Group. The portfolio encompasses over 600,000 net rentable square feet.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: self-storage

Size: 600,000 SF

Loan Amount: $47.7M

COMPANIES/PEOPLE
