Chicago proposes $287M TIF raid for Related Midwest’s megaproject The 78

The Real Deal — Chicago — Mon, 13 Jul 2026 22:55:50 +0000

SUMMARY

Infrastructure Investment / Government Policy

Chicago city officials are proposing to reallocate $287 million in property tax revenue from the Canal/Congress TIF district to support Related Midwest's 'The 78' megaproject in the South Loop. This revised tax increment financing (TIF) subsidy aims to reduce the developer's reliance on future tax revenue generated by the project itself, which includes a new stadium for the Chicago Fire soccer team. The move has drawn criticism from some aldermen who argue it diverts funds from other essential infrastructure projects, such as Union Station maintenance.
