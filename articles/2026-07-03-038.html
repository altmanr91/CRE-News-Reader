CBRE Opens Hospitality-Inspired Office in Columbus

LEASE

Connect CRE — Thu, 02 Jul 2026 20:19:22 +0000

SUMMARY

CBRE has opened a new 19,500-square-foot office in downtown Columbus, relocating to 10 W. Nationwide Blvd. within the Arena District. The new space, designed under CBRE's Workplace360 program, emphasizes technology and collaborative areas to support hybrid work models. The design draws inspiration from hospitality concepts, reflecting Columbus's local culture.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Address: 10 W. Nationwide Blvd., Columbus, OH

Size: 19,500 SF

Notable Features: Located in a trophy office building within the Arena District; designed with hospitality-inspired elements and technology to support hybrid work.

Project Notes: CBRE is the first tenant in the building at this address.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

TENANT: CBRE — Michael Copella, Executive Managing Director and Market Leader, Central Ohio

LANDLORD: Nationwide Realty Investors
