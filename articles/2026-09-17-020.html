Vango Development Launches Leasing at Third Rutherford-Area Property

LEASE

Connect CRE — Wed, 16 Sep 2026 21:33:17 +0000

SUMMARY

Vango Development has commenced leasing at Parker East, a new five-story luxury rental community in East Rutherford, NJ. The property offers 86 one- and two-bedroom apartments with immediate occupancy, featuring a robust amenity package and convenient access to mass transit and downtown Rutherford. This development expands Vango's presence in the area, building on the success of its previous projects in neighboring Rutherford.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Parker East

Address: 263 Hackensack St, East Rutherford, NJ

Units: 86

Notable Features: Luxury rental community located 0.2 miles from the Rutherford NJ Transit station, offering indoor and outdoor lifestyle amenities.

Project Notes: Residences are priced from $3,250. Immediate occupancy is available.
