Hercules Development Partners Divests of Dylan Apartments Near Berkeley, California for $83.5M

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Mon, 12 Oct 2026 15:07:49 +0000

SUMMARY

Hercules Development Partners has sold The Dylan, a 232-unit multifamily community in Hercules, California, to Interstate Equities Corp. for $83.5 million. JLL Capital Markets represented the seller in the transaction, which also included JLL arranging a $54.3 million acquisition loan for the buyer through Freddie Mac.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: The Dylan

Address: 2200 John Muir Pkwy, Hercules, CA*

Units: 232

Sale Price: $83.5M

$/Unit: $359,914/unit (calculated)

Year Built: 2022

Notable Features: The five-story community features 217 market-rate units and 15 below-market-rate units, with amenities including a lounge, sports lounge, fitness center, resident courtyard with barbecue and fire pit areas, and bike storage with a repair station.
