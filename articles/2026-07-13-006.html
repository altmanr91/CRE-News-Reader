Inside the Underwriting Process: What Affordable Housing Lenders Look for That Developers Don’t Always See

REJournals — Mon, 13 Jul 2026 09:58:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

This article, authored by a managing director at Bridgewater Bank, offers an inside look into the underwriting process for affordable housing projects from a lender's perspective. It emphasizes that lenders evaluate not just the vision but also the readiness and execution capacity of a development team, scrutinizing project assumptions, funding reliability, and organizational bandwidth. The author highlights the importance of conservative operating assumptions and a deep understanding of compliance and mission alignment, asserting that strong projects are built on transparent and responsive partnerships.
