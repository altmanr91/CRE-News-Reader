Audi Building $4B Plant in C. Georgia

CONSTRUCTION

Connect CRE — Mon, 26 Oct 2026 14:03:32 +0000

SUMMARY

Audi plans to invest approximately $4 billion to construct a new manufacturing plant at the Middle Georgia Megasite in Peach County, Georgia. This will be the first development at the 1,534-acre state-owned industrial site, which is strategically located near major transportation infrastructure and energy resources. The facility is projected to begin construction in 2027, with vehicle production commencing in the early 2030s, and is expected to create around 4,500 jobs, significantly boosting the regional economy and supply chain.

Project Notes: The 1,534-acre site was previously acquired by the State of Georgia for approximately $18.6 million. Construction is scheduled to begin in 2027.
