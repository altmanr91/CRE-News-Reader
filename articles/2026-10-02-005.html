Joyland Sells Staten Island Shopping Center for $57M

SALE

Commercial Observer — Thu, 01 Oct 2026 19:42:05 +0000

SUMMARY

Phillips Edison & Company has acquired South Shore Commons, a 157,347-square-foot outdoor shopping center in Staten Island, from Joyland Management for $57 million. This marks the second sale of the property in four years, highlighting its continued appeal in the retail market. The transaction was handled in-house by Joyland Management, with Newmark representing the seller and procuring the buyer.
