NBCUniversal Renews 244K-SF Offices at 1221 Avenue of the Americas

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Thu, 09 Jul 2026 20:19:34 +0000

SUMMARY

NBCUniversal has renewed its 244,185-square-foot office lease at 1221 Avenue of the Americas in Midtown Manhattan. The media giant initially leased space in the building in 2012 as part of a significant investment in its New York City operations. This renewal is one of several large office lease renewals occurring in Manhattan during the second quarter, contributing to a robust leasing market.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Property Name: 1221 Avenue of the Americas

Address: 1221 Avenue of the Americas, New York City, NY

Size: 244,185 SF

Project Notes: NBCUniversal first leased space in the building in 2012 as part of a $190 million investment in its NYC operations, which also included upgrades to its headquarters at 30 Rockefeller Plaza. NBC last renewed a secondary office lease in the building in 2021, bringing its total footprint to 339,933 square feet.
