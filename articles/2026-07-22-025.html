Big Tech, big offices: Google supersizes Miami presence on heels of Larry Page, Sergey Brin’s resi spree

LEASE

The Real Deal — Miami — Tue, 21 Jul 2026 19:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Alphabet, Google's parent company, has significantly expanded its office footprint in Miami, quadrupling its space at the 1450 Brickell Avenue tower to approximately 45,000 square feet. This expansion, which closed in the spring, signals a serious commitment by major tech firms to Miami, countering the perception that companies are only establishing small satellite offices in the region. The move follows recent high-profile residential acquisitions in South Florida by Google co-founders Larry Page and Sergey Brin.
