Spear Street Capital Buys Lower Manhattan Office, Retail Building for $50.5M

ACQUISITION

REBusinessOnline — Mon, 13 Jul 2026 14:37:53 +0000

SUMMARY

Spear Street Capital has acquired 76 Eighth Avenue, a 10-story office and retail building in Lower Manhattan, for $50.5 million. The property, completed in 2022, was fully leased at the time of sale. JLL represented the seller, G4 Capital Partners, and also arranged $27.7 million in acquisition financing for the buyer through DekaBank.
