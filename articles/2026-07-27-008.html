JLL Capital Markets closes sale of Medicine Lake Apartments in Plymouth

SALE

REJournals — Mon, 27 Jul 2026 09:48:00 +0000

SUMMARY

JLL Capital Markets has successfully brokered the sale of Medicine Lake Apartments, an 81-unit lakefront multifamily community in Plymouth, Minnesota. The property, known for its unique direct access to Medicine Lake and private boat slips, was sold by Bigos Management to Weber Organization. This transaction highlights the appeal of well-maintained assets in highly desirable suburban markets with strong local amenities and proximity to major employers.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Medicine Lake Apartments

Address: 1300 W Medicine Lake Dr., Plymouth, MN

Units: 81

Year Built: 1977

Notable Features: Lakefront community with 12 private boat slips, 24-hour fitness center, sauna, outdoor pool, multi-sport court, resident clubroom, and direct access to the Luce Line Regional Trail. Located near three Fortune 500 headquarters and within the top-ranked Wayzata School District.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

SELLER: Bigos Management

BUYER: Weber Organization

SELLER BROKER: JLL Capital Markets — Josh Talberg, Managing Director; Joseph Peris, Director; Jack Graveline, Analyst
