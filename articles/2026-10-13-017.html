BH Properties Affiliate Acquires 346-Unit Leander Multifamily Project

ACQUISITION

Connect CRE — Tue, 13 Oct 2026 15:47:12 +0000

SUMMARY

Haven Housing, the affordable and multifamily housing platform of BH Properties, has acquired The Evergrove at Crystal Village, a 346-unit, Class-A apartment community in Leander, TX. Completed in 2024, the garden-style property is approximately 92% occupied and is part of the larger Crystal Village master-planned, mixed-use community. This acquisition expands Haven Housing's Texas portfolio, reflecting their strategy of long-term ownership to provide stable housing and create value.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: The Evergrove at Crystal Village

Address: 2090 Raider Way, Leander, TX

Units: 346

Occupancy at Sale: 92.0%

Year Built: 2024

Site Area: 15 acres

Notable Features: Class-A, garden-style apartment community located within the Crystal Village master-planned, mixed-use community, offering private balconies or patios, a swimming pool with cabanas, a 24-hour fitness center, and a co-working clubhouse.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: Haven Housing

OWNER: BH Properties

GENERAL CONTRACTOR: LMF Construction
