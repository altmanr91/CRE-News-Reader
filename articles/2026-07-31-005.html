Karim Alibhai Started at Best Western. Now He Rents Rooms at $2,500 a Night.

Commercial Observer — Thu, 30 Jul 2026 18:15:25 +0000

SUMMARY

Feature / Profile

Karim Alibhai, CEO of Gencom, has expanded his luxury hotel portfolio with approximately $1 billion in acquisitions in New York City over the past two years, including the Ritz-Carlton New York, Central Park for $320 million. Gencom's strategy is driven by identifying markets with low supply and increasing demand, such as New York City, and capitalizing on the strong post-pandemic recovery of the leisure resort segment. Alibhai notes the resilience of the luxury hotel market to price increases, with room rates at properties like the Ritz-Carlton New York, Central Park ranging from $1,500 to $2,500 per night, significantly higher than pre-pandemic levels.
