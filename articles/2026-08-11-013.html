Lenexa’s Henderson Companies adds pair of leaders in venue sector

REJournals — Tue, 11 Aug 2026 09:58:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Promotion

Henderson Companies, the parent company of Henderson Engineers and Henderson Building Solutions, has appointed two new leaders within its venue sector, which focuses on the design and construction of large-scale, multipurpose facilities across the nation. Bridget Lowe has been hired as convention center practice director, bringing 15 years of industry experience to oversee client experience, technical quality, innovation, business development, and employee engagement. Additionally, Jason Kartak has been promoted to entertainment practice director, where he will lead the firm's entertainment practice, specializing in performing arts centers, amphitheaters, and other immersive environments, leveraging his more than 30 years of industry experience.
