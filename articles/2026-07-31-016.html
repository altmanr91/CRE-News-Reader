CRE Tech’s Next Evolution: From More Software to Better Connections

Connect CRE — Fri, 31 Jul 2026 05:01:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

This article discusses the evolving landscape of commercial real estate technology, highlighting a shift from acquiring new software to integrating existing systems for better connectivity and optimized portfolio performance. Drawing insights from a CBRE article, the piece emphasizes that fragmented technology hinders efficiency and that a connected approach is crucial. It outlines three primary technology types—Integrated Workplace Management Systems (IWMS), Vendor-Provided Technology, and Best-in-Class Point Solutions—and introduces Connected Portfolio Intelligence Platforms as a solution to link these systems, convert data into actionable insights, and enhance overall value. The article concludes by stressing the importance of accurate data, consistent governance, and strategic integration to maximize technology ROI, rather than simply adding more software.
