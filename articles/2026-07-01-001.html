The Plan: Balance and Nuance Reign at Gowanus’ Douglass Port

Commercial Observer — Wed, 01 Jul 2026 10:00:52 +0000

SUMMARY

Development

Charney Companies and Tavros have completed Douglass Port, a 15-story, 260-unit residential building at 251 Douglass Street in Brooklyn's Gowanus neighborhood. This property is part of the larger Gowanus Wharf campus, a multi-building development offering thousands of mixed-income units. Residents of Douglass Port, which began leasing in June and is currently 20% leased, have access to shared amenities across the entire campus, including a rooftop pool at the Union Channel building and a basketball court at Douglass Port, fostering a strong community environment.

SPONSOR PIPELINE

Union Channel at 240 Third Avenue: Residential building within the Gowanus Wharf campus with a rooftop pool.
