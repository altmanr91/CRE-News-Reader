Innocean USA Signs 100K-SF lease with Hackman Capital in Headquarters Relocation

LEASE

Connect CRE — Fri, 07 Aug 2026 21:42:56 +0000

SUMMARY

Advertising agency Innocean USA has signed a 101,000-square-foot office lease at Hackman Capital Partners' 888 N. Douglas property in El Segundo, marking a significant headquarters relocation and expansion from its previous 50,000-square-foot space in Huntington Beach. The move, anticipated for 2027 upon construction completion, is described by the tenant's broker as a transformative change designed to foster creativity and employee thriving in a unique facility.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Property Name: 888 N. Douglas

Address: 888 N. Douglas, El Segundo, CA

Size: 101,000 SF

Completion: 2027

Notable Features: 30-acre campus with four buildings totaling approximately 550,000 square feet, located across from the Lakers' practice facility and the L.A. Times headquarters.

Project Notes: Hackman Capital Partners acquired the 1930s-era aerospace manufacturing site in 2016 and invested $100 million to redevelop it into a creative office campus.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

LANDLORD: Hackman Capital Partners

TENANT: Innocean USA
