Stream Realty Partners Nears Completion of 215,891 SF Industrial Project in Spring, Texas

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 16 Oct 2026 15:03:41 +0000

SUMMARY

Construction

Stream Realty Partners is nearing completion of Summit Grove Business Park, a 215,891-square-foot industrial project in Spring, Texas. Building One, a 124,930-square-foot rear-load facility, is currently available for lease. Building Two, comprising 90,961 square feet, has been fully preleased to an energy services company, indicating strong demand for industrial space in the northern Houston submarket.
