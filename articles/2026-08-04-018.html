BFC, SAA Canopy Land $269M Financing for Syracuse Redevelopment

LOAN

Connect CRE — Mon, 03 Aug 2026 21:30:24 +0000

SUMMARY

BFC Partners and SAA Canopy Group have secured a $269 million construction loan to redevelop the Parkside Commons housing complex in Syracuse, NY. The project will deliver 393 affordable apartments through a combination of renovations and new construction. The financing will enable the renovation of six existing buildings and the construction of two new ones, with plans for future phases of development on the cleared land from demolished older structures. The developers aim to provide high-quality affordable housing that enhances the community.
