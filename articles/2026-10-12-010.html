Colliers brokers $38.16 million industrial acquisition in Plainfield

ACQUISITION

REJournals — Mon, 12 Oct 2026 13:03:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Electric Plus, an electrical contractor, acquired a 500,192-square-foot industrial facility at 501 Airtech Parkway in Plainfield, Indiana, for $38.6 million. The company will relocate its headquarters to the new facility to accommodate its expanding operations. Colliers represented Electric Plus in the acquisition and will also handle the leasing of a portion of the property, which will have 115,000 to 265,000 square feet available for lease.
