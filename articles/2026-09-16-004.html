$420M CMBS Loan Backed by 51 West 52nd Exits Special Servicing, Secures Extension

REFINANCE

Commercial Observer — Tue, 15 Sep 2026 22:55:27 +0000

SUMMARY

The $420 million CMBS loan secured by 51 West 52nd Street, an 893,000-square-foot office tower in Midtown Manhattan, has exited special servicing after its sponsor, Harbor Group International, secured an extension with Deutsche Bank. The loan was transferred to special servicing as a technical step to execute the already negotiated upsize and extension. Harbor Group International acquired the property in 2021 for $760 million and has since invested $150 million in renovations and tenant improvements, with the building now reported to be fully leased.
