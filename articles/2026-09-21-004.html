Affinius Capital, Alliance Residential Dispose of 545-Unit Seniors Housing Portfolio in Northern California

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Mon, 21 Sep 2026 14:21:30 +0000

SUMMARY

A joint venture between Affinius Capital and Alliance Residential has sold a two-property seniors housing portfolio in Northern California. The portfolio, comprising 545 units across Sonrisa Senior Living in Roseville and The Watermark at Almaden in San Jose, was acquired by a publicly traded REIT. This transaction represents one of the largest seniors housing portfolio sales in the region in recent years.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: seniors housing

Property Name: Sonrisa Senior Living and The Watermark at Almaden

Units: 545

Notable Features: Amenities at the communities include outdoor pools, fitness centers, salons and spas, theaters and bocce ball and pickleball courts.

Project Notes: Sonrisa Senior Living was constructed in 2021 and 2023. The Watermark at Almaden opened in 2021.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

SELLER: Affinius Capital

SELLER: Alliance Residential

BUYER: publicly traded REIT

SELLER BROKER: JLL Capital Markets — Aaron Rosenzweig; Dan Baker; Sandis Seale
