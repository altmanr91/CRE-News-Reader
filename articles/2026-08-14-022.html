With Data Centers Devouring Construction Materials, Mass Timber May Finally Have Its Moment

Bisnow Latest News Feed — Thu, 13 Aug 2026 21:03:49 -0400

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

The increasing demand for construction materials, particularly from the booming data center sector, is creating an opportune moment for mass timber. This eco-friendly building material is gaining traction due to its sustainable benefits and stable pricing, appealing to major technology companies and supported by legislative initiatives. The shift towards mass timber could offer a viable alternative to traditional construction materials, addressing both environmental concerns and supply chain stability.
