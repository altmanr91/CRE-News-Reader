Marcus & Millichap Brokers Self-Storage Property Deal in Chicago

SALE

Connect CRE — Tue, 07 Jul 2026 21:01:18 +0000

SUMMARY

Marcus & Millichap has brokered the sale of a newly constructed Public Storage facility in Chicago, Illinois. The 916-unit, climate-controlled self-storage property was completed in 2024 and is strategically located in a densely populated area with high visibility. The transaction highlights Chicago's robust self-storage market, driven by constrained new supply and strong rental rates, which continues to attract investors seeking high-quality assets.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: self-storage

Property Name: Public Storage

Address: 5251 N. Kedzie Ave., Chicago, IL

Size: 72,899 SF

Year Built: 2024

Notable Features: Climate-controlled units, institutional-quality facility, strong visibility along North Kedzie Avenue, situated in a densely populated area with a predominantly renter-occupied housing base.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

SELLER: Illinois-based LLC

BUYER: Texas-based LLC

SELLER BROKER: Marcus & Millichap — Jeffrey Herrmann, Senior Director of Investments; Sean Delaney, Investment Specialist

TENANTS AT PROPERTY

Public Storage

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Chicago is identified as one of the strongest self-storage rental rate markets due to above-average development complexity constraining new supply.
