Marcus & Millichap brokers sale of three-property retail portfolio in West Allis

SALE

REJournals — Tue, 06 Oct 2026 14:03:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Marcus & Millichap has brokered the sale of the Southtown Shopping Center, a three-property retail portfolio in West Allis, Wisconsin. The portfolio, which includes a Panera Bread outlot and a Krispy Kreme ground lease, was 98% leased at the time of sale with a strong weighted tenant tenure of nearly 16 years. The buyer, an East Coast-based 1031-exchange investor, was procured by Marcus & Millichap.
