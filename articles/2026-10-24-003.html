Revamp NYC Wants to Bring 99 Units to the Financial District’s 160 Pearl Street

DEVELOPMENT

Commercial Observer — Fri, 23 Oct 2026 19:36:56 +0000

SUMMARY

Revamp NYC has filed an application with the New York City Department of Buildings to construct a 25-story, 99-unit residential building with ground-floor retail at 160 Pearl Street in Manhattan's Financial District. The project would add to the neighborhood's growing residential community, though it is currently unclear if the developer plans to include affordable housing units to qualify for tax incentives or if the existing commercial building will be demolished.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: mixed-use

Address: 160 Pearl Street, New York City, NY

Size: 2,394 SF

Units: 99

Notable Features: Proposed amenities include indoor and outdoor recreation space and a yoga room.

Project Notes: The project may qualify for New York's 485-x tax incentive for buildings with 99 or fewer units that include affordable housing.
