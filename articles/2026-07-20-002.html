Korean Grocery Sensation H Mart Wants to Conquer the Sun Belt

Commercial Observer — Mon, 20 Jul 2026 10:30:32 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Retail Expansion

H Mart, a Korean grocery chain, is aggressively expanding into Sun Belt markets including Florida, Texas, California, and Nevada, capitalizing on its growing popularity among both Asian American communities and a broader American audience. The company, which generates $2 billion in annual sales from over 100 locations, has distinguished itself by offering authentic imported Asian ingredients and a welcoming shopping experience. This expansion is driven by increasing immigration from East Asia and the rising purchasing power of Asian American households, particularly second-generation immigrants who are more affluent and seeking sophisticated products.

DATA POINTS

H Mart Annual Sales: $2 billion

H Mart Locations: Over 100 across U.S. and Canada

Asian American Household Income Growth (2017-2024): 57% (from $99,000 to $155,000)

Asian American Household Spending Growth (2017-2024): 62% (from $453 billion to $735 billion)

Las Vegas H Mart Size: 54,500 SF

Orange County, CA H Mart Size: 70,000 SF

Orlando, FL H Mart Size: 100,000 SF
