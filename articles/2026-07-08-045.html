Brookfield JV Buys Site For 6,500-Unit Project In Boston Suburbs

ACQUISITION

Bisnow News Feed — Tue, 07 Jul 2026 14:12:20 -0400

SUMMARY

A joint venture between Brookfield Properties and New England Development has acquired a significant portion of the former South Weymouth Naval Air Station site for $65 million. The developers plan a massive mixed-use project, including 6,500 housing units and 2 million square feet of commercial space, making it the largest residential and commercial development in Massachusetts. This acquisition marks a major milestone for the long-stalled redevelopment of the 1,400-acre site, with infrastructure improvements expected to begin this fall.

Project Notes: The site was previously controlled by Washington Capital Management after LStar Ventures' redevelopment efforts stalled. The Southfield Redevelopment Authority selected the Brookfield and New England Development venture as the new master developer in early 2020. New zoning guidelines were approved in November 2025.
