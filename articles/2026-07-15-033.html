Texas’ top construction permits for the week ending July 14, 2026

The Real Deal — Texas — Tue, 14 Jul 2026 18:30:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Construction / Development Permits

John Peter Smith Hospital is undertaking the largest construction project in Texas, a $300 million central utility plant in Fort Worth. The Houston Independent School District is developing a 216,000-square-foot career and technical center, while Amazon is building a 1.2 million-square-foot distribution center in Terrell. Additionally, the city of Dallas is renovating the Dallas Museum of Art, Schlosser Development is managing a tenant improvement project for Mission 42 Ventures in Austin, and SteriTek is upgrading its industrial facility in Lewisville with a new e-beam sterilization machine.
