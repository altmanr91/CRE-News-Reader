Colliers Brokers Sale of 24,500 SF Industrial Building in Stafford, Texas

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Wed, 08 Jul 2026 15:16:32 +0000

SUMMARY

Colliers has brokered the sale of a 24,500-square-foot industrial building in Stafford, Texas. The property, originally built in 2006 and renovated in 2023, was sold by an undisclosed seller. Colliers represented the seller in this transaction.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Address: 13715 N. Promenade Blvd., Stafford, TX

Size: 24,500 SF

Year Built: 2006

Notable Features: Building renovated in 2023.
