JLL Arranges $78.7M Loan for Refinancing of East Dallas Apartment Community

REFINANCE

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 04 Sep 2026 12:33:00 +0000

SUMMARY

JLL has arranged a $78.7 million refinancing loan for The Flynn at Live Oak, a newly constructed 327-unit apartment community in East Dallas. The loan was secured through Blue Owl Capital on behalf of the ownership partnership between Conor Commercial Real Estate and Globe Corp. The five-story property features a strong amenity package designed to appeal to residents.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: The Flynn at Live Oak

Address: 4931 Live Oak St, Dallas, TX*

Units: 327

Loan Amount: $78.7M

Loan Per Unit: $240,673/unit (calculated)

Completion: newly constructed

Notable Features: Amenities include a pool, outdoor courtyards with fire pits and grilling stations, a sky lounge, fitness center with outdoor yoga space, coworking lounge and a grab-and-go convenience store.
