Realterm Acquires 24-Acre Industrial Outdoor Storage Facility in Hutchins, Texas

ACQUISITION

REBusinessOnline — Wed, 08 Jul 2026 15:20:37 +0000

SUMMARY

Realterm, a global investment manager specializing in transportation-focused properties, has acquired a 24-acre industrial outdoor storage (IOS) facility in Hutchins, a Dallas suburb. The property includes three buildings totaling 43,800 square feet, featuring extensive maintenance and office space. The seller and transaction price were not disclosed.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Address: 1096 S. I-45 Service Road, Hutchins, TX

Size: 43,800 SF

Site Area: 24 acres

Notable Features: Features 23 drive-in doors, nine drive-through maintenance bays, and one wash bay.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: Realterm
