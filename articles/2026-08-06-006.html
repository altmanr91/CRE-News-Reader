Lee & Associates’ Q2 Report: Office, Retail Markets Gain Momentum as Industrial, Multifamily Face Headwinds

REBusinessOnline — Thu, 06 Aug 2026 11:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Quarterly Report

Lee & Associates' Q2 2026 North America Market Report indicates a varied recovery across commercial real estate sectors. The office and retail markets are gaining momentum, with office experiencing its best year since the pandemic, marked by declining vacancies and increased investor confidence in premium assets. Conversely, the industrial sector faces uneven demand due to trade uncertainties and a supply overhang, leading to a slight increase in vacancy rates and slowed rent growth. Multifamily fundamentals are stabilizing as new supply moderates, while investors and occupiers across all property types remain highly selective in an evolving market.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Leading industrial markets in net absorption over the past 12 months include Dallas-Fort Worth (28.7M SF), Phoenix (23.5M SF), Houston (18.3M SF), Indianapolis (15.5M SF), and Columbus (12.7M SF). Markets with the least empty industrial space at the end of Q2 2026 were Cleveland (4.4%), Detroit (4.9%), Chicago (5.4%), Pittsburgh (5.5%), St. Louis (5.5%), Columbus (6.4%), Los Angeles (6.5%), and Orange County (6.5%). Markets with the highest industrial vacancy rates include Austin (14.3%), San Francisco (12.5%), Phoenix (10.5%), Seattle (10.1%), and Charlotte (9.8%). Office markets surging include New York City, Dallas, Austin, Houston, San Francisco, and San Jose.
