Real Estate Firm Debuts With $126M Purchase Of Historic D.C. Building

ACQUISITION

Bisnow Latest News Feed — Mon, 31 Aug 2026 18:15:33 -0400

SUMMARY

A newly formed real estate firm, Montclif, in partnership with FCP, has acquired the historic Hamilton Square building in Washington D.C. for $126 million. This acquisition marks the debut transaction for Montclif, a company founded this year by experienced D.C. real estate professionals.
