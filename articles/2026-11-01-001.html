Sunday Summary: Sweet and Loudoun

Commercial Observer — Sun, 01 Nov 2026 14:00:07 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

This article analyzes the unexpected political backlash against data centers, particularly in Northern Virginia's Loudoun County, despite their rapid growth and economic benefits. It highlights the significant increase in data center inventory and credit extended to the sector, alongside record-low vacancy rates. The piece explores the arguments of developers like CloudHQ's Hossein Fateh, who counter criticisms regarding water usage, and introduces innovative solutions like LT350's rooftop data centers. The article also notes the emergence of alternative development hubs, such as Querétaro, Mexico, as the industry seeks new locations.
