Charles Cohen revives West Palm office project after resolving site foreclosure

The Real Deal — Miami — Fri, 10 Jul 2026 16:12:08 +0000

SUMMARY

Development

Charles Cohen's Cohen Brothers Realty is restarting its 400,000-square-foot West Palm Point office project in downtown West Palm Beach after resolving a foreclosure lawsuit on a $10 million site loan. The 25-story development, which includes ground-floor retail and an 11-story parking garage with a rooftop amenity deck, has begun the permitting process. This revival follows a period of legal challenges for Cohen, including other debt-related lawsuits in New York, and comes as downtown West Palm Beach has seen significant office market growth.
