Marcus & Millichap sells PNC Bank property in San Antonio

SALE

REJournals — Fri, 28 Aug 2026 17:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Marcus & Millichap has facilitated the sale of a newly constructed PNC Bank ground lease property in San Antonio. The property, an outparcel to a Sam's Club within the Stone Oak trade area, was marketed by Marcus & Millichap on behalf of a Texas-based developer and acquired by a private investor. The transaction involves a new 20-year ground lease with scheduled rent increases, capitalizing on the area's ongoing residential, multifamily, and mixed-use development.
