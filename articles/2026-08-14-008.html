Clay Development Breaks Ground on 63,440 SF Spec Industrial Project in North Houston

CONSTRUCTION

REBusinessOnline — Thu, 13 Aug 2026 15:06:12 +0000

SUMMARY

Clay Development & Construction has commenced construction on Kennedy Greens Distribution Center V, a 63,440-square-foot speculative industrial project in North Houston. This development represents the final phase of the 145-acre Kennedy Greens South Business Park. The facility is designed with a front-load configuration and 32-foot clear heights, with completion anticipated in the first quarter of next year.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Property Name: Kennedy Greens Distribution Center V

Address: 13740 John F Kennedy Blvd, Houston, TX*

Size: 63,440 SF

Completion: Q1 2027

Notable Features: Front-load configuration, 32-foot clear heights, 130-foot truck court depths, 2,600 square feet of office space, and 73 car parking spaces.

Project Notes: Final phase of the 145-acre Kennedy Greens South Business Park.

* Address sourced separately, not in original article.
