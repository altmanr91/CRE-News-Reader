Producer Prices Jump 1.3%, Largest Monthly Increase Since March 2022

Connect CRE — Thu, 15 Oct 2026 12:55:26 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Inflation Report

Wholesale prices experienced a significant surge in September, marking the largest monthly increase since March 2022, primarily driven by a sharp rise in energy costs. The Producer Price Index for final demand climbed 1.3% for the month, contributing to a 6.3% annual increase, the highest since December 2022. Goods prices, particularly energy and gasoline, were the main contributors to this inflationary trend, with services also seeing an increase.
