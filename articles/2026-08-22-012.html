Lee & Associates brokers pair of transactions in Orland Park

SALE

REJournals — Fri, 21 Aug 2026 16:01:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Lee & Associates recently brokered two separate transactions in Orland Park, Illinois. In one deal, an off-market 10,892-square-foot office building was sold by Marquette Bank to a private investor. Separately, Vision Land, LLC acquired a 13.3-acre retail land site, with plans for a future mixed-use commercial development.
