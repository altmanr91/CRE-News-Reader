NY Dirt: Hit the gym

The Real Deal — New York — Fri, 10 Jul 2026 11:30:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Retail Report

The New York City retail market is experiencing a significant surge in high-end wellness concepts, with boutique gyms and health-focused medical groups driving leasing activity. This trend is highlighted by major leases such as Chelsea Piers' 76,000-square-foot deal at 250 Water Street and Life Time's 71,000-square-foot lease in North Williamsburg. The increased demand for self-care and mental well-being, a lasting impact of the Covid-19 pandemic, has contributed to record-low retail vacancies in Manhattan's prime submarkets, with Madison Avenue and Soho reporting the lowest availability rates.

DATA POINTS

Madison Avenue and Soho availability rates: 8%
