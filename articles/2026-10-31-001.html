Gencom-Led JV Buys InterContinental in Downtown Miami for $300M

ACQUISITION

Commercial Observer — Fri, 30 Oct 2026 21:55:52 +0000

SUMMARY

A joint venture led by Gencom and White Bridge Capital has acquired the InterContinental Miami hotel for approximately $300 million. The 34-story, 652-key waterfront property, located in downtown Miami, was sold by China's Dajia Insurance Group, which had owned the asset since 2005. The buyers noted the hotel's recent capital investment, strong operating performance, and diversified business mix as key drivers for long-term value creation.
