Big tech still values the office, but in a different way

REJournals — Wed, 01 Jul 2026 10:04:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Office Strategy

A new report from Newmark indicates that major technology companies are reshaping their office footprints, focusing on fewer buildings, better locations, and higher-quality workplaces. The study, "Big Tech's U.S. Office Strategy Is Clear: Concentrate, Upgrade, Expand," reveals that the 100 largest office leases by U.S. tech occupiers over the past year totaled 16.5 million square feet across just 14 markets, with an average deal size of 165,000 square feet. This trend is largely driven by artificial intelligence firms and a desire to be near top talent and venture capital sources, particularly in the San Francisco Bay Area, New York City, and Boston. The report highlights that 83% of these leases were in top-tier properties, with most companies expanding their footprints rather than contracting, and lease terms are lengthening, signaling a continued commitment to physical office space as a strategic asset.
