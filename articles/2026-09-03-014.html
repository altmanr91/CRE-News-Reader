Flying Biscuit Cafe opens new location in Magnolia

LEASE

REJournals — Wed, 02 Sep 2026 16:05:00 +0000

SUMMARY

The Woodmont Company has secured a new lease with Flying Biscuit Cafe for a 3,925-square-foot space at The Egyptian in Magnolia, TX. The popular Southern-inspired restaurant is expected to open in November 2026, bringing its breakfast, brunch, and lunch offerings to the rapidly expanding Magnolia community. This lease brings The Egyptian to approximately 90% occupancy, reflecting strong demand along the FM 1488 corridor.
