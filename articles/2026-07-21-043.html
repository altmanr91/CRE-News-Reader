The $70B Quantum Boom Is Building A New Real Estate Sector

Bisnow News Feed — Mon, 20 Jul 2026 15:50:09 -0400

SUMMARY

Market Research / Emerging Sector

A new real estate sector is emerging to support the $70 billion global investment in quantum computing, distinct from the artificial intelligence boom. This "quantum real estate" includes research labs, data centers, and fabrication sites, with over 240 facilities worldwide and a $4 billion construction pipeline. Unlike traditional data centers, early quantum facilities are concentrated in educational hubs like Boston, Chicago, Los Angeles, and San Francisco, prioritizing access to researchers and "quantum ecosystems" over cheap electricity. JLL projects that commercial quantum advantage will emerge between 2030 and 2032, with the quantum computing facilities market eventually reaching about 5% of the total data center market.
