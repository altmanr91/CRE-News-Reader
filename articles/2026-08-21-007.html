Dwight Capital Provides Two HUD-Insured Loans Totaling $95.5M for Refinancing of Corpus Christi Apartments

REFINANCE

REBusinessOnline — Thu, 20 Aug 2026 15:22:20 +0000

SUMMARY

Dwight Capital has provided two HUD-insured loans totaling $95.5 million for the refinancing of two garden-style apartment complexes in Corpus Christi, Texas. The loans, originated through HUD's 223(f) program, will be used by Azali Homes to retire existing debt, fund property enhancements, cover closing costs, and establish replacement reserve accounts for future capital improvements. The properties, La Joya by Azali and Azali Heights, offer a combined 648 units with various amenities.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: La Joya by Azali / Azali Heights

Units: 648

Loan Amount: $95.5M

Notable Features: Both properties offer one-, two- and three-bedroom units with private patios and balconies, and amenities such as pools, business centers, fitness centers, dog parks and playgrounds.

Project Notes: La Joya by Azali was built in 2015 and Azali Heights was built in 2024. The loans were originated through HUD's 223(f) program.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

LENDER: Dwight Capital — Andrew Tichy

SPONSOR: Azali Homes
