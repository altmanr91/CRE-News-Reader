Rick Caruso, Jeff Worthe, Kilroy back Xavier Becerra’s run for California governor

The Real Deal — San Francisco — Mon, 13 Jul 2026 20:31:35 +0000

SUMMARY

Non-CRE / Political & Crime News

Several prominent California real estate figures and firms have shifted their political contributions to Xavier Becerra's gubernatorial campaign following the June primary. Billionaire Rick Caruso, Jeff Worthe of Worthe Real Estate Group, and REIT Kilroy Realty each donated $39,200 to Becerra. This move comes after their initial candidate of choice, Matt Mahan, did not advance past the primary. Becerra's campaign has garnered over half a million dollars from the real estate sector post-primary, indicating a significant pivot in industry support.
