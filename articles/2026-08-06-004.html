Return-to-Office Gained New Momentum in First Half of 2026: Report

Commercial Observer — Wed, 05 Aug 2026 17:35:44 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Office Report

A new report indicates that U.S. office attendance gained momentum in the first half of 2026, with visits rising 6 percent year-over-year and achieving the narrowest gap from pre-pandemic levels to date. However, the return-to-office trend shows significant regional divergence, with markets like Miami and New York nearing baseline visitation while Denver and Chicago lag considerably. The report suggests that while hybrid work remains prevalent, its popularity may be waning in some areas, and the increasing number of companies mandating office attendance could further shift dynamics in the latter half of the year.

DATA POINTS

U.S. office visits H1 2026 year-over-year increase: 6%

U.S. office visits H1 2025 year-over-year increase: 1.9%

U.S. office visits H1 2026 vs. 2019 levels: 31.2% below

Miami office attendance H1 2026 vs. 2019 levels: 11.5% below

Denver office attendance H1 2026 vs. 2019 levels: 40% below
