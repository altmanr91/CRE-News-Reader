BridgeInvest Provides $114M Refi for Robert Rivani ‘Concierge Office’ in Miami Beach

REFINANCE

Commercial Observer — Fri, 18 Sep 2026 13:22:34 +0000

SUMMARY

Developer Robert Rivani has secured a $114.3 million refinancing package for The Rivani, a 165,170-square-foot office and retail complex in Miami Beach. The new debt, provided by BridgeInvest, will retire $38 million in existing renovation debt and cover leasing costs to stabilize the property. The asset, which is 83 percent leased, has been transformed into a 'concierge office' with hospitality-inspired amenities, demonstrating strong demand for a differentiated workplace experience.

Project Notes: Property was acquired in 2024 for $62.5 million. Developer is seeking voter approval for a $50 million expansion to add 47,000 square feet above an existing parking garage, which would reduce parking spots from 712 to 387.
