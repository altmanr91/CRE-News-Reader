Peninsula Land & Capital scoops up San Jose offices from Hudson Pacific for $25M

ACQUISITION

The Real Deal — San Francisco — Mon, 06 Jul 2026 19:23:23 +0000

SUMMARY

Peninsula Land & Capital has acquired a 152,300-square-foot office complex in San Jose, California, from Hudson Pacific Properties for $25 million. The transaction, which closed in June, represents a significant discount from the property's assessed value and aligns with Peninsula Land & Capital's strategy of acquiring high-quality office and R&D assets in core Bay Area markets at favorable prices. The buildings are part of the larger Gateway Place campus near San Jose Mineta International Airport.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

SELLER: Hudson Pacific Properties

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

San Jose offices have been trading at a rapid clip this year, with several properties selling at discounts. Recent transactions include a 58,000-square-foot office building at 110 Baytech Drive for $13.5 million, offices at 350 East Plumeria Drive for $33 million, a research and office building at 5729 Fontanoso Way for $18.8 million, and the seven-building Rio Tech Office Park for $164.3 million. Another office and research building at 2581 Junction Avenue sold for $12.2 million, a 61.9 percent reduction from its 2018 sale price.
