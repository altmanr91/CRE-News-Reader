Entre Commercial Realty closes sale of 47,036-square-foot industrial facility in Elk Grove Village

SALE

REJournals — Mon, 31 Aug 2026 14:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Entre Commercial Realty has brokered the sale of a 47,036-square-foot industrial facility in Elk Grove Village, Illinois. The freestanding property was acquired by a local distribution company, which plans to establish its corporate headquarters there. The buyer was attracted to the property's rare freestanding nature and its strategic location within the O'Hare submarket, which will support the company's regional growth and accommodate its expanding operations.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Address: 2405 Estes Ave., Elk Grove Village, IL

Size: 47,036 SF

Notable Features: Freestanding industrial property with three docks, one drive-in door, high-end office buildout, 24’ clear height, and ample car parking.
