Glencoe mansion seeks $7M in bid to crack off-lakefront pricing record

The Real Deal — Chicago — Tue, 07 Jul 2026 18:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Property Listing / For Sale

A luxury single-family home in Glencoe, IL, located two blocks from Glencoe Beach, has been listed for sale at $6.95 million. The 10,000-square-foot residence, built in 2019, features six bedrooms and nine bathrooms. The asking price aims to set a new record for a non-lakefront home in the affluent North Shore suburb, reflecting its high-quality construction, design, and amenities.
