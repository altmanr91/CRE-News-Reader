With Bump In Defense Spending, Massachusetts Reaches For A Bigger Slice Of The Pie

Bisnow News Feed — Thu, 16 Jul 2026 16:49:39 -0400

SUMMARY

Infrastructure Investment / Government Policy

Massachusetts is actively pursuing a larger share of increased federal defense spending, leveraging its strong innovation economy, academic institutions, and existing defense contractor presence. The Healey-Driscoll administration launched the Strategic Hub for Innovation, Exchange and Leadership in Defense (SHIELD) to foster growth in the state's defense sector. This initiative aims to attract defense contractors expanding their real estate footprints, with recent examples including a proposed 1M SF manufacturing facility by VulcanForms in Devens and GE Aerospace's $42M investment in its Lynn manufacturing plant. The state's defense sector contributed $50B to the Massachusetts economy in fiscal year 2024, surpassing its life sciences sector, and officials are optimistic that continued federal investment will further boost the local economy.
