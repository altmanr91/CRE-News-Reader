Battersea Power Station Lands £280M Refinancing For Final Phases

REFINANCE

Bisnow Latest News Feed — Tue, 13 Oct 2026 12:22:38 -0400

SUMMARY

Battersea Power Station has secured a £280 million refinancing package from a consortium of banks to fund the ongoing development of its final 16 acres. This significant loan will support the completion of the remaining phases of the iconic London project.
