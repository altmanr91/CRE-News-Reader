NAI Wisinski closes sale of 13,262-square-foot commercial building in Grand Rapids market

SALE

REJournals — Wed, 09 Sep 2026 13:31:00 +0000

SUMMARY

NAI Wisinski of West Michigan successfully brokered the sale of a 13,262-square-foot multi-tenant commercial building at 312 Baldwin Street in Jenison, MI. The sales strategy for the property, located in the Grand Rapids market, was adjusted mid-process to target owner-users after a long-term tenant announced plans to vacate a significant portion of the space. This shift in positioning, coupled with active marketing of the vacant space for lease, generated substantial buyer interest and ultimately led to a sale to an owner-user.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: commercial

Address: 312 Baldwin Street, Jenison, MI

Size: 13,262 SF

Notable Features: Multi-tenant commercial property.

Project Notes: Approximately 3,500 square feet (26% of the building) became vacant during the sales process, prompting a shift in marketing strategy to include owner-users.
