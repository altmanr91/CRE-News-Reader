Associated Bank Finalizes Loan for Twelve-Acre Retail Project in OH

LOAN

Connect CRE — Tue, 25 Aug 2026 21:48:26 +0000

SUMMARY

Associated Bank has finalized a $9 million loan to GTZ Properties for the development of Trailhead Crossing, a multi-building retail project in Delaware, Ohio. The twelve-acre development will feature several freestanding buildings and a three-tenant strip, with a later phase planned to include a big-box national retailer. This transaction marks the 18th loan provided by Associated Bank to GTZ Properties' principal.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: retail

Address: 1135-1131 US-36, Delaware, OH

Loan Amount: $9.0M

Project Notes: One pad-ready site is pending sale to a national daycare company. A later phase will be anchored by a big-box national retailer.
