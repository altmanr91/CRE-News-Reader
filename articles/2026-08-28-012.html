CanTex Capital acquires 18,535-square-foot industrial building in New Braunfels

ACQUISITION

REJournals — Fri, 28 Aug 2026 16:05:00 +0000

SUMMARY

CanTex Capital has acquired an 18,535-square-foot industrial building in New Braunfels, Texas, marking its first investment in the Austin-San Antonio corridor. The property, situated on six acres, includes industrial outdoor storage (IOS) and is fully leased to a national tenant. This acquisition aligns with CanTex Capital's strategy to expand its portfolio of IOS assets in Texas markets with high barriers to entry, capitalizing on the property's strategic location and scarcity of similar assets.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Address: 7570 Interstate 35, New Braunfels, TX

Size: 18,535 SF

Site Area: 6 acres

Notable Features: Includes industrial outdoor storage (IOS) and offers visibility and access along the primary freight and population corridor connecting Austin and San Antonio.
