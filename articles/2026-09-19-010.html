Avison Young Arranges $115M in Agency Financing for Queens Apartment Building

LOAN

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 18 Sep 2026 14:25:50 +0000

SUMMARY

Avison Young has secured $115 million in Fannie Mae financing for a 301-unit apartment building in Long Island City, Queens. Wells Fargo provided the loan for the property, which is the second phase of the Eagle Lofts Collection development. The financing was arranged on behalf of the owner, Rockrose Development.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Eagle Lofts Collection, Phase II

Units: 301

Loan Amount: $115.0M

Loan Per Unit: $382,060/unit (calculated)

Notable Features: Amenities include a spa, speakeasy sports parlor with bowling, golf simulator, ski simulator, rooftop pool, fitness center, children's playroom, and pet washing station. 30% of units are designated as affordable housing.

Project Notes: Loan provided through Fannie Mae's Delegated Underwriting Service (DUS) program. This property represents Phase II of the Eagle Lofts Collection.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

SPONSOR: Rockrose Development

LENDER: Wells Fargo

MORTGAGE BROKER: Avison Young — Scott Singer; Andy Singer; Kevin Swartz; Kathleen McSharry
