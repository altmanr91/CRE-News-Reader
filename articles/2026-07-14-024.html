Judge slams Russian investor who defied court orders in Trump Palace condo case

The Real Deal — Miami — Mon, 13 Jul 2026 20:55:45 +0000

SUMMARY

CRE Legal / Regulatory

A Miami-Dade Circuit judge has ordered a Russian investor to pay $2.3 million to a former Russian government official in a dispute over the sale of an LLC that owned a condo unit at Trump Palace in Sunny Isles Beach. The judge issued a default judgment against the investor, Svyatoslav Mangushev, after finding he repeatedly violated court orders, failed to comply with discovery, and orchestrated a scheme to delay trial through false medical claims. The court found Mangushev breached the agreement to acquire Trump Palace 5507 LLC, failed to pay the agreed-upon amount, and committed fraud and embezzlement related to the property's sale proceeds.
