25 E. Washington Wins AIA’s 2026 People’s Choice Award After Establishing Vintage Class-A Building Class Status

REJournals — Wed, 21 Oct 2026 09:43:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Development

The 25 E. Washington office building in Chicago, IL, has received the AIA's 2026 "People's Choice Award" in the retail and hospitality category, following a $20 million renovation completed in 2025. The extensive redevelopment, highlighted by the creation of The Atrium Club, has repositioned the historic Daniel Burnham-designed property as a model for "Vintage Class A" office space. The project demonstrates how a century-old building can be modernized to offer high-quality amenities, flexibility, and a tenant experience comparable to new Class A properties.
