Limited supply, strong demand boosts Chicago-area multifamily sector

REJournals — Wed, 26 Aug 2026 09:25:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Multifamily Report

Chicago's multifamily market continues to demonstrate robust performance, with strong demand and rising rents, according to Cross Street's Q2 2026 report. The city's average effective apartment rent increased 4.3% year-over-year to $1,974 per unit, while the vacancy rate held steady at 4.4%. This strength is attributed to a relatively modest supply of new units compared to demand, keeping vacancy rates below the national average and supporting rent growth. Adaptive reuse projects are becoming a significant source of new apartment supply, particularly in downtown Chicago, where they are expected to account for nearly 44% of all units delivered this year.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Chicago's multifamily market is characterized by limited new construction relative to demand, which is supporting above-average rent growth and favorable long-term fundamentals. Concessions are declining in both downtown and neighborhood submarkets. Monthly leasing activity exceeded 2025 levels throughout the first half of 2026. While other markets face challenges from new apartment construction, Chicago's limited supply is an advantage.
