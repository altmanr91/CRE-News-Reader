Columbus office market nets fifth straight quarter of positive net absorption

REJournals — Thu, 23 Jul 2026 10:08:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Office Report

The Columbus, OH office market recorded its fifth consecutive quarter of positive net absorption in Q2 2026, totaling 68,171 square feet and bringing the year-to-date total to 179,294 square feet. This steady leasing activity has contributed to a gradual decline in the overall vacancy rate, which fell 30 basis points to 20.3%. The recovery is primarily driven by suburban submarkets, which accounted for the majority of positive absorption, while downtown Columbus continues to face challenges with negative absorption and higher vacancy rates. Limited new construction is also helping stabilize market fundamentals, with only 195,048 square feet currently under construction.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

The Columbus office market's overall vacancy rate declined 30 basis points to 20.3% in Q2 2026. Suburban office submarkets accounted for 71,465 square feet of positive absorption, while downtown Columbus posted negative absorption of 3,294 square feet. No new office buildings were delivered in H1 2026, with only 195,048 square feet remaining under construction. Q2 2026 leasing volume was 620,323 square feet, 20% below the 16-year Q2 average. Average asking rents held steady at $23.13 per square foot, representing 4.1% year-to-date growth. Average office sale prices climbed to $104 per square foot, but excluding The Pointe at Polaris sale, the average was $57 per square foot.
