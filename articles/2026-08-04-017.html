IPA Team Closes Sale of Pomona Multifamily

SALE

Connect CRE — Mon, 03 Aug 2026 21:38:07 +0000

SUMMARY

Institutional Property Advisors (IPA) has completed the sale of Terramonte at Foothill, a 138-unit multifamily property in Pomona, for $36.7 million. The transaction, which represents one of only four market-rate multifamily sales of over 100 units in Pomona in the last five years, highlights the limited supply of such assets in the market. IPA represented the seller, Positive Investments, and secured the buyer, Sack Capital Partners.
