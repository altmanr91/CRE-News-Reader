REItrades to Launch AI-Powered Marketplace to Streamline CRE Investment Sales

REJournals — Thu, 17 Sep 2026 14:08:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Non-CRE / Business News

REItrades is launching an AI-powered commercial real estate marketplace designed to streamline investment sales of properties valued at $5 million and above. The platform, which underwent a beta soft launch in 2025, connects brokers and sellers with a curated network of over 3,400 pre-vetted buyers, each with at least $10 million in assets under management. It offers features like private listing options, an AI-powered marketing engine for generating professional content, and a secure interactive deal room for managing offers and due diligence, aiming to centralize and accelerate the fragmented CRE sales process.
