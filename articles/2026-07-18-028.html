Lendlease and Magellan’s condo tower sees sales spike in Chicago’s Lakeshore East

The Real Deal — Chicago — Fri, 17 Jul 2026 15:09:28 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Residential Sales

Sales volume at Cirrus Condominiums, a 47-story luxury tower in Chicago's Lakeshore East, more than doubled in the first half of 2026 compared to the previous year, with 46 units closed and 15 under contract. This surge in sales, driven by scant inventory and an aging population returning to the city, signals a comeback for Chicago's downtown condo market. The property, developed by Lendlease and Magellan Development, welcomed its first residents in early 2022 and offers 350 condos.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

The lack of inventory in Lakeshore East is reflected city-wide, with only 3,337 homes on the market for sale in Chicago in May 2026, a 30% decline from May 2025. Chicago showed the strongest rate of growth nationally at 6.5% annually among 20 major cities.
