IPA Arranges Sale of 288-Unit Apartment Complex in Red Oak, Texas

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Tue, 20 Oct 2026 15:28:55 +0000

SUMMARY

Institutional Property Advisors (IPA) has facilitated the sale of Ovilla at Legacy Square, a 288-unit apartment complex in Red Oak, Texas. The property, developed by Thompson Realty Capital and Trez Capital, was acquired by Fogelman Properties, which intends to implement capital improvements. This transaction highlights continued investor interest in well-located multifamily assets in the Dallas-Fort Worth metroplex.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Ovilla at Legacy Square

Address: 241 N Overlook Dr, Red Oak, TX*

Units: 288

Year Built: 2023

Site Area: 18 acres

Notable Features: Amenities include a pool, clubhouse, outdoor grilling and dining stations, and a dog park.
