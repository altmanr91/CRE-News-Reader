MMCC Arranges $8.2M Loan for Refinancing of Self-Storage Facility in DeSoto, Texas

REFINANCE

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 07 Aug 2026 15:25:21 +0000

SUMMARY

Marcus & Millichap Capital Corp. (MMCC) has facilitated an $8.2 million refinancing loan for Grizzly Self Storage, a self-storage facility located in DeSoto, Texas. The 10-year loan was arranged by Doug Brooks of MMCC for an undisclosed borrower, with the direct lender also remaining unnamed.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: self-storage

Property Name: Grizzly Self Storage

Address: 1480 N Hampton Rd, DeSoto, TX*

Size: 132,265 SF

Loan Amount: $8.2M

Loan Per SF: $62/SF (calculated)

Notable Features: Offers both personal and commercial storage spaces.

* Address sourced separately, not in original article.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

MORTGAGE BROKER: Marcus & Millichap Capital Corp. (MMCC) — Doug Brooks
