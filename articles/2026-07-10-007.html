Newmark Arranges Sale of 120,923 SF Office Building in North Dallas

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Thu, 09 Jul 2026 15:12:38 +0000

SUMMARY

Newmark has facilitated the sale of 8020 Park Lane, a 120,923-square-foot office building in North Dallas. The five-story property, built in 2015, was fully leased to a single tenant at the time of the transaction. Newmark also arranged acquisition financing for the buyer.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Property Name: 8020 Park Lane

Address: 8020 Park Lane, Dallas, TX

Size: 120,923 SF

Occupancy at Sale: 100.0%

Year Built: 2015

Notable Features: Located within The Shops at Park Lane mixed-use district.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE
