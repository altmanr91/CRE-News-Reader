Highland Partners drops $119M on Magellan’s River North tower

ACQUISITION

The Real Deal — Chicago — Mon, 13 Jul 2026 22:30:13 +0000

SUMMARY

Highland Partners has acquired the 298-unit Exhibit on Superior apartment tower in Chicago's River North neighborhood for $119 million from Magellan Development. This transaction marks Highland Partners' largest multifamily purchase in Chicago to date, reflecting their strategy to expand their presence in the city's multifamily market. The buyer believes the acquisition price is below replacement cost, indicating confidence in the property's future value appreciation.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Chicago's multifamily market is experiencing constrained new supply due to increasing development costs and bureaucratic hurdles. The city led the U.S. in rent growth through the end of 2025 at 5.4 percent. A supply mismatch, with only 370 units delivered in 2025 against 1,700 units absorbed, pushed local vacancy down to 5.1 percent, particularly insulating luxury assets.
