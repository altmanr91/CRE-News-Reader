Shopoff Sells Site of Proposed Newport Beach Condo Development

SALE

Connect CRE — Wed, 05 Aug 2026 21:31:48 +0000

SUMMARY

Shopoff Realty Investments has sold the 1.06-acre Park Palm site, entitled for 23 townhomes, to Shea Homes. The property is part of Shopoff's larger Uptown Newport Village master-planned community in Newport Beach, which the firm acquired in 2010 and subsequently entitled for a mixed-use residential village. This sale will complete Phase I of the master plan, with construction on the townhomes expected to begin in 2027.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: condo

Property Name: Park Palm

Units: 23

Completion: 2028-2029

Phase I: Park Palm will round out Phase I

Site Area: 1.06 acres

Project Notes: Site is part of the 25-acre Uptown Newport Village master-planned community, which was acquired by Shopoff in December 2010 and entitled in 2013 for 1,244 residential units, 11,500 SF of commercial/retail space, and two one-acre parks.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

SELLER: Shopoff Realty Investments

BUYER: Shea Homes
