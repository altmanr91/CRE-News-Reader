Williamsburg Mixed-Use Sale Completes “Round Trip” for Seller

SALE

Connect CRE — Fri, 31 Jul 2026 21:35:03 +0000

SUMMARY

Marcus & Millichap has brokered the sale of two mixed-use buildings in Brooklyn's Williamsburg neighborhood for $16.4 million. The transaction represents a successful "round trip" for the seller, who had previously acquired the property in 2021 for $12.3 million. The buyer utilized a 1031 exchange to complete the acquisition of the turnkey property, which is situated on the prominent Kent Avenue corridor.
