On the edge of the next growth cycle for industrial real estate?

REJournals — Thu, 30 Jul 2026 10:02:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Industrial Outlook

Colliers' Q2 2026 U.S. Industrial Outlook indicates that the national industrial real estate market is nearing the end of its post-construction-boom adjustment, with occupier demand now outpacing new supply for the first time since the construction surge began. This shift, marked by nearly 59 million square feet of net absorption exceeding 53.4 million square feet of new deliveries, suggests the market is effectively working through the speculative development wave of 2023-2024. The national vacancy rate has edged down to 7.3%, and while rental rates show a mixed picture with a slight national decline, the construction pipeline is expanding again, reflecting cautious developer confidence. Colliers anticipates healthy demand will continue through 2026, leading to a more balanced and sustainable market.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

The South accounted for roughly half of all national net absorption in Q2 2026, with Houston leading the nation at 7.5 million square feet, followed by Dallas-Fort Worth, Atlanta, Los Angeles, and Phoenix. Leasing activity was driven by manufacturing users, third-party logistics firms, retailers, food and beverage companies, and businesses tied to data center development. New industrial deliveries reached their lowest quarterly total since 2016.
