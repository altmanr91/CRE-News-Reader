Klyde Warren Park expansion set to bring $4B in real estate value to Dallas

The Real Deal — Texas — Wed, 01 Jul 2026 18:10:33 +0000

SUMMARY

Development

The planned 1.7-acre expansion of Klyde Warren Park in Dallas is projected to generate approximately $3.5 billion in new real estate value through investment and tourism. The expansion, which includes a new green space called Jacobs Lawn and a two-story, 24,000-square-foot event and retail building, aims to further enhance the area's economic vitality. This initiative builds on the park's existing impact, which has already contributed an estimated $5.9 billion in real estate value to the Arts District and Uptown, resulting in significant incremental tax revenue for the city and county.
