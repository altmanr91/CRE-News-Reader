Owner-Occupant Pays $25M for East Rutherford Industrial

SALE

Connect CRE — Fri, 23 Oct 2026 20:57:50 +0000

SUMMARY

An owner-occupant has acquired an 86,000-square-foot industrial warehouse at 180 Manor Rd. in East Rutherford, NJ, for $25 million. The property will serve as the buyer's new headquarters, allowing for expansion while remaining in the area. The transaction highlights the challenges owner-users face in securing quality industrial properties in high-barrier-to-entry markets.
