Amid Higher-for-Longer Mortgage Rates, Multifamily Pros Find a Silver Lining

Commercial Observer — Fri, 04 Sep 2026 10:43:03 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

This article analyzes how persistently high mortgage rates, which have remained elevated since late 2022 and spiked to nearly 8% in late 2023, are creating a silver lining for the multifamily and single-family rental sectors. With homeownership becoming significantly more expensive than renting, demand for rental housing is surging as households are priced out of buying. This shift, coupled with a contracting pipeline for new home construction, is expected to drive healthy rent growth and increased deal flow for multifamily assets, which also benefit from shorter lease terms allowing for quicker repricing in an inflationary environment.
