Logistics Firm Highline Commerce Grows to 60K SF at Industry City, Thanks to Robots

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Fri, 17 Jul 2026 16:07:55 +0000

SUMMARY

Third-party logistics provider Highline Commerce has renewed its lease and expanded its industrial footprint at Industry City in Brooklyn, bringing its total space to 60,000 square feet. This five-year deal marks the company's second expansion at the mixed-use campus in four years, driven by growth in e-commerce fulfillment, partly attributed to the integration of humanoid robots from a fellow Industry City tenant. The expanded space will primarily support fulfillment operations, complemented by office and social areas.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Property Name: Industry City

Address: 67 34th Street, Brooklyn, NY

Size: 60,000 SF

Rental Rate: $28.00/SF/yr

Notable Features: Mixed-use commercial campus with on-site manufacturing, printing, and fulfillment capabilities; utilizes humanoid robots for order fulfillment.

Project Notes: Highline Commerce initially leased 27,000 SF in 2022, expanding to 42,000 SF during its first four-year lease.
