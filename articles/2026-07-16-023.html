Luxury “landmaxxing”: The billionaire push for private compounds

The Real Deal — Miami — Wed, 15 Jul 2026 21:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

The ultra-luxury real estate market is experiencing a significant trend of "landmaxxing," where billionaires and wealthy individuals are acquiring multiple adjacent properties to create expansive private compounds. This strategy allows for greater privacy, extensive amenities, and the preservation of views, particularly in highly desirable waterfront locations. Notable figures like Ken Griffin, Jeff Bezos, Larry Ellison, and David MacNeil have been actively assembling these large estates in South Florida and other luxury markets, driving up demand for buildable land and luxury properties despite broader housing market strains.

DATA POINTS

U.S. luxury property searches (Jan-May): doubled year-over-year

Searches for buildable land: jumped 97 percent

Average luxury sales volume (first five months): rose 2.8 percent year-over-year

Median luxury price (first five months): $1.8 million (up 4.7 percent)
