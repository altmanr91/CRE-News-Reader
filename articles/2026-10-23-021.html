'Illegitimate' Or 'Leveling The Playing Field'?: Why Habitability Cases Are Surging In Eviction Court

Bisnow Latest News Feed — Thu, 22 Oct 2026 19:11:57 -0400

SUMMARY

CRE Legal / Regulatory

Landlords and tenant advocates are at odds over the legitimacy of a rising number of habitability claims being filed in eviction courts. The surge in these cases has sparked debate, with landlords often viewing them as tactics to delay eviction, while tenant advocates argue they are essential for ensuring safe and livable housing conditions.
