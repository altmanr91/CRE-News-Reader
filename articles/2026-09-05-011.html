Marcus & Millichap brokers sale of net-leased bank in Dallas

SALE

REJournals — Fri, 04 Sep 2026 16:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

DP Investments LP, a California limited partnership, has acquired a newly constructed 2,300-square-foot net-leased bank property in Dallas from seller 3662 Investors LP, a Texas limited partnership. The seller also developed the property, which was built in 2026. Marcus & Millichap brokered the transaction, which involved resolving complex title matters and the seller agreeing to complete a new plat post-closing.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: retail

Address: 3230 W Camp Wisdom Rd, Dallas, TX

Size: 2,300 SF

Year Built: 2026

Site Area: 0.63 acres

Project Notes: Seller agreed to complete processing of a new plat following the closing. Complex title matters were resolved during the transaction.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

SELLER: 3662 Investors LP
