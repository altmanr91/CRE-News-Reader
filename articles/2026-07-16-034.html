Fremont Marriott fetches $53M amid sea of bargain-basement distress sales

SALE

The Real Deal — San Francisco — Wed, 15 Jul 2026 19:22:42 +0000

SUMMARY

SRE Acquisitions has purchased the 357-room Fremont Marriott Silicon Valley for approximately $53 million from Ashford Hospitality Trust. This transaction represents a positive indicator for the lodging market, which has seen numerous distressed sales in the Bay Area. The sale price reflects investor confidence in Fremont, a growing hub for artificial intelligence and technology manufacturing, and provides an opportunity for new capital to revitalize the property.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

The hotel market in Silicon Valley has been slower to recover compared to the housing and office sectors. Another hotel in Fremont, the 151-room Hyatt Place Fremont/Silicon Valley, recently sold for $13.2 million, significantly below its assessed value of $20.1 million.
