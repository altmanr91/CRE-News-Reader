$81M Nora Hotel Debuts in West Palm Beach

DEVELOPMENT

Connect CRE — Wed, 28 Oct 2026 14:47:18 +0000

SUMMARY

The Nora Hotel, a new 201-key luxury hotel, has officially opened in West Palm Beach's Nora District. Developed by a partnership including NDT Development, Place Projects, and Wheelock Street Capital, the project has an estimated value of $81.7 million. BD Hotels, a key partner in the hotel's concept, secured a $75 million construction loan for the development in 2024. The hotel features multiple dining options, a rooftop pool, and a spa, contributing to the area's evolving mixed-use landscape.
