Stewards Sets Up $240M Deal for Two South Florida Multifamily Properties

ACQUISITION

Commercial Observer — Tue, 15 Sep 2026 22:05:59 +0000

SUMMARY

Stewards, a financial and real estate company, has signed letters of intent to acquire two multifamily properties in Broward County, Florida, for an aggregate implied value of $240 million. The transaction will involve Stewards acquiring the equity interests in the property-owning entities and issuing new stock to the sellers. This acquisition is part of Stewards' strategy to expand its real assets platform across private credit, real estate, and technology.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Pixl apartments / Envy apartments

Address: 4300 Northwest Ninth Court, Plantation, FL / 425 East Atlantic Boulevard, Pompano Beach, FL

Units: 544

Sale Price: $240.0M

Year Built: 2025

Project Notes: The $240 million figure represents the properties' aggregate implied value, including existing property-level debt, rather than the value of stock Stewards will issue for the deal. Pixl was completed in 2025, and Envy opened in 2021.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: Stewards — Shaun Quin, CEO and President; Glen Steward, Chairman

DEVELOPER/SPONSOR: Invesca Development Group

SPONSOR PIPELINE

1818 Park: Mixed-use building in Hollywood, FL, acquired in 2025 for $190 million in an all-stock deal. Refinanced in June with a $69 million senior mortgage and a $10 million mezzanine loan.
