Aerospace manufacturer moves HQ to Buda’s defense hub

The Real Deal — Texas — Wed, 22 Jul 2026 17:30:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Lease

Even Platforms Incorporated, an aerospace manufacturer, is relocating its headquarters from the Dallas metro to Buda, Texas, after receiving a $50,000 incentive from the Buda City Council. The company will occupy a 9,000-square-foot facility within Tower Business Park, joining other defense companies in what is becoming a regional hub for the aerospace and defense industry. The incentive will support electrical service upgrades at the new facility, which Even Platforms plans to open for high school tours and internships to help revitalize the machine shop industry.
