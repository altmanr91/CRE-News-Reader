Berkadia Arranges Sale, Financing of 270-Unit VA Multifamily Property

SALE

Connect CRE — Mon, 20 Jul 2026 22:27:29 +0000

SUMMARY

Berkadia has facilitated the sale and financing of Masons Keepe, a 270-unit garden-style multifamily community in Manassas, Virginia. The property was sold by University Village Apartments, LLC, and acquired by Bridge Investment Group. Berkadia also secured a five-year, fixed-rate loan through Freddie Mac for the buyer.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Masons Keepe

Address: 9000 Landings Drive, Manassas, VA

Units: 270

Year Built: 2004

Notable Features: Garden-style community with one- and two-bedroom apartment homes, offering convenient access to employment centers in Prince William County and the greater Washington, D.C. metropolitan area.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

SELLER: University Village Apartments, LLC

BUYER: Bridge Investment Group

LENDER: Freddie Mac

MORTGAGE BROKER: Berkadia — Brian Crivella, Senior Managing Director; Bill Gribbin, Managing Director; Yalda Ghamarian, Managing Director; Jack Canepa, Associate Director; Drew White, Senior Managing Director; Brad Williamson, Senior Managing Director; Christopher Ellis, Senior Managing Director

FINANCING

Five-year, fixed-rate loan with a three-year interest-only period, a 35-year amortization schedule, and a 70% loan-to-value ratio.
