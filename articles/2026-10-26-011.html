Essex Realty Group closes sale of 12-unit multifamily building in Chicago’s Rogers Park neighborhood

SALE

REJournals — Mon, 26 Oct 2026 13:47:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Essex Realty Group brokered the sale of a 12-unit multifamily apartment building located at 1706–1716 W. Farwell Avenue in Chicago's Rogers Park neighborhood. The property, which underwent a complete renovation in 2013, was acquired by a California-based 1031 exchange investor. The building features a mix of two-bedroom and three-bedroom duplex apartments with modern amenities.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Address: 1706–1716 W. Farwell Avenue, Chicago, IL

Units: 12

Notable Features: Completely renovated in 2013; features eight two-bedroom, two-bathroom apartments and four three-bedroom, three-bathroom duplex apartments; unit amenities include hardwood floors, granite countertops, stainless steel appliances, in-unit laundry, and individual tenant-paid HVAC. Located near public transportation and Loyola University Chicago.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

SELLER BROKER: Essex Realty Group, LLC — Doug Imber, Principal; Kate Varde, Principal; Rick Ofman, Managing Director

BUYER: California-based 1031 exchange investor
