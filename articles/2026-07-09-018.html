Norges, Asana join forces on $500M retail acquisition spree

ACQUISITION

The Real Deal — National — Wed, 08 Jul 2026 16:30:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Norges Bank Investment Management and Asana Partners have formed a $500 million joint venture to acquire retail properties across the United States. The new fund, Asana Partners Strategic Partners I, will target grocery-anchored centers, unanchored centers, street retail, and mixed-use properties. The partnership reflects a shared belief in the resilience of neighborhood retail real estate.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: retail

Project Notes: The fund is launching with a 50% stake in an undisclosed portfolio of grocery-anchored retail centers.
