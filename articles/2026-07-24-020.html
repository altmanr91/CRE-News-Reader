COGE greenlights permit, city contract ballot questions

The Real Deal — New York — Thu, 23 Jul 2026 23:31:03 +0000

SUMMARY

Infrastructure Investment / Government Policy

New York City voters will consider five proposed charter amendments in November, aimed at reforming the city's permitting and procurement systems. Key proposals include establishing a centralized hub for construction permits, streamlining the waterfront permitting process under the Department of Buildings, and expediting the city's procurement of goods and services. The measures also seek to give the city more flexibility in repurposing small public lots and acquiring office space, as well as setting a reserves target for the city's rainy day fund and simplifying outdoor dining applications. The Commission on Government Efficiency (COGE) unanimously approved these recommendations, with the goal of making New York more affordable and efficient.
