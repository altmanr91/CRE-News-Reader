Gotham wins final go-ahead for Brooklyn waterfront apartment towers

DEVELOPMENT

The Real Deal — New York — Fri, 17 Jul 2026 17:50:04 +0000

SUMMARY

The Gotham Organization has received final City Council approval for its Monitor Point development in Greenpoint, Brooklyn, which will feature 1,324 apartments across two towers. The approval followed a last-minute agreement to increase the number of affordable units to 662, including homes for seniors and formerly homeless residents. The project, located at 40-56 Quay Street on a 2-acre site, also includes significant public benefits such as funding for Bushwick Inlet Park and transit accessibility improvements.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Monitor Point

Address: 40-56 Quay Street, New York City, NY

Units: 1,324

Site Area: 2 acres

Notable Features: Includes 662 affordable units, 161 for seniors and 110 supportive units for formerly homeless residents. Features 50,000 square feet of public waterfront and is near Bushwick Inlet Park.

Project Notes: The project received final City Council approval after an agreement to increase affordable housing and provide $300,000 annually for Bushwick Inlet Park maintenance, two public bathrooms, and Nassau Avenue G train station accessibility improvements. The site was previously a truck wash station owned by the Metropolitan Transportation Authority.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

DEVELOPER/SPONSOR: Gotham Organization

OWNER: Metropolitan Transportation Authority
