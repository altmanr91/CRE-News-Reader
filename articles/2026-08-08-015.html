Selldorf Architects Signs Long-Term Renewal at 860 Broadway

LEASE

Connect CRE — Fri, 07 Aug 2026 21:53:29 +0000

SUMMARY

Selldorf Architects has renewed its 13,815-square-foot office lease at 860 Broadway for another 10 years. The architecture firm has been a tenant in the building for two decades, and its decision to recommit to the property underscores the enduring appeal of the iconic Manhattan office building and the long-term strength of the market, according to the landlord's broker.
