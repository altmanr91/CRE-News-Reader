Chicago Fire Stadium Nears $425M TIF Boost

Bisnow News Feed — Mon, 13 Jul 2026 13:08:41 -0400

SUMMARY

Infrastructure Investment / Government Policy

A city committee in Chicago has approved a $425 million tax increment financing (TIF) boost for the new Chicago Fire stadium at The 78 development. This revised TIF plan will transfer funds from a nearby TIF district to cover infrastructure costs, reducing the financial risk for the developer. The $750 million stadium, privately financed by the team's owner, is expected to open in Q2 2028 and serve as a significant anchor for the long-planned mixed-use development.

FINANCING

$425M in tax increment financing (TIF) approved by city committee for infrastructure costs.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

The Canal/Congress TIF District generates over $65M in new TIF revenue annually, while the Roosevelt/Clark District is expected to generate an average of $17M annually over the next 15 years.
