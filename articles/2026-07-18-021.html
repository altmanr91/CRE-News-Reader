Miami design board swoons over OKO Lilli, 13th Floor’s Nobu condo towers

The Real Deal — Miami — Fri, 17 Jul 2026 18:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Development

Two luxury condominium towers in Miami, the OKO Lilli Tower in Edgewater and the Nobu-branded residential building in Brickell, received favorable recommendations from the Miami Urban Development Review Board. OKO Group's 53-story, 117-unit OKO Lilli Tower, designed by Adrian Smith + Gordon Gill Architecture and ODP Architecture and Design, will feature Aman-curated amenities and a public baywalk. 13th Floor Investments and Key International's 75-story, 321-unit 619 Brickell by Nobu, designed by Foster + Partners and Sieger Suarez Architects, is planned for a site adjacent to a historic church, which will remain intact. Both projects are advancing through the city's planning process, with the Nobu tower having already secured over $1 billion in unit reservations.
