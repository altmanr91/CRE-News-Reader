A real rarity: Sturtevant manufacturing campus a unicorn in today’s industrial market

REJournals — Thu, 13 Aug 2026 10:02:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Property Listing / For Sale

Cushman & Wakefield | Boerke has been selected to market for sale the former Cree Lighting manufacturing campus in Sturtevant, Wisconsin. This nearly 642,000-square-foot facility on 30 acres is considered a rare offering in the Upper Midwest industrial market due to its combination of advanced manufacturing infrastructure, corporate office, engineering, and research space. The property is being offered below replacement cost, providing a faster and more cost-effective path to production for owner-occupants compared to new construction, which typically involves lengthy timelines and significant capital investment. The broker anticipates strong demand from large advanced-manufacturing owner-occupants, particularly those in energy, electrical equipment, power-generation technologies, life sciences, and medical products, as well as companies evaluating the greater Chicago market.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

The U.S. manufacturing sector is experiencing positive momentum driven by reshoring, supply chain diversification, automation investments, and domestic capital expenditures. Vacancy statistics for Kenosha County, Racine County, and Milwaukee South industrial submarkets are distorted by five specific buildings totaling over 3.5 million square feet delivered or vacated between Q4 2022 and Q3 2024. Excluding these buildings, the underlying market is one of the tightest industrial markets in the region, tightening for two-and-a-half consecutive years. Southeast Wisconsin has become a major destination for technology and advanced-manufacturing investment, with Microsoft completing its first Mount Pleasant data center and Eli Lilly developing a $4 billion investment in Kenosha County.
