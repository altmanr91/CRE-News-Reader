PwC’s Move to Century City Is Another Blow to Downtown L.A.

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Tue, 07 Jul 2026 20:00:28 +0000

SUMMARY

PricewaterhouseCoopers (PwC) is relocating its Los Angeles office from Downtown L.A. to Century City, signing a 15-year lease for approximately 150,000 square feet at 2121 Avenue of the Stars. The move, valued at roughly $200 million, is driven by PwC's need for additional space and improved accessibility to transportation and airports. This relocation is another significant blow to Downtown L.A.'s office market, which has seen several major tenants depart in recent years, while Century City continues to attract high-profile firms with its premier amenities and strong market performance.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Greater L.A. recorded nearly 4 million square feet of office leasing during Q2, a 15% increase from the prior quarter and 8% above the same period last year, exceeding the market’s five-year quarterly average by 22%. Century City commanded the region’s highest average asking rents in Q2 at $7.67 per square foot per month, compared to Beverly Hills at $6.71 per square foot. Downtown L.A.'s overall office vacancy has climbed 20 percentage points since 2019, with almost 40% of financial district office space available for lease at the end of 2025.
