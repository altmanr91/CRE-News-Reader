Chicago's Apartment Pipeline Is Slowing, But This Lender Bets On Rehab Instead

Bisnow Latest News Feed — Wed, 05 Aug 2026 19:41:03 -0400

SUMMARY

Market Research / Lending Trends

Community Investment Corporation (CIC) has a long history of financing the rehabilitation of multifamily properties in Chicago. Since 1984, the organization has provided approximately 2,800 loans, totaling $1.8 billion, to support the renovation of nearly 70,000 apartment units. This focus on existing stock comes as the new apartment development pipeline in Chicago is reportedly slowing.
