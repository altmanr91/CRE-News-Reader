Stream Realty Partners closes 56,256-square-foot office lease in Houston

LEASE

REJournals — Tue, 11 Aug 2026 09:58:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Stream Realty Partners represented Superior Energy Services, Inc. in a 56,256-square-foot office lease for its new corporate headquarters at 8020 Katy Freeway in Houston. The relocation from Downtown Houston to the Spring Branch submarket provides a more central location and improved connectivity for the company's long-term business objectives.
