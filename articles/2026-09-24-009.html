IPA Arranges Joint Venture Equity for Cliff at Green Valley Ranch Adaptive Reuse Project in Henderson, Nevada

DEVELOPMENT

REBusinessOnline — Thu, 24 Sep 2026 13:17:45 +0000

SUMMARY

IPA Capital Markets has arranged joint venture equity for VAC Development's adaptive reuse project, The Cliff at Green Valley Ranch, in Henderson, Nevada. This development will transform two former office buildings into a 100,000-square-foot open-air retail, dining, and lifestyle campus, marking the first major project of its kind in Henderson since 2004. The project emphasizes experiential retail, chef-driven dining, wellness, and community gathering spaces, with tenant deliveries expected to begin in the second quarter of 2027.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: retail

Property Name: The Cliff at Green Valley Ranch

Address: 2500-2550 Paseo Verde Parkway, Henderson, NV

Size: 100,000 SF

Year Built: 2002

Completion: Q2 2027

Site Area: 10 acres

Notable Features: Adaptive reuse of two obsolete office buildings into a Class A, pedestrian-friendly campus with 38 units, a 26,000-square-foot covered outdoor dining lounge, landscaped courtyards, and public art.
