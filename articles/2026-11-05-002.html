Sports Clothing Brand RBX Direct Signs 5K-SF Garment District Lease

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Thu, 05 Nov 2026 15:46:40 +0000

SUMMARY

RBX Direct, an activewear brand, has signed a lease for 4,800 square feet of office and showroom space at 102 West 38th Street in Manhattan's Garment District. The transaction reflects the landlord's strategy of securing tenants that align with the building's overall tenant mix and support business growth.
