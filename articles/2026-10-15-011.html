Cadence McShane breaks ground on Parkhill Middle School in Dallas

CONSTRUCTION

REJournals — Thu, 15 Oct 2026 14:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Cadence McShane Construction Company has broken ground on the new Parkhill Middle School in Dallas, Texas, for the Richardson Independent School District. This three-story, 201,209-square-foot facility is part of a district-wide initiative to convert junior high schools into middle schools serving grades six through eight. The project includes extensive site improvements and is expected to be completed by December 2028.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: school

Property Name: Parkhill Middle School

Address: 16500 Shadybank Drive, Dallas, TX

Size: 201,209 SF

Completion: December 2028

Notable Features: Features classrooms, collaborative learning spaces, administrative offices, a library, a cafeteria, competition gyms, and locker rooms. Site improvements include a track, practice field, tennis courts, and a courtyard plaza.

Project Notes: Developed through a multi-phase construction program to maintain campus operations during construction. Part of RISD’s district-wide initiative to transform junior high schools into middle schools serving grades six through eight.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

GENERAL CONTRACTOR: Cadence McShane Construction Company

OWNER: Richardson Independent School District (RISD)

ARCHITECT: Stantec
