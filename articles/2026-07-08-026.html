Nahla’s $1B Raleigh Miami Beach expansion proposal secures blessing from board despite height concerns

DEVELOPMENT

The Real Deal — Miami — Tue, 07 Jul 2026 21:10:04 +0000

SUMMARY

Nahla Capital has secured a recommendation from the Miami Beach Planning Board to increase the floor area ratio for its proposed Raleigh Miami Beach project, a luxury condo and hotel development. This approval would add over 66,000 square feet to the project, allowing for two additional floors on the residential tower. The $1 billion development, which Nahla Capital acquired in October for $270 million, still requires final approval from the Miami Beach City Commission and the city’s historic preservation board. The project aims to revitalize the iconic Art Deco hotel, which has been closed since 2017, and will feature a Rosewood-branded hotel, luxury condominiums, and a private members' club.
