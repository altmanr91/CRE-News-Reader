Joint Venture Acquires 122-Unit Seniors Housing Community in San Diego

ACQUISITION

REBusinessOnline — Mon, 02 Nov 2026 17:09:32 +0000

SUMMARY

A joint venture between a private equity group and a local operator has acquired Canyon Villas, a 122-unit seniors housing community in San Diego's Clairemont neighborhood. The San Diego Christian Foundation sold the property for an undisclosed price, which a board member noted was significantly higher than initial broker estimates. The community offers independent living, assisted living, and memory care options.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: seniors housing

Property Name: Canyon Villas

Address: 4282 Balboa Ave, San Diego, CA*

Units: 122

Year Built: 1991

Notable Features: Features independent living, assisted living, and memory care residences; amenities include a courtyard, library, gazebo, activity room, and transportation services.
