Google Relocation Leads D.C.’s Office Market in Q3

Commercial Observer — Fri, 02 Oct 2026 20:50:11 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Office Report

Washington, D.C.'s office market saw a steady third quarter with 1.8 million square feet leased, aligning with the five-year quarterly average. However, a significant disparity persists between trophy properties, which maintain below 10% availability, and the broader market. This scarcity in premier space is driving tenants, particularly law firms and government entities, to secure leases well in advance of expirations, prompting landlords to undertake building repositioning projects. The quarter's largest lease was a 210,000-square-foot consolidation by a major tech company.

DATA POINTS

Q3 2026 Office Leasing Volume: 1.8 million square feet

Overall Office Availability: 24%

Trophy Office Space Availability: below 10%

Class A Asking Rent: $62.31 per square foot per year

Office Inventory Decline (YoY): 4.7 million square feet

Total Office Inventory: 118.1 million square feet
