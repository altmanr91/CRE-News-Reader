Gilbane-Led Team Breaks Ground on 200-Unit Raleigh Affordable Housing Project

CONSTRUCTION

Connect CRE — Thu, 29 Oct 2026 14:01:16 +0000

SUMMARY

A development team led by Gilbane, Harmony Housing Affordable Development Inc., and F7 International Development has broken ground on Chapanoke, a 200-unit affordable housing community in Raleigh, NC. The nearly $58 million project, located at 326 Chapanoke Road, will also feature 10,000 square feet of retail space. The development is designed to serve households earning between 30% and 70% of the Area Median Income and is expected to be completed in early 2028.

Project Notes: The development will serve households earning between 30% and 70% of Area Median Income (AMI). Wake County and the City of Raleigh are contributing subordinate public financing, including $7.1 million in gap financing from Wake County.
