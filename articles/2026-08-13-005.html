Office Furniture Manufacturer Haworth Renews 30K SF at SL Green’s 125 Park Avenue

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Wed, 12 Aug 2026 17:56:28 +0000

SUMMARY

Office furniture manufacturer Haworth has renewed its 30,365-square-foot office and showroom lease at 125 Park Avenue in Midtown Manhattan for another 10 years. The landlord, SL Green Realty, represented itself in the transaction, while Cushman & Wakefield represented the tenant. The building's prime location across from Grand Central Terminal and recent renovations were cited as key factors in maintaining high occupancy.

Notable Features: Located across the street from Grand Central Terminal; recently underwent lobby and elevator cab renovations.

Project Notes: Haworth has been a tenant in the property for over a decade, previously renewing its lease in 2015.
