Domain Cos. Nears Completion of 255-Unit Apartment Building in Brooklyn

REBusinessOnline — Wed, 16 Sep 2026 13:46:19 +0000

SUMMARY

Construction

Domain Cos. is nearing completion of Majestic, a 255-unit apartment building in Brooklyn's Gowanus neighborhood. The 10-story building, designed by Handel Architects with interiors by GoodRich Design, will offer a mix of studio, one-, two-, and three-bedroom units, with 25 percent designated as affordable housing. Amenities will include a landscaped roof terrace, an indoor-outdoor fitness center, and a courtyard garden, with an opening anticipated for next year.
