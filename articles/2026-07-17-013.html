Bradford Allen closes pair of leases with Illinois Retina in Chicago market

LEASE

REJournals — Fri, 17 Jul 2026 09:52:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Illinois Retina, a medical services provider, has executed two long-term leases in the Chicago metropolitan area. The company renewed and expanded its presence at Concourse Office Plaza in Skokie, Illinois, taking 16,228 square feet, and relocated to a new 8,728-square-foot facility at 9200 Calumet Ave. in Munster, Indiana. These transactions represent a continued commitment to serving patients across the region, with the Skokie lease marking a significant expansion from its previous footprint.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: medical office

Property Name: Concourse Office Plaza

Address: 4709-4711 Golf Road, Skokie, IL

Size: 16,228 SF

Notable Features: The Skokie property comprises two 12-story medical office buildings, located 1 mile from the Edens Expressway. The Munster property is a single-story medical office complex located less than 2 miles from the Borman Expressway.

Project Notes: The Skokie transaction represented a renewal and expansion from approximately 3,000 square feet. The Munster location relocated from a smaller facility.
