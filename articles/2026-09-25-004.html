South Florida Starchitect Kobi Karp: 5 Questions

Commercial Observer — Thu, 24 Sep 2026 18:55:43 +0000

SUMMARY

Feature / Profile

Kobi Karp, founder of Kobi Karp Architecture and Interior Design, discusses the evolving landscape of South Florida development, highlighting the strong demand for multifamily apartments, particularly those with extensive amenities. He notes the success of the Live Local Act in facilitating the creation of affordable housing units within market-rate developments, which has garnered community support and financial feasibility. Karp also emphasizes the significant improvement in building quality and materials over the past decades, with a focus on durable and aesthetically pleasing finishes. He identifies the biggest challenge as meeting the overwhelming demand for new housing units across Florida, and reflects on how the Champlain Towers collapse underscored the critical importance of consistent building maintenance.
