BlackRock Finances $12B Meta Data Center, Closes Aligned Acquisition

LOAN

Bisnow News Feed — Tue, 21 Jul 2026 14:34:27 -0400

SUMMARY

BlackRock has significantly expanded its presence in the artificial intelligence data center market by orchestrating two major transactions totaling approximately $57 billion. The firm is leading a $12 billion debt financing for a new Meta data center campus in El Paso, Texas, where BlackRock holds an 80% joint venture stake. Concurrently, BlackRock's subsidiary Global Infrastructure Partners, in partnership with MGX, finalized the $40 billion acquisition of data center provider Aligned Data Centers from Macquarie Asset Management, with an additional $5 billion investment planned for expansion. These moves underscore BlackRock's strategic positioning as a dominant force in hyperscale data center development and financing.
