Wall Street landlords flip to sellers after housing law passage

The Real Deal — National — Wed, 22 Jul 2026 16:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Housing Policy

Institutional investors are increasingly listing single-family homes for sale following the passage of the 21st Century Road to Housing Act, which restricts their ability to acquire existing single-family properties. For-sale listings from these landlords have more than doubled in the last six months, reaching over 9,400 homes with a combined asking price of $3.1 billion. This shift indicates a strategic pivot by institutional investors, who are now net sellers, potentially reallocating capital towards the built-to-rent sector, which remains a viable growth path under the new legislation.
