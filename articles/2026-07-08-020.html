“Freak accident”: Pfizer building developer denies risk of collapse

The Real Deal — New York — Wed, 08 Jul 2026 00:52:45 +0000

SUMMARY

Construction

The developer of the former Pfizer headquarters conversion in Midtown, MetroLoft, has addressed concerns following an emergency response at the construction site. Nathan Berman of MetroLoft stated that reports of an impending collapse were exaggerated, asserting the building was never at risk despite sagging upper floors and buckled support columns. He attributed the issue to added weight during construction, causing two specific columns to give way, and emphasized that the problem is fixable and will not significantly delay the project's 2027 completion.
