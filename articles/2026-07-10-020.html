East End’s middle market steals spotlight in second quarter sales

The Real Deal — New York — Thu, 09 Jul 2026 15:38:48 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Sales Report

The East End's real estate market saw a significant shift in the second quarter, with middle-market transactions driving sales volume in both the Hamptons and the North Fork. This represents a departure from previous quarters where high-end trophy properties dominated. While overall transaction activity declined, properties priced between $1 million and $5 million in the Hamptons, and above $1 million on the North Fork, accounted for the largest share of deals. This trend, coupled with constrained inventory, contributed to continued price appreciation in the Hamptons, while the North Fork experienced mixed signals with a rising average sale price but a declining median sale price.
