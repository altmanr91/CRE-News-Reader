San Antonio broker and coffee shop owner indicted for identity theft and bank fraud

The Real Deal — Texas — Wed, 01 Jul 2026 20:37:40 +0000

SUMMARY

CRE Legal / Regulatory

A San Antonio real estate broker and coffee shop owner has been indicted on charges of bank fraud and identity theft. The charges stem from allegations that he misled a company into loaning him $2.12 million for an office property purchase in Grand Prairie, falsely claiming a key tenant had renewed its lease. This indictment is the latest in a series of legal challenges for the broker, including multiple default judgments in other real estate deals and the foreclosure sale of his coffee shop's flagship location.
