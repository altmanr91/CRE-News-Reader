Prologis to develop 1-million-square-foot logistics facility in Minooka

DEVELOPMENT

REJournals — Wed, 09 Sep 2026 20:45:49 +0000

SUMMARY

Prologis has acquired a 69-acre site in Minooka, Illinois, where it plans to develop Minooka Exchange, a 1-million-square-foot Class-A logistics facility. This project aims to address the demand for large-format distribution space in Chicago's I-80 submarket, a key bulk distribution corridor. The development reflects Prologis's commitment to strengthening its presence in the region and supporting local supply chain needs with modern logistics infrastructure.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Property Name: Minooka Exchange

Size: 1,002,000 SF

Site Area: 69 acres

Notable Features: Single-story, cross-dock building with 40-foot clear height, 290 car parking spaces, 185-foot truck court, and modern exterior with full-height glass entryways and wood accents. Located with convenient access to I-80 via the upgraded Ridge Road interchange and adjacent to Canadian National Railway’s Chicago Logistics Hub.

Project Notes: Prologis acquired the 69-acre site for this development.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

DEVELOPER/SPONSOR: Prologis — Josh Bauer, Vice President and Investment Officer; Josh Zemon, Senior Vice President and Investment Officer

SPONSOR PIPELINE

Central DuPage submarket: 454,000 SF of Class A space planned on 26 acres

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Chicago's I-80 submarket is one of the region’s primary bulk distribution corridors. In 2024, Prologis facilities in Illinois saw $199 billion in throughput, supporting a $21.2 billion GDP impact and generating $4.1 billion in tax revenues. Prologis currently owns 341 properties totaling over 79 million SF in Chicago.
