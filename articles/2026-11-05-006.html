Provident Industrial Completes 497,280 SF Project in El Paso

CONSTRUCTION

REBusinessOnline — Thu, 05 Nov 2026 16:17:38 +0000

SUMMARY

Provident Industrial, a subsidiary of Provident, has completed the fourth and final phase of Gateway Logistics Park in El Paso, Texas. This phase adds three rear-load industrial buildings totaling 497,280 square feet to the park. The completion brings the entire Gateway Logistics Park to approximately 1.4 million square feet across six buildings, enhancing the region's industrial capacity.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Property Name: Gateway Logistics Park - Phase IV

Size: 497,280 SF

Completion: November 2026

Total Project (All Phases): 1.4 million square feet

Notable Features: Consists of three rear-load buildings (Buildings 4, 5, and 6). Buildings 4 and 5 each span 147,420 square feet with a shared 205-foot truck court. Building 6 totals 202,440 square feet with a 130-foot truck court and 44 trailer parking stalls.

Project Notes: Construction of Phase IV began in spring 2025. This is the fourth and final phase of the park.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

DEVELOPER/SPONSOR: Provident Industrial
