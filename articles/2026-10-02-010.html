Henry S. Miller, Lincoln Property Company to tackle redevelopment of North Dallas’ Pepper Square shopping center

DEVELOPMENT

REJournals — Fri, 02 Oct 2026 15:09:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Henry S. Miller Company and Lincoln Property Company have finalized agreements to co-develop the first phase of the Pepper Square shopping center redevelopment in North Dallas. This initial phase will feature a 313-unit multifamily community and over two acres of public open space, with construction anticipated to begin in May 2027. The broader three-phase project, spanning 15.51 acres, is expected to include at least 1,050 apartment homes and approximately 65,000 square feet of new retail space, transforming a long-standing retail center into a vibrant mixed-use destination.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: mixed-use

Property Name: Pepper Square

Address: 14902 Preston Rd, Dallas, TX*

Units: 313

Site Area: 15.51 acres

Notable Features: Redevelopment of a longstanding shopping center, including significant public open spaces and pedestrian improvements.

Project Notes: Phase 1 is a 313-unit, five-story wrap-style multifamily community with 90,000 square feet of public open space. The overall redevelopment encompasses 15.51 acres within the larger 23-acre Pepper Square property. The existing Trader Joe's and Hobby Lobby locations are outside the redevelopment area. An entity owned by Gerald Ford will retain an investment as a limited partner with Henry S. Miller Company. Henry S. Miller's involvement with Pepper Square dates to 1994.
