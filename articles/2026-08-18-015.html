Marcus & Millichap’s Azzi Group Closes Value-Add Sale of West Hollywood Multifamily

SALE

Connect CRE — Mon, 17 Aug 2026 22:24:34 +0000

SUMMARY

Marcus & Millichap's Azzi Group has facilitated the sale of a 30-unit multifamily property in West Hollywood for $7.98 million. The transaction provides the buyer with a value-add opportunity to renovate vacant units and improve operations, capitalizing on strong rental demand in the area due to limited housing supply and desirable location.

Address: 1203 N. Kings Road, West Hollywood, CA
