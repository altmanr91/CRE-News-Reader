Harkinson making first major office buy in decades in Richardson

ACQUISITION

The Real Deal — Texas — Mon, 06 Jul 2026 17:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Harkinson Investment Corporation, an Addison, Texas-based REIT, has acquired a 229,000-square-foot office building at 2600 North Central Expressway in Richardson, marking its largest office investment in over two decades. The nine-story Class A building was purchased from a subsidiary of Japan-based Kajima Corporation. Harkinson executives noted the availability of Class A office space at previously unimaginable prices, with the acquired property being 92 percent leased and benefiting from recent tenant renewals.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Address: 2600 North Central Expressway, Richardson, TX

Size: 229,000 SF

Occupancy at Sale: 92.0%

Notable Features: Nine-story Class A office building located in the "Telecom Corridor" along Hwy 75.

Project Notes: The building has a 5.2-year weighted average lease term, with most tenants renewing leases since 2022. Occupancy was previously around 50%.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: Harkinson Investment Corporation

SELLER: Kajima Corporation

LEASING AGENT: Forge Commercial Real Estate
