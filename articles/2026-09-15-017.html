Upper Manhattan Empowerment Zone Signs New Headquarters Lease

LEASE

Connect CRE — Mon, 14 Sep 2026 21:46:26 +0000

SUMMARY

The Upper Manhattan Empowerment Zone Development Corporation (UMEZ) has signed a new 9,000-square-foot headquarters lease on the sixth floor of the Urban League Empowerment Center at 119 W. 125th St. UMEZ has been involved with the mixed-use development since 2020, providing construction financing for the project which includes affordable housing, commercial office and retail space, and a civil rights museum. The organization will join other notable office tenants in the building.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Property Name: Urban League Empowerment Center

Address: 119 W. 125th St, New York City, NY

Size: 9,000 SF

Notable Features: Mixed-use development including affordable housing, commercial office and retail space, and the Urban Civil Rights Museum.

Project Notes: UMEZ served as a lender on the construction of the project since 2020.
