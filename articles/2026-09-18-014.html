Draper and Kramer brokers $99 million loan for acquisition of 724,400-square-foot shopping center in Skokie

LOAN

REJournals — Thu, 17 Sep 2026 17:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Draper and Kramer's Commercial Finance Group brokered a $99.55 million loan for Fairbourne Properties to acquire Village Crossing, a 724,400-square-foot retail center in Skokie, IL. The financing will also cover tenant improvement and leasing commission costs for new leases at the property, which is anchored by a grocery store, movie theater, and sporting goods retailer.
