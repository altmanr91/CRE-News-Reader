Hoar Construction Breaks Ground on 32,000 SF Medical Office Building in East Austin

CONSTRUCTION

REBusinessOnline — Wed, 22 Jul 2026 15:08:34 +0000

SUMMARY

Hoar Construction has broken ground on the Colony Park Health & Wellness Center, a four-story medical office building spanning 32,000 square feet in East Austin. The facility, owned by Central Health and designed by HKS Architects, will feature a pharmacy, lab, kitchen, patient care rooms, and a dental office across its upper floors, with utilities on the ground level. Completion is anticipated for summer 2027.
