Happier Grocery to Open Second NYC Location on the Upper East Side

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Tue, 22 Sep 2026 19:45:27 +0000

SUMMARY

Happier Grocery has signed a 20,500-square-foot lease for its second New York City location on the Upper East Side, transforming a former movie theater space into a wellness-focused destination. The new health food store, similar to its Canal Street flagship, is expected to open in the second half of 2027. This lease marks the first deal for the property since CSC Real Estate acquired it as part of a $70 million portfolio purchase in June.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: retail

Address: 210 East 86th Street, New York City, NY

Size: 20,500 SF

Notable Features: Former movie theater space being redeveloped into a health food store with a curated, high-end grocery experience and social atmosphere.

Project Notes: The property is part of a three-building portfolio acquired by CSC Real Estate for $70 million in June 2026.
