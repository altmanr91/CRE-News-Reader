Evergreen Development Acquires 11.7 Acres in Mesa, Arizona, Plans Mixed-Use Project

ACQUISITION

REBusinessOnline — Tue, 08 Sep 2026 13:07:23 +0000

SUMMARY

Evergreen Development has acquired an 11.7-acre parcel in Mesa, Arizona, from Brookfield, with plans to develop a mixed-use project. The development will feature 261 multifamily units across nine buildings and a retail center totaling approximately 10,860 square feet, including a multi-tenant building and a freestanding drive-thru restaurant. Construction is slated to begin in early 2027, with lease-up anticipated in mid-2028.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: mixed-use

Site Area: 11.7 acres

Notable Features: Mixed-use project featuring multifamily and retail components, including a drive-thru restaurant.

Project Notes: Groundbreaking planned for Q1 2027, with lease-up beginning Q2 2028. Retail portion includes an 8,500 SF multi-tenant building and a 2,360 SF freestanding Chipotle with a drive-thru. Multifamily portion will have 261 apartments across nine three-story buildings.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: Evergreen Development

SELLER: Brookfield
