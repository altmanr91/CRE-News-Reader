Apricus Realty Capital acquires 18.56-acre industrial outdoor storage property in Houston market

ACQUISITION

REJournals — Wed, 08 Jul 2026 17:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Apricus Realty Capital, in partnership with ABR Capital Partners, has acquired an 18.56-acre industrial outdoor storage (IOS) property in Northwest Houston. The site features 53,000 square feet of warehouse and service facilities and is fully leased to a major steel pipe distributor. This acquisition expands Apricus's portfolio and demonstrates its continued investment in the specialized IOS asset class.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Address: 11403 N. Houston Rosslyn Road, Houston, TX

Size: 53,000 SF

Occupancy at Sale: 100.0%

Site Area: 18.56 acres

Notable Features: Large-scale pipe storage and distribution yard strategically located between Beltway 8, U.S. Highway 290, and Interstate 45.
