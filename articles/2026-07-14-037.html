Signature Development’s Uptown Oakland apartments headed to foreclosure

FORECLOSURE

The Real Deal — San Francisco — Mon, 13 Jul 2026 21:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

A 105-unit apartment building in Uptown Oakland, Mason at Hive, is facing foreclosure with an auction scheduled for July 28. The ownership entity, managed by Signature Development, defaulted on a $39.7 million loan, with approximately $35 million in unpaid debt. The property, part of the larger Hive mixed-use development, was valued at roughly $36 million by the Alameda County tax assessor's office. The foreclosure reflects the challenges faced by Oakland's apartment market post-pandemic, though recent data indicates a stabilizing trend with rents climbing 6.2% year-over-year.

The apartment building is part of the larger Hive mixed-use development, a seven-building project that transformed a long-vacant car dealership into apartments, offices, retail and restaurants.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Oakland rents climbed 6.2% year over year, representing the city's strongest annual increase in three years, according to Zumper data. Developers delivered roughly 10,000 new housing units between 2018 and 2023 in Oakland.
