Blood in the water: San Jose Sharks bite on Google offices next to SAP Center

ACQUISITION

The Real Deal — San Francisco — Mon, 13 Jul 2026 22:58:14 +0000

SUMMARY

The San Jose Sharks, an NHL team, have acquired an office building located directly across from their home arena, the SAP Center, in San Jose, CA. The property was purchased from Google for an undisclosed amount. This acquisition is part of a larger strategy as the SAP Center undergoes a $425 million renovation, with the team planning to relocate its corporate operations into the newly acquired 22,000-square-foot building by the end of the year. The move will free up space within the arena for new locker rooms for both the Sharks and visiting teams.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Address: 450 West Santa Clara Street, San Jose, CA

Size: 22,000 SF

Year Built: 1999

Notable Features: Located directly across the street from the SAP Center.
