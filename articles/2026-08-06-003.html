Brookfield Asset Management Raises Record $77B in Q2

Commercial Observer — Wed, 05 Aug 2026 18:10:59 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Earnings Report

Brookfield Asset Management reported a record-breaking second quarter in 2026, raising $77 billion and increasing its assets under management to over $1 trillion. The firm deployed $21 billion during the quarter, including significant capital into its private equity and infrastructure funds, which are on track to be the largest of their vintages. Brookfield also secured $5 billion for its inaugural AI Infrastructure Fund and expanded its financing framework with Bloom Energy to $25 billion for AI infrastructure power projects. On the real estate side, the firm raised $4.3 billion and deployed $5.2 billion, acquiring a U.S. manufactured-home portfolio and an outdoor industrial storage portfolio.
