Moratorium on waterfront project applications in downtown West Palm heads to final vote

The Real Deal — Miami — Wed, 15 Jul 2026 23:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Infrastructure Investment / Government Policy

West Palm Beach city commissioners are set to take a final vote on a six-month moratorium on new high-rise waterfront development applications in a specific downtown corridor. This pause is a direct response to resident concerns about rapid transformation and overdevelopment, particularly of luxury apartment and condo buildings. The city plans to use this period to conduct a zoning and economic analysis and draft new regulations, aiming for a more comprehensive plan for neighborhood development.

DATA POINTS

Number of luxury condo projects in the works: More than a dozen

Total units in luxury condo projects in the works: Over 2,000 units
