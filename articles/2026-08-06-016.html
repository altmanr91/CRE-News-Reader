Former Blue Shield of CA Offices Trade for Residential Conversion

ACQUISITION

Connect CRE — Wed, 05 Aug 2026 21:49:42 +0000

SUMMARY

The Olson Company acquired a 7.9-acre office campus in Monterey Park, CA, for $24.85 million, with plans to redevelop the site into a new residential community. The campus, which includes two office buildings totaling 120,191 square feet, was formerly owned and occupied by Blue Shield of California. This transaction highlights a growing trend of converting well-located, underutilized office properties into housing in the San Gabriel Valley, addressing the region's demand for new residential units.

DATA POINTS

Year Built: 1980
