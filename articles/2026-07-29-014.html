Urban Innovations closes 19 office deals in Chicago’s River North neighborhood during first half of 2026

LEASE

REJournals — Wed, 29 Jul 2026 09:19:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Urban Innovations completed 19 office lease transactions, including 18 new leases and one expansion, across its River North portfolio in Chicago during the first half of 2026. These deals collectively accounted for over 50,000 square feet. The company noted continued strong leasing momentum, with tenants prioritizing high-quality, move-in-ready spaces and well-maintained building common areas, including modern lobbies, elevators, and updated finishes.
