CBRE Arranges 118K-SF Price Zone Lease in Commerce

LEASE

Connect CRE — Tue, 18 Aug 2026 21:56:40 +0000

SUMMARY

CBRE has arranged a nearly 118,000-square-foot industrial lease at Harris Business Center in Commerce, CA, for Price Zone, a wholesale distributor and importer of consumer goods. This transaction enables Price Zone to consolidate its operations from three separate facilities into a single, more efficient location. The deal was mutually beneficial, providing the tenant with desired operational efficiency and economics, while helping the landlord maximize occupancy within the business park.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Property Name: Harris Business Center

Address: 6000 Rickenbacker Rd, Commerce, CA

Size: 118,000 SF

Notable Features: The facility features a 24-foot warehouse clearance, 19 dock-high loading positions, three ground-level doors, and rail service provided by the Los Angeles Junction Railway.
