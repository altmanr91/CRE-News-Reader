Musk’s companies prep $5M Seaholm office renovation

CONSTRUCTION

The Real Deal — Texas — Fri, 17 Jul 2026 15:27:33 +0000

SUMMARY

Several companies affiliated with Elon Musk are undertaking a $5.2 million renovation of their 35,000-square-foot office space at the Seaholm Power Plant in downtown Austin. The renovation plans were initiated by Wit Tech, a company linked to SpaceX. This office activity is part of a broader expansion of Musk's ventures in the Austin area, which includes significant land acquisitions and the development of the Terafab semiconductor manufacturing plant in Grimes County.
