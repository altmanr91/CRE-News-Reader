Principle Construction Completes 55,468 SF Facility for Orozco Trucking in Elgin, Illinois

CONSTRUCTION

REBusinessOnline — Mon, 27 Jul 2026 14:14:15 +0000

SUMMARY

Principle Construction Corp. has completed a 55,468-square-foot build-to-suit truck maintenance and warehouse facility for Orozco Trucking in Elgin, Illinois. The new headquarters provides the climate-controlled and dry freight specialist with a permanent home, relocating from a leased site. The facility is designed with a mix of office, maintenance, and warehouse space, and is situated on undeveloped land with room for future expansion.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Address: 2580 Mason Road, Elgin, IL

Size: 55,468 SF

Notable Features: Features 23,000 SF of two-story office space, 16,800 SF of maintenance space, and 15,000 SF of warehouse space with 26-foot clear height, four dock doors, and four drive-in doors. The site offers immediate access to I-90 and room for future expansion.

Project Notes: Build-to-suit project for Orozco Trucking, which relocated from a leased site in Elk Grove Village.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

GENERAL CONTRACTOR: Principle Construction Corp.

OWNER: Orozco Trucking

ARCHITECT: Harris Architects
