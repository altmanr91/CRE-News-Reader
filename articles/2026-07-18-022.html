Palm Beach County nixes proposed Project Tango data center

The Real Deal — Miami — Fri, 17 Jul 2026 16:45:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Development

Palm Beach County commissioners have rejected a zoning application for Project Tango, a proposed 1 million-square-foot data center and 2.3 million square feet of warehouses. The decision, made after a lengthy public hearing, cited concerns about the project's potential impact on local infrastructure and surrounding communities. The developer, PBA Holdings Inc., can resubmit a similar proposal, as the motion was made without prejudice. This rejection follows the county's recent decision to pause new data center proposals to establish regulatory guidelines, reflecting a broader national trend of increased scrutiny on data center developments due to power supply and environmental concerns.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Lawmakers in at least 11 states have introduced legislation that would temporarily ban data centers. South Florida has seen few data center projects due to the state's flood and hurricane risk.
