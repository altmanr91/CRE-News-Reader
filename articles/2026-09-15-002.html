CMBS Loan Backed by Hyatt Regency in Jersey City Sent to Special Servicing

LOAN

Commercial Observer — Tue, 15 Sep 2026 13:12:52 +0000

SUMMARY

A $100 million CMBS loan secured by the Hyatt Regency hotel in Jersey City, NJ, has been transferred to special servicing due to an anticipated maturity default in October 2026. The sponsorship team, Taconic Capital Advisors and HEI Hotels & Resorts, indicated an inability to repay the loan. The hotel has consistently underperformed its net cash flow projections since the loan's 2016 origination, even reporting negative cash flow in 2024.
