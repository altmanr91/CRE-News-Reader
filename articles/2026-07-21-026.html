Lender snaps up Stonerock’s downtown Miami office buildings for $15M

REO

The Real Deal — Miami — Mon, 20 Jul 2026 18:02:26 +0000

SUMMARY

An affiliate of Ardent Companies, the lender on two downtown Miami office buildings, acquired the properties for $15 million in a foreclosure auction. The acquisition follows a two-year legal dispute and a $65.7 million foreclosure judgment against the previous owner, Stonerock Capital. Stonerock had purchased the buildings in 2022 for $56.7 million with a $58.3 million loan from Ardent, but allegedly defaulted on the loan's maturity and property tax payments. The lender's successful bid highlights ongoing distress in South Florida's commercial office market.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Address: 44 West Flagler Street, Miami, FL

Sale Price: $15.0M

Notable Features: The portfolio includes a 25-story building completed in 1974 and a 12-story building completed in 1958.

Project Notes: The properties were previously acquired by Stonerock Capital in 2022 for $56.7 million, financed by a $58.3 million loan from Ardent. The foreclosure judgment against Stonerock was for $65.7 million.
