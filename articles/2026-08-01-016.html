AI Tech Firm Treeswift Signs Four-Year Lease for New Manhattan Headquarters

LEASE

Connect CRE — Fri, 31 Jul 2026 21:57:37 +0000

SUMMARY

Treeswift Inc., an AI-powered infrastructure technology company, has signed a four-year lease for 7,487 square feet at 256 W. 38th St. in Midtown Manhattan, relocating its New York headquarters. The move reflects a broader trend among growing AI companies seeking efficient, well-connected headquarters that foster collaboration and attract talent, rather than exclusively targeting trophy towers.
