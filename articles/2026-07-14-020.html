Brookfield eyes 10% stake in Hudson Square Properties

ACQUISITION

The Real Deal — New York — Mon, 13 Jul 2026 17:51:21 +0000

SUMMARY

Brookfield is in exclusive negotiations to acquire a 10% stake in Hudson Square Properties, a 13-building office complex in Manhattan, valuing the entire portfolio at $3.5 billion. The deal would make Brookfield the long-term operating partner for the 6.25 million-square-foot tech and media hub, which has seen increased leasing activity from technology and artificial intelligence firms. The transaction is expected to close in the coming months.

Office availability around Hudson Square has dipped by 3 percentage points since last summer, while asking rents have jumped nearly 20 percent to more than $87 per square foot. In Q2 2025, office availability at Hudson Square Properties was 17.1%, above the citywide rate of 14.1%.
