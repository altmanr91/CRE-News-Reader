Alterra IOS Expands Baltimore-Area Footprint with Acquisition

ACQUISITION

Connect CRE — Fri, 09 Oct 2026 21:07:36 +0000

SUMMARY

Alterra IOS has expanded its Baltimore-area portfolio with the acquisition of a fully leased industrial property in Rosedale, Maryland. This transaction marks Alterra's seventh acquisition in the Baltimore metropolitan area, underscoring the firm's continued investment in a market characterized by strong demand and tight supply for industrial outdoor storage (IOS) properties.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Address: 9729 Philadelphia Road, Rosedale, MD

Size: 22,500 SF

Site Area: 4.7 acres

Notable Features: Located in close proximity to I-95 and I-695, along a key freight corridor connecting Baltimore with Philadelphia and New York.

Project Notes: This is Alterra's seventh acquisition in the Baltimore MSA.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: Alterra IOS

SELLER BROKER: MacKenzie Companies — Andrew Meeder

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

The Baltimore market for industrial outdoor storage (IOS) properties has a vacancy rate of approximately 2.5%, driven by strong demand from logistics and transportation users and its strategic proximity to the Port of Baltimore.
