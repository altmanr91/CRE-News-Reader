Janus Living Pays $123.5M for Greensboro Senior Living Center

ACQUISITION

Connect CRE — Wed, 07 Oct 2026 14:55:44 +0000

SUMMARY

Janus Living has acquired Harmony at Greensboro, a 174-unit senior living community in Greensboro, NC, for $123.5 million. The facility, which offers independent, assisted, and memory care units along with rehabilitation services, was sold by Smith/Packett. Smith/Packett will continue to operate the center, which opened in March 2020. This acquisition marks Janus Living's entry into the North Carolina market.
