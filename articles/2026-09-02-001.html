The Plan: At 44 West Eighth Street, the Goal Is a ‘Wonderful Visual Cacophony’

Commercial Observer — Wed, 02 Sep 2026 10:00:59 +0000

SUMMARY

Development

T30 Capital and InWorkshop are developing 44 West Eighth Street, a new five-unit condominium project in Manhattan's Greenwich Village. The building will feature a unique facade made from handmade Danish Petersen brick, with each brick bearing the thumbprints of its maker, and partially hand-pressed and extruded terra cotta. The design emphasizes integrating exterior materials into the interior, with crushed brick remnants used in the lobby flooring. The project aims to offer exceptionally spacious condominiums, with full-floor homes starting at $9.75 million and a duplex penthouse potentially priced up to $20 million.
