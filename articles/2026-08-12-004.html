AI Wealth Management Platform Vise Grabs 12K SF at 50 Ninth Avenue

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Tue, 11 Aug 2026 20:13:47 +0000

SUMMARY

AI wealth management platform Vise is relocating its headquarters to 50 Ninth Avenue in New York City's Meatpacking District, quadrupling its office footprint to nearly 12,000 square feet. The 10-year lease for the second-floor space, represented by Current Real Estate Advisors for the tenant and Newmark for the landlord, Tavros Capital, reflects Vise's focus on upgrading its office environment and culture. The space features high ceilings and an atrium outdoor garden, with a custom build-out planned by the owner to meet the tenant's specific needs.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Address: 50 Ninth Avenue, New York City, NY

Size: 11,949 SF

Rental Rate: $150.00/SF/yr

Notable Features: Space spans the second floor of combined historic townhomes, featuring over 20-foot-high pitched ceilings, an atrium outdoor garden, and dramatic vaulted ceilings with ample natural light. It is located directly across from the Apple store and above the new Delilah club.

Project Notes: The landlord will build out the space to Vise's specifications. A creative solution involved a ground-floor retailer expanding upstairs to allow Vise to secure its desired space and budget.
