CBRE Arranges Valencia Office Sale for Harbor Associates

SALE

Connect CRE — Mon, 21 Sep 2026 22:05:19 +0000

SUMMARY

Harbor Associates has sold Commons at Valencia, a two-building Class A office campus in Valencia, to Strauss Investments LLC for $32 million. The property attracted significant investor interest due to its strong tenant roster, recent capital improvements, and strategic location within a premier Southern California suburban office market. The leasing momentum and long-term growth prospects of the Valencia submarket made this a compelling investment opportunity.
