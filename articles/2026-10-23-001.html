Podcast: Reducing CRE Energy Costs with Physical AI, with R-Zero CEO Jennifer Nuckles

Commercial Observer — Thu, 22 Oct 2026 22:05:33 +0000

SUMMARY

Non-CRE / Business News

This podcast features Jennifer Nuckles, CEO of R-Zero, discussing how her company's physical AI platform helps commercial real estate owners reduce HVAC energy costs by 20-40% without major retrofits. Nuckles explains that while building waste has been known for decades, recent advancements in hardware, software, and regulatory pressures like Local Law 97 have made solutions more viable. She highlights that energy efficiency investments can significantly improve net operating income by leveraging these savings, moving buildings towards an autonomous continuum.
