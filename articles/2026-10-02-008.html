BLS: U.S. Economy Adds 29,000 Jobs in September, Falling Short of Expectations

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 02 Oct 2026 15:11:05 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Labor Report

The U.S. economy added 29,000 jobs in September, significantly missing the Dow Jones economists' estimate of 84,000 new jobs. The unemployment rate saw a slight increase to 4.2 percent. Additionally, job gains for July and August were revised downward by a combined 60,000 jobs. While the healthcare sector continued to add jobs, its pace slowed, and construction employment remained relatively flat.
