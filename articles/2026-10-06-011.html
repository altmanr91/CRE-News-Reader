Colliers closes sale of Mohr Logistics Park Building 6 in Whiteland

SALE

REJournals — Tue, 06 Oct 2026 15:37:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Colliers has successfully brokered the sale of Mohr Logistics Park Building 6, a substantial 1,057,350-square-foot industrial facility in Whiteland, Indiana. The property, fully leased to a global power technology company through August 2036, was sold by Mohr Capital, which also developed the facility. This transaction highlights the continued demand for large-scale, well-located industrial assets in the Indianapolis market, particularly those offering strategic access to major transportation hubs and corporate supply chains.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Property Name: Mohr Logistics Park Building 6

Address: 5635 North Graham Road, Whiteland, IN

Size: 1,057,350 SF

Occupancy at Sale: 100.0%

Notable Features: Features 40-foot clear height, 120 dock doors, four drive-in doors, 652 auto parking spaces, 270 trailer parking spaces, and a 140-foot truck court. Provides convenient access to Indianapolis International Airport and a global power technology company's corporate and supply-chain presence.

Project Notes: Developed as Building 6 within the 475-acre Mohr Logistics Park, a master-planned industrial development projected to total over 6 million square feet at full buildout.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

SELLER: Mohr Capital
