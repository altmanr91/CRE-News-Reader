SRS Real Estate Partners Brokers $6.2M Sale of 31,086 SF Retail Property in Las Vegas

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Wed, 19 Aug 2026 14:30:00 +0000

SUMMARY

SRS Real Estate Partners has facilitated the all-cash sale of a 31,086-square-foot retail property in Las Vegas for $6.2 million. The asset, which has been occupied by a discount apparel retailer for over four decades, was sold by a West Coast private seller to a private investor from California. The transaction closed at a 6.5 percent capitalization rate.
