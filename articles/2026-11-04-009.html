Northwind Group Provides $231M Construction Loan for Office-to-Residential Conversion Project in Manhattan

LOAN

REBusinessOnline — Wed, 04 Nov 2026 16:00:48 +0000

SUMMARY

Northwind Group has provided a $231 million construction loan for the office-to-residential conversion of 675 Third Avenue in Manhattan. The project, undertaken by a joint venture between Metro Loft Management and David Werner Real Estate Investments, will transform the 32-story office tower into as many as 450 new apartments, including a 25 percent affordable housing component. This conversion addresses the demand for residential supply in Midtown East.
