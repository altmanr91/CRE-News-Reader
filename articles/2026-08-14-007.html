JLL Negotiates Sale of 291,958 SF Shopping Center in Paris, Texas

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Thu, 13 Aug 2026 15:10:13 +0000

SUMMARY

JLL has brokered the sale of Paris Towne Center, a 291,958-square-foot shopping center in Paris, Texas. The property, originally built in 1976, was sold by College Station-based Culpepper Realty Co. to Dallas-based Corsair Property Co. The center features a strong tenant mix including several national retailers.
