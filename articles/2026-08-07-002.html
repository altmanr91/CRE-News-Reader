Nordstrom Rack Takes 22K SF in Wellington, Fla.

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Thu, 06 Aug 2026 18:25:03 +0000

SUMMARY

Nordstrom Rack has signed a 22,000-square-foot lease at The Shoppes at Isla Verde in Wellington, FL, with an anticipated opening in spring 2027. The discount retailer will occupy space in the MetLife Investment Management-owned shopping center, which is managed by Urban Retail Properties. This new lease fills a vacancy left by a previous tenant, contributing to the center's evolving tenant mix.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: retail

Property Name: The Shoppes at Isla Verde

Address: 800-1020 South State Road 7, Wellington, FL

Size: 22,000 SF

Completion: Spring 2027

Project Notes: The 207,000-square-foot shopping center was previously acquired by MetLife in 2018 for $73.75 million. The space was formerly occupied by a fabric retailer that closed in 2025.
