Marcus & Millichap Brokers Sale of 1,042-Unit Self-Storage Portfolio in Greater Houston

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Tue, 27 Oct 2026 14:47:18 +0000

SUMMARY

Marcus & Millichap has brokered the sale of a five-property self-storage portfolio totaling 1,042 units across the greater Houston area. The portfolio comprises 148,745 net rentable square feet of climate- and non-climate-controlled space, along with boat and RV storage, and includes over 12 acres for future expansion. The seller and buyer were not disclosed.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: self-storage

Size: 148,745 SF

Site Area: 12 acres

Notable Features: Includes climate- and non-climate-controlled space, as well as boat and RV storage.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

SELLER BROKER: Marcus & Millichap — Dave Knobler; Mixson Staffel; Charles LeClaire
