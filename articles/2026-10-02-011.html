CenterSquare acquires three-building industrial property in Houston

ACQUISITION

REJournals — Fri, 02 Oct 2026 15:02:00 +0000

SUMMARY

CenterSquare has acquired the three-building Beltway 249 Business Center, a 107,020-square-foot infill service industrial property in Houston. The acquisition was an off-market transaction, and CenterSquare plans to invest additional capital to reposition vacant suites, drive leasing velocity, and stabilize occupancy, alongside various capital improvements.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Property Name: Beltway 249 Business Center

Size: 107,020 SF

Occupancy at Sale: 62.0%

Notable Features: Features an average suite size of 4,116 SF, 14’-22’ clear heights, and oversized grade-level loading doors, with access to 45% of the MSA’s population within 30 minutes.

Project Notes: Acquired off-market; plans include repositioning vacant suites, driving leasing velocity, and completing capital improvements such as monument signage and concrete repairs.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: CenterSquare
