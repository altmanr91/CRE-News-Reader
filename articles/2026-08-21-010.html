Cushman & Wakefield Arranges $450M Sale of Tri-State Commercial Portfolio

ACQUISITION

REBusinessOnline — Thu, 20 Aug 2026 15:06:06 +0000

SUMMARY

A partnership between Saber-Hightower, Waterfall Asset Management, and Lincoln Property Co. has acquired a four-property commercial portfolio across the greater New York City area for $450 million. The portfolio includes existing multifamily, retail, healthcare, and industrial assets totaling approximately 4 million square feet, along with 300 acres of development sites. Cushman & Wakefield represented the undisclosed seller and also arranged acquisition financing for the buyer.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: mixed-use

Size: 4,000,000 SF

Sale Price: $450.0M

Site Area: 300 acres

Notable Features: Portfolio includes iPark 84, a 270-acre industrial campus in East Fishkill, NY, with 1.8 million square feet and capacity for 1.5 million square feet of new development; Edgewater Harbor, a 262-unit multifamily complex with 60,000 square feet of retail in Edgewater, NJ; a 400,000-square-foot mixed-use development in Norwalk, CT, anchored by a medical outpatient facility; and Trilogy Lofts, a 97-unit apartment building in Yonkers, NY.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: Saber-Hightower

BUYER: Waterfall Asset Management

BUYER: Lincoln Property Co.

SELLER BROKER: Cushman & Wakefield — Andy Merin; Frank DiTommaso; Ryan Dowd
