Prometheus picks up Sunnyvale apartments for $70M as AI-driven housing crunch tightens in Silicon Valley

ACQUISITION

The Real Deal — San Francisco — Mon, 06 Jul 2026 22:53:34 +0000

SUMMARY

Prometheus Real Estate Group has acquired The Highlander, a 173-unit apartment complex in Sunnyvale, for $69.5 million. The property was sold by a joint venture between Northwest Investments and Central Investments. This acquisition expands Prometheus' multifamily portfolio in the Bay Area, a market experiencing a housing crunch driven by the growing artificial intelligence sector and an influx of tech workers.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Average rents in Santa Clara County are approximately $3,400 per month and are projected to increase to $3,600 over the next two years. Rent growth in Silicon Valley is expected to be the second highest nationwide, after San Francisco. New apartment construction starts reached 1,050 units in the latest quarter, but this is below the pre-pandemic quarterly average of about 1,600 units. In the second half of 2024, no new units began construction in the region.
