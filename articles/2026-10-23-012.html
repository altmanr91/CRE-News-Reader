One platform for data management and visualization? It’s a key tool wielded by Southfield-based Village Green

REJournals — Fri, 23 Oct 2026 09:26:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Non-CRE / Business News

This article highlights the increasing importance of data-driven decision-making in commercial real estate, noting that a significant majority of professionals still rely on intuition or outdated spreadsheet methods. Southfield-based property management firm Village Green developed its own customizable platform, VG Dash, to address this gap. The platform integrates data management and visualization for metrics like resident satisfaction, retention, and turnover, enabling real-time insights for owners and property managers. This in-house solution offers cost savings, flexibility, and enhanced collaboration by providing a single, transparent source of truth for property performance, ultimately streamlining operations and decision-making.
