Continental Buys 125K-SF Centennial Shopping Center

ACQUISITION

Connect CRE — Thu, 05 Nov 2026 16:26:17 +0000

SUMMARY

Continental Realty Corporation has acquired the 125,000-square-foot Quebec Village Shopping Center in Centennial, CO, for $31 million. This transaction marks CRC's second retail acquisition in the Denver metropolitan area, expanding its regional presence. The property includes a significant vacant anchor space, offering future repositioning opportunities.
