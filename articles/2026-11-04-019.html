Coral Gables Pays $24M for 53K-SF MOB

ACQUISITION

Connect CRE — Wed, 04 Nov 2026 14:12:36 +0000

SUMMARY

The City of Coral Gables acquired a 53,000-square-foot medical office building at 475–495 Biltmore Way from ShareMD for $24 million. This acquisition gives the City control of the entire block, which is shared with Coral Gables City Hall, providing flexibility for future planning and maximizing site utility for residents. ShareMD's property management affiliate, Medicus Asset Management LLC, will continue to manage the building for an initial one-year term.
