NYC EDC Snapshot Sees Mixed Indicators for Office, Retail, Residential

Connect CRE — Mon, 12 Oct 2026 18:01:54 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Economic Snapshot

The New York City Economic Development Corporation's latest Economic Snapshot for August reveals a mixed performance across commercial real estate sectors. While citywide office visitation saw a modest year-over-year increase, month-over-month figures declined, attributed to seasonal factors. Both office availability and vacancy rates showed slight improvements. The retail sector remained flat with a 4.5% vacancy rate, higher than pre-pandemic averages. Residential asking rents continued their upward trend for the seventh consecutive month, exacerbating affordability concerns as median rents have risen significantly and inventory has decreased over the past two years.

DATA POINTS

Citywide office visitation year-over-year increase (August): 2.3 percentage points

Citywide office visitation month-over-month (August) vs. pre-pandemic: 70%

Class A+ office visitation month-over-month (August) vs. pre-pandemic: 76%

Citywide retail vacancy rate (August): 4.5%

2019 12-month average retail vacancy rate: 3.4%

Increase in citywide median asking rent over two years: $403

Citywide median asking rent (August): nearly $4,000

Decline in rental inventory over two years: 14%
