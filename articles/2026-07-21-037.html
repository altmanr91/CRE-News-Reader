Former Yelp HQ for sale after CIM Group defaults on $62M loan

The Real Deal — San Francisco — Mon, 20 Jul 2026 18:38:46 +0000

SUMMARY

Property Listing / For Sale

The former Yelp headquarters at 55 Hawthorne Street in San Francisco's Financial District is being marketed for sale by a court-appointed receiver after owner CIM Group defaulted on a $61.5 million loan. The 145,200-square-foot office tower, which CIM Group acquired in 2016 for $123 million, saw its occupancy plummet to 12 percent after Yelp's master lease expired last year. The property's value has significantly declined, with a recent appraisal valuing it at $51 million, prompting the sale to address the distressed debt.

The south Financial District submarket has captured 44 percent of AI-related leasing since last year. There are 268 active requirements in the market averaging 39,600 square feet, totaling over 8 million square feet of space needed. Tech firms account for about half of the demand for space across the city, with AI companies making up about a quarter of citywide demand.
