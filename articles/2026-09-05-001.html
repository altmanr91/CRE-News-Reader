Related Secures $65M Refi for Office Property in L.A.’s South Bay

REFINANCE

Commercial Observer — Fri, 04 Sep 2026 19:55:55 +0000

SUMMARY

Related Companies and Cruzan have secured a $64.7 million refinancing loan for The Torrance, an eight-story Class A office property in Los Angeles' South Bay. The loan was provided by Waterfall Asset Management and Delaware Life. The property, which is 93% occupied, was acquired by the current owners in 2017 for $106.8 million and previously refinanced in 2022.

The South Bay market is experiencing surging demand from aerospace and defense users for both office and industrial space. The Greater Los Angeles office market has an overall vacancy rate of 25.3% as of Q2 2026, while trophy properties have a 13.5% vacancy rate.
