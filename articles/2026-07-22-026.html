Landmaxxing: Billionaire David MacNeil adds to Manalapan compound with $36M buy

ACQUISITION

The Real Deal — Miami — Tue, 21 Jul 2026 18:21:50 +0000

SUMMARY

Billionaire David MacNeil, founder of WeatherTech, has expanded his Manalapan compound with the acquisition of a neighboring 1.9-acre property for $36 million. This purchase adds to MacNeil's contiguous land holdings, which now total 5.8 acres, as he continues to assemble a larger estate in the area. The sellers, Ralph and Mary Gesualdo, nearly tripled their investment on the property, which they had acquired in 2020.
