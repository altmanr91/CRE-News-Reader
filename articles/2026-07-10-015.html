Marcus & Millichap closes sale of childcare facility in New Berlin

SALE

REJournals — Thu, 09 Jul 2026 16:26:00 +0000

SUMMARY

A Florida-based real estate investor specializing in early childhood education properties sold a net-leased childcare facility in New Berlin, Wisconsin, for $3.057 million. The 11,080-square-foot property, occupied by Cadence Academy under a corporate-guaranteed absolute net lease, was acquired by a private, Illinois-based investor. Marcus & Millichap Capital Corporation arranged $2.14 million in acquisition financing for the buyer.
