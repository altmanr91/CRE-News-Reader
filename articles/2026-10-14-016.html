Inflation Accelerates to 3.7% as Gasoline Prices Surge

Connect CRE — Wed, 14 Oct 2026 12:57:24 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Inflation Report

U.S. consumer prices saw an accelerated increase in September, with annual inflation reaching 3.7%, up from 3.4% in August. This surge was primarily driven by a significant 9.1% jump in gasoline prices, which accounted for over half of the monthly increase. Core inflation, excluding volatile food and energy components, also rose to 2.6% annually, suggesting broader inflationary pressures beyond just energy costs. Shelter costs continued their upward trend, contributing to the overall inflationary environment.
