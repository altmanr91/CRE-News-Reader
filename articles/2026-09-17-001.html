Shorenstein to Buy Fully Leased SoHo Office Property From Tishman Speyer

SALE

Commercial Observer — Thu, 17 Sep 2026 13:49:51 +0000

SUMMARY

Shorenstein Properties is acquiring the fully leased, 12-story office and retail building at 148 Lafayette Street in SoHo from Tishman Speyer for approximately $135 million. Tishman Speyer is making a significant profit on the sale, having purchased the property in May 2025 for $105.5 million. The building, which is 100 percent leased, has seen substantial new leasing activity in its office portion since January 2025, with rents ranging from $70 to $120 per square foot.
