RealSource Group closes $5.8 million Shake Shack sale in Houston market

SALE

REJournals — Mon, 03 Aug 2026 09:59:00 +0000

SUMMARY

RealSource Group has brokered the sale of a newly constructed Shake Shack drive-thru ground lease in Richmond, Texas, within the Houston metropolitan area, for $5.8 million. The transaction achieved a 5.00% cap rate, setting a new record low for a Shake Shack ground lease in Texas and ranking among the lowest nationwide. The property, developed by Read King, was acquired by a private 1031 exchange investor from New York City.
