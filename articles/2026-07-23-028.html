Alonzo Mourning, HTG net $55M financing package for affordable housing

LOAN

The Real Deal — Miami — Wed, 22 Jul 2026 21:01:15 +0000

SUMMARY

Alonzo Mourning's AM Affordable Housing and Housing Trust Group secured a $55.4 million financing package for Artisan Pointe, a 115-unit affordable housing complex under construction in Naranja, an unincorporated area of south Miami-Dade County. The financing includes low-income housing tax credit equity, a permanent loan arranged by Berkadia, and funds from the Naranja Lakes Community Redevelopment Agency, along with a construction loan from U.S. Bancorp. This project is the first phase of a larger master-planned affordable development and is expected to be completed in January 2028, addressing the high demand for affordable housing in South Florida.
