“Manic” Cape Cod homebuyer sues JPMorgan over $5.5M mortgage loan

The Real Deal — National — Fri, 17 Jul 2026 21:03:21 +0000

SUMMARY

CRE Legal / Regulatory

A retired New York attorney has filed a federal lawsuit against JPMorgan Chase Bank, seeking to invalidate a $3.85 million mortgage loan he received for a $5.5 million Cape Cod home. The attorney claims he was in a state of "manic psychosis" due to bipolar disorder when he purchased the property in late 2021, arguing that the bank should have recognized his incompetence to enter into the contract. The house, located at 1440 Chequessett Neck Road in Wellfleet, was demolished in February 2025 due to coastal erosion, and the land's value has since been reassessed at $385,000.
