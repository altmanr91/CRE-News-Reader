Starwood files data center plan as Virginia residents revolt

DEVELOPMENT

The Real Deal — National — Wed, 22 Jul 2026 19:30:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Starwood Capital Group has filed a permit application for a two-building data center project, Plaza 500, on a 34-acre site near the Alexandria town line in Virginia. The first building, Plaza 500 South, is planned for over 226,000 square feet. The project, which includes a substation, faces significant opposition from local residents concerned about its proximity to homes. Starwood previously acquired the site four years ago for $165 million.
