Houston’s Antinozzi Group flips back to Compass, this time for good

The Real Deal — Texas — Wed, 08 Jul 2026 16:39:46 +0000

SUMMARY

Non-CRE / Business News

The Antinozzi Group, a Houston-based residential real estate team led by Joe Antinozzi, has rejoined Compass, citing the brokerage's technology platform as the primary driver for the move. This marks the team's second transition to Compass from Remax, with Antinozzi stating this move is permanent. The team, which closed $30 million in residential sales in 2025, focuses on properties in Spring, Montgomery, and The Woodlands, with plans for expansion into Austin and Dallas.
