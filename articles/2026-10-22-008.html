Jackson Walker Signs 110,000 SF Office Lease at The Republic in Downtown Austin

LEASE

REBusinessOnline — Thu, 22 Oct 2026 14:07:46 +0000

SUMMARY

Jackson Walker has signed a significant 110,000-square-foot office lease at The Republic, a newly constructed 48-story office tower in downtown Austin. The law firm will occupy three mid-level floors, marking a notable commitment to the city's central business district. The building is owned by a partnership of DivcoWest, Phoenix Property Group, and Lincoln Property Co., with Lincoln also managing the leasing.
