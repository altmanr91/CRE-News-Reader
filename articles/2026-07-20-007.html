Bonner Carrington, Sycamore Development announce opening of 168-unit multifamily development in North Dallas

DEVELOPMENT

REJournals — Mon, 20 Jul 2026 09:54:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Bonner Carrington and Sycamore Development have opened Cypress Creek Montfort Drive, a 168-unit mixed-income multifamily community in North Dallas. The development provides much-needed affordable housing, with 116 units designated for residents earning 30% to 80% of the area median income and 52 market-rate units. The project aims to offer quality housing in a high-opportunity area, fostering a stronger workforce and supporting local businesses.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Cypress Creek Montfort Drive

Address: 14119 Montfort Drive, Dallas, TX

Units: 168

Notable Features: Mixed-income community with 116 income-restricted units and 52 market-rate units; features a four-story building with structured parking, resort-style pool, fitness center, business center, and pet spa.

Project Notes: Financing included Federal 9% Low-Income Housing Tax Credit equity, construction and permanent financing, a soft loan from the City of Dallas, and funding from the U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development’s Community Development Block Grant Disaster Recovery (CDBG-DR) Program. 41 units are supported by the Walker Settlement Voucher program.
