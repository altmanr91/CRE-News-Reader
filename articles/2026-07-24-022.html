SL Green cashes in on Manhattan office rebound

The Real Deal — New York — Thu, 23 Jul 2026 20:30:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Leasing Report

SL Green Realty Corp. has significantly raised its full-year earnings guidance, driven by robust Manhattan office leasing activity, particularly in Midtown's Class A market. The company's CEO, Marc Holliday, noted a growing scarcity of premier space due to limited new construction and conversions of older buildings to residential use, which has created a landlord-favorable environment. Strong demand from financial services and AI-driven tech companies, exemplified by a 100,000-square-foot lease at 11 Madison Avenue, has contributed to substantial rent growth across SL Green's portfolio.

DATA POINTS

Q2 Manhattan Office Leases: 53 leases, 445,000 SF

Q1 Manhattan Office Leases: 51 leases, 929,000 SF

Earnings Guidance Increase: $1.20 per share (26% increase)

11 Madison Avenue Lease: 100,000 SF

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Midtown's Class A office space supply is tightening due to a dearth of new construction and conversions of older office buildings to apartments. Asking rents have been rising throughout the year, with strong leasing spreads anticipated. The financial services sector is performing exceptionally well, contributing to an "extraordinary, prolonged surge in business activity" in the city. AI-driven tech leasing is also a significant driver of demand.
