Partnership Acquires Shuttered Mall in North Houston, Plans $150M Industrial Redevelopment

ACQUISITION

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 25 Sep 2026 15:22:03 +0000

SUMMARY

A partnership between Lincoln Property Co. and New York Life Investment Management has acquired the former Greenspoint Mall in North Houston. The partnership plans a $150 million redevelopment to transform the 80-acre site into CityNorth Industrial Park, featuring 1.2 million square feet of industrial space along with retail and service uses. Demolition of the mall is imminent, with project completion anticipated in early 2028.
