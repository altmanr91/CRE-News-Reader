CRE's Clearance Rack Is Empty, Leaving Landlords To Create Their Own Value

Bisnow Latest News Feed — Tue, 01 Sep 2026 19:16:52 -0400

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

Commercial real estate analysts are observing a market where distressed assets, often referred to as the 'clearance rack,' are no longer readily available. This scarcity is prompting landlords to proactively enhance the value of their properties through strategic management and improvements, rather than relying on opportunistic acquisitions of undervalued assets. The current market environment emphasizes the importance of net operating income as the primary driver for asset appreciation.
