Development Site Sale Closes Nearly Nine Years After Going Under Contract

ACQUISITION

Connect CRE — Wed, 08 Jul 2026 20:56:14 +0000

SUMMARY

Sunrise Senior Living has acquired a 2.84-acre development site at 374 Atlantic Ave. in Oceanside, NY for $7.5 million, with plans to develop an 84-unit assisted living and memory care community. The transaction, brokered by Lee & Associates NYC, took nearly nine years to close due to a complex entitlement process, public hearings, and legal proceedings, including challenges during the COVID-19 pandemic. The successful closing highlights the perseverance required in commercial real estate brokerage, especially for projects with extended planning and approval phases.
