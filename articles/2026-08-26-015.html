Burlington, Planet Fitness leases bring Chicago’s Joffco Square retail center to 100% occupancy

LEASE

REJournals — Tue, 25 Aug 2026 14:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Nassimi Realty has secured two significant long-term leases with Burlington and Planet Fitness at Joffco Square in Chicago, bringing the urban retail center to full occupancy. Burlington signed a 10-year lease for 23,000 square feet, relocating to a more efficient format within the same trade area, while Planet Fitness committed to a 12-year lease for 22,000 square feet, entering the market to capitalize on local population growth. These transactions represent the successful completion of a major repositioning effort for the property, which was previously 50% vacant.
