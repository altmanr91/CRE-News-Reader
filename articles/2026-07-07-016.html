Brookfield-backed data center firm to go public

The Real Deal — National — Mon, 06 Jul 2026 19:15:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Non-CRE / Business News

CSquare, a Dallas-based data center services provider backed by Brookfield, is preparing for an initial public offering (IPO) to raise up to $1.35 billion. The company plans to sell 50 million shares at a price range of $23 to $27 per share, which would give it a market valuation of $4.18 billion at the top end. The proceeds from the IPO are intended to repay $734 million in outstanding debt from a revolving credit facility and a $75 million promissory note.
