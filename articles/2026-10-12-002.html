NewcrestImage Acquires 174-Key The Hotel Zags in Downtown Portland

ACQUISITION

REBusinessOnline — Mon, 12 Oct 2026 15:04:30 +0000

SUMMARY

NewcrestImage has acquired The Hotel Zags, a 174-key luxury lifestyle boutique hotel located in Portland's Cultural District. The Dallas-based firm will manage the property in-house. The transaction terms were not disclosed.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: hotel

Property Name: The Hotel Zags

Address: 515 SW Clay St, Portland, OR*

Keys: 174

Notable Features: Features 2,700 square feet of meeting/event space, a retro game arcade, local artwork, a nature-focused courtyard with a living wall, and an onsite restaurant.

* Address sourced separately, not in original article.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: NewcrestImage
