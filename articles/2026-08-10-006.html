The Cost of Trying to Fix a Failed 1031 Exchange

REJournals — Mon, 10 Aug 2026 10:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

CRE Legal / Regulatory

The article analyzes the landmark tax case Dobrich v. Commissioner, which serves as a cautionary tale for real estate investors attempting Section 1031 exchanges. The case highlights the strict, unforgiving nature of the 45-day identification deadline for replacement properties. The taxpayers in Dobrich not only failed to meet this deadline but also attempted to falsify and backdate documents to create a fraudulent paper trail, resulting in a 75% civil fraud penalty in addition to the loss of tax deferral. The key takeaway for investors is the critical importance of meticulous pre-sale planning and strict adherence to all 1031 exchange timelines, emphasizing that honesty and integrity are paramount even when an exchange appears to have failed.
