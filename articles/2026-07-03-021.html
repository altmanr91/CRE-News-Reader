WATCH: Author Michael Gross on billionaire playground St. Barts

The Real Deal — New York — Thu, 02 Jul 2026 18:30:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Feature / Profile

This article features an interview with author Michael Gross, whose new book "Treasured Island: St. Barts and Its Barbarians, Billionaires, and Beauties" explores the transformation of St. Barts into a luxury destination for the global elite. Gross discusses the island's evolution from a private hideaway to a high-profile playground for billionaires, examining the tension between development driven by wealthy investors and the desire of locals and environmentalists to preserve the island's character. He highlights real estate as a lens through which to understand the evolution of power dynamics on the island.
