Fairstead celebrates completion of $26 million rehab of Houston affordable housing community

CONSTRUCTION

REJournals — Wed, 30 Sep 2026 14:02:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Fairstead has completed a $26 million rehabilitation of Coppertree Village, a 324-unit affordable housing community in Houston's Acres Homes neighborhood. The extensive renovation, which began after Fairstead acquired the property in September 2021 and concluded in August 2025, included significant upgrades to apartment interiors, building exteriors, community amenities, and security features. This project underscores Fairstead's commitment to preserving and enhancing affordable housing, aiming to provide residents with a high-quality living environment that is comparable to market-rate properties.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Coppertree Village

Address: 1415 West Gulf Bank Road, Houston, TX

Units: 324

Total Project Cost: $26.0M

$/Unit: $80,247/unit (calculated)

Notable Features: Features renovated kitchens with ENERGY STAR appliances, upgraded bathrooms, new ENERGY STAR LED lighting, LVT flooring, exterior improvements including ENERGY STAR windows, a new outdoor playground, and a reimagined community building with a Wi-Fi-enabled computer lab and library. Enhanced security includes perimeter fencing and controlled vehicular access.

Project Notes: Fairstead acquired Coppertree Village in September 2021. Renovations were completed in August 2025.
