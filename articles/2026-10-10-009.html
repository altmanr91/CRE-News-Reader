Alera Group Signs 13,668 SF Office at Lease in Northwest San Antonio

LEASE

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 09 Oct 2026 16:27:51 +0000

SUMMARY

Alera Group, an insurance and financial services company, has signed a 13,668-square-foot office lease in northwest San Antonio. The company will occupy space on the 10th floor of Concord Plaza, a mixed-use development featuring an 11-story office tower, a three-story corporate building, and ground-level retail.
