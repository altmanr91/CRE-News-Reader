The Forces Impacting CRE as AI, Energy and Supply Constraints Take Hold

Connect CRE — Fri, 07 Aug 2026 05:01:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Global Outlook

JLL's mid-year "Global Real Estate Outlook" report highlights six structural forces reshaping international commercial real estate markets, revising an earlier optimistic 2026 forecast due to geopolitical conflicts and trade tensions. The report emphasizes that cost management has become a primary business driver, with supply constraints creating opportunities for retrofitting existing properties. It also notes the increasing importance of building quality, amenities, and AI-enabled features in occupier decisions, as artificial intelligence transitions from experimentation to operational impact, potentially altering office demand. Furthermore, energy security is now a critical factor in development and leasing, while private capital is expanding its role in CRE investment amid improving pricing clarity and a competitive debt market.
