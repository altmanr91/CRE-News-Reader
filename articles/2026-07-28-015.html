Affiliated Closes Financing on Mixed-Income Fort Lauderdale Apartment Venture

LOAN

Connect CRE — Mon, 27 Jul 2026 22:52:48 +0000

SUMMARY

Affiliated Development has secured a $74 million construction loan from Pacific Life Insurance Company for The Cove, a mixed-use, mixed-income multifamily development in Fort Lauderdale. Over half of the 374 units will be designated for families earning 120% of the Area Median Income, in exchange for tax rebates from Fort Lauderdale and Broward County. Construction is set to begin immediately on the site of a former hotel.
