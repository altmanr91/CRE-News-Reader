Silverstein Adds 55K SF of Leases at Downtown L.A.’s U.S. Bank Tower

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Wed, 07 Oct 2026 14:55:22 +0000

SUMMARY

Silverstein Properties has secured over 55,000 square feet of new and expanded office leases at the U.S. Bank Tower in Downtown Los Angeles. The transactions include an expansion by a prominent injury law firm, a full-floor lease by an engineering and design firm, a new headquarters for the Los Angeles Area Chamber of Commerce, and a relocation for a consultant firm. These deals contribute to over 145,000 square feet of leasing activity at the tower in the past year, exceeding the landlord's expectations following a $60 million upgrade.

Countywide office leasing in Los Angeles reached 4 million square feet in the third quarter, marking the strongest three months since 2019, with availability declining compared to the previous year. Downtown L.A. continues to face challenges with elevated vacancy and financial distress.
