Historic downtown Miami courthouse heads to auction with development caveats

The Real Deal — Miami — Wed, 01 Jul 2026 14:30:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Property Listing / For Sale

The historic Miami-Dade County Courthouse, a nearly century-old vacant building in downtown Miami, is being auctioned off, presenting a significant redevelopment opportunity. The 28-story property, listed on the National Register of Historic Places, is valued at $43 million and is zoned for high-density residential development, allowing up to 500 units per acre. While the exterior must be preserved, the interior offers flexibility for conversion, with a hotel or residential redevelopment considered more likely than office space due to the building's historic architecture and modern tenant expectations. The county previously attempted to redevelop the property, with one proposal from GFO Investments for a hotel, office, and residential tower, and the firm is expected to bid again.
