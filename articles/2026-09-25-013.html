The importance of green space? Turning parking lots into lawns boosts fortunes of Indianapolis’ Carmel Gateway office park

REJournals — Fri, 25 Sep 2026 10:47:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

Carmel Gateway, a Class-A office park in Indianapolis, has transformed over two acres of underutilized parking lots into new green amenity spaces, known as the East and West Lawns. This initiative aims to enhance employee health, productivity, and retention by offering resort-style outdoor areas for work, breaks, and social events, including a waterside amphitheater with AV capabilities. The project reflects a strategic response to evolving tenant demands for flexible and healthy work environments, making the office park more attractive to prospective tenants and contributing to recent relocations to the property.
