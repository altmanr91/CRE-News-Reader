D.C. Reveals Vision For RFK Campus With Up To 6,500 Homes

Bisnow News Feed — Tue, 30 Jun 2026 13:46:27 -0400

SUMMARY

Development

The District of Columbia has unveiled its draft master plan for the 180-acre RFK Campus, envisioning a large-scale mixed-use development anchored by a new 70,000-seat stadium for the Washington Commanders. The plan, developed after extensive community feedback, proposes between 5,000 and 6,500 residential units, with 30% designated as affordable, alongside retail, hotel, and recreational spaces across six distinct districts. The D.C. Office of Planning aims to finalize the plan by year-end, with developer solicitations expected in late 2027 or early 2028, marking a significant step towards transforming the Anacostia Riverfront site into a 365-day community hub.
