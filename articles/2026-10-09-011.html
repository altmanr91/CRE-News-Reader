Integrate Construction Partners wraps work on $5.2 million ice rink renovation in Kirkwood

CONSTRUCTION

REJournals — Fri, 09 Oct 2026 15:05:55 +0000

SUMMARY

Integrate Construction Partners has completed a $5.2 million renovation of the municipal ice rink at the Kirkwood Community Center in Kirkwood, MO. The project, which was the final phase of an $11 million upgrade to the entire community center, involved replacing the aging refrigeration system with a modern ammonia-based one, installing new underground drain tile and concrete ice slab with 12 miles of refrigeration piping, and upgrading various other systems. The renovation was completed within a tight five-month schedule, with construction beginning in late February and the rink reopening on August 1.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: recreational

Property Name: Kirkwood Community Center Ice Rink

Address: 111 S Geyer Road, Kirkwood, MO

Total Project Cost: $5.2M

Year Built: 1999

Completion: August 2026

Notable Features: Renovation included replacement of an R-22 refrigeration system with a modern ammonia-based system, new underground drain tile, concrete ice slab with 12 miles of refrigeration piping, and upgraded heating, dehumidification, and LED lighting systems.

Project Notes: This renovation was the final phase of an $11 million, three-phase project to upgrade the entire community center. Demolition required creating a temporary opening through a foundation wall to accommodate dump trucks for debris removal.
