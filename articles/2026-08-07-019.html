Fintech Firm More Than Doubles Footprint at 10 Astor Place

LEASE

Connect CRE — Thu, 06 Aug 2026 21:50:38 +0000

SUMMARY

Fintech firm Maybern has significantly expanded its presence at 10 Astor Place in New York City, more than doubling its office footprint from 11,000 square feet to 30,000 square feet. The long-term lease expansion, which includes the entire fourth floor, demonstrates the tenant's strong confidence in the boutique building and its offerings. The landlord noted that such expansions by existing tenants are a powerful indicator of a property's value, particularly for buildings that offer enduring architecture and character.
