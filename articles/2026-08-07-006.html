Stena Real Estate to Renovate 580,558 SF Office Campus in Houston’s Energy Corridor

REBusinessOnline — Thu, 06 Aug 2026 15:02:37 +0000

SUMMARY

Construction

Stena Real Estate AB, the American ownership arm of Swedish investment company Stena Property, is undertaking a significant renovation of Woodbranch Plaza, a four-building, 580,558-square-foot office campus in Houston's Energy Corridor. The capital improvement program will focus on upgrading the lobby, common areas, and restrooms, activating new shared amenity spaces, and enhancing outdoor landscaping. Additionally, Stena is constructing speculative office suites to attract new tenants.
