Ares Buys Two Fully Leased Miami Warehouses for $109M

ACQUISITION

Commercial Observer — Fri, 04 Sep 2026 16:40:46 +0000

SUMMARY

Ares Management has acquired two fully leased industrial warehouses in Miami-Dade County from BGRE for a combined $108.7 million. The properties, totaling 372,619 square feet, were recently developed by BGRE and are fully occupied by strong tenants. This acquisition further expands Ares Management's growing industrial footprint in South Florida.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Size: 372,619 SF

Sale Price: $108.7M

Project Notes: The properties were completed between 2022 and 2023.
