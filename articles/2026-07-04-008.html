Marcus & Millichap closes sale of Classic Collision in San Antonio

SALE

REJournals — Fri, 03 Jul 2026 14:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Marcus & Millichap facilitated the sale of a net-leased auto service property in San Antonio, Texas, to a Minnesota-based buyer completing a 1031 exchange. Concurrently, Marcus & Millichap Capital Corporation arranged acquisition financing for the buyer, featuring a 5-year term and a 6.04% interest rate.

Address: 9809 Fredericksburg Rd., San Antonio, TX

Size: 23,560 SF

Site Area: 1.97 acres

COMPANIES/PEOPLE
