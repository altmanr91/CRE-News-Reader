Extell scores $185M refi on 1,400-apartment Yonkers luxury development

REFINANCE

The Real Deal — New York — Fri, 17 Jul 2026 17:02:33 +0000

SUMMARY

Extell Development secured a $185 million refinancing package for the initial phase of its Hudson Piers luxury multifamily development in Yonkers, NY. The financing, provided by NewBridge Lending and Meridian Capital, replaces previous commercial and mezzanine loans. The first phase, comprising 369 luxury apartments across two mixed-use buildings, is part of a larger 1,400-unit project that aims to transform Yonkers' industrial waterfront into a residential hub. The development has benefited from significant tax incentives from the city, reflecting a pro-housing stance by local administration.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Address: 45 and 55 Riverside Drive, Yonkers, NY

Units: 369

Loan Amount: $185.0M

Loan Per Unit: $501,355/unit (calculated)

Total Project (All Phases): 1,400 apartments and four public parks

Notable Features: Two seven-story, mixed-use buildings with ground-floor retail and parking, located on the Hudson River opposite the Palisades.

Project Notes: The development, formerly known as Point Street Landing, received $21 million in tax breaks from the city government. The first phase, Piers 3 and 4, began welcoming tenants in 2025. Each of the project's six buildings are expected to open within a year of each other.
