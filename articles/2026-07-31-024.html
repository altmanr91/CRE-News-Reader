Equinix Ramps Up Spending Plans Amid Faster-Than-Expected AI Shift

Bisnow News Feed — Thu, 30 Jul 2026 14:24:47 -0400

SUMMARY

Market Research / Data Center

Equinix, the world's largest data center firm, has significantly increased its revenue outlook and planned development spending, reflecting strong confidence in its ability to capitalize on the accelerating shift towards AI inference workloads. The company's CEO noted this as the largest single guidance raise in its history, driven by robust Q2 earnings and over 30% booking growth, primarily from AI-related deals. Equinix believes its core business model, focused on smaller, connectivity-rich facilities near population centers, is uniquely positioned for the growing demand for AI inference, which requires proximity to end-users and seamless data transfer between cloud providers. The firm plans to concentrate approximately 80% of its development spending in its top 25 existing markets, leveraging its established infrastructure and ability to deliver capacity in power-constrained areas.
