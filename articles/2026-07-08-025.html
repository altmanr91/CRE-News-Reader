AvalonBay buys city block in South Miami as investors get in position for multifamily bounceback

ACQUISITION

The Real Deal — Miami — Tue, 07 Jul 2026 21:30:00 +0000

SUMMARY

AvalonBay Communities acquired a 1.2-acre full city block development site in South Miami for $22 million, where it plans to build a 16-story mixed-use project with 251 apartments and ground-floor retail. This transaction represents the highest per-unit price ever paid for a development site in South Miami. Separately, investor Adam Marcuvitz, through Midas Equities LLC, purchased two apartment properties in Fort Lauderdale for a combined $12.8 million, including a Flagler Village redevelopment site with 43 units and an 18-unit building in Harbordale. Marcuvitz intends to operate the Flagler Village property while awaiting improved development conditions, anticipating a multifamily market rebound as new supply deliveries taper off.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Avalon South Miami II

Address: 5920 Sunset Drive to 5946 South Dixie Highway, South Miami, FL

Units: 251

Sale Price: $22.0M

Site Area: 1.2 acres

Notable Features: 16-story mixed-use development with ground-floor retail, pool, rooftop lounge, and 394 parking spaces. The site has 300 feet of waterfrontage and is one of the few remaining full-block development sites in downtown South Miami.

Project Notes: The historic corner building at 5900 to 5904 Sunset Drive was not included in the sale. The Flagler Village site has redevelopment potential for 200 to 210 units. The Harbordale apartment building was built in 1973.

FINANCING

Flagler Village acquisition partially financed with a $4.4 million seller-carried mortgage. Harbordale acquisition partially financed through a $1.9 million seller-carried mortgage.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Multifamily deliveries are tapering off and development is slowing across South Florida after a post-pandemic building boom, according to CBRE.
