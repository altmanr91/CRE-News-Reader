Lincoln Property Buys Charlotte Developer In Bid To Boost Deals

ACQUISITION

Bisnow News Feed — Tue, 14 Jul 2026 16:39:44 -0400

SUMMARY

Lincoln Property Co. has acquired Charlotte-based developer The Spectrum Cos., a move designed to significantly expand Lincoln's presence across the Carolinas and into northern and central Florida's multifamily sector. The acquisition merges Spectrum's substantial multifamily development expertise and portfolio with Lincoln's existing platform, aiming to boost the combined firm's development and acquisition pipeline. Executives from both companies emphasized the complementary nature of their portfolios, particularly Spectrum's strong focus on multifamily projects, which will enhance Lincoln's regional growth strategy.

DATA POINTS

Project Notes: Spectrum's 43 employees will relocate to Lincoln's headquarters at Piedmont Town Center.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: Lincoln Property Co. — Johno Harris, Senior Executive Vice President

SELLER: The Spectrum Cos.

OWNER: Cousins Properties

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Charlotte is considered one of the most attractive markets for investors, according to CBRE's 2026 North America Investor Intentions Survey. The multifamily market in Charlotte is maintaining stronger demand fundamentals than most peer markets, with a 6.2% vacancy rate in Q1 indicating demand has absorbed new supply.
