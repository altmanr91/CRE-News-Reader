Office’s Real Story Is the Spread Between Prime Space and Everything Else

Commercial Observer — Tue, 08 Sep 2026 13:00:16 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

The office market is experiencing a recovery, with positive net absorption, increased leasing activity, and rising asking rents. However, this recovery is not uniform, as a significant spread exists between prime and commodity office spaces. The author argues that while the "flight to quality" consensus correctly identifies the importance of physical attributes, it overlooks the operational capabilities of a building. The true opportunity for investors lies in understanding how a building is configured, programmed, and managed, as this operational quality drives tenant experience and retention, creating value that is not yet priced into the market.
