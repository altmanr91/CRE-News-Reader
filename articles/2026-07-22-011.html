Colliers negotiates 56,760-square-foot headquarters lease in Chicago

LEASE

REJournals — Wed, 22 Jul 2026 09:47:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Swanson, Martin & Bell, LLP, a law firm, has restructured and renewed its long-term headquarters lease for 56,760 square feet at 330 N. Wabash Ave. in Chicago. The firm, which has grown significantly since its founding, will continue to occupy space in the iconic 52-story office tower, which is notable as the last building designed by Ludwig Mies van der Rohe in the U.S.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Address: 330 N. Wabash Ave., Chicago, IL

Size: 56,760 SF

Notable Features: The 52-story office tower was the last building in the U.S. designed by Ludwig Mies van der Rohe, featuring 35,000 SF floor plates, floor-to-ceiling windows, 12' ceilings, panoramic city views, and direct access to the Chicago Riverwalk.
