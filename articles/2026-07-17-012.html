A varied economy? It’s the secret to success of Lansing’s CRE market

REJournals — Fri, 17 Jul 2026 10:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Economic Report

Lansing, Michigan's commercial real estate market is demonstrating resilience due to its diversified economy, despite some sectors experiencing a slight slowdown, according to a new report from Colliers. The region's strong economic fundamentals, including its ranking among the top U.S. mid-sized metros for economic growth and workforce education, are supporting demand across industrial, office, and retail sectors. While industrial and office markets show increased vacancy and negative absorption, retail and multifamily sectors remain healthy with stable occupancy and rising rents, indicating a balanced but selective market for tenants and investors.

Average Retail Asking Rent (H1 2026): $13.84/SF

Multifamily Occupancy Rate (Q2 2026): 93.7%

Average Monthly Effective Multifamily Rent (Q2 2026): $1,311

Multifamily Rent Growth (YoY): 3.8%

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Greater Lansing ranks among the top 10 U.S. mid-sized metros for economic growth and among the top 15 regions nationally for workforce education levels. Industrial vacancy rates remain below the national average. Industrial rental rates increased from $7.28/SF at the end of 2025 to $7.52/SF in H1 2026. Office asking rents held steady at $17.04/SF despite elevated vacancies. Retail asking rents increased to $13.84/SF.
