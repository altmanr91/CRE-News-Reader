INDUS Starts Work on 500K-SF Nashville-Area Warehouse Facility

LOAN

Connect CRE — Tue, 06 Oct 2026 14:29:41 +0000

SUMMARY

INDUS Realty Trust has secured $36.1 million in construction financing for the initial phase of INDUS Commerce Center @ 840, a speculative logistics facility spanning 507,000 square feet in Lebanon, TN. The project, located approximately 30 miles east of downtown Nashville, is designed to eventually expand into a 2.2 million-square-foot master-planned industrial park. Construction is set to commence immediately.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Property Name: INDUS Commerce Center @ 840

Size: 507,000 SF

Loan Amount: $36.1M

Loan Per SF: $71/SF (calculated)

Phase I: 507,000-square-foot speculative logistics facility

Total Project (All Phases): 2.2 million-square-foot master-planned park

Notable Features: Located directly off Interstate 840 in Wilson County, with close access to interstates 40, 24 and 65, and near Smyrna Airport and Nashville International Airport.

Project Notes: The five-year, floating-rate loan is for the first phase of a master-planned park intended to include two to three additional buildings ranging from 400,000 to 1 million square feet.
