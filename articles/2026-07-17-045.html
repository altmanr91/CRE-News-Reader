Silicon Valley Cities See Spike In Office Demolitions

Bisnow News Feed — Thu, 16 Jul 2026 17:46:41 -0400

SUMMARY

Market Research / Development Trends

A growing trend of office demolitions and conversions to multifamily housing is emerging across Silicon Valley cities like Sunnyvale, San Mateo, and Menlo Park. This shift is driven by state mandates for increased housing density, significant appreciation in apartment rents, and the obsolescence of many lower-tier office buildings. Developers are finding that razing outdated office structures to build new apartments, or converting well-suited office towers, is a more viable strategy than attempting to lease vacant office space, especially given the challenges of converting buildings with unfavorable layouts. The upzoning efforts by local municipalities, spurred by state housing requirements, are making these projects financially feasible by allowing for higher density and maximizing unit counts.
