Rexford Sells Fullerton Industrial Building For $50.2M: The Los Angeles Deal Sheet

SALE

Bisnow Latest News Feed — Sun, 01 Nov 2026 19:50:25 -0500

SUMMARY

Rexford Industrial has sold a 136,000-square-foot industrial building in Fullerton, CA for $50.2 million to Terra Industrial. The buyer, a manufacturing company, will consolidate its operations into this single facility, having previously occupied two separate facilities totaling 180,000 square feet. This acquisition represents a strategic move for Terra Industrial to optimize its operational footprint.
