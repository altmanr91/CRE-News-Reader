Downtown Chicago office vacancy dips for first time since pandemic

The Real Deal — Chicago — Wed, 08 Jul 2026 17:34:14 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Office Vacancy Report

Downtown Chicago's office market is showing its first signs of recovery since the pandemic, with the vacancy rate dipping for the first time in 15 quarters. While the overall vacancy remains high, there's a notable increase in tenant demand for larger spaces, reaching pre-pandemic levels. This shift is driven by owners investing in amenity upgrades for trophy assets or converting properties to mixed-use, alongside companies opting to occupy previously subleased space.

DATA POINTS

Downtown office vacancy rate Q2 2026: 28%

Downtown office vacancy rate Q1 2026: 28.6%

Pre-pandemic office vacancy rate: 13.8%

Q2 2026 net absorption: -97,000 SF

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Downtown Chicago office vacancy dipped for the first time in 15 consecutive quarters, from 28.6% to 28%. Demand from tenants seeking over 25,000 square feet has reached pre-pandemic highs. The second quarter saw a 97,000 SF dive in net absorption, but overall occupancy was bolstered by companies choosing to occupy previously subleased space.
