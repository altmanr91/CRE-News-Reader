ShipBob Signs 301,826 SF Industrial Lease in Bethlehem, Pennsylvania

LEASE

REBusinessOnline — Wed, 29 Jul 2026 14:53:03 +0000

SUMMARY

ShipBob, an e-commerce fulfillment services provider, has signed a 301,826-square-foot industrial lease for the entirety of Building E at Lehigh Valley Trade Center in Bethlehem, Pennsylvania. This transaction brings the third phase of the 171-acre trade center to full occupancy, highlighting strong demand for industrial space in the Lehigh Valley market.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Property Name: Building E at Lehigh Valley Trade Center

Size: 301,826 SF

Project Notes: Building E is part of the third phase of the 171-acre Lehigh Valley Trade Center.
