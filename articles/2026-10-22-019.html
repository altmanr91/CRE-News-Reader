Gilbane Development Acquires Sheepshead Bay Site Suited to Large-Scale Affordable Housing

ACQUISITION

Connect CRE — Wed, 21 Oct 2026 21:06:04 +0000

SUMMARY

Gilbane Development has acquired a 96,000-square-foot development site at 2185 Coyle St. in Brooklyn's Sheepshead Bay for $37 million. The property, recently rezoned, offers 408,400 buildable square feet and is considered ideal for a large-scale affordable housing project. The transaction was facilitated by Ariel Property Advisors on behalf of the seller, a joint venture between AAG Management and ABS Partners.
