South Florida Retail Markets Show Sub-5% Vacancy

Commercial Observer — Thu, 16 Jul 2026 21:45:48 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Retail Report

South Florida's retail sector is demonstrating strong performance, with low vacancy rates, resilient rental growth, and robust leasing and investment sales activity across Miami-Dade, Broward, and Palm Beach counties. Miami-Dade County leads with the lowest vacancy rate at 3 percent and rising asking rents, while Palm Beach County also saw positive absorption and rent increases. Broward County experienced a slight increase in vacancy and a decline in asking rents, though it still recorded significant leasing activity. The overall market performance indicates a healthy retail environment, defying earlier concerns about the sector's post-pandemic outlook.
