CrestMarc, Midloch acquire 294-unit apartment property near Sam Houston State University

ACQUISITION

REJournals — Fri, 31 Jul 2026 14:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

CrestMarc and Midloch Investment Partners have acquired The Forum at Sam Houston, a 294-unit apartment property in Huntsville, Texas, catering to the Sam Houston State University community. The property, built in 2010, offers a mix of unit types and amenities, and is strategically located near the university. The acquisition is supported by the university's stable and growing student population and a lack of new competitive developments in the submarket, which is expected to drive sustained leasing strength and rent growth.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: The Forum at Sam Houston

Address: 5050 Sam Houston Ave, Huntsville, TX*

Units: 294

Year Built: 2010

Notable Features: Located 0.4 miles from Sam Houston State University; features a resort-style pool, sand volleyball court, basketball court, 24-hour clubhouse, fitness center, bark park, and private shuttle to campus.
