Cross Ocean, Lincoln Buy 1M-SF Dallas Offices, Plan $100M Makeover

ACQUISITION

Connect CRE — Tue, 03 Nov 2026 15:57:37 +0000

SUMMARY

Cross Ocean Partners and Lincoln have acquired The Colonnade, a three-tower, 1.08 million-square-foot office campus in Dallas, TX. The joint venture plans a $100 million repositioning of the asset, which was previously owned by Fortis Property Group and fell into receivership after a loan default. The renovation will introduce new amenities and upgrade existing facilities, with the first phase of improvements expected by 2027.

Completion: 2027

Site Area: 12.48 acres

Notable Features: Three-tower office campus located along the Dallas North Tollway.
