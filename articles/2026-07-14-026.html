Judge tosses suit over Two Roads’ Four Seasons Bahamas. Not so fast, plaintiff says

The Real Deal — Miami — Mon, 13 Jul 2026 18:19:55 +0000

SUMMARY

CRE Legal / Regulatory

A New York County Supreme Court judge dismissed a lawsuit filed by Soho Development against Two Roads Development concerning a Four Seasons-branded condo project in the Bahamas. Soho Development, led by Roger Stein, alleged that Two Roads excluded them from the $300 million development, despite utilizing Soho's project contracts and confidential information without compensation, breaching a 2021 confidentiality and non-disclosure agreement. Two Roads, in turn, characterized the lawsuit as an "unabashed money grab." While the judge sided with Two Roads, stating the NDA did not expressly prohibit Two Roads from proceeding without Soho, Soho Development claims the ruling addressed an outdated complaint, not their recently amended filing.
