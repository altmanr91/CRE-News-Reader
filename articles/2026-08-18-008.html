Partners Capital Acquires 41,041 SF Shopping Center in McAllen, Texas

ACQUISITION

REBusinessOnline — Mon, 17 Aug 2026 14:49:33 +0000

SUMMARY

Partners Capital, the investment arm of Partners Real Estate, has acquired Colonnade of McAllen, a 41,041-square-foot shopping center in McAllen, Texas. Built in 2004, the property was fully leased to a diverse mix of service-oriented tenants at the time of sale. The acquisition expands Partners Capital's retail portfolio in the region.

DATA POINTS

Address: 6500 N 10th St, McAllen, TX*
