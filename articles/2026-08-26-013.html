Northmarq closes $31 million multifamily sale in Sheboygan

SALE

REJournals — Tue, 25 Aug 2026 16:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Northmarq's Midwest Investment Sales team facilitated the $31 million sale of Harbor Estates, a 186-unit garden-style multifamily community in Sheboygan, Wisconsin. The property, built in 2002, was sold by a partnership between Harmoniq Residential and Midloch Investment Partners. Its strategic location near Lake Michigan, major employers, and highly-rated schools, combined with a strong amenity package, positions it for continued investment performance.
