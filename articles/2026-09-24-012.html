Marcus & Millichap brokers sale of 248-unit multifamily property in Bryan

SALE

REJournals — Thu, 24 Sep 2026 13:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Marcus & Millichap has brokered the sale of Retreat at 2818, a 248-unit multifamily property in Bryan, Texas. A California-based real estate investment firm acquired the asset from a Texas-based private client. The buyer plans to implement a value-add strategy, investing approximately $3 million in renovations during the first year of ownership.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Retreat at 2818

Address: 3301 Providence Ave, Bryan, TX

Units: 248

Year Built: 1977

Site Area: 18.84 acres

Notable Features: Located near Texas A&M University and the Bryan-College Station BioCorridor.

Project Notes: Property previously received over $2.8 million in capital improvements. Buyer plans an additional $3 million in renovations.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

SELLER BROKER: Marcus & Millichap — Alec Schenk; Nick Fluellen; Bard Hoover; John McGregor

SELLER: Texas-based private client

BUYER: California-based real estate investment firm
