Holt Lunsford Negotiates 20,258 SF Industrial Lease Renewal in Northeast Dallas

LEASE

REBusinessOnline — Thu, 09 Jul 2026 15:05:16 +0000

SUMMARY

Holt Lunsford Commercial has successfully negotiated a 20,258-square-foot industrial lease renewal in northeast Dallas. The transaction involved a fragrance company renewing its occupancy at a property owned by ATCAP Partners. This renewal underscores continued demand for industrial space in the Dallas market.
