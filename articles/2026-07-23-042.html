Walker Webcast: Willy Walker Cuts Through the Market Noise

Connect CRE — Wed, 22 Jul 2026 22:07:54 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

Willy Walker, Chairman and CEO of Walker & Dunlop, addressed the commercial real estate market's slow recovery, noting that the anticipated rebound in 2025 did not materialize, leading to a new industry mantra of "slice of heaven in '27." He emphasized the importance of analyzing data to cut through market noise, highlighting the steady employment rate despite AI fears. Walker also pointed out the growing disparity in AI token usage between China and the U.S., raising national security concerns. He discussed the disconnect between low consumer sentiment and a strong stock market, suggesting political divisiveness or ongoing conflicts as potential causes. Finally, Walker warned of a significant wave of CRE loan maturities expected between 2029 and 2031 due to a recent trend of shorter loan durations.
