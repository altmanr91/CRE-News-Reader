PNC Bank Provides $92M Construction Loan for Luxury D.C. Multifamily

LOAN

Commercial Observer — Mon, 14 Sep 2026 15:22:10 +0000

SUMMARY

Carr Properties has secured a $92 million construction loan from PNC Bank and United Bank for a 299-unit luxury multifamily complex in Downtown Washington, D.C. The project, located at 2121 Virginia Avenue, will feature extensive amenities and is expected to begin construction this month, with units available in 2028. The financing was facilitated by Berkadia and includes a 20-year tax abatement, reflecting strong lender confidence in the sponsor's experience and the demand for new housing in the Foggy Bottom neighborhood.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Address: 2121 Virginia Avenue, Washington, D.C.

Units: 299

Loan Amount: $92.0M

Loan Per Unit: $307,692/unit (calculated)

Completion: 2028

Notable Features: Class A multifamily complex with approximately 16,000 square feet of amenity space, including an indoor pool, club room, fitness center, coworking spaces, library, game room, and outdoor courtyards. Located near George Washington University.

Project Notes: Loan structured with a five-year term and 20-year tax abatement. Construction expected to begin in September 2026.
