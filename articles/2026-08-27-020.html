Velocity Financial to Acquire Toorak Operating Platform, Manage $3B Loan Portfolio

Connect CRE — Thu, 27 Aug 2026 16:07:58 +0000

SUMMARY

Non-CRE / Business News

Velocity Financial, Inc. has agreed to acquire the operating platform of Toorak Capital LLC, a lending and asset management platform for single-family and multifamily properties. Concurrently, a third-party investment firm will acquire Toorak's existing $3 billion loan portfolio. Velocity will manage this portfolio and also handle future loan production for the third-party firm and other counterparties, expanding its market presence and operational capabilities.
