CDK Lending Supplies $37M Loan for Newark Apartments Project

LOAN

Commercial Observer — Wed, 22 Jul 2026 17:46:14 +0000

SUMMARY

RBH Group secured $36.7 million in pre-development financing from CDK Lending for a 420-unit mixed-use multifamily project in Newark, NJ. The loan retires previous debt from Goldman Sachs and will advance the long-planned development, which includes ground-floor retail. The project is situated on a 5.65-acre land assemblage in the Four Corners neighborhood, a site that has been under assembly since 2005.
