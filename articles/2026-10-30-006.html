Toro Development Opens $560M Medley Mixed-Use Destination in Johns Creek, Georgia

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 30 Oct 2026 15:05:27 +0000

SUMMARY

Development

Toro Development Co. has officially opened Medley, a $560 million mixed-use destination in Johns Creek, Georgia. The 43-acre project includes 25 new restaurants and retailers, 112,000 square feet of office space that opened fully leased, and 881 luxury residences. A 150-room boutique hotel under Marriott's Tribute Portfolio is also planned to open in 2028.
