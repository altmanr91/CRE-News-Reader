Four Seasons Resort Palm Beach, East Miami hotel score refi totaling $546M

REFINANCE

The Real Deal — Miami — Wed, 15 Jul 2026 18:50:50 +0000

SUMMARY

Fort Partners and Blackstone Real Estate have secured significant refinancing loans for two luxury hotels in South Florida, totaling $546 million. Blackstone obtained a $205 million loan from JPMorgan Chase for the East Miami hotel, which it acquired last year for $300 million. Separately, Fort Partners secured a $341 million refinancing from JPMorgan and Citi Real Estate Funding for the Four Seasons Resort Palm Beach, increasing the existing loan balance by $103 million. Both properties are prominent assets in their respective markets, with the Four Seasons Resort Palm Beach being an iconic oceanfront destination and the East Miami hotel a key component of the Brickell City Centre mixed-use development.
