Teeple Partners Breaks Ground on 312-Unit Multifamily Project in Bryan, Texas

CONSTRUCTION

REBusinessOnline — Wed, 14 Oct 2026 14:21:34 +0000

SUMMARY

Teeple Partners has commenced construction on Oaks Reserve Apartments, a 312-unit multifamily development in Bryan, Texas. This project is the second phase of a larger 53-acre development that will ultimately comprise over 1,000 units. The first phase, Brazos Oaks, was completed earlier this year.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Oaks Reserve Apartments

Units: 312

Phase I: Brazos Oaks (315 units)

Total Project (All Phases): 1,000+ units across 53 acres

Notable Features: Amenities will include a pool, fitness center, conference room, pickleball courts, and multiple dog parks.

Project Notes: This is the second of three phases of a larger development.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

DEVELOPER/SPONSOR: Teeple Partners
