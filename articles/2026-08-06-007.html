University of St. Thomas Breaks Ground on 400-Bed Residence Hall Project in Houston

CONSTRUCTION

REBusinessOnline — Wed, 05 Aug 2026 15:04:25 +0000

SUMMARY

The University of St. Thomas has commenced construction on a new 400-bed residence hall within its Houston campus. This 95,000-square-foot development will feature a dedicated chapel, prayer room, courtyard, multi-use lounges, and collaborative study areas. The units will offer various bedroom configurations, including two-, three-, and four-bedroom layouts, designed for single or double occupancy, along with two staff apartments.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: student housing

Size: 95,000 SF

Beds: 400

Notable Features: Includes a dedicated chapel and prayer room, courtyard, multi-use lounges, and collaborative study zones. Units will be offered in two-, three-, and four-bedroom configurations for single or double occupancy, plus two staff apartments.

Project Notes: Tentative completion date was not announced.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

OWNER: University of St. Thomas

ARCHITECT: Corgan
