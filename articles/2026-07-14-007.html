JLL Brokers Sale of 72,184 SF Shopping Center in Northwest Houston

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Mon, 13 Jul 2026 15:24:59 +0000

SUMMARY

JLL has brokered the sale of Vintage Marketplace, a 72,184-square-foot shopping center in northwest Houston. The property, anchored by a prominent grocery store and boasting a strong tenant mix, was approximately 95 percent leased at the time of sale. Brixmor Property Group acquired the asset from an undisclosed seller.
