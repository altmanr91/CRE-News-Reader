Delshah Capital Acquires Five Upper West Side Apartment Buildings for $37M

ACQUISITION

Commercial Observer — Thu, 30 Jul 2026 19:18:05 +0000

SUMMARY

Delshah Capital has acquired a portfolio of five multifamily apartment buildings on Manhattan's Upper West Side from Quality Capital USA for $36.9 million. The portfolio comprises two neighboring buildings on West 108th Street with approximately 48 units and three buildings on Manhattan Avenue with around 99 units. This acquisition represents a significant discount from the portfolio's previous sale price in 2018.
