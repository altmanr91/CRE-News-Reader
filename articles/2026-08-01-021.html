Avison Young Had Assets Frozen By Bank Over Unpaid Taxes

Bisnow News Feed — Fri, 31 Jul 2026 12:25:33 -0400

SUMMARY

Non-CRE / Business News

Avison Young's UK accounts were frozen by NatWest Group due to unpaid taxes, prompting a winding-up petition from His Majesty's Revenue and Customs. A judge ordered the unfreezing of assets, allowing Avison Young to pay its outstanding tax obligations. The brokerage has faced ongoing cash flow issues since 2022, exacerbated by declining real estate deal volumes and a lawsuit from ICG over alleged negligent valuations.
