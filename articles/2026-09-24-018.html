JLL Arranges JV Equity, Acquisition Debt on Jersey City Multifamily

LOAN

Connect CRE — Wed, 23 Sep 2026 21:47:43 +0000

SUMMARY

JLL Capital Markets has arranged joint venture equity and acquisition financing for Jasmin Terrace II, a newly constructed 56-unit luxury apartment building in Jersey City. The transaction involved Tower Management Service LP, making its debut in the Jersey City market, securing a joint venture equity investment from Lakerock, forming a new partnership. JLL then secured a $16.5 million, five-year, fixed-rate acquisition loan through RWC Lending for the partnership.

Address: 412 Whiton St, Jersey City, NJ
