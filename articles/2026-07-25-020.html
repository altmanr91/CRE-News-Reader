BioMed Hands Boston Lab, Office Property To Lender

REO

Bisnow News Feed — Fri, 24 Jul 2026 12:17:54 -0400

SUMMARY

A partnership between KKR Real Estate Finance Trust and AllianceBernstein has taken ownership of a two-building lab and office complex in Boston's South End from BioMed Realty. The lenders had provided a $322 million floating-rate loan to BioMed Realty in April 2021 when it acquired the properties with the intention of converting them from office to lab space. While one building was partially converted and leased to life sciences tenants, the other remained office space, and the life sciences market in Boston has since seen a significant increase in vacancy.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: lab / office

Address: 1000 Washington St. and 321 Harrison Ave., Boston, MA

Notable Features: Properties are connected by a central lobby and footbridge, with amenities including a fitness center, outdoor patio, amenity lounge, conference center, and kitchenette.

Project Notes: BioMed Realty secured a $322M floating-rate loan in April 2021 to acquire the properties from CIM Group with the intention of converting them to labs. Approval for 321 Harrison Ave. conversion was granted in July 2021, and for 1000 Washington St. in 2023.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Boston's life sciences market has seen a dramatic shift since 2021, with a surge of speculative lab projects coinciding with a drop in demand, leading to a vacancy rate of 28.7% across Greater Boston as of Q2.
