Soar AI Secures $1B Equity For Industrial-To-Data-Center Development

DEVELOPMENT

Bisnow Latest News Feed — Mon, 12 Oct 2026 14:15:29 -0400

SUMMARY

Soar AI, a company founded by a former private jet operator, has secured a significant $1 billion equity commitment. This funding will be used to develop AI campuses by transforming existing industrial sites into data centers. The initiative aims to capitalize on the growing demand for specialized infrastructure to support artificial intelligence operations.
