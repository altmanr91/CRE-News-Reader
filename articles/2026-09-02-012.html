Signs of life in Chicago’s office market? Yes, but not everywhere

REJournals — Wed, 02 Sep 2026 10:11:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Office Report

Chicago's office market is experiencing a "flight-to-quality" trend, with tenants, especially large corporate occupiers and professional services firms, gravitating towards top-tier buildings in prime locations. This is evident in the Central Business District's continued momentum through August, with trophy properties accounting for a significant portion of new leasing activity. The West Loop, Fulton Market, and River North submarkets are leading in leasing volume and average new lease sizes, indicating strong demand for high-quality, amenitized spaces that support modern workplace strategies.

DATA POINTS

West Loop leasing volume (through August): 3.2 million square feet

Trophy properties' share of new CBD leasing activity: 28.8%

Leases of 100,000 SF or more as % of total CBD leasing activity: 16.1%
