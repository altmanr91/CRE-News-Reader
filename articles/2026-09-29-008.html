JLL Negotiates Sale of Shopping Center in Northwest Austin

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Tue, 29 Sep 2026 14:47:18 +0000

SUMMARY

JLL has facilitated the sale of Casis Village, a shopping center located in the Tarrytown neighborhood of northwest Austin. The property, anchored by a prominent grocer and 92% leased at the time of sale, was acquired by an undisclosed buyer from a joint venture between Ivy Cos. and 35 South Capital.
