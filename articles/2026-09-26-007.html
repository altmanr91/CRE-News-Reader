Cain Food Industries Signs 121,290 SF Industrial Lease in West Dallas

LEASE

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 25 Sep 2026 15:10:43 +0000

SUMMARY

Cain Food Industries Inc., a wholesale supplier of baking ingredients, has signed a 121,290-square-foot industrial lease in West Dallas. The company will occupy space at 1107 Conveyor Lane, a 220,741-square-foot building owned by Crow Holdings. This transaction highlights continued demand for industrial space in the West Dallas submarket.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Address: 1107 Conveyor Lane, Dallas, TX

Size: 121,290 SF

Notable Features: Building features 32-foot clear heights, 62 dock-high doors, 97 parking spaces, and approximately 3,000 square feet of office space.
