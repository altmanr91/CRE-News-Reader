Enverra Real Estate Partners acquires four-building office portfolio in Minneaoplis

ACQUISITION

REJournals — Fri, 28 Aug 2026 14:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Enverra Real Estate Partners has acquired the West End Office Park, a four-building Class A office portfolio in Minneapolis, MN. The portfolio, approximately 90% leased with a strong tenant mix, is strategically located at the intersection of I-394 and Highway 100, offering convenient access to both the urban core and affluent western suburbs. Enverra plans a $7 million capital improvement program to enhance the tenant experience with hospitality-driven amenities and a dedicated Tenant Experience Manager.
