Cooler Master Corp. Signs 97,285 SF Industrial Lease in West Houston

LEASE

REBusinessOnline — Tue, 21 Jul 2026 14:50:09 +0000

SUMMARY

Cooler Master Corp. has signed a 97,285-square-foot industrial lease for the entirety of Building 2 within the Grand West Crossing development in West Houston. This transaction represents a significant commitment to the area, as Grand West Crossing is a multi-phase project with additional buildings under construction, indicating ongoing industrial growth in the market.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Property Name: Grand West Crossing, Building 2

Size: 97,285 SF

Project Notes: Building 2 is part of the Grand West Crossing development, which is planned to include six buildings, with two completed and two under construction.
