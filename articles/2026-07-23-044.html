Historic Cinerama Dome, Adjacent 14-Screen Complex Set for 2028 Reopening

LEASE

Connect CRE — Wed, 22 Jul 2026 21:48:31 +0000

SUMMARY

The historic Cinerama Dome and an adjacent 14-screen movie complex in Hollywood, which have been closed since the pandemic, are slated to reopen in 2028 under the management of Alamo Drafthouse Cinema, a subsidiary of Sony Pictures Entertainment. The Cinerama Dome, a cultural landmark since 1963, will undergo restorative renovations to preserve its unique moviegoing experience and iconic branding. This lease agreement is seen as a significant endorsement for the future of theatrical entertainment and the enduring appeal of shared cinematic experiences.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: movie theater

Property Name: Cinerama Dome / Dome Entertainment Center

Address: 6360 Sunset Blvd, Los Angeles, CA

Year Built: 1963

Completion: early 2028

Notable Features: Historic Cinerama Dome with a unique geodesic design; adjacent 14-screen complex; will retain 'Cinerama' name and exterior branding.

Project Notes: The adjacent complex was formerly operated by ArcLight Cinemas.
