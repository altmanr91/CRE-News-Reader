Newsom Signs Bills Aimed At Cold Storage Post-Boyle Heights Fire

Bisnow Latest News Feed — Mon, 28 Sep 2026 19:57:53 -0400

SUMMARY

CRE Legal / Regulatory

California Governor Gavin Newsom has signed two bills into law that aim to improve safety regulations for cold storage facilities, following a significant fire at a Boyle Heights cold storage plant. The legislation, initially limited in scope, will expand to a statewide application in 2028. These measures are intended to prevent future incidents and enhance the safety standards of such industrial properties.
