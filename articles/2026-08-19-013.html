Provider of critical infrastructure services signs 25,352-square-foot lease in Fort Bend County mixed-use development

LEASE

REJournals — Tue, 18 Aug 2026 15:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Primoris Services Corporation, a Fortune 500 infrastructure services provider, has signed a 25,352-square-foot office lease at 2150 Town Square Place within the Sugar Land Town Square mixed-use development in Fort Bend County, Texas. This lease comes as the development undergoes a significant redevelopment initiative, including a refreshed lobby and new amenity suite, aimed at enhancing the tenant experience and attracting corporate employers. Primoris is expected to begin occupancy in 2027.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Property Name: Sugar Land Town Square

Address: 2150 Town Square Place, Sugar Land, TX

Size: 25,352 SF

Completion: 2027

Notable Features: Mixed-use development undergoing a $12.5 million reinvestment to enhance tenant experience, including a refreshed lobby, new amenity suite with an F&B-powered tenant lounge, conference center, fitness center, and café.

Project Notes: The City of Sugar Land approved a $12.5 million reinvestment in 2025 for property enhancements at Town Square One (2150 Town Square Place) and Town Square Two (2277 Plaza Drive). Renovations began in mid-2026 and are expected to be complete by spring 2027.
