Largely Vacant Santa Monica Offices Get New Life as School Campus

SALE

Connect CRE — Fri, 17 Jul 2026 20:49:43 +0000

SUMMARY

Colliers arranged the all-cash sale of Ocean Park Plaza, a largely vacant 100,700-square-foot office and medical building in Santa Monica, to an undisclosed co-educational school. The school plans to relocate and establish its new campus at the property, transforming the underutilized office asset into an active community use. This transaction is seen as a positive outcome for Santa Monica, providing the buyer with a prime Westside campus and bringing new activity and stability to the neighborhood.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Property Name: Ocean Park Plaza

Address: 2701 Ocean Park Blvd, Santa Monica, CA

Size: 100,700 SF

Occupancy at Sale: 27.0%

Notable Features: Two-story office and medical building.

Project Notes: The property was largely vacant with approximately 73% of its space available for immediate occupancy prior to the sale.
