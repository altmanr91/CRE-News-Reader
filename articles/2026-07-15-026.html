TFG International expands to South Florida amid wealth migration

The Real Deal — Miami — Tue, 14 Jul 2026 20:59:21 +0000

SUMMARY

Non-CRE / Business News

Luxury real estate group TFG International is expanding its operations into South Florida, establishing a presence in Miami through ONE Sotheby's International Realty. This move creates a bi-coastal operation connecting the Los Angeles and South Florida markets, driven by the migration of wealthy Californians to the Sunshine State. The firm aims to serve its existing ultra-luxury clients who are acquiring homes in South Florida, with plans to recruit additional agents for other market segments. The expansion is a strategic response to the synergistic market dynamics between Los Angeles and Miami, fueled by Florida's lifestyle, business-friendly environment, and the potential impact of a proposed wealth tax in California.
