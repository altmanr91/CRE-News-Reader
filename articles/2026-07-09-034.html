Naterra eyeing a $34M combination office and warehouse in Coppell

DEVELOPMENT

The Real Deal — Texas — Wed, 08 Jul 2026 21:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Naterra International, a cosmetics and beauty company, is developing a new $34 million combination headquarters and warehouse facility in Coppell, Texas. The expansive 442,010-square-foot facility will serve as the company's new base of operations, replacing its existing Coppell headquarters. Construction is being led by Pross Design Group, with completion anticipated by December 2027.
