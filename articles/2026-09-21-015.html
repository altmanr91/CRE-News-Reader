USF Breaks Ground on $500M Mixed-Use Venture

DEVELOPMENT

Connect CRE — Mon, 21 Sep 2026 13:45:10 +0000

SUMMARY

The University of South Florida (USF) and its development partners have broken ground on the initial $500 million phase of Uptown Landing, a new mixed-use project in Tampa. This first phase, spanning 30 acres, will feature student housing, graduate student and employee apartments, retail space, a hotel and conference center, and academic research facilities. The project is being delivered through a master development agreement with ACE Fletcher, a partnership formed by Capstone Development Partners, Aureate Development, and Ellison Development, with completion anticipated in fall 2028.
