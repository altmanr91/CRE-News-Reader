Developer Wolfe Landau Buys FiDi Redevelopment Site for $35M

ACQUISITION

Commercial Observer — Tue, 14 Jul 2026 21:36:06 +0000

SUMMARY

Wolfe Landau's Peninim Water has acquired two adjacent Financial District lots at 78 Pearl Street and 46-48 Water Street in New York City for $35 million. The irregularly-shaped, block-through site spans over 10,000 square feet and includes a vacant lot and an empty mixed-use building. The properties were sold by a consortium including the Milstein Organization, Swig Company, and Weiler Arnow Management, who had owned the parcels since the 1970s and 80s.
