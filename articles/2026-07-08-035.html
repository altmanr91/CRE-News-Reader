SF brokers have a new question for homebuyers: Got an extra million?

The Real Deal — San Francisco — Tue, 07 Jul 2026 21:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

San Francisco's luxury housing market is experiencing unprecedented competition, with 144 homes selling for at least $1 million over asking price in the first half of 2026, a significant increase from previous years. This trend is partly driven by a deliberate pricing strategy where homes are listed low to ignite bidding wars, capitalizing on high demand and scarce inventory. While the booming AI industry and upcoming IPOs are often cited, market analysts suggest the surge is more closely tied to the broader stock market's record highs, which translates into liquid wealth for tech employees in the region.

DATA POINTS

Homes sold for $1M+ over asking (H1 2026): 144

Homes sold for $1M+ over asking (June 2026): 44

Homes sold for $1M+ over asking (H1 2025): 8

Homes sold for $1M+ over asking (H1 2024): 6

Average listing price for $1M+ over asking sales: $3.8 million

Average sale price for $1M+ over asking sales: $5.3 million

San Francisco buyers vs. sellers (May): Buyers outstripped sellers by 14%

Oakland sellers vs. buyers (May): Sellers outnumbered buyers by more than 30%

Los Angeles sellers vs. buyers (May): Sellers outnumbered buyers by 56%

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

San Francisco is the only housing market west of the Mississippi River with more buyers than sellers, with buyers outstripping sellers by 14% in May. In contrast, Oakland saw sellers outnumber buyers by over 30%, and Los Angeles by 56%.
