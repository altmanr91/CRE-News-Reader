Data Center Firms DataBank, EdgeCore Reportedly Courting Buyers

Bisnow News Feed — Tue, 14 Jul 2026 14:00:24 -0400

SUMMARY

Property Listing / For Sale

Several prominent data center firms, including DataBank and EdgeCore, are reportedly exploring the sale of ownership stakes in their companies, driven by record demand and high valuations in the sector. DataBank, a Dallas-based operator with a significant U.S. and UK presence, is considering a sale of a majority stake that could reach $25 billion. EdgeCore, backed by Partners Group, is also seeking offers for a stake in its hyperscale-focused portfolio. This trend reflects a broader increase in data center operator mergers and acquisitions, with private equity firms expected to be key buyers in these platform-level transactions.

DATA POINTS

Data center M&A volume last year: exceeded $60B

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Data center M&A activity more than doubled last year, surpassing $60 billion, indicating a strong market for platform-level transactions.
