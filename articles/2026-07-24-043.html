Office’s New Purpose: Affiliation

Connect CRE — Fri, 24 Jul 2026 05:01:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Office Utilization Report

A new report from CBRE indicates that global office utilization saw its largest annual gain in four years in 2025, rising to 53% from 38% the previous year. The report, "Global Workplace & Occupancy Insights," highlights that organizations designing their office spaces for collaboration, culture, and connection are experiencing the strongest attendance gains. The primary purpose of the office has shifted from individual workspaces to fostering interaction, with 93% of respondents citing colleague collaboration as a main reason for coming in. Consequently, space dedicated to individual workstations decreased from 56% to 35% between 2021 and 2025, while collaborative areas expanded from 12% to 56%. The report suggests that office performance is now measured by what the workplace enables, rather than just its occupancy rate, and that quality environments and hybrid coordination are more effective than mandates for encouraging attendance.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Vacancy in prime office buildings offering plentiful amenities is measurably lower than the broader market. Office utilization is expected to continue rising through 2027, though economic uncertainty, slower hiring, workforce reductions, and resistance to rigid attendance policies could temper growth.
