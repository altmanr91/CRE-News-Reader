Gardner Tanenbaum Completes $60M Adaptive Reuse Project in Downtown Oklahoma City

CONSTRUCTION

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 11 Sep 2026 15:11:23 +0000

SUMMARY

Gardner Tanenbaum Holdings has completed The Harlow, a $60 million adaptive reuse project in downtown Oklahoma City. The project transformed two historic buildings, the Tradesmen National Bank building and the Medical Arts building, both constructed in the early 1920s, into a 265-unit apartment complex with additional retail space. The property offers a variety of unit types and a comprehensive amenity package.
