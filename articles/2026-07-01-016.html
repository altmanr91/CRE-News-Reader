CREFC’s Aidasani: Capital is Back but is Selective (VIDEO)

Connect CRE — Tue, 30 Jun 2026 20:39:11 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

Raj Aidasani, managing director of research with the CRE Finance Council, notes that while capital is available in 2026, it is highly selective compared to the riskier lending environment of 2021. Lenders are now prioritizing strong underwriting assumptions, robust property fundamentals, and sponsor strength to ensure durable cash flows. Aidasani also clarified that while the Federal Reserve's actions influence the overnight Fed funds rate, the commercial real estate industry is more concerned with the longer end of the interest rate curve.
