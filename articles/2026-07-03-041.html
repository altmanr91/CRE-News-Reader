Rithm Capital Lands $515M Financing for Midtown Office Tower

REFINANCE

Connect CRE — Thu, 02 Jul 2026 19:45:54 +0000

SUMMARY

Rithm Capital has secured $515 million in fixed-rate financing for its 29-story office tower at 31 W. 52nd St. in Midtown Manhattan. The financing package, led by Wells Fargo, includes a $415 million senior mortgage, a $40 million B-Note, and a $60 million mezzanine loan. This refinancing follows Rithm Capital's acquisition of a larger office portfolio from Paramount Group, and the successful securing of this substantial financing from a consortium of major lenders underscores continued confidence in well-located, high-quality office assets in New York City's prime business districts.
