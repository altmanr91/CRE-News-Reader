Inside developer Michael Stern and his Italian investor’s breakup

The Real Deal — Miami — Wed, 08 Jul 2026 19:02:18 +0000

SUMMARY

CRE Legal / Regulatory

A legal dispute has erupted between developer Michael Stern of JDS Development Group and Italian investor Gianluca Vacchi, stemming from a joint venture formed in 2024 to invest in a $4 billion pipeline of Miami developments. Vacchi alleges Stern orchestrated a "deliberated and calculated fraud" by misrepresenting project statuses and diverting investments, accusing him of running a Ponzi scheme. Stern, in turn, claims Vacchi's lawsuit is an abuse of the legal system designed to damage his reputation and extract a settlement, alleging Vacchi failed to meet capital obligations due to liquidity issues and lack of business experience. The dispute impacts projects like Mercedes-Benz Places Miami and the Dolce & Gabbana-branded 888 Brickell, both of which are also facing separate lawsuits, including a foreclosure action on Mercedes-Benz Places.
