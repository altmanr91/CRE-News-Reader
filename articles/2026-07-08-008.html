Avison Young Negotiates 61,861 SF Industrial Lease in Grand Prairie, Texas

LEASE

REBusinessOnline — Tue, 07 Jul 2026 15:24:30 +0000

SUMMARY

Avison Young has brokered a 10-year industrial lease for 61,861 square feet in Grand Prairie, Texas. The tenant, Flow Control Group, will occupy space within Building 2 of the industrial property at 3550 N. Highway 161. The transaction highlights continued demand for industrial space in the Dallas-Fort Worth metroplex.

DATA POINTS

Property Name: Building 2
