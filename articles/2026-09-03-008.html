Merchants Capital Provides $138.5M in Financing for White Plains Multifamily Project

LOAN

REBusinessOnline — Thu, 03 Sep 2026 13:20:17 +0000

SUMMARY

Merchants Capital has provided $138.5 million in financing for the North White Plains Workforce Transit Hub, a 296-unit multifamily project in White Plains, NY. The financing package includes a $120.5 million construction loan from Merchants Bank and an $18 million revolving loan fund from the New York State Homes and Community Renewal’s Housing Acceleration Fund (HAF) program. Developed by BRP Cos., the project will feature two six-story buildings with a mix of market-rate and affordable units, along with retail space, a parking garage, and a public park.
