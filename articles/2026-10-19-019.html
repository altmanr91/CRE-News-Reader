Little Havana Apartment Owners Land $50.8M Refi

REFINANCE

Connect CRE — Mon, 19 Oct 2026 14:03:24 +0000

SUMMARY

STRE Management and Lloyd Jones secured a $50.8 million refinancing for The Vibe, a 194-unit luxury multifamily community in Miami's Little Havana. The five-year, fixed-rate CMBS loan was arranged by JLL's Capital Markets Debt Advisory team. The property benefits from Florida's Live Local Act, which offers a significant property tax exemption for qualifying affordable units.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: The Vibe

Address: 700 SW 1st St, Miami, FL*

Units: 194

Loan Amount: $50.8M

Loan Per Unit: $261,856/unit (calculated)

Occupancy at Close: 96.4%

Year Built: 2021

Notable Features: Two eight-story buildings with private balconies or terraces, sundeck with pool, and fitness center. Includes 7,625 square feet of fully leased commercial space.

Project Notes: Refinancing structured as a five-year, fixed-rate CMBS loan. Property benefits from The Live Local Act (Florida SB 102) providing a 75% ad valorem property tax exemption on units restricted to residents earning 80-120% of area median income.
