Pappas Properties Breaks Ground on Publix-Anchored Retail Center in Metro Charlotte

CONSTRUCTION

REBusinessOnline — Tue, 06 Oct 2026 15:31:57 +0000

SUMMARY

Pappas Properties has commenced construction on a new Publix-anchored retail center within the 84-acre Cadia Village Matthews mixed-use development in metro Charlotte. The approximately 50,000-square-foot grocery store, which will include a drive-thru pharmacy, is slated for a fourth-quarter 2027 opening. The broader retail village will also feature medical offices, a bank, various dining options, and neighborhood services, complementing the residential component being developed by David Weekley Homes.
