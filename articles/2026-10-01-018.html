Investor Duo Score $68.3M Refi on Charlotte Office Pair

REFINANCE

Connect CRE — Thu, 01 Oct 2026 13:40:26 +0000

SUMMARY

Childress Klein Properties and Ascentris Real Estate secured a $68.3 million refinancing package for two Class A office buildings, The Nexus and Hub at Waverly, located within the larger Waverly mixed-use development in Charlotte. The five-year permanent loan was arranged by CBRE and provided by a life insurance company. The properties, completed in 2017 and 2019, are key components of the 90-acre mixed-use project.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Property Name: The Nexus and Hub at Waverly

Address: 6325 and 6210 Ardrey Kell Road, Charlotte, NC

Size: 312,738 SF

Loan Amount: $68.3M

Completion: 2017 and 2019

Notable Features: Part of the 90-acre Waverly mixed-use development, which includes retail, restaurants, a hotel, apartments, and single-family homes.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

SPONSOR: Childress Klein Properties

SPONSOR: Ascentris Real Estate

MORTGAGE BROKER: CBRE — Nate Sittema; Will Chapman

LENDER: Life Insurance Company
