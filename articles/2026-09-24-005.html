Nomic AI Inks 5K-SF Deal at 99 University Place Near Union Square

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Wed, 23 Sep 2026 20:21:06 +0000

SUMMARY

Four tenants have signed new office leases totaling 15,593 square feet at 99 University Place, a 10-story office building near Union Square in Manhattan. Design software firm Nomic AI, therapy space provider Therapeutic Alliance Suites, and construction systems developer T Moriarty & Son each secured full-floor leases of 4,890 square feet. The building's appeal stems from its prime Greenwich Village location, authentic character, high ceilings, natural light, and manageable full-floor layouts, which are increasingly sought after in a market with limited supply.
