Newmark Arranges $76.2M in Bridge Financing for New Orlando Multifamily Community

LOAN

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 16 Oct 2026 14:48:24 +0000

SUMMARY

Newmark has arranged $76.2 million in construction take-out bridge financing for Citron at The Packing District, a 354-unit multifamily community in Orlando. The financing was secured on behalf of EMBREY, the landlord of the property. The community, delivered in 2025, is part of the larger $700 million Packing District mixed-use redevelopment.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Citron at The Packing District

Units: 354

Loan Amount: $76.2M

Loan Per Unit: $215,254/unit (calculated)

Completion: 2025

Notable Features: Amenities include a top-floor deck with a media room, resort-style swimming pool with lounge and poolside cabanas, a Wi-Fi lounge with a conference room and micro-offices, and an adjoining six-story structured parking garage.

Project Notes: The multifamily community is situated within the 202-acre, $700 million Packing District mixed-use redevelopment.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

MORTGAGE BROKER: Newmark — Matthew Williams; Rob Wright; David Williams; Kyle Schlitt; James Maynard

SPONSOR: EMBREY

DEVELOPER/SPONSOR: Dr. Phillips Charities
