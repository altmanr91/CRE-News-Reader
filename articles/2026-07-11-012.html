Four Mile Capital purchases 212-unit apartment community in Louisville

ACQUISITION

REJournals — Fri, 10 Jul 2026 15:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Four Mile Capital, with joint-venture equity partner Midloch Investment Partners, has acquired Frontgate Apartments, a 212-unit garden-style multifamily community in Louisville, KY, for $38.25 million. The transaction involved the assumption of an existing HUD mortgage. This acquisition marks Four Mile Capital's second apartment property in the Louisville market, and Midloch Investment Partners' first investment in the area.
