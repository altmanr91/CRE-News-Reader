Habitat lists One333 apartment high-rise near The 78 amid rent surge

The Real Deal — Chicago — Wed, 22 Jul 2026 21:15:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Property Listing / For Sale

Habitat is marketing its 305-unit One333 apartment high-rise in Chicago's South Loop for sale, nine years after acquiring it for $125 million. The property is being positioned to capitalize on its proximity to The 78 megadevelopment, which will include a new MLS stadium, and a projected surge in rents due to a slowdown in the area's multifamily construction pipeline. The building is 96% occupied with average rents of $2,799 per month, and its sale reflects a broader trend of downtown landlords cashing out amid rising prices.
