Lincoln Property, SVP Complete Redevelopment of Playa Vista Creative Campus

Connect CRE — Thu, 20 Aug 2026 21:57:30 +0000

SUMMARY

Development

Lincoln Property Company and Strategic Value Partners, LLC, have completed the redevelopment of The Bluffs at Playa Vista, a 500,000-square-foot Class A office campus. The repositioning focused on creating a hospitality-inspired environment with enhanced shared spaces and indoor-outdoor connectivity, aligning with Playa Vista's innovation-driven identity. The project has already seen significant leasing activity, including two new leases totaling over 57,000 square feet, demonstrating strong market fundamentals for the reimagined campus.
