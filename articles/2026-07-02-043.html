Alexandria plots life sciences-to-housing pivot in Seattle’s Lower Queen Anne

The Real Deal — Seattle — Wed, 01 Jul 2026 15:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Property Listing / For Sale

Alexandria Real Estate Equities is planning to redevelop a bioscience property in Seattle's Lower Queen Anne neighborhood into a 254-unit apartment building. The proposed eight-story project will span 274,500 square feet, with 174,000 square feet dedicated to residential space. Concurrently, Alexandria is marketing the 1.3-acre site for sale, aligning with its strategy to divest assets and diversify beyond life sciences real estate.
