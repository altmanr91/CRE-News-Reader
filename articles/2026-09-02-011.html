Partners Capital closes sale of 115,512-square-foot retail center in Austin

SALE

REJournals — Wed, 02 Sep 2026 13:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Partners Capital has completed the sale of Oak Hill Plaza, a 115,512-square-foot retail center located in Austin, Texas. The property, situated at a prominent intersection in Southwest Austin, was originally acquired by Partners Capital in August 2021. This disposition represents the third property sale from Partners Capital's Opportunity Fund IV investment vehicle.

DATA POINTS

Address: 7101 State Hwy 71, Austin, TX

Notable Features: Located at the intersection of US Highway 290 West and Texas State Highway 71, a heavily traveled retail corridor known as the "Oak Hill Y."

Project Notes: Partners Capital originally acquired the property in August 2021.
