More than 100,000 square feet of new, renewed leases at Chicago’s The Wrigley Building

LEASE

REJournals — Thu, 01 Oct 2026 14:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

The Wrigley Building in Chicago has secured over 100,000 square feet in new and renewed leases, demonstrating strong leasing momentum for the iconic property. Global design firm Perkins&Will renewed its substantial lease through 2038, while materials manufacturer Oil-Dri Corporation of America committed to a new 15-year lease for newly designed space. These transactions, alongside other recent long-term commitments, highlight the building's appeal, which is further enhanced by ongoing investments in modernization and tenant amenities by the owner.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Property Name: The Wrigley Building

Address: 400-410 N. Michigan Avenue, Chicago, IL

Size: 100,000 SF

Notable Features: Historic landmark located at the intersection of the Chicago River, Michigan Avenue, and the Magnificent Mile, offering move-in-ready furnished suites and a comprehensive amenity package.

Project Notes: Owner Mansueto Office Inc. is making substantial investments including a multi-million-dollar elevator modernization program and planned enhancements to tenant amenities.
