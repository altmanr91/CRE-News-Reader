Fengate Asset Management, Mavrek Development celebrate groundbreaking of 25-story residential development in Chicago’s West Loop

CONSTRUCTION

REJournals — Fri, 18 Sep 2026 14:24:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Fengate Asset Management and Mavrek Development have broken ground on Julep West Loop, a 25-story mixed-use residential development in Chicago's West Loop. The project will feature 380 residential units, including 76 affordable units, and over 21,500 square feet of commercial space, anchored by an international grocery store. This development addresses strong housing demand and limited new residential supply in the rapidly growing West Loop neighborhood, offering residents convenient access to major employment centers and amenities.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: mixed-use

Property Name: Julep West Loop

Address: 1016 W. Jackson Blvd., Chicago, IL

Size: 21,500 SF

Units: 380

Completion: early 2028

Notable Features: Includes 76 affordable units, rooftop pool with cabanas, pickleball court, half-court basketball court, dog run, EV charging stations, and targets National Green Building Standard (NGBS) certification.

Project Notes: Construction is expected to generate approximately 740,000 union construction labor hours and $69 million in income for Chicago's union construction workforce between 2026 and 2028. The investment is supported by an anchor commitment from the LiUNA Pension Fund of Central and Eastern Canada.
