Trump, Mamdani Advance Sunnyside Yards Housing Discussion

Commercial Observer — Tue, 22 Sep 2026 13:56:54 +0000

SUMMARY

Development

President Donald Trump and New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani have agreed to continue discussions regarding a long-stalled plan to develop 12,000 affordable housing units over Sunnyside Yards in Queens. The leaders met to advance the project, which requires significant federal approvals and potential federal funding, and will establish a joint team to explore avenues for the development. The proposed project, which includes parks and schools, aims to have half of its housing units funded by New York State's Mitchell-Lama affordable housing program.
