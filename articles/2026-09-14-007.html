Endeavor Breaks Ground on 198,000 SF Office, Retail Project Near Downtown Austin

CONSTRUCTION

REBusinessOnline — Mon, 14 Sep 2026 14:46:35 +0000

SUMMARY

Endeavor Real Estate Group has commenced construction on 5th & Walsh, a new mixed-use development near downtown Austin. The project will feature 198,000 square feet of office and retail space, with Endeavor planning to relocate its headquarters to 25,000 square feet within the building. Shell construction is anticipated to conclude in late 2028.
