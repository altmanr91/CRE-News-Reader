Kelly Slater’s Austin Surf Club faces $16M in liens as it looks to restart construction

The Real Deal — Texas — Wed, 01 Jul 2026 12:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Development

The Austin Surf Club, a private artificial surf park and condo project, is facing over $16 million in construction liens, leading to a halt in work. The development, backed by notable figures in surfing and real estate, is seeking to establish a Municipal Management District to generate property tax revenue and fund infrastructure improvements. The project, planned for 178 private residences and a large surf basin, is located on the former NLand Surf Park site.
