Prime Finance buys Jonathan Holtzman’s East Loop apartments for $59M, ending legal fight

ACQUISITION

The Real Deal — Chicago — Tue, 21 Jul 2026 11:30:00 +0000

SUMMARY

An affiliate of Prime Finance acquired the 190-unit MDA Apartments in Chicago's East Loop for $59 million, resolving a two-year foreclosure lawsuit against the previous owner, City Club Apartments. The transaction effectively dismissed a $69 million debt owed on a $66 million mortgage, with the lender accepting a discount to take ownership of the property. The building had faced operational issues, including broken boilers and an elevator, leading to a court-appointed receiver stabilizing the asset prior to the sale.
