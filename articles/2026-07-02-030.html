Zillow kicks off antitrust showdown against Chicago MLS and Compass

The Real Deal — Chicago — Thu, 02 Jul 2026 00:21:46 +0000

SUMMARY

CRE Legal / Regulatory

Zillow is engaged in a federal antitrust showdown against Midwest Real Estate Data (MRED), a Chicago-based multiple listing service, and brokerage giant Compass. The dispute centers on MRED's decision to temporarily cut off Zillow's listing feed, which Zillow alleges was a collusive effort by MRED and Compass to restrict market access and harm consumers. Zillow's Listing Access Standards, which prohibit listings previously marketed on private networks from appearing on its platform, are at the heart of the conflict, with MRED arguing these standards violate its objective criteria for listing display. The preliminary injunction hearing is examining whether MRED and Compass are unfairly limiting competition by restricting Zillow's access to crucial listing data.
