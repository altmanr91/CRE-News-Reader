Firms tied to Argentine soccer money probe bought four Miami-Dade properties

The Real Deal — Miami — Thu, 16 Jul 2026 19:43:29 +0000

SUMMARY

Non-CRE / Political & Crime News

An FBI investigation into unusual financial transactions involving the Argentine Football Association (AFA) has revealed that a South Florida couple, Javier Faroni and Erica Gillette, purchased four properties in Miami-Dade County totaling $11 million. The couple's company, TourProdEnter, which served as the AFA's commercial agent, routed millions of dollars in AFA revenue to other Miami-Dade firms, some of which are believed to be shell companies. The property purchases, made between 2022 and 2025 through LLCs controlled by Faroni and Gillette, appear to have been all-cash transactions, raising questions about the beneficial owners and the source of funds.
