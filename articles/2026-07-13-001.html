John Roesch Exits Meridian to Start Roesch Real Estate Group

Commercial Observer — Mon, 13 Jul 2026 12:00:43 +0000

SUMMARY

Feature / Profile

John Roesch, a veteran retail leasing broker, has departed Meridian Retail Leasing after nearly a decade to establish his own firm, Roesch Real Estate Group (RREG). The new company will initially concentrate on retail leasing, with plans to expand into investment sales, aiming to bring institutional-level execution to the middle market. Roesch emphasized that his departure was driven by a readiness to lead his own venture, building on the experience and confidence gained at Meridian.
