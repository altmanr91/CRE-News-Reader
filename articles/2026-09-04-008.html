Summit RE Negotiates Sale of 14,895 SF Retail Strip Center in Midlothian, Texas

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 04 Sep 2026 12:31:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Summit RE has facilitated the sale of Harvest Hill Town Center II, a 14,895-square-foot retail strip center in Midlothian, Texas. The property features a strong tenant mix, including national brands. The seller and buyer, along with the transaction price, were not disclosed.
