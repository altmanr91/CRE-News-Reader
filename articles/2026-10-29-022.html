SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

The upcoming 2026 midterm elections in Georgia have the potential to significantly alter the state's energy oversight board, which could in turn affect the costs and rates for data centers. The outcome of this race is particularly important given the nearly 20 GW pipeline of data center development in the state, suggesting that changes in energy policy could have a substantial impact on the industry's growth and operational expenses.
