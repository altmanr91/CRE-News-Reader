Controlled Turbulence Or Chaos? CBRE's Spencer Levy Checks In On 2026

Bisnow News Feed — Mon, 20 Jul 2026 13:28:32 -0400

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

CBRE Global Client Strategist Spencer Levy believes the commercial real estate industry is largely on track with his earlier prediction of "controlled turbulence" for 2026, despite a Bisnow survey showing industry leaders are divided on market sentiment. Levy notes that while macro noise persists, particularly post-pandemic, the real estate sector has adapted to interest rate changes. This adaptation includes strategic shifts by major investors, who are reallocating capital to core-plus or value-add assets due to challenges in raising funds for core properties where returns are comparable to bonds but with higher risk. Levy also dismisses concerns about an impending "wall of maturities," stating that no reckoning is coming.
