Seattle Social Housing Developer to Buy West Seattle Apartments for $68M

ACQUISITION

Connect CRE — Mon, 02 Nov 2026 18:20:20 +0000

SUMMARY

The Seattle Social Housing Developer (SSHD) is set to acquire Youngstown Flats, a nearly 200-unit apartment building in West Seattle, for approximately $68 million. This marks SSHD's second acquisition, expanding its portfolio to almost 350 units and surpassing its 2026 housing pipeline goal. The acquisition is significant as it aims to re-establish permanent affordability for about 30 residents, whose previous Multifamily Tax Exemption program had lapsed under the prior owner, thereby protecting them from potential economic displacement.
