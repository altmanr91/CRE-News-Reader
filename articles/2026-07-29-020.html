Clarion Partners Acquires Grocery-Anchored Fairfield County Retail

ACQUISITION

Connect CRE — Tue, 28 Jul 2026 20:50:04 +0000

SUMMARY

Clarion Partners Real Estate Income Fund Inc., advised by Franklin Templeton and Clarion Partners, has acquired Main Avenue Shopping Plaza, a 156,568-square-foot grocery-anchored community shopping center in Norwalk, CT. The fully leased property, anchored by a major grocery chain and other national retailers, was sold by a joint venture between Sagehall Partners and The Prusik Group. This acquisition aligns with Clarion's strategy to invest in well-located, grocery-anchored retail centers that offer stable cash flow and long-term value, benefiting from strong occupancy and a strategic location in an affluent market.
