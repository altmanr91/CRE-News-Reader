New York Investor Buys Palm Beach County Nursing Home for At Least $24M

ACQUISITION

Commercial Observer — Tue, 13 Oct 2026 21:35:42 +0000

SUMMARY

Tordjman Equities has acquired the Pine Trail Nursing and Rehab Center in Greenacres, FL, for at least $23.8 million. The 59,760-square-foot facility, built in 1988, features 93 beds and sits on an 8.7-acre property. The transaction was financed with a $150 million acquisition loan from Pinnacle Bank, which also backed another recent senior housing acquisition in Florida.
