Timber Equities Receives $27.5M Loan for Refinancing of Manhattan Apartment Building

REFINANCE

REBusinessOnline — Mon, 24 Aug 2026 15:24:59 +0000

SUMMARY

Timber Equities has secured a $27.5 million refinancing loan for The Sophia, a recently completed 60-unit apartment building in Manhattan's Inwood neighborhood. The 11-story property, which also includes 1,700 square feet of retail space, was completed in fall 2025. The financing was arranged through LaSalle Investment Management.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: The Sophia

Units: 60

Loan Amount: $27.5M

Year Built: 2025

Notable Features: Amenities include a fitness center, resident lounge, package room, and a rooftop deck.
