CMK grabs key permit to launch 28-story South Loop high-rise

CONSTRUCTION

The Real Deal — Chicago — Mon, 20 Jul 2026 20:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

CMK Companies has secured a key permit for its 28-story mixed-use high-rise at 910 South Wells Street in Chicago's South Loop, allowing foundation work to begin. The development, known as Riverline Parcel F Building B, will feature 350 residential units and 3,300 square feet of retail space. This project is part of CMK's larger Riverline vision, an eight-acre portion of a previously larger master plan.

DATA POINTS
