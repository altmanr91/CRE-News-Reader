Astoria Favorite :3Coffee Roasters Expands to Halletts Point

LEASE

Connect CRE — Fri, 14 Aug 2026 21:22:37 +0000

SUMMARY

The Durst Organization has secured a 1,232-square-foot retail lease with :3Coffee Roasters at 30 Halletts Point in Astoria, Queens. This new location marks the coffee roaster's second outpost in Astoria and will be the first retail tenant for the recently opened 647-unit residential buildings at 20 and 30 Halletts Point. The Durst Organization expressed enthusiasm for welcoming a retailer that adds value to the growing Halletts Point community, which offers impressive views of Manhattan and Queens.
