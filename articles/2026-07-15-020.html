Defense Department signs 10-year lease for Long Island office

LEASE

The Real Deal — New York — Tue, 14 Jul 2026 16:30:00 +0000

SUMMARY

The government agency formerly known as the Department of Defense has signed a 10-year lease for 23,600 square feet at 1 Old Country Road in Carle Place, NY. The five-story office building, owned by DBD Realty Group, recently underwent a $22 million renovation. This significant lease contributes to the property's strong demand from institutional and government tenants, which value its scale, infrastructure, and accessibility.
