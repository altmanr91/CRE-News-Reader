Joseph Kavanagh Co. Completes Sale of HQ, Manufacturing Facility

SALE

Connect CRE — Tue, 07 Jul 2026 20:37:49 +0000

SUMMARY

Joseph Kavanagh Co. has sold its 19,000-square-foot corporate headquarters and manufacturing facility in Dundalk, Maryland, to Titan Steel Corporation for $2.3 million. The property, which includes office, industrial, and warehouse space, is situated on a 2.27-acre site with excellent access to major transportation routes.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Address: 8100 Lynhurst Road, Dundalk, MD

Size: 19,000 SF

Sale Price: $2.3M

$/SF: $121/SF (calculated)

$/Acre: $1,013,216/acre (calculated)

$/Land SF: $23.26/land SF (calculated)

Site Area: 2.27 acres

Notable Features: Features 14- to 16.5-foot ceilings, two drive-in loading docks, approximately 2,500 square feet of office space, and a fenced outdoor storage yard. The site fronts Interstate 696 and is located near Interstate 95 and the Dundalk Marine Terminal.
