NRP Group Breaks Ground on 348-Unit Affordable Housing Project in South Austin

CONSTRUCTION

REBusinessOnline — Tue, 07 Jul 2026 15:35:50 +0000

SUMMARY

The NRP Group, in partnership with the Housing Authority of Travis County, has broken ground on Sanara, a 348-unit affordable housing project in South Austin. The development will feature 13 three-story residential buildings with units restricted to renters earning 60 percent or less of the area median income. MetLife Investment Management is the tax credit investor, and PIMCO provided construction and permanent financing for the project.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Sanara

Units: 348

Completion: Spring 2027

Site Area: 56 acres

Notable Features: Units will be rent restricted to renters earning 60 percent or less of the area median income; amenities include a fitness center, business center, clubhouse, outdoor pool, children's activity center, and playground.

Project Notes: Residents will have access to services such as afterschool care, financial literacy courses, ESL classes, and first-time homebuyer programs.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

DEVELOPER/SPONSOR: The NRP Group

DEVELOPER/SPONSOR: Housing Authority of Travis County

EQUITY/FINANCING: MetLife Investment Management

LENDER: PIMCO

MORTGAGE BROKER: Berkadia
