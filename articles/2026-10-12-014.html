DART Seeking Ideas for Southern Dallas Station Redevelopment

Connect CRE — Mon, 12 Oct 2026 17:04:34 +0000

SUMMARY

Development

Dallas Area Rapid Transit (DART) is seeking proposals for the redevelopment of over 18 acres at its Westmoreland Station in Southern Dallas. The city is currently rezoning the site to permit mixed-use development, which is anticipated to include housing, retail, and office components. This initiative is part of DART's broader strategy to leverage its station properties for transit-oriented development.

SPONSOR PIPELINE

Palladium Buckner Station: 300-unit multifamily community with green space and direct access to bus and rail service.

SMU/Mockingbird Station: Multiphase mixed-use destination, including a 394-unit apartment community.

Trinity Mills Station: First phase of a 25-acre development opened in 2025.

Addison Junction: Mixed-use development near a new Silver Line station with housing, retail, restaurants, office space, and a hotel, with groundbreaking planned for 2027.
