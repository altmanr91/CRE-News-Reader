Blackstone Sells Fort Lauderdale Industrial Campus for $57M

SALE

Commercial Observer — Mon, 05 Oct 2026 17:50:24 +0000

SUMMARY

Blackstone's industrial division, Link Logistics, has sold a seven-building industrial campus in Fort Lauderdale, FL, for $57 million. The 210,000-square-foot property was acquired by a joint venture between BKM Capital Partners and Kayne Anderson Real Estate. The campus, which is 97% leased, is strategically located near I-95 and the Dania Pointe retail complex, offering a strong rent roll with staggered lease expirations.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Address: 2201, 2301 and 2381 Stirling Road, Fort Lauderdale, FL

Size: 210,000 SF

Sale Price: $57.0M

Occupancy at Sale: 97.0%

Year Built: 1985

Notable Features: Seven small-bay buildings located less than a mile west of Interstate 95, near the Dania Pointe retail complex.

Project Notes: The campus last traded for $17.5 million in 2013. The weighted average lease term is 3.9 years.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: BKM Capital Partners

BUYER: Kayne Anderson Real Estate

SELLER: Link Logistics
