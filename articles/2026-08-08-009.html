Marcus & Millichap Brokers Sale of 12,000 SF Industrial Building in Midland, Texas

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 07 Aug 2026 15:22:55 +0000

SUMMARY

Marcus & Millichap has brokered the sale of a 12,000-square-foot industrial building in Midland, Texas. The single-tenant property, built in 2019 on 6.6 acres, was fully leased at the time of sale. The undisclosed buyer acquired the asset through a 1031 exchange.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Size: 12,000 SF

Occupancy at Sale: 100.0%

Year Built: 2019

Site Area: 6.6 acres

Project Notes: Buyer acquired the building via a 1031 exchange.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

SELLER BROKER: Marcus & Millichap — Davis Cagle; Chase Beasley; Adam Abushagur

TENANTS AT PROPERTY

Coastal Production Systems
