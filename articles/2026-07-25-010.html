IPA Arranges $15.4M in Financing for Dallas Industrial Property

LOAN

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 24 Jul 2026 15:05:31 +0000

SUMMARY

Institutional Property Advisors (IPA) has secured $15.4 million in financing for a 60,159-square-foot industrial property in Dallas. The five-year loan, provided by an undisclosed local credit union, represents a 75 percent loan-to-value ratio for the asset, which is fully leased to a commercial kitchen operator.
