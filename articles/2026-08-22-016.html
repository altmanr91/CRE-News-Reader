Zimmet Law Group Signs 10-Year Lease for 515 Madison Ave.

LEASE

Connect CRE — Fri, 21 Aug 2026 21:47:50 +0000

SUMMARY

Zimmet Law Group P.C. has signed a 10-year lease for 5,476 square feet of office space at 515 Madison Avenue in Manhattan's Plaza District. The law firm is relocating its Manhattan office from 477 Madison Avenue and is expected to take occupancy later this year after the completion of its new space. The landlord noted that professional services firms continue to seek high-quality, well-located office space, underscoring the appeal of the building and the Plaza District.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Address: 515 Madison Ave, New York City, NY

Size: 5,476 SF

Project Notes: Zimmet Law Group will occupy Suite 603 on the sixth floor.
