Bradford Commercial brokers sale of headquarters property in Fort Worth market

SALE

REJournals — Thu, 29 Oct 2026 13:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Bradford Commercial Real Estate Services facilitated the sale of Sunair Products Inc.'s former 22,030-square-foot office/warehouse headquarters in Haltom City, TX, to Firstex Industries Inc. The property, located at 2329 Solona St., will allow Firstex Industries to significantly expand its operations. This transaction marks the third deal this year for Sunair, which is relocating to a larger facility.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office/warehouse

Address: 2329 Solona St, Haltom City, TX

Size: 22,030 SF

Site Area: 1.03 acres

Notable Features: Features two floors of offices and a conference center (8,500 SF), 13,530 SF of single-story warehouse space with loading docks, and a fenced quarter-acre of outside storage with an electric gate. Accessible from two streets and close to TX 121 and Loop 820.

Project Notes: This is Sunair Products Inc.'s original headquarters, which they are vacating for a larger facility.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

SELLER: Sunair Products Inc.

BUYER: Firstex Industries Inc.

SELLER BROKER: Bradford Commercial Real Estate Services — Michael Clardy

BUYER BROKER: The Mercer Co. — Nick Fulton

BUYER: Solona Street LLC
