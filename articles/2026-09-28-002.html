Solidcore Signs Lease for Fitness Studio at 1695 Second Avenue on Upper East Side

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Mon, 28 Sep 2026 16:41:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Solidcore, a national strength training brand, has signed a retail lease for the entire commercial component of The Langdon, a new 23-story residential building on Manhattan's Upper East Side. The fitness studio will occupy 2,507 square feet at 1695 Second Avenue, providing a valuable amenity for residents and bringing a premium fitness offering to the neighborhood. The landlord's broker noted that the tenant's loyal following and distinctive fitness experience will contribute positively to the property and the surrounding area.
