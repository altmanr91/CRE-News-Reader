Fresno Apartments Fetch $44M in First-Ever Sale

SALE

Connect CRE — Tue, 21 Jul 2026 20:12:22 +0000

SUMMARY

The Mogharebi Group represented JBT Property Management in the sale of Maroa Park Apartments, a 248-unit multifamily community in Fresno, to a private buyer for $44 million. This transaction marks the first time the property has been on the market since its construction in 1986, offering the buyer a rare opportunity to acquire a well-maintained, large-scale asset directly from its original owner. The acquisition is expected to enhance the buyer's regional presence and improve operational efficiency without requiring significant capital investment, capitalizing on the property's immediate income upside and strong market fundamentals in the Central Valley.
