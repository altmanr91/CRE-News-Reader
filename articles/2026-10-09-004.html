Westwood Financial Pays $56M for Huntington Beach, Calif., Shopping Center

ACQUISITION

Commercial Observer — Thu, 08 Oct 2026 20:15:49 +0000

SUMMARY

Westwood Financial has acquired Peninsula Marketplace, a 95,416-square-foot grocery-anchored shopping center in Huntington Beach, CA, for approximately $56.2 million. The property, which was sold by Blackstone, is 98.8 percent occupied and anchored by a supermarket chain that has been a tenant for over 25 years. This acquisition represents Westwood Financial's largest in Southern California in more than a decade, highlighting strong investor demand for well-located, daily-needs retail assets in markets with limited new supply and affluent demographics.
