CRE Continues Holding Up as Occupiers and Investors are More Selective

Connect CRE — Fri, 14 Aug 2026 05:01:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

Despite persistent inflation, stable interest rates, trade uncertainty, and a softening labor market in the first half of 2026, the commercial real estate sector has demonstrated resilience. A recent CBRE report indicates strong occupier demand, increased investment activity, and improving fundamentals across property types. This performance is attributed to limited new supply, a redefined concept of 'quality' in real estate, and the anticipated impact of artificial intelligence on productivity and workplace dynamics. The outlook suggests continued optimism for the remainder of 2026, with returns driven by income growth and strategic asset selection rather than cap-rate compression.
