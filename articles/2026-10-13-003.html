Proof Stacking — Or, the Compounding Value of Credibility in Business

Commercial Observer — Tue, 13 Oct 2026 13:48:44 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Editorial

This article, authored by a veteran commercial real estate broker, introduces the concept of "proof stacking" as a method for building credibility in business. The author argues that in an increasingly digital world where expertise is easily claimed, true credibility is established not through singular achievements or assertions, but through the consistent accumulation of credible evidence over time. This includes a documented track record, demonstrated market knowledge, professional conduct, and a history of keeping commitments, all of which collectively make trust a logical conclusion for clients. The author draws on his experience at Massey Knakal to illustrate how systematic efforts, like quarterly market newsletters, contributed to this compounding credibility, emphasizing that reputation is built through behavior and thousands of interactions, not just accomplishments.
