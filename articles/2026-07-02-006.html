Northmarq Secures $13.7M Bridge Loan for Innerbelt Business Center in Metro St. Louis

LOAN

REBusinessOnline — Wed, 01 Jul 2026 13:49:46 +0000

SUMMARY

Northmarq has secured a $13.7 million bridge loan for Fountain Real Estate Capital to finance the acquisition of Innerbelt Business Center, a 191,925-square-foot flex/showroom property in Overland, MO. The five-year loan includes three years of interest-only payments and future funding. The borrower plans to stabilize the property and increase rents to market rates as existing leases expire.
