Making affordable housing more affordable to build

REJournals — Thu, 06 Aug 2026 09:50:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

This article argues that early collaboration and strategic planning during the preconstruction phase are crucial for making affordable housing developments more cost-effective and efficient. The author, drawing on experience as a general contractor, emphasizes that engaging the construction team early allows for better constructability evaluations, more efficient system recommendations, and proactive identification of logistical challenges, ultimately reducing costs and risks. Strong partnerships among owners, architects, contractors, and other stakeholders, along with thoughtful procurement strategies, are also highlighted as key factors in successfully delivering affordable housing projects within tight budgets and schedules.
