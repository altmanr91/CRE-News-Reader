BHI Lends $72M on New Jersey Apartments Build

LOAN

Commercial Observer — Thu, 24 Sep 2026 18:51:07 +0000

SUMMARY

A joint venture between Prestige Development and Anak Development secured a $71.5 million construction loan from BHI for The Standard, a 250-unit multifamily project in Bayonne, NJ. The development aims to address the increasing demand for housing in the area and is strategically located near public transit. The project is expected to be completed in summer 2027.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: The Standard

Address: 197 Avenue East, Bayonne, NJ

Units: 250

Loan Amount: $71.5M

Loan Per Unit: $286,000/unit (calculated)

Completion: summer 2027

Notable Features: Located steps from 22nd Street Station on the Hudson-Bergen Light Rail line, with ground-floor retail, a parking garage, and amenities including a swimming pool, fitness center, coworking areas, and a golf simulator.

Project Notes: Situated at the former site of the Villanova Catering Hall.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

DEVELOPER/SPONSOR: Prestige Development — Michael Shahkohi, principal

DEVELOPER/SPONSOR: Anak Development

LENDER: BHI — David Kesselman, vice president of commercial real estate

MORTGAGE BROKER: Maven Capital — Sean Shahkohi

ARCHITECT: MHS Architecture
