Urban Story Ventures, Hill Partners to Develop $350M Mixed-Use District at The Bend in Chattanooga

DEVELOPMENT

REBusinessOnline — Tue, 25 Aug 2026 14:18:48 +0000

SUMMARY

Urban Story Ventures and Hill Partners are collaborating on a new $350 million mixed-use retail and residential district within The Bend, a 120-acre redevelopment in downtown Chattanooga. The project, designed with an open-air layout, will feature a mix of restaurants, shops, service retailers, offices, and residences. Construction is slated to begin next year, with completion anticipated by fall 2028, contingent on preleasing activity.
