Morgan celebrates opening of 345-unit apartment community in North Austin

DEVELOPMENT

REJournals — Thu, 15 Oct 2026 13:51:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Morgan has announced the opening of The Watson, a new 345-unit apartment community in North Austin, TX. The seven-story, podium-style property offers studio, one-, two-, and three-bedroom homes with high-end finishes and a wide array of amenities, including two pools, co-working spaces, and multiple sky terraces. The development is strategically located in North Austin's tech corridor, near The Domain and future public transit, with approximately 45 units designated for households earning up to 60% of the Area Median Income.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: The Watson

Address: 11901 Burnet Road, Austin, TX

Units: 345

Completion: Fall 2026

Notable Features: Designed for NGBS Green Silver certification and pursuing Austin Energy Green Building 1-Star certification, with 10-foot ceilings, quartz countertops, and private sky terraces in select units. Features a skyline plunge pool overlooking The Domain and a sunset roof terrace.

Project Notes: Approximately 45 residences are reserved for households earning up to 60% of the Austin Area Median Income. Includes 522 parking spaces with EV charging stations and Wi-Fi connectivity.
