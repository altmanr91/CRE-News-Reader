JLL Arranges Recapitalization of Downtown Pittsburgh Mixed-Use Property

REFINANCE

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 07 Aug 2026 15:17:27 +0000

SUMMARY

JLL has arranged the recapitalization of Tower Two-Sixty, a mixed-use property in downtown Pittsburgh, valued at $69 million. The transaction involved a ground-lease bifurcation sale of the land to Woodbranch Investments Corp. and leasehold financing provided by Dollar Bank. The property, completed in 2016, includes office, retail, a hotel, and a parking garage.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: mixed-use

Property Name: Tower Two-Sixty

Address: Pittsburgh Plate Glass Bldg, 260 Forbes Ave, Pittsburgh, PA*

Size: 144,000 SF

Keys: 197

Sale Price: $69.0M

Year Built: 2016

Notable Features: Includes 130,000 square feet of office, 14,000 square feet of retail, a 197-key Hilton-branded hotel, and a 321-space parking garage.
