Closing Window: Austin’s office recovery is real, but tenants still hold the leverage

REJournals — Mon, 03 Aug 2026 10:02:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

Austin's office market is experiencing a genuine recovery, marked by four consecutive quarters of positive absorption and a two-point reduction in vacancy over the past year, now at 24%. Despite this, tenants continue to hold significant leverage, with landlords offering substantial concessions like free rent and tenant improvement allowances to secure deals, often to protect face rates that influence valuations and loan covenants. The recovery is supported by a diversifying tenant base, including advanced manufacturing, defense, aerospace, and life sciences, moving beyond traditional tech dependency. While hybrid work remains common, companies are increasingly planning for full capacity, driven by investments in high-quality office environments designed to attract employees back. Demand is concentrated in areas like The Domain, CBD, and East Austin, with tenants willing to pay a premium for walkable amenities, while some are exploring well-located Class B spaces due to cost pressures.
