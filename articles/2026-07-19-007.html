“There is no ceiling”: Welcome to Area AI

The Real Deal — San Francisco — Sat, 18 Jul 2026 12:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

The article describes how the artificial intelligence industry has revitalized San Francisco's real estate market, particularly in the southeastern district known as Mission Bay and its adjacent neighborhoods like Potrero Hill. The influx of major AI companies and high-earning employees has led to a surge in demand for both commercial and residential properties, driving up prices and rents significantly. Mission Bay, once characterized by warehouses, has transformed into a bustling hub with near-zero office vacancy rates, while Potrero Hill has become a sought-after residential area for AI professionals and 'hacker houses,' experiencing substantial home price appreciation and intense bidding wars.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Mission Bay office vacancy rates are nearly out of room. The median price for a condo in Mission Bay rose 13.2 percent over the last year to $1.2 million. In Potrero Hill, the median price for a single-family home rose 16 percent over the last year, reaching more than $2 million, with homes selling for 42 percent above asking on average.
