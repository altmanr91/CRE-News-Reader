Tyler Herro lists Pinecrest estate after trade to Milwaukee Bucks

The Real Deal — Miami — Mon, 06 Jul 2026 20:30:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Celebrity Residential / Non-CRE

NBA player Tyler Herro has listed his Pinecrest estate for nearly $12 million following his trade to the Milwaukee Bucks. Herro purchased the 9,500-square-foot residence in late 2022 for $10.5 million, financing it with a $7.4 million mortgage. The property features eight bedrooms, eight and a half bathrooms, and sits on a nearly 1-acre lot.
