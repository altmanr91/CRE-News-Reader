Texas Icon Mehrdad Moayedi: Developing communities with long-term civic responsibility

REJournals — Mon, 06 Jul 2026 10:02:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Feature / Profile

This article profiles Mehrdad Moayedi, President and CEO of Centurion American Development Group, highlighting his four-decade career in real estate development across North Texas and Colorado. Moayedi's approach emphasizes long-term community building, civic responsibility, and collaboration with municipalities to shape growth and redevelop key urban sites. His work is characterized by a commitment to philanthropy and a personal drive to give back to the community, stemming from his own immigrant experience.
