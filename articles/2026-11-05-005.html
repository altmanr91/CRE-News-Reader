The Co-op Comeback: New York Buyers Are Reconsidering the Condo Trade-Off

Commercial Observer — Thu, 05 Nov 2026 12:00:54 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

New York City's residential market is seeing a resurgence in co-op appeal as buyers reconsider the long-standing preference for condominiums. The increasing cost of new condo developments, coupled with the significant price advantage of co-ops, is driving this shift. Some co-op boards are also adapting by streamlining application processes and easing subletting restrictions, addressing historical concerns that made co-ops less attractive. This trend is particularly noticeable in the luxury market, where Manhattan co-ops recently surpassed condos in sales for the first time since 2022, indicating a broader market re-evaluation of the co-op trade-off.
