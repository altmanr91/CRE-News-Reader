Fort Worth’s Trademark Property Company closes financing on four development projects across Texas

LOAN

REJournals — Wed, 07 Oct 2026 14:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Trademark Property Company has closed financing on four development and redevelopment projects across Texas, totaling over 830,000 square feet of retail space with a combined value of $417 million. These projects include the redevelopment of Lincoln Square into Anthem in Arlington, the construction of Shivers Farm in Southlake, and Dunham Pointe in Cypress, along with the acquisition of Oak Hill Plaza in Austin. The activity reflects a positive shift in the retail real estate market, characterized by low national vacancy rates and investor interest in new retail development, particularly grocery-anchored centers.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

National retail vacancy held at 4.4% in Q2 2026, well below the 7.4% historical average. New retail construction remains scarce nationally. Dallas, Houston, and Austin account for 21% of the current national construction pipeline. In JLL’s June 2026 U.S. retail report, 48% of investors stated they were interested in investing in new retail development in 2026. Malls are outperforming all commercial property sectors with values up 13%.
