Partnership Nears Completion of 35,000 SF Retail Redevelopment Project in Houston

DEVELOPMENT

REBusinessOnline — Thu, 30 Jul 2026 14:43:07 +0000

SUMMARY

A partnership between MetroNational and Radom Capital is nearing completion of Greenside, a 35,000-square-foot retail redevelopment project in Houston's Memorial City district. The project involves the adaptive reuse of three warehouse buildings and incorporates open green space. The partnership has already secured leases with five new tenants, with full completion anticipated later this year.
