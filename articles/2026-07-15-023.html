PTM Partners, Peacock launch subsidy-free workforce housing platform

DEVELOPMENT

The Real Deal — Miami — Tue, 14 Jul 2026 23:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

PTM Partners and Peacock Capital have launched Inception Housing, a new platform focused on developing workforce housing in Florida without government subsidies. The platform aims to reduce development costs by managing design, construction, and material procurement in-house, utilizing standardized cold-formed steel construction to achieve significant savings in both cost and construction time. This approach allows Inception Housing to offer lower-cost rental units to middle-income earners, addressing the critical housing shortage in high-growth Florida markets.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Total Project Cost: $300.0M

Project Notes: Inception Housing has nearly 1,600 units in its development pipeline.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

DEVELOPER/SPONSOR: PTM Partners — Michael Tillman, CEO

DEVELOPER/SPONSOR: Peacock Capital

SPONSOR PIPELINE

Magnolia Flats: 74 units across two buildings on vacant land, in addition to 14 existing townhomes, targeting workers near the airport and businesses in South Orlando.

Spring Lake: 168 apartments across six buildings in Winter Haven.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

South Florida has a housing shortage of at least 30,460 units, while the statewide shortage in Florida is approximately 120,850 units.
