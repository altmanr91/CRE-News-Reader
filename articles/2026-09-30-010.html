Sands Investment Group closes sale of travel centers in Tennessee, Texas

SALE

REJournals — Wed, 30 Sep 2026 15:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Sands Investment Group brokered the sale of two travel centers in Tennessee and Texas for a total of $36.7 million. The transaction included both the business operations and the underlying real estate, delivered free of fuel contracts. The properties are high-volume centers, and the deal highlights the brokerage team's ability to execute complex sales.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: retail

Sale Price: $36.7M

Notable Features: Properties delivered free and clear of fuel contracts.

Project Notes: Transaction included the sale of both the business and the underlying real estate.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

SELLER BROKER: Sands Investment Group — Yossi Freeman; Matt Montagne; Tyler Ellinger
