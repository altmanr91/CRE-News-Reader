First Pioneer Properties Buys Queens Retail Center for $24M

ACQUISITION

Commercial Observer — Fri, 14 Aug 2026 16:42:16 +0000

SUMMARY

First Pioneer Properties, in partnership with ABS Partners Real Estate, has acquired a 24,000-square-foot retail center in Bayside, Queens, from Levy Properties for $23.5 million. The one-story property, which includes significant air rights, currently houses an urgent care facility and a baby store. The buyers' plans for the site are not yet clear, but the acquisition comes amidst a wave of multifamily development activity in Queens.
