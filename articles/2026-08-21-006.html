Core Spaces Breaks Ground on 2,201-Bed Student Housing Project Near Texas A&M University

CONSTRUCTION

REBusinessOnline — Thu, 20 Aug 2026 15:29:58 +0000

SUMMARY

Core Spaces has commenced construction on Hub College Station, a substantial student housing development near Texas A&M University. The project will feature two seven-story buildings offering 2,201 beds across 570 units, with completion anticipated for the 2028-2029 academic year. Amenities will include a rooftop pool, a 14,000-square-foot wellness center, and various study and social spaces. Pacific Life provided the construction financing for this significant development.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: student housing

Property Name: Hub College Station

Address: 170 Century Square Dr, College Station, TX*

Units: 570

Beds: 2,201

Completion: 2028-2029 academic year

Notable Features: Rooftop pool and hot tub; 14,000-square-foot wellness center with indoor/outdoor training tracks, indoor half-court basketball, yoga studios, putt-putt, golf simulator, and spa; study lounges with private and collaboration rooms; indoor and outdoor social spaces.
