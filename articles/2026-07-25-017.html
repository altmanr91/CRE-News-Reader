1031 Exchange Solutions Provider Joins Marcus & Millichap’s Preferred Partner Program

Connect CRE — Fri, 24 Jul 2026 21:08:27 +0000

SUMMARY

Non-CRE / Business News

Marcus & Millichap has added Deferred, a technology-powered qualified intermediary for 1031 exchanges, to its Preferred Partner Program. This collaboration aims to offer Marcus & Millichap's clients streamlined access to qualified intermediary services and tech-enabled 1031 exchange solutions. The partnership is particularly relevant given current market conditions, interest rates, and tax considerations, as it helps commercial real estate owners maximize after-tax proceeds and maintain purchasing power by leveraging 1031 exchanges as an effective tool for equity preservation and capital repositioning.
