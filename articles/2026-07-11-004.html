Siemens Financial, First Horizon Lend $65M on Palm Beach Gardens Medical Complex

LOAN

Commercial Observer — Fri, 10 Jul 2026 16:35:24 +0000

SUMMARY

Sina Companies secured $64.8 million in construction financing from Siemens Financial and First Horizon for the Health Park at Avenir project in Palm Beach Gardens, FL. The development will feature a two-story neighborhood hospital and a three-story medical outpatient building, fully pre-leased to Jupiter Medical Center. This project aims to expand access to high-quality healthcare within the Avenir master-planned community.
