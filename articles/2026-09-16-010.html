Basis Industrial Receives $24M Construction Loan for Self-Storage Project in Wayne, New Jersey

LOAN

REBusinessOnline — Wed, 16 Sep 2026 13:34:43 +0000

SUMMARY

Basis Industrial has secured a $24 million construction loan for a new self-storage facility in Wayne, New Jersey. The project, which will be operated by ExtraSpace Storage, is currently under construction and is expected to be completed in early 2028.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: self-storage

Address: 555 Hamburg Turnpike, Wayne, NJ

Units: 586

Loan Amount: $24.0M

Loan Per Unit: $40,956/unit (calculated)

Completion: early 2028

Notable Features: Climate-controlled units.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

SPONSOR: Basis Industrial

LENDER: NexBank

LENDER: NexPoint

OWNER: ExtraSpace Storage
