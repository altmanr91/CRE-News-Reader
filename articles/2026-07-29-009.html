Gilbane Development Breaks Ground on 756-Bed Student Housing Project in Norman, Oklahoma

CONSTRUCTION

REBusinessOnline — Tue, 28 Jul 2026 14:31:55 +0000

SUMMARY

Gilbane Development has commenced construction on a 756-bed student housing community in Norman, Oklahoma, near the University of Oklahoma campus. The project, a partnership with Coleraine Capital Group, will feature 298 units on a 33-acre site and is slated for completion by fall 2028. Santander Bank and Fifth Third Bank are providing the construction financing.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: student housing

Units: 298

Beds: 756

Completion: fall 2028

Site Area: 33 acres

Notable Features: Located less than a mile from the University of Oklahoma campus, with a landscaped open-space amenity area and onsite parking.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

DEVELOPER/SPONSOR: Gilbane Development

DEVELOPER/SPONSOR: Coleraine Capital Group

LENDER: Santander Bank

LENDER: Fifth Third Bank
