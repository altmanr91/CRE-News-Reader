Cortland Sells West Palm Rental at Discount for $208M

SALE

Commercial Observer — Fri, 28 Aug 2026 17:30:09 +0000

SUMMARY

Fairfield Residential acquired the 812-unit Portofino Place Apartments in West Palm Beach, FL, from Cortland for $208 million. The transaction, which closed through two deeds, represents a discount for the seller compared to its 2021 purchase price. The buyer assumed two Freddie Mac-backed loans totaling over $178 million as part of the financing.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Portofino Place Apartments

Address: 4400 and 4600 Portofino Way, West Palm Beach, FL

Units: 812

Sale Price: $208.0M

Year Built: 2003

Site Area: 40 acres

Project Notes: The property was previously acquired by Cortland for $229 million in 2021. The sale occurred through two deeds: one for $108 million covering 416 units built in 2003, and another for $100 million covering 396 units built in 2006.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: Fairfield Residential

SELLER: Cortland

LENDER: Freddie Mac
