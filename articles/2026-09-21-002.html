Commercial Real Estate Investment’s Curious Rise Amid Higher Inflation, Rates

Commercial Observer — Mon, 21 Sep 2026 11:48:31 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

Despite persistent inflation and rising interest rates, commercial real estate investment activity has shown surprising resilience, with significant increases in investment sales volume in the first half of 2026. This counterintuitive trend suggests that the industry is adapting to a new economic reality where instability has become the norm. One expert posits that the relevance of minor interest rate adjustments has diminished for sophisticated investors, who are instead prioritizing asset value, potential for appreciation, and exit strategies. The abundance of global capital, including a "mountain of available capital" in private equity, is also driving continued investment as funds seek deployment opportunities beyond traditional low-yield options.
