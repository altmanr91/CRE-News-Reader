Marcus & Millichap brokers $7.1 million sale of industrial property in Albert Lea

SALE

REJournals — Thu, 20 Aug 2026 15:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

A single-tenant industrial property in Albert Lea, Minnesota, occupied by Green Bay Packaging, has been sold for $7.1 million. Marcus & Millichap facilitated the transaction, representing the seller and procuring the buyer, St. Paul Properties. Acquisition financing of $4.798 million was also arranged by Marcus & Millichap Capital Corporation.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Address: 1851 Margaretha Ave, Albert Lea, MN

Size: 127,869 SF

Sale Price: $7.1M

$/SF: $56/SF (calculated)

Year Built: 1952

Site Area: 10.5 acres

Notable Features: Located at the intersection of Interstates 35 and 90, offering potential for future tenant expansion.

Project Notes: The property has more than seven years remaining on its initial lease term, with extension options through 2041.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: St. Paul Properties

SELLER: Minnesota-based partnership

SELLER BROKER: Marcus & Millichap — Don Schmidt; Paul Rider; Jon Ruzicka

LENDER: National Bank

TENANTS AT PROPERTY

Green Bay Packaging

FINANCING

Acquisition financing of $4,798,000 was secured with a 5-year term, 5.3% interest rate, and 25-year amortization period. The loan is non-recourse.
