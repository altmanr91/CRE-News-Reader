Faena Residences developers presell $30M penthouse for record price along Miami River

SALE

The Real Deal — Miami — Thu, 16 Jul 2026 23:25:24 +0000

SUMMARY

A joint venture between KAR Properties and Fortune International Group has presold a penthouse unit at their Faena Residences luxury condominium development along the Miami River for $30 million. This presale sets a new record for the area in terms of both total price and price per square foot. The duplex penthouse, located in the east tower, will feature extensive interior and outdoor living spaces, including a private rooftop pool, wellness area, and entertainment spaces.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: condo

Property Name: Faena Residences

Address: 90 Southwest Third Street, Miami, FL

Size: 8,200 SF

Sale Price: $30.0M

$/SF: $3,659/SF (calculated)

Notable Features: Duplex unit with 7,000 square feet of outdoor space, private rooftop with a pool, wellness space, and entertainment areas. The development will include a 45,000-square-foot community center.

Project Notes: The 440-unit project is planned for a site where KAR Properties previously intended to develop One River Point. The development will consist of two towers connected by a three-story sky bridge.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

DEVELOPER/SPONSOR: KAR Properties

DEVELOPER/SPONSOR: Fortune International Group

ARCHITECT: Rafael Viñoly Architects
