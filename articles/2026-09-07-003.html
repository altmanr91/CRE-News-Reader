This article provides a summary of various commercial real estate news items, focusing on potential legislative impacts in New York, emerging financing options for affordable housing, and significant transactions across different asset classes. The upcoming New York State elections are highlighted as a potential catalyst for changes in retail rent control, rent-stabilized apartment regulations, and data center development moratoriums, though they may also foster affordable housing initiatives. The piece also covers the growing use of tax-exempt CMBS debt for affordable housing and the implications of the new 21st Century ROAD to Housing Act. Additionally, it details several major leases in New York City's retail and office sectors, a large student housing portfolio acquisition, and significant development and financing activities in South Florida and Washington, D.C.
