HarbourVest Partners Signs 19,752 SF Office Lease in Midtown Manhattan

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 16 Oct 2026 13:50:05 +0000

SUMMARY

HarbourVest Partners, a Boston-based private equity firm, has secured a 10-year office lease for 19,752 square feet on the 49th floor of 9 West 57th Street in Midtown Manhattan. This transaction reflects continued demand for prime office space in the city.
