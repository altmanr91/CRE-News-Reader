Publix Building $200M Expansion at Lakeland Logistics Campus

CONSTRUCTION

Connect CRE — Mon, 26 Oct 2026 15:11:39 +0000

SUMMARY

Publix Super Markets is undertaking a significant $200 million multi-phase expansion of its main distribution and logistics campus in West Lakeland, Florida. This investment will add a 400,000-square-foot warehouse distribution center and a 58,000-square-foot return center, solidifying Lakeland's role as the central supply chain hub for the grocer's extensive network across the Southeastern U.S.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Property Name: Lakeland Logistics Campus

Size: 458,000 SF

Total Project Cost: $200.0M

$/SF: $437/SF (calculated)

Project Notes: The 2.6-acre site was previously acquired in late 2023 for $8.25 million. The existing campus already spans over 1.1 million square feet.
