Tannos secures $28M in construction financing for Houston area mixed-use project

LOAN

The Real Deal — Texas — Mon, 20 Jul 2026 19:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Tannos Development Group has secured $28 million in mid-construction financing for The Albritton, a mixed-use development located in Friendswood, TX. The project, which will feature multifamily units, ground-floor retail, and office space, is expected to be completed in 2028. Concord Summit Capital arranged the debt, provided by S3 Capital, to carry the project through the remainder of its construction.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: mixed-use

Property Name: The Albritton

Address: 408 South Friendswood Drive, Friendswood, TX

Units: 111

Loan Amount: $28.0M

Loan Per Unit: $252,252/unit (calculated)

Completion: 2028

Notable Features: The project will include 111 multifamily units, 11,000 square feet of ground-floor retail, and 9,000 square feet of office space.

Project Notes: The debt has a 70 percent loan-to-value ratio.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

SPONSOR: Tannos Development Group

LENDER: S3 Capital

MORTGAGE BROKER: Concord Summit Capital — Daniel J. Eidson; Ben Applebaum

SPONSOR PIPELINE

Friendswood City Center: A $750 million mixed-use district on 106 acres at FM 528 and Blackhawk Boulevard, developed in partnership with Wolfgramm Capital. Construction started in spring 2024.

Magnolia Town Center: A $1 billion, 200-acre project in Magnolia, developed in partnership with Maritia. It will feature a luxury hotel, 250,000 square feet of retail, 200,000 square feet of medical and office space, 350 multifamily units, a senior living facility, and single-family homes. Tannos is donating 32 acres to the city for walking trails and two lakes.
