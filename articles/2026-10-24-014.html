Skechers Headquarters in Manhattan Beach Listed for Sale

Connect CRE — Fri, 23 Oct 2026 21:38:42 +0000

SUMMARY

Property Listing / For Sale

The Skechers corporate headquarters, a nearly 30,000-square-foot Class A office building at 228 Manhattan Beach Blvd. in Manhattan Beach, CA, has been listed for sale. The three-story property, which was extensively renovated in 2018 and includes a 69-stall subterranean parking garage, is being offered through a competitive bidding process and will be delivered vacant. This represents a rare opportunity to acquire a high-quality, immediately occupiable office asset in a desirable coastal market.
