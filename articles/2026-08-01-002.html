Izo Capital Lends $26M on Wyoming Apartments Near Jackson Hole

LOAN

Commercial Observer — Fri, 31 Jul 2026 17:55:09 +0000

SUMMARY

Sundance Properties secured $25.6 million in construction financing from Izo Capital for the completion of Phase 1 of its Flats at Alpine Junction multifamily development in Alpine, WY. The loan will fund the construction of 48 additional units, bringing the first phase to 64 units, with 16 units already completed and leased. The project is strategically located near Jackson Hole, addressing a critical need for attainable rental housing in a supply-constrained market with high home prices and strong demand drivers, including a new medical center and a charter school.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Flats at Alpine Junction

Address: 303 Highway, Alpine, WY

Units: 64

Loan Amount: $25.6M

Loan Per Unit: $400,000/unit (calculated)

Total Project (All Phases): 160 units

Notable Features: Located 35 miles from Jackson Hole mountain valley with direct access to the START Bus system connecting Alpine and Jackson. Benefits from local demand drivers including a newly opened medical center and a public charter school.

Project Notes: The loan is a 24-month senior construction loan to fund the completion of Phase 1, which includes three additional buildings comprising 48 apartments. The first phase is slated for completion in spring 2027.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

SPONSOR: Sundance Properties — Ken Cady, Principal

LENDER: Izo Capital — Peter Porraro, Co-founder and Chief Investment Officer; David Israel

MORTGAGE BROKER: CBRE — Peter J. Gineris

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Median home prices in Jackson Hole are close to $3 million, with townhomes starting at around $1 million, indicating a strong demand for more attainable rental housing. Izo Capital is on pace to close around $150 million of loans in 2026, largely focused on multifamily deals in supply-constrained markets, with a focus on stretch construction loans covering up to 80-85% of development costs combined with a preferred equity piece.
