JLL Capital Markets provides $76 million in financing for 291-unit multifamily development in West St. Paul

LOAN

REJournals — Tue, 08 Sep 2026 13:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

JLL Capital Markets has secured $76.25 million in financing for the development of Thompson Oaks, a 291-unit multifamily project in West St. Paul, Minnesota. The financing package includes a $63 million floating-rate loan from First International Bank & Trust and an additional $13.25 million from WhiteStar Advisors. This development, a public/private partnership with the City of West St. Paul, will feature a mix of apartment units and townhomes, along with commercial retail space and significant park improvements, marking the initial phase of a master redevelopment for the former YMCA site.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Thompson Oaks

Address: 150 Thompson Ave., West St. Paul, MN

Units: 291

Loan Amount: $76.2M

Phase I: initial phase of a master redevelopment

Notable Features: Five-story development with 19 townhome-style units, 342 garage parking stalls, and 7,100 square feet of commercial retail space. Amenities include an outdoor pool, golf simulator, wellness center, and work-from-home spaces.

Project Notes: Public/private partnership with the City of West St. Paul, which is providing Tax Increment Financing. The project includes significant park improvements and connections to the regional trail system.
