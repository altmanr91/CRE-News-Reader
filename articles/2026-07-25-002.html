Waste Management Buys 146-Acre Miami-Dade Development Site for $380M

ACQUISITION

Commercial Observer — Fri, 24 Jul 2026 18:20:12 +0000

SUMMARY

Waste Management has acquired a 146-acre development site in Medley, Florida, for nearly $380 million, with plans to potentially develop another landfill. The transaction included significant seller financing from the Lowell Dunn Company, which also granted an easement for stormwater infrastructure. This acquisition is part of a trend of waste management companies expanding their footprint in Miami-Dade County.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Address: 9515 Northwest 95th Avenue, Medley, FL

Sale Price: $380.0M

$/Acre: $2,602,740/acre (calculated)

$/Land SF: $59.75/land SF (calculated)

Site Area: 146 acres

Notable Features: Site is parallel to Route 27 near Everglades National Park and adjacent to Waste Management's existing Medley Landfill complex.

Project Notes: Seller financing of $366.8 million was provided, maturing in 2046. An easement was granted for underground stormwater pipes across a neighboring property.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: Waste Management

SELLER: Lowell Dunn Company

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Waste Connections recently paid $51 million for a 12-acre surface trucking parking lot in Miami Gardens, FL.
