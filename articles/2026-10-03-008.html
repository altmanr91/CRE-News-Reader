Draper and Kramer brokers $27.5 million in loans for industrial complex and multifamily property in Chicago area

LOAN

REJournals — Fri, 02 Oct 2026 15:58:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Draper and Kramer's Commercial Finance Group recently brokered two loans totaling $27.5 million for properties in the Chicago area. One loan, for $13.5 million, financed the acquisition of a three-building industrial complex in Bridgeview. The second transaction was a $14 million bridge loan for a newly constructed 56-unit multifamily property in Chicago's Edgewater neighborhood.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

MORTGAGE BROKER: Draper and Kramer, Incorporated — Dan Freedman, Senior Vice President; Chris Beck, Senior Vice President

SPONSOR: Curtis Ashton Partners

SPONSOR: Burnham Industrial Partners

LENDER: Correspondent Life Insurance Company
