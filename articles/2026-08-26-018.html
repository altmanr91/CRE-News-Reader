Hanover Company Launches Construction on Its First SoCal Industrial

CONSTRUCTION

Connect CRE — Tue, 25 Aug 2026 21:52:16 +0000

SUMMARY

Hanover Company, in partnership with a real estate fund advised by Crow Holdings Capital, has commenced construction on its inaugural industrial development in Southern California. The project, located in Simi Valley, will deliver approximately 200,000 square feet of industrial space designed to accommodate various users, including corporate headquarters, packaging and assembly, or distribution. This marks Hanover Industrial's first venture into the Southern California market, establishing a presence in the dynamic Simi Valley area.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Address: 903 Quimisa Dr, Simi Valley, CA

Size: 200,000 SF

Site Area: 13 acres

Notable Features: Features 36’ clear heights, an 8.5” slab, skylights, and ample employee parking, with immediate proximity to CA 118.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

DEVELOPER/SPONSOR: Hanover Company — Anthony Feenick, Development Partner

EQUITY/FINANCING: Crow Holdings Capital
