National Property Holdings Unveils Plans for 1.1 MSF Industrial Park in Katy, Texas

DEVELOPMENT

REBusinessOnline — Fri, 24 Jul 2026 15:16:29 +0000

SUMMARY

National Property Holdings has announced plans for Katy Prairie Business Park, a two-phase industrial development totaling approximately 1.1 million square feet in Katy, Texas. The park will feature two buildings designed for manufacturing and logistics, offering clear heights of 36 and 40 feet, ESFR sprinkler systems, and flexible configurations. CBRE has been appointed as the leasing agent for the project.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Property Name: Katy Prairie Business Park

Size: 1,083,420 SF

Phase I: 402,480 square feet

Notable Features: 36- and 40-foot clear heights, ESFR sprinkler systems, onsite trailer parking, flexible configurations.

Project Notes: Developed in two phases, each comprising a single building.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

DEVELOPER/SPONSOR: National Property Holdings

LEASING AGENT: CBRE
