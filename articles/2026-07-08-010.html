Newmark Arranges $515M Refinancing of Midtown Manhattan Office Building

REFINANCE

REBusinessOnline — Tue, 07 Jul 2026 15:08:16 +0000

SUMMARY

Newmark has arranged a substantial $515 million refinancing package for 31 West 52nd Street, a prominent 785,000-square-foot office building in Midtown Manhattan. The debt, secured by local investment firm Rithm Capital, comprises a $415 million senior mortgage, a $40 million B-note, and a $60 million mezzanine loan. A consortium of major financial institutions, led by Wells Fargo, provided the financing.
