Systima Closes $153M Tax-Exempt CMBS Affordable Housing Deal

LOAN

Commercial Observer — Thu, 23 Jul 2026 21:54:42 +0000

SUMMARY

Systima Capital Management closed a $153 million tax-exempt CMBS deal for a portfolio of 1,272 affordable housing units across five states. The transaction, which was significantly oversubscribed by institutional investors, highlights the growing potential for CMBS to finance affordable housing projects. J.P. Morgan served as the lead underwriter, with Wells Fargo as co-manager, demonstrating strong market confidence in the credit protections and demand for affordable housing investments.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Units: 1,272

Loan Amount: $153.0M

Notable Features: Portfolio of properties subsidized by the federal Low-Income Housing Tax Credit (LIHTC) program, geared toward families earning at or below 60 percent of area median income.

Project Notes: The original loan proceeds were used to fund acquisition, construction, rehabilitation, and preservation of the affordable housing communities. Systima retained the subordinate Class B certificates.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

SPONSOR: Systima Capital Management — Ryan Paszczykowski, Head of Structured Investing

LENDER: J.P. Morgan — Jason Kahn, Executive Director

LENDER: Wells Fargo

FINANCING

Tax-exempt CMBS debt through the Public Finance Authority securitized by a pool of loans on seven properties. Class A-1 certificates rated A-minus and Class A-2 certificates rated BBB-plus by S&P Global Ratings.
