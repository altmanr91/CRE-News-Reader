Browning brings its Velocity formula to Columbus

CONSTRUCTION

REJournals — Fri, 14 Aug 2026 13:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Browning, an Indianapolis-based developer, has broken ground on Velocity Hill Road, a 200,200-square-foot speculative industrial building in Canal Winchester, Ohio, marking its first ground-up development in the Columbus market. The project is part of Browning's Velocity industrial park brand and is strategically sized to meet demand for smaller industrial spaces, a niche identified by the developer in a market that has predominantly seen larger facility construction. The company undertook extensive research to ensure Columbus's logistics infrastructure, population growth, and manufacturing investment align with its development strategy, with the building expected to be completed by the end of 2026.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Property Name: Velocity Hill Road

Size: 200,200 SF

Site Area: 16.33 acres

Notable Features: Targeting LEED Silver certification, features 32-foot clear height, 10 dock doors, two drive-in doors, ESFR sprinklers, LED lighting, 2,000-amp electrical service, and parking for 200 vehicles. Located along the U.S. 33 corridor with quick highway access and walking distance to community amenities.

Project Notes: Part of Browning's Velocity industrial park brand. The building can be divided into as many as four spaces, with additional land for trailer parking or outdoor storage.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

DEVELOPER/SPONSOR: Browning — Mark Susemichel, Chief Development Officer and Principal; Jon Rolwing, Senior Vice President of Development

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Approximately 7.1 million square feet of speculative industrial space is under construction across the Columbus market, with an overall industrial vacancy rate of 5.6%. In the Southeast U.S. 33 Corridor, where Velocity Hill Road is located, there is no other speculative industrial product under construction and vacancy was 4.3%. Last year, the Columbus market recorded substantial absorption, including four 1-million-square-foot buildings being leased.
