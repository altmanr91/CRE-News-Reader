Foundry Commercial Underway on 13-Acre IOS Facility in Hutchins, Texas

CONSTRUCTION

REBusinessOnline — Thu, 06 Aug 2026 14:51:21 +0000

SUMMARY

Foundry Commercial is developing Wintergreen Gardens, a 13-acre industrial outdoor storage (IOS) facility in Hutchins, Texas. The project, which began construction earlier this summer, will feature 9 acres of concrete-paved outdoor storage and two industrial buildings totaling 25,000 square feet, with delivery expected in the first quarter of 2027.
