The Boulder Group closes sale of Chipotle property in Lansing

SALE

REJournals — Fri, 03 Jul 2026 15:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

The Boulder Group recently facilitated the sale of a single-tenant Chipotle property in Lansing, Illinois, for $2.45 million. The newly constructed 2,465-square-foot building, developed in 2024, was acquired by a California-based family trust as a long-term passive investment. The property features an absolute triple net lease with approximately 13 years remaining, offering built-in rental escalations and no landlord responsibilities.
