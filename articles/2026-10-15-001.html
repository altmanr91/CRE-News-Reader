Your Family Lives There. So Why Is New York Imposing a Pied-à-Terre Tax?

Commercial Observer — Thu, 15 Oct 2026 13:03:25 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Editorial

This opinion piece argues that New York City's pied-à-terre tax is being inadvertently applied to primary residents due to common real estate ownership structures like trusts and LLCs. The author contends that the tax, intended for absentee owners of high-value residential property, is unfairly burdening families who have a member living in the apartment full-time. The article highlights how the tax rules fail to 'look through' these entities to recognize the primary resident, leading to unintended tax liabilities and potential disputes among beneficiaries. The author urges the New York City Department of Finance to clarify its guidance to align with the law's original intent and suggests interim strategies for owners to restructure their holdings to qualify for exemptions.
