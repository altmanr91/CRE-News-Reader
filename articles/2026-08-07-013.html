Indianapolis’ Premier Commercial Real Estate Services adds associate broker

REJournals — Thu, 06 Aug 2026 16:35:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Promotion

Premier Commercial Real Estate Services, an Indianapolis-based firm, has expanded its brokerage division by appointing Andrew Klink as an Associate Broker. This move is part of the firm's strategy to cultivate new talent and enhance its service capabilities across Indiana and surrounding regions. Klink, a former intern, will gain experience in various commercial real estate services, including landlord and tenant representation, investment sales, and site selection, under the mentorship of senior brokers.
