Seven Equity buys former WeWork building in Tenderloin out of receivership

ACQUISITION

The Real Deal — San Francisco — Mon, 13 Jul 2026 19:44:32 +0000

SUMMARY

Seven Equity Group, a New York-based investor, has acquired a 50,000-square-foot office building at 25 Taylor Street in San Francisco's Tenderloin district for nearly $6.8 million. The property, formerly occupied by WeWork, was purchased out of receivership after years of financial distress, including a loan default and special servicing. This acquisition continues Seven Equity's strategy of buying distressed office assets in San Francisco at significant discounts, reflecting a bet on the market's recovery.

DATA POINTS

Project Notes: The property's $18.5 million loan fell into default in 2021 and transferred to special servicing in 2023 with an outstanding balance of $16.9 million. A court appointed a receiver in 2023, leading to the sale. WeWork previously occupied 42,565 square feet under a lease for nearly a decade before vacating in 2021.
