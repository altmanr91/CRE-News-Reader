Spanish Hotel Operator Buys Chemists’ Club Hotel at 52 East 41st Street for $95M

ACQUISITION

Commercial Observer — Fri, 31 Jul 2026 18:49:55 +0000

SUMMARY

Eurostars Hotel Company, a Spanish hotel chain, has acquired the 107-key Chemists' Club Hotel at 52 East 41st Street in Midtown Manhattan for approximately $95 million from Azora. The transaction marks a return of the property to its previous ownership roots, as Eurostars is a subsidiary of Grupo Hotusa, which previously owned the hotel. The historic 14-story building, originally constructed around 1910 as the headquarters for the Chemists' Club, was redeveloped into a hotel in 1997 and recently underwent a significant redesign and rebranding.

Notable Features: The 14-story building was originally the headquarters of the Chemists' Club and features a ground-floor steakhouse, lounge bar, coworking spaces, and a fitness center.

Project Notes: The property was previously acquired in 2007 for $77 million by Grupo Hotusa and Losan Hotels. It was redeveloped in 1997 and opened as the Dylan Hotel in 2000, then heavily redesigned and rebranded as the Chemists' Club Hotel last year.
