DWS Group REIT Misses Redemption Requests By More Than 30%

Bisnow News Feed — Thu, 02 Jul 2026 11:45:15 -0400

SUMMARY

Market Research / REIT Performance

DWS Group's nontraded REIT, RREEF Property Trust, failed to meet 32.4% of shareholder redemption requests in June, fulfilling only 67.6% of the cash sought. This shortfall was due to the REIT hitting its quarterly redemption cap of 5% of net asset value, a recurring issue for the fund over the past two years. Despite a $15 million capital injection from DWS in February, the relatively small fund, with $337 million in assets as of March, continues to face challenges with investor withdrawals. The diversified REIT, which owns eight properties across various asset classes, reported a $425,000 net loss in the first quarter.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Nontraded REITs faced significant redemption requests post-pandemic, with major funds like Blackstone Real Estate Income Trust capping withdrawals in 2022 before clearing its backlog by March 2024. Starwood Capital Group's nontraded REIT, however, fully halted redemptions in April amid a strategic review. By October, nontraded REITs had largely closed the gap on redemption requests, returning $56 billion to investors, with less than 2% of requests outstanding, primarily from Starwood.
