Thor Equities Closes on $218M Manhattan Office Buy

ACQUISITION

Connect CRE — Thu, 05 Nov 2026 15:37:10 +0000

SUMMARY

Thor Equities Group has acquired 1359 Broadway, a 22-story, 486,000-square-foot office building in Manhattan's Garment District, from Empire State Realty Trust for $217.5 million. This acquisition reinforces Thor's confidence in the long-term strength of the Manhattan market and adds a significant asset to its portfolio, building on the property's established position in Midtown. Newmark will handle the exclusive leasing for the property.
