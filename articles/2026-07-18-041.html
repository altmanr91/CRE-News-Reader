Brookfield's Csquare Misses IPO Target As Data Center Cost Skepticism Rises

Bisnow News Feed — Fri, 17 Jul 2026 12:05:10 -0400

SUMMARY

Market Research / Capital Markets

Csquare, a data center operator backed by Brookfield Asset Management, fell short of its initial public offering target, raising $1.01 billion after commissions, compared to its goal of $1.35 billion. The company, which owns 63 North American data centers and one in London, began trading on the New York Stock Exchange. The proceeds are intended to pay down debts, including a significant revolving credit facility and promissory notes to Brookfield. This IPO performance, alongside other recent AI infrastructure IPOs, suggests a potential cooling of investor enthusiasm for data center and AI-related infrastructure.
