SVN|Chicago Commercial closes sale of 6,000-square-foot retail property in Chicago’s North Center neighborhood

SALE

REJournals — Tue, 18 Aug 2026 17:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

SVN | Chicago Commercial recently facilitated the sale of a 6,000-square-foot retail property in Chicago's North Center neighborhood for $1,507,500. The two-story asset garnered significant interest, receiving multiple offers and ultimately selling for 8% above its asking price, with the transaction closing in under a month.
