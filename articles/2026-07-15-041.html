Slatt Capital Arranges Life Company Loan for Rohnert Park Offices

LOAN

Connect CRE — Tue, 14 Jul 2026 20:13:02 +0000

SUMMARY

Slatt Capital secured a $12 million non-recourse acquisition loan for a 68,094-square-foot multi-tenant office building in Rohnert Park, CA. The financing, provided by The Standard, features a staggered term and a 30-year amortization schedule, offering the buyer long-term permanent debt for the acquisition. This transaction highlights the lender's confidence in the stabilized asset's location, occupancy, and sponsorship, particularly in a challenging office lending market where non-recourse terms are increasingly selective.
