Hyundai Transys to Invest $560M for Factory Expansion in West Point, Georgia

CONSTRUCTION

REBusinessOnline — Wed, 14 Oct 2026 13:53:32 +0000

SUMMARY

Hyundai Transys Georgia Powertrain, an automotive parts manufacturer, is investing $560 million to expand its manufacturing operations in West Point, GA. The project involves upgrading two existing buildings and constructing a new third facility to increase production capacity for electrified powertrains for hybrid vehicles. This expansion is expected to create 320 new jobs.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial

Address: 6801 Kia Parkway, West Point, GA

Total Project Cost: $560.0M

Notable Features: Expansion to support production of electrified powertrains for hybrid vehicles.

OWNER: Hyundai Transys Georgia Powertrain
