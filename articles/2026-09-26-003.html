Developers File Plans for 402-Unit Housing Project Along Astoria Waterfront

DEVELOPMENT

Commercial Observer — Fri, 25 Sep 2026 19:56:16 +0000

SUMMARY

Astoria developers Dominick Acquista and Lorenzo Devardo, operating as Across the River Realty Development, have filed plans with the New York City Planning Commission for a 20-story residential tower in Astoria, Queens. The project, located at 31-29 Vernon Boulevard, proposes 402 housing units, with approximately 100 designated as affordable under the city's Mandatory Inclusionary Housing program. The developers are seeking a zoning change and text amendment to accommodate the building's height and residential use, which would replace existing industrial structures.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Address: 31-29 Vernon Boulevard, Astoria, NY

Units: 402

Notable Features: Approximately 100 units will be affordable under Mandatory Inclusionary Housing requirements. The project also includes 10,108 square feet of commercial space and 3,069 square feet of community facility space.

Project Notes: The developers are seeking a zoning change and text amendment to allow for housing above current height restrictions.
