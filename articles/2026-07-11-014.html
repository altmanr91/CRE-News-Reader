Inland National Development Company closes sale of pair of Hilton hotels in Holland

SALE

REJournals — Fri, 10 Jul 2026 13:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

An affiliate of Inland National Development Company, LLC has brokered the sale of two Hilton-branded hotels in Holland, Michigan, for a combined price of $32 million. The transaction includes a Tru by Hilton and a Home2 Suites by Hilton, marking the firm's second and third hotel sales in recent years and highlighting its growth in the hospitality sector.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: hotel

Property Name: Tru by Hilton Holland and Home2 Suites by Hilton Holland

Keys: 207

Sale Price: $32.0M

Notable Features: Strategically located along US 31, providing convenient access to Holland State Park, Ottawa Beach, and Veldheer Tulip Gardens.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

SELLER: Inland National Development Company, LLC
