Harvest Properties buys 25-acre R&D campus in Palo Alto, weighs housing redevelopment

ACQUISITION

The Real Deal — San Francisco — Mon, 20 Jul 2026 23:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Harvest Properties has acquired a 24.5-acre research and development campus in Palo Alto for $163 million from Alexandria Real Estate Equities. The transaction reflects a significant price reduction from its previous sale in 2019, highlighting the downturn in the life sciences market. Harvest Properties is reportedly considering redeveloping approximately half of the property into housing, including apartments and townhomes, to address the strong demand for residential units in the area.
