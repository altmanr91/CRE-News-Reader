Brookfield, CPP Taking LXP Industrial Private in $5.2B Deal

ACQUISITION

Connect CRE — Mon, 20 Jul 2026 11:39:57 +0000

SUMMARY

Brookfield Asset Management and Canada Pension Plan Investment Board have agreed to acquire LXP Industrial Trust in an all-cash transaction valued at approximately $5.2 billion, including net debt and preferred equity. LXP Industrial Trust owns a significant portfolio of 108 warehouse and logistics facilities, totaling approximately 53 million square feet, primarily located in the Sunbelt and Midwest regions of the U.S. This acquisition represents the culmination of LXP's strategic repositioning to become a pure-play industrial REIT with a high-quality portfolio and development program, aligning with Brookfield's strategy of investing in real estate with durable cash flows and value creation opportunities.

DATA POINTS
