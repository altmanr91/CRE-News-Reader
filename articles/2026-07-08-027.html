“You get what you pay for”: Fort Lauderdale will pursue $217M city hall project

DEVELOPMENT

The Real Deal — Miami — Tue, 07 Jul 2026 20:34:43 +0000

SUMMARY

Fort Lauderdale commissioners have preliminarily approved a plan to develop a new $217.1 million city hall from the ground up, rather than retrofitting an existing building. The project, to be developed by FTL City Hall Partners LLC, will cost the city $15.8 million annually over 30 years. This decision follows a previous delay due to cost concerns and a subsequent reduction of approximately $50 million from the original design-build proposal, achieved by eliminating the developer's fee and equity stake. The city's former city hall was demolished after sustaining damage in a 2023 flood.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: government

Property Name: Fort Lauderdale City Hall

Address: 100 North Andrews Avenue, Fort Lauderdale, FL

Total Project Cost: $217.1M

Original Plan: 14-story, oval-shaped building with a sci-fi-reminiscent design for $268 million

Project Notes: The city will pay $15.8 million annually for 30 years, totaling $474 million. The original proposal was $268 million, with annual payments of $24.1 million, totaling $723 million over 30 years. The lower price was achieved by scrapping a $12 million developer fee and 10 percent equity. The former city hall was demolished after flood damage in 2023.
