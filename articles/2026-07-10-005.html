Heart to Heart Homecare Takes 13K SF for Office in South Bronx

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Thu, 09 Jul 2026 19:44:45 +0000

SUMMARY

Heart to Heart Homecare, a provider of home health and personal care services, has signed a 10-year lease for 12,500 square feet of office space in the South Bronx. The new office at 369 148th Street will expand the agency's presence in the borough, where it offers nonmedical assistance to seniors and individuals with disabilities. The asking rent for the space was $38 per square foot.
