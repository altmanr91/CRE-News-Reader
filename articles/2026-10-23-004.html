DEVELOPMENT

Commercial Observer — Thu, 22 Oct 2026 20:45:24 +0000

SUMMARY

Mast Capital is expanding its luxury residential development focus to West Palm Beach, Florida, with the Banyan Tree Residences, an 88-unit condominium project. The company's Chief Investment Officer, Jordan Kornberg, highlighted West Palm Beach's significant growth, walkability, and increasing investment as key drivers for their expansion. He emphasized the importance of understanding the local market dynamics and buyer preferences, noting that the luxury condo market in West Palm Beach is evolving with demand for high-quality, service-rich residential experiences. The project aims to offer a unique living environment with features like private corner units and large terraces, catering to both local downsizers and buyers from the Northeast and other domestic markets seeking second homes.
