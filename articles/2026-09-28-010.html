Connolly Unveils First Wave of Tenants at Publix-Anchored Shopping Center in Metro Atlanta

REBusinessOnline — Mon, 28 Sep 2026 14:12:10 +0000

SUMMARY

Development

Connolly has announced the initial tenants for Woodstock Mill District, a 118,000-square-foot shopping center currently under development in downtown Woodstock, Georgia. The project, which includes a new 11-acre development and an existing 28,000-square-foot retail center acquired in 2021, is now 79 percent leased. Tenants are expected to begin occupancy this fall, with a grand opening anticipated in early 2027.
