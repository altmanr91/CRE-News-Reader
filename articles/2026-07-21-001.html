Experiential Retail: What Works Now and What Doesn’t

Commercial Observer — Tue, 21 Jul 2026 11:00:34 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

The global experiential retail market, valued at $132 billion in 2025, is projected to more than quadruple by 2035 with a compound annual growth rate of 15.2 percent, driven by evolving consumer preferences among millennials and Gen Z for immersive and interactive shopping experiences. This shift moves beyond traditional transactional retail to focus on creating memorable, emotional connections and increasing dwell time and brand loyalty. Successful experiential retail development requires a fundamental change in approach, emphasizing storytelling, meticulous curation, and intentional design details to stand out in a competitive landscape where online shopping handles product exchanges. Developers must create destinations that offer layered and elevated experiences to make in-person visits worthwhile for consumers.
