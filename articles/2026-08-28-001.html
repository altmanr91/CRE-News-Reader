KPC Group’s Oceanwide Plaza Bet Started With a Supertall Dream for Manhattan

ACQUISITION

Commercial Observer — Fri, 28 Aug 2026 20:25:35 +0000

SUMMARY

KPC Group has acquired the unfinished Oceanwide Plaza mixed-use development in Downtown Los Angeles for $517 million in cash and credit, following a bankruptcy court approval. The firm plans to complete the 2 million-square-foot project, which includes hotel, residential, and retail components, with an estimated total cost of $850 million or more. The acquisition marks a significant step for KPC Group's founder, Dr. Kali Chaudhuri, in developing a trophy property in a major U.S. market, despite the project's history of stalled construction and financing challenges. The firm is currently focused on cleaning up extensive graffiti on the property and securing entitlements within a six-month deadline.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: mixed-use

Property Name: Oceanwide Plaza

Address: 1100 Flower St, Los Angeles, CA*

Size: 2,000,000 SF

Sale Price: $517.0M

Total Project Cost: $850.0M

$/SF: $425/SF (calculated)

Notable Features: Will feature a massive LED screen adjacent to Crypto.com Arena and L.A. Live, envisioned as the 'Times Square of Los Angeles'.

Project Notes: The project stalled approximately seven years ago and became known for its graffiti-covered exterior. KPC Group acquired the EB-5 loan for the project after it went into default, which later gave them priority to bid in the bankruptcy auction. The firm is keeping the original design to expedite entitlements.
