Tishman Speyer refinances two-tower The Franklin office building in Chicago’s West Loop

REFINANCE

REJournals — Thu, 03 Sep 2026 09:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Tishman Speyer has refinanced The Franklin, a two-tower office complex in Chicago's West Loop, with a $340 million CMBS loan. The financing, led by JPMorgan with Bank of America and Deutsche Bank as co-lenders, is a five-year floating rate loan designed to retire existing debt and support ongoing leasing initiatives. The property has seen significant leasing activity, securing 460,000 square feet in new leases and extensions since June 2025, bringing its occupancy to 84 percent.

Project Notes: The loan is a Single Asset Single Borrower (SASB) CMBS loan with a two-year initial term and three 12-month extension options.
