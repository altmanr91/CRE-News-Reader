Outpost Acquires Two IOS Facilities Near Shipping Corridors in New Jersey

ACQUISITION

Commercial Observer — Tue, 03 Nov 2026 15:29:05 +0000

SUMMARY

Outpost, an industrial outdoor storage (IOS) firm, has acquired two properties in Kearny and Newark, New Jersey, for $68.5 million. The nearly 15-acre acquisition, financed with a $52.2 million loan from Forbright Bank, expands Outpost's national IOS portfolio and will support nearby maritime ports and Newark Liberty International Airport. The firm noted that these properties are critical logistics assets in a supply-constrained market, with one being a stabilized, fully leased asset and the other offering immediate value-add opportunities.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: industrial outdoor storage

Sale Price: $68.5M

Loan Amount: $52.2M

Site Area: 15 acres

Notable Features: The Kearny facility at 76 Central Avenue includes two small office buildings and capacity for 234 shipping containers, 66 trailers, 28 tractors, and 54 cars; the Newark site at 2120 McCarter Highway spans 6.9 acres and can hold 163 55-foot trailers. Both are located near maritime ports and Newark Liberty International Airport.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

BUYER: Outpost — Greg Pearson, Chief Investment Officer; Chenying Zhao, Managing Director

LENDER: Forbright Bank

MORTGAGE BROKER: JLL Capital Markets

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Outpost is deploying a $1 billion fund to target investments in cities near major container ports with dense populations, little undeveloped land, and tight policies around new development. Demand for large, functional, properly zoned logistics facilities continues to grow while such sites are increasingly scarce.
