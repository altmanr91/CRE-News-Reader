Segro Agrees £3B JV With PSP: The London Deal Sheet

DEVELOPMENT

Bisnow News Feed — Thu, 02 Jul 2026 08:59:54 -0400

SUMMARY

Segro and PSP have agreed to form a 50-50 joint venture to develop and operate three major UK logistics parks totaling approximately 10M SF, with a fully developed gross asset value of around £3B. The joint venture will acquire the initial assets, which include 2.4M SF of fully leased properties and 380 acres of developable land across Radlett, Coventry, and Northampton, for approximately £1B. An additional £820M in development capital expenditure is anticipated to complete the parks by 2030, with funding expected from partner equity and nonrecourse third-party debt. The fully let parks are projected to generate £135M in headline rent.
