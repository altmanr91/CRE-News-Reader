U.S. Government Seeks To Downsize EPA's Atlanta Office By 45%

Bisnow News Feed — Thu, 02 Jul 2026 11:23:27 -0400

SUMMARY

Non-CRE / Government Use

The U.S. General Services Administration (GSA) is seeking congressional approval to downsize the Environmental Protection Agency's (EPA) Atlanta office by approximately 45%, moving from its current 324,000 SF space at the federally owned Sam Nunn Atlanta Federal Center to a new leased space of up to 178,000 SF. This move is part of a broader Trump administration initiative to reduce the federal government's real estate footprint and address significant deferred maintenance costs in federal buildings. The GSA noted that the Sam Nunn building requires substantial repairs, and the EPA's space needs have been standardized, allowing for a smaller footprint without staff reductions.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Atlanta's privately owned office market saw corporate leasing activity rise by nearly 500,000 SF to 2.3M SF in Q2, pushing vacancy rates down 230 basis points to 25.6% year-over-year. The Trump administration has terminated 264 federal leases totaling 3.6M SF since January 2025.
