Get to Know the Broker: Joshua Young of Brown Harris Stevens

Commercial Observer — Thu, 22 Oct 2026 11:15:41 +0000

SUMMARY

Feature / Profile

This article profiles Joshua Young, Executive Vice President at Brown Harris Stevens and founder of the Young New Development Group, which specializes in marketing, leasing, and sales for new development projects. Young, who has over 20 years of experience in residential and commercial real estate, emphasizes the rewarding aspect of shaping a project from its inception, including unit mix, layouts, amenities, positioning, and pricing, rather than just focusing on sales numbers. He highlights the importance of collaboration with developers and the satisfaction of seeing these early decisions translate into market success.
