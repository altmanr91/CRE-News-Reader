Cushman & Wakefield Brokers $87M Sale of Apartment Community in Augusta

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Mon, 06 Jul 2026 14:45:57 +0000

SUMMARY

Cushman & Wakefield has brokered the $87 million sale of Mason Augusta, a 462-unit apartment community in Augusta, GA. Waypoint Residential sold the property to Southwood Realty Co. The community was developed in two phases, completed in 2022 and 2024, and was 95 percent occupied at the time of sale.
