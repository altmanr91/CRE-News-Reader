The Boulder Group closes sale of Chase Bank location in Louisville

SALE

REJournals — Tue, 03 Nov 2026 14:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

The Boulder Group facilitated the sale of a single-tenant Chase Bank branch in Louisville, KY, for $2.8 million. The property, operating as a ground lease since 2004, was acquired by a Midwest-based investor from a private seller. The transaction's cap rate was below the retail net lease sector average, reflecting the strength of the investment-grade tenant and the favorable ground lease structure with zero landlord responsibilities and CPI-based rent escalations.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: retail

Property Name: Chase Bank

Address: 2943 Richland Ave., Louisville, KY

Sale Price: $2.8M

Year Built: 2004

Notable Features: Located in a dense retail corridor with convenient access to Interstates 264 and 64, and surrounded by national retailers and medical facilities. The lease includes CPI-based rent escalations every five years with no cap.

Project Notes: The property is secured by a triple net ground lease expiring January 29, 2034, with two 10-year renewal options. The lease carries zero landlord responsibilities.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

The Louisville MSA ranks 43rd in the United States by population size. The retail net lease sector average cap rate for Q2 2026 was 6.60%. Deposits at this Chase Bank branch exceeded $119,000,000 in 2025.
