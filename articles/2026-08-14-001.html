Podcast: How Landlords Can Solve the Tenant Visibility Problem – Sytes’ Rafael Weiss

Commercial Observer — Thu, 13 Aug 2026 21:33:59 +0000

SUMMARY

Non-CRE / Business News

This article features an interview with Rafael Weiss, co-founder of Sytes, a marketplace platform designed to address the information asymmetry in commercial real estate leasing. Sytes connects landlords, developers, and brokers with active tenants, aiming to solve the problem of invisible tenant demand that can lead to missed deals. The platform currently serves a significant number of tenants and lists over 35,000 active sites nationwide, with notable clients including major REITs and retail brands. Weiss, based in Boca Raton, Florida, discusses the platform's functionality, its subscription model, and the broader resurgence of the retail sector.
