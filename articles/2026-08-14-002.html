Barry Sternlicht’s Starwood Scoops Up More Miami-Dade Affordable Housing

ACQUISITION

Commercial Observer — Thu, 13 Aug 2026 21:17:48 +0000

SUMMARY

Starwood Asset Management, led by Barry Sternlicht, has expanded its affordable housing portfolio in Miami-Dade County with the acquisition of two additional properties, Lafayette Plaza and Emerald Terrace, for a combined $51.6 million. These transactions, along with two previous acquisitions last week, bring Starwood's total investment in the area to $115.4 million across 570 units. The properties were sold by Gatehouse Group, with financing for both recent deals provided by CBRE Capital Markets and backed by Freddie Mac.
