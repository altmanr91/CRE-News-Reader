S3 Capital Supplies $20M for Boutique Miami Beach Condo

LOAN

Commercial Observer — Fri, 09 Oct 2026 16:50:55 +0000

SUMMARY

Trinity Southern has secured a $19.5 million construction loan from S3 Capital for its boutique condominium development, Twenty Nine Indian Creek, in Miami Beach. The developer also closed on the $8 million acquisition of the 15,000-square-foot waterfront development site from JMH Development and has broken ground on the project. The eight-story building will feature 16 units, with 10 units already pre-sold, indicating strong market demand for luxury condos in the area.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: condo

Property Name: Twenty Nine Indian Creek

Address: 2901 Indian Creek Drive, Miami Beach, FL

Units: 16

Loan Amount: $19.5M

Loan Per Unit: $1,218,750/unit (calculated)

Notable Features: Eight-story boutique condo development located on the waterfront in the Mid-Beach neighborhood, near the Miami Beach Edition hotel.

Project Notes: The 15,000-square-foot site was previously acquired for $8 million from JMH Development, which had initially proposed the condo development. 10 units have already sold.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

SPONSOR: Trinity Southern — Dana Lozito

LENDER: S3 Capital

SELLER: JMH Development

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Luxury condo developments in Miami Beach and South Florida continue to attract lenders. A Terra-led partnership recently secured a $507 million construction loan for a South Beach condo tower from Tyko Capital, notably before sales had launched. Last year, Mast Capital and Starwood Capital Group obtained $390 million in construction debt for an OMA-designed project in Mid-Beach.
