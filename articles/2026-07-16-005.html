Iris Escarrá of Greenberg Traurig: 5 Questions

Commercial Observer — Wed, 15 Jul 2026 18:05:40 +0000

SUMMARY

Feature / Profile

This article features an interview with Iris Escarrá, co-chair of the land use practice at Greenberg Traurig, discussing her work on high-profile projects in Miami. She highlights the Miami Freedom Park, a 73-acre mixed-use development anchored by Nu Stadium, the home of Inter Miami CF. Escarrá emphasizes that while the stadium is a catalyst, the surrounding retail, hotel, and office components, which will be developed over the next decade, are crucial for creating a vibrant, year-round district and mitigating traffic. She also reflects on Miami's sustained development boom, driven by an influx of new residents and a subsequent demand for diverse property types, and shares insights from her transition from government to private sector law.
