Todd Rosenberg’s Pebb Capital Buys South Beach Office Building

ACQUISITION

Commercial Observer — Thu, 23 Jul 2026 21:50:11 +0000

SUMMARY

Pebb Capital, led by Todd Rosenberg, has acquired the Uno office building in South Beach, Miami, amidst increasing demand from boutique financial firms seeking a presence in the area. The seven-story building, constructed in 2002, offers approximately 100,000 square feet of leasable space and recently underwent renovations, including a 10,000-square-foot expansion by converting a terrace. The transaction was financed with a $69 million loan from Acore Capital, indicating a sale price likely exceeding that amount.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

South Beach is experiencing an increase in new Class A office buildings, driven by demand from wealthy executives seeking to work near their homes. The Fifth, a nearby boutique office building, is nearing completion, and another luxury office project, 1100 Fifth Street, is in the pipeline across the street. Ares Management opened a 10,500-square-foot office at the Eighteen Sunset development in Sunset Harbour, completed in 2024.
