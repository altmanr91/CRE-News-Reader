Law Firms Load Up On Office Space To Handle AI Surge, Regulatory Chaos

Bisnow News Feed — Wed, 01 Jul 2026 15:39:01 -0400

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

Law firms nationwide are significantly expanding their office footprints, reversing a decade-long trend of downsizing. This surge in demand for office space is driven by an explosion in caseloads related to increased artificial intelligence usage, rising regulatory uncertainty, and a more litigious business climate. Firms are signing larger leases, renewing early, and establishing satellite offices in growing markets to compete for specialized legal talent and accommodate new collaboration areas, war rooms, and high-end amenities. The legal sector's office growth reflects its recession-proof nature and consistent office-centricity, with firms strategically investing in placemaking and technology support staff.
