WNC Closes on 23rd California-Focused Affordable Housing Fund

LOAN

Connect CRE — Tue, 08 Sep 2026 21:23:13 +0000

SUMMARY

WNC & Associates, Inc. has successfully closed its WNC Institutional Tax Credit Fund X California Series 23, L.P., raising $66.3 million. This marks the company's 23rd consecutive California-focused affordable housing fund for institutional investors. The fund will support the development of six new-construction affordable housing properties, totaling 650 units, across Los Angeles, San Benito, and San Diego counties, utilizing 4% Low-Income Housing Tax Credits.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Units: 650

Loan Amount: $66.3M

Notable Features: All six properties will serve families and utilize 4% Low-Income Housing Tax Credits.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

LENDER: WNC & Associates, Inc. — Will Cooper Jr., President and CEO
