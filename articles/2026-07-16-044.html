Janeese Lewis George Wants To Stabilize Rent. The Question Is How

Bisnow News Feed — Wed, 15 Jul 2026 18:27:54 -0400

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

Presumptive D.C. mayor-elect Janeese Lewis George is prioritizing tenant protection and rent stabilization, but her specific policy proposals remain undefined. Her advisors indicate that all options are being considered, emphasizing a desire for predictable housing costs for residents. However, real estate developers and investors express significant concern that expanding rent control, or implementing a rent freeze, would deter investment and new housing production in the city, ultimately hindering affordability goals. The debate highlights a fundamental disagreement between tenant advocates, who seek expanded rent stabilization, and the development community, which argues that such policies stifle supply and investment.
