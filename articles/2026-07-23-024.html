Mamdani turns to familiar figure to lead EDC

The Real Deal — New York — Wed, 22 Jul 2026 15:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Non-CRE / Government Use

Zohran Mamdani has appointed Anthony Shorris as the new president and CEO of the New York City Economic Development Corporation (EDC), filling a seven-month vacancy. Shorris, a veteran of New York City government with experience across multiple administrations and sectors, will lead the critical agency responsible for managing over 60 million square feet of real estate and overseeing more than 100 active development projects totaling $6 billion in construction investment. The EDC plays a significant role in city land development, business incentives, and other strategic initiatives, including piloting city-owned grocery stores.

DATA POINTS

EDC active projects: 100+

EDC construction investment: $6 billion
