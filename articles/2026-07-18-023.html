Florida’s largest Live Local Act project is bold. Its ambitious developer is controversial

The Real Deal — Miami — Fri, 17 Jul 2026 14:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Development

Pablo Castro, a Spanish developer, is facing multiple lawsuits related to his ambitious HueHub project in West Little River, Florida. The project, which is the largest known multifamily complex planned under Florida's Live Local Act, is designed to include 4,040 attainably priced units across seven 34-story towers. Castro is accused by a local investor and developer, an architecture firm, and a chamber of commerce of reneging on agreements and failing to compensate them for their foundational work on the project. Despite the legal challenges, Castro maintains his innocence and is proceeding with the development, which aims to address Miami-Dade County's significant need for affordable housing.

SPONSOR PIPELINE

Various residential projects in Spain: Developed over 15,000 residential units in Spain over 30 years, including through a joint venture with the Grifols family.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Miami-Dade County has an estimated need for approximately 90,000 attainably priced housing units.
