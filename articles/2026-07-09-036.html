JVP’s spec office construction underway at $3B Frisco project The Mix

CONSTRUCTION

The Real Deal — Texas — Wed, 08 Jul 2026 18:45:00 +0000

SUMMARY

JVP Development has commenced construction on a 110,000-square-foot speculative office building within The Mix, a sprawling 112-acre mixed-use development in Frisco, TX. This office project is a significant step in the multi-phase, multi-decade development, which will eventually include retail, two hotels, a 9-acre park, and 635 apartments. The development is strategically located near other major Frisco landmarks and is expected to attract strong tenant interest due to its blend of amenities and green space.
