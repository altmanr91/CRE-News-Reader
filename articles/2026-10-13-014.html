Lee & Associates closes 10,000-square-foot industrial lease in Haltom City

LEASE

REJournals — Tue, 13 Oct 2026 15:06:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Lee & Associates Dallas-Fort Worth recently facilitated a new lease agreement for a 10,000-square-foot industrial property in Haltom City, Texas. The tenant, Elasco Urethane, an experienced leader in polyurethane elastomer technology, secured the space. The landlord, SE & AE Investments, was represented by LanCarte Commercial.
