CBRE Adds Freddie Mac’s Robert Koontz to Lead Multifamily Debt Capital Markets

Commercial Observer — Mon, 13 Jul 2026 20:40:22 +0000

SUMMARY

Promotion

CBRE has appointed Robert Koontz as the new head of multifamily debt capital markets, a strategic move aimed at expanding the brokerage's multifamily loan business. Koontz, who previously led multifamily capital markets at Freddie Mac for 17 years, will focus on enhancing CBRE's balance sheet and risk transfer strategies, including bridge debt offerings. His appointment comes as CBRE's multifamily debt origination volume has seen significant growth, with a 60 percent increase this year following over $30 billion in originations in 2025.
