AT&T Signs Five-Year Extension on Retail Space at 3 Times Square

LEASE

Connect CRE — Wed, 21 Oct 2026 21:38:37 +0000

SUMMARY

AT&T has renewed its 6,623-square-foot retail lease at 3 Times Square for another five years. The space includes a ground-floor storefront on Seventh Avenue and lower-level space. This renewal underscores the continued appeal of Times Square as a high-traffic retail destination, following recent capital improvements to the building by the landlord.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: retail

Property Name: 3 Times Square

Size: 6,623 SF

Year Built: 2001

Notable Features: Ground-floor storefront on Seventh Avenue and lower-level space; building recently underwent extensive capital improvements.
