Marcus & Millichap Brokers Sale of 94,532 SF Shopping Center in Wichita Falls, Texas

SALE

REBusinessOnline — Mon, 03 Aug 2026 14:58:03 +0000

SUMMARY

Marcus & Millichap has brokered the sale of Parker Square Shopping Center, a 94,532-square-foot retail center in Wichita Falls, Texas. The property, built in 1955 and anchored by a fitness center, was 97 percent leased at the time of sale. Both the seller and buyer requested anonymity in the transaction.
