Bon appetit! Moishe Mana buys Wynwood building leased to Pastis for $25M

ACQUISITION

The Real Deal — Miami — Thu, 09 Jul 2026 21:32:30 +0000

SUMMARY

Moishe Mana, a prominent landowner in Wynwood, has acquired a 13,000-square-foot building and its ground lease in Wynwood, Miami, for $24.5 million. The property, fully leased to a high-end Parisian brasserie and a luxury lamp retailer, was sold by TriCap. This transaction highlights the increasing appeal of Wynwood for luxury retail and dining establishments, with the broker noting the significance of the brasserie's opening as a turning point for the neighborhood.
