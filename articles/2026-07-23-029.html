$16M West Palm mansion breaks city’s record for priciest non-waterfront sale

SALE

The Real Deal — Miami — Wed, 22 Jul 2026 19:30:00 +0000

SUMMARY

A newly constructed luxury mansion in West Palm Beach has sold for $15.7 million, setting a new record for the city's most expensive non-waterfront single-family home transaction. The sale of the 8,600-square-foot property reflects a growing trend of buyers seeking more spacious and relatively affordable luxury inland properties, especially as coastal prices and insurance premiums continue to rise. The transaction highlights the increasing demand for high-end homes away from the immediate waterfront, with prices per square foot in coastal West Palm Beach still considered a relative bargain compared to Palm Beach island.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Coastal West Palm Beach homes are trading for $1,500 to $2,000 per square foot, while non-waterfront properties on Palm Beach island regularly trade for $3,000 to $4,000 per square foot. Inland luxury properties set a record last year with 123 transactions totaling $2.12 billion, a 65 percent increase in value and 41 percent increase in volume.
