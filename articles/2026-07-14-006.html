StreetLights Residential Completes 134-Unit Apartment Building in Houston

CONSTRUCTION

REBusinessOnline — Mon, 13 Jul 2026 15:29:26 +0000

SUMMARY

StreetLights Residential has completed The Langley, a 134-unit luxury apartment building in Houston's Museum District, near Rice University. The 20-story development offers spacious two- and three-bedroom units with high-end finishes and smart technology. The property features extensive indoor and outdoor amenities, with leasing having commenced in February.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: The Langley

Address: 1717 Bissonnet St, Houston, TX*

Units: 134

Completion: July 2026

Notable Features: Luxury apartment building with units ranging from 2,165 to 3,396 square feet, featuring walk-in closets, wine coolers, smart technology, and service kitchens with secondary refrigerators. Amenities include a pool, grilling stations, outdoor yoga space, dog run, fitness center, lounge, library, coffee bar, and conference room.

Project Notes: Monthly rents start at $9,480. Leasing began in February 2026.

* Address sourced separately, not in original article.
