Prime Finance Refis Denver-Area Apartments With $46M Loan

REFINANCE

Commercial Observer — Thu, 02 Jul 2026 17:30:48 +0000

SUMMARY

A joint venture between Oak Coast Properties and BMC Investments secured a $46.25 million floating-rate loan from Prime Finance to refinance the 388-unit Aspen Park apartment community in Northglenn, CO. The property is strategically located in a well-connected suburban community north of Downtown Denver, offering residents strong access to employment, transportation, and amenities.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Aspen Park

Address: 301 East Malley Drive, Northglenn, CO

Units: 388

Loan Amount: $46.2M

Loan Per Unit: $119,201/unit (calculated)

Notable Features: Garden-style apartments with a clubhouse, fitness center, indoor pool, business center, and game room.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

SPONSOR: Oak Coast Properties

SPONSOR: BMC Investments

LENDER: Prime Finance

MORTGAGE BROKER: Walker & Dunlop — Dustin Stolly, Senior Managing Director of Capital Markets Institutional Advisory; Justin Nelson; Aaron Appel; Jonathan Schwartz; Keith Kurland; Adam Schwartz; Sean Reimer; Michael Ianno; Eric Norris; Jason Schwartzberg
