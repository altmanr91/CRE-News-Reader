RAMROCK Buys West Fort Worth Mall, Plans 930,960 SF Industrial Redevelopment

ACQUISITION

REBusinessOnline — Thu, 08 Oct 2026 15:19:23 +0000

SUMMARY

RAMROCK Real Estate has acquired Ridgmar Mall in West Fort Worth, Texas, with plans to redevelop the site into a 930,960-square-foot industrial park named Ridgmar 30 Logistics Crossing. The project will feature six buildings, and RAMROCK is partnering with KBC Advisors and Lincoln Property Co. for planning, leasing, and development execution.
