Westfield Montgomery Owner Pays Off $350M Loan on Bethesda, Md., Mall

REFINANCE

Commercial Observer — Wed, 02 Sep 2026 20:35:36 +0000

SUMMARY

Unibail-Rodamco-Westfield (URW) has successfully paid off a $350 million loan secured by the Westfield Montgomery shopping mall in Bethesda, MD. This payoff is notable given the current market cycle where many mall loans are receiving extensions, and it occurred more than two years after the debt was transferred to special servicing. The 10-year loan, originated in 2014, was tied to 836,000 square feet of the 1.2 million-square-foot property and had received a two-year extension after missing its original August 2024 maturity date.
