Infinium Wall Systems Takes 30K SF of Office, Retail Space at ESRT’s 1359 Broadway

LEASE

Commercial Observer — Mon, 20 Jul 2026 20:30:52 +0000

SUMMARY

Infinium Wall Systems, a designer and installer of office wall systems, has signed a new lease for 30,017 square feet of office and retail space at 1359 Broadway in Midtown Manhattan. The tenant was drawn to the building's quality, sustainable practices, and the opportunity to create a differentiated flagship space with features like southern exposure, a private entrance, and an outdoor terrace. This transaction reflects the continued demand from innovative companies for modernized, well-located office space in New York City.
