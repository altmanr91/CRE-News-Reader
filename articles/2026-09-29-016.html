Rental Condos Debut in N. Miami

DEVELOPMENT

Connect CRE — Tue, 29 Sep 2026 15:04:10 +0000

SUMMARY

Fortune International Group and Blue Road have delivered Nexo Residences, a 16-story short-term rental condominium project in North Miami Beach. The development, built by KAST Construction, features 254 fully furnished units ranging from studios to three-bedroom floorplans, as well as three- and four-bedroom townhomes. Owners will benefit from rental flexibility managed by AvantStay, a hospitality management company, with an on-site team handling guest services.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: condo

Property Name: Nexo Residences

Address: 13899 Biscayne Blvd., North Miami Beach, FL

Size: 2,438 SF

Units: 254

Notable Features: Units are delivered fully furnished and offer short-term rental flexibility; amenities include a tech hub with coworking spaces, a luxury pool deck, fitness center, and a rooftop observatory deck.

COMPANIES/PEOPLE

DEVELOPER/SPONSOR: Blue Road
