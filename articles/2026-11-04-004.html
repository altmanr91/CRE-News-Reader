New York City Apartment Rents Reach Record Highs: Report

Commercial Observer — Wed, 04 Nov 2026 12:00:42 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Rental Report

New data from Realtor.com indicates that New York City apartment rents reached record highs in the third quarter of 2026, with the median asking rent climbing to $3,785 per month, a 4.5% year-over-year increase. This marks the highest rent recorded since data collection began in 2019, surpassing pre-pandemic levels by 27.5%. The report highlights a challenging affordability landscape for renters, particularly for smaller units, as rising rents coincide with significantly higher homeownership costs driven by elevated mortgage rates.
