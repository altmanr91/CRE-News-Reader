Former Hotel-Turned-University-Campus Trades for Repositioning

SALE

Connect CRE — Fri, 28 Aug 2026 21:53:32 +0000

SUMMARY

A former hotel that was converted into a university campus in Lancaster, CA, has been sold for $14.4 million. The buyer, 44049 Sierra Hwy Propco, LLC, plans to reposition the 87,598-square-foot property into a regional hub for community and social services in collaboration with Los Angeles County. The seller was University of Antelope Valley, LLC, which previously operated a private university on the site until its closure in 2024. The property's flexible layout and existing infrastructure were key factors in its suitability for a new mission-driven use.
