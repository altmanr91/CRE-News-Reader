Endeavor Real Estate Group provides financing for 265-unit multifamily property in Dallas’ Uptown neighborhood

LOAN

REJournals — Wed, 15 Jul 2026 17:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Endeavor Real Estate Group, in partnership with Canyon Partners Real Estate, has secured construction financing from Helaba for Lucille, a 22-story luxury multifamily tower in Dallas' Uptown neighborhood. The 265-unit project, which will also include 10,000 square feet of ground-floor retail, is expected to break ground later this summer and open in late 2028. The development is strategically located in a highly walkable area with strong job growth, aiming to attract working professionals with its best-in-class amenities and prominent visibility.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: Lucille

Address: 2700 McKinney Avenue, Dallas, TX

Units: 265

Completion: Q4 2028

Site Area: 1.14 acres

Notable Features: 22-story luxury tower with 10,000 SF ground-floor retail, rooftop pool deck, and a Walk Score of 97; located adjacent to the historic State Thomas neighborhood.

Project Notes: Endeavor has been working since 2018 to bring this project to fruition, including rezoning and design efforts with city staff and stakeholders.
