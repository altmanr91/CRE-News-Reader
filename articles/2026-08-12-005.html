SME Capital Forced to Hand Over Hudson Yards Condo Building to Residents

Commercial Observer — Tue, 11 Aug 2026 19:01:09 +0000

SUMMARY

CRE Legal / Regulatory

SME Capital Ventures is transferring ownership of 441 West 37th Street, a nine-unit condominium building in Hudson Yards, to its condominium board following an investigation by the New York Attorney General's office. The firm, which acquired the property out of foreclosure in 2023, failed to pay bills and address safety violations, leading to a settlement that includes a $700,000 payment and the handover of control to the homeowners. The Attorney General emphasized that private equity firms must adhere to legal responsibilities to homeowners, while SME Capital's attorney stated the firm resolved the matter to alleviate residents' suffering caused by the original developer's conduct.
