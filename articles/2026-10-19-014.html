Noah Properties, Daniel Management Group celebrate opening of 220-unit multifamily property in Buffalo Grove

DEVELOPMENT

REJournals — Mon, 19 Oct 2026 13:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Noah Properties and Daniel Management Group have announced the opening of 945 Nanusha Residences, a new 220-unit multifamily property in Buffalo Grove, Illinois. The luxury development offers a variety of floor plans and resort-style amenities, including a landscaped courtyard with a pool, fitness center, and co-working spaces, catering to both community engagement and modern living needs.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: multifamily

Property Name: 945 Nanusha Residences

Address: 945 N Old Arlington Heights Rd, Buffalo Grove, IL*

Units: 220

Notable Features: Luxurious new development with high-end finishes, resort-style amenities including a landscaped courtyard with a pool, fire pit lounges, grilling stations, state-of-the-art fitness center, private walking path, sauna, dog park, dog spa, co-working spaces, Wi-Fi access, and a complimentary coffee bar.
