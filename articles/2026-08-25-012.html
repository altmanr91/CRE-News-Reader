The Boulder Group closes sale of Huntington Bank branch in Buffalo Grove

SALE

REJournals — Mon, 24 Aug 2026 15:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

The Boulder Group recently facilitated the sale of a single-tenant net-leased Huntington Bank branch in Buffalo Grove, Illinois, for $2.77 million. The property, situated on a prominent corner with high traffic counts, was acquired by a West Coast-based individual investor from a Midwest-based real estate fund. The transaction highlights the continued demand for well-located, net-leased assets with strong tenant commitment, as Huntington Bank recently exercised a renewal option, extending its lease through July 2031.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: retail

Property Name: Huntington Bank branch

Address: 401 Deerfield Pkwy, Buffalo Grove, IL

Size: 4,680 SF

Sale Price: $2.8M

$/SF: $592/SF (calculated)

Year Built: 1995

Site Area: 2.5 acres

Notable Features: Located on a signalized hard corner with five drive-thru lanes, adjacent to a major industrial corridor, and walkable to the Buffalo Grove Metra Station.

Project Notes: Huntington Bank operates under a triple net (NNN) lease expiring July 31, 2031, with two five-year renewal options. Rental escalations are the greater of ten percent or the cumulative change in the Consumer Price Index at each renewal option period. Huntington Bank exercised its most recent renewal option in December 2025.
