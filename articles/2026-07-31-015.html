Gerdom Realty helps bring new tenant to Brownstown retail center

LEASE

REJournals — Fri, 31 Jul 2026 09:50:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Gerdom Realty and Investment facilitated a new lease for a retail tenant, Smoker's Vibes of Brownstown, at a 1,500-square-foot freestanding building in Brownstown, Michigan. The property, formerly occupied by KeyBank, is located on Telegraph Road and is expected to open later this year.
