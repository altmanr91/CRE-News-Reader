Integra Acquires Evacuated Broward Condo Complex Via Court-Ordered Auction

ACQUISITION

Bisnow News Feed — Fri, 24 Jul 2026 10:23:13 -0400

SUMMARY

Integra Investments has acquired the 304-unit Heron Pond condo complex in Pembroke Pines, Florida, for $20.5 million through a court-ordered receivership auction. The 37-year-old, 19-building complex, originally built as apartments in 1988 and converted to condos in 2000, was deemed unsafe and evacuated in summer 2024 due to severe maintenance issues. Integra plans to pursue entitlements for the 25-acre site, which could take up to two years, before deciding on its development strategy, potentially including a partnership or resale. The site's current zoning allows for 321 units, with potential for more through upzoning.
