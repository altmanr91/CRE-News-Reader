Grover Corlew Buys Discounted Fort Lauderdale Tower for $70M

ACQUISITION

Commercial Observer — Thu, 22 Oct 2026 21:20:48 +0000

SUMMARY

Grover Corlew has acquired the 21-story 200 East Broward office tower in Downtown Fort Lauderdale for $69.7 million, marking another discounted office sale in the South Florida market. The buyer plans a $20 million renovation over the next two years to add an amenity deck, gym, and conference facilities. The transaction represents a significant loss for the seller, Banyan Street Capital, which had purchased the property for $81.5 million in 2017.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Other Fort Lauderdale office towers have recently sold at a discount, including the 30-story 110 Tower for $89 million (21% less than its prior sale price) and two office buildings sold by DWS for over $400 million (approximately their acquisition cost).
