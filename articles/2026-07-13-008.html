Texas Icon Nikhil Dhanani: Building an Institutional-Grade Real Estate Platform from a Family Enterprise

REJournals — Mon, 13 Jul 2026 09:44:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Feature / Profile

This article profiles Nikhil Dhanani, President of Dhanani Private Equity Group (DPEG), highlighting his role in transforming a family-owned operation into a vertically integrated, institutional-grade real estate platform. Over the past decade, Dhanani has been instrumental in scaling DPEG's assets under management from approximately $20 million to over $2.5 billion, diversifying its portfolio across retail, multifamily, and mixed-use developments. His leadership emphasizes disciplined execution, proactive asset management, and strategic repositioning of assets to create long-term value, particularly through attracting national and lifestyle brands to DPEG's retail centers.
