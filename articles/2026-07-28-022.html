Self-Storage Sector Bounces Back As Investors Move Past Market 'Trough'

Bisnow News Feed — Mon, 27 Jul 2026 09:57:01 -0400

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

The self-storage sector is experiencing a rebound in investment activity after a period of decline caused by high interest rates and reduced home sales. While overall sales volume in the first half of 2026 was down compared to the previous year, the price per square foot for self-storage units has significantly increased, indicating renewed investor confidence. Industry experts believe the market has bottomed out and is now on an upward trajectory, driven by a slow but steady recovery in U.S. home sales and the sector's proven resilience during economic downturns.
