Brightshore Acquires Newly Built Long Island City Luxury Apartments

ACQUISITION

Connect CRE — Mon, 02 Nov 2026 19:00:29 +0000

SUMMARY

Brightshore Capital LP, operating through its Opportunity Zone Fund II, has acquired DŌMI, a newly constructed 190-unit luxury multifamily property in Long Island City, for over $143 million. The acquisition was supported by a $98.5 million loan from Affinius Capital. The property's appeal stems from Long Island City's strong residential growth, excellent transportation, and ongoing infrastructure investments, which Brightshore believes will drive long-term value given the limited new development opportunities in the area.
