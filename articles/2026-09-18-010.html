Lanoha developing 150,000-square-foot office building in Omaha’s Heartwood Preserve

DEVELOPMENT

REJournals — Fri, 18 Sep 2026 13:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Lanoha is developing a 150,000-square-foot trophy office building, named The Row, within Omaha's Heartwood Preserve mixed-use development. The building will serve as the future headquarters for a prominent law firm. Construction is slated to begin in late 2027, with completion expected in early 2030, bringing a significant office presence to the district's urban center.

DATA POINTS

Property Type: office

Property Name: The Row

Address: 145th Street and Canopy Avenue, Omaha, NE

Size: 150,000 SF

Completion: early 2030

Notable Features: Modern architecture, elevated finishes, and contemporary amenities with direct access to dining, retail, and green spaces; located directly across from the 18-acre Heartwood Park.

Project Notes: Construction expected to begin in late 2027.
