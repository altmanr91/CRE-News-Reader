A calmer U.S. industrial market? That’s what Colliers has found

REJournals — Tue, 21 Jul 2026 10:02:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Industrial Report

Colliers' latest U.S. industrial report indicates a stabilizing market where demand is now outpacing new supply, leading to a slight decrease in the national vacancy rate to 7.3% in Q2 2026. This marks a significant shift after two years of increasing vacancies, with 63% of tracked markets showing stable or declining rates. Leasing demand strengthened considerably, with net absorption nearly doubling year-over-year to 59 million square feet, predominantly in the South. New deliveries have slowed to their lowest quarterly total since 2016, helping to rebalance the market, though the construction pipeline is growing again, reaching 312 million square feet. Rental rates are also stabilizing, with average asking rents at $10.34 per square foot, reflecting modest corrections in some coastal markets.

Housing Notes: Explaining the silver tsunami with surveys

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

This article analyzes two seemingly contradictory surveys regarding Baby Boomer housing preferences: one by Freddie Mac suggesting most boomers plan to downsize, and another by NAR indicating boomers are buying larger homes. Buckling scare puts MetroLoft’s ‘scoop-and-stack’ playbook under microscope

The Real Deal — New York — Mon, 20 Jul 2026 18:30:59 +0000

SUMMARY

Development / Structural Incident

A structural failure at MetroLoft's conversion of Pfizer's former Midtown headquarters at 235 East 42nd Street in New York City has drawn attention to the developer's "scoop-and-stack" redevelopment technique. This method involves hollowing out lower floors to create lightwells and transferring development potential to new floors added at the top, addressing the challenge of deep floor plates in postwar office-to-residential conversions. While the incident is under investigation, the developer attributed the buckling to insufficiently reinforced columns rather than a flaw in the overall concept, with missing reinforcing steel plates identified as a potential cause.
