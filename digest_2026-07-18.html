Housing Notes: Road to nowhere? 21st Century ROAD to Housing Act is now law

The Real Deal — National — Fri, 17 Jul 2026 14:00:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

The "21st Century ROAD to Housing Act," a bipartisan housing package, aims to increase housing supply and refine federal housing programs through over 40 provisions. While presented as transformative, the author argues its impact on affordability will likely be incremental because it primarily repackages existing tools rather than introducing new solutions or significant funding. The act focuses on streamlining new construction, supporting manufactured housing, modernizing federal programs, and limiting large institutional investor purchases of single-family homes. However, the author contends it critically misses addressing the mortgage-rate "lock-in effect," which is identified as the primary driver of low existing housing inventory and a major contributor to the affordability crisis. The author suggests that expanding capital-gains exclusions on home sales, a measure not included in the act, would be more effective in bringing existing homes to market.

SUMMARY

Construction

A structural failure at the former Pfizer Building, undergoing an office-to-residential conversion, was reportedly caused by the omission of critical steel reinforcement plates in two columns.
