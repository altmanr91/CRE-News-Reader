MARKET: LOS ANGELES, CA

L.A. Industrial Vacancy Falls for First Time in Two Years

Commercial Observer — Fri, 16 Oct 2026 17:30:21 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Industrial Report

The Los Angeles County industrial market experienced its first annual decline in vacancy in two years during the third quarter, driven by 1.9 million square feet of positive net absorption. This marks the fourth consecutive quarter of positive absorption, bringing the year-to-date total to 5.1 million square feet. Demand was particularly strong for larger warehouses, while smaller properties continued to struggle with negative absorption. Despite improving occupancy, average direct asking rents remained flat, still significantly below peak levels from 2023.

DATA POINTS

Q3 2026 Net Absorption: 1.9 million square feet

Year-to-Date Net Absorption: 5.1 million square feet

Vacancy Rate: 5 percent

Q3 2026 Leasing Activity: 13.7 million square feet

Q3 2026 Absorption for properties >= 250,000 SF: 2.1 million square feet

Q3 2026 Absorption for properties < 50,000 SF: -320,000 square feet

Year-to-Date Absorption for properties < 50,000 SF: -1.43 million square feet

Average Direct Asking Rent (triple-net): $1.19 per square foot per month

Q3 2026 Construction Completed: 717,000 square feet

Construction Pipeline: 4.48 million square feet

Speculative Projects Scheduled for Q4 Delivery: 1.42 million square feet

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Los Angeles County's industrial market saw its vacancy rate fall 20 basis points quarter-over-quarter and year-over-year to 5 percent. Leasing activity increased 6.5 percent year-over-year but declined 15.7 percent from the second quarter. Average direct asking rents held steady at $1.19 per square foot per month, which is 4 percent below the average from a year ago and 30.4 percent below the Q3 2023 peak of $1.71 per square foot. Central L.A. led in absorption with 1.11 million square feet, while the South Bay was the most active leasing market with 4.46 million square feet of transactions. The construction pipeline expanded for the first time since early 2025.
