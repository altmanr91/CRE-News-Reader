Commercial Mortgage-Backed Securities Distress Is Peaking — Again

Commercial Observer — Mon, 31 Aug 2026 10:55:53 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

The commercial mortgage-backed securities (CMBS) market is experiencing a significant increase in distress, with the overall distress rate approaching a peak and office distress reaching an all-time high of 8.89 percent in July. A substantial "maturity wall" is looming, with $37 billion in CMBS loans facing hard maturities by the end of 2026, particularly concentrated in the fourth quarter. While some experts remain optimistic, citing aggressive loan resolutions and robust new loan origination activity, others express concern over the sheer volume of maturities coinciding with elevated interest rates and tightening refinancing liquidity. The market is also bifurcated, with multifamily assets struggling due to stagnant operating incomes and rising costs, while the office sector shows unexpected resilience, especially in markets like San Francisco and Manhattan, driven by return-to-office policies and demand for Class B space.

Legal Sector Reaches Quarterly Leasing Record of 7 Million Square Feet

Connect CRE — Mon, 31 Aug 2026 15:11:33 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Office Leasing Report

The legal sector demonstrated exceptional strength in the second quarter of 2026, achieving a record 7.3 million square feet in office leasing activity, a 27% increase year-over-year. This robust performance contributed significantly to the overall office market, with legal firms accounting for 14% of total office leasing across 10 major markets. New York City led this surge, representing 30% of the quarterly volume, followed by strong activity in Chicago and the D.C. Metro area. The report anticipates continued positive contributions from the legal sector to U.S. office demand through the remainder of 2026.
