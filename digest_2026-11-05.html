For Mall Giant Macerich, More Turnaround in Q3

Commercial Observer — Thu, 05 Nov 2026 13:40:26 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Earnings Report

Mall owner Macerich reported improved financial performance in the third quarter of 2026, with net operating income and revenue increasing year-over-year, signaling progress in its "Path Forward Plan." The Co-op Comeback: New York Buyers Are Reconsidering the Condo Trade-Off

Commercial Observer — Thu, 05 Nov 2026 12:00:54 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

New York City's residential market is seeing a resurgence in co-op appeal as buyers reconsider the long-standing preference for condominiums. The increasing cost of new condo developments, coupled with the significant price advantage of co-ops, is driving this shift. Some co-op boards are also adapting by streamlining application processes and easing subletting restrictions, addressing historical concerns that made co-ops less attractive. This trend is particularly noticeable in the luxury market, where Manhattan co-ops recently surpassed condos in sales for the first time since 2022, indicating a broader market re-evaluation of the co-op trade-off.

ULI Emerging Trends Report: Manhattan, Jersey City, Among Top 10 Markets to Watch in 2027

REBusinessOnline — Thu, 05 Nov 2026 15:39:32 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Emerging Trends Report

The annual Emerging Trends in Real Estate 2027 report, a collaboration between the Urban Land Institute (ULI) and PwC, identifies Manhattan and Jersey City as top markets to watch, ranking them 7th and 8th respectively. Manhattan's significant improvement from its 2022 ranking of 42nd is attributed to diversified strengthening across office, retail, and tourism sectors, bolstered by a substantial infrastructure pipeline. The report highlights AI companies as a key driver of office absorption in Manhattan, alongside planned office-to-residential conversions. Jersey City continues to be a strong performer due to its robust multifamily construction and its appeal as a more affordable, well-connected alternative to Manhattan.
