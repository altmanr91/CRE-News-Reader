Power Tech & AI 2026

Commercial Observer — Tue, 06 Oct 2026 12:00:48 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

This article highlights the pervasive influence of artificial intelligence (AI) across various facets of commercial real estate (CRE), from data analysis and building operations to leasing, sales, and lending. AI is identified as the primary driver behind the robust data center market and is being integrated into established software-as-a-service (SaaS) platforms. While the industry is currently in a "honeymoon period" with AI, experts anticipate an accelerated pace of adoption, particularly within institutional real estate. However, the number of standalone AI firms serving CRE is expected to consolidate due to competition and the efficiencies brought about by AI itself.

CREFC Sentiment Index Reaches Lowest Level in Three Years

Connect CRE — Tue, 06 Oct 2026 14:49:30 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Sentiment Index

The CRE Finance Council's (CREFC) Third-Quarter 2026 Board of Governors Sentiment Index has dropped to its lowest point in three years, falling 17.5% to 83.3. This broad-based decline reflects significant concerns among members regarding the negative impact of mortgage and capitalization rates on commercial real estate finance businesses, with 92% of respondents anticipating adverse effects. The sentiment also indicates a growing pessimism about the U.S. economy's performance over the next 12 months, with 62% expecting a worsening outlook, driven by worries extending beyond immediate Federal Reserve decisions to broader issues like inflation, fiscal deficits, and long-term borrowing costs.

Miami-Dade Office Rents Rise and Vacancy Tightens

Commercial Observer — Mon, 05 Oct 2026 22:00:26 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Office Report

Miami-Dade County's office market continues to demonstrate strength with declining vacancy rates and increasing asking rents, according to a new report from Savills. The market's activity is notably bifurcated, with significant leasing in both the affordable Airport West submarket and the high-end Brickell financial district.
