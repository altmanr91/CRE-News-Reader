Manhattan’s Available Office Supply Falls to Lowest Level Since 2020: Colliers

Commercial Observer — Thu, 01 Oct 2026 15:14:03 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Office Report

Manhattan's office market demonstrated robust performance in the third quarter of 2026, with leasing volume surpassing both five-year and 10-year quarterly averages, according to Colliers.
