The AI Infrastructure Industry Has Entered Its Coordination Crisis

Commercial Observer — Mon, 05 Oct 2026 17:59:49 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

The AI infrastructure industry is facing a "coordination crisis" where the disparate timelines for power, cooling, electrical equipment, permitting, and construction are hindering project delivery, despite individual components being available. The author argues that the primary bottleneck is no longer a single resource but the complex orchestration required to align these various systems and stakeholders. This challenge is transforming AI infrastructure development from a traditional real estate play into a more integrated industrial systems engineering and energy infrastructure endeavor, favoring firms capable of coordinating diverse elements to deliver functional campuses on customer timelines.

Steve Wylder On Safehold’s Plan to Fill Affordable Housing Capital Gaps

Commercial Observer — Mon, 05 Oct 2026 17:55:38 +0000

SUMMARY

Opinion / Market Commentary

Safehold, a real estate investment trust, is leveraging its ground lease strategy to address capital stack deficiencies in affordable housing developments, particularly for 4 percent Low-Income Housing Tax Credit (LIHTC) projects. The company's 99-year ground leases provide low-cost capital at a premium to land value, increasing permanent proceeds for developers by 10 to 20 percent and offering a lower blended cost of capital. This approach helps bridge financing gaps caused by elevated interest rates and construction costs, which have made affordable housing projects increasingly challenging to complete. Safehold has seen significant traction in Southern California, where it has closed over 25 ground leases for LIHTC developments, and is expanding its platform into markets like Texas, the Southeast, Mid-Atlantic, and Midwest.
