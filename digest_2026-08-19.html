At New York City Council Hearing on Pied-à-Terre Tax, Complaints of a Sloppy Rollout

Commercial Observer — Tue, 18 Aug 2026 18:42:46 +0000

SUMMARY

CRE Legal / Regulatory

The New York City Council held a hearing to evaluate the problematic rollout of the city's new pied-à-terre tax, which has been criticized for its sloppy implementation and the publication of a broad list of potential second homes. Councilmember Gale Brewer highlighted that the Department of Finance's list included up to 900,000 properties, far exceeding estimates of actual second homes, and placed an undue burden on homeowners to prove primary residency. The Real Estate Board of New York also expressed concerns about the tax's administration, urging the city to pause implementation until it can ensure fairness and accuracy. Despite a temporary restraining order, an appeals court has allowed the city to continue implementing the tax while litigation is pending.
