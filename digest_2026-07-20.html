SUMMARY

Market Research / Retail Expansion

H Mart, a Korean grocery chain, is aggressively expanding into Sun Belt markets including Florida, Texas, California, and Nevada, capitalizing on its growing popularity among both Asian American communities and a broader American audience. A bounce-back quarter for Minneapolis’ industrial sector

REJournals — Mon, 20 Jul 2026 10:05:00 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Industrial Report

The Minneapolis-St. Paul industrial market experienced a rebound in the second quarter, achieving nearly 488,000 square feet of positive net absorption, a significant improvement from the previous quarter's negative figures. This positive absorption occurred despite several large tenant move-outs, driven by strong demand for modern industrial facilities. While overall vacancy rates edged up slightly to 4.4% due to older building vacancies, and average asking rents softened to $9.12 per square foot, new construction activity accelerated with 3.4 million square feet under development, indicating continued investment in high-quality industrial space.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

The Minneapolis-St. Paul industrial market saw a 20-basis-point increase in overall vacancy from the previous quarter and a 30-basis-point increase year-over-year. However, properties built in 2022 or later experienced a 20-basis-point decline in vacancy. Average asking rents declined 2% quarterly and 4.3% year-over-year, but remain 3.4% higher than three years ago. Leasing activity was down 24.4% from Q1 and 27.1% year-over-year, but the average lease size increased by nearly 17% quarterly and 20% year-over-year.

SUMMARY

The Evanston City Council has approved zoning for a five-story, 30-unit apartment complex at 1103 and 1105 Emerson Street, despite objections from the adjacent Ebenezer AME Church. The church raised concerns about the development's height, density, potential impact on its structural integrity, and noise from future tenants. As a result, the developer, PM Properties, agreed to a "good neighbor" ordinance, which includes specific requirements for building materials, noise monitoring, and a taller fence for the church's parking lot.
