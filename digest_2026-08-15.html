Survey: 61% of Investors Hold Negative Multifamily Outlook in 2026, Per Berkadia

Commercial Observer — Fri, 14 Aug 2026 18:02:16 +0000

SUMMARY

Market Research / Investor Survey

A recent survey by Berkadia indicates that 61% of real estate investors hold a negative outlook for the multifamily market in the second half of 2026, primarily due to persistent capital markets volatility. This sentiment contrasts with earlier optimism, as 52% of respondents reported worse-than-anticipated performance in the first half of the year. Despite this caution, investors are not losing confidence in multifamily as an asset class, with 82% planning portfolio expansion and 83% expecting market improvement by late 2027. The shift reflects a greater focus on execution risk, with investors scrutinizing rent growth assumptions, exit cap rates, and financing structures more closely, prioritizing core strategies over value-add opportunities. The Midwest region has emerged as a preferred investment destination, favored by 58% of investors for its durable cash flow potential and stronger rental growth assumptions compared to other regions.
